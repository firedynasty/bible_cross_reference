# Story Time — Ezekiel 13

---

The scene opens with a somber stillness over the land of Israel. Ezekiel, the prophet, stands alone, a solitary figure called to speak truth to a nation caught in a web of deceit. The air is thick with anticipation, and a heavy burden rests on his shoulders. God has given him a message — a message not of comfort, but of divine judgment against those who have led His people astray.

Ezekiel begins his proclamation, his voice cutting through the silence like a clarion call. God has directed him to confront the false prophets, those who claim to speak in His name but have seen nothing, heard nothing. **"Woe to the foolish prophets who follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing,"** he cries. These false prophets are a danger to Israel, offering only the illusions of their own hearts.

The false prophets have whispered sweet lies, pretending to see visions of peace when war is at their doorsteps. They have plastered flimsy walls of deceit, covering cracks with whitewash rather than building on the solid foundation of truth. How often, Ezekiel wonders, do people choose the comfort of lies over the challenge of truth?

God's anger burns against them. He declares a threefold judgment: they will be cut off from the people, removed from the records of Israel, and denied entry into the promised land. Complete rejection awaits those who deceive in His name. Their spiritual lineage will be severed, and their place among God's people will be erased. **They have failed to "go up into the gaps,"** leaving the breaches in Israel's defenses wide open. In their negligence, they have abandoned their duty to protect and intercede.

Ezekiel's message rings out like a trumpet, a warning to all who would listen. True prophets stand firm in the gap, they build protective hedges through repentance and reformation. The false prophets, however, are like cunning foxes, moving with slick deception but producing nothing of value. They chase popularity, not truth, leaving God's people vulnerable in the wilderness.

As Ezekiel speaks, a vision unfolds — a storm approaches, a torrent ready to wash away the weak structures built by deceit. The whitewashed walls cannot withstand the deluge. They crumble and fall, exposing the lies for what they are. **This is a moment of reckoning**, a reminder that God’s truth stands unyielding against the tempest.

In this chapter, we see the heart of God's concern: His people must be rooted in truth. The false prophets serve as a judgment upon a people who have drifted from genuine faith. God's sovereignty is evident; He holds both the deceiver and the deceived in His hand. Here, we find a timeless warning against the seduction of easy lies and an invitation to embrace the hard, saving truth. In a world of false assurances, real prophecy breaks through like light in the darkness.

Truth matters. Integrity matters. In Ezekiel 13, God calls His people back to the only foundation that will not crumble. Will they listen? Will we?
