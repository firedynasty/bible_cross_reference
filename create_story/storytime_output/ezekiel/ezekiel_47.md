# Story Time — Ezekiel 47

---

### Setting the Scene

The landscape of Ezekiel's vision has been shifting—a glorious new temple arises, representing a future full of God's presence and promise. Ezekiel stands, transfixed, watching as another chapter unfolds in this divine revelation. His heart pounds with curiosity and awe. He is not alone; a mysterious guide has been leading him through this alternate world, showing him the intricacies of God's eternal plan.

### The River Begins

It starts as a trickle. From the temple's threshold, water drips steadily, flowing east. Ezekiel watches, knowing this is no ordinary stream. This is divine grace in liquid form, the very essence of life flowing from the heart of God's dwelling.

Why the right side? A clue for those who look—**gospel-blessings are right-hand blessings**, Henry reminds us. From this sacred place, life-giving water begins its profound journey.

The guide gestures for Ezekiel to follow, and they walk together through this sacred imagery.

### Increasing Depths

They step further, and the waters climb to Ezekiel's ankles. A few more paces, and they're at his knees. The water, relentless and vibrant, reaches his waist. Ezekiel is immersed now.

What's happening here? It's a portrait of **spiritual growth**—an invitation for believers to plunge deeper into divine mysteries. **God makes known his mind and will to his people by degrees**, says Henry.

Each step challenges Ezekiel—and us—to delve deeper, to explore beyond the familiar shallows and into the depths where only faith can sustain us.

Finally, Ezekiel finds himself at the river's edge, gazing at a torrent of water so deep and wide that not even the strongest swimmer could traverse it. It’s beyond him, beyond sight, beyond understanding.

It is divine grace, unfathomable and infinite.

### The Healing Waters

They walk again, but now along the riverbanks. The landscape transforms around them. Trees, lush and verdant, spring to life. The water's touch is transformative, turning stark desert into a vibrant garden.

Ezekiel listens as his guide explains: This river will flow into the Dead Sea, and what was once lifeless will teem with fish and life. **The gospel's transformative power is at work**—turning **dead sinners alive and living saints lively**.

But not everywhere. Some marshy places resist, becoming saline wastelands, a sobering reminder that some may reject grace. They stand as testaments to divine justice, just as Henry mentions.

### Slowing Down at the Trees

Ezekiel’s gaze lingers on the trees lining the river. Their leaves never wither, and their fruit is perpetual. This is no ordinary vegetation. This is a vision of **grace and joy**—life sustained by God's eternal goodness.

**From him those living waters flow**, notes Henry, echoing through Ezekiel’s vision as a promise—a glimpse of glory begun.

### Reflection

The river in Ezekiel 47 is a story of beginnings and boundless grace. It’s about the **expanding reach of the gospel**, calling believers to dive deeper into the heart of God. It reminds us of our mandate to be part of this life-giving work, to allow grace to transform us and flow through us to a world in need.

Here, in these waters, we find the essence of redemption. An invitation to wade into God's unending love—shallow enough for a child, deep enough for eternity.
