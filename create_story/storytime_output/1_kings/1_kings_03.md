# Story Time — 1 Kings Chapter 3

---

### Setting the Scene

The dust has settled on King David's reign. Solomon, the son of Bathsheba, now sits upon the throne of Israel, navigating the burdensome legacy of his father. The air is thick with anticipation and the hope of a new beginning. Solomon, though young, carries the weight of an entire nation on his shoulders.

In the palace, amidst the echoes of a blood-soaked legacy, a young king emerges. But already, whispers of his devotion to God ripple through the corridors. Solomon is known not for his might, but for his heart — a heart that loves the Lord.

### Key Moments in the Chapter

Solomon, fresh in his reign, travels to Gibeon, where the great high place stands. The sun sets as Solomon stands before the altar, offering a thousand burnt offerings. "Waste not," some might murmur. But not Solomon. He knows that nothing is wasted when it's given to God. **The air smells of sacrifice and whispered prayers**.

That night, Solomon dreams. Within his slumber, the Lord appears, offering a blank check: "Ask what I shall give thee." What would you choose if offered such a divine promise? Riches? Long life? Victory over your enemies?

Solomon asks for none of these. Instead, he requests an "understanding heart to judge thy people." He yearns to discern between good and evil. His plea is not for himself, but for those he serves. In this moment, we see Solomon's soul laid bare — a humble king, seeking wisdom over wealth.

God is pleased. In granting Solomon's wish, He bestows not just wisdom, but also the very riches and honor that Solomon did not ask for. How often are we reminded that when we seek first God's kingdom, all other things are added?

The chapter crescendos with a story of profound insight. Two women, fighting over a child, stand before the king. A dilemma unsolvable by ordinary means. But Solomon, with God-given wisdom, proposes to divide the child. It's a test of love, and the true mother's heart is revealed.

### Slowing Down on the Dream

Let's linger on that dream for a moment. Can you imagine it? In the quiet stillness of night, God's voice cuts through the darkness. Solomon's choice is pure, unclouded by the desires that often ensnare. It's in his sleep, a space where pretense falls away, that God plants His grace. **Solomon's choice echoes the call of our own hearts to seek what truly matters**.

### Weaving in Matthew Henry

Matthew Henry observes that Solomon loved the Lord, a love mirrored in his actions and choices. This love, profound and genuine, is emblematic of the relationship between God and His people. Henry notes that true worship is costly, yet worthwhile. Solomon's offerings at the high place, while imperfect according to the law, are met with divine patience — what grace God extends!

In Solomon's prayer, we find a template of humility. As Henry points out, the best leaders are those aware of their own insufficiency. Solomon's request for wisdom reflects an understanding that the heart's knowledge is the greatest guide in duty.

### Reflection: What This Chapter Is Really About

At its core, 1 Kings 3 is a story of priorities. It's about a king who chooses wisdom over wealth, pleasing God through the simplicity of a child-like request. Solomon's reign teaches us that when we love God and seek His wisdom above all else, blessings follow. This chapter beckons us to examine our hearts. What do we ask of God? Do we seek what's truly important? 

In Solomon's request, we find a call to pursue wisdom, a gift freely given when sought with sincerity. When we prioritize the heart's true desire, we walk in step with the divine.
