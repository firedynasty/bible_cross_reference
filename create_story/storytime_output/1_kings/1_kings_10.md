# Story Time — 1 Kings Chapter 10

### Setting the Scene

The air hums with stories of Solomon’s unparalleled wisdom and prosperity. News of his splendor has journeyed far and wide, his reputation crossing deserts and seas. In a distant land, the queen of Sheba, curious and eager, decides it’s time to see for herself what the world speaks of in awe. Is this king truly as wise as they say, or are these tales just the whispers of exaggeration?

### The Queen's Arrival

Picture her entourage: camels laden with spices, gold, and precious gems, a caravan that stretches as far as the eye can see. She arrives in Jerusalem, her eyes keen and searching, not just for treasures, but for answers. The queen's intent is clear — she seeks understanding about the name of the LORD, driven by a thirst that riches alone cannot quench.

In the courts of Solomon, she presents her questions, riddles of deep thought. Solomon, with a calm that speaks of wisdom beyond his years, answers each one with ease. **The queen is astounded.** Not just by the answers but by the king himself, his palace, his table, his servants — there is a harmony and grandeur she had not anticipated.

### The Moment of Realization

Pause here. In this moment, the queen declares, "Happy are thy men, happy are these thy servants." She sees more than just wealth; she perceives a life of purpose and wisdom bestowed by God. It's not just Solomon’s riches that impress, but the order, the justice, the righteousness that flows from his throne.

Her declaration becomes a blessing: "Blessed be the LORD thy God." She recognizes that this magnificence is not for Solomon’s merit alone but a testament to divine grace. In Solomon’s greatness, she glimpses the shadow of a greater kingdom, one where all nations will seek the light.

### Insights from Matthew Henry

Here, Matthew Henry's reflections breathe life into our understanding. He sees Solomon as a type of Christ — a hint, a precursor of someone greater. Christ, who offers not just answers to riddles but eternal wisdom and life. The Queen of Sheba’s journey mirrors the Gentiles' future search for truth in Christ, who "having lain in his bosom, was much better able to instruct them."

Leaders today can learn from Solomon’s example — to seek wisdom continually, to rule with justice and righteousness, mindful that leadership is more about service than prestige.

### A Bounty Beyond Measure

As the queen prepares to leave, she gifts Solomon with riches, yet it is she who returns enriched beyond measure. Her journey was not in vain; her eyes have beheld things she didn’t dare dream of. Her words echo in the heart: "Indeed, not even half was told me."

### Closing Reflection

This chapter isn't just about earthly splendor and wisdom. It's a whisper of something more, a call to seek divine wisdom and truth. It’s about our own journey, like the queen’s, to the seat of true understanding. With Christ, all questions find their answer, all riddles their resolution.

**In the end, 1 Kings 10 invites us to see beyond Solomon’s grandeur to a greater kingdom, where Christ reigns with wisdom and grace far surpassing even that of Israel’s wisest king.**
