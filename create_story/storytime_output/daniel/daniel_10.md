# Story Time — Daniel Chapter 10

---

### Setting the Scene

The air is heavy with mystery. We find Daniel, now an elderly yet steadfast prophet, embroiled in a quiet storm of spiritual discipline. For three weeks, his days are marked by fasting and mourning. But why? The recent return of the Jews to Jerusalem is like a flickering candle, barely holding against the winds of opposition. The great work of God seems to stumble, and Daniel, though far from the ruins, feels every tremor in his soul.

In this shadowed time, the banks of the Tigris River become the stage for the extraordinary. Daniel stands, weary but resolute, unaware that what is about to unfold will be breathtaking and terrifying.

---

### A Vision Unfolds

Suddenly, the air shimmers. A man of dazzling brilliance appears before him. His clothing is linen, pristine and symbolic of purity, girdled with the finest gold. Imagine **eyes like flaming torches**, a face like lightning itself, and a voice that sounds as though it were the roar of a multitude. 

This is no mere messenger. This is the pre-incarnate Christ, the eternal Word, and yet, here He stands before Daniel, clothed in such splendor. But isn't it remarkable? In assuming the form of a man, Christ has once drawn a veil over such majesty, choosing for a time the humble garb of a servant.

Daniel is paralyzed by the encounter. Like Paul on the Damascus road, his companions see nothing yet are struck with terror. Daniel alone receives this vision — a divine revelation reserved for God's favored servant.

---

### Spiritual Warfare

The scene shifts from the celestial to the warfare unseen. The angelic being speaks of battles fought in spiritual realms. For twenty-one days, the **prince of the kingdom of Persia** — a dark, opposing force — withstood him.

Yet, Daniel learns that the tide turns with the arrival of Michael, the great prince, who joins the fight. Here, we glimpse a cosmic struggle that underpins human events. Spiritual warfare is real, suggesting that behind every earthly conflict, there lies a greater celestial conflict.

---

### A Moment to Breathe

The most profound moment comes when Daniel, overwhelmed by the glory and knowledge of what is to come, finds himself prostrate on the ground. Human weakness meets divine glory.

But then, a touch. The touch that raises him, reassures him. The voice that says, "Fear not, greatly beloved."

Isn't this the heart of the divine revelation? It's about accessibility, about a God who reaches down and lifts us up, even as His splendor remains beyond our grasp.

---

### Closing Reflection

Daniel Chapter 10 is a tapestry woven with the threads of divine revelation, spiritual warfare, and the reassuring presence of God. Here, in the quiet of Daniel's mourning, a world unseen becomes visible. His vision speaks to the eternal struggle and the personal touch of a loving God, reminding us that in every struggle, we are not alone. 

God's revelations are not just grand cosmic narratives; they are deeply personal stories that touch even the most hidden corners of our hearts.
