# Story Time — Daniel Chapter 12

---

### Setting the Scene

The prophetic visions are nearing their climax, and Daniel finds himself once more in the presence of celestial beings. The air is thick with anticipation. He stands at the edge of revelation, his heart a mix of awe and weariness. This is a world on the brink of apocalyptic change—a world poised for the ultimate unveiling of divine mysteries. The weight of what he has seen presses upon him like a burden of stars. 

### The Unfolding Drama

**Michael, the great prince, rises.** In the unseen realms, he is more than just a warrior; he is the protector, the patron of Daniel’s people. As Henry insightfully shares, Michael is none other than Christ, the redeemer who stands firm during the fiercest persecution. Even now, in the darkest of nights, he guards his flock from the shadows that threaten to swallow them whole.

But there’s more. Daniel sees a vision of a time of trouble unlike any before. It echoes through the ages—Antiochus's persecution, the fall of Jerusalem, and the final judgment at the end of days. This prophecy, layered with meaning, speaks to both immediate trials and ultimate fulfillment.

**And then, the resurrection.** It's a moment that pulses with hope and awe. The dust of the earth gives way to life as countless rise—some to everlasting life, others to shame and everlasting contempt. Matthew Henry sees this as both a literal and spiritual resurrection, offering comfort to those enduring suffering, buoyed by the promise that their struggles are not in vain.

The vision moves, shifting its focus to those who shine like stars. These are the wise, the soul-winners, the ones who have turned many to righteousness. They glitter in the firmament, their light a testament to lives lived in service and truth. Their reward is eternal, their impact immeasurable. 

### The Key Moment

But focus now on Daniel, as he learns these secrets are to be sealed until the time of the end. **Why seal them?** Because their beauty, like a treasure, is meant for a future time when they will be fully understood and fulfilled. This is a lesson in patience and trust—in God’s timing and revelation.

Daniel’s heart beats with questions, the mystery of "time, times, and half a time" stirring his thoughts. Yet, the final words come with assurance: "Blessed is he that waiteth." Here is the promise of perseverance, the blessing reserved for those who endure.

### Weaving in Henry’s Insights

Matthew Henry perceives this sealing not as a loss, but as a preservation. The prophecy is a treasure chest—its true worth revealed only when the appointed time comes. To wait, to hope, to endure—these become acts of faith, hallmarks of those who trust in God’s unfailing promises.

### Closing Reflection

Daniel ends with a quiet benediction. He will rest. He will stand at the end of days, secure in the lot assigned to him. This chapter is about **hope**—hope in the face of turmoil, **the triumph of life over death**, and the enduring faithfulness of God. 

In God's narrative, **no struggle is wasted, no promise forgotten**, and every suffering saint finds their place in the eternal story.
