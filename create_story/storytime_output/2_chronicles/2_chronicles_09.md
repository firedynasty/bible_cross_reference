# Story Time — 2 Chronicles Chapter 9

## Setting the Scene

In the golden age of Israel, under the reign of King Solomon, the kingdom flourishes with wisdom and wealth unparalleled. Solomon’s fame has spread far beyond the borders of his land, whispering into distant palaces and echoing through bustling trade routes. The air hums with expectation, for a unique visitor is on her way.

The queen of Sheba, a renowned monarch herself, is intrigued by tales of Solomon’s profound wisdom and the splendor of his court. Driven by curiosity and a desire for truth, she sets out on a grand journey, laden with precious gifts and burning questions.

## The Queen's Arrival

Sheba's caravan arrives in Jerusalem, opulent and magnificent. The queen steps forth with regal grace, yet beneath her royal crown lies a mind hungry for understanding. As she enters Solomon's court, she is met with a spectacle that surpasses even her loftiest expectations.

Solomon welcomes her with dignity and warmth, his eyes reflecting both wisdom and kindness. The queen begins her inquiry, posing riddles and questions meant to test the king's famed intellect. Solomon answers each with ease, his responses enriched with the whispers of divine insight.

## The Awe of Wisdom

As she listens, her initial skepticism fades into admiration. The wisdom flowing from Solomon is not merely human; it is mingled with the divine, an echo of the Creator’s own designs.

**"Happy are thy servants,"** she exclaims, marveling at the blessedness of those who dwell in Solomon's presence, continually absorbing his teachings. The queen is struck not only by his wisdom but also by the orderly worship and the peace that fills his house. It is here that she sees tangible evidence of God's favor, for surely the Lord of Israel delights in Solomon.

## A Moment to Breathe

In a moment of profound realization, the queen blesses the God of Israel for Solomon’s prosperity, acknowledging it as a token of divine love and favor. **"Because thy God delighted in thee,"** she declares, recognizing a higher hand orchestrating this grandeur.

Can you imagine her wonder, peering into a world where wisdom and wealth serve a greater purpose — to reflect the glory of God? She no longer sees the temporal, but glimpses the eternal, and her heart responds with gratitude.

## The Exchange of Gifts

With her questions satisfied, the queen offers her gifts — gold, spices, precious stones — symbols of her respect and admiration. Solomon, in his generosity, shares abundantly with her, for he knows **"freely he had received, and freely he gave."** Their exchange is more than political; it is a celebration of divine wisdom shared across kingdoms.

## Closing Reflection

This chapter is a tapestry of **divine providence** and human response. As Solomon's wisdom draws admiration from afar, it mirrors the spiritual glory of a kingdom yet to come — one where wisdom made flesh will outshine even Solomon. The queen of Sheba's journey encourages us to **pursue wisdom** at any cost, to rejoice in the gifts of others, and to see in every blessing a reflection of God’s eternal love.

In the end, the majesty of Solomon’s reign points beyond itself to God’s ultimate plan — **a kingdom not of this world, but one that calls us to seek and to find.**
