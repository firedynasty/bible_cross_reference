# Story Time — 2 Chronicles Chapter 1

---

## The Dawn of a New Reign

The scene is set: Solomon, the young king, steps into a world still resonating with the legacy of King David, his father. The air is filled with both anticipation and trepidation. The people of Israel, gathered from near and far, watch with bated breath. Will Solomon capture the spark of his father’s greatness? Will he honor the God who established their kingdom?

**Solomon stands at the threshold of his reign**, and what he chooses to do first will speak volumes.

---

## A Thousand Flames of Worship

Solomon knows where to begin. He calls the entire assembly — captains, judges, and leaders — to Gibeon, where the ancient tabernacle rests. The ark may be in Jerusalem, but here, where the sacred altar awaits, is where he chooses to offer his devotion.

In a grand display of reverence, Solomon offers **a thousand burnt offerings**. The smoke rises, a sweet aroma ascending to the heavens. It’s a moment of **divine reciprocity**; Solomon gives generously from his heart, and his heart is tested in return.

**What drives a king to such lavish devotion?** Perhaps Solomon understands: true leadership begins at the altar.

---

## A Divine Conversation

Night falls, and with it, a holy encounter. God appears to Solomon in a dream. The Almighty asks the question that would reveal a king’s heart: “Ask what I shall give thee.”

Pause here. This is no mere wish. It’s a test of character, a moment where **eternal priorities** overrule the transient desires of power. Solomon, bearing the weight of a kingdom, chooses wisely. He asks for wisdom, not wealth.

In his request, Solomon acknowledges his father’s legacy and the covenant promises of God. Here, he reveals himself as a child not of this world, but of light.

**This moment of choice defines Solomon.** He asks for wisdom to lead, and in doing so, aligns his heart with God’s grand narrative.

---

## The Blessing of Wisdom and Wealth

God is pleased with Solomon's request. Not only does He grant wisdom, but He opens the storehouses of heaven, promising riches and honor beyond any other king’s. **God's faithfulness to those who honor Him first** is manifest, and Solomon’s reign begins under the blessing of divine approval.

Yet, Solomon's growing wealth is a double-edged sword. He builds grander stables and accumulates horses from Egypt—a questionable venture, considering the warnings of Deuteronomy.

**Is Solomon’s heart slipping, even as his wisdom blossoms?**

His wealth and trade flourish, a fulfillment of promises but also a cautionary tale of balance and priorities.

---

## Reflection

This chapter is more than the start of a reign; it's a lesson on where true success begins — at the altar before the throne. Solomon's story invites us to examine our choices, to place godly wisdom over earthly gain. **For in seeking God first, everything else is given in its rightful measure.**

In the quiet places of our own lives, what do we choose when offered the chance to ask for anything? Like Solomon, may we seek wisdom and find all else added unto us.
