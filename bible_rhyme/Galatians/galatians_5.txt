Stand firm in the freedom that Christ gave to thee,  
Don’t get tangled up in chains, set yourself free.  

Paul says, "If you get circumcised, it’s a waste,  
Christ won’t help you, you’ll lose the grace."  

If you choose the law, you owe it all,  
You’re falling away, can’t hear the call.  

By the Spirit, through faith, we await,  
Righteousness and hope, a future so great.  

In Christ, it’s not about what you do or don’t,  
But love and faith, that’s what counts.  

You were running well, what got in your way?  
Who tripped you up, made you stray?  

This thought ain't from the One who calls,  
A little yeast spreads, it enthralls.  

I trust you'll see the truth and stay,  
And whoever troubles will pay.  

"If I preach circumcision, why the strife?  
The cross removes the stumbling knife."  

I wish those who trouble would just leave,  
You’re called for freedom, don't be deceived.  

Don’t use freedom just to get your way,  
Serve each other, love will repay.  

The law summed up in this decree:  
"Love your neighbor, let it be!"  

But if you bite and fight, beware,  
You'll consume each other, stripped and bare.  

Walk by the Spirit, avoid fleshly lust,  
Spirit and flesh, in conflict they thrust.  

Led by the Spirit, the law's not your guide,  
Works of the flesh, let them slide.  

Adultery, immorality, impurity and lust,  
Hatred, strife, and things unjust.  

Jealousy, anger, divisions, and such,  
These deeds won’t let you in God’s clutch.  

Spirit fruit is love, joy, and peace,  
Patience, kindness, let them increase.  

Faith, gentleness, self-control,  
Against these, the law has no hold.  

Belong to Christ? Flesh crucified at last,  
With its passions, its lust, a thing of the past.  

Live and walk by the Spirit's might,  
Avoid conceit, provoking, don’t fight.
