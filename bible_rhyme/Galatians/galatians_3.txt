Oh foolish Galatians, who cast that wicked spell?  
You saw Christ crucified, you know the tale well.  

I gotta ask you now, what's your foundation?  
Spirit by law, or faith's proclamation?  

Started with Spirit, now flesh you chase?  
Suffered in vain, is that the case?  

He gives the Spirit, works wonders, no delay,  
By law's deeds, or faith's powerful play?  

Abraham believed, righteousness was his due,  
Faith’s line continues, and it includes you.  

Scriptures knew Gentiles, justified by faith,  
God told Abraham, "Blessings all nations embrace."  

Faithful like Abraham, that blessing you'll find,  
But under the law, a curse is entwined.  

“Cursed if you don't do it all,” that's the say,  
Righteousness by faith, that's the only way.  

The law ain't faith, but do it and live,  
Christ took the curse, a life He did give.  

Hung on that tree, cursed for our sake,  
So Gentiles through Him, that blessing partake.  

Brothers, think human terms, a man's deal stands,  
Once confirmed, no changes by man’s hands.  

Promises to Abraham, to offspring, one,  
Christ is that seed, God’s promise begun.  

Law came later, can’t cancel God's grace,  
Inheritance by promise, not law’s embrace.  

Why then the law? For transgression’s sight,  
Till the promised seed came, bringing the light.  

Mediator’s role, through angels it came,  
But God is one, His promise the same.  

Law ain't against God's promises, see,  
If life came by law, then righteousness would be.  

Scripture locked all under sin's decree,  
Faith in Christ, the promise set free.  

Before faith, the law was our guard,  
Till faith was revealed, no longer barred.  

Law tutored us, led us to Christ's face,  
But now faith's here, no law's embrace.  

Children of God, through faith you stand,  
Baptized in Christ, clothed by His hand.  

No Jew or Greek, slave or free,  
No male, no female, just unity.  

In Christ, Abraham's heirs you'll be,  
By promise, your true identity.
