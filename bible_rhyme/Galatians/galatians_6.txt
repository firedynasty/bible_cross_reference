When a brother falls, lend a helping hand,  
Lift him up gentle, like a grain of sand.  

Carry each other's load, fulfill Christ’s law,  
Check yourself too, don't let pride be your flaw.  

If you think you're big when you're just a man,  
You're fooling yourself; check your own plan.  

Each one should look at the work they do,  
With pride in themselves, not just passing through.  

Everyone’s got their own weight to bear,  
Share with your teachers, show them you care.  

"Don’t be misled," God’s not fooled,  
What you plant now, later it's schooled.  

Sow to the flesh, reap decay and dust,  
Sow to the Spirit, in life you trust.  

Don't tire of doing good, the time will come,  
You'll gather the harvest, if you don’t succumb.  

Use every chance to do good, make it plain,  
Especially to your faith family, don’t refrain.  

Look at these large letters I write, see my hand,  
Some push law to avoid cross reprimand.  

Even those who follow rules don't play it neat,  
They want you in line so their boast is complete.  

But I'll only boast in our Lord’s cross,  
Through Him, the world’s a loss—I'm the boss.  

Circumcision or not, it’s a new creation,  
Peace and mercy to those of God's nation.  

Don't trouble me now, I bear His mark,  
Embedded on my body, His claim is stark.  

May the grace of our Lord Jesus, pure and bright,  
Be with your spirit, brothers—walk in His light.  
Amen.
