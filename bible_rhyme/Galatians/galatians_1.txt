Paul's in the house, an apostle on the rise,  
Not from men, but through Christ, the one who defies.  

To Galatia's crew, with the brothers by my side,  
Grace and peace from God; in Him we abide.  

Christ gave Himself for sin, to break this evil chain,  
According to God's will, His glory will remain.  

But I'm amazed, you turn so fast, chasing another way,  
Different "good news"; but there's no other to sway.  

Some are here to twist, to trouble, to mislead,  
But if they preach another word, let them be cursed indeed.  

We've said it once, now hear it twice again,  
If they preach a different word, let them be condemned.  

Am I seeking man's favor, or striving to please?  
If I was pleasing men, I'd not serve Christ with ease.  

The Good News I shared wasn't crafted by man's hand,  
It came through revelation, not from any land.  

You heard of my past, how I persecuted with zeal,  
In the Jews' faith, I was a big deal.  

But God's grace called me, set me apart,  
To reveal His Son and give me a fresh start.  

I didn't check with men, I went my own way,  
Off to Arabia then back to Damascus I strayed.  

Three years passed, then off to Peter I went,  
Fifteen days with him, my time was well spent.  

Saw no other apostle, just James, Christ's kin,  
And all I write is true, before God, no sin.  

To Syria and Cilicia, my path then led,  
In Judea's assemblies, of me, they had no dread.  

They heard, "The one who persecuted now preaches the way,"  
And they glorified God, for His work in me today.
