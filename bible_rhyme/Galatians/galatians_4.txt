When the heir's still a child, he's just like a slave,  
Under guardians' watch till the day father gave.  

We were once kids, bound to the world's way,  
But in time's fullness, God's Son came to stay.  

Born of a woman, under law's call,  
To redeem us and grant us adoption for all.  

"Abba, Father!" cries out from inside,  
The Spirit of His Son in our hearts abides.  

No longer a slave, but a son, take a stand,  
An heir through Christ, part of God's grand plan.  

Once you were bound, not knowing God's face,  
Now you know Him, why return to that place?  

Observing seasons, years with such zeal,  
I'm afraid for you, is my labor unreal?  

Brothers, be like me, I'm like you too,  
You treated me well, preached Good News to you.  

In weakness, you didn't despise nor cast,  
You welcomed me as Christ, like angels past.  

Where's that joy you once had, so alive?  
You'd have given your eyes, I testify.  

Am I now your enemy 'cause I speak true?  
Some seek to sway you, but not for good, it's skewed.  

Be zealous in good, not just when I'm near,  
I wish I could change this tone you hear.  

You want the law, do you know what it says?  
Abraham's sons, two different ways.  

One by the servant, flesh's decree,  
One by the free, a promise to see.  

Two covenants, one from Sinai's land,  
Children in bondage, Hagar's hand.  

But above, Jerusalem's free, our mother,  
"Rejoice, you barren!" God's promise, none other.  

We're like Isaac, children of the promise true,  
Flesh-born persecutes Spirit-born, old news.  

Scripture says, "Throw the servant out,  
The free woman's son will be about."  

Brothers, we're not children of the slave,  
We belong to the free, the promise He gave.
