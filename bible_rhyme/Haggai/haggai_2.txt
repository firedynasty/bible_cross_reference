Seventh month, twenty-first day, Yahweh spoke,  
Haggai the prophet, delivering hope's cloak.  
"Zerubbabel, Joshua, listen to my call,  
And to all the people, stand strong and tall.  

Who remembers this house in former glory?  
Now it looks like nothing, just a shadow story.  
Yet be strong, Zerubbabel," Yahweh repeats,  
"Be strong, Joshua, stand firm on your feet.  

All you people, rise, for I am here,  
Yahweh of Armies speaks, have no fear.  
I pledged my word when Egypt was left behind,  
My Spirit lives with you, peace of mind.  

Once more I'll shake earth, sea, and sky,  
Nations will tremble, as glory draws nigh.  
Silver and gold, all belong to me,  
This house will shine, greater it shall be.  

On the twenty-fourth day, ninth month rolled,  
Yahweh's new word, by Haggai was told.  
"Ask the priests about the sacred law,  
Does holy meat make other food pure?"  

"No," said the priests, "it won't transfer the grace."  
Haggai replied, "This is Israel's case.  
Unclean they are, in all they present,  
Their works defiled, they must repent.  

Consider the past, when stones were first laid,  
Shortfall of harvest, in work they stayed.  
Blight, mildew, hail, struck all that you grew,  
Yet back to me you never flew.  

From this day forth, mark it clear,  
Blessings will flow, no more fear.  
Seed in the barn, fruits yet unshown,  
From today’s end, blessings I’ll loan.”  

Again Yahweh spoke, same day’s light,  
To Zerubbabel, His promise bright.  
"Heavens and earth, I'll shake once more,  
Thrones will crumble, kingdoms will pour.  

Chariots, riders, all will fall,  
Each by the sword of his brother’s call.  
In that day, Zerubbabel, my chosen seal,  
As a signet ring, my covenant real."
