In Darius's year two, month six, day one,  
Came Yahweh's word, a message begun.  

Through Haggai the prophet, to leaders it flew,  
Zerubbabel and Joshua, what they must do.  

Yahweh declared, “These people say,  
'Not yet time for my house; we'll delay.'”  

But Haggai spoke, words sharp and clear,  
"Is it time for your homes, while mine's left mere?"  

“Consider your ways,” said Yahweh with might,  
“You've sown, yet little comes to sight.  

You eat, not satisfied; you drink, still dry,  
You clothe yourselves, yet warmth's a lie.  

Wages drop through holes, nothing you hold,  
Consider your ways, hear what I’ve told.  

Go to the mountains, bring wood, build anew,  
I'll take pleasure, glorify what's true.”  

"You hoped for much, and it came to small,  
I blew it away, why?" His voice did call.  

“Because my house lies waste, while yours you chase,  
Thus heavens hold dew, earth withholds its grace.  

I called drought on land, grain, and oil,  
On men, beasts, all hands’ toil.”  

Zerubbabel, Joshua, and people in fear,  
Heard Yahweh's voice, obeyed, drew near.  

Then Haggai said, "Yahweh's with you,"  
Spirit stirred in hearts, they knew.  

Zerubbabel and Joshua, leaders bold,  
With the people, built as they were told.  

On the twenty-fourth day, in month six’s fame,  
In year of Darius, they honored His name.
