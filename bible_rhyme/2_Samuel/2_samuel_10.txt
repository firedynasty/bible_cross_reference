The king of Ammon passed, Hanun took his throne,  
David said, "I'll show kindness, the seed's been sown."  
Sent his servants to Hanun, in his grief to console,  
But Ammon's princes whispered, sowing doubt in the soul.  

"Is David here to honor, or is it a spy game?  
Is he here to comfort or to claim Ammon's fame?"  
Hanun listened, took the servants, shaved half their beards,  
Cut their garments, sent them off in shame and fears.  

Word reached David, he met them, saw their disgrace,  
"Stay in Jericho till your beards replace."  
Ammon saw they were foul in David's sight,  
Called Syrians for battle, gearing up for a fight.  

David heard the news, sent Joab with the force,  
Ammon lined up at the gate in full course.  
Joab, seeing foes both front and back,  
Chose Israel's best, prepared for the attack.  

"If Syrians press, then come lend me aid,  
If Ammon's too strong, I'll come with brigade.  
Be brave for our people, for the cities we hold,  
Let Yahweh decide what's good, what's bold."  

Joab charged, Syrians fled from his sight,  
Ammon saw them run, took flight from the fight.  
Joab returned to the city, back home they trod,  
Syrians regrouped, plotting against the rod.  

Hadadezer summoned forces, crossed the river wide,  
Shobach led with might, ready to collide.  
David gathered Israel, crossed Jordan's wide stream,  
Met Syrians in Helam, battle's brutal dream.  

Syrians fled, David's strength held strong,  
Seven hundred charioteers, forty thousand gone.  
Struck down Shobach, their captain fell that day,  
Kings under Hadadezer saw defeat, turned away.  

Peace they sought, with Israel they made,  
Syrians feared to help Ammon, with their aid they stayed.
