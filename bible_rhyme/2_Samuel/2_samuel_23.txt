David, son of Jesse, raised on high,  
Anointed by God, the sweet psalmist guy.  

"Yahweh’s Spirit spoke by me," he said,  
"His word on my tongue, guiding where I tread.  

The God of Israel, the Rock spoke clear,  
'Rule with justice, and live in fear.  

Like morning light when the sun does shine,  
Grass springing up, after rain, divine.'  

My house with God, secure and tight,  
An everlasting covenant, shining bright.  

The ungodly like thorns, thrust away,  
Iron and spear, in fire they stay."  

Mighty men with David, names held high,  
Josheb Basshebeth, eight hundred did die.  

Eleazar next, stood firm in fight,  
Hand froze to the sword, in battle's might.  

Yahweh granted victory, his strength unfurled,  
People came back, to plunder the world.  

Shammah stood firm, in lentil plot's space,  
Defending the ground, Philistines he faced.  

Three mighty men, to Adullam came,  
David in stronghold, Philistines' fame.  

David longed for water, Bethlehem's well,  
Mighty men broke through, and dared to dwell.  

But David poured it out, "This is their blood,  
Who risked their lives, through the enemy's flood."  

Abishai, brother of Joab, spear raised high,  
Three hundred he laid down, none could deny.  

Benaiah, valiant, lion in snow he slew,  
An Egyptian's spear he took, and ran him through.  

Mighty deeds marked by honor and might,  
Yet not among the three, though his deeds were bright.  

Asahel, Elhanan, Shammah stood tall,  
Elika, Helez, and Ira, they did call.  

Names of valor, strength, and renown,  
Each mighty man, held their ground.  

Thirty-seven in all, loyal and true,  
David's guard, in battle they flew.
