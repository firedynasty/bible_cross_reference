Three years of famine, land dry and bare,  
David sought Yahweh, asked Him in prayer.  
Yahweh spoke, said it clear and plain,  
"It's Saul's deeds, causing the pain."  

David called the Gibeonites near,  
"What can I do, to make things clear?"  
No silver, no gold, was their request,  
They sought justice to lay souls to rest.  

"Give us seven sons of the man who slew,  
We'll hang them high, for what’s due."  
David spared Mephibosheth, oath held tight,  
But gave seven others, to make it right.  

Rizpah's vigil on the rock so cold,  
Watched over the bodies, fierce and bold.  
David heard of her sorrowful quest,  
Gathered Saul and Jonathan's bones to rest.  

They buried them deep in the land of kin,  
And God answered prayers, peace ushered in.  
Wars came again, Philistines arose,  
Giants fell, David's might imposed.  

Ishbibenob with spear so weighty,  
Thought to slay David, felt real hasty.  
Abishai saved him, struck the foe,  
"Stay back, David, let the light glow."  

More battles at Gob, giants met their end,  
Sibbecai and Elhanan, warriors to send.  
A six-fingered man, defied with might,  
Jonathan took him, brought day out of night.  

Four giants fell by David's hand and crew,  
Restoration done, God's blessings renew.
