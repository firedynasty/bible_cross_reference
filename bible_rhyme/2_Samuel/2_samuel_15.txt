Absalom set the scene with horses and a ride,  
Fifty men running, showing his pride.  

At the gate early, he'd stand and wait,  
Catching those seeking judgment from the king's estate.  

"What city you from?" he'd call out loud,  
The men replied, feeling proud.  

Said Absalom, "Your case is right,  
But no one's here from the king tonight."  

He'd add, "If I were judge, I'd hear your plea,  
Justice and fairness, you'd get from me."  

When folks bowed down, he'd pull them near,  
With a kiss and a smile, he'd steal their cheer.  

He charmed all Israel, their hearts he won,  
Turning their loyalty, one by one.  

Forty years later, he asked the king,  
"Let me go to Hebron, my vow to bring."  

The king said, "Go in peace," so off he went,  
But spies he sent, with secret intent.  

“When the trumpet sounds, say this thing,  
‘Absalom in Hebron, is now the king!’”  

Two hundred men in innocence came along,  
Unaware of the plan, they did no wrong.  

He called Ahithophel, wise and sly,  
The conspiracy grew, reaching the sky.  

A messenger came, “Absalom’s the one,  
The hearts of Israel, he has won.”  

David rose, with urgency in his tone,  
“Let’s flee now, or lose the throne.”  

The servants pledged their loyalty true,  
Ready for whatever David would do.  

The king left ten to guard his place,  
Then departed with dignity, yet in haste.  

David's crew moved on, loyal and tight,  
From Gath, six hundred, ready to fight.  

Ittai the Gittite, David tried to send back,  
But Ittai swore loyalty, ready to attack.  

"Where you go, I'll be," Ittai vowed,  
In life or death, standing proud.  

They crossed the brook with tears and cries,  
Leaving Jerusalem under clouded skies.  

Zadok came with the ark in tow,  
But David sent it back, trusting God’s flow.  

“If God’s with me, I’ll return again,  
If not, let Him choose my end and reign.”  

“Zadok, take peace, go back to the land,  
With your sons, keep me in command.”  

David climbed Olives, weeping, head low,  
Barefoot, mourning, with sorrow in tow.  

News reached him of betrayal deep,  
“Yahweh, make Ahithophel’s counsel cheap.”  

Hushai met him, a friend in despair,  
David gave a mission, with care.  

“Go back and serve; to Absalom speak,  
Defeat Ahithophel, make him weak.”  

With Zadok and Abiathar near,  
Listen in secret, bring word clear.”  

Hushai returned, the plan in place,  
While Absalom came, seeking his space.
