There was a wicked man, Sheba by name,  
Blew the trumpet, called Israel to his game,  
"No part in David, no son of Jesse for me,  
Every man to his tents, let’s break free!"  

Israel heard and followed his lead,  
But Judah stayed with David, their king indeed.  
Back in Jerusalem, David returned home,  
Kept his concubines, but left them alone.  

To Amasa, the king gave command,  
"Gather Judah in three days, take a stand."  
But Amasa delayed, took too long,  
David saw Sheba could grow strong.  

"Abishai, go, Sheba's worse than before,  
Take the troops, catch him, close the door."  
Joab's men marched, with warriors galore,  
To pursue Sheba, they went to war.  

Met at Gibeon, Joab saw Amasa,  
Greeted him warmly, but it was a facade.  
With a sword hidden low, Joab struck hard,  
Amasa fell, caught off guard.  

Joab called, "For David and me,  
Follow now, pursue Sheba, set him free."  
Amasa lay, blood in the street,  
They moved him aside, their mission complete.  

Through tribes and towns, they chased him down,  
To Abel of Beth Maacah, where he was found.  
Built a siege, the city was tight,  
Walls battered down, ready for fight.  

Then a wise woman called from the height,  
"Joab, come near, let's end this right."  
Joab listened, she spoke her peace,  
"Why destroy a mother, let this cease?"  

Joab replied, "It's not my aim,  
Sheba's against the king, he's to blame.  
Give him to me, and we'll depart,  
No need to tear this town apart."  

The woman, wise, told the city’s tale,  
They cut off Sheba's head, didn't fail.  
Tossed it to Joab, the deed was done,  
He blew the trumpet, battle was won.  

Joab returned to David, his king,  
Order restored, ready for the next thing.  
Joab led all, Benaiah had his crew,  
The kingdom was steady, the troubles few.
