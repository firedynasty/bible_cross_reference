David stepped up, struck the Philistine land,  
Took their city's power, wrested it from their hand.  

He marched to Moab, with a line he measured,  
Two parts gone, one part kept, a decision he treasured.  

Servants they became, tribute they brought,  
David's reign expanded, battles he fought.  

Hadadezer of Zobah, next in line,  
David took his soldiers, claimed them as mine.  

Seventeen hundred horsemen, twenty thousand on foot,  
Chariots hamstrung, a hundred he took.  

Syrians of Damascus tried to lend aid,  
David struck twenty-two thousand, made them afraid.  

Garrisons in Syria, they served him now,  
Tribute to David, Yahweh's gift, take a bow.  

Golden shields from Hadadezer brought to town,  
Betah and Berothai's bronze made David renown.  

Toi of Hamath, hearing of David's might,  
Sent his son Joram, with gifts shining bright.  

Silver and gold, they arrived at his feet,  
David dedicated all to Yahweh, complete.   

Nations he subdued, from Moab to Philistine,  
Ammon, Amalek, all he did oversee.  

In the Valley of Salt, David's name became known,  
Eighteen thousand Syrians, his power was shown.  

Edom secured, garrisons placed all around,  
The Edomites served, Yahweh's victory profound.  

David ruled Israel with fairness and might,  
Justice and righteousness were his guiding light.  

Joab, the commander, led with a strong hand,  
Jehoshaphat recorded the events of the land.  

Zadok and Ahimelech were priests in the scene,  
Seraiah the scribe, keeping the records clean.  

Benaiah over guards, the Cherethites and Pelethites,  
David’s sons as ministers, guiding people’s rights.
