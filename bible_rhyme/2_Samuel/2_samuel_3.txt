In Hebron, David’s house grew strong and bold,  
While Saul’s house weakened, its story told.  

David had sons, born one by one,  
Amnon, Chileab, Absalom, their life begun.  

Adonijah, Shephatiah, Ithream too,  
In Hebron these sons of David grew.  

Abner gained power, Saul’s house on his side,  
But Ishbosheth’s words made his anger rise wide.  

“Am I a dog’s head with Judah’s crew?  
I’ve kept you safe, yet you question what I do!”  

“God do more if I don't keep David’s throne,  
From Saul’s house, to rule from land to known.”  

Abner sent word, “Let’s make this deal,  
Bring all Israel, for David’s appeal.”  

David replied, “Make this treaty be,  
But bring Michal, and you’ll see me.”  

Ishbosheth sent for her, took her away,  
Paltiel wept, but to Bahurim he’d stay.  

Abner spoke to Israel, “David’s your king,  
Yahweh’s promise, salvation he’ll bring.”  

Abner came to David with twenty men,  
And David feasted, peace reigned again.  

Joab returned from a raid with his band,  
He learned Abner came, but left the land.  

“What have you done?” Joab asked the king,  
“Abner’s deceit, he knows everything.”  

Joab called Abner back, at the gate he did meet,  
Struck him down, for his brother's defeat.  

David mourned, “I’m guiltless of this blood,  
Let Joab’s house bear the flood.”  

“Clothe in sackcloth, mourn Abner this day,”  
David followed the bier, leading the way.  

“Should Abner fall like a fool in the dust,  
With no bonds or chains, betrayed by unjust?”  

David wept, the people joined the cry,  
He swore not to eat till the sun passed by.  

The people saw and it pleased them right,  
The king’s heart was clear in their sight.  

“A great man’s fallen, though I’m weak today,  
May Yahweh judge the wicked, for their wicked way.”
