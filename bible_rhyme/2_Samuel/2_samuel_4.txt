Saul's son heard of Abner's fall,  
His hands grew weak, his spirit small.  
Israel trembled, stricken with fear,  
Two raider captains were drawing near.  

Baanah and Rechab, brothers in crime,  
From Beeroth's land, Benjamin's line.  
Jonathan's boy, young but lame,  
Mephibosheth was his name.  

Rechab and Baanah, in the heat of the day,  
To Ishbosheth's house, they made their way.  
Pretended to fetch wheat, all was still,  
Struck him down as he lay in the chill.  

In his bed, they took his breath,  
Killed and beheaded, sealing his death.  
They seized his head, off to David they sped,  
Through the night, in the Arabah, they tread.  

To Hebron with Ishbosheth's head they came,  
"Here's Saul's son's head," they proclaimed.  
"Yahweh avenged you, King, this day,  
Saul's end is here," they dared to say.  

David replied with a heart full of fire,  
“As Yahweh lives, my soul's true supplier,  
When news of Saul's death came to me,  
I rewarded the bearer with fatal decree.  

Now you wicked men, who killed a just soul,  
In his own bed, you've paid the toll."  
David commanded, justice was set,  
Hands and feet cut, a grim silhouette.  

Beside Hebron's pool, their fate was sealed,  
Ishbosheth's head, in Abner's field.  
David made sure it all was done,  
Justice served, the light of the sun.
