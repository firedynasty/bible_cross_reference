The king sat at peace, his battles all done,  
Said to Nathan, "Look, I've got a house of cedar, son.  
But the Ark of God stays behind a curtain thin."  
Nathan said, "Do what's in your heart; Yahweh's with you within."  

That night Yahweh spoke to Nathan clear,  
"Tell David, 'Should you build me a house here?  
I've roamed in tents since Egypt set them free,  
Did I ask for cedar walls surrounding me?  

Remember David, where you came from, the sheep,  
I made you a prince, gave you enemies' defeat.  
I'll make your name great, like giants of old,  
Plant Israel firm, their story unfold.  

I'll give them peace, their troubles will cease,  
And David, I'll build you a house in my lease.  
When your days are done, and you're laid to rest,  
Your offspring will rise, kingdom blessed.  

He'll build for my name, his throne will last,  
I'll be his father, his future's cast.  
If he errs, he'll bear the human rod,  
Yet my love won't leave, unlike Saul's flawed.  

Your house, your reign, forever secure,  
Your throne established, this promise is pure."  
Nathan told David every word, every sight,  
The king sat with Yahweh, in this divine light.  

"Who am I, Lord Yahweh? What is my line?  
That you've brought me this far, in your grand design.  
This promise extends beyond what I see,  
You've chosen my house for eternity.  

No words can express, for you know me well,  
Your greatness is clear, no story can tell.  
Israel redeemed, a people of your own,  
You made them a name, in lands they were shown.  

Yahweh, confirm your word, let it be,  
Magnify your name, for all to see.  
You promised a house to stand evermore,  
Blessed be this house, forever in your lore."
