You were dead in sin, that's where you began,  
Walking on a path, led by the world’s plan.  

Following the air prince, the spirit of disobey,  
Living in the flesh, just going astray.  

But God in His mercy, so great and wide,  
Loved us deeply, pulled us to His side.  

Even though we were lost, buried in our strife,  
He made us alive with Christ, giving us new life.  

"By grace you're saved," He raised us up,  
Seated us with Jesus, filling our cup.  

In ages to come, His riches will unfold,  
Kindness and grace, His story told.  

"By grace through faith," not by what you do,  
A gift from God, something brand new.  

We're His handiwork, crafted in Jesus' name,  
For good deeds prepared, to walk without shame.  

Remember you Gentiles, once far apart,  
Called "uncircumcision," with a lonely heart.  

Separated from Christ, no promise in sight,  
Hopeless and godless, in the world’s night.  

But now in Jesus, blood brings you near,  
He shattered the wall, wiped away the fear.  

Made one in peace, hostility erased,  
Creating one new man, with harmony embraced.  

He reconciled us all, through the cross,  
Killing enmity, turning loss into gloss.  

Peace preached to the far and near,  
Access to the Father, through one Spirit clear.  

No longer aliens, no longer alone,  
Citizens with saints, in God's home.  

Built on apostles, prophets laid the stone,  
Christ the cornerstone, making us known.  

In Him, the structure grows, Holy temple's call,  
Together we’re built, God’s Spirit in us all.
