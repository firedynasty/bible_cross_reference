I'm Paul, a prisoner for Christ's cause,  
For you Gentiles, busting through the laws.  

You've heard of the grace God gave me to preach,  
A mystery revealed, beyond human reach.  

Wrote it down short, so you could see,  
The mystery of Christ, clear as can be.  

Once hidden, now revealed by Spirit's flame,  
To apostles and prophets, this truth came.  

Gentiles, heirs with us, in one body, one place,  
Sharing the promise in Christ through grace.  

By God's power, servant I became,  
To spread Christ's riches, to proclaim His name.  

To show the mystery hidden in God's plan,  
Creator of all, through Jesus, the Man.  

Now, God's wisdom, through the assembly's might,  
Revealed to powers in heaven's height.  

Through Christ, God's purpose stands strong and clear,  
In Him, boldness and confidence, without fear.  

So don't lose heart over my woes,  
For your glory, this story grows.  

I bow my knees to the Father above,  
From whom all families are named, in love.  

Praying you’d be strengthened deep inside,  
With Spirit's power, let Christ reside.  

Rooted in love, grounded and strong,  
With all saints, to grasp how His love's wide and long.  

To know Christ's love, beyond what we claim,  
Filled with God's fullness, in Jesus' name.  

Now to Him who does beyond our dreams,  
By His power in us, bursting the seams.  

To Him, glory in the church and Christ's fame,  
Forever and ever, praise His name. Amen.
