Children, listen up, obey your folks, it's right,  
"Honor your father, your mother," in God's sight.  

It's the first command with a promise to hold,  
Live long and prosper, that's what you're told.  

Fathers, don't push your kids to frustration,  
Raise them up with love and God's foundation.  

Servants, serve your masters with sincere heart,  
Not just when watched, but play your part.  

Serve like Christ, doing God's will with care,  
Whether bound or free, His reward's always fair.  

Masters, treat them right, drop the threat,  
God's above all, don't you forget.  

Be strong in the Lord, mighty in His power,  
Put on God's armor, stand firm every hour.  

Not just flesh and blood we wrestle today,  
But dark forces, spiritual wickedness at play.  

So gear up with truth, righteousness as your vest,  
Peace on your feet, Good News at your chest.  

Shield of faith, quench those fiery darts,  
Helmet of salvation, Word of God in your hearts.  

Pray in the Spirit, watch and persist,  
Stay strong for the saints, never resist.  

Pray for me too, to speak bold and true,  
Ambassador in chains, the Good News to pursue.  

Tychicus will tell you all about my state,  
Comfort your heart, ease the weight.  

Peace and love through faith from above,  
Grace to all who love Christ, pure as a dove. Amen.
