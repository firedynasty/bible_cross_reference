Be imitators of God, like children in the light,  
Walk in love, like Christ, who gave with all His might.  

No talk of immorality, let thanks be our speech,  
Stay pure and clean, in all things, peace we reach.  

No filthy words, no greed or nastiness,  
In Christ's Kingdom, there's no room for darkness.  

"Don’t be fooled by empty words," God’s wrath will arise,  
Stay away from disobedience, don’t fall for lies.  

Once you were darkness, now you're light in the Lord,  
Walk as light's children, let your goodness be adored.  

Truth in the Spirit, fruit that pleases Him,  
Expose the dark deeds, bring them to brim.  

"Awake, you who sleep, arise, and Christ's light you'll see,"  
Walk wise, redeem the time, let foolishness flee.  

Don't be drunk with wine, let the Spirit fill your soul,  
Sing hymns, give thanks, make melody whole.  

Wives, submit to husbands, as to the Lord you must,  
For the husband leads the wife, like Christ leads with trust.  

Husbands, love your wives, just as Christ loved all,  
He gave Himself to cleanse, making sure not to fall.  

Love your wife as you love yourself, cherish every day,  
For we are one body with Christ, a unity we obey.  

"A man will leave parents and with his wife unite,"  
In this great mystery, God’s truth is in sight.  

Love each other fully, respect and hold tight,  
In the fear of Christ, walk daily in His light.
