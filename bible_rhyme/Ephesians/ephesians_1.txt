Paul, an apostle, sent by God's decree,  
To the faithful at Ephesus, in Christ, you're free.  

"Grace and peace," he begins, setting the tone,  
"From God the Father, the Lord on the throne."  

Blessed is God, who gives blessings so grand,  
In heavenly places, crafted by His hand.  

He chose us in love before the world's start,  
To be holy and blameless, set apart.  

He destined us as kids in Jesus' name,  
"To the praise of His glory," forever proclaim.  

Through His blood, redemption we find,  
Forgiveness of trespasses, leaving sin behind.  

He poured out grace, wisdom flowed wide,  
Revealed the mystery, His will as our guide.  

In Christ, all things align, heaven and earth,  
In Him, our inheritance, His glorious birth.  

Chosen by purpose, His counsel, His might,  
Hope in Christ, bathed in His light.  

You heard the truth, the Good News came,  
Believed and sealed, Holy Spirit's claim.  

A pledge of our future, redemption's goal,  
To God's praise, the story unfolds.  

I thank God for your faith, never cease,  
Mention you in prayers, for wisdom's increase.  

Eyes of hearts opened wide, to see,  
Hope of His calling, His riches to be.  

Great is His power for those who believe,  
Strength He displayed when Christ did leave.  

Raised from the dead, to His right hand He soared,  
Above all rule, forever adored.  

Placed all things beneath His feet,  
Head of the assembly, Christ's body complete.  

In fullness, He fills all in all,  
The mystery of grace, a heavenly call.
