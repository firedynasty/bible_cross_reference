I'm locked up for the Lord, got a message to share,  
"Walk worthy of your calling," handle it with care.  

Stay humble, stay patient, love each other tight,  
Keep the Spirit's unity, bond of peace in sight.  

One body, one Spirit, one hope you were called,  
One Lord, one faith, one baptism—stand tall.  

One God and Father, above and through all,  
He's in us, around us, answering the call.  

Grace for each, a gift from Christ's own hand,  
He ascended high, leading captives to stand.  

"He ascended," but first, he went down below,  
Then rose above heavens, so all things could grow.  

Apostles, prophets, teachers on the scene,  
Perfecting the saints, keep the body clean.  

Till we reach unity, faith, and full stature,  
No more children, caught in tricks and fracture.  

Speak truth in love, grow into Christ the head,  
Each part working together, love widespread.  

Now hear me, don't walk in a futile mind,  
Darkened understanding, leave that behind.  

Callous hearts, blinded by lust and greed,  
Not the way of Christ, not the way to succeed.  

Put off the old, corrupt and deceitful ways,  
Be renewed in spirit, in truth, walk in grace.  

Put on the new, righteous, holy, and just,  
Speak truth to your neighbor, in God, we trust.  

"Be angry, don't sin," let go of the wrath,  
Don't give the devil a foothold on your path.  

Don't steal anymore, work hard with your hands,  
Give to those in need, fulfill God’s plans.  

Watch your words, let them build up and bless,  
Grace to the listeners, nothing less.  

Don't grieve the Spirit, sealed 'til redemption day,  
Put away malice, let kindness lead the way.  

Be tender, forgiving, as Christ forgave you,  
In love and unity, find strength anew.
