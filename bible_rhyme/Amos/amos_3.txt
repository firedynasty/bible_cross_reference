Hear this word that Yahweh speaks, it's true,  
Against you, Israel, the ones He brought through.  

"I chose you alone from the families wide,  
Now for your sins, you'll face the tide."  

Do two stroll together without making a pact?  
Will a lion roar without its prey intact?  

Do young lions shout when they've caught no meat?  
Can a bird get trapped without a snare beneath its feet?  

Does a trumpet blast not bring fear to the town?  
When trouble strikes, is it Yahweh who's down?  

For the Lord reveals to prophets what's concealed,  
The lion's roared, fear is revealed.  

Proclaim in Ashdod, Egypt, rise to see,  
On Samaria's hills, witness the unrest and plea.  

"They don't know to do right," says the Lord,  
They stash their loot, hoard upon hoard.  

So Yahweh declares, "An enemy will invade,  
Your fortresses fall, your defenses unmade."  

"As a shepherd saves from the lion's lair  
Two legs or an ear, what's left to spare,  

So shall Israel be from Samaria's heights,  
On couches they sit, with silken delights."  

"Listen and testify," Yahweh calls,  
Against Jacob's house, as the judgment falls.  

"I'll visit Israel's sins, Bethel's altar's fate,  
The horns will fall, down they'll break."  

Winter and summer houses both meet their demise,  
Ivory walls crumble, to end all their lies,"  

Thus says Yahweh, the Lord of Armies, true,  
His word against Israel, what will they do?
