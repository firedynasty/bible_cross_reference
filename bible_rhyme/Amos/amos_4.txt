Listen up now, you Bashan crew,  
On Samaria's hills, I'm talkin' to you.  
Oppressing the poor, crushing the weak,  
"Bring us drinks!" is all you speak.  

The Lord's got a word, don't you doubt,  
"Days are comin', they'll fish you out.  
Hooks gonna pull you, line by line,  
Through breaks in the wall, it's a sign."  

"Run to Bethel, go on, sin again,  
Gilgal too, add to your shameful spin.  
Bring your gifts, brag and boast,  
'Cause that's the thing you love the most."  

"Teeth are clean, but your plates are bare,  
Bread's run out, but you don't care.  
Rain's been scarce, crops don't grow,  
Yet back to Me, you refuse to go."  

"Rain on one, drought on the next,  
Cities scramble, feeling perplexed.  
Blight and mildew, locust swarms,  
Yet still, no return to My arms."  

"Plagues like Egypt, smell of decay,  
Young ones fallen, horses taken away.  
Burning cities, sticks in fire's blaze,  
Yet you turn not, in all your ways."  

"Prepare, Israel, 'cause here's the call,  
Gonna meet your God, the Lord of all.  
Mountains formed, winds He creates,  
Darkness falls, as judgment waits."  

"The God of Armies, His name rings true,  
And He's got a message just for you."
