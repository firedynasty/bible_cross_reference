Woe to the folks chillin' in Zion's ease,  
Secure on Samaria's heights, taking their peace.  
Notable men, where Israel flocks,  
See Calneh, Hamath, Gath—check their blocks.  

Are they better than these lands you claim?  
Is their border broader, or just the same?  
Pushin' the evil day, bringin' violence near,  
Lyin' on ivory beds, no fear.  

Stretchin' on couches, lambs they devour,  
Calves from the stalls, feast hour by hour.  
Strummin' harps, instruments they invent,  
Drinkin' from bowls, oils for their scent.  

But never grieved for Joseph's pain,  
Their lounging feast will go down the drain.  
"The Lord Yahweh swears, I hate Jacob's pride,  
The city's fate? All inside will be fried."

Ten men in a house, death'll be their fate,  
Relatives carry bodies, silence, don't relate.  
"Hush, don't mention Yahweh's name," they say,  
Yahweh commands, the great house breaks away.  

Do horses run on rock, do oxen plow the hard?  
You've turned justice into poison, righteousness marred.  
Rejoice in nothing, claim strength by your horn,  
But Yahweh will raise a new nation to scorn.  

From Hamath's gate to the Arabah's flow,  
Yahweh's bringing the affliction, you'll know.
