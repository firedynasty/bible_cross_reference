Standing by the altar, the Lord did say,  
"Strike those pillars, let them shake and sway.  

Break them on the heads, let justice be served,  
None will escape, none will be preserved.  

Dig to Sheol, my hand will bring you back,  
Climb to heaven, I'll pull you from your track.  

Hide on Carmel's peak, I'll find you there,  
Dive to ocean's depths, the serpent's snare.  

Captives before enemies, the sword will call,  
My eyes set against you, for evil to fall.  

The Lord of Armies, with a touch, land melts away,  
Mournful cries rise like Egypt's river sway.  

He builds in the heavens, his vault laid on earth,  
Calls ocean waters, pouring them forth.  

"Like Ethiopians, Israel, to me you belong,"  
Brought you from Egypt, now hear my song.  

The Lord's eyes on the kingdom of sin,  
Destroy, but Jacob's house will begin again.  

"I'll sift Israel through the nations' sieve,  
Not a kernel lost, all who live.  

Sinners claiming 'Evil's far away,'  
The sword will find them, come what may.  

In that day, David's tent I'll restore,  
Patch up the breaches, build it once more.  

Possess Edom's remnant, and nations alike,  
Called by my name, their future bright.  

"Days are coming," Yahweh's words resound,  
Plowman overtakes reaper, on fruitful ground.  

Sweet wine dripping, mountains flow,  
Hills bring abundance, joy will grow.  

I'll bring back my people, rebuild the land,  
Vineyards planted by their own hand.  

Cities renewed, gardens bloom in sight,  
Planted firm, forever in their rightful light."
