Amos stepped up from Tekoa, herdsman by trade,  
With visions of Israel, God's warnings he displayed.  
He spoke of roaring from Zion, a voice from Jerusalem's core,  
Shepherds' pastures mourning, Carmel's peak no more.  

"Yahweh says: For three sins of Damascus, and then four,  
Punishment's coming, can't ignore, they've threshed with iron for sure.  
I'll send fire on Hazael's house, Ben Hadad's palaces will fall,  
Break the bar of Damascus, no escape for them at all."  

"Yahweh continues: Gaza's next, for three sins, then one more,  
Captives delivered to Edom, justice they can't ignore.  
Fire on Gaza's walls, palaces burned to ash,  
Ashdod and Ashkelon will face my mighty clash."  

Ekron's fate sealed, Philistines' remnant fade,  
Yahweh's hand against them, in darkness they'll be laid.  
"Yahweh warns Tyre: for three, plus four,  
Covenant forgotten, they'll face my fiery roar."  

"Edom, you're guilty, brother pursued with the sword,  
Pity cast aside, wrath forever stored.  
Fire on Teman's stronghold, Bozrah's grand halls,  
Edom's anger won't save them from the fiery calls."  

"Ammon's children, hear; for three sins, and then four,  
Cruelty to Gilead's pregnant, can't be ignored.  
Rabbah's walls will blaze, palaces devoured in the fight,  
Their king and princes in chains, lost in the night."  

"Yahweh speaks, judgment true,  
Nations judged for what they do."  
Amos brings the warning clear,  
God's justice will appear.
