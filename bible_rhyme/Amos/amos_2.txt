"For three sins of Moab, for four, the score’s been tallied,  
Burned bones of Edom’s king, now justice will be rallied.  

I’ll send fire on Moab, palaces will fall,  
With tumult and shouting, hear the trumpet’s call.  

Judges cut down, princes meet their fate,  
This is Yahweh’s word, no one can debate.  

For three sins of Judah, yes, for four they stray,  
Rejected my law, let lies lead the way.  

So fire on Judah, Jerusalem’s in flames,  
Their history’s forgotten, only they’re to blame.  

For Israel’s transgressions, for four and more,  
Sold the righteous, traded the poor, a shameful score.  

Dust on the heads of the trampled poor,  
Justice denied, profaning like never before.  

Beside altars they lay, in clothes pledged,  
Drink wine of the fined, sinning unhedged.  

Yet I took down the Amorite tall as the cedars stood,  
Destroyed them root and fruit, for your own good.  

Brought you from Egypt, through wastelands wide,  
To claim Amorite land, with me as your guide.  

Raised prophets and Nazirites from your own tribes,  
But you gave them wine, silenced their scribes.  

"Behold," says Yahweh, "I’ll crush you down,  
Swift won’t escape, strength won’t be found.  

The mighty will fall, no bowman will stand,  
Even the horse can’t deliver man’s hand.  

On that day, the brave will flee away,  
Stripped bare by truth, naked they’ll stay."
