Hear this word, a lament so deep,  
Israel's virgin fallen, in her land she'll weep.  
No one to lift her, no chance to rise,  
A thousand march out, but a hundred survive.  

"Seek Me, live; don't to Bethel roam,  
Gilgal and Beersheba won't be your home."  
"Fire in Joseph's house, it'll blaze,  
If you don't seek Me, no one quells that blaze."  

Justice turned to wormwood's bitter taste,  
Righteousness cast down, all laid to waste.  
Seek the One who made the stars shine,  
Turns night to day, calls waters to align.  

Destruction on the strong, the fortress falls,  
They hate the truth in judgment halls.  
Trample the poor, build homes of stone,  
But you'll live in them? No, you'll be alone.  

Offenses are many, sins are vast,  
You bribe and ignore the needy cast.  
Prudent remain silent in times so grim,  
Evil reigns, but don't let it win.  

"Seek good, not evil, so you may live,  
God of Armies, His presence will give.  
Hate evil, love good, justice in your courts,  
Remnant of Joseph, may find Yahweh's support."  

Wailing in streets, from farmers to fields,  
Yahweh's presence, through the middle He yields.  
"Desire My day? It's darkness, not light,  
Flee from a lion, into a bear's bite."  

"I loathe your feasts, your solemn show,  
Burnt offerings, peace offerings, I won't know.  
Noise of your songs, away, I demand,  
Let justice roll, like rivers through the land."  

Sacrifices in the wild for forty years,  
Idol tents and gods you revered.  
Captivity beyond Damascus, you'll face,  
Says Yahweh, God of Armies, in this place.
