Yo, the Lord Yahweh showed a scene so true,  
A basket of summer fruit, I said, "I see it too."  

"The end's come for Israel, no more will I pass,"  
Songs of the temple turn to wailing, alas.  

Bodies lie everywhere, silence will reign,  
Those who oppress the poor will bear the pain.  

"When's the new moon gone, so we can trade?"  
Cheating the scales, deceit's been displayed.  

Buy the poor for silver, sandals for the needy,  
Selling the sweepings, oh so greedy.  

Yahweh swore by Jacob's pride, "I won't forget,  
The land will tremble, its people will fret."  

Rise like a river, then sink again low,  
Darkness at noon, the sun won't glow.  

Feasts into mourning, songs into cries,  
Sackcloth and baldness, tears in their eyes.  

"A famine's coming," says Yahweh's decree,  
Not of bread or water, but His word they'll seek.  

From sea to sea, they'll wander in vain,  
Young men and virgins, thirst is their pain.  

Those who swear by false gods will fall,  
And never again will they stand tall.
