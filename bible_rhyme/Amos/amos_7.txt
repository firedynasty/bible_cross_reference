Locusts in the land, formed by His hand,  
Eatin' the grass, leavin' nothing to stand.  

"Forgive, Lord Yahweh, I beg and plea,  
How can Jacob rise? He's small, you see."  

Yahweh relented, "It shall not be,"  
Mercy shown, setting Jacob free.  

Then came fire, scorching the ground,  
"Dried up the deep, devoured all around."  

"Stop, Lord Yahweh, spare the land,  
How can Jacob rise? He's small, understand."  

"Again," said Yahweh, "I’ll hold my hand.  
This too shall not be, I’ll heed your command."  

Next, a plumb line in His grip so tight,  
"Amos, what is it?" "A plumb line, right."  

"In Israel's midst, a plumb line I’ll place,  
Won't pass them by, no more grace."  

High places, desolate, sanctuaries laid waste,  
Against Jeroboam, with sword, I’ll haste.  

Amaziah ran to Jeroboam, he cried,  
"Amos conspires, can't let this slide."  

"Jeroboam will fall, Israel's fate is grim,  
Captivity awaits, future’s lookin' dim."  

"Away, you seer, leave Bethel's gate,  
Prophesy elsewhere, leave the king's estate."  

"I’m no prophet," Amos replied,  
"A herdsman, a farmer, where I abide."  

"Yahweh called, from the flock He took,  
'Prophesy to Israel,' with a steady look."  

"Hear Yahweh’s word, listen and heed,  
You can't silence truth with your royal decree."  

"Your wife, a fate she won’t resist,  
Sons, daughters fall, land divided, list."  

"You’ll die in a place unclean and bare,  
Israel captive, taken from there."
