Michael stands up, the great protector in sight,  
For your people in trouble, ready to fight.  
A time of chaos like never seen,  
But deliverance for those whose names gleam.  

The dust will awaken, the sleepers will rise,  
Some to life everlasting, some to despise.  
The wise will shine like the sky's bright face,  
Turning many to righteousness, holding their place.  

“Daniel, lock these words, seal them tight,  
Till the end when knowledge takes flight.”  
I saw two figures by the river's side,  
One asked the man above the tide,  

“How long till these wonders cease?”  
He raised his hands, swore by peace,  
“A time, times, and half a time,  
When holy power breaks, all will align.”  

I heard, but could not understand,  
I asked, “What’s the end, tell me the plan?”  
“Go your way, Daniel, the words are sealed,  
Till time’s end, when all’s revealed.  

“The pure will purify, the wise will learn,  
The wicked, in darkness, will never discern.  
From offering end to desolation's stay,  
Twelve ninety days will mark the way.  

“Blessed are those who wait and survive,  
To reach thirteen thirty-five.  
Go your way, rest till the end of days,  
Inherit your lot, in eternal praise.”
