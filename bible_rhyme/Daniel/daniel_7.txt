In Babylon's realm, Daniel had a dream so grand,  
Visions of beasts rising from the sea, across the land.  

Four winds whipped the ocean's might,  
Four beasts emerged, each a startling sight.  

First, a lion with eagle wings it bore,  
Plucked and stood upright, a man's heart at its core.  

Next came a bear, raised on one side,  
With ribs in its jaws, "Devour!" it cried.  

Then a leopard, swift with four wings and four heads,  
Dominion it seized, as the prophecy spreads.  

The fourth beast was fierce, iron teeth did it wield,  
Crushed and devoured all, with strength unconcealed.  

Ten horns on its head, but then a new horn arose,  
Eyes like a man, words bold as it grows.  

Thrones were set, the Ancient of Days took his seat,  
Clothed in snow-white, with a fiery throne beneath.  

A river of fire flowed, books opened wide,  
Countless stood before Him, His judgment was applied.  

The boasting horn silenced, that beast cast to flame,  
Others lost dominion, but time still remained.  

With clouds came One like a Son of Man,  
To the Ancient of Days, He approached, took His stand.  

Given glory and a kingdom, forever to reign,  
All nations and languages served Him, no end to His domain.  

Daniel's spirit was troubled, visions weighed on his mind,  
He sought understanding, the truth he did find.  

"These beasts are kings, four rising from earth's face,  
But saints of the Most High will claim the kingdom's place."  

The fourth beast, Daniel's focus, so strong and so wild,  
Iron and bronze, breaking nations, more fierce than a child.  

Ten horns, ten kings, yet another will rise,  
Three kings he’ll topple, with great boasts and lies.  

Waging war on the saints, for a time they'll bear the strain,  
Till the Ancient of Days comes, and justice will reign.  

The kingdom eternal, to saints it will go,  
An everlasting dominion with a righteous glow.  

Daniel's mind was troubled, thoughts hard to release,  
But he kept them within, seeking future peace.
