Nebuchadnezzar dreamed, kept him up at night,  
Called magicians and Chaldeans to make it right.  

"I dreamed a dream, troubled I am," he claimed,  
Reveal it to me, or you'll be shamed.  

"Tell us the dream, we'll interpret, that's fair,"  
"The dream's gone from me, interpret, if you dare."  

"Else you'll be cut, your houses laid waste,  
Reveal it now, or I'll show no grace."  

Chaldeans replied, "No man can fulfill,  
Only gods know, and they don't dwell here still."  

The king enraged, wise men to be dead,  
Daniel heard, approached with a head steady and led.  

"Why so urgent?" Daniel questioned wise,  
Arioch explained, Daniel's faith did rise.  

Daniel told friends, "Pray for mercy from above,  
That God might reveal, save us with His love."  

In a vision of night, secrets were shown,  
Daniel praised God for wisdom He's known.  

"Blessed be God, who changes seasons and kings,  
Reveals deep secrets, to Him my heart sings."  

To Arioch Daniel went, "Don't kill the wise,  
Bring me to the king, I'll interpret in his eyes."  

Before the king, Daniel stood with grace,  
"The secret can't be told by man's face.  

But there's a God who reveals the unseen,  
Your dream and vision, I'll make them clean."  

"O king, you saw a mighty bright image,  
Head of gold, legs and feet with iron mixed.  

A stone uncut, struck the feet, broke the frame,  
It became a mountain, earth filled with its name."  

"The dream is true, interpretation clear,  
God's kingdom will stand, no end to fear."  

Nebuchadnezzar fell, worshiped on his face,  
"Your God is the God of gods, sets the pace."  

Daniel was honored, made ruler of the land,  
Shadrach, Meshach, Abednego, appointed by his hand.
