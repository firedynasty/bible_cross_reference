Darius set up a hundred twenty men,  
To guard his kingdom, keep it secure, amen.  
Over them three presidents, Daniel was key,  
His spirit was excellent, as good as can be.  

Presidents and governors, scheming a plan,  
Trying to catch Daniel, the faithful man.  
"No fault in him, only his God's law," they said,  
So they gathered together, put ideas in the king's head.  

"King Darius, live forever!" they cried with a grin,  
"Let's make a law, let no prayers begin,  
Unless they're to you, for thirty days long,  
Break it and face the lions, where they belong."  

Darius signed it, the decree was done,  
But Daniel kept praying, his faith never spun.  
Windows wide, towards Jerusalem he prayed,  
Three times a day, he was never swayed.  

Caught him in prayer, they ran to the king,  
"Hasn't that law prohibited this thing?"  
The king heard it, distressed, his heart in a bind,  
But Medes and Persians' laws are set in mind.  

Daniel in the lion's den, the king felt low,  
"Your God will deliver you," he hoped so.  
A stone sealed the den, the king fasted that night,  
No sleep, no music, no joy in sight.  

At dawn, he rushed to the lion’s den,  
"Daniel, servant of God, did He save you again?"  
Daniel replied, "My God sent an angel down,  
Shut the lions' mouths, no harm found."  

The king rejoiced, pulled Daniel out,  
No scratch on him, no doubt.  
Those who schemed, met their fate,  
The lions showed them no delay, no wait.  

Darius declared, to every nation and land,  
Fear Daniel's God, mighty and grand.  
He saves, He rescues, His kingdom's no lie,  
Daniel prospered, under kings far and nigh.
