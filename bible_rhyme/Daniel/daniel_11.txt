In Darius's first year, I stood tall,  
To confirm and strengthen, giving it my all.  
"Listen now, the truth I will show,  
Three kings in Persia, then a fourth with riches to grow.  

He'll stir up trouble against Greece with might,  
A mighty king will rise, doing as he likes.  
But his kingdom will shatter, scattered like the breeze,  
Not to his kin, but divided with ease.  

The southern king strong, but his prince stronger still,  
They'll join through a daughter, her strength will chill.  
Yet she'll falter, given up in the end,  
Along with those who tried to defend.  

From her roots, one will rise in place,  
Into the fortress, he'll take his space.  
With gods and treasures, Egypt's where he'll go,  
Then return to his land, with what he stole.  

His sons will rise, assemble great powers,  
Overflow the lands, like heavy showers.  
The southern king, angry, will wage a fight,  
But the northern king's force will take the night.  

A multitude will rise, but pride's in vain,  
He'll cast down many, yet won't maintain.  
The north will gather, stronger than the past,  
With armies and wealth, they'll come at last.  

In those times, many will rise against the south,  
But the violent will fall, failing their mouth.  
The north will take cities, the south will break,  
No strength to stand, no path to make.  

He'll do as he pleases, in the land of delight,  
Destruction in hand, he'll harness his might.  
He'll send his daughter, but she won't stand,  
His plans will falter, not as he planned.  

Turning to islands, his reach will expand,  
But a prince will stop him, on his own land.  
Then he'll stumble, fall, and disappear,  
As a tax collector briefly appears.  

A contemptible one will rise instead,  
Through flattery, to the throne he's led.  
Breaking treaties, deceit in play,  
He'll grow strong, in a clever way.  

Insecurity breeds his daring schemes,  
Scattering spoils, fulfilling dreams.  
He'll plot against strongholds, bide his time,  
Prepare for battle, in rhythm and rhyme.  

Against the south, with strength he'll clash,  
But betrayal within will cause a crash.  
Both kings lie, seeking their gain,  
But the end's appointed, their plans in vain.  

He'll return wealthy, against the covenant rage,  
Taking action, turning the page.  
The south he'll approach, not as before,  
Kittim's ships will push him to the shore.  

Profaning the sanctuary, setting abomination high,  
Corrupting the wicked, spinning a lie.  
But those who know God will take a stand,  
Strong and resilient across the land.  

The wise will instruct, though they'll fall,  
Helped by few, but answered the call.  
Refined in trials, pure they'll become,  
Until the end, when time's appointed sum.  

A king will do as he wishes, above all gods,  
Marvels against God, sparking the odds.  
He'll prosper until the wrath is complete,  
Ignoring the gods, himself he'll seat.  

Honoring fortresses with unknown gold,  
Ruling over many, stories untold.  
At the end, the south will face his wrath,  
The north's whirlwind, on a conquering path.  

He'll enter the glorious land, overthrowing domains,  
Edom, Moab, Ammon, escaping chains.  
Egypt's treasures, under his power,  
Libya and Ethiopia, follow his tower.  

But news from afar will trouble his mind,  
Fury unleashed, destruction he'll find.  
Between sea and holy mount, he plants his tent,  
Yet his end will come, and none will relent.
