Nebuchadnezzar's gold image was tall and grand,  
Sixty cubits high, shining on Dura's land.  

He called the governors, deputies, and all,  
To gather and worship, they answered the call.  

The herald shouted, "Listen up, every nation,  
When you hear the music, bow in adoration.  

Whoever don’t bow will face the fiery blaze,  
A furnace of fire, in a matter of days."  

Music played, and all nations did the same,  
They worshiped the image, in fear of the flame.  

But some Chaldeans came with a report,  
With accusations against Jews in the court.  

"Those Jews you appointed, they don’t show respect,  
They won't worship your gods, what do you expect?"  

In rage, the king called for the three men,  
"Is this on purpose? You’re defying me then?"  

"Bow when you hear music, if you wish to survive,  
Or in my furnace, you won’t leave alive."  

But they answered calm, "No need to defend,  
Our God can save us, He’s our true friend.  

Even if He doesn’t, know this, O King,  
We won’t serve your gods, nor the gold you bring."  

Anger filled the king, his face turned,  
He ordered the furnace seven times to burn.  

Mighty men bound them, threw them in,  
The heat so fierce, it burned their skin.  

Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego fell,  
Into the fiery furnace, as stories tell.  

The king jumped up and questioned in surprise,  
"Weren't three men thrown in before my eyes?"  

"True, O King," came the counselor's reply,  
"But four walk free, unharmed by the fire's high.  

One looks divine, a godly son,"  
Nebuchadnezzar knew something great was done.  

"Servants of the Most High, come out, come near!"  
The three walked out, and the crowd did cheer.  

The fire hadn’t touched them, no smell, no singe,  
A miracle happened, the king had to cringe.  

"Blessed be your God," Nebuchadnezzar proclaimed,  
"Who sent His angel, none can be blamed.  

No god can deliver like this," he decreed,  
"Anyone speaking against Him, punished indeed."  

He promoted the three, gave them Babylon's trust,  
For they had shown faith, in God they did trust.
