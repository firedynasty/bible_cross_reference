Listen up, let me set the scene,  
Authority’s from God, unseen but keen.  

Resisting power, you resist His plan,  
Judgment’s coming, understand, man.  

Rulers ain’t no terror if you do what's right,  
Do good, and they’ll praise, keep you in sight.  

They're servants of God, wielding the sword,  
For those who do wrong, feel His accord.  

Submit not just from fear of wrath,  
But for conscience’ sake, stay on the path.  

Pay your taxes, give what’s due,  
Respect and honor, it’s up to you.  

Owe no debt but love to your brother,  
Loving your neighbor fulfills the law, like no other.  

“Don’t steal, don’t kill,” these commands are clear,  
Love your neighbor, keep them near.  

Love never harms, it completes the call,  
Love’s the law, the greatest of all.  

The time is now, wake from your sleep,  
Salvation's nearer, rise from the deep.  

Night's almost done, day is near,  
Shed darkness, in light appear.  

Walk in the day, shun revelry and strife,  
Put on Christ, live that righteous life.  

Fleshly desires, don’t give them place,  
Let His light guide you, in His embrace.
