Without excuse, man, you judge, you condemn,  
You do the same, can't hide from them.  

God's truth judges all who act this way,  
Think you'll escape? Well, no, not today.  

Despise God's riches, patience, and grace?  
Don't you know His goodness leads to a new place?  

Your hard heart's storing up wrath on that day,  
Judgment's coming, can't keep it at bay.  

"To each by their works," God will repay,  
Persevere in good, eternal life's your stay.  

But self-seeking hearts, who stray from truth,  
Wrath, indignation, that's the proof.  

Anguish on evil, whether Jew or Greek,  
But glory and peace for those who seek.  

God shows no partiality, it's true,  
Law or no law, judge or be judged too.  

Not hearers, but doers justified before God,  
Even Gentiles with law in their hearts, it's odd.  

Secrets revealed on that judgment day,  
By Jesus Christ, no time to play.  

You call yourself Jew, rest on the law,  
Guide the blind, but what do you draw?  

Preach don't steal, but do you take?  
Don't commit adultery, but do you fake?  

Glory in law, yet dishonor God,  
Gentiles blaspheme, ain't that odd?  

Circumcision counts if you do the right,  
Transgress and it loses all its might.  

Uncircumcised keeping law, will they not?  
Judge you who break it, got you caught.  

True Jew's inward, not just the flesh,  
Heart's circumcision, spirit refresh.  

Praise from God, not from men,  
That's the truth, again and again.
