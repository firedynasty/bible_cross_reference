By faith we're justified, peace with God we claim,  
Through Jesus Christ, our Lord, we call His name.  

In this grace we stand, with hope we rejoice,  
In both joy and suffering, we lift our voice.  

Suffering brings endurance, character's next,  
Character brings hope, never perplexed.  

Hope won't let us down, God's love's in our heart,  
The Spirit's gift within, a work of art.  

When we were weak, Christ died in our place,  
A rare act of love, none can erase.  

"While you were sinners," God shows His love,  
Christ died for us, sent from above.  

Justified by His blood, saved from wrath's fire,  
Reconciled through His death, lifted higher.  

We rejoice in God, through Jesus our Lord,  
Reconciliation's gift, our great reward.  

Through one man's sin, death took its reign,  
But grace through Christ breaks every chain.  

One man's trespass brought many to fall,  
But grace through Christ redeems us all.  

Death once ruled, but now grace abounds,  
Through Jesus, life and righteousness resounds.  

Through disobedience, sin spread wide,  
Through obedience, righteousness is supplied.  

Where sin increased, grace abounded more,  
Through Jesus Christ, eternal life we restore.
