I'm speaking truth, no lie, my conscience clear and bright,  
Pain in my heart, heavy, day and night,  

Wish I could be cursed, for my kin's sake alone,  
The Israelites, my brothers, flesh and bone.  

They’ve got the glory, the law, the covenants made,  
From them came Christ, blessed, unafraid.  

The word of God stands, it's not undone,  
Not all from Israel are Israel's son.  

Not all Abraham's seed are on this ride,  
“From Isaac,” God said, “your line's derived.”  

Children of promise, not flesh, are the heirs,  
“Sarah will have a son,” God declares.  

Rebekah conceived, two boys in her womb,  
God’s purpose stands, they hadn’t met their doom.  

“The elder will serve the younger,” was decreed,  
“Jacob I loved, Esau not freed.”  

Is God unjust? Never! Hear His call,  
“I show mercy and compassion, that’s all.”  

Not by man's will or race, but by grace,  
God's mercy alone sets the pace.  

To Pharaoh He spoke, “You've been raised high,  
To show my power, my name will fly.”  

He shows mercy or hardens, His choice, His voice,  
“Why fault us then?” some will voice.  

But who are we to question the Divine?  
Clay in the potter's hands, design refined.  

Some for honor, some for wrath,  
His patience endures on a righteous path.  

From Jews and Gentiles, the call rings out,  
“My people,” He calls where none had clout.  

In Hosea’s words, love will expand,  
“Children of the living God,” throughout the land.  

Isaiah cries, “A remnant will be saved,  
The Lord’s work, righteous, no delay.”  

Like Sodom and Gomorrah, we’d be,  
Without His seed, His mercy, His decree.  

Gentiles found righteousness, faith their guide,  
Israel pursued law, missed the ride.  

Why stumble? Works, not faith, their stone,  
“Behold, in Zion, a rock's been thrown.”  

Believe in Him, you won't be dismayed,  
Trust in the promise, the foundation laid.
