Did God reject his people? No, not at all,  
An Israelite like me, I stand, I recall.  

From Abraham's line, Benjamin's tribe I hail,  
God's word to Elijah tells a vivid tale.  

"Lord, they've killed prophets, altars torn down,  
I'm the last one left, they're seeking my crown."  

But God replied, "Seven thousand I’ve saved,  
Never bowed to Baal, always behaved."  

Today, a remnant by grace, held with care,  
Grace not by works, it's the gift we declare.  

Israel sought but didn't find, others chosen,  
A spirit of stupor, eyes and ears frozen.  

David said, "Let their table be a snare,  
Blind their eyes, bend their backs in despair."  

Did they stumble to fall? Not quite,  
Through their fall, salvation shone bright.  

Gentiles enriched by their fall from grace,  
Imagine their fullness, their return to the race.  

Gentiles, hear me, my mission's for you,  
To provoke my own, save a few, maybe two.  

Rejection brought the world to peace,  
Acceptance, life from the deceased.  

Holy first fruit, holy lump shared,  
Roots support us, be aware.  

Wild olive grafted, now you're in,  
Don't boast over branches, humble within.  

"Branches cut so I might stand," you say,  
True, but by faith, so don't sway.  

God's goodness and severity here,  
Continue in goodness, stay clear.  

Cut out from wild, grafted in,  
Natural branches, graft again, begin.  

Mystery in part, Israel's tale,  
'Til Gentiles fill, salvation won't fail.  

Deliverer from Zion, sins swept away,  
Covenant firm, his promise will stay.  

Enemies for now, beloved they remain,  
Gifts and calling irrevocable, plain.  

Disobedient once, now mercy received,  
Mercy for them too, once they believed.  

God bound all, mercy for every soul,  
Depths of wisdom, judgments untold.  

"Who's known God's mind, or given first?"  
All things from him, quench our thirst.  

To him be glory, forever and more,  
Through him, to him, let our praises soar.
