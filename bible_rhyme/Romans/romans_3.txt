What's the deal, what's the Jew's gain, what's circumcision's claim?  
Plenty in every way, they got God's truth to proclaim.  

If some folks lack belief, does it cancel God's stand?  
"No way!" God's always true, while man's truths turn to sand.  

"If our wrong highlights God's right, should He still bring wrath?"  
"No way! God judges the world, guiding all on His path."  

If my lie shows God's glory, why am I called to pay?  
"Let’s do evil for good"—those folks are led astray.  

So who's better—us or them? Not one stands without sin,  
All are under its power, no one's righteous within.  

Scripture tells it straight, "None seeks, none does good,  
Like graves with open mouths, deceitful tongues understood."  

“Their mouths curse, their feet rush to blood and strife,  
No peace, no fear of God, this is their life.”  

The law speaks to all, stops mouths in their tracks,  
No flesh justified by works; law shows sin’s cracks.  

But now, without the law, God's righteousness shines,  
Faith in Jesus, no difference, for those in His lines.  

All have sinned and missed God's glorious light,  
Yet through grace in Jesus, He makes wrongs right.  

God sent His Son, blood spilled to show,  
His righteousness shines now, for those who believe and know.  

Where’s the boasting now? It’s gone, no doubt,  
Not by works, but by faith that we shout.  

God’s not just for Jews, He's for Gentiles, too,  
Justifying all by faith, making all things new.  

Do we nullify law with faith's embrace?  
Never! We uphold it, in God's righteous space.
