Brothers, listen up, by God's mercy, I plead,  
Offer your bodies as living sacrifice, holy indeed.  
Stay true, don't conform to this worldly way,  
Transform your mind, let it guide the play.  

Prove what's good, pleasing, perfect in God's sight,  
Think wisely, keep your ego light.  
We're all in one body, many parts we hold,  
Different gifts, different roles, let them unfold.  

If you've got prophecy, share your faith's voice,  
In service, teaching, or exhorting, make your choice.  
Give with generosity, rule with care,  
Show mercy with cheer, love's everywhere.  

Love sincere, hate wickedness, cling to the good,  
Tender affection, honoring like we should.  
Be diligent, fervent, serving the Lord's call,  
Hope brings joy, endure troubles, pray through it all.  

Help the saints, open doors, be hospitable too,  
Bless those who hurt you, that's what we do.  
Rejoice with the joyful, weep with the sad,  
Think alike, stay humble, don't be too glad.  

Pay no evil, respect all that's right,  
Live in peace, avoid the fight.  
"Vengeance is mine," God says, let Him repay,  
Feed your enemy, give them drink, that's the way.  

Coals of fire on their head, kindness will shine,  
Overcome evil with good, let love define.
