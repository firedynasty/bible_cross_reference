Paul’s on the scene, called to preach the Word,  
God's Good News, the best you ever heard.  
Prophets spoke it, Scriptures defined,  
About God's Son, from David's line.  

Declared the Son with power, he rose from the dead,  
Jesus Christ our Lord, the one who led.  
Grace and apostleship through Him we receive,  
Faith across nations, in His name, believe.  

In Rome, you're called, beloved and known,  
"Grace and peace," from God’s own throne.  
I thank God for you, your faith worldwide,  
Unceasing in prayer, with you side by side.  

I long to see you, gifts spiritual to share,  
Together encouraged, each other’s faith there.  
Often I planned, but hindered I was,  
Fruit among you, the reason, the cause.  

To Greeks and foreigners, my debt is wide,  
Wise and foolish, I'm on that ride.  
Eager to preach, with strength and might,  
Not ashamed, it's the power and light.  

Reveals God's righteousness, faith to faith declared,  
"The righteous shall live by faith," prepared.  
God’s wrath from heaven on ungodliness shown,  
Suppressing truth, unrighteousness grown.  

Invisible things, since creation, are clear,  
Everlasting power, divine, near.  
Though knowing God, they failed to praise,  
Became vain, their hearts in a daze.  

Claimed wisdom, but fool's crown embraced,  
God's glory for images, disgrace.  
So God gave them to lusts and shame,  
Honored the creature, forgot His name.  

Vile passions took hold, against nature they turned,  
Men and women, in lust they burned.  
God gave them a mind that strayed,  
Unrighteous acts, they displayed.  

Filled with immorality, malice, and strife,  
Envy, murder, deceit filled their life.  
Arrogant, boastful, haters of God,  
Unmerciful and unkind, they trod.  

Knowing God’s law, ignoring His call,  
Approved those who sinned, led them to fall.
