No condemnation now, if you're in Christ,  
Walk by the Spirit, let the flesh take flight.  

Spirit's law gives life, sin's grip is gone,  
God sent His Son, sin's curse withdrawn.  

Law was weak, flesh couldn't fulfill,  
But with Spirit we walk, God's will is our thrill.  

Mind on the flesh leads to decay,  
Spirit's mind brings life, peace all the way.  

Hostile flesh can't please the Lord,  
But Spirit in you, your soul is restored.  

"Christ in you," your body's dead,  
But Spirit's alive, righteousness is spread.  

Raised Jesus up, He'll raise you too,  
Spirit within, life's promise new.  

Not debtors to flesh, let it die,  
Spirit-led life lets your soul fly.  

"Abba, Father!" we boldly cry,  
Spirit of adoption, no more sigh.  

Children of God, heirs with His Son,  
Suffer now, glory's battle won.  

Creation waits, eager to see,  
God's children revealed, in liberty.  

Groans of birth, till now still ring,  
For redemption, our bodies sing.  

Hope unseen, patience is key,  
Spirit intercedes, sets us free.  

Hearts searched, Spirit's prayer,  
God's purpose, love we share.  

All works for good, for those He calls,  
Predestined, justified, glorified — all.  

If God's for us, who could stand?  
Gave His Son, open hand.  

No charge sticks to God's elect,  
Christ intercedes, we are kept.  

What can cut us from His embrace?  
Famine, sword, or perilous chase?  

More than conquerors in His love,  
Nothing separates us, below or above.  

Neither death, nor life, nor power can sever,  
From God's love, in Christ forever.
