What did Abraham find, our ancient kin?  
Just by his flesh, could he win?  

If works could justify, he'd boast for sure,  
But not with God, there's more to explore.  

Scripture says, "He believed, it was right,"  
Faith was his key, not strength or might.  

A worker's reward is what he's owed,  
But faith gives grace, it's not a loan.  

David spoke of blessing, so clear,  
"Blessed are they with sins covered here."  

Sins forgiven, God won't charge,  
Faith not works, in Abraham's barge.  

Circumcised or not, does blessing apply?  
Faith made Abraham righteous, that's why.  

Was it before the knife's incision?  
His faith was counted in uncircumcision.  

Circumcision, a seal, a sign of faith,  
Father of believers, no matter their state.  

Not by law, but through faith, the heir,  
A promise given, beyond compare.  

If law made heirs, faith would be void,  
But God's promise, faith employed.  

Law brings wrath, disobedience appears,  
Faith brings grace, calms all fears.  

God said, "Father of nations you'll be,"  
In hope, Abraham set his gaze free.  

Old and worn, yet faith stayed strong,  
Sarah's womb, still he belonged.  

God's promise held, unwavering trust,  
Faith and glory, in Him he thrust.  

"Credited as righteousness," his story's told,  
Not just for him, but for the bold.  

For us who believe in God’s power today,  
Who raised Jesus, washed sins away.  

Delivered for sin, raised to justify,  
In faith, in promise, our hope lies high.
