Listen up, brothers, you know the deal,  
The law rules a man while he's real.  

A wife is bound by law to her man,  
But if he dies, she's free to stand.  

While her husband's alive, she can't stray,  
But if he’s gone, she's free to play.  

Like her, you're dead to law, through Christ's body,  
Joined to the One who rose, mighty and godly.  

In the flesh, sin's passions did thrive,  
Bringing death, no chance to survive.  

But now, released, we're alive anew,  
Serving in spirit, not in old views.  

Is the law sin? No way, my friend!  
It taught me sin, showed where I offend.  

It said, "Don't covet," so I saw,  
Sin flared up, breaking the law.  

Without the law, sin lay dead,  
But it revived, took me instead.  

The command meant for life, brought death,  
Sin tricked me, took my breath.  

So the law is holy, good, and right,  
But sin showed its full might.  

What's good didn't bring me death,  
It was sin that took my breath.  

The law is spiritual, but I'm sold,  
Under sin's weight, I'm controlled.  

I don't do what I desire,  
The sin I hate lights the fire.  

If I do what I hate, it shows,  
The law is right, as everyone knows.  

Sin lives in me, takes control,  
Even when good's the goal.  

Good I want, I just can't do,  
Evil comes through and through.  

Evil's present when I try,  
The law in me, it won't lie.  

I delight in God's law deep inside,  
But sin's law takes me for a ride.  

Oh, wretched man, who'll rescue me,  
From this body, set me free?  

Thank God for Jesus, our Lord,  
With my mind, God’s law's adored.  

But in the flesh, sin remains,  
The struggle real, the fight sustains.
