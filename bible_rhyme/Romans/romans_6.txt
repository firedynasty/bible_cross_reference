What’s the deal, should we keep sinning just to get grace?  
Nah, that’s not the way, we left sin, no need to embrace.  

We’re baptized into Christ, into his death we went,  
Buried with him, risen anew, our old life spent.  

United in his death, we share resurrection’s fire,  
Our old self crucified, sin’s chains we retire.  

He died, freed from sin’s grip tight,  
And if we died with him, we’ll live in his light.  

Christ rose, death can’t hold him anymore,  
"Death’s got no dominion," knocking on heaven’s door.  

He died to sin once, now living to God so bright,  
Consider yourselves alive to God, step into the light.  

Don't let sin rule your flesh, don’t let it guide,  
Offer yourselves to God, instruments of righteousness, stride.  

Sin’s got no hold, grace is your new place,  
Not under law, but under grace, you’re set in a new space.  

Shall we sin ‘cause of grace? No way, we obey,  
Who you serve is your master, sin or the righteous way.  

Thanks to God, you’ve been freed, no more sin’s slave,  
From the heart obeying truth, righteousness is what you crave.  

Speak in plain words for weakness in flesh,  
Serve righteousness, leave wickedness, refresh.  

Servants of sin, no righteousness in sight,  
What fruit did you gain, leading to death’s night?  

Now free from sin, God’s servants, you’ve grown,  
Fruit of sanctification, eternal life’s shown.  

The wages of sin? Death, in full strife,  
But God’s free gift is eternal life.
