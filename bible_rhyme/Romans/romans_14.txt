Accept the weak without debate, don't fight over what they ate,  
One eats it all, one's veggie-fed, don't judge, appreciate.  

To God each one will rise or fall, He's got the strength to make them stand,  
One celebrates a special day, another holds every day in hand.  

"Observe it or don't," the Lord's the key, thanks given high,  
Live or die, we belong to the Lord, that's the reason why.  

Christ died and rose, the living and the dead are in His sight,  
Why judge your brother? We'll face Christ, His judgment's right.  

"As I live," declares the Lord, "to me, knees will bend,  
Every tongue will confess to God," where we will all attend.  

So let’s not trip each other up, no stumbling in our way,  
Nothing's unclean unless you think it, love should guide what you say.  

If food offends, it grieves the soul, don't let love depart,  
Don't destroy a brother for whom Christ died, keep a loving heart.  

God’s Kingdom's not in food or drink, but righteousness and peace,  
Joy in the Spirit, serving Christ, that’s the real feast.  

Pursue what builds and brings us near, in peace let’s walk the land,  
Don't wreck God’s work for food alone, let love's building stand.  

Faith you hold, keep it strong, with God, your secret keep,  
Happy is the one who trusts, no judgment as they sleep.  

Doubt brings guilt if you partake, if faith ain't in the mix,  
All that's not of faith is sin, in Christ, find your fix.  

To Him who can establish you, according to good news,  
Jesus Christ revealed the mystery, left no clues.  

Eternal God commanded it, for faithful obedience shown,  
To the wise God be glory, through Christ, forever known.  

Amen.
