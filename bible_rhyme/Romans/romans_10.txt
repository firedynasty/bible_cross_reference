I pray for Israel, my heart's on fire,  
For their salvation, that's my desire.  
They've got zeal for God, I can testify,  
But lacking knowledge, they reach for the sky.  

They chase their own path, their own way,  
While God's righteousness they push away.  
Christ completes the law, that's the belief,  
For all who trust, He brings relief.  

Moses wrote the law, to live by and do,  
But faith says don't climb or descend, it's all in you.  
"The word's near your mouth and heart," it declares,  
The faith we preach, it's everywhere.  

Confess with your mouth, believe in your heart,  
Jesus is Lord, that's where you start.  
Believe He rose, and you'll be saved,  
Righteousness and salvation, paved.  

Scriptures promise, "No shame for those who believe,"  
Jew or Greek, the same Lord, they receive.  
"Call on His name, and you'll be saved,"  
Riches of mercy, for all enslaved.  

How can they call if they haven't believed?  
How will they know if they haven't received?  
Without a preacher, how will they hear?  
"Gospel's feet are beautiful," bringing cheer.  

But not all listened, Isaiah lamented,  
"Who believed the news we presented?"  
Faith comes by hearing, the word's the way,  
Yes, they heard, spread to the world, night and day.  

Did Israel know? Moses made clear,  
Jealousy from a nation without fear.  
Isaiah said boldly, "Found by those who don't seek,"  
Revealed to those who don't speak.

But Israel stands, with arms stretched wide,  
Yet they turn away, with stubborn pride.
