Paul and Tim, apostles by God’s hand,  
To Corinth’s church and saints in Achaia's land.  

"Grace and peace," from the Lord we impart,  
Blessings from God, who comforts the heart.  

In every trial, He’s there to bring calm,  
So we can comfort others with healing balm.  

Christ's sufferings hit, but comfort does too,  
Through Him, it abounds, flowing right to you.  

Afflicted for your salvation and peace,  
Comfort comes, making endurance increase.  

Our hope's rock solid, knowing you’ll find,  
Partake in His comfort, leaving pain behind.  

Don’t be clueless, we suffered in Asia,  
Burdened so heavy, almost lost our erasure.  

The death sentence loomed, but we trust the Divine,  
In God who raises, let His power shine.  

Delivered from death, He's our constant hope,  
With your prayers, together we cope.  

We boast in our conscience, pure and sincere,  
In God’s grace, not wisdom of flesh, it’s clear.  

We write no deceit, just truth to convey,  
In Christ’s day, we’ll boast, in a holy display.  

I planned to visit, so blessings could be,  
Journey to Macedonia, then back to thee.  

Was I fickle in this, with a “Yes and No”?  
No, God is faithful, in Him truth flows.  

Christ is preached, not with doubt or delay,  
For in Him, every promise is "Yes" to stay.  

God sealed us in Christ, Spirit in our hearts,  
He's the down payment, where true life starts.  

I spared you in Corinth, God is my guide,  
Fellow workers in joy, with faith as our pride.
