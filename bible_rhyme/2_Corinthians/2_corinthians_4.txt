Ministry's ours, mercy's been granted, we're in the fight, we don't get slanted.  
Hidden shame, we renounce and claim, no deceit in our game, truth's our fame.  

News might be veiled, but those who are frail, blinded by the world, can't see the tale.  
Preaching not us, but Jesus the Lord, servants for Him, His love our reward.  

God said, "Let there be light in the dark," shines in our hearts, igniting the spark.  
Treasure in clay, God's power displayed, not from us, but through Him conveyed.  

Pressed but not crushed, perplexed yet in sight, pursued but not lost, struck down, still we fight.  
In our bodies, Christ’s death we carry, His life revealed, His truth won't tarry.  

Living, yet near to death's silent keep, so you might have life, it runs deep.  
Faith's in our heart, "I believed, so I spoke," we too stand firm, it's no joke.  

The One who raised Christ, will lift us anew, present together, me and you.  
All for your sake, grace multiplied, thanksgiving rises, glory amplified.  

We don't faint, though outward decays, inward renewed, day by day it stays.  
Momentary troubles, they fade from our sight, eternal weight of glory, our guiding light.  

Seen is but temporary, unseen is real, eternal it stands, unchanging our zeal.
