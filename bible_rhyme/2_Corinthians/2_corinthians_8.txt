Listen up, brothers, I've got something to say,  
About God's grace in Macedonia, displayed every day.  
In trials and troubles, their joy overflowed,  
Deep in poverty, generosity showed.  

They gave what they could, and then some more,  
With hearts wide open, rich or poor.  
Begging us to share in this divine grace,  
Helping the saints, all in their place.  

First they gave themselves to the Lord above,  
Then to us, through His will and His love.  
We urged Titus to complete what he began,  
In this grace, fulfill the plan.  

You abound in faith, in love, in speech,  
Make sure this grace you also reach.  
I don't command, but test your love's sincerity,  
Through others' earnestness, your charity.  

Christ was rich, yet became poor,  
So you through His poverty could open the door.  
You started this work, the first to be willing,  
Now finish the deed, fulfill the filling.  

If readiness is there, it's what counts,  
Not by what you lack, but by what amounts.  
It's not about easing others while you’re distressed,  
It's about equality, that's the test.  

Your surplus fills their need, their excess fills yours,  
Balance in giving, and love that endures.  
As it's written, gather much, have none to spare,  
Gather little, have enough, it’s fair.  

Thank God for Titus, with care in his heart,  
He came to you, ready to take part.  
With him, a brother praised throughout the land,  
Appointed to travel, to lend a hand.  

We're cautious, avoiding blame or shame,  
Honorable in sight of men, and the Lord's name.  
Another brother joins, earnest as can be,  
Confident in you, for all to see.  

Titus is my partner, working for your cause,  
Brothers, apostles, glory without pause.  
Show your love's proof, let it be known,  
Before the assemblies, our boasting overgrown.
