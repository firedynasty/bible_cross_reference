Paul here, speaking with Christ's gentle grace,  
Lowly in presence, but bold in this space.  

I beg you now, when I'm there, not to show,  
The courage I have, against those in the know.  

We walk in the flesh, but our wars aren't the same,  
Weapons of God, mighty to bring down the flame.  

Tearing down thoughts that stand against His name,  
Bringing each thought to Christ, into the frame.  

Ready to revenge disobedience, I say,  
When your full obedience leads the way.  

Do you just see what's before your face?  
Consider again, we're all in Christ's embrace.  

I boast of authority given for your good,  
Building you up, like I said I would.  

“His letters are strong,” they claim with disdain,  
But presence is weak, and speech is plain.  

Let them think, what we write in our lines,  
We do when present; our deeds align.  

We don't compare, with those who self-praise,  
They measure themselves, trapped in that maze.  

We keep our boasting within God's set line,  
Reaching to you, spreading His design.  

Not boasting in others' work, our hope remains,  
As your faith grows, our influence gains.  

Preaching beyond, not where others have been,  
Boast in the Lord, let His glory be seen.  

Not self-approved, but by God's commend,  
He who boasts, in the Lord, let him depend.
