Yo, we work together, don’t take grace in vain,  
God said, “I heard you, salvation's not in vain."  
Right now’s the time, today’s that day,  
We’re giving no cause to fall along the way.  

Enduring in hardships, through beatings and pain,  
Imprisonments, riots, we stand, remain.  
With pureness, knowledge, kindness, we strive,  
Holy Spirit, sincere love, keep us alive.  

Truth in our words, God’s power we show,  
Righteous armor on, ready for the flow.  
Through glory and shame, truth in disguise,  
Unknown yet known, with open eyes.  

Sorrowful, but still we rejoice,  
Poor, yet rich, we lift our voice.  
Got nothing, yet everything's ours,  
Our hearts reach out, breaking the bars.  

Corinthians, hear us, hearts open wide,  
It's your own affections that keep you tied.  
Speak like children, open your hearts,  
Don’t be yoked with unbelievers, play your part.  

Righteousness and sin, they can't unite,  
Like darkness and light, they don’t fit right.  
Temple of God, not idols, you see,  
God says, “I’ll dwell, walk in thee.”  

“Come out, be separate,” says the Lord,  
“Touch nothing unclean, get on board.  
I’ll be your Father, you my sons and daughters,”  
Says the Almighty, living waters.
