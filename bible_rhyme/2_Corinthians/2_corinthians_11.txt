Bear with me in a bit of what seems like jest,  
Jealous for you, 'cause I want for you the best.  

Married you to one, a pure gift to Christ,  
Afraid you'll get tricked like Eve, enticed.  

If they preach a Jesus we never spoke,  
Or a spirit you ain’t met, or a different yoke—  

You put up with it, but I've got the knowledge,  
No false prophet, I'm college.  

I preached for free, no wages taken,  
Humbled myself, no mistake I'm makin'.  

Macedonian brothers filled my need,  
Kept me from burdens, planted my seed.  

In Achaia, my boasting won't quit,  
God knows my love, my truth legit.  

Cut off the false, apostles in disguise,  
Satan's mask won't fool my eyes.  

I speak a bit foolish, let me boast,  
Many boast in flesh, I boast the most.  

Took stripes, faced dangers, shipwrecked seas,  
Perils and fastings, on my knees.  

Hebrew, Israelite, Abraham's kin,  
Servant of Christ, labor and sin.  

In weakness I find my claim,  
Boasting in Christ, not for earthly fame.  

Damascus guard wanted me in chains,  
But through the wall, basket escape sustains.  

My journey's hard, but I stand strong,  
In weakness and truth, my boast belongs.
