Yo, are we trying to prove our worth?  
Need letters of praise to show our birth?  

Nah, you're our letter, clear for all to see,  
Written in hearts, Spirit set us free.  

With ink not, but Spirit's stroke,  
Hearts of flesh, not stone that broke.  

Confidence through Christ, we boldly stand,  
Not by our might, but by God's hand.  

Sufficient in Him, a new way we bring,  
Not just the letter, but life the Spirit does sing.  

The stone-carved law had its own shine,  
But couldn't hold, like a face in decline.  

If death's writ had its moment of grace,  
The Spirit's life takes a higher place.  

Condemnation had its glory, true,  
Yet righteousness shines brighter, a greater hue.  

What fades was glorious, that's clear,  
But what remains, it shines so near.  

Hope lets us speak with fearless tone,  
Unlike Moses, whose face was overthrown.  

Veil hid the end of what would fade,  
Their minds were hard, old ways they stayed.  

But turn to the Lord, the veil's away,  
In freedom's Spirit, there's a brighter day.  

With unveiled faces, His glory we see,  
Transformed from glory, Spirit sets us free.
