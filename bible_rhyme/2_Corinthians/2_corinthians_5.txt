In this tent we groan, our home’s not here,  
God’s got a house in heaven, crystal clear.  

We long for clothes from heavenly lands,  
A dwelling eternal, not made with hands.  

These mortal coils, they weigh us down,  
But life’s the crown, it’ll swallow the frown.  

God built us for this, He gave the Spirit,  
A promise so deep, you can almost hear it.  

Confident we stand, though apart from the Lord,  
We walk by faith, trusting in His word.  

Courageous we are, ready to leave,  
To be home with the Lord, that’s what we believe.  

Our aim’s to please, whether here or there,  
Facing the judgment seat, we’re aware.  

Everyone’s deeds will be laid bare,  
Good or bad, the truth we’ll share.  

Knowing God’s fear, we persuade all,  
Revealed before Him, hear His call.  

We don’t boast of ourselves, it’s not for show,  
We speak for you, in love we grow.  

For Christ’s love compels, He died for all,  
So we might live, responding to His call.  

Old ways gone, all becomes new,  
In Christ’s creation, a fresh view.  

God reconciled us through His Son,  
Ministry of peace, with Him we’re one.  

Ambassadors for Christ, hear our plea,  
Be reconciled to God, embrace and be free.  

He who knew no sin, took it for our sake,  
So God’s righteousness we could partake.
