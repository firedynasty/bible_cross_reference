Promises in hand, let's make a stand,  
Cleanse our lives, both mind and land.  

Open hearts wide, no wrongs have we done,  
Corruption and advantage, we've taken none.  

Not to condemn, but to remind you,  
We're in this together, in life and death too.  

I boast of you, my words run deep,  
Comfort fills me, my joy does leap.  

Macedonia's trials left us no rest,  
Fightings outside, fears in our chest.  

Yet God, who lifts the low and frail,  
Sent Titus to tell us your heartfelt tale.  

He spoke of your longing, your tearful zeal,  
And my heart rejoiced, his comfort was real.  

Though my letter brought grief, I do not regret,  
For it led to repentance, and I'm glad we met.  

Godly sorrow brings a change so true,  
Worldly sorrow's path leads to rue.  

Your earnest care shone through like light,  
With zeal and longing, you made it right.  

I wrote not for him who did the wrong,  
But to show your care, where we belong.  

Comforted now in joy's embrace,  
Titus refreshed by your grace.  

I boasted of you, and truth was shown,  
His love for you in abundance grown.  

With trembling hands, you received him well,  
In all things now, my confidence swells.
