I said to myself, "No more visits in sorrow,  
If I make you grieve, who'll bring joy tomorrow?"  
I wrote this to you, so when I arrive,  
We'll share in the joy, not feel deprived.  

My heart was heavy, tears filled my eyes,  
I wrote with love, no disguise.  
If someone's caused pain, it's not on me,  
But to you all, that's plain to see.  

The punishment's done, enough's the toll,  
Forgive and comfort, heal the soul.  
"You've got to show your love anew,  
Prove your obedience, see it through."  

I've forgiven for your sake, for the peace,  
No room for Satan, let his schemes cease.  
In Troas I went for the Good News to share,  
But without Titus, my heart was bare.  

So off to Macedonia, I took my leave,  
Thanks to God, in Christ we believe.  
We're a sweet aroma, scent fills the air,  
To the saved and lost, truth laid bare.  

From death to death, or life to life,  
Who can bear this, with all its strife?  
We ain't peddling words for gain or show,  
In sincerity, in Christ we grow.
