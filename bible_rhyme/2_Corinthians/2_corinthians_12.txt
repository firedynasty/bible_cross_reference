Boasting ain't my thing, but visions I will share,  
Fourteen years back, a man caught in the air.  

Was he in the body? Or was he out of sight?  
God knows the truth, he soared in heaven's light.  

He heard words unspoken, too holy to repeat,  
In Paradise he stood, where heaven's realms meet.  

I'll boast of this man, but not of my own might,  
In my weakness alone, I find my true light.  

Boasting's not foolish when truth's what I convey,  
But I hold back, so truth's all you see in my way.  

Revelations grand, a thorn pierced my skin,  
A torment from Satan, to keep pride from within.  

Three times I pleaded, "Lord, take this from me!"  
But He said, “My grace is enough, let it be.”  

In weakness, I glory, for Christ's power's there,  
When I'm weak, I'm strong, His strength I declare.  

Foolish in boasting, but you pushed me to say,  
I've shown signs and wonders, in my own humble way.  

No burden I brought, though I'm less than the rest,  
Yet I showed you the signs of an apostle's best.  

Ready to come again, no burden to bear,  
I seek not your wealth, but your souls with care.  

Gladly I'll spend, for your souls I give more,  
If my love grows stronger, will I be loved less than before?  

No tricks did I play, no deception was spun,  
Titus, my brother, took advantage of none.  

In the spirit we walked, in steps that are true,  
All for your building, in everything we do.  

But I'm afraid when I come, I may find strife,  
Jealousy and anger, disrupting your life.  

May God humble me, if sins still abound,  
Repent and turn, let no lustfulness be found.
