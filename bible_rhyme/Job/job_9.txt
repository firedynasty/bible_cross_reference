Job spoke up, feeling the weight,  
"How can man with God debate?

If He argues, who can reply?  
A thousand questions, no answer why.

Wise in heart, His strength's immense,  
Who defies Him, with no defense?

Mountains move, unaware they shift,  
Earth shakes, pillars lift.

He commands the sun, yet it stays,  
Seals the stars, dims their rays.

He alone treads the ocean's waves,  
Creates the constellations, none He saves.

Past finding out, His wonders soar,  
Marvels countless, forevermore.

He passes by, unseen, unknown,  
Who can stop Him, claim His throne?

His anger won't withdraw or fade,  
Even Rahab's helpers, dismayed.

How could I, a mortal, speak,  
When even righteousness feels weak?

Call and answer, still I doubt,  
In storms, He breaks me, inside and out.

Strength and justice, He defines,  
Righteousness, my word confines.

I am blameless, but life I scorn,  
Blameless perish, in God's storm.

Injustice reigns, judges blind,  
If not Him, then who's defined?

Life's swift, like runner's path,  
Eagles swoop, ships cut the wrath.

Forget my woes, put on a smile,  
Yet fear stalks me all the while.

Condemnation feels near at hand,  
Why labor in this barren land?

Snow and lye can't make me clean,  
In the ditch, my clothes demean.

He's not like man, a different breed,  
No umpire here to intercede.

Remove His rod, fear aside,  
Then I'd speak, with nothing to hide."
