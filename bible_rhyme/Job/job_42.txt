Job replied to Yahweh, standing tall,  
"You're the one who can do it all.  
You asked, 'Who's this with words so dense?'  
I spoke of things that made no sense.  

You said, 'Listen close, I will talk to you,  
I'll ask the questions; answer when I'm through.'  
I'd heard of you in whispers, that was clear,  
But now I see you, standing near.  

I despise myself, in dust I descend,  
Repentance is where my journey ends."  
After Yahweh spoke, anger burned bright,  
Against Eliphaz and friends, He set it right.  

"Your words missed the mark, not like my Job,  
Take your offerings and to him go.  
Seven bulls and rams, make your plea,  
My servant Job will pray for thee."  

They obeyed Yahweh and brought the flame,  
And Yahweh accepted, ended Job's pain.  
He turned Job's fate, doubled his wealth,  
Restored his fortune, restored his health.  

Brothers and sisters gathered, no longer apart,  
They comforted him, each with a generous heart.  
Gave him silver, rings of gold,  
Tales of his blessings would be retold.  

Yahweh blessed him more, the end greater than the start,  
Fourteen thousand sheep, oxen and camels took part.  
Seven sons and three daughters, beauty untold,  
Inheritance shared, timeless and bold.  

Job lived on, one hundred forty years,  
Saw generations, their laughter, their tears.  
Old and full of days, his story spun,  
A life complete, his journey done.
