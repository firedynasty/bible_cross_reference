Eliphaz stepped up, ready to speak,  
"Will you be grieved, if I take a peek?  
You've taught the masses, made weak hands strong,  
Your words lifted the fallen, helped them along.  

But now it's on you, and you start to wail,  
It touches your soul, makes your spirit frail.  
Your piety's your confidence, your ways your hope,  
Isn't that enough for you to cope?  

Remember now, who perished guilt-free?  
The upright never cut off, can't you see?  
Those who plow trouble, reap the same,  
By God's breath, they perish, by His anger's flame.  

Lions roar, fierce and loud,  
But their teeth break, even the proud.  
Old lions waste, prey won't be found,  
Lioness cubs scattered all around.  

A secret came, whispered to me at night,  
As the visions danced, and sleep took flight.  
Fear and trembling shook my bone,  
A spirit passed, left me alone.  

Couldn't see its form, but I heard it say,  
"Can man be just, as God any day?  
He trusts not His servants, charges them all,  
Even angels, before Him, fall.  

And how much more, those made of clay,  
Crushed to dust, at the end of the day.  
From dawn to dusk, they fade,  
Gone forever, without accolade.  

Their tent cords plucked, wisdom none,  
They die silent, their journey done."
