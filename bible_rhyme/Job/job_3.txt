Job opened his mouth, cursed his birth,  
"Perish the day I was brought to this earth.  

Let darkness claim the day I was born,  
Let no light shine, let it be forlorn.  

Let shadows lay claim, let clouds cover,  
Let blackness reign, no day to discover.  

Let that night be seized by thickest shade,  
No joy, no voice, let its presence fade.  

Curse it like those who call the beast,  
Let stars stay dark, let their shine cease.  

Why didn’t the womb become my grave,  
Why wasn't I spared from life to save?  

Why did knees hold me, why did I suckle,  
Why wasn’t I spared life's relentless struggle?  

In death, I'd rest with kings and the wise,  
With princes in gold, where no trouble lies.  

Where the wicked stop their troubling ways,  
Where weary souls find restful days.  

Prisoners free from master's call,  
Small and great, there equal in all.  

Why is light given to those in sorrow,  
Who dig for death, no hope for tomorrow?  

Rejoicing in grave's embrace at last,  
Why light to the lost, whose hope has passed?  

My sighs come before I eat each meal,  
My fears realized, my fate's a cruel deal.  

I'm not at ease, no rest in my heart,  
Trouble finds me, tearing me apart."
