Gather 'round, let me set the scene right,  
Heavenly beings came, shining bright.  

They stood before Yahweh, presenting their case,  
But Satan joined them with a bold face.  

God asked, "Where you been, Satan? Speak your mind."  
Satan said, "Roaming earth, walking to find."  

Yahweh spoke, "Have you seen my man Job?  
Upright and blameless, around the globe."  

Satan smirked, "Skin for skin, man'll trade it all,  
Touch his flesh, watch him fall."  

Yahweh allowed, "He's in your hand,  
But spare his life, understand?"  

Satan struck Job with sores so dire,  
From head to toe, they burned like fire.  

Job scraped his skin, sat in the ashes alone,  
His wife's words cut, but he stood firm like stone.  

She said, "Curse God, let death be nigh."  
He replied, "Foolish talk, I'll not defy."  

"Shall we take the good and not the bad?"  
In all his pain, his lips didn't go mad.  

Job's friends heard, they came to see,  
Eliphaz, Bildad, Zophar—three.  

They came to mourn the friend they knew,  
But from afar, they couldn't construe.  

They tore their robes, dust in the air,  
Silent vigil, just showing they care.  

Seven days, seven nights, no word said,  
For they saw his grief, heavy as lead.
