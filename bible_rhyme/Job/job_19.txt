Job stood up, felt the weight on his chest,  
"How long you gonna crush me, leave me distressed?  

You’ve slammed me ten times, no shame in your attack,  
If I’ve slipped up, my mistake’s got my back.  

You’re piling it on, making me feel small,  
But know it’s God’s net that’s got me in thrall.  

I shout out for justice, but it’s out of reach,  
He’s boxed me in, darkness on every beach.  

Stripped of my glory, crown's taken away,  
Hope’s uprooted, like a tree in decay.  

His wrath's burning, counts me as His foe,  
Troops gather ‘round, in my tent they grow.  

My brothers are distant, friends turn their backs,  
I’m a stranger in my home, under attack.  

I call to my servant, but he won’t even hear,  
Beg with my mouth, yet no one comes near.  

Wife finds my breath foul, kids shun the air,  
Young ones mock me, without a care.  

Friends I cherished now hold me in scorn,  
Skin clings to bone, from suffering worn.  

"Have pity, my friends, it's God's hand here,  
Why chase me like God, leaving me in fear?  

"Oh, if my words were etched in stone,  
For all time, in a place of their own.  

But I know, yes, my Redeemer lives,  
And in the end, He'll stand and give.  

After I’m gone, in my flesh I’ll see,  
God as my own, not a stranger to me.  

Heart’s consumed, burning with this flame,  
You plot ‘gainst me, seek root in my name.  

Be warned of the sword, wrath brings it near,  
Judgment’s real, so take heed, hear."
