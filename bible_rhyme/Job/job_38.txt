Out of the whirlwind, Yahweh spoke,  
"Who dims counsel with words that provoke?  
Stand up, brace yourself like a man,  
I'll question, you answer, if you can.  

Where were you when earth's corners I laid?  
Declare your wisdom, don't be afraid.  
Who marked its measures, if you know?  
Who set its corner so stars could glow?  

Who shut the sea behind its gate,  
Wrapped it in clouds, its bounds create?  
I told the waves, ‘No further here,  
Your proud waters stop, have no fear.’  

Have you commanded the morning light,  
Told dawn its place, chased off the night?  
Shaken wicked from earth’s face,  
Changed the clay, dawn's embrace?  

Have you walked the sea's spring deep,  
Or seen death’s gate in shadowed sleep?  
Tell me, if you know all the earth,  
Its width, its length, its place of birth.  

What's the path where light resides?  
Darkness, where's its house besides?  
Were you born when days were old?  
You know treasures of snow, I'm told.  

Who sends lightning, wind to roam,  
Channel floodwaters, lead them home?  
Rain on land bare and dry,  
Grass grows where no man’s nigh.  

Rain's father, who can claim?  
From whose womb does ice proclaim?  
Waters hard, frozen like stone,  
Depths turned solid, all alone.  

Can you bind Pleiades’ stars so tight,  
Or loosen Orion’s belt at night?  
Guide constellations, lead the Bear,  
Set heavens' laws, declare their care?  

Can you call to clouds, command the rain,  
Lightning serve you, by your name?  
Who gave wisdom to the heart’s core,  
Understanding to the mind's door?  

Who counts clouds, pours sky’s wine,  
When dust clumps, earth aligns?  
Can you hunt for lioness,  
Feed young lions in their rest?  

Who feeds ravens when they cry,  
Wanders young for food, asking why?"
