I made a pact with my own eyes, won't stare or fantasize,  
"What's my share from God on high? Is it ruin for those who lie?"  

If I've hurried to deceive, if falsehood's the path I weave,  
"Let God weigh me and see my heart, keep my feet from falling apart."  

If my eyes have led astray, if my hands have dirtied the way,  
"I'll plant the seeds, another can eat, pull up my field, roots and wheat."  

If temptation's held my heart, or by a neighbor's door I start,  
"Let my wife be another's prize, let her bed be shared, my demise."  

For that crime's a burning fire, consuming all I desire,  
A judge would find my actions wrong, destroying what I've built so strong.  

If I've despised a servant's plea, when they argued against me,  
"What then when God stands tall? What will I answer when He calls?"  

Same Creator made us both, in the womb, He gave us growth,  
If I've denied the poor their need, or let the widow's hope recede,  

If I ate alone, denied the orphan, kept my bread,  
"Let my shoulder break, my arm be torn, for God's terror keeps me warned."  

If I trusted in my gold, if wealth's the story I've told,  
If I praised the sun or secret moon, and kissed my hand to them too soon,  

"The judges would find me in sin, denying God, where do I begin?"  
If I've rejoiced when foes fell hard, or cursed them when they were scarred,  

My tent's guests never lacked for meat, no stranger slept out on the street,  
If I hid my sins like Adam did, or from the crowd, my guilt I hid,  

"Oh, that someone hears my plea, let the Almighty answer me!"  
I'd wear my faults like a crown, stride to Him, no looking down.  

If my land cried out in pain, if my harvest came from gain,  
"Let thorns grow where wheat was due, stinkweed instead of barley grew."  

With that, Job's words are through.
