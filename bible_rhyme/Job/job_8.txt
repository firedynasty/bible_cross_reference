Bildad stepped in, ready to reply,  
"How long you gonna let those words fly by?  

A mighty wind, that's all I hear,  
Does God twist justice? Nah, that's clear.  

Kids sinned, they faced the cost,  
Handed to their deeds, the line they crossed.  

Seek God hard, lay it down with might,  
If pure and upright, you'd find the light.  

From small starts, you’d grow large,  
Inquire the past, let their wisdom charge.  

Our days on earth, just a shadowy mix,  
Learn from the fathers, knowledge they fix.  

Papyrus and rush, can't grow alone,  
Without mire and water, they ain't grown.  

Green's the sight, not yet cut down,  
Withers faster than reeds in town.  

Forget God, and your hopes will fade,  
Trust like a web, too easy frayed.  

Lean on your house, it won’t stand tall,  
Cling hard as you might, it’ll still fall.  

Sunshine green, shoots spread wide,  
Roots hug rock, where stones abide.  

When uprooted, earth won't recall,  
From the ground, others will stand tall.  

God won't cast the blameless aside,  
Evildoers? He ain't on their side.  

Fill your mouth with laughter's cheer,  
Lips bursting with shouts sincere.  

Haters dressed in shame's attire,  
The wicked's tent consumed by fire."
