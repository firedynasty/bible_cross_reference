"Stick with me a bit, I've got more to say,  
On behalf of God, I'll show you the way.  

Knowledge from afar, righteousness I declare,  
My words aren't false, perfect wisdom is here to share.  

Behold, God's mighty, doesn't despise a soul,  
Strong in understanding, makes the whole world roll.  

Wicked lives aren't spared, justice for the low,  
His eyes on the righteous, he lets their power show.  

In chains they confess, their pride on display,  
He opens their ears, guides them back to his way.  

Listen and serve, and you'll prosper for years,  
Ignore, face the sword, die with unlearned fears.  

Godless hearts are angry, no cry when restrained,  
They die young, unclean, where pride has reigned.  

Affliction delivers, ears open in pain,  
He'd lead you to freedom, abundance your gain.  

Judgment grips you tight, let not wealth seduce,  
No bribe should sway, don't let riches reduce.  

Can wealth save in distress, or strength lift you high?  
Desire not the night, when folks say goodbye.  

Take heed of iniquity, avoid the wrong call,  
God's power exalted, teacher above all.  

Who can command him, say he's done wrong?  
Remember his works, in praises and song.  

All men see his glory, though from afar they gaze,  
God's greatness unknown, years unsearchable amaze.  

He gathers water drops, rains from vapor pour,  
Skies unleash abundance, blessing evermore.  

Who understands the clouds, his thunderous might?  
His light spreads around, sea depths in sight.  

By these he judges, feeds in plenty and need,  
Lightning in his hands, his commands they heed.  

Creation speaks of him, storms they foretell,  
Nature knows his power, his story to tell."
