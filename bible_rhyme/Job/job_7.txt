Isn't life on earth like a hired hand’s plight?  
Days drag on like laborers longing for night.  

I’m stuck with months of misery, can't seem to sleep,  
Toss and turn in the dark, the night not mine to keep.  

My flesh is dressed in worms, my skin breaks anew,  
Days fly like a weaver’s swift shuttle, hope’s out of view.  

Life's but a breath, soon you'll see me no more,  
Like a cloud that vanishes, gone to Sheol’s door.  

No coming back home, home's now a ghost,  
Silent I won’t be, as my spirit's engrossed.  

"Am I a sea monster, needing a guard?  
My bed offers comfort, but dreams hit me hard."  

Terrified by visions, I choose death over breath,  
Loathe this life, just leave me alone, embrace death.  

Why point me out, make me your concern,  
Visit each morning, test at every turn?  

If I've sinned, tell me, O watcher of men,  
Why this burden, why this mark, again and again?  

Pardon my faults, take my sins away,  
Soon I'll lie in dust, search all you may.
