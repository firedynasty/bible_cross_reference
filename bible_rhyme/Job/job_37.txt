Heart's pounding, can't hold still, it's a thunderous chase,  
Listen close, hear that voice, shaking the whole space.  

Sky's his canvas, lightning paints the earth's ends,  
A roar follows, majestic might it sends.  

God's voice is marvelous, beyond what we comprehend,  
He commands the snow, "Fall!" Rainstorms without end.  

Seals every hand, so all men will know,  
Animals hide, in dens they lie low.  

Storms from chambers, cold winds from the north,  
God breathes ice, waters freeze in their morph.  

Thick clouds loaded, lightning flashes wide,  
They turn, they move, as God decides.  

Correction or kindness, whatever his plan,  
God commands it all across the land.  

"Listen up, Job! Stand still, see the wondrous scene,  
Do you know how lightning strikes, where clouds have been?"  

"Can you spread the sky, like a metal mirror cast?  
Teach us what to say, when darkness holds fast."  

Men miss the light, skies clear with the wind,  
Golden splendor from the north, majesty pinned.  

Can't reach the Almighty, power so vast,  
In justice and righteousness, his will's steadfast.  

Men revere, wise hearts he won't regard,  
In awe of God's greatness, we stand on guard.
