Job stood up, his words were bold,  
"My complaint's rebellious, my story told.  

If only I knew where to find his throne,  
I'd plead my case, wouldn't stand alone.  

I'd lay my arguments, strong and neat,  
Wait for his answers, straight from his seat.  

Would he crush me with power, make me small?  
No, he'd listen, catch me if I fall.  

The upright reason, free from their plight,  
Delivered from judgment, into the light.  

East or west, I search, he's not there,  
North or south, his presence is rare.  

But he knows my way, he knows my soul,  
When he tests me, I'll come out like gold.  

My feet held fast, followed his way,  
Kept his commandments, every day.  

Treasured his words, more than my food,  
Stood by his laws, my spirit renewed.  

He stands alone, none can deny,  
What he desires, that's what he'll try.  

He performs what he sets for me,  
His plans are vast, like the open sea.  

Terrified at his mighty presence,  
Fear in my heart, his power immense.  

God's made my heart faint with dread,  
The Almighty haunts my head.  

Darkness surrounds, yet I'm still here,  
He didn't cut me off, my path is clear."
