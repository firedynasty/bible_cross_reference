Job sighed, then he spoke, feeling weary and sore,  
Said, "Y'all ain't helping, you're a miserable chore.  

Will these empty words ever cease, I ask?  
Why do you keep talking, what's the task?  

If our roles were flipped, I'd do just like you,  
But I'd use my words to comfort, it's true.  

I talk, but my sadness doesn't fade away,  
Silent or speaking, the pain's here to stay.  

God, look what you've done, worn me to the bone,  
Left me desolate, feeling alone.  

You've shriveled me up, I stand as a witness,  
My own body screams out my fitness.  

Your wrath tears me apart, a relentless attack,  
My enemies circle, never holding back.  

They gape at me, strike me, mock my face,  
Together against me, they set their pace.  

God's thrown me to the wolves, into wicked hands,  
Once I was safe, now I'm shattered like sands.  

He grabbed my neck, dashed me to pieces,  
Made me a target, my torment increases.  

Arrows fly 'round me, piercing deep inside,  
He spills my guts, no mercy to abide.  

Breached again, and again, like a giant's blow,  
Sackcloth on my skin, head in dust, lying low.  

Tears stain my face, eyes dark with sorrow,  
Hands clean of violence, prayers pure tomorrow.  

"Earth, don't hide my blood, let my cries rise,  
My witness in heaven, up in the skies.  

My friends mock me, eyes tearful, I plead,  
God, defend my cause, fulfill this need.  

Just a few years more, then I'll depart,  
To the place of no return, with a heavy heart."
