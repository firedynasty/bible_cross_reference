Job stood up, with words so profound,  
"Helping the powerless, how does that sound?  
Saving the weak, the arm that's so frail,  
When you speak wisdom, does it prevail?  

Who hears your words, what spirit's in play?  
Who trembles beneath the watery sway?  
Sheol's naked, God's eyes see it all,  
Abaddon uncovered, none can stall.  

He stretches the north over void and space,  
Hangs the earth in its perfect place.  
Clouds hold the waters, thick and profound,  
Yet they don't burst, they don't crash down.  

He cloaks his throne in cloud's embrace,  
Marks the boundary of light and dark's face.  
Heaven's pillars shake in fright,  
At his rebuke, they quake with might.  

The sea he stirs, power untamed,  
By his wisdom, Rahab's named.  
His Spirit paints the heavenly sky,  
With his hand, the serpent flies by.  

These are just whispers of his grand ways,  
Thunder's roar, who can appraise?"
