In the whirlwind's roar, Yahweh spoke,  
"Who dares to question, who dares provoke?  
If you contend with Me, answer straight,  
Speak up now, don't hesitate."  

Job replied with humble tone,  
"I'm too small, I'll leave it alone.  
Once I spoke, but no more words,  
I'll silence my voice, let actions be heard."  

From the storm's heart, Yahweh's voice came,  
"Prepare yourself, answer the same.  
Would you discredit my decree,  
Condemn me just to set you free?  

Do you have strength like God's own arm?  
Thunderous voice, a cosmic charm?  
Adorn yourself with majesty's light,  
Show your power, display your might.  

Bring the proud down, make them low,  
Crush the wicked where they show.  
Hide them deep in the earth's embrace,  
Bind them tight, erase their trace.  

If you can do all this alone,  
Then I'll admit your strength is your own.  

Observe behemoth, my crafted beast,  
He munches grass, his strength released.  
His power dwells in muscles tight,  
His tail moves with the cedar's might.  

Bones like bronze, limbs of iron,  
From him, creation won't tire.  
He roams the mountains, eats his fill,  
In fields where creatures play at will.  

Lotus shade, the willows bend,  
When rivers rise, his calm won't end.  
Even when the Jordan swells high,  
He stands unshaken as waters fly.  

Can anyone catch him, take him down,  
Snare his nose, bring him to the ground?"
