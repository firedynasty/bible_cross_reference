Bildad stepped up, ready to speak,  
"Dominion's His, it's not for the weak.  

In high places, He makes peace,  
His armies are countless, they never cease.  

His light shines on everyone, far and wide,  
How can man, with God, abide?  

Born from a woman, how can he be pure?  
When even the moon's light isn't sure.  

Stars lack brilliance in His holy sight,  
What about man, like a worm in the night?"
