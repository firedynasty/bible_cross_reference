Behold, I've seen it all, heard it clear,  
What you know, I know, I'm not below, my dear.  

I speak to the Almighty, that's my desire,  
But you're spinning lies, stoking no fire.  

If you'd hold your tongues, you'd show some grace,  
Hear my plea, let my words take their place.  

Will you speak for God with a deceitful tone?  
Show favoritism? Stand alone?  

Will He not find you out when you deceive?  
His majesty, a force you can't perceive.  

Your wise words turn to ashes in the breeze,  
Your defenses crumble, like clay with ease.  

"Be silent, let me speak, bring whatever's due,  
Why risk my life, when I've got naught to pursue?"  

He could kill me, hope fading to gray,  
Still, I'll stand my ground, come what may.  

This will be my saving grace,  
No godless man shall see His face.  

Listen close to what I declare,  
I hold my cause, my righteousness bare.  

Who dares to challenge me? Stand and see,  
Then I'll be silent, spirit set free.  

"Just spare me two things, then I'll have peace,  
Withdraw your hand, let the terror cease."  

Call, and I'll answer, or let me speak,  
How many sins have I, so bleak?  

Why hide your face, why make me an enemy?  
Chase a leaf, pursue dry stubble endlessly?  

You've written bitterness, sins of the past,  
Bound my feet, marked my path fast.  

I'm decaying, like a moth-eaten dress,  
A rotten thing, under stress.
