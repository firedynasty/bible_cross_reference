Bildad stepped up, ready to speak,  
"How long will you search for the words you seek?  

Why treat us like beasts, unclean in your sight?  
Will the world change for your anger, your might?  

The wicked's flame will flicker and die,  
In darkness his light won't touch the sky.  

His mighty steps will falter, drawn down,  
Trapped by his own feet, he will drown.  

A snare grabs his heel, a trap will snap,  
A hidden noose on his path, a cruel lap.  

Terrors chase him, haunting his side,  
His strength will vanish, nowhere to hide.  

His body consumed, firstborn of death,  
Pulled from his tent, robbed of breath.  

His home, not his, sulfur scattered around,  
His roots gone, branches cut to the ground.  

Forgotten completely, no name in the street,  
From light to darkness, driven in defeat.  

No sons, no kin, none remain,  
A past that haunts, a future in vain.  

Those who come will tremble, those before were awed,  
Such is the fate of those who defy God."
