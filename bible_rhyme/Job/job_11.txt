Zophar steps up, ready to reply,  
"Should all those words just pass on by?  

Should endless talk make you seem right?  
When you boast, should we stay quiet, not fight?  

You claim you're pure, clean in sight,  
But oh, if God would speak, show His might!  

He'd reveal wisdom, secrets in store,  
Much more than you've ever bargained for.  

Can you grasp the mystery of God's plan?  
His heights and depths, beyond any man.  

Higher than the heavens, deeper than the grave,  
Wider than the earth, broader than the waves.  

If He acts, who can oppose His might?  
He sees the false, detects wrong in the night.  

An empty mind grows wise, it's said,  
Like a wild donkey's colt, untamed, unled.  

So set your heart, hands stretched to the sky,  
If wrong's in your grasp, cast it goodbye.  

Then pure you'll stand, no need to fear,  
Your past like water, no longer near.  

Life will shine brighter, clearer than noon,  
Darkness like morning, gone too soon.  

Secure you'll rest, hope in your soul,  
Safe and sound, none will take control.  

But the wicked's eyes will fail, they see,  
No escape, their hope's to cease to be."
