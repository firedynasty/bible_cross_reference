In those days of old, when God had my back,  
His light led me through every shadowy track.  

When the Almighty was my constant friend,  
My kids gathered ’round, joy with no end.  

Steps were washed with butter, rocks poured oil,  
At the city gate, my presence was royal.  

Young men hid when I passed by,  
While elders rose, not one could deny.  

Princes held tongues, hands on their lips,  
Nobles fell silent, no words, no quips.  

Ears blessed me for the words I'd say,  
Eyes saw me, gave honor my way.  

I rescued the poor, answered their plea,  
Made the widow's heart sing with glee.  

Righteousness clothed me, justice my crown,  
For the blind and the lame, I never backed down.  

Father to the needy, I fought for their right,  
Broke wicked jaws, plucked prey from the night.  

I said, "I’ll die in my house, days like sand,”  
Roots to water spread, dew on my land.  

My glory stayed fresh, bow strong in hand,  
Men listened in silence, heard my command.  

They waited for words like rain from the sky,  
Drank in my speech, let out a sigh.  

I shone my smile, their doubts did chase,  
Led them as chief, comforted with grace.  

Like a king in the army, I took my stand,  
Bringing solace, a steady hand.
