Eliphaz stepped up, with a question in his gaze,  
"Should a wise man talk nonsense, just a wind in a blaze?  

Talk without purpose, speeches doing no good,  
You're throwing off fear, hindering your Godhood.  

Your sins teach your tongue, crafty words you choose,  
Your own mouth condemns you, you just can't refuse.  

Were you the first born, before the hills stood tall?  
Did you hear God's secrets, like you know it all?  

What do you know that we don't understand?  
With us are the elders, gray heads in this land.  

Is God's comfort too small? Are his words too light?  
Why's your heart flashing, setting God in your sight?  

What is man to be pure, born of woman, to be right?  
He trusts not his holy ones, even heaven's not in his sight.  

How much less a man, corrupt, drinking sin like water?  
Listen close now, let me show you the slaughter.  

The wicked twists in pain, every day he cries,  
A terror in his ears, the destroyer never lies.  

He doubts the light, waits for the sword,  
He searches for bread, darkness his reward.  

Distress grabs him tight, like a king ready to fight,  
He's been proud against God, running blind in the night.  

He’s covered in fat, lived in cities desolate,  
His wealth won't last, darkness seals his fate.  

The flame will dry his branches, blown away like a breath,  
Trusting in nothing, leading himself to death.  

His time will come short, the vine won't see fruit,  
The olive tree barren, deceit in pursuit.  

The godless are barren, fire consumes their tents,  
They birth mischief and lies, and their hearts are bent."
