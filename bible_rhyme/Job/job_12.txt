Job stood up, voice strong and clear,  
"Y'all think you're wise, like wisdom's gonna disappear.  

But I've got insight, I've got my own,  
I know what you know, I'm not alone.  

I'm the punchline, though I call on the Lord,  
The righteous, the blameless, they get ignored.  

To those at ease, misfortune's a joke,  
Ready to pounce on those who choke.  

Robbers thrive, while provoking God's their game,  
Holding their idols, they feel no shame.  

But check the animals, they'll teach you things,  
Birds in the sky, their wisdom sings.  

Talk to the earth, it'll show you the way,  
Fish of the sea got plenty to say.  

Who doesn’t see Yahweh's hand in all,  
Crafting life's breath, both great and small?  

Doesn’t the ear test words like food,  
With age comes wisdom, that's understood.  

With God there's wisdom and power with might,  
He breaks what’s built, locks you tight.  

He dries the waters, or floods the ground,  
Strength and wisdom, endlessly profound.  

Counselors stripped, judges turned to fools,  
Kings and their bonds, he adjusts the rules.  

Priests stripped bare, the mighty fall,  
Trust and wisdom, he takes it all.  

Princes shamed, the strong laid low,  
He uncovers shadows where secrets grow.  

Nations rise, nations fall,  
Enlarge, destroy, he does it all.  

Chiefs lose sight, they wander around,  
Stagger like drunks, their lost ground."
