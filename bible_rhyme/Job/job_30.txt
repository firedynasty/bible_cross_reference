Younger folks laugh, no respect for my name,  
Their fathers not fit for my hounds in the game.  

What good's their strength, when age they've lost?  
Gaunt and famished, in desolation they're tossed.  

Gnawing on dry ground, salt herbs they find,  
Broom tree roots, that's their food type, unkind.  

Driven from men, like thieves they flee,  
In valleys and rocks, in holes they be.  

Braying in bushes, 'neath nettles they hide,  
Children of fools, cast out worldwide.  

Now I'm their song, a mocking refrain,  
They despise me, spit in disdain.  

"He's loosened his cord, afflicted my soul,"  
Rabble rises, my feet they control.  

They tear at my path, promote my fall,  
Without a hand, they ruin it all.  

Wide breaches they come, rolling with might,  
Terrors chase honor, gone like twilight.  

Soul poured out, affliction's tight grip,  
Night brings pain, bones gnaw and rip.  

Clothing disfigured, binds me tight,  
Thrown in the mire, dust and plight.  

"I cry to You, no answer returns,  
You stand and gaze, while my soul burns."  

Cruel You’ve turned, with powerful hand,  
Lifted by wind, driven by sand.  

To death's house, I know I'm led,  
Stretching a hand in my dread.  

Didn't I weep for the troubled and weak?  
Yet darkness came, for light I seek.  

Heart troubled, no rest in sight,  
Affliction's days, I mourn in the night.  

A brother to jackals, with ostriches I roam,  
Skin black and peeling, my bones burn like stone.  

Harp turned to mourning, pipes weep in despair,  
My once joyful song, now cries fill the air.
