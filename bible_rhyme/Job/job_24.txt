Why ain't the Almighty sharing His times?  
Why don't His people see the signs?  

Some move landmarks, steal and scheme,  
Take the flocks, live the dream.  

They snatch the donkey, leave the fatherless bare,  
Take the widow’s ox, like they don't care.  

They push the needy out, make the poor hide,  
Like wild donkeys, they roam far and wide.  

In the desert, they seek their bread,  
Children fed, while they lie in dread.  

Cutting fields, gleaning vines that are cursed,  
Naked in cold, with hunger they thirst.  

Wet from the mountains, rock is their bed,  
They snatch the child, the poor they mislead.  

Naked they wander, carrying sheaves,  
In the walls they toil, yet thirst deceives.  

From the city, groans rise like the tide,  
The wounded cry, but God seems to hide.  

"Rebels against light," God declares,  
They shun its paths, caught unawares.  

Murderer at dawn, taking life's breath,  
Poor and needy fall to death.  

Adulterer waits till twilight's stealth,  
Hides his face, in darkness he dwells.  

Digging through houses, they find their gain,  
Daylight’s alien, darkness their domain.  

Morning to them like thickest night,  
Lost in terror, they shun the light.  

Foam on waters, cursed they remain,  
Sin consumes like drought’s domain.  

Forgotten by womb, worm’s sweet feast,  
Unrighteousness falls like a broken beast.  

Devours the barren, no kindness shown,  
Widows weep, their cries unknown.  

Yet God keeps the mighty standing tall,  
In His power, they rise from the fall.  

Secure in His watch, they rest in peace,  
Exalted then gone, their striving cease.  

Cut off like grain, low they lie,  
Could you prove me wrong, or is this truth in disguise?
