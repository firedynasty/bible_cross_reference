Call out now, who hears your call?  
To which holy one will you turn at all?  

Resentment slays the foolish man,  
Jealousy kills, that's the plan.  

I've seen fools rise and take their place,  
But cursed is their dwelling, gone without a trace.  

Their kids are lost, far from safe embrace,  
Crushed at the gate, no saving grace.  

Hungry take their harvest, no mercy shown,  
Traps await, substance overthrown.  

Trouble's not born from dust alone,  
Man's born to spark like a rolling stone.  

"But me, I seek God, commit my cause,"  
He does great wonders, without pause.  

"He gives rain, fields are watered, renewed,  
Raises the lowly, mourners are blessed, pursued."  

Crafty plans He frustrates, hands can't scheme,  
Wise in craftiness, their plans lose steam.  

Darkness in daytime, they grope around,  
He saves the needy, justice is found.  

The poor have hope, injustice shut tight,  
Happy the corrected, God's light is bright.  

"He wounds and binds, heals every scar,  
Delivers in troubles, no evil's bar."  

In famine, redeemed from death's cold sword,  
Hidden from tongues, peace is restored.  

Laugh at destruction, no fear of beast,  
Stones and animals, all make peace.  

Tent in peace, nothing missed,  
Your offspring great, in life's list.  

Grave in full age, like grain in season,  
Hear this wisdom, for life's good reason.
