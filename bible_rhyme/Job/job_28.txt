Silver and gold, they come from the ground,  
Iron and copper, they're smelted down.  

Man digs deep, cuts through the night,  
Searching for stones hidden from sight.  

Breaks open shafts where no feet tread,  
Far from men's homes, where he's led.  

Earth gives bread, but fire's below,  
Sapphires, gold dust, all in a row.  

No bird can see that hidden trail,  
No lion's paw, no falcon's wail.  

Reaches in rock, mountains he moves,  
Cuts channels deep, precious grooves.  

Streams are bound, light's brought to bear,  
Hidden things laid open and bare.  

"But where's wisdom? How is it found?  
Not in the living, not in the ground."  

Deep says, "Not here," the sea agrees,  
Gold can’t buy it, no price can seize.  

Ophir's gold, onyx, and sapphire's glow,  
Can't match wisdom's worth, don't you know?  

Coral and crystal, rubies and gold,  
Wisdom's value never told.  

Where's it from? Where does it stay?  
Hidden and kept from the light of day.  

Destruction and Death, they only hear,  
A whisper of wisdom, never near.  

"God knows its path, sees its place,  
Under all skies, across all space.  

He sets the winds, measures the seas,  
Rain and thunder, all decrees."  

He saw and declared, searched it true,  
"Fear of the Lord is wisdom for you.  

To shun evil, that's understanding's cue."
