Zophar stepped up with words in haste,  
His mind couldn't wait, his thoughts had a taste.  
"I've heard the shame, felt the sting,  
Spirit inside me's ready to sing.  

Know this truth from times of old,  
Wicked's triumph's short, their joy's not bold.  
Though they rise to the cloud's high throne,  
They'll vanish quick, like dust, like bone.  

Gone like a dream, they fade from sight,  
Chased away like visions of the night.  
No eyes will see them anymore,  
Their place empty, like before.  

Their kids beg the poor, try to regain,  
Their wealth returns like summer rain.  
Youthful bones turn to dust,  
What felt sweet turns to rust.  

Wickedness sweet under their tongue,  
Hidden away, but can't be undone.  
Riches swallowed, but soon regurgitate,  
God pulls them out, sealing their fate.  

Venom's in the veins, poison from snakes,  
No more sweet streams, no more cake.  
He built his house on stolen ground,  
Now nothing left, just hollow sound.  

No peace within, no joy to hold,  
Feasted on all, yet he’s left cold.  
Fullness turns to distress and fear,  
Wrath rains down, bringing him near.  

Flees from steel, but bronze will find,  
Arrows pierce, terror's unkind.  
Darkness swallows all his gain,  
Fire consumes, leaves only pain.  

Heavens unveil his wicked ways,  
Earth rejects him, counts his days.  
His house will crumble, swept away,  
This is the fate for those who stray."
