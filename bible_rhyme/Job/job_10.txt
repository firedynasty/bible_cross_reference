I'm weary of my soul, man, tired of this life,  
Gonna speak my pain, cut through like a knife.  

To God, I cry, "Don't condemn me, show me why,  
Why you contend with me, make me want to die.  

Is it good you oppress, despise what you've made,  
Smile on the wicked, like a twisted charade?  

Do you see with eyes like a mortal man's gaze?  
Are your days like ours, passing in a haze?  

You seek out my sins, iniquity you find,  
Though you know I'm clean, I'm trapped in this bind.  

Your hands shaped me, framed every bone,  
Yet here I am, feeling so alone.  

Remember, I'm clay, your creation, your plan,  
Will you turn me to dust, like the fate of a man?  

Poured out like milk, curdled like cheese,  
Clothed in skin and bone, knit with such ease.  

You gave life, kindness, preserved my soul,  
But kept your plans hidden, a mystery whole.  

If I sin, you mark it, no forgiveness in sight,  
Wicked or righteous, my head can't take flight.  

If I stand tall, you hunt me like a beast,  
Your power unleashed, never to cease.  

Witnesses against me, your anger's no jest,  
Life's a battle, no peace, no rest.  

Why was I born? Should've skipped this strife,  
Wished I’d gone from womb to grave, no life.  

Aren't my days few? Give me a break,  
Let me find comfort before shadows take.  

To the land of darkness, where no light shows,  
Where order's gone, only midnight knows."
