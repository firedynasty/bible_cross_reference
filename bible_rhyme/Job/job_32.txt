Yo, these three stopped talkin' to Job, their words run dry,  
'Cause in his own eyes, righteous, he stood by.  

Elihu's anger rose, son of Barachel's line,  
Against Job, it blazed, for justifying, not divine.  

Mad at his friends too, their arguments weak,  
Condemned without answers, their logic antique.  

Young Elihu waited, respect for age in his stance,  
But silence from the old gave him his chance.  

"I'm young, you're old," Elihu did proclaim,  
"I held back my thoughts, didn't seek the fame.  

Time should speak, years teach the wise,  
But wisdom's not just found in the great or the skies.  

Spirit in man, from Almighty it flows,  
Understanding deep, it graciously bestows.  

Listen to me, hear my opinion unfold,  
For I waited, your stories, patiently told.  

Gave you my ear, but none could sway,  
No answer for Job, not a word to say.  

Don't claim wisdom, saying God will refute,  
Job ain't aimed here, no need to dispute.  

They stand amazed, silent and still,  
Shall I wait, or speak, my heart's full to spill?  

Words fill me up, like wine in a skin,  
Ready to burst, let the speaking begin.  

I'll speak my mind, refresh my soul,  
No flattery here, just truth to extol.  

No titles given, no man will I praise,  
For my Maker would end my days."
