Man born of woman, days so few,  
Full of troubles and struggles too.  
Like a flower, he blooms then falls,  
Shadows flee; heeds no calls.  

Do You watch this fleeting breath?  
Judge him now or in his death?  
Clean from unclean, can it be made?  
Days are numbered, bounds are laid.  

Let him rest till his work is done,  
Hope for the tree, when cut, it’s won.  
Roots may age, trunk may die,  
Water’s scent makes it rise high.  

But man lies low, spirit flown,  
Waters dry, rivers moan.  
Man lies down, doesn’t rise,  
Till the heavens, new skies.  

"Hide me, Lord, till wrath has passed,  
Set a time, remember at last.  
If he dies, will life restart?  
I’d wait for peace, for a new heart."  

"You’d call, I’d reply,  
Your work—desired, no lie.  
Now you count each step I take,  
Watch my sins, each mistake."  

Mountains fall, rocks move away,  
Water wears stones, dust is prey.  
Hope's destroyed, man departs,  
You change his face, torn apart.  

Sons may rise, he doesn’t see,  
Brought low, no clarity.  
Pain in flesh, soul mourns within,  
Life’s brief tale, where to begin?
