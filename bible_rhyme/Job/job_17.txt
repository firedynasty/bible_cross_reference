My spirit's consumed, my days are through,  
The grave's wide open, ready for you.  

Mockers surround, my eyes on their taunt,  
Provoking me with every flaunt.  

"Give a pledge," I cry, "Stand with me,"  
But hearts are closed, no one to see.  

They've hidden understanding, won't exalt,  
Denouncing friends, their eyes halt.  

Made a byword, spit hits my face,  
Sorrow dims my eye, shadows take place.  

The upright, shocked, the innocent rise,  
Righteous hold on, strength in their eyes.  

Return, I say, no wisdom found,  
My days are gone, plans unbound.  

They turn night into day, with false light,  
Darkness persists, hope out of sight.  

Is Sheol my home, darkness my bed,  
Corruption my father, worms where I'm led?  

Where's my hope? Who'll witness my fear?  
Will it descend with me, disappear?
