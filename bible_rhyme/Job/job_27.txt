As long as God lives, my rights have been stripped,  
The Almighty's hand made my soul feel clipped.  

Life's still here in me, God's breath in my chest,  
My lips won't speak lies, in truth I invest.  

I'll never justify you, not while I'm alive,  
Integrity's my armor, I'll always strive.  

Let those against me be cursed like the bad,  
For godless hope fades when life turns sad.  

Does God hear their cries when trouble's near?  
Do they call on Him with devoted fear?  

I'll teach of God's power, won't hide the truth,  
You've seen it yourselves, don't be uncouth.  

The wicked's fate, from God they receive,  
Their children face swords, no bread to relieve.  

Their remnants buried, widows won’t weep,  
Though riches they stack, justice will keep.  

His house like a moth’s, frail and light,  
He lies down wealthy, but vanishes from sight.  

Terrors flood like waters in the night,  
The storm takes him, he's swept from sight.  

The east wind lifts, and he's gone in haste,  
Men clap and hiss, he's erased from his place.
