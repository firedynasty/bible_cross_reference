"Can you snag Leviathan with a hook, or tie him down with a single look?  
Can you rope his nose, pierce his jaw, make him stop with just your awe?  

Will he beg you for a break, whisper soft for your own sake?  
Will he serve you like a slave, forever bound, forever brave?  

Will you play with him like a bird in the sky, bind him up and watch him fly?  
Can merchants trade, split him like a pie, fill his skin with barbs and make him cry?  

Touch him once, remember that fight, don't do it again, it won't be right.  
Hope is empty when he's near, just the sight will fill with fear.  

None's so bold to stir his rest, who can stand before the best?  
"Who gave to me first, that I should pay? Everything's mine under heaven's sway."  

Limbs and strength, I won't keep quiet, mighty frame, no need for riot.  
Strip his coat? Who'll dare to try it, jaws of terror, you'd deny it.  

Face like doors, his teeth a fright, scales so tight, they seal the night.  
No air between, they hold on fast, stuck together, they hold steadfast.  

Sneezes flash like beams of light, eyes like dawn, burn so bright.  
Mouth sparks fire, flames leap high, nostrils smoke like pot on fire.  

Breath ignites coals, flames rush out, strength in neck, terror shouts.  
Flesh so firm, it won't be moved, heart like stone, never soothed.  

Rising high, the mighty fear, retreat before his thrashing near.  
Swords can't cut, nor spear, nor dart, iron and bronze, weak from the start.  

Arrows, stones, they can't compel, clubs are stubble, javelin's swell is just a spell.  
Undersides like potsherds sharp, leaving trails through the muddy tarp.  

Makes the deep boil like a pot, ocean's calm, he stirs it hot.  
Path behind him shines so bright, deep's white hair, a dazzling sight.  

No equal on this earth, king of pride, fearless from birth, his reign worldwide."
