Eliphaz spoke, with a tone so bold,  
"Can a man profit God? Is that what you're told?  
Is it joy to the Almighty, you being right,  
Or gain to Him, in your perfect sight?  

Is it your piety that brings reproof,  
Or judgment's weight under heaven's roof?  
Ain't your wickedness stacked high like a wall?  
No ending to iniquities, so tall.  

You take from brothers, pledges for naught,  
Strip the naked, without a second thought.  
Thirsty souls, you didn't quench or feed,  
Hungry folks left in their need.  

Mighty men roam the earth they claim,  
Honorable lives with no shame.  
Widows went away empty, tears left unspoken,  
Fatherless arms cruelly broken.  

Snares surround, fear grips tight,  
Darkness blinds, floods blotting light.  
Isn't God on high in heaven's throne?  
Stars so high, beyond stone and bone.  

You question, 'What does God perceive?  
In thick clouds, does He grieve?'  
Old ways of men, wicked paths they tread,  
Quickly snatched, like streams they're led.  

Yet God filled their homes with His good grace,  
The wicked's counsel's a distant place.  
Righteous rejoice, innocence in their laugh,  
Enemies cut off, like a fire's last chaff.  

"Get to know Him, find peace, my friend,  
Goodness follows, no need to pretend.  
Hear His words, store them deep,  
Return to Him, righteousness keep.  

Lay treasure down, dust and stones,  
Make the Almighty your precious tones.  
Delight in God, lift your face up high,  
Pray, He listens, vows reach the sky.  

Speak a decree, it'll be your way,  
Light will shine, brighten your day.  
When cast down, just say 'rise again,'  
He saves the humble, time and again.  

Deliverance comes, not for the pure alone,  
But cleansed hands will atone."
