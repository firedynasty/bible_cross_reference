Woe to the city, full of deceit and theft,  
Prey never leaves, it's all that's left.  

Whips crack, wheels clatter loud,  
Horses prance, chariots proud.  

A rider mounts, swords flash bright,  
Spears gleam, bodies lost to the fight.  

Heap of corpses, can't find an end,  
Stumbling over bodies, around the bend.  

Prostitution's charm, witchcraft's might,  
Nations and families sold in the night.  

"Behold, I'm against you," Yahweh declares,  
"I'll shame your face, strip you bare.  

Nations will see, kingdoms will know,  
I'll make you vile, a spectacle show.  

Folks will flee, saying, 'Nineveh's waste,  
Who'll mourn for her, in such haste?'"  

Better than No-Amon, sitting 'midst rivers grand,  
With waters, the sea was her wall and stand.  

Cush and Egypt her strength unbound,  
Put and Libya her helpers, around.  

Yet she was taken, captive she went,  
Children dashed, her men in torment.  

"You'll be drunk, seek strongholds wide,  
Gates open, fire devours inside."  

Troops like women, fortresses like figs,  
Shaken, they fall, into the eater's digs.  

Draw water, strengthen the stronghold's might,  
Tread the clay, make bricks tight.  

Fire will consume, sword will sever,  
Devour like locusts, now or never.  

Merchants like stars, locusts they flee,  
Guards like swarms, nowhere to see.  

Shepherds sleep, nobles lay low,  
People scatter, mountains they go.  

No healing your wound, fatal you be,  
All clap their hands, acknowledging your cruelty.
