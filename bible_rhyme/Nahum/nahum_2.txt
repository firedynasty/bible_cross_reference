The one who shatters comes right at you,  
Guard the fort, watch your path—do what you gotta do!  

Yahweh lifts Jacob's honor high,  
Though destroyers tore 'em down, left 'em dry.  

Red shields gleam on the warrior's arm,  
Chariots flash, spears cause alarm.  

In the streets, those chariots storm,  
Like torch-lit lightning, they transform.  

Troops are summoned, stumble, and fall,  
Rushing, they dash, right to the wall.  

River gates open, palace dissolves,  
All is exposed, as mystery evolves.  

Nineveh’s pool, long been still,  
Now folks flee, against their will.  

"Stop! Stop!" the cries ring out,  
But no one turns, without a doubt.  

Plunder silver, plunder gold,  
Endless treasures, stories told.  

Empty, void, waste in the air,  
Hearts melt, knees shake in despair.  

Where lions roamed without a care,  
Now silence reigns where none would dare.  

Lions once feasted, caves filled with prey,  
Now Yahweh speaks, hear what He'll say:  

"Behold, I'm against you," Yahweh declares,  
"Chariots burn, and lions beware.  

Your prey cut off, voices fade away,  
No more messengers, no more sway."
