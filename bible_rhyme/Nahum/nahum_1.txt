Nahum sees a vision, Nineveh in sight,  
Yahweh’s wrathful, ready for the fight.  

God's jealous, takes vengeance with a flame,  
Anger's slow, yet His power's the same.  

Enemies quake, they feel His might,  
In the storm and whirlwind, He takes flight.  

The sea dries up, rivers run no more,  
Bashan, Carmel, Lebanon, sore.  

Mountains quake, hills melt away,  
Earth trembles where He holds sway.  

"Who can stand when anger ignites?  
Fire pours, rocks break in His sights."  

But Yahweh’s good, a refuge strong,  
He knows His own, where they belong.  

An overflowing flood will end the strife,  
Enemies pursued into the night of life.  

"What plot you against the Lord’s plan?  
Once ended, affliction won’t rise again."  

Thorns entangle, they drown in their drink,  
Consumed like stubble, dry as you think.  

One devises evil, wicked in his ways,  
Yahweh sees him, ends his days.  

God says: "Though they’re strong and many,  
I'll cut them down, no longer any."  

"I've afflicted, but no more,  
Break the yoke, bonds I’ll restore."  

"No name will bear on this vile place,  
Images gone, a grave in disgrace."  

See the feet on the mountains bright,  
Bringing good news, peace in sight.  

Judah, keep your vows and feast,  
The wicked’s gone, forever ceased.
