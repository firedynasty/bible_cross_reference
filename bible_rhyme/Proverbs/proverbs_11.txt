A crooked scale's an abomination, true and bright,  
But honest weights bring Yahweh delight.  

Pride rolls in, bringing shame in its wake,  
But wisdom's the gift the humble take.  

Integrity guides the upright's feet,  
While the treacherous fall, their end they meet.  

Riches won't help on the wrathful day,  
But righteousness saves, keeps death at bay.  

Blameless paths are directed right,  
The wicked trip on their own spite.  

Delivered are the upright, the unfaithful snared,  
By the desires that left them unprepared.  

When the wicked die, their hopes all fade,  
Expectations of power quickly degrade.  

The righteous escape, trouble sheds its weight,  
While the wicked end up in their place of fate.  

The godless mouth can destroy a friend,  
But the righteous with knowledge will defend.  

When the righteous prosper, the city's glad,  
But when the wicked fall, cheers are had.  

The upright's blessing lifts a town high,  
The wicked's words bring it down to die.  

Despise your neighbor, and wisdom's lost,  
Understanding keeps peace, no cost.  

Gossip betrays like a serpent's hiss,  
But a trustworthy spirit keeps secrets amiss.  

Without wise guidance, nations fall apart,  
But many counselors bring victory to the heart.  

Collateral for strangers brings pain and woe,  
Those who refuse find security, though.  

A gracious woman finds honor, it's true,  
While violent men count riches they accrue.  

Merciful souls do good within,  
While cruelty troubles the flesh, the sin.  

Wicked people earn deceitful pay,  
But righteousness sows rewards that stay.  

True righteousness brings life to the soul,  
Pursue evil, and death takes its toll.  

Perverse hearts are Yahweh's disdain,  
Blameless ways bring delight, no shame.  

Evil won't go unpunished; see it plain,  
The righteous's offspring will be delivered again.  

Like gold in a pig's snout, a beauty with no sense,  
Desires of the righteous are good and intense.  

One scatters and gains, more than before,  
Hoarding brings poverty, nothing more.  

The generous soul grows rich and fat,  
He who waters, gets watered, imagine that.  

Withholding grain, the people curse loud,  
Bless those who sell, make a crowd proud.  

Seeking good brings favor, a light,  
Chasing evil, it comes like night.  

Trust in riches, and you'll surely fall,  
Righteousness flourishes, a green leaf tall.  

Trouble your house, inherit the wind's blow,  
Fools serve the wise, as they surely know.  

The righteous bear fruit, a tree of life,  
Wise souls gather, away from strife.  

Righteousness rewarded here on earth,  
More so the wicked, their sin's worth.
