"Listen up, my son, keep my words tight,  
"Keep my commands for life, guard them in your sight.  

"Wrap them on your fingers, like ties that don't part,  
"Etch them deep, write on the tablet of your heart.  

"Call wisdom your sister, understanding your kin,  
"They'll guard you from strangers, from seductive sin.  

From my window, looking through the lattice wide,  
I saw a young man, lacking sense, from his stride.  

He passed near her corner, by her house he crept,  
In twilight's shadow, into darkness he stepped.  

There she met him with attire of deceit,  
Loud, defiant, roaming every street.  

She caught him, kissed him, brazen face,  
"Peace offerings are mine, I’ve found my place.  

"I came to meet you, sought you with care,  
"My couch is ready, with tapestries rare.  

"Perfumed with myrrh, aloes, and spice,  
"Let's revel till morning, in love we'll entice.  

"My man’s not home, gone on his way,  
"With money in hand, he'll return another day."  

Her words were smooth, her trap was set,  
He followed her path, without regret.  

Like an ox to the slaughter, a fool to his fall,  
An arrow in his liver, unaware of it all.  

Now sons, listen, heed my call,  
Don’t walk her path, don't let your heart stall.  

Many are wounded, mighty her slain,  
Her house leads to Sheol, paths of death's domain.
