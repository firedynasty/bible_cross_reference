Better a dry crust in peace’s embrace,  
Than a house full of feasts with strife in place.  

A wise servant takes the lead in the game,  
Rules over sons who bring their family shame.  

Silver in the pot and gold in the fire,  
But Yahweh tests the heart's true desire.  

Evildoer's ears love wicked lips,  
Liars heed mischievous tips.  

Mock the poor and you mock your Maker’s grace,  
Joy in disaster? You'll find disgrace.  

Grandkids are old folks' shining crown,  
Kids find glory in their parents renowned.  

Arrogant speech doesn't suit a fool’s role,  
Lying lips can't fit a prince's goal.  

A bribe's a gem, makes the giver thrive,  
Turns everything golden in his life.  

Cover an offense, spread love around,  
Repeat it? Friendships will hit the ground.  

Rebuke sinks deep in the wise one’s mind,  
While fools need lashes, a hundred at a time.  

Rebellion’s the quest of an evil man's heart,  
A cruel messenger is set to play his part.  

Meet a bear robbed of her cubs if you aim,  
Better that than a fool lost in his shame.  

Evil for good brings evil your way,  
Never will leave, no matter what you say.  

Strife begins like a dam’s first tear,  
Stop it quick, handle it with care.  

Justifying the wicked, condemning the right,  
Both are abominations in Yahweh's sight.  

Why give a fool cash to buy wisdom he lacks,  
Understanding's a road he never tracks.  

A friend loves always, a brother’s there for strife,  
Born to stand by you through the storms of life.  

A man without sense strikes hands, takes a stand,  
Becomes collateral in his neighbor's land.  

Love disobedience, and strife you’ll adore,  
Build high gates? Destruction’s at your door.  

A twisted heart can’t find its way,  
A deceitful tongue will lead astray.  

Father of a fool finds sorrow anew,  
No joy in folly, just a bitter view.   

A cheerful heart’s the best remedy known,  
A crushed spirit dries up the bone.  

In secret, the wicked twist justice’s path,  
Perverse hearts find nothing but wrath.  

Wisdom’s in front of those who see,  
Fools wander aimlessly, never free.  

A foolish son brings grief, bitter strife,  
To both his father and his mother’s life.  

Punishing the righteous isn’t right,  
Flaying the just, none in Yahweh’s sight.  

Few words show knowledge, temper keeps sight,  
Silent fools are thought wise in the night.
