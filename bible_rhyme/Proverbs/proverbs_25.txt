Solomon's proverbs, wisdom on display,  
Kings and God, they both have their say.  

Heavens so high, earth's depth profound,  
A king’s heart's secrets can't be found.  

Silver refined, dross cast away,  
Wicked gone, the throne will stay.  

"Don't lift yourself before the king's face,  
Better rise up than to fall from grace."  

Don't rush to court with your claims and your blame,  
Your neighbor might turn and put you to shame.  

Talk it out, keep secrets tight,  
Loose lips bring shame, reputation’s blight.  

A right word spoken, golden and bold,  
Like apples of gold, in silver it's told.  

A wise word, like gold on the ear,  
Brings wisdom clear, it’s music to hear.  

Cold snow refreshes, harvest time's friend,  
A faithful messenger, the soul he'll mend.  

Clouds with no rain, gifts that don’t come,  
Promises broken, a heart goes numb.  

Patience persuades, calm words break bone,  
Soft power’s might is quietly shown.  

Find honey? Eat just what you need,  
Too much sweet, you'll lose the lead.  

Don't wear out your welcome, give space,  
Your neighbor might tire, hate your face.  

False witness, a weapon sharp and keen,  
A faithless friend, like a painful scene.  

Songs to a heavy heart, miss the beat,  
In cold, take a coat—don't bring the heat.  

Feed your enemy, quench his thirst,  
Heap coals on his head, let peace come first.  

North wind brings rain, gossip brings wrath,  
A quiet home better than a contentious path.  

Cold water revives, far news like gold,  
A righteous man polluted, loses his hold.  

Don't eat too much honey, don't seek your own fame,  
Without restraint, your spirit's to blame.
