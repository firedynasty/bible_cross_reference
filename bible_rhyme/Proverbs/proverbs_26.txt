In summer, snow's not right, nor rain when crops need to bloom,  
Honor suits not a fool, that's clear, no room for that buffoon.  

Sparrows flutter, swallows dart, curses that miss their mark,  
Whip for the horse, bridle for donkey, fools need a rod, not a spark.  

"Don't answer fools with their own game, or you'll look like a clown,"  
"But answer them with wisdom, or their folly will bring you down."  

Sending messages with a fool’s hand, you're dancing with danger,  
Like cutting off feet, sipping violence, inviting true anger.  

Loose legs of the lame can't hold, a fool’s mouth spins parables cold,  
Honor to fools, like stones tied in a sling, folly uncontrolled.  

Thorns in the drunkard's grasp, stinging, yet fools don’t sense the sting,  
Archers wounding all, hiring fools leads to havoc and suffering.  

Dogs returning to their mess, fools repeat their folly with zest,  
Wise men think they know it all, but for fools, there’s still more hope, even in jest.  

Sluggards cry, "Lions roam the street!" in bed they stay,  
Turning like doors on hinges, never seizing the day.  

Hand in the dish, too lazy to feed, wisdom they think they own,  
Seven wise men can’t match the sluggard's self-conceit, it’s overblown.  

Grabbing a dog’s ears, meddling in fights that aren’t their own,  
Madmen shoot flames, then claim, "Just a joke!" as deception’s throne.  

No wood, no fire; no gossip, peace will conspire,  
Contentious tongues kindling strife, whispers dig deeper, inspire.  

Silver gloss on clay pots, fervent lips, yet hearts of rot,  
Charming speech hides evils deep, deception sewn in the plot.  

"Don’t believe the charming voice, seven abominations hide,"  
Malice wrapped in deceit, in public, evil can't abide.  

Dig a pit, fall in it; roll a stone, watch it roll back home,  
Lying tongues breed hate’s tone, and flattery leads to ruin alone.
