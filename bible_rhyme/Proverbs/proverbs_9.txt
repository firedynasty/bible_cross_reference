Wisdom’s got a house built, pillars stand tall,  
Carved seven strong, she’s got it all.  

Meat’s prepared, the wine mixed fine,  
Table’s set, ready for dining time.  

Maidens sent, voices on high,  
Calling from the city's sky:  

“Simple ones, come inside,  
Turn here, let wisdom be your guide.”  

“Eat my bread, drink my wine,  
Leave your past, walk in line.”  

Correct a mocker, you’ll get scorn,  
Reprove the wicked, face their storm.  

But tell a wise one, they’ll love you,  
Teach the righteous, their learning’s new.  

"Fear of Yahweh starts it all,  
Understanding stands tall."  

By wisdom, your life extends,  
Years multiply, no pretend.  

Wise or mock, it’s yours to own,  
Bear your burden all alone.  

The foolish woman, loud and proud,  
Undisciplined, she draws a crowd.  

Sitting high, she calls out loud,  
To those passing by in the crowd:  

“Simple ones, come right here,  
Empty minds, don’t you fear.”  

“Stolen water, sweet as can be,  
Secret food, pleasures you’ll see.”  

But spirits linger in her hall,  
Her guests in Sheol, they fall.
