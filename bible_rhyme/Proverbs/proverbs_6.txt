If you pledged for your neighbor, struck hands in a deal,  
You’re trapped by your words, caught in what you feel.  

Listen up, son, get out of this snare,  
Run to your neighbor, lay it all bare.  

No sleep for your eyes, keep them awake,  
Escape like a gazelle, quick moves you must make.  

Look to the ant, lazy one, be wise,  
No boss, yet in summer her food she supplies.  

How long will you sleep? When will you rise?  
A nap here, a nap there, poverty in disguise.  

A rogue with a perverse mouth, causes pain,  
Winks his eye, signals feet, motions are plain.  

Evil in his heart, discord he sows,  
Sudden calamity, that’s how it goes.  

Six things the Lord hates, seven revile,  
Prideful eyes, false tongues, hands blood-stained vile.  

Scheming hearts, quick feet to do wrong,  
False witness and discord, they don’t belong.  

Son, heed your dad’s command, don’t stray,  
Mom’s teaching guides you every day.  

Keep them close to your heart, tie them tight,  
When you walk, sleep, or wake, they’re your light.  

Commands are lamps, laws shine bright,  
To guard you from danger in the night.  

Beware the smooth words of a wife astray,  
Her beauty’s trap, don’t fall, walk away.  

A harlot’s price is a crumb of bread,  
She hunts lives while leading them to dread.  

Can fire be held without burning clothes?  
Walk on coals, escape unscathed, who knows?  

Touch your neighbor’s wife, pay the price,  
Punishment follows, heed this advice.  

Steal to eat, folks understand,  
But when caught, return sevenfold from your hand.  

Adultery lacks sense, destroys your soul,  
Wounds and disgrace take their toll.  

Jealousy’s fury, husband’s rage,  
No ransom will soothe, no gift will engage.
