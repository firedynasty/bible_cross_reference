Listen up, if you take these words, plant them deep in your soul,  
Tune your ear to wisdom, make understanding your goal.  

Cry out for insight, raise your voice to the sky,  
Seek wisdom like silver, search for her, don't pass by.  

Then you'll get the fear of Yahweh, find knowledge, pure and grand,  
For wisdom flows from His mouth, understanding is in His hand.  

He's got wisdom stored for those walking straight and true,  
He's a shield for the righteous, protecting them through and through.  

He guards the paths of justice, secures the saints' way,  
Then you'll know what's right and fair, in every step you stay.  

Wisdom enters your heart, like music to your soul's beat,  
Discretion's your watchman, keeping danger off the street.  

Rescues you from evil ways, from men speaking twisted lies,  
Who leave light's path for darkness, where only wickedness flies.  

They take joy in doing wrong, delight in crooked tales,  
Keep you safe from the stranger with words that tip the scales.  

Her house leads to the grave, her path's a one-way track,  
No one returns from her hold, no one's coming back.  

So walk with good men, righteous paths keep you in line,  
For the upright stay in the land, their roots deep like a vine.  

But wicked fall away, treacherous can't hold their ground,  
Only the pure remain, while the corrupt are not found.
