A wise woman builds her house with grace,  
The foolish one tears it down, leaves no trace.  

The upright walks with reverence, fears the Lord,  
The perverse despises, ignoring His word.  

Foolish talk brings pain like a rod,  
But wise lips shield against that prod.  

No oxen, the crib is spotless and clean,  
But strength of the ox brings what’s unseen.  

Truthful witness won't speak a lie,  
False tales from liars never die.  

A scoffer seeks wisdom, but leaves it unfound,  
While knowledge to the discerning comes around.  

Avoid the foolish and their empty chimes,  
For wisdom on their lips, you won't find.  

Prudent wisdom considers its way,  
Fools deceive, led astray.  

Fools laugh at atonement, ignore the price,  
Among the upright, goodwill’s precise.  

The heart knows its joy and pain alone,  
Not shared with strangers, kept like a stone.  

The wicked's house will crash, it's known,  
The upright's tent will thrive and grown.  

A way may seem right, bright and deft,  
But lead to death, leaving bereft.  

Laughter hides sorrow, joy can end,  
Heavy hearts, like an unknown friend.  

Unfaithful repaid in their own ways,  
The good rewarded in brighter days.  

The simple believes everything told,  
But the prudent, their path they hold.  

The wise steer clear of evil’s call,  
Fools rush in, reckless in brawl.  

Quick anger leads to folly’s snare,  
Crafty ways bring hate, beware.  

Simple inherit folly, blind quest,  
Prudent crowned with knowledge, blessed.  

The evil bows to the righteous stand,  
The wicked kneel at the good's command.  

The poor shunned, the rich with friends,  
Yet caring for the poor, blessings extends.  

Plotters stray in evil's dark hood,  
While love and faith plan what's good.  

Hard work brings profit, a tested claim,  
Idle talk leads to poverty's shame.  

The wise wear riches like a crown,  
Fools crowned by folly, worn down.  

Truthful witnesses save souls from plight,  
False witnesses deceive in the night.  

In fear of Yahweh, a fortress strong,  
A refuge for children, where they belong.  

This fear is life’s fountain, clear,  
Turning from death's snare, drawing near.  

In people's numbers, a king's glory bright,  
A lack destroys, dims that light.  

Slow to anger, understanding blooms,  
Quick temper leads folly to its dooms.  

Peaceful heart, life’s body feeds,  
But envy rots like bones, it bleeds.  

Oppress the poor, show contempt for Him,  
Kindness to the needy, honors brim.  

The wicked falls in calamity’s storm,  
The righteous finds refuge, a new form.  

Wisdom rests in the understanding heart,  
It’s known even in fools, a hidden part.  

Righteousness lifts a nation high,  
Sin disgraces, leaves it dry.  

A wise servant earns the king’s grace,  
Shame brings wrath, a losing race.
