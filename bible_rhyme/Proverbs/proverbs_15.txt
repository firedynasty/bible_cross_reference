A gentle word can cool you down, harsh words just provoke a frown.  
The wise commend with knowledge deep, while fools in folly's waters seep.  

Yahweh's eyes see far and near, watching all that's done right here.  
A gentle tongue's a life-filled tree, deceit just crushes, makes you flee.  

A fool ignores his father's say, the prudent listens, finds his way.  
In righteous homes, treasures abound, but wicked income brings you down.  

The wise lips spread knowledge wide, the fools' hearts have nothing inside.  
Wicked sacrifices Yahweh hates, but upright prayers open gates.  

The wicked ways Yahweh detests, but loves the righteous, they're blessed.  
Discipline's for those who stray, hate correction, death will pay.  

Sheol and Abaddon lie in sight, how much more hearts filled with light.  
A scoffer hates reproof and wise, wisdom's counsel he denies.  

A glad heart makes a face that's bright, a broken spirit's lost its light.  
Understanding seeks the truth, fools just feed on folly's sleuth.  

Troubled days are filled with dread, a cheerful heart's a feast instead.  
Better little with Yahweh's fear than treasure with trouble near.  

A simple meal where love abides beats hatred with a calf that hides.  
A wrathful man just stirs the pot, while patience eases every spot.  

The sluggard's path's a thorny mess, the upright walks with no distress.  
A wise son brings his father joy, a foolish man his mother destroys.  

Joy in folly's fool's delight, but understanding keeps it right.  
Without counsel, plans will fall, many advisors strengthen all.  

Joy's found in a timely word, uplifting speech is always heard.  
Wisdom's path leads upward flight, keeping Sheol out of sight.  

Yahweh uproots the proud with might, but keeps the widow's border right.  
He hates wicked thoughts and lies, but pure thoughts are what He buys.  

Greed brings trouble to your place, but hating bribes, you'll find your grace.  
The righteous heart weighs what to say, the wicked mouth just spews away.  

Yahweh's distant from the vile, but righteous prayers make Him smile.  
The eyes' light makes the heart rejoice, good news strengthens bones with choice.  

Ears that hear the wise's call will find their place among them all.  
Refuse correction, despise your soul, listen and gain understanding's goal.  

Fear of Yahweh brings wisdom's light, before honor, humility's right.
