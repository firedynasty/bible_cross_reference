A good name’s worth more than riches untold,  
Better than silver, more precious than gold.  
Rich or poor, we all share this call:  
"Yahweh's the maker of them all."  

The wise man sees danger and steps aside,  
The simple keep walking, with no place to hide.  
Humility and fear of Yahweh bring life,  
Wealth and honor, free from strife.  

Thorns and snares lie for the wicked, beware,  
Guard your soul, and you'll stay clear of their snare.  
Teach a child the path to know,  
When he's grown, he'll never let it go.  

The rich rule the poor, that's just how it goes,  
Borrowers bow to lenders, everyone knows.  
Sow wickedness, reap trouble, feel the sting,  
But with a generous eye, blessings will spring.  

Drive out the mocker, watch strife take flight,  
Quarrels will vanish, insults out of sight.  
Love purity, speak grace, and you'll find,  
Friend to the king, with heart and mind.  

Yahweh’s eyes guard knowledge, the faithful He'll bless,  
But frustrates the unfaithful, they find no success.  
"There’s a lion outside!" the sluggard cries,  
Excuses to hide while ambition dies.  

An adulteress's words, a deep pit they make,  
Fall in that trap, and your soul will ache.  
Folly's tight in a child's heart,  
But discipline drives it apart.  

Oppress not the poor, nor give the rich more,  
Both paths lead to poverty’s door.  
Listen up, take in wise words,  
For pleasant truths should be eagerly heard.  

"I teach you now," Yahweh’s wisdom to gain,  
"Trust in Me, and you'll sustain."  
Thirty truths written, counsel and knowledge to impart,  
Reliable words to guide your heart.  

Exploit not the poor, the needy don’t crush,  
For Yahweh defends them, He will hush.  
Avoid hot-tempered friends, anger leads astray,  
Lest you learn their ways and lose your way.  

Avoid the handshake on debts you can’t pay,  
Or your bed may be taken away.  
Don’t move ancient stones, respect the past,  
For skilled men serve kings, their work will last.
