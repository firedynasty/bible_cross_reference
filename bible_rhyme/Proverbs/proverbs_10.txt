Solomon's got proverbs, wisdom to share,  
A wise son brings joy, a fool's a mother's despair.  

Wicked treasures profit none, they fall flat,  
Righteousness saves from death, remember that.  

Yahweh feeds the righteous, keeps them full,  
But the wicked's desires, He'll never pull.  

Lazy hands bring poverty, no wealth in sight,  
But diligence and hard work, that's what takes flight.  

Gather in summer, be the wise son indeed,  
Sleep through the harvest, shame's what you'll breed.  

Heads of the righteous, blessings abound,  
But the wicked's mouth? Violence is found.  

Righteous memories bless, they don't rot,  
But wicked names decay, they soon forgot.  

Wise hearts take commands, they understand,  
A chattering fool? Watch him fall, can't stand.  

Walk blameless, walk sure, don't deceive,  
Perversion's found out, soon you'll grieve.  

A wink of the eye, sorrow it brings,  
Chattering fools, fall like broken wings.  

Righteous mouths, springs of life they provide,  
While the wicked speak violence, can't hide.  

Hatred stirs conflict, love heals all,  
Wisdom on lips, the foolish fall.  

Lay up your knowledge, wise men do,  
Foolish mouths near ruin, they lack the clue.  

Rich man's wealth, his city strong,  
Poverty's the poor's destruction song.  

Righteous labor leads to life, it's plain,  
Wicked sin increases, brings the pain.  

Heed correction, find life's way,  
Forsake reproof, lead others astray.  

Hide hatred, lie, or speak slander, you're a fool,  
Words without limit, disobedience rules.  

Righteous tongue, choice silver shines,  
Wicked hearts, worth little, no refined lines.  

Lips of the righteous feed many, they thrive,  
Foolish lack understanding, fail to survive.  

Yahweh's blessing brings wealth, trouble's none,  
Foolish love wickedness, wisdom's understanding won.  

Wicked fears will find them, righteous desires met,  
Whirlwind takes the wicked, righteous stand yet.  

Vinegar to teeth, smoke to eyes,  
Sluggard's a burden, leaves sender’s cries.  

Fear Yahweh, lengthen days,  
Wicked years shorten, their hope decays.  

Righteous joy, their hope a stronghold,  
Wicked ways destruct, as truth is told.  

Righteousness stands firm, they won't be moved,  
The wicked will fall, their place disproved.  

Righteous mouths speak wisdom, truth they impart,  
Perverse tongues cut off, deceitful at heart.  

Righteous lips know what's right, they preserve,  
Wicked mouths twist truth, they swerve.
