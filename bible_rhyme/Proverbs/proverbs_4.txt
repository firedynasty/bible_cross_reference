Yo, listen up, sons, hear your father’s sound,  
Pay attention 'cause wisdom is what’s going down.  

I was once a child, my father’s pride,  
Tender in my mother’s sight, love amplified.  

He said, “Keep my words, let 'em sink in your heart,  
Hold tight to my teachings, let life truly start."  

“Get wisdom, get understanding, don’t you forget,  
Stick to my words, they’re the best you’ll ever get."  

“Don’t let wisdom go, she’ll guard your way,  
Love her deeply, she’ll keep you every day.”  

“Wisdom is key, no matter the price,  
She’ll lift you up, make your life nice."  

“She’ll crown your head with splendor and grace,  
Embrace her; you’ll find honor in her embrace."  

Hear me, my son, take in these sayings,  
Long life’s the reward, with wisdom’s displaying.  

I’ve shown you the straight paths to tread,  
In your walk, you'll never be misled.  

Run with freedom, stumble you won’t,  
Hold instruction tight, let her go, you don’t.  

Shun the path of wicked, don’t even glance,  
Turn away quick, don’t give evil a chance.  

They don’t sleep 'til they’ve done some wrong,  
Their bread's wickedness, violence's their song.  

But the righteous path, it’s like dawn's light,  
It shines brighter ’til day becomes bright.  

The wicked’s way is shadowed in dark,  
They stumble blind, never hitting the mark.  

“My son, my words, keep them close,  
In your heart, let them be the most."  

“They’re life and health to the body and soul,  
Guard your heart; it makes you whole."  

“Avoid perverse talk, let your lips be true,  
Keep focused, let your gaze cut through."  

“Make your path level, keep it straight,  
Don’t turn aside, steer clear from hate."  

"Remove your foot from evil's track,  
Stay on course, never look back.”
