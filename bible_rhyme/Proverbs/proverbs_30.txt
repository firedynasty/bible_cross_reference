Yo, Agur speaks, his words unfold,  
To Ithiel and Ucal, stories bold.  
He claims no wisdom, says he's blind,  
Never grasped the Holy kind.  

"Who ascends to heaven, then comes down?  
Who grips the wind, makes the waters drown?  
Who laid the earth from end to end?  
Tell me his name, the name he'll send."  

"Every word of God, pure as gold,  
A shield to those in His hold.  
Don't twist His words, don't add lies,  
Or He’ll call you out, no disguise."  

"Two things I seek, don't pass me by,  
Before my last breath, before I die.  
Keep lies and falsehoods far away,  
Give neither wealth nor empty tray.  

Lest I be full, forget His name,  
Or steal in shame, and bring Him blame."  
"Don’t slander a servant, beware the curse,  
Or guilt will trap you, things get worse.  

A generation that curses sires,  
Eyes so lofty, stoking fires.  
Pure they think, yet drenched in sin,  
Swords for teeth, they grin and win."  

"The leech cries 'Give,' never enough,  
Four things crave, always tough:  
Sheol, a womb bare and dry,  
Thirsty earth, fire reaching high."  

"Mock your dad, disobey your mom,  
Ravens feast, young eagles come."  
"Three amaze, four perplex my mind:  
Eagle, serpent, ship, man with a kind.  

An adulterous woman, sneaky game,  
Eats, wipes her mouth, feels no shame."  
"Three things make earth shake with fear,  
Four it can't bear, let me make it clear:  

Servant as king, a full fool's might,  
An unloved wife's wedding night.  
Four small, yet wisdom they claim:  
Ants store food, hydrax seek rock’s fame.  

Locusts, no king, yet advance strong,  
Lizards in palaces, where they belong."  
"Three march proud, four walk with grace,  
Lion's roar, greyhound's pace.  

Male goat's stride, king's command,  
None dare rise, none dare stand.  
Lift your pride, think evil and loud,  
Cover your mouth, don’t be proud.  

As milk churns butter, nose yields blood,  
Wrath forced too far, brings a flood."
