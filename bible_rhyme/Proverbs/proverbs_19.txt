Better to be poor, walk straight and true,  
Than be a fool with lies, twisting the view.  

Zeal with no knowledge? A dangerous game,  
Rushing with haste can lead to shame.  

Foolish hearts rage against the Lord,  
They twist their paths, can't find the accord.  

Wealthy folks got friends galore,  
But when you're broke, they slam the door.  

False witness won't escape their fate,  
Lies won't let them pass through the gate.  

People beg favors from the high and mighty,  
Gift-givers have friends, they stay tight, rightly.  

Poor man's kin turn their backs and run,  
He chases them down, but they’re done.  

Wisdom loves the soul it finds,  
Understanding brings good, rewinds.  

False witness, watch out, you're through,  
Lies will perish, that's true for you.  

Fools in high places, fools in the court,  
Servants ruling princes? Not the sort.  

A wise man's slow to anger, sees the light,  
His glory's in forgiveness, setting things right.  

King's wrath roars like a lion on the prowl,  
His favor's like morning dew, soft and how.  

A foolish son brings father's pain,  
A wife's nagging is like endless rain.  

House and wealth from dad's hand,  
But a wise wife is from Yahweh's land.  

Laziness leads to a deep, cold sleep,  
Idlers find hunger, no food to keep.  

Keep the command, protect your soul,  
Contempt leads to death, swallows you whole.  

Pity the poor, lend to the Lord,  
He'll pay you back, it’s in His word.  

Discipline your child, there's hope to find,  
Don't let folly lead him blind.  

Hot tempers pay their price,  
Rescue once, you might pay twice.  

Listen and learn, grow wise at the end,  
Yahweh's counsel will always transcend.  

Kindness is key, desired and true,  
Better poor than a liar in view.  

Fear the Lord, find life and rest,  
Contentment follows, you're blessed.  

Sluggard can't lift his hand to eat,  
Can't even bring his food to his seat.  

Flog the scoffer, teach the simple clear,  
Rebuke the wise, knowledge appears.  

Steal from parents, bring them shame,  
Such a son earns a bad name.  

Stop listening, you'll stray off course,  
Ignoring wisdom, you'll face remorse.  

Corrupt witness mocks justice, grins wide,  
The wicked swallow sin, let it slide.  

Penalties for scoffers, pain for fools,  
These are life's unchanging rules.
