A wise son tunes in, hears his dad's advice,  
The scoffer just laughs, won't listen twice.  

By sweet words, a man enjoys what's right,  
But the unfaithful seek the violent fight.  

Guard your mouth, keep your soul intact,  
Open it wide, and watch the ruin act.  

The lazy soul craves but gets no share,  
The diligent worker finds reward there.  

The righteous loathe lies, it's truth they chase,  
While the wicked bring only shame and disgrace.  

Integrity's path, righteousness defends,  
Wicked ways lead to a sinner's end.  

Some act rich, but they're just pretending,  
Some act poor, but wealth they're defending.  

A man's life's price, in riches it's found,  
The poor hear no threats, they're not bound.  

The light of the righteous shines so bright,  
While the wicked's lamp fades out of sight.  

Pride starts fights, it breeds the brawl,  
But wisdom lives in those who heed the call.  

Dishonest wealth will fade away slow,  
But hand-gathered riches will surely grow.  

Hope on hold makes the heart feel sick,  
Fulfilled desires, life's tree, a magic trick.  

Despise the rules, you'll pay the fare,  
Respect the command, and rewards are there.  

Wise teaching flows like life's fresh spring,  
Turning from death, life's wisdom bring.  

Good understanding wins favor strong,  
Unfaithful ways make the road long.  

Prudent folks act from knowledge's moat,  
While fools expose their folly, gloat.  

A wicked messenger's bound for strife,  
But the faithful envoys bring healing life.  

Poverty and shame for the undisciplined man,  
Heed correction, and honor's the plan.  

Longing fulfilled is sweet as a goal,  
But fools hate turning, clinging to their role.  

Walk with the wise, and wisdom you'll grow,  
With fools, harm follows everywhere you go.  

Misfortune chases sinners, can't let go,  
But prosperity's gift to the righteous flow.  

A good man leaves gifts for children's kin,  
But a sinner’s wealth to the righteous ends.  

Fields of the poor hold food in store,  
Injustice sweeps it, leaves no more.  

Spare the rod, and your child's needs you miss,  
True love disciplines, leads to bliss.  

The righteous eat, their soul's content,  
But the wicked's belly stays hungry, spent.
