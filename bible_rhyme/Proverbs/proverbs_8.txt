Wisdom's crying out, she's raising her voice loud,  
On high places, crossroads, she's drawing a crowd.  
At the city gates, by the entry doors she stands,  
Shouting to the people, reaching out her hands.  

"I call to you, men! My voice to mankind,  
Simple ones, get wise! Fools, open your mind!  
Hear me speak excellent things, truth from my lips,  
Wickedness I shun, no deception in my scripts.  

Every word I speak, in righteousness it flows,  
Plain to the understanding, clear to those who know.  
Take my instruction over silver, my knowledge over gold,  
Wisdom beats rubies, can't be bought or sold.  

I, wisdom, dwell with prudence, find knowledge and taste,  
Hate evil and pride, leave arrogance and waste.  
Counsel and sound knowledge belong to me,  
Kings reign by my power, ruling righteously.  

I love those who love me, seek and you'll find,  
Riches and honor, prosperity of the kind.  
My fruit's better than gold, my yield's choice and rare,  
I walk the path of justice, distributing fair share.  

Yahweh owned me in the beginning, before the earth's dawn,  
From everlasting, before the world was on.  
When depths were empty, I was there, when springs weren't born,  
Before mountains and hills, before fields of corn.  

When he set the heavens' circle, I stood beside,  
Clouds formed above, deep waters abide.  
Sea's boundary he gave, earth's foundations laid,  
I was there, his craftsman, in delight I stayed.  

Rejoicing always in his world, sons of men my joy,  
Now listen, my sons, blessed are those who deploy,  
Hear instruction, be wise, don't turn away,  
Blessed is the one who hears, by my gates they'll stay.  

Find me and find life, Yahweh's favor they’ll see,  
But sin against me, harms the soul, not free.  
Those who hate me love death, choosing the wrong course,  
Wisdom calls you still, listen to the source."
