Don't envy evil men, don't desire their way,  
Their hearts plot violence, with mischief they play.  
Through wisdom a house is built, strong and tall,  
Understanding establishes, knowledge fills it all.  

A wise man holds power, with strength he grows,  
With guidance he wins, as his victory shows.  
Wisdom's too high for a fool, can't open at the gate,  
Plotting evil brands you schemer, sealed fate.  

Schemes of folly are sin, mockers men detest,  
If you falter in trouble, your strength's not the best.  
Rescue those led to death, hold back their fall,  
Say "We didn't know," but He weighs hearts all.  

"My son, eat honey, it's sweet to your taste,  
Wisdom's sweet for your soul, no reward goes to waste."  
Don't plot against the righteous, leave his place,  
He falls seven times, stands up, saves face.  

Don't rejoice at your enemy's fall,  
Yahweh sees, He'll turn His wrath from it all.  
Don't fret for evildoers, don't envy the bleak,  
The wicked's lamp snuffed out, future's weak.  

"My son, fear Yahweh and the king,  
Don't join rebels, who knows what calamity they'll bring?"  
To show partial judgment isn't good,  
Call the wicked righteous, nations will curse, understood.  

An honest answer's a kiss on the lips, so true,  
Prepare your field, build your house anew.  
Don't bear false witness, don't deceive with your word,  
Don't repay evil, haven't you heard?  

Passed by the sluggard's field, the vineyard laid bare,  
Thorns and nettles covered, stone wall in disrepair.  
I saw and learned, a lesson to keep,  
A little slumber, poverty like a thief will creep.
