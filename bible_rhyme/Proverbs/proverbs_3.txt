Yo, my son, don't forget what I teach, hold it tight,  
Keep my commands in your heart, day and night.

They'll add to your life, days and years of peace,  
Kindness and truth, let 'em never cease.

Wrap 'em 'round your neck, carve 'em deep in your heart,  
With favor and understanding, you'll play a smart part.

"Trust in Yahweh with all your soul,  
Don't lean on your thoughts, let Him make you whole."

In every step, acknowledge His might,  
He'll straighten your path, make the crooked right.

"But don't think you're wise in your own eyes,  
Fear Yahweh, depart from lies."

It'll be health to your body, bones made strong,  
Honor Yahweh with your wealth, and you can't go wrong.

Fill your barns with plenty, vats overflow with wine,  
Don't despise His discipline, correction is divine.

For Yahweh corrects the ones He loves,  
Just like a father's gentle shoves.

Happy is the one who finds wisdom’s prize,  
Her worth's beyond silver, gold, or skies.

More precious than rubies, she stands tall,  
Her riches and honor, in her hands, call.

Her paths are peace, pleasant every way,  
She's a tree of life, hold tight, don’t sway.

By wisdom, Yahweh formed the earth we see,  
Understanding holds the heavens, wild and free.

"Let wisdom and discretion never leave your sight,  
They'll be life to your soul, grace in your light."

Walk secure, never stumble on your track,  
When you lie down, sweet sleep has your back.

Don't fear sudden dread, nor wicked’s fall,  
Yahweh's your confidence, He hears your call.

"Don’t withhold good when it's in your hand,  
To neighbors, be kind, understand."

Don't scheme against those who trust you near,  
Don't fight without reason, keep your conscience clear.

Don't envy violence or choose its path,  
Yahweh's with the righteous, but the wicked face wrath.

He mocks the mockers, gives grace to the meek,  
The wise gain glory, fools face defeat.
