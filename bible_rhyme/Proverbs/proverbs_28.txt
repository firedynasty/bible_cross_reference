The wicked run off with no one in chase,  
But the righteous stand bold, lions in their place.  

Rebels crown rulers, chaos their fate,  
Yet knowledge and wisdom keep order straight.  

The poor man oppressing, like rain that won't stop,  
Leaves the fields barren, no food in the crop.  

Abandon the law, and wicked you praise,  
Keep it strong, and justice you raise.  

Evil's blind to justice, lost in the night,  
Seek Yahweh, and you'll find the light.  

Integrity trumps riches, better to be poor,  
Than twisted in wealth, lost at the core.  

Wise sons follow law, not gluttons' delight,  
Bringing shame to fathers, it's not right.  

Wealth that grows from greed, what a trap,  
Goes to those who help, bridging the gap.  

Close your ears to the law, your prayers go unheard,  
Injustice leads astray, with every word.  

The blameless walk straight, they inherit the good,  
While traps catch the wicked, misunderstood.  

The rich think they're wise, but can't fool the poor,  
For understanding sees through, wisdom galore.  

When righteous win, it's glory aflame,  
In wicked rise, men hide in shame.  

Conceal your sins, no prosper you'll find,  
Confess and renounce, mercy's not blind.  

Blessed is the one who walks with fear,  
Harden your heart, and trouble draws near.  

A wicked ruler's a roaring lion,  
People are helpless, in fear they're cryin'.  

Tyrants lack judgment, but those against ill-gain,  
Their days will be long, without any pain.  

A fugitive of blood, tormented till death,  
No support to find, till their last breath.  

Walk blameless, and safety you'll embrace,  
But perverse ways lead to a fall from grace.  

Work your land, food will abound,  
Chasing fantasies, poverty's ground.  

Faithful and blessed, no riches to chase,  
Hunger for wealth, leads to disgrace.  

Partiality's wrong, for bread men will fall,  
Stingy and hasty, poverty will call.  

Rebuke with truth, find favor and grace,  
Flattery falls flat, leaves no trace.  

Robbing parents, claiming no wrong,  
Partners with destruction, they belong.  

Greedy hearts stir strife, while trust brings peace,  
Yahweh prospers those who find release.  

Trust in yourself, end up the fool,  
Walk in wisdom, and be kept cool.  

Give to the poor, and lack you'll not know,  
Close your eyes, curses will show.  

Wicked rise, men hide away,  
When they fall, the righteous sway.
