Than love that's hidden, nowhere near.

While enemy kisses might mean something.

But to the hungry, bitter’s never too much.

Wandering, wondering, no place to roam.

Like a friend’s counsel, a treasure found.

Stay close, not just through bloodlines.

So I can face my foes, nothing to hide.

The simple walk on, only to meet defeat.

Hold tight when he's tangled with a wayward lover.

He’ll take it the wrong way, like a curse drawn.

A friend’s countenance, in that space.

Serve the master, honor will flow.
