Solomon speaks, son of David's line,  
Wisdom and knowledge, both divine.  

Learn the ways of justice and right,  
For the young and simple, bring insight.  

Wise folks listen, grow their brain,  
Understanding deepens, like the rain.  

"The fear of Yahweh starts it all,"  
While fools ignore wisdom's call.  

"My son, heed your father's creed,  
Mom's teachings, let them lead.  

A crown of grace upon your head,  
Chains of honor where you're led.  

If sinners say, 'Come join our ride,'  
Stand firm, don’t step to their side.  

If they plot to spill blood, lie in wait,  
Avoid their path, don't take the bait.  

Their feet rush to evil, a wicked race,  
But it’s their own lives they disgrace.  

Greed for gain, it snatches life,  
Cuts like a knife, brings strife."  

Wisdom shouts in the street's loud hum,  
In the city gates, she says, "Come!  

How long, simple ones, will you love the plain?  
Turn to me, break free from the chain.  

I've called you out, yet none would heed,  
So laugh I will, when you’re in need.  

When disaster hits, like a stormy blast,  
You'll seek me then, but the chance has passed.  

Hate knowledge, ignore the divine,  
Eat the fruits of your own design.  

Fools' ease leads to their demise,  
But those who listen, they’re the wise.  

They'll find safety, peace to claim,  
Fear of harm, just a forgotten name."
