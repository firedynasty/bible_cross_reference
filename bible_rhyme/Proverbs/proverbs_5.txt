Listen up, son, hear my wisdom call,  
Tune your ears, don't let knowledge fall.  

An adulteress's lips drip honey so sweet,  
But her end is bitter, sharp as a sword's deceit.  

Her steps lead down to the grave,  
Crooked paths she won't save.  

So heed my words, don't stray away,  
Keep far from her, don’t be led astray.  

Or you'll lose honor to those who deceive,  
Years lost to cruelty, wealth they thieve.  

You'll groan at the end, body worn and torn,  
"Why did I hate wisdom, why did I scorn?"  

"I ignored my teachers, their words I defied,  
Now here I stand, on ruin's side."  

Drink from your own well, let it flow,  
Keep it yours, let no others know.  

Bless your spring, joy in your youth's bride,  
Her love's a treasure, with her abide.  

Why chase the allure of another's snare?  
Yahweh sees all, beware of despair.  

Wicked deeds ensnare, sins tightly bind,  
Without instruction, he's lost in his mind.  

In folly's shadow, astray he'll stay,  
Lost and wandering, far from the way.
