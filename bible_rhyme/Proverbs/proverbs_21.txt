The king's heart's in Yahweh's hands, like rivers' flow,  
He turns it any way He wants, His power to show.  

Every man thinks he's always right, no surprise,  
But Yahweh’s the one who weighs what's inside.  

Righteousness and justice, God favors them more,  
Than any sacrifice you bring through the door.  

The proud and the haughty, their lamp's full of sin,  
The plans of the steady, that's where profits begin.  

But rush if you want, poverty's what you'll find,  
Lies for treasure? Just a vapor, leaving death behind.  

The wicked's violence pushes them away,  
'Cause they won't do what's right, they stray.  

Guilty paths are twisted, but the upright stays true,  
Better a rooftop corner than conflict in view.  

The wicked soul craves evil, no mercy to give,  
Mockers punished teach the simple, the wise receive.  

"Righteous One sees the wicked, and brings their end,"  
Stop your ears to the poor, and no one will defend.  

A secret gift calms anger, a cloak hides the bribe,  
Justice brings joy, but ruins iniquity's tribe.  

Stray from understanding, you'll rest with the dead,  
Love pleasure, find poverty, that's where it led.  

The wicked ransom the righteous, the treacherous fall,  
Better a desert than strife in the hall.  

The wise have treasures, oil — fools swallow it up,  
Follow righteousness, find life, drink from its cup.  

The wise man scales the city, breaks down its might,  
Guard your words and your tongue, keep your soul right.  

Proud scoffers work in arrogance, pride is their game,  
The lazy covet, but refuse the labor flame.  

The righteous give, they never hold back a bit,  
A wicked sacrifice, with a mind unfit.  

False witness perishes, truth speaks to eternity,  
The wicked man hardens, the upright sets his path free.  

No wisdom, understanding, counsel against Yahweh stands,  
Prepare the horse for battle, but victory's in His hands.
