A man's alone, chasing dreams, all about himself,  
Ignores wise words, leaves good sense on the shelf.  

Fools, they talk, but they don't seek to know,  
Their own opinions, that's all they show.  

Wicked comes, with contempt by its side,  
Shame and disgrace, nowhere to hide.  

Words run deep, like waters that flow,  
Wisdom's a brook, clear and slow.  

Playing favorites with the wicked ain't right,  
Justice for the innocent, must be in sight.  

Fool's lips stir trouble, invite a fight,  
His mouth's a trap, a snare in the night.  

Gossip tastes sweet, like morsels you savor,  
But it digs deep, it's no kind of favor.  

Slacking on your work, destruction's kin,  
The Lord's name's a tower, that's where you win.  

Rich man's money, his wall so tall,  
But pride comes 'fore a great fall.  

Answer quick, before you hear,  
That's shame and folly, let it be clear.  

Spirit strong can take the hit,  
But a crushed soul can't bear a bit.  

Wise hearts seek knowledge, ears tuned to learn,  
A gift opens doors, respect you earn.  

First plea sounds right, till questions fly,  
The lot settles disputes, keeps the strong ones shy.  

An offended brother's a fortress tight,  
Words fill the belly, take appetite.  

Tongue's got power, life and death it speaks,  
Truth and lies, it moves and it leaks.  

Find a good wife, favor from the Lord,  
Poor cry for mercy, rich give a harsh word.  

Many friends may lead to ruin's brink,  
But a true friend holds tighter than you think.
