Wine’s a joker, beer’s a fight, leads astray, not bright,  
King’s roar like a lion, cross him, lose the light.  

It’s honor to stay away from strife,  
Yet fools keep quarrels in their life.  

Winter’s cold, sluggard won’t plow,  
Come harvest time, nothing to chow.  

Deep waters hold counsel inside,  
With understanding, draw it wide.  

Men claim love, but who stands true?  
Righteous walk in integrity, blessings accrue.  

King on the throne with judging eyes,  
Evil scatters, truth never lies.  

“Pure heart, clean hands?” Who can say?  
False weights are wrong in Yahweh’s way.  

Child’s deeds show what’s right or not,  
Hearing and seeing, God’s hand wrought.  

Love sleep? Poverty’s your fate,  
Open eyes, bread on your plate.  

“No good,” cries buyer, then boasts with pride,  
Gold and rubies can’t match knowledge inside.  

Hold a garment for a stranger’s bet,  
Sweet fraud turns to gravel regret.  

Plans by advice, wage war wise,  
Tale-bearers spread secrets, cut those ties.  

Curse your folks, darkness falls,  
Quick riches, long-term stalls.  

“Pay back evil?” Wait on Yahweh’s call,  
Dishonest scales, detested by all.  

Steps are from God, path unknown,  
Rash vows trap, wisdom’s shown.  

Wise king cleanses wicked’s game,  
Yahweh’s lamp searches the inner flame.  

Love and truth keep the king’s throne strong,  
Youth’s strength, old men’s gray hair belong.  

Wounds cleanse evil, beatings refine,  
Purging hearts in Yahweh’s design.
