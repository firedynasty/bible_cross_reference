When you’re rebuked and stay stiff, watch out, my friend,  
Destruction comes quick with no remedy to mend.  
Righteous folks bring joy, people cheer, they thrive,  
But with wicked rulers, folks barely survive.  

Love wisdom, make your father beam with pride,  
But waste it all, and you’ll be left high and dry.  
A king with justice keeps the land secure,  
But bribes tear down the house, that’s for sure.  

Flattering words lay traps, keep your wits keen,  
The evil get caught, while the righteous stay serene.  
The righteous seek justice, care for the poor,  
But the wicked stay blind, needing more, more, more.  

Mockers cause trouble, but the wise make things right,  
In court, fools rage, lacking peace, no insight.  
Bloodthirsty folks hate the upright with might,  
While fools vent their anger, wise men hold it tight.  

When rulers heed lies, wickedness spreads far,  
Both rich and poor see life through His bar.  
A fair king for the poor, his throne stands tall,  
Correct with the rod, avoid the shameful call.  

Wickedness rises, but sin meets its end,  
Correct your son; peace and joy he’ll send.  
No revelation, chaos breaks loose,  
But laws bring blessings, a strong truce.  

Words won't teach a servant who won't heed,  
Hasty words outpace fools, planting a bad seed.  
Raise a servant well, watch him shine,  
Angry folks stir strife, in sin they entwine.  

Pride drags you down, humility brings grace,  
Befriend a thief, your soul's in a bad place.  
Fear's a trap, trust in Yahweh, be safe,  
Justice comes from Him, not man's embrace.  

The wicked despise the upright's way,  
The righteous detest the wicked each day.
