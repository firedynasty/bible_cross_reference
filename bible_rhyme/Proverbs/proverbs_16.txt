Plans in the heart, a man's own song,  
But Yahweh's tongue sets right from wrong.  

Man thinks he's clean, sees with his own eyes,  
But Yahweh weighs motives, no disguise.  

"Commit your deeds to me," Yahweh decreed,  
And every plan of yours shall succeed.  

He made it all with purpose, He won't miss,  
Even the wicked for their day of abyss.  

Prideful hearts, Yahweh won't abide,  
Unpunished they won't, in His eyes they'll hide.  

Mercy and truth can clear the slate,  
Fear Yahweh, and from evil separate.  

When man's ways please Yahweh, peace is found,  
Enemies fall silent, no war sound.  

Better a little with right in your stride,  
Than unjust wealth with greed as your guide.  

A man's heart plans his course, it's true,  
But Yahweh directs each step you pursue.  

Words of the king, wise and fair,  
Betrayal not found, truth in the air.  

Honest scales, that's Yahweh's game,  
All weights in the bag, by His name.  

Kings doing wrong, that won't stand,  
Their throne built on righteousness in the land.  

Righteous lips, kings favor and prize,  
Truth-speakers they hold with high eyes.  

The king's wrath, a deadly affair,  
But wisdom calms with tender care.  

Life in the light of the king's face,  
His favor, like spring rain with grace.  

Wisdom over gold, understanding's worth,  
Better than silver, the richest on earth.  

Upright paths leave evil behind,  
Keep your way, and peace you'll find.  

Pride leads to fall, destruction's call,  
Better lowly with the poor than proud with it all.  

Heeding the Word brings prosperity,  
Trust in Yahweh, blessed you'll be.  

Wise hearts lead with prudent grace,  
Pleasant lips, instruction's place.  

Understanding, life's fountain, it flows,  
Fools punished by their folly's woes.  

Wise hearts instruct their speech,  
Learning to lips, wisdom they teach.  

Pleasant words, honeycomb sweet,  
Health to the bones, a life complete.  

Paths seem right, how they deceive,  
Lead to death, don't be naive.  

Laboring man's hunger drives him on,  
His mouth's thirst, where his strength is drawn.  

Worthless men spark mischief's fire,  
Their words scorch, full of desire.  

Perversity breeds strife and divides,  
Whispering lies, friendship it hides.  

Violent men lead neighbors astray,  
Evil plots, lead them away.  

Winking eyes plot with deceit,  
Compressed lips march evil's beat.  

Gray hair, glory's crown, rightly earned,  
A life in righteousness, wisdom discerned.  

Slow to anger, mightier than might,  
Rules his spirit, wins the fight.  

The lot is cast, fate in its role,  
Yet Yahweh decides, He holds control.
