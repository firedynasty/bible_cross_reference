Paul, Silvanus, Tim, to the Thessalonian crew,  
Grace and peace from God, it's true.  

We're thankful, your faith grows fast,  
Love's abounding, it’s sure to last.  

We boast to the world, your faith ain't shakin',  
Through trials and pain, you ain't breakin'.  

A sign of God's judgment, righteous and clear,  
Worthy of His Kingdom, even through fear.  

God will repay those who cause you pain,  
Relief is coming, like fresh, cool rain.  

When Jesus returns, heaven will blaze,  
With angels in fire, in a mighty display.  

Those who don’t know Him, they'll face the cost,  
From His presence, eternally lost.  

On that day, He'll be glorified bright,  
In His saints, admired, shining in light.  

We pray you stay true, worthy of the call,  
Work of faith and power, through it all.  

That His name in you is glorified high,  
Through grace from God and Christ, no lie.
