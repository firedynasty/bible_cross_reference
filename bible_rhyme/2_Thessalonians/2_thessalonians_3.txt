Brothers, sisters, pray for us, let His word fly,  
Spread fast, get honored, reaching far and high.  

Deliver us from men who misbehave,  
In faithless acts, but God is the one who’ll save.  

The Lord is faithful, He holds you tight,  
Guards you from evil, through day and night.  

We trust in Him, that you’ll do as we say,  
Walking His path, growing in love every day.  

“Step back,” we urge, from rebels who stray,  
Follow traditions, not what they display.  

You’ve seen how we lived, working with might,  
Paid for our bread, to keep your burdens light.  

Not for lack of rights, but to show how to lead,  
Living examples, so you can succeed.  

“If they don’t work, they don’t eat,” we declared,  
Some still wander, with work unprepared.  

To those we say, “Quietly do your part,  
Eat your own bread, with a willing heart.”  

Don’t get tired doing good, stay on the track,  
If one strays from this letter, step back.  

Don’t treat him as a foe, but guide him right,  
As a brother, help him see the light.  

May peace from the Lord flow in every way,  
His presence with you, each and every day.  

Paul’s greeting, my hand signs the end,  
May His grace be with you, His blessings extend.  

Amen.
