Brothers, listen close, hear the call,  
About Jesus' coming, the gathering for all.  
Don't let your minds be shaken, don't be swayed,  
No spirit, no word, no letters to dismay.  

Let no one deceive you, don't fall for the spin,  
The day won't come without rebellion and sin.  
The man of sin, the son of destruction revealed,  
He raises himself, his dark fate sealed.  

He opposes God, exalts his own name,  
Sits in the temple, playing a dangerous game.  
"Remember," I said, these words I shared,  
When I was with you, how I truly cared.  

You know what's restraining, holding him back,  
Till the time comes, and he steps off the track.  
Lawlessness lurks, a mystery, silent,  
Held by one till it's no longer quiet.  

Then the lawless one will emerge, unmasked,  
The Lord will slay him, his doom won't last.  
With breath of His mouth, a moment divine,  
Darkness defeated; the light will shine.  

This one comes with Satan's deceit,  
Signs and wonders, the wicked elite.  
Those lost to the lie, they turned from the light,  
Refused to love truth, embracing the night.  

God sends an error, they believe the lie,  
Judgment falls on all who truth defy.  
But we thank God, for you, brothers, in His sight,  
Chosen for salvation, through Spirit's might.  

Called through the Good News, glory awaits,  
Stand firm, hold traditions, don't hesitate.  
Now Jesus Christ, and God, our Father above,  
Comfort your hearts, strengthen with love.  

In every good work, in every word you say,  
May His grace be with you, each and every day.
