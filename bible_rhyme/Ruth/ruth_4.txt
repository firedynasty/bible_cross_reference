Boaz at the gate, sat down to wait,  
The kinsman passed by, Boaz called, "Don't hesitate!"  

"Come here, sit down," Boaz did greet,  
Ten elders joined them, took their seat.  

"Naomi's land's up for sale, you see,  
Elimelech's plot, will you redeem?" said he.  

"If you redeem, go on and take the land,  
But if not, let me know, understand?"  

The kinsman agreed, "I'll redeem it, sure,"  
But Boaz dropped a detail, couldn't ignore.  

"When you buy that field, Ruth comes too,  
To honor the dead, that's what you'll do."  

The kinsman stepped back, "Can't mess with my own,  
Take my right, Boaz, the land's your zone."  

In Israel, they swapped shoes for the deal,  
The kinsman passed it, Boaz sealed it with zeal.  

"You all witnessed today," Boaz declared with pride,  
"I've bought all Elimelech's, and Ruth as my bride."  

The elders and people said, "We witness this,  
May she be like Leah, Rachel, bring bliss."  

Boaz took Ruth, she became his wife,  
Yahweh blessed her, with new life.  

Naomi cradled the boy, her heart's delight,  
"Blessed be Yahweh, who makes things right."  

Named Obed, he'd carry the family flame,  
Father of Jesse, David's claim to fame.  

From Perez to Hezron, Ram to Amminadab,  
Nahshon to Salmon, lineage they had.  

Salmon to Boaz, who fathered Obed,  
Obed to Jesse, then King David was bred.
