Naomi had a kin, a wealthy man indeed,  
From Elimelech's line, Boaz was his seed.  

Ruth the Moabitess, with courage anew,  
Said, "Let me glean in the fields, find favor too."  
Naomi nodded, "Go ahead, my dear,"  
Ruth ventured forth, her goal was clear.  

She landed in Boaz's field, no plan,  
A twist of fate led her to that man.  
Boaz arrived from Bethlehem's land,  
"May Yahweh bless you," he commanded the band.  

"Who's this young lady?" Boaz did inquire,  
His servant replied, "The Moabite with Naomi, she came to inspire.  
She asked to glean, from dawn 'til now,  
Just a short rest, she took a bow."  

Boaz spoke to Ruth, "Listen, my daughter true,  
Stick with my maidens, don’t leave this crew.  
Watch the field, follow them near,  
I told the men, no harm comes here."  

"When you're thirsty, drink from our well,  
Safety and kindness, with us you'll dwell."  
Ruth bowed low, "Why such favor to me?  
A foreigner here, yet kindness I see."  

Boaz replied, "I've heard of your deed,  
For Naomi's sake, how you've left your creed.  
May Yahweh repay, His wings your shield,  
Israel's protection, in His field."  

Ruth thanked him, eyes shining bright,  
Boaz invited her to dine that night.  
"Come, eat bread, dip morsel in the sauce,  
Join my reapers, no need for loss."  

She ate her fill, saved some for later,  
Boaz ensured her gleaning was greater.  
"Let her glean among sheaves, leave her some grain,  
Do not rebuke her, let her sustain."  

Ruth worked 'til dusk, her hands did strive,  
An ephah of barley, she managed to derive.  
Back to the city, her bounty in hand,  
Naomi saw, her joy was grand.  

"Where did you glean? Who showed such grace?"  
"With Boaz," Ruth said, "In his field, I found my place."  
Naomi blessed him, "For his kindness shown,  
He's kin to us, we're not alone."  

Ruth recalled his words, "Stay near my crew,  
'Til harvest ends, his protection's true."  
Naomi agreed, "Stay close, my daughter dear,  
In Boaz's field, you'll find no fear."  

So Ruth stayed close, through barley and wheat,  
With Naomi, her days were replete.
