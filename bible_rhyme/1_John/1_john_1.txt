From the beginning, we've seen and heard,  
Touched the Word, life was stirred.  

Life revealed, eternal and bright,  
With the Father, brought to light.  

What we've seen, we're telling you,  
Join our fellowship, be true.  

Father and Son, our fellowship found,  
In these words, joy's unbound.  

"This message we share: God is light,"  
No darkness in Him, pure and right.  

Say you walk with Him, but hide in the night?  
You're fooling yourself, not living right.  

Walk in the light, like He’s in the glow,  
Together, in truth, our bonds grow.  

His Son's blood cleanses deep,  
In the light, no secrets we keep.  

Claim no sin, you're tricking your mind,  
Truth missing, you're walking blind.  

Confess your wrongs, He's faithful and just,  
Forgives and cleanses, in His grace, trust.  

Deny your wrongs, call Him a liar,  
His Word's not in you, truth's not higher.
