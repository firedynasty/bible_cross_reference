Believe in Christ, born of God, you're in the fold,  
Love the Father, love His child, watch love unfold.  

Keep His commands, that's how love's defined,  
And these commands, not burdensome, but aligned.  

Born of God, you’re beating the world's strong tide,  
Faith is your victory, let it be your guide.  

Who overcomes, but the one who believes,  
Jesus is the Son, He’s the one who achieves.  

He came by water and blood, not water alone,  
Spirit testifies, truth in every tone.  

Three give witness: Spirit, water, and blood,  
Together they agree, like a rushing flood.  

Man's witness is fine, but God's is greater still,  
"Concerning My Son," His words fulfill.  

He who believes has the testimony inside,  
Doubt makes God a liar, faith's where truth will reside.  

God gave eternal life, in His Son it lies,  
With the Son, you have life, without Him, it dies.  

"I write this to you, who believe in His name,  
That you may know life eternal's your aim."  

Boldly we ask, when aligned with His will,  
He listens, petitions granted, calm and still.  

See a brother sin, not leading to death's door,  
Ask, and God gives life, balance restored.  

Unrighteousness is sin, yet some sins don't end,  
We keep ourselves, the evil can't bend.  

Of God we're made, though the world is dark,  
The Son came, gave us light, and ignited the spark.  

In Him who is true, we take our stand,  
Jesus Christ, our life, by His command.  

Children, keep from idols, don't stray off the path,  
Stay true to God, avoid the wrath.
