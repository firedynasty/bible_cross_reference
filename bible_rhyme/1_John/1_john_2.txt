Listen up, my children, I'm writing so you don't go astray,  
But if you slip, we've got Jesus, our guide every day.  

He’s the sacrifice for sins, not just for you and me,  
But for the whole wide world, setting everyone free.  

If you say you know him, then keep his commands,  
If you don’t, you’re lying, truth slipping through your hands.  

God’s love shines bright in those who obey,  
Walk like he walked, that’s the faithful way.  

No new commandment, it’s the same old song,  
But with new light shining, darkness won't last long.  

Claim you’re in the light, but hate your brother?  
You’re stumbling in darkness, lost like no other.  

Love your brother, stay in the light,  
No room for stumbling, you’re walking right.  

I'm writing to you, ‘cause your sins are forgiven,  
Fathers, young men, little ones, keep on living.  

Don't cling to the world, its desires will fall,  
Do God’s will, and stand tall through it all.  

These are the last days, the Antichrist is near,  
Many have come; it’s the final hour, clear.  

They left, showing they didn’t belong,  
But you’ve got the truth, stay strong.  

Who denies Christ is the Antichrist, plain,  
Denying the Father, brings no gain.  

Confess the Son, have the Father too,  
Stay in what you heard, it's forever true.  

This promise we hold, eternal life ahead,  
Stay anointed, keep your spirit fed.  

Remain in him, so when he appears,  
We stand bold, no shame, no fears.  

He’s righteous, we know it’s true —  
Those who live right are born anew.
