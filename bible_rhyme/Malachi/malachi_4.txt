A day is coming, hot as a furnace blaze,  
Proud hearts and wicked ways, they'll go up in flames.  
"Not a root or a branch will remain," Yahweh declares,  
Fire will consume them, leaving only air.  

But those who fear my name, rise with healing light,  
"Leaping like the calves, you'll dance out of night."  
You'll tread down the wicked, ashes under your feet,  
On the day I act, feel the heat of my beat.  

"Remember Moses' law, given in Horeb's sight,  
Statutes and ordinances, keep them in your life."  
Elijah's coming, before that great day,  
Turning hearts around, in a brand new way.  

Fathers to children, children to their dads,  
Or else I'll strike the earth—no time for feeling sad.  
Yahweh of Armies speaks, His word prevails,  
From the furnace to the healing, His truth never fails.
