Listen up, priests, this command is laid for you,  
If you won’t listen, hear my truth, it’s due.  

"Glorify my name," Yahweh speaks forth,  
Or curses will rain, blessings lost, henceforth.  

Your lineage rebuked, dung on your face,  
From feasts you uphold, in disgrace.  

"You’ll know I sent this word anew,  
My covenant with Levi, it stands true."  

"Life and peace I gave, that reverence be found,  
He honored my name, with awe profound.  

Truth on his lips, no wrong in his stride,  
Walked in peace, led many to my side."  

A priest’s lips hold knowledge, they seek my way,  
Messenger of Yahweh, in truth they stay.  

But you've strayed, caused many to fall,  
Corrupted Levi’s vow, hear my call.  

Contemptible, wicked, the people see,  
For you’ve twisted my law, shown partiality.  

One Father we have, one God made us all,  
Why betray each other, make the covenant fall?  

Judah's betrayal, an unholy affair,  
With foreign gods, they lay bare.  

Yahweh cuts off those who stray,  
From Jacob’s tents, they’ll fade away.  

You cover altars with tears and sighs,  
But no more is your offering prized.  

"Why?" you ask, Yahweh bears witness true,  
Against your youth’s wife, you've been untrue.  

One he made, with Spirit’s breath,  
For godly offspring, not treacherous death.  

"Hate and divorce," Yahweh shouts loud,  
"Violence you wear, like a shroud.  

Pay heed to your spirit, stay faithful, stay right,  
Unfaithful hearts weary me, in the dead of night."  

You weary Yahweh with words you speak,  
"How weary?" you ask, confused and weak.  

Claiming evil is good in Yahweh's sight,  
Demanding justice, as darkness turns to light.
