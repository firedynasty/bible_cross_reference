Yahweh speaks to Israel, a word through Malachi's pen,  
"I've loved you," He declares, "How?" you question again.  
"Esau and Jacob were brothers, remember the tale?  
I loved Jacob, but Esau's land I made a barren vale."

Edom boasts, "We'll rise, rebuild from the ground,"  
But Yahweh says, "They'll build, yet I'll tear it down.  
Men will call them 'The Wicked Land,' forever under my frown."  
Your eyes will see it, and you'll proclaim, "Yahweh is renowned!"

"A son honors his dad, a servant his boss, right?  
Where's my honor, my respect? You priests, you slight.  
You ask how you've despised my name, it's plain,  
Polluted bread you offer on my altar, again and again."

"When you bring the blind for sacrifice, ain't that wrong?  
The lame and sick you offer, you think that's strong?  
Would your governor take it, be pleased with such things?"  
Yahweh asks, "Show me the respect that royalty brings."

"Entreat God's favor, seek His grace," Yahweh suggests,  
"Would He accept you now, after all this mess?"  
"If just one would close the doors, stop this hollow flame,  
I take no joy in you," Yahweh of Armies proclaims.

"From day's first light to its final fall,  
My name's revered among nations, pure offerings all.  
But you profane it, call my table a burden to bear,  
Bring what's beaten, lame, and sick, yet think I'd care?"

"Cursed be the deceiver with a male in his fold,  
Who vows a good gift, but offers what's cold.  
For I am a great King," Yahweh's voice rings,  
"My name is awesome among all earthly things."
