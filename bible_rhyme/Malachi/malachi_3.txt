"Behold, the messenger's on his way,  
Prepping the road, clearing the fray.  
The Lord you seek will hit the scene,  
In His temple, quick and keen.  

"Can you stand the day He arrives?  
Like fire and soap, refining lives.  
He'll purify Levi's sons with ease,  
Offerings in righteousness, aiming to please.  

Judah, Jerusalem, bringing the past back,  
Offerings now pleasant, no more slack.  
A swift witness, I'll come near,  
Against injustice, let it be clear.  

"I, Yahweh, never change,  
Sons of Jacob, don't rearrange.  
You've strayed, turned from My call,  
Return to Me, and I'll restore all."  

"How do we return?" you cry,  
"Robbing God," you deny.  
Tithes and offerings you've withheld,  
Cursed the nation, truth be upheld.  

Bring tithes, fill My storehouse's space,  
Test Me, I'll pour blessings, fill the place.  
Rebuke the devourer, fields shall thrive,  
Nations call you blessed, see you alive.  

"Your words against Me, hard, you say,  
Serving's vain, you question the way.  
The proud seem happy, wicked rise,  
Tempt God, escape, no surprise."  

But those who fear, together they stand,  
Yahweh listens, a book in His hand.  
"They're My own, they'll be spared,  
As a son who's loved, deeply cared."  

Return, discern, the good and the bad,  
Who serves God, and who's been had.
