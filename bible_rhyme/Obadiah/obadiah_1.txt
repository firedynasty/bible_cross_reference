Yo, Obadiah saw a vision, here's the tale,  
Yahweh speaks on Edom, no way they prevail.  
News comes from Yahweh, nations hear the call,  
"Rise up, get ready, we're gonna watch her fall."  

"Behold, you're small in the nation game,  
Despised and hated, that's your claim to fame.  
Pride's got you tricked in your rocky high place,  
Thinking, ‘Who can touch us? Who's got the space?’"  

"Fly high like the eagle, nest with the stars,  
But I'll bring you down, no matter how far,"   
Says Yahweh. "Thieves at night, coming for your gold,  
Grape pickers show up, won't leave a grape untold."  

"Oh, Esau’s treasures, they're all sought out,  
Your allies ditch you, without a doubt.  
Friends at your table, they lay a snare,  
You’re lost in confusion, unaware."  

"Wise men of Edom, they won’t stay wise,  
On the mountain of Esau, wisdom dies."  
Mighty men of Teman, feel the dismay,  
Esau’s mountain falls, everyone’s in the fray.  

Violence to Jacob brings you shame,  
Cut off forever, that’s your name.  
When strangers plundered, you stood aside,  
Joining in the chaos, you were allied.  

"Don't look on Judah in their distress,  
Don’t speak with pride in their mess.  
Don’t walk through their gates on that dark day,  
Don’t take their wealth, just walk away."  

"Don’t block the escapees, let them flee,  
Don’t trap the survivors, let them be.  
The day of Yahweh’s near, it's coming soon,  
Your deeds boomerang back, your own tune."  

"As you drank on my mountain high,  
Nations will drink, then say goodbye.  
But in Zion, some will escape,  
Jacob's house will take the shape."  

"Jacob’s a fire, Joseph's a flame,  
Esau’s stubble, that's the name.  
They'll burn and consume, Esau’s no more,  
Yahweh's word seals the score."  

South claims Esau, lowlands take Philistine,  
Fields and lands, Ephraim and Samaria seen.  
Benjamin holds Gilead, captives break free,  
Possessing Negev, claiming destiny.  

On Mount Zion, saviors rise,  
Judging Esau, claiming the prize.  
Yahweh's kingdom, it will reign,  
The vision's clear, Yahweh's domain.
