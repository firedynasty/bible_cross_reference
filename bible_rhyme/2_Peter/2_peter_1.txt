Simon Peter speaks, listen up, my friends,  
To all who've found faith, a message he sends.  

Grace and peace, may they multiply,  
Through Jesus our Lord, who is always nigh.  

His divine power grants all for life,  
Through knowledge of Him, rise above the strife.  

Exceedingly great promises, He’s given,  
So we can partake in the nature of heaven.  

Add to your faith, be diligent and wise,  
Moral excellence, let it rise.  

In excellence, knowledge, and in that, control,  
Perseverance and godliness for the soul.  

Brotherly affection, love at its core,  
These make you fruitful, in Jesus, explore.  

Without these, you're blind, forgetting the past,  
Cleansed from sin, hold the truth fast.  

Be diligent, brothers, confirm your call,  
Do these things, you'll never fall.  

Rich entrance waits in the Kingdom above,  
For the Savior’s embrace, His endless love.  

I’ll remind you, though you already know,  
Keeping the truth in your hearts aglow.  

While I’m here, I'll stir you up true,  
As my time to depart swiftly comes due.  

After I'm gone, remember my creed,  
We didn’t follow fables, we know His deed.  

Eyewitnesses to His majesty, profound,  
God's voice spoke with a heavenly sound:  

"This is my Son, beloved, so pleased,"  
Heard on the mountain, where glory seized.  

Prophecy’s sure, heed it like a light,  
Shining bright, in the darkest night.  

No prophecy by man’s mere will was spoken,  
Holy men, Spirit-moved, words unbroken.
