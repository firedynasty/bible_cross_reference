False prophets crept in, they came undercover,  
Teaching lies on the sly, their words hover.  

Denying the Master, they face swift destruction,  
Many follow their ways, truth's interruption.  

With greedy schemes, they deceive and exploit,  
But judgment's not sleeping, their fate’s set to anoint.  

God didn’t spare angels who fell from grace,  
Cast to darkness, awaiting their case.  

The ancient world drowned, but Noah was saved,  
A flood for the ungodly, righteous were braved.  

Sodom and Gomorrah, ashes in the air,  
An example of wrath, for those who dare.  

Lot, tormented soul in a wicked place,  
God knows how to save, His mercy and grace.  

The unrighteous held for the judgment day,  
Those defiling flesh, daring in their sway.  

Angels stay silent, though mightier they be,  
While fools speak evil ignorantly.  

These like beasts, ignorant and bold,  
Their end is destruction, as prophecy's told.  

Reveling in daylight, their deceit is a feast,  
Eyes full of sin, they never cease.  

Trained in greed, they stray from the right,  
Following Balaam, blinded by the light.  

A donkey spoke to halt his path,  
Madness stopped by a creature's wrath.  

Wells without water, clouds storm-tossed,  
In darkness eternal, forever lost.  

Swelling words of emptiness entice,  
False liberty leads to vice.  

Once escaped, then entangled again,  
Worse off now than they were back when.  

Better to not have known the way,  
Than to turn from truth, in disarray.  

Like a dog to its vomit, a sow to mire,  
Back to corruption, lost in the fire.
