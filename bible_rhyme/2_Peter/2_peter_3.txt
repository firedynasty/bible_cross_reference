I'm writing you again, my friend, to remind,  
Keep the words of prophets clear in your mind.  

In the last days, mockers will arise,  
Chasing desires, spreading their lies.  

"Where's His promise?" they’ll scoff and say,  
"Since ancestors slept, it’s all the same way."  

They forget the world was formed by His word,  
Water shaped earth, history stirred.  

That world was drowned, it perished in the sea,  
Now fire’s reserved, a day will be.  

But remember, God's time isn't like ours,  
A day or a thousand, His patience empowers.  

He’s not slow, but waiting for all to repent,  
Wishing none perish, His patience is lent.  

The Lord’s day will come like a thief in the night,  
Heavens will roar, elements ignite.  

So since all this will melt away,  
Live holy and godly, each new day.  

Look for the day when new heavens come,  
In righteousness, we’ll find our home.  

Be at peace, spotless, blameless in sight,  
His patience means salvation, hold tight.  

Paul wrote of these things, wisdom he shared,  
Some twist his words, so be prepared.  

Knowing this, don't be led astray,  
Stay firm in truth, don’t sway.  

Grow in the grace, in knowledge of Christ,  
To Him be glory, now and for all life. Amen.
