If a man takes a wife, he writes her a note,  
Sends her away, if love won't float.  
She can marry again, find another man,  
But if he leaves her, or dies, as planned,  

The first can't take her back as his bride,  
For Yahweh says that's not to abide.  
Don't let the land fall to sin, my friend,  
The gift from God must not descend.  

If you marry anew, stay home for a year,  
Cheer up your wife, keep her near.  
Don't pledge a millstone, don't take a life,  
Keep your dealings clear, free of strife.  

Caught stealing a brother, selling for gain?  
That thief must die, cleanse the stain.  
Leprosy strikes, follow the priests,  
Observe their ways, or face the beasts.  

Remember Miriam on Egypt's road,  
Lending means outside, no house code.  
Return the pledge, let him sleep tight,  
Be righteous before God, all's right.  

Don't oppress the poor who serve,  
Pay them daily, give what they deserve.  
No death for another's sin,  
Each answers for their own within.  

Justice for strangers, orphans, widows too,  
Remember Egypt, God saved you.  
Leave the sheaf, the olives, the vine,  
For those in need, let them dine.  

Don't forget you were once a slave,  
Now do what's commanded, be brave.
