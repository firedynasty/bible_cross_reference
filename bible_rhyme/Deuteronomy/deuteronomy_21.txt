In the land that God gave, a slain man's found,  
With no clue who struck him down, lying on the ground.  

Elders and judges, measuring the scene,  
Find the nearest town, to make things clean.  

To the valley with waters, they take a heifer pure,  
Break its neck there, to make the land secure.  

Levi's sons, priests of God, they come near,  
Bless in His name, make judgments clear.  

Elders wash hands, and declare their plea,  
"Our hands are clean, from this blood we're free."  

"Forgive, Yahweh, your people, redeemed,  
Keep innocent blood from being deemed."  

Put away the guilt, do what's right,  
In Yahweh's eyes, bring justice to light.  

Going to battle, foes delivered in hand,  
Captives taken, you see a woman stand.  

She's beautiful, you want her as wife,  
Bring her home, let her mourn her life.  

Shave her head, trim her nails, she's torn,  
Change her clothes, for a month she's to mourn.  

If the delight fades, let her go free,  
Don't sell her, no slave shall she be.  

Two wives, one's loved, the other's scorned,  
Firstborn's rights, to the hated's son adorned.  

Double portion, the firstborn's due,  
Acknowledge the strength, his rights are true.  

A stubborn son, rebellious, won't hear,  
Parents bring him to the elders, voice their fear.  

"This son won't obey, eats and drinks his fill,"  
Men stone him, remove the evil, fulfill God's will.  

Sin worthy of death, a man on a tree,  
Bury him that day, don't let the curse be.  

Don't defile the land, your inheritance pure,  
In God's given land, keep justice sure.
