In the month of Abib, make it clear,  
Celebrate Passover, Yahweh's deliverance near.  
Sacrifice the lamb at a chosen place,  
Where His name's dwelling, a sacred space.  

No leavened bread for seven days straight,  
Unleavened bread, remember your fate.  
Escape from Egypt, in haste you fled,  
Keep this in mind, with unleavened bread.  

No yeast in sight, keep your borders clean,  
Meat from the first night, gone by morning's sheen.  
Not in your gates, but where Yahweh's name stays,  
Sacrifice at sunset, remember those days.  

Roast it up, eat where He will choose,  
In the morning, depart, no time to lose.  
Six days of unleavened, on the seventh, rest,  
A solemn assembly, no work, just bless.  

Count seven weeks, when the sickle strikes,  
Feast of Weeks coming, bring what He likes.  
Rejoice with Yahweh, bring your whole crew,  
Son, daughter, servants, and Levites too.  

Remember Egypt, a slave you were bound,  
Observe the statutes, let joy abound.  
Feast of Booths, when the harvest is done,  
Gather and celebrate, everyone’s welcome.  

Rejoice in the feast, with all of your kin,  
Yahweh's blessings, let the joy begin.  
Three times a year, all males appear,  
In the place Yahweh chooses, bring no fear.  

Give what you can, from the blessings received,  
Judges and officers, justice achieved.  
No perverted justice, no bribes in sight,  
Follow what's right, in Yahweh's light.  

No Asherah trees by His altar's side,  
No sacred stones, in Yahweh's eyes, denied.
