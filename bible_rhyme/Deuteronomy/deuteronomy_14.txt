Listen up, children of Yahweh, this is how you roll,  
Don't cut yourselves or make bald spots—a holy role.  
You're chosen by God, a special crew,  
Above all the nations, yes, that's you.  

Don't eat what's nasty, here's what you can chew,  
Ox, sheep, goats, and deer, true to you.  
Eat the gazelle and the wild goat too,  
Hoof-split cud-chewers, they're good for you.  

But hey, don't munch the camel or rabbit,  
They're unclean, don't make it a habit.  
Pigs, they got split hooves but don't chew,  
So don't eat their meat or touch 'em too.  

In the waters, fins and scales you seek,  
If it ain't got those, don't you eat.  
Clean birds are cool, but some are foul,  
Eagles, vultures, owls, don't be on the prowl.  

No eating what just up and dies,  
Give it to outsiders, don't tell lies.  
You’re holy to Yahweh, keep it real,  
Boil no goat kid in its mom's milk meal.  

Tithe your grain, wine, oil, and herd,  
Before Yahweh, keep His Word.  
If the place is far, turn it to cash,  
Buy what you want, eat and splash.  

Don't forget the Levite at your gates,  
No land or share, he's in dire straits.  
Every three years, gather the store,  
Feed the Levite, the foreigner, the needy, and more.  

Do this right, let blessings flow,  
Yahweh will prosper the work you show.
