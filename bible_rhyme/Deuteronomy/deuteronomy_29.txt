Moses stood and called all of Israel, "Hear!  
You've seen what Yahweh did, made Pharaoh disappear.

Signs and wonders, trials so grand,  
But hearts and eyes not fully understand.

Forty years I led you, wilderness wide,  
Clothes and sandals, still good, did not divide.

No bread, no wine, to make you see,  
That Yahweh's your God, guiding free.

When Sihon and Og came for the fight,  
We struck them down, claimed our right.

Gave their land to Reuben, Gad, Manasseh's zone,  
Keep Yahweh's covenant, make it your own.

Stand today in Yahweh's sight,  
Heads, tribes, elders, in the light.

Little ones, wives, and strangers too,  
All who cut wood, and draw water for you.

Enter the covenant, Yahweh’s decree,  
"Be my people, let me be."

Not just you, but those to come,  
Remember Egypt, where you were from.

See the idols, stone and gold,  
Turn away, stay in Yahweh's fold.

No root of poison, no heart astray,  
Yahweh’s wrath for those who stray.

"The curse will fall," Yahweh declares,  
Blot out their name, no pardons, no dares.

Future folks will see the land,  
Sulfur, salt, no grass, barren sand.

"Why this wrath?" the nations will cry,  
"They broke Yahweh's bond, that's why."

Served other gods, left Yahweh behind,  
Anger burned, land undermined.

Secret things are Yahweh's own,  
But what’s revealed, is ours alone.

To keep the law, to do what's right,  
Yahweh's word, our guiding light.
