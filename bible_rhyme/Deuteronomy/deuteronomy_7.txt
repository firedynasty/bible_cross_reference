When God brings you to the promised land,  
Seven nations fall by His mighty hand.  

Greater and mightier than you, they stand tall,  
But when He delivers them, you'll see them fall.  

No covenants made, no mercy to show,  
No marriage bonds—let those ties not grow.  

For their gods will turn your sons away,  
And ignite God's anger without delay.  

"Break their altars, smash their stones,  
Burn their idols, leave nothing but bones.  

You're my chosen, holy and true,  
Above all others, I've set you."  

Not for numbers or size, He made his choice,  
But because He loves, so let’s rejoice.  

He brought you out with a mighty hand,  
From Pharaoh’s grip, to this blessed land.  

Know your God, faithful to the end,  
A thousand generations, His love will extend.  

Repaying those who hate, face to face,  
To those who love Him, endless grace.  

Keep His commands, heed His calls,  
And blessings will rain like waterfalls.  

Multiply your seed, bless your ground,  
Free from sickness, with health abound.  

Against these nations, take your stand,  
Remember Egypt, how He freed this land.  

Signs and wonders, His arm stretched wide,  
Just like before, He'll be your guide.  

The hornet will chase them, one by one,  
You need not fear, for the battle's won.  

Little by little, they'll be cast away,  
Till not a trace of them is left to stay.  

Burn their idols, leave no trace,  
Covet not gold, avoid disgrace.  

Detest what's unholy, keep your heart pure,  
And in this promise, stand secure.
