"Don't bring Yahweh a sheep with a flaw,  
A defect or evil—that's against the law.  

If someone in your gates does wrong,  
Breaking Yahweh's covenant, worshiping the wrong song,  

Serving gods or stars in the sky,  
You hear about it, don't just let it slide by.  

Investigate, find out if it's true,  
If evil's done, here's what you do:  

Bring them out, man or woman who strays,  
Stone them at the gates, that's how evil decays.  

Two or three witnesses must agree,  
One alone's not enough for the guilty plea.  

The witnesses throw the first stone,  
Then the people join in, evil's overthrown.  

If a case is too tough, a judgment unclear,  
Go to Yahweh's chosen, and they'll make it appear.  

The Levite priests and judge will decide,  
Follow their verdict, let them be your guide.  

Don't stray from the judgment they proclaim,  
To the right or left, stay in the same lane.  

If someone won't listen, presumes to rebel,  
Remove the evil, let their punishment tell.  

The people shall hear and respect,  
No more presumption, keep Yahweh's laws checked.  

When you enter the land Yahweh gives,  
And want a king like the neighbor lives,  

Choose the one Yahweh selects,  
A brother from among you, not foreign rejects.  

The king can't hoard horses, nor return to old scenes,  
Yahweh said, "Don't go back to Egypt's means."  

He won't multiply wives, won't let his heart stray,  
Nor seek silver and gold in a greedy display.  

When he sits on the throne, a book he'll write,  
The laws of Yahweh, in his sight.  

Read it daily, learn to fear and obey,  
Keep the statutes, walk Yahweh's way.  

His heart stays humble, not lifted above,  
He walks right, not turning, in Yahweh's love.  

So his kingdom endures, his days extend,  
He and his children, in Israel's land, their story won't end."
