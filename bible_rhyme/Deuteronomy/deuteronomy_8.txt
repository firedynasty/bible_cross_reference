Yo, listen up, gotta keep these commands,  
So you live, multiply, and take the lands.  

Remember the journey, forty years past,  
Yahweh led you through the wilderness vast.  

He humbled you, let hunger be your test,  
Fed you manna unknown, gave you His best.  

Clothes stayed fresh, your feet felt no pain,  
Disciplined like a son, through sun and rain.  

Keep His ways, walk, and fear His name,  
Yahweh's bringing you to that land of fame.  

A land where water flows, springs and streams,  
Wheat, barley, figs, and honey in your dreams.  

Bread with no scarcity, iron in the stone,  
Dig copper from hills, call this land your own.  

Eat and be full, bless Yahweh’s hand,  
Don’t forget Him in this promised land.  

When houses grow big, silver and gold,  
Don’t let your heart grow proud or cold.  

Remember Egypt, that land of dread,  
Yahweh freed you, where snakes and scorpions tread.  

Water from rock, manna every day,  
To humble and prove you, guiding the way.  

Don’t say, “It’s my strength that got me this,”  
Remember Yahweh, who gives wealth, don’t miss.  

Forget Him, chase gods, serve and bow,  
You’ll perish like those who fall before now.  

Listen close, to Yahweh’s voice,  
Stay true to His word, make the right choice.
