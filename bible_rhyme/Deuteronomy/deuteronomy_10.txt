Yahweh spoke to me, "Cut stone tablets anew,  
Bring 'em up the mountain, make an ark too.  

I'll write those words that you broke in two,  
Place them in the ark, that's what you'll do."  

So I crafted an ark from acacia wood,  
Cut the stone tablets just like I should.  

Climbed that mountain with tablets in hand,  
Yahweh's words inscribed, by His command.  

Came down the mountain, tablets in the ark lay,  
Just as Yahweh commanded, they stayed that way.  

Israel journeyed, from Beeroth they moved,  
At Moserah, Aaron died, his life removed.  

Eleazar stepped up, took the priestly place,  
From Gudgodah to Jotbathah, they resumed their pace.  

Yahweh chose Levi, to bear His sacred ark,  
No land for Levi, but Yahweh left His mark.  

Forty days on the mountain, like before,  
Yahweh listened, wouldn't destroy, He swore.  

"Arise," He said, "Lead the people, take their stand,  
To possess the promised, father-sworn land."  

Israel, what does Yahweh need you to see?  
Fear Him, love Him, walk faithfully.  

Keep His commands, for your own good,  
He owns the heavens, that's understood.  

He loved your fathers, chose their seed,  
Circumcise your heart, from stubbornness freed.  

God of gods, Lord of lords, great and mighty,  
No respecter of persons, no bribes, He's rightly.  

Justice for the fatherless, loves the alien,  
Feed and clothe them, show compassion.  

Love the stranger, you were once foreigners too,  
Fear Yahweh, serve Him, be true.  

He is your praise, your God above,  
Great things He's done, a labor of love.  

Seventy went to Egypt in the past,  
Now like stars, your numbers are vast.
