Every seven years, debts get erased,  
Promises of freedom, a fresh clean slate.  

"Release what's lent," says God's decree,  
Freedom for neighbors, let them be.  

Foreigners might still owe you back,  
But your brother's debt, you must unpack.  

No poor among you, bless the land,  
If you heed God's command.  

"Listen to My voice," Yahweh will say,  
Lend to nations, lead the way.  

A brother in need, don't close your grip,  
Open your hand, let generosity rip.  

Avoid wicked thoughts as years roll near,  
Give with an open heart, not with fear.  

For God will bless your labor's span,  
When you give without grief, to your fellow man.  

Poor will always be around, that's true,  
Open your hand, it's what you must do.  

If a Hebrew brother serves you six years long,  
In the seventh year, set him free, make him strong.  

Don't send him away with nothing but air,  
Give from your flock, your grain, and share.  

Remember you were slaves, redeemed in the past,  
That's why this command today is cast.  

If he loves your house and wants to stay,  
Pierce his ear, serve you forever, Yahweh's way.  

Letting go shouldn't feel too tough,  
For he has served long and hard enough.  

Firstborn males, a gift divine,  
Dedicate them to God, let them shine.  

Eat them where God chooses, year by year,  
Lame or blind, don't bring them near.  

Share within your gates, clean or not,  
But pour the blood like water, on the spot.
