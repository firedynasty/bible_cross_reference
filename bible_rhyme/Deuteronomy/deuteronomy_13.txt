A prophet or a dreamer might rise in your crew,  
With signs and wonders that seem so true.  

If they say, "Let's chase gods never known,"  
Don't heed their words; don't let them be shown.  

"Yahweh's testing your heart and soul,"  
Keep Him first; make Him your goal.  

Walk with Yahweh, fear and obey,  
Serve and cling to Him every day.  

The dreamer who rebels, speaking lies,  
Must be removed — that evil dies.  

From Egypt's bondage, He set you free,  
Cut off the one who talks deceitfully.  

Even if a brother, or friend so close,  
Says, "Serve other gods," don't be morose.  

Don't listen, don't pity, don't hide their sin,  
Justice begins with you and spreads to your kin.  

Stone them, let all Israel hear,  
So wickedness won't come near.  

If in any city, folks lead astray,  
Investigate, see if evil's at play.  

If true, strike down that city's greed,  
Destroy it all, remember this creed.  

Burn the city, leave it a heap,  
Not to be built again, let it sleep.  

Don't keep the spoils, let nothing remain,  
So Yahweh's mercy and love will reign.  

Hear His voice, His commands obey,  
Do what's right in Yahweh's way.
