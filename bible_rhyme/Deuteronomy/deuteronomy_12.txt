Listen up, here’s what you gotta know,  
In the land that Yahweh’s givin', let His commandments flow.  

Smash their altars, break their stone,  
Burn down their idols, don’t leave a bone.  

"Seek the place that I choose to honor My name,  
Bring your offerings there, don’t play no game."  

Celebrate with your people, let joy overflow,  
In Yahweh's blessings, let your gratitude show.  

Don’t follow your own desires, or what you think is right,  
Wait for the rest, your inheritance in sight.  

When Yahweh gives you peace, rest from your foes,  
Bring your offerings where His presence grows.  

Rejoice with your family, servants, and kin,  
Remember the Levite, don’t leave him thin.  

"Don’t sacrifice just anywhere, heed My call,  
Where I choose, there you’ll bring it all."  

Kill and eat within your gates, enjoy the feast,  
But let the blood pour out, for life’s in the beast.  

No tithes at home, take them to Yahweh’s place,  
Rejoice in His blessings, seek His face.  

If you're craving meat, and the journey’s too wide,  
Slaughter at home, let your soul be satisfied.  

But pour the blood like water on the earth,  
Respect the life, honor its worth.  

Listen to what Yahweh commands,  
It’ll go well for you and your lands.  

When Yahweh clears the nations, don’t follow their ways,  
Don’t serve their gods or prolong their days.  

"Whatever I command, observe and do,  
Don’t add or subtract, keep My words true."
