We turned and faced Bashan, Og's crew came for war,  
Met us at Edrei, ready to settle the score.  
Yahweh said, "Don't fear, I've given you the land,  
Do to him what you did with Sihon's hand."  

Yahweh delivered Og, we struck him down bold,  
No one was left, their story was told.  
Sixty cities fell, each fortified and tall,  
Walls, gates, and bars, we seized them all.  

Villages without walls, we left none behind,  
Destroyed like with Sihon, wiped out their kind.  
Livestock and treasure, we took it as our own,  
From Arnon to Hermon, the land was shown.  

One giant remained, Og's bed of iron fame,  
Nine cubits long, the children of Ammon know his name.  
We claimed the land, split it among tribes,  
To Reuben, Gad, Manasseh, all the lands we'd describe.  

Jair named Havvoth Jair, his legacy to stay,  
Gilead to Machir, among the tribes it lay.  
To Reubenites, Gadites, lands of fertile worth,  
From Arnon to Jabbok, stretching across the earth.  

Then I said, "Arm up, men of valor, go fight,  
But your families stay safe, until comes the night."  
Joshua was told, "You've seen Yahweh's might,  
He fights for you, so bring victory's light."  

I pleaded with Yahweh, "Let me see the land,  
Beyond the Jordan, the mountains so grand."  
But Yahweh was firm, "Stop this talk of yours,  
Climb Pisgah, look over, but cross no shores."  

"Commission Joshua, give him strength to lead,  
He'll guide the people, fulfill the promised deed."  
So we lingered near Beth Peor, in the valley's expanse,  
Waiting for the time when our destiny would advance.
