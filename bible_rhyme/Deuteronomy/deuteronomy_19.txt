When Yahweh cuts off the nations, the lands to you He gives,  
Move in, take the cities, and there you shall live.  

Set apart three cities in the land He provides,  
For the manslayer to flee, where safety abides.  

Prepare the roads, make the borders clear,  
A refuge for the one who didn’t kill out of fear.  

If by accident a neighbor falls by your hand,  
Flee to these cities, safe in this land.  

“The avenger may chase with anger in his heart,  
But he didn’t mean to, that's the crucial part.”  

Three cities, I command, set them apart,  
For the innocent's life, a place to restart.  

If Yahweh expands your borders wide,  
Add three more cities, keep justice on your side.  

“Innocent blood mustn't stain your ground,  
In the land Yahweh gives, let peace be found.”  

But if hatred leads to a deadly fate,  
The elders will judge, and they won’t hesitate.  

“You shall not pity, purging guilt from your view,  
Keep Israel pure, let justice be true.”  

Don’t move the landmarks of old, leave them in place,  
Inherit the land, respect and keep pace.  

One witness isn’t enough for any crime,  
Two or three must speak to stand the test of time.  

If someone falsely speaks, trying to deceive,  
Before priests and judges, their lies we'll relieve.  

“If found false, their fate you’ll reverse,  
Remove the evil, end the curse.”  

Let all hear and fear, and change their ways,  
Justice served sharp, it’ll end dark days.  

“Life for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth,  
Hand for hand, foot for foot, pursue the truth.”
