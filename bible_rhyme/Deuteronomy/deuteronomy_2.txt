In the wilderness, we wandered, circling round,  
Mount Seir’s paths like a merry-go-round.  
God spoke, "You've circled long enough, take a turn,  
Head north, the lesson’s learned, it's time to discern."

"Pass through Esau's land, but don't provoke,  
They'll feel fear, but keep it gentle, no need to poke.  
Their land's not yours, just tread and buy,  
Food and water with silver, let peace be your tie."

"For forty years, I've watched you roam,  
Blessed your toil, you’ve never been alone.  
Passed Seir's paths, to Moab's edge,  
But don't fight Moab, respect the pledge."

Giants lived there, tall and strong,  
Called Emim by Moab, a name that’s long.  
Esau’s kin took the Horites’ place,  
Just as Israel would, in their race.

"Cross Zered's brook," God’s command was clear,  
Thirty-eight years pass, warriors disappear.  
God’s hand led, till they were gone,  
Now to Moab’s border, the journey drawn.

"Pass Ammon's land, but keep it calm,  
It’s Lot's possession, don't raise an arm.  
Filled with giants once, like Zamzummim,  
But Israel succeeded, with God's seraphim."

"Rise and move to Arnon's vale,  
Sihon's kingdom, it’s time to assail.  
Dread and fear on all will fall,  
As you approach with courage tall."

Sent words of peace to Sihon’s ear,  
"Let us pass through, have no fear.  
We'll buy food and drink, stay on the road,  
Just pass through, no heavy load."

But Sihon’s heart, God made hard,  
Wouldn’t let us pass, kept us barred.  
God said, "His land, now yours to claim,  
Begin the fight, make your name."

Sihon met us at Jahaz ground,  
God delivered him, victory profound.  
Cities fell, none left alive,  
Livestock taken, we did thrive.

From Arnon’s edge to Gilead’s grace,  
No city too high, God cleared the space.  
Only Ammon’s land stayed untouched,  
God’s command respected, His plan trusted much.
