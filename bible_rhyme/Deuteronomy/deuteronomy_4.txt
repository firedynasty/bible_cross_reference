Listen up, Israel, hear the laws I share,  
Live by them, possess the land with care.  

Don't add, don't take from the words I speak,  
Keep Yahweh's commands, stay strong, not weak.  

You've seen Baal Peor's fate, how they fell,  
But the faithful are alive, and doing well.  

I've taught you statutes, as Yahweh decreed,  
In this land you're to live, follow and lead.  

Keep them close, that's your wisdom bright,  
Nations will see, and say, "What a sight!”  

What nation has a God as near as He,  
Who hears our call, who sets us free?  

Be careful, remember what you saw,  
Pass it to your children, the teachings, the law.  

Remember Horeb, the fire's display,  
He spoke from the flames, no form that day.  

"Gather 'round," He said, "Hear my word,  
Fear me, teach it, let it be heard."  

Ten commandments on stone He wrote,  
For you to live by, the covenant's note.  

In the fire's midst, no image was shown,  
So make no idols, let God's word alone.  

No carved images of beast or bird,  
No worship of stars, let His voice be heard.  

From Egypt’s furnace, He brought you out,  
His people of inheritance, without a doubt.  

Though I can't cross Jordan's line,  
You'll possess that good land, it's yours in time.  

Don't forget Yahweh's solemn pact,  
Stay true, or face the fact.  

He is a fire, a jealous flame,  
Stray, and you'll call His anger's name.  

In future days, if you stray and fail,  
Heaven and earth will hear your tale.  

Scattered you'll be, few in the land,  
Serving gods made by human hand.  

But seek Him with all your heart and soul,  
You'll find Him again, He’ll make you whole.  

Oppression comes, but turn around,  
Yahweh will hear, His mercy profound.  

Look to the past, see deeds untold,  
His wonders from days of old.  

No nation has heard His voice in fire,  
Or seen His might, His wonders inspire.  

He chose you from nations, with power and hand,  
To give you this promised land.  

Know Yahweh's God, heaven and earth His domain,  
Keep His commands, and blessings you'll gain.  

Moses set cities, for refuge they stand,  
For those who’ve shed blood, not by hand.  

Bezer, Ramoth, and Golan named,  
Safe havens, where peace is claimed.  

These are the laws Moses laid down,  
For Israel, as they left Egypt's frown.  

Beyond the Jordan, battles fought,  
In lands of kings, victories wrought.  

From Arnon's edge to Mount Sion high,  
Possession secured, beneath the sky.
