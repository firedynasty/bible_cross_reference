"Love Yahweh your God," that's the command,  
Keep His words, His laws, with a steady hand.  

"Not to your children, this message I bring,  
But to you who've seen Yahweh do mighty things."  

In Egypt's land, great signs were made,  
Pharaoh's army in the Red Sea laid.  

He led you through desert's demanding test,  
To this very place, where you now rest.  

Dathan and Abiram felt the earth's embrace,  
Swallowed whole, gone without a trace.  

Your eyes have seen all these wonders and more,  
So keep the commandments, victory's at your door.  

That you may be strong, take the land you see,  
A land of milk and honey, flowing free.  

Not like Egypt, where seeds were sown,  
But hills and valleys where rains have grown.  

Yahweh’s eyes upon it, all through the year,  
"Love Me, serve Me," His voice you'll hear.  

"I'll send the rains, early and late,  
Grain, wine, and oil, on your plate."  

Fields with grass, full stomachs you'll find,  
But stray from Him, and rain won't be kind.  

"Bind my words, close to your heart,  
Teach them daily, never depart."  

Write them down, on your home's door,  
That your days be multiplied evermore.  

Keep these words, cling to His way,  
And you'll drive out nations, come what may.  

From wilderness to sea, your feet shall roam,  
Every path, every place, yours to call home.  

"Before you today, a choice to make,  
Blessing or curse, paths you take."  

A blessing if you heed, His commands you hold,  
A curse if you stray, if other gods you behold.  

When Yahweh brings you, to the land so wide,  
Set the blessing on Gerizim, curse on Ebal's side.  

Cross the Jordan, take the land He gives,  
Observe His statutes, in His ways you live.
