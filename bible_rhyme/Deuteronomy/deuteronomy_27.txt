Moses said to Israel, "Listen, hear what I say,  
Keep these commandments, follow them today.  

When you cross the Jordan, to the promised land,  
Set up great stones, coat them with plaster by hand.  

Write on those stones, all the laws you know,  
In the land of milk and honey, where you'll go.  

On Mount Ebal, set those stones real high,  
Build an altar with uncut stones that touch the sky.  

No iron on them, just as I command,  
Burnt offerings to Yahweh, with your hands.  

Rejoice and eat, peace offerings made,  
His words on stones, in plain view laid.  

Moses and priests called out to the crowd,  
"Be silent and listen!" their voices loud.  

"You are Yahweh's people, obey His call,  
His statutes and commands, follow them all."  

Mount Gerizim will bless, with voices strong,  
Simeon, Levi, Judah, they stand where they belong.  

Mount Ebal will curse, it's Reuben's stand,  
Levites shout curses, all over the land.  

"Cursed is the one with idols secret and sly,"  
And all the people answer, "Amen," they cry.  

"Cursed who dishonors parents, or moves a stone,  
Leads the blind astray, leaves justice alone."  

"Cursed who lies with kin or kills in the night,  
Takes a bribe to shed blood, in Yahweh's sight."  

"Uphold the law, or cursed you will be,"  
All the people say, "Amen," in unity.
