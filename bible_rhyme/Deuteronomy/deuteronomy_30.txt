When blessings and curses fill your mind,  
Recall them all, don't leave them behind.  

Among the nations where you're driven,  
Turn back to Yahweh, your heart's been given.  

With all your soul, obey His call,  
Yahweh will release you, break the wall.  

From every land where you've been scattered,  
He'll gather you, your heart unshattered.  

He'll bring you back to your fathers' land,  
You'll possess it, like a king you'll stand.  

He'll do you good, your numbers grow,  
More than before, His mercy flow.  

Yahweh will change your heart's direction,  
To love Him fully, feel His affection.  

Enemies cursed, while you arise,  
Obey His word, make it your prize.  

Prosperity in all your labor,  
Fruit of your body, Yahweh's favor.  

His voice, His law, hold them tight,  
Turn to Him with all your might.  

Commands not distant, they're near and clear,  
No need to ask, "Who's bringing it here?"  

Not beyond seas, no distant quest,  
The word's in your heart, it knows you best.  

Life and death, they stand before,  
Choose life today, and live much more.  

Love Yahweh, follow His ways,  
In His land, prolong your days.  

But if you turn and walk astray,  
Worship idols, lose your way.  

Then perish you will, in this land,  
Across the Jordan, without His hand.  

Heaven and earth bear witness true,  
Life and death set before you.  

Choose life, you and your kin shall live,  
Love Yahweh, obey, and give.  

For He's your life, your days to prolong,  
In the land He promised, where you belong.
