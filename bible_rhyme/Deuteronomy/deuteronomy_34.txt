Moses climbed up from Moab's plain, to Nebo's peak so high,  
He's opposite Jericho, with a view that touched the sky.  

God showed him lands wide, from Gilead to Dan,  
Through Naphtali, Ephraim, Manasseh—what a plan!  

All the land of Judah, stretching to the Western Sea,  
Jericho, the city of palms, down to Zoar's territory.  

God spoke clear, "Here's the land I swore to Abraham's line,  
To Isaac and Jacob, their offspring's future sign.  

I've let you see it with your eyes, but you won't cross that shore.”  
So in Moab's land, Moses lived no more.  

He died as God said, buried deep where none can say,  
In Moab's valley, his grave unknown to this day.  

At a hundred and twenty, his sight still strong,  
His strength not waned, though his days were long.  

Israel wept thirty days, mourning in the Moab sand,  
Till tears dried and they moved by God's command.  

Joshua stood ready, wisdom in his hand,  
For Moses touched him, making him grand.  

No prophet like Moses has risen since that day,  
Face to face with God, leading in His way.  

In Egypt's land, he performed every sign,  
Mighty deeds and wonders, his legacy divine.  

Moses’ mighty hand, awesome deeds in sight,  
A leader unmatched, leading them to light.
