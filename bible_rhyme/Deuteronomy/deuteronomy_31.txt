Moses stepped up, words ready to flow,  
"Listen up, Israel, there's something you should know.  
I'm one-twenty today, can't come and go no more,  
Yahweh said, 'Moses, you ain't crossing this Jordan shore.'

But don't you worry, God Himself will lead,  
He'll wipe out nations, make 'em scatter like seed.  
Joshua's your man, he's gonna guide your way,  
Just like Yahweh promised on another day.

Remember Sihon and Og, those kings got beat,  
Yahweh crushed their lands, laid 'em down at your feet.  
Be strong, courageous, don't you dare feel small,  
Yahweh's got your back, He won't let you fall."

Moses called Joshua for all to see,  
"Be strong, be brave, lead 'em to victory.  
Yahweh's with you, He won't let you go,  
Don't be scared, don't let your courage get low."

Moses penned the law, gave it to the priests,  
"Every seven years, read it for the feasts.  
Gather everyone, men, women, and kids,  
Teach 'em to fear Yahweh, do all He bids."

Then Yahweh spoke, “Moses, time's running out,  
Bring Joshua to Me; let there be no doubt.”  
In the Tent of Meeting, a cloud did descend,  
Yahweh said, "Moses, your journey's at an end.

This people will stray, chase gods not their own,  
My anger will rise, they'll reap what they've sown.  
But teach them this song, let it ring in their ears,  
A witness for Me through all of their years.

When the land's theirs, flowing with milk and sweet honey,  
They'll get fat, turn away, forget who saved 'em from money.  
Troubles will come, but this song will stay true,  
A reminder of what they forgot to do."

Moses wrote the song, taught it that day,  
Commissioned Joshua, "Lead them the right way.  
Take the law, place it by the Ark's side,  
A witness against you, let it be your guide.

I know you're stubborn, even while I'm alive,  
After I'm gone, will your faith survive?  
Gather the elders, let them hear this word,  
Let heaven and earth be the witnesses stirred."

Moses sang the song, every word clear,  
To remind Israel of Yahweh, keep Him near.
