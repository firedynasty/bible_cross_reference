When you hit the land Yahweh your God gives true,  
Claim it, live it, make it home—it's all for you.  

Gather the fruit, the first from the soil,  
Put it in a basket, begin your loyal toil.  

Go to the place Yahweh sets His name,  
Meet the priest, declare your claim.  

Say, "I profess to God today,  
We've reached the land promised in His way."  

The priest takes the basket, sets it down,  
Before Yahweh's altar, in the holy town.  

"My father was a Syrian, on the brink of decay,  
Went to Egypt, few but grew strong in a mighty way.  

Egyptians oppressed us, made our lives tough,  
But we cried to Yahweh, He said enough’s enough.  

With a mighty hand, He took us from that place,  
Signs and wonders, He showed His divine grace.  

Brought us here, to this promised ground,  
Flowing with milk and honey—all around.  

Now I give the first fruits, Yahweh, it’s for You,  
Lay it down, worship, gratitude runs true."  

Rejoice in the bounty, in the Levite's cheer,  
For you, the stranger, all who are near.  

In the third year, tithe is the name of the game,  
Give to Levite, foreigner, orphan, widow—feed the flame.  

Say to Yahweh, "I've done what You've said,  
Given what was holy, in Your path I tread.  

Didn't eat in mourning, nor when unclean,  
Listened to Your voice, kept my actions keen.  

From Your holy place, hear my prayer,  
Bless us, Yahweh, in Your loving care."  

Yahweh commands, keep these words close,  
With heart and soul, follow what He chose.  

You've declared Yahweh, your God, today,  
Promised to walk in His righteous way.  

Yahweh declares you're His cherished lot,  
High above nations, a holy people's plot.  

In praise, name, and honor, you'll stand tall,  
As Yahweh's own, His command is all.
