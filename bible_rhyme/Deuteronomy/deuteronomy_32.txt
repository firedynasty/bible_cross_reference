Listen up, skies, hear me, earth, when I speak,  
My words like rain, gentle, so unique.  

"Yahweh's name I’ll proclaim, so great,  
The Rock, his ways perfect, never late."  

But the people turned, crooked and wrong,  
They forgot the God to whom they belong.  

"Is this how you repay the Almighty's grace,  
The Father who saved you, set your place?"  

Remember the past, the old days of yore,  
Ask your elders, they'll tell you more.  

God set the nations, each with its own,  
Israel's his portion, his very own throne.  

Found in the desert, waste howling and wild,  
He cared and protected, like a father to a child.  

Like an eagle's wings, spread and wide,  
Yahweh led them, no others by his side.  

He gave them riches from the land so sweet,  
Milk, honey, and grain, at their feet.  

But Jeshurun grew fat, kicked and turned,  
Forgot the Rock from whom they’d learned.  

Provoked by idols, anger was stirred,  
Sacrificed to demons, not his word.  

"See now, I’ll hide my face, see their fate,  
A faithless generation, filled with hate."  

Jealousy burned, with gods they adored,  
A fire consumed, from earth to the core.  

"I’ll heap evils, spend arrows in strife,  
Hunger and beasts, they’ll know my knife."  

Nations void of counsel, so blind,  
Enemies will rise, leave them behind.  

"See your gods, where’s their aid?  
Can they save you, the choices you made?"  

"I alone am He, no one can save,  
I wound, I heal, from cradle to grave."  

"Vengeance is mine, when the time is near,  
Calamity rushes, instills them with fear."  

Yahweh will judge, compassion he’ll give,  
For his people, in mercy, they’ll live.  

"Rejoice, nations, Yahweh’s right,  
Avenge his own, lead to the light."  

Moses spoke the words, the song of his heart,  
Joshua beside, ready to impart.  

"Keep these words, they’re your life and way,  
Cross over Jordan, where you will stay."  

God spoke to Moses, "Climb up, see the land,  
But not to enter, just understand."  

Moses would see from afar, not near,  
For past missteps, the consequence clear.  

"Look, but don’t touch, this is the decree,  
Israel will enter, but not you, nor me."
