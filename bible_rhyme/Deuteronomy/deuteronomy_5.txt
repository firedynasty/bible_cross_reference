Moses called them up, "Listen up, Israel, hear my voice,  
Statutes and laws, you gotta make the right choice.  

Yahweh made a deal with us, not just the old crew,  
Face to face, He spoke, flames like nothing you knew.  

I stood in between ‘cause the fire made you fear,  
God’s words, I shared, crystal clear.  

'I’m Yahweh, took you from Egypt’s land,  
No gods before me, that’s my command.  

No carved images, none from the sky or sea,  
Don’t bow, don’t serve, it’s just you and me.  

I’m a jealous God, watch for generations,  
But love abounds through my creations.  

Don’t misuse my name, I won’t let it slide,  
Keep it holy, stay on my side.  

Six days you labor, on the seventh, rest,  
Sabbath’s sacred, follow my behest.  

Remember Egypt, you were slaves once too,  
Yahweh freed you, power’s in His view.  

Honor mom and dad, long life on this land,  
Yahweh gives this, it’s His command.  

No murder, no adultery, keep it right,  
No stealing, no lies, in darkness or light.  

Don’t covet what your neighbor owns,  
His wife, his field, leave his stuff alone.'  

Yahweh spoke from the fire, His voice so vast,  
Wrote it down on stone, a word to last.  

You saw the fire, felt the heat,  
Heads of tribes gathered at my feet.  

'Yahweh showed us His glory and might,  
We heard His voice, lived through the night.  

But this fire’s fierce, why should we die?  
You go, Moses, hear God’s reply.  

Tell us His words, just you alone,  
We’ll hear it, do it, make it our own.'  

Yahweh heard your voice, spoke to me,  
'They said well, but where's their loyalty?  

A heart that fears, that keeps my way,  
Forever blessed, each and every day.  

Tell them go back, to their tented home,  
But you, stay here, don't roam.  

Learn my laws, teach them each one,  
In the land I give, let my will be done.'  

Walk straight, no left or right,  
Follow Yahweh, stay in the light.  

Live long, prosper, in the land you possess,  
Keep to the path, and you'll be blessed."
