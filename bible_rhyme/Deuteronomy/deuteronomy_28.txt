Listen close, hear the voice, God's commands ain't a choice,  
"Lift you high," He said, "above every nation's noise."

Blessings rain down, city and field,  
Your body and ground, with abundance they'll yield.

Your basket, your dough, blessed as they rise,  
When you enter and exit, blessings in your eyes.

Enemies come one way, scatter seven-fold,  
Barns fill with blessings, more than you can hold.

"Be my people," God swore, "walk in my way,"  
Nations will see, and they'll tremble in dismay.

Skies open treasure, rain falls on your land,  
Lend to nations, never borrow, as God planned.

"Head not tail," God said, "above, never below,"  
Obey His word, don't stray, keep it in tow.

But if you turn away, curses will unfold,  
City and field, they'll leave you cold.

Basket and dough, cursed in the strife,  
In and out, cursed, in every part of life.

Enemies rise, confusion will reign,  
Until you're destroyed by the weight of your pain.

Pestilence clings, fever and blight,  
Skies of bronze, ground of iron, no respite.

"Rain dust down," said God, "until you're undone,"  
Enemies strike, and you'll be on the run.

Bodies for birds, no one to shoo,  
Boils and madness, blindness too.

Grope at noon, in perpetual night,  
Oppression and robbery, no savior in sight.

Wife with another, house not your home,  
Vineyard's fruit, you'll never own.

Ox taken, donkey gone,  
Children to strangers, hope withdrawn.

Foreigners rise, you fall low,  
Lend to them, not to you, that's how it will go.

Curses chase you, till you're no more,  
For not heeding God's voice, closing the door.

"Nations will come," fierce and unknown,  
Eat your fields, leave nothing to own.

Walls besieged, trust in vain,  
In hunger and siege, you'll eat flesh in pain.

Tender man turns, eyes to evil gaze,  
No food to share, in distressful days.

Delicate woman, her heart goes cold,  
In hunger's grip, even love grows old.

If you disregard the words in this book,  
Plagues will follow, as fear's hook.

Diseases return, Egypt's dread,  
Till you're few, stars dimmed and fled.

Scattered wide, earth's ends to roam,  
Serve strange gods, far from home.

No rest found, heart in constant dread,  
Morning to night, wish for what's ahead.

Return to Egypt, by sea's dark route,  
Offer yourself, but no one's about.
