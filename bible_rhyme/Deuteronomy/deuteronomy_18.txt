The Levites, the priests, got no land to claim,  
Instead, they serve Yahweh and honor His name.  

No land to own, no inheritance to share,  
"Yahweh is your portion," that's the care.  

From sacrifices, they get their due,  
The shoulder, cheeks, inner parts, all for the crew.  

The first fruits, wine, oil, and sheep's fleece,  
Given to them, so their service won't cease.  

Yahweh chose them, out of all tribes,  
To minister forever, they keep the vibes.  

If a Levite comes with his heart full of desire,  
To Yahweh's chosen place, he'll never tire.  

He serves like his brothers, in Yahweh's name,  
Receiving portions, no need for shame.  

When you enter the land Yahweh gives to you,  
Avoid the abominations, stay pure and true.  

No passing kids through fire, no magic schemes,  
No diviners, sorcerers, or enchanted dreams.  

For those practices Yahweh detests,  
It's why those nations are dispossessed.  

"You shall be blameless," Yahweh commands,  
No listening to sorcerers in these lands.  

A prophet will rise from among your kin,  
Listen to him as he speaks within.  

Just like in Horeb when the people cried,  
"Don't let us hear Yahweh's voice," they sighed.  

Yahweh agreed, "They've spoken right,  
I'll raise a prophet to give them light."  

"My words in his mouth, he'll speak them all,  
If they ignore, it's their own fall."  

But if a prophet presumes, makes up lies,  
"That prophet shall die," Yahweh replies.  

When a prophet speaks, watch what comes true,  
If it doesn't happen, his word's not from Yahweh's view.  

Don't fear him, don't be misled,  
His words are false, like a book unread.
