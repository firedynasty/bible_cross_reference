When there's beef and they head to court,  
The judges speak, give their report.  
The good gets praise, the bad gets shame,  
If he's guilty, stripes mark his name.  

No more than forty, that's the rule,  
Don't go over, don't be cruel.  
Just let him learn, but not degrade,  
He's still your brother, let respect be paid.  

Don't muzzle the ox when it's working hard,  
Let it eat, don't disregard.  
If brothers live and one brother's gone,  
His widow's not left to mourn alone.  

The brother steps up, takes her hand,  
Raises a child to honor his man.  
But if he won't, she speaks her mind,  
"He's left his brother's name behind."  

Elders call him, hear his plea,  
If he insists, "I won't agree."  
She removes his sandal, spits in his face,  
A sign he's failed his brother's place.  

Known as the man with no shoe to claim,  
The one who forgot his brother's name.  

If a wife intervenes in a brawl,  
Grabs what's private, heed the call.  
Her hand is lost, don't show pity,  
Harsh but true in this ancient city.  

Keep your scales and measures right,  
Honest weights in the day's light.  
Yahweh sees all deceitful ways,  
For just weights, you'll live long days.  

Remember Amalek’s sneak attack,  
Hit the weak ones at the back.  
When rest comes from every foe,  
Erase Amalek, let their memory go.  

Inherit the land Yahweh assigns,  
Don't forget, keep this in your minds.
