Listen up, here's the command, statutes that stand,  
Yahweh told us, follow them, in this promised land.  

Fear Yahweh, keep His word, every single day,  
You, your sons, and their sons, in every way.  

Hear, Israel, do it well, so you might grow,  
In a place with milk and honey flow.  

Yahweh's our God, only one in the sky,  
Love Him with your heart, soul, and every try.  

These words today, keep them close to your chest,  
Teach your kids when you move, when you rest.  

Bind them on your hand, keep them in your sight,  
Write them on your doors, gates shining bright.  

Yahweh brings you to cities you didn't build,  
Houses filled with goods, your hunger filled.  

Be careful, don't forget the God who saved,  
Out of Egypt's land, where you were enslaved.  

Fear Yahweh, serve Him, swear by His name,  
Don't chase other gods, playing a dangerous game.  

Yahweh's jealous, His anger's a flame,  
Don't tempt Him like Massah, don't bring Him shame.  

Keep His commandments, do what's right in His view,  
So you thrive in the land, as He promised to you.  

When your son asks, "What do these rules mean?"  
Tell him the story, of what we've seen.  

"We were slaves in Egypt, under Pharaoh's might,  
Yahweh freed us, with wonders and signs in sight."  

"He led us here, to a land He swore,  
A place of promise, forevermore."  

"Yahweh's commands keep us well and alive,  
Righteousness ours, as we continue to strive."
