Moses spoke to Israel, out in the wild,  
Beyond the Jordan, where the desert's not mild.  

Eleven days from Horeb, the journey was made,  
In the fortieth year, all commandments conveyed.  

He hit Sihon and Og, kings who fell,  
In Moab, Moses had the law to tell.  

God said, "You’ve stayed long at this mount,  
Turn, take your journey, make every step count.  

To the Amorites' hills, to every land,  
Go, take what I promised, as I planned.  

I can't bear your burdens all alone,  
God's multiplied you, like stars you’ve grown."  

Moses chose leaders, wise and sound,  
Judged with fairness, the truth they found.  

"Don't fear man," Moses spoke clear,  
"God's judgment reigns, bring cases near."  

Through the wilderness, they traveled far,  
Reached Kadesh Barnea, under God's star.  

"God's given this land, go, don’t be dismayed,"  
Scouts sent ahead, the promised land surveyed.  

But fear took hold, hearts turned cold,  
"Giants await in cities bold."  

Moses said, "Don't be afraid,  
God fights for you, He’s got you made."  

Yet disbelief lingered, hearts of stone,  
God guided by fire, led alone.  

God's anger rose, the promise delayed,  
"Only Caleb and Joshua," He said, "will be repaid."  

"Your children will enter, though you disobeyed,  
Turn back to the desert," Moses conveyed.  

They tried to fight, weapons in hand,  
God said, "Don’t go, I’ll not stand."  

But they went, disregarding the voice,  
The Amorites chased them, left no choice.  

Weeping came, but Yahweh didn’t hear,  
Stayed in Kadesh, the lesson was clear.
