Listen up, Israel, you're crossing the line,  
The Jordan's waiting, it's your time to shine.  
Nations bigger and badder, cities touch the sky,  
The Anakim are giants, and they make you ask why.

But know this today, Yahweh's blazing ahead,  
A devouring fire, their end's what He said.  
"Drive them out, make them perish quick,"  
Yahweh's words are sharp, they cut like a stick.

Don't think it's your deeds, or your heart so pure,  
It's the nations' wickedness, that's for sure.  
Not your righteousness, but His promise stands,  
To Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, He expands.

You're a stiff-necked crowd, remember the past,  
Provoked Him to anger, from first to last.  
In Horeb, you pushed Him, His wrath did ignite,  
Forty days on that mountain, day turned to night.

Stone tablets from Yahweh, with His finger wrote,  
Words from the fire, the assembly took note.  
Forty days passed, tablets placed in my hold,  
Then He said, "Arise, your people are bold."

"They've turned from My way, made an image of gold,"  
Yahweh saw the stiff-necked, rebellious and cold.  
"Leave me be," He said, "let destruction unfold,  
A mightier nation from you I'll mold."

Down the mountain I came, fire all around,  
With the covenant tablets, I touched the ground.  
Saw the calf you made, turned from Yahweh's way,  
Broke the tablets before you, anger on display.

Fell before Yahweh, forty nights and days,  
No food or water, in repentance I stayed.  
Feared His anger, but He listened again,  
Prayed for Aaron too, despite all the sin.

Took your golden calf, burned it to dust,  
Into the brook it went, a symbol of mistrust.  
At Taberah, Massah, wrath was provoked,  
Time and again, His commands you revoked.

From Kadesh Barnea, "Go possess the land,"  
But rebellion took hold, ignored His command.  
Since I knew you, rebellious you've been,  
On my face, pleading, for Yahweh to relent.

"Lord Yahweh," I prayed, "don't destroy Your own,  
Your redeemed ones, from Egypt they've grown.  
Remember Your servants, Abraham's kin,  
Don't see their stubbornness, their rebellious sin."

Lest the nations mock, "Yahweh couldn't stand,  
To bring them to promise, or hated the land."  
Yet they are Yours, Your power brought them far,  
By Your outstretched arm, beneath the guiding star.
