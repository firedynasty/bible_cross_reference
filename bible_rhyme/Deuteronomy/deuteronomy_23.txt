Don't step into Yahweh's crew, if you've been crushed or cut,  
The forbidden union's kids, in the tenth gen, still shut,  

No Ammonites, no Moabites, in Yahweh's place they won't belong,  
For bread or water they didn't bring, hired Balaam to do you wrong,  

But God flipped the script, turned Balaam's curse into a blessing,  
'Cause God loved you, that's the lesson,  

"Don't seek their peace, don't chase their gain forever,"  
But Edomite's your kin, and Egyptian's where you lived, so don't sever,  

Their third-gen kids can join the assembly crew,  
But when at war, keep yourself clean, obey what's true,  

If by night unclean you become, step outside and wait,  
Bathe at evening, come back when the sun's late,  

Outside the camp, make sure you dig a hole,  
Cover what you leave, keep the camp whole,  

For God walks among you, delivering and protecting,  
Keep it holy, or he'll be rejecting,  

Don't return an escaped servant to his master's land,  
Let him choose his home, welcome him with open hand,  

No daughters or sons in Israel shall prostitute,  
Don't bring their wages to God, that's absolute,  

Lend to your brother without interest, keep it fair,  
Charge a foreigner, but let the brother's burden be air,  

When you vow to God, don't you lag or slack,  
Fulfill your promise, or sin will attack,  

But if silent you stay, no sin in your way,  
Keep your word, do as you say,  

In a neighbor's vineyard, eat grapes at your leisure,  
But no basket, just hand for your pleasure,  

In their fields, pluck grain by hand, no sickle touch,  
Respect what's theirs, don't take too much.
