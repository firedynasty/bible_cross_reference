Moses stood and blessed the crowd, his words were strong, his voice loud.  
"Yahweh came from Sinai's peak, from Seir He rose, His glory did speak.  

From Mount Paran He shone bright, ten thousand holy ones in His sight.  
At His right, a fiery law He gave, to His people, the ones He'd save.  

He loves them all, they're in His hand, they sit at His feet, they understand.  
Moses gave the law to keep, an inheritance for Jacob deep.  

Reuben's tribe, let them survive, never let their numbers dive.  
For Judah, he called on Yahweh’s might, to bring him home, to win each fight.  

"Levi," he said, "has sacred stones, proven in trials, they've held their own.  
Teach the laws, the incense burn, bless their skills, for justice yearn."  

Benjamin dwells under Yahweh's care, safe and sound in a love so rare.  
Joseph's land, blessed with earth’s best, mountains and fruits, he's truly blessed.  

Zebulun and Issachar, rejoice and call, gather the people, offer it all.  
Gad's a lioness, he takes his claim, righteousness in Yahweh's name.  

Dan, a cub, leaps fierce and free, Naphtali's favor, full as can be.  
Asher's strength like iron bars, blessed with children and under the stars.  

No one like God who rides the skies, beneath His arms, His people rise.  
Israel safe, with grain and wine, Yahweh saves, His help divine.  

Happy is Israel, saved by Yahweh's might, tread on high places, His shield is right."
