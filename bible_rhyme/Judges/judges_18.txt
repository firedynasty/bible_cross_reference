In the days when no king led, Danites sought land to tread,  
Inheritance elusive still, so they sent heroes with skill.  

Five men from Zorah set to roam, to spy and claim a home,  
"Go explore," their kin said loud, till they reached Micah's crowd.  

They knew a voice, a Levite keen, asked, "Who brought you to this scene?"  
"I'm a priest for hire," he said, "Micah's house is where I'm led."  

"Consult God for us," they pled, "We seek success ahead."  
"Go in peace," the priest replied, "Yahweh's favor's on your side."  

To Laish these five men went, found a land without torment,  
Safe, secure, no ruler's sway, far from Sidon, lone to stay.  

Back they rushed to brothers bold, "The land is good as gold.  
Arise, no time to waste, it's wide, and ours to take with pride."  

The Danites armed and ready, marched on, six hundred steady,  
Camped in Judah for a night, then onward, hearts alight.  

"Do you know in Micah's place, lie treasures, idols, and grace?"  
Five men whispered this clue, "Consider what you must now do."  

To Micah's house they came once more, with weapons at the door,  
Took the ephod, teraphim, images carved and grim.  

"What are you doing?" the priest implored,  
"Join us," they said, "and be adored.  

"Hold your peace, come be our guide, a priest for tribe and kin abide."  
The priest agreed, glad with choice, took the treasures, joined their voice.  

They took off with treasures in tow, leaving Micah in sorrow.  
Micah rallied but couldn't win, against Dan's might he could not begin.  

"You stole my gods and priest," he cried, "What’s left for me inside?"  
Danites warned, "Hold your tongue, or meet angry men among."  

Realizing their strength too grand, Micah returned to his own land.  
To Laish went Dan's fierce band, took the city with sword in hand.  

They burned it down, no rescue came, built anew and changed its name.  
Named it Dan for lineage's pride, once Laish, now their side.  

They set up Micah's image high, with priests to lead and not defy,  
Till Shiloh's house held God's might, the image stood in Dan’s sight.
