Jephthah the Gileadite, a mighty man of fame,  
Born to a prostitute, but he made his own name.  
Brothers pushed him out, said, "You can't stay,  
You're not one of us, go away today."  

He fled to Tob, gathered outlaws, a crew,  
Living on the edge was all they knew.  
Then Ammon came to war, Gilead was in need,  
The elders called for Jephthah to take the lead.  

"Be our chief, fight Ammon, take the throne,"  
He answered, "You drove me out, left me alone.  
Why call me now when trouble's near?  
Do you think I'll just volunteer?"  

But they swore, "Yahweh is witness, it's true,  
Lead us in battle, and we'll follow through."  
So Jephthah took the charge, made a vow,  
Before Yahweh in Mizpah, he spoke it loud.  

He sent word to Ammon's king, "Why this fight?  
Claiming land from Egypt's flight isn't right."  
Ammon's king replied, "Return what you stole,  
Give back the land, make us whole."  

Jephthah countered, "Israel took not your land,  
Passed through wilderness by Yahweh's hand."  
He recounted the history, laid it plain,  
"Your gods didn't help, but Yahweh reigns."  

With Yahweh's Spirit, Jephthah crossed over,  
Made his vow, hoping to discover,  
"If Ammon's delivered, I'll give what's first seen,  
From my house door, be it clean."  

He fought Ammon with God's aid and power,  
From Aroer to Minnith, they fell by the hour.  
Returning home, his daughter came out in dance,  
His only child, his heart felt a lance.  

Tore his clothes, cried, "Daughter, my vow,  
Troubles me now, but I can't back down."  
She answered, "Father, do as you must,  
Yahweh's avenged us, in Him we trust."  

Two months she mourned on the mountainside,  
With her friends, in grief did she bide.  
She returned, and the vow took its course,  
Her life given, a deep, solemn force.  

And so it was, Israel's daughters each year,  
Honored Jephthah's daughter with a tear,  
Remembered the tale of courage and plight,  
Four days they sang, her memory in light.
