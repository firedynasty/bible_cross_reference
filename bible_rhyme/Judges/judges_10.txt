After Abimelech, Tola arose, Issachar's son,  
In Shamir of Ephraim, his work was done.  

For twenty-three years, he judged and then passed,  
In Shamir, he was buried, his legacy cast.  

Next came Jair, Gilead's pride and joy,  
Judged for twenty-two years, a righteous envoy.  

Thirty sons on donkey colts, thirty cities they claim,  
Havvoth Jair, their land, now known by that name.  

Jair's life ended, Kamon his rest,  
But Israel's troubles were far from addressed.  

They turned to Baals, Ashtaroth, gods galore,  
From Moab to Ammon, they worshipped more.  

Yahweh's anger burned hot, and He let them fall,  
Philistines and Ammonites answered the call.  

Eighteen years of oppression, Israel's plight,  
Across Jordan, Judah, and Benjamin, a constant fight.  

In distress, they cried out, "Oh Yahweh, we see,  
We've sinned, and now we plea!"  

Yahweh replied, "Didn't I save you before?  
From Egypt, Ammon, Philistines, and more?  

Yet you turned, left Me for false gods' charms,  
Cry to them now, let them hold you in their arms!"  

Israel cried, "We've sinned, do as you deem fit,  
But save us now, don't let our hope split."  

They cast away idols, turned back to the Lord,  
Yahweh's heart ached, His love was restored.  

Ammon gathered in Gilead, ready for war,  
Israel assembled, seeking a leader to restore.  

The princes asked, "Who'll fight this fight?  
Lead us to victory, bring us to the light."
