These nations were left for Israel's test,  
To teach them war, not to let them rest.  
Philistines' five lords, Canaanites nearby,  
Sidonians, Hivites on Lebanon high.

God was watching to see if they'd obey,  
Commandments from Moses, would they stay?  
Israel lived 'mongst Canaan's tribes,  
Taking daughters, joining their vibes.

They served false gods, forgot the One,  
Yahweh's anger burned bright like the sun.  
Sold to Cushan Rishathaim's might,  
Eight long years, lost in the night.

Israel cried out, and God sent a man,  
Othniel rose with Yahweh's plan.  
Spirit upon him, he went to fight,  
Prevailed against Cushan, brought back the light.

Forty years of peace, then Othniel slipped away,  
Israel again did wrong, went astray.  
Eglon of Moab, strengthened by God,  
Struck Israel, took lands they trod.

Eighteen years beneath Moab's hand,  
Cried to Yahweh, He'd the plan.  
Ehud the lefty, son of Gera, sent to pay,  
With secret sword hidden away.

A message for Eglon, secrets to share,  
In the cool upper room, in Eglon's lair.  
Ehud's left hand, swift and sly,  
Plunged the sword deep, Eglon would die.

Locked the doors, Ehud slipped out fast,  
While servants outside, waiting at last.  
Opened the door, Eglon on the floor,  
Ehud had gone, they saw no more.

Trumpet blew in Ephraim's hills,  
Israel rose, with renewed wills.  
“Follow me,” Ehud called, “Moab's ours to take,”  
They struck ten thousand, no escape to make.

Moab was subdued, Israel's hand was strong,  
Eighty years of rest before the next wrong.  
Shamgar came, with an ox goad in hand,  
Six hundred Philistines fell, he took his stand.
