Israel gathered, a mighty scene, from Dan to Beersheba, all keen,  
In Mizpah they met, standing as one, four hundred thousand strong with sword drawn.

Benjamin heard of Israel's rise, asking, “What caused this despise?”  
The Levite spoke, his voice did quake, “In Gibeah, my concubine they did take.”

“They aimed to kill, surrounded our place, they harmed her and left disgrace.  
I sent her parts through Israel's land, a call for justice—take a stand!”

Israel vowed as one to fight, “We won't return home 'til we make it right.  
Against Gibeah we'll cast the lot, ten men per hundred to gather a plot.”

They sought Benjamin, demanded the men, “Give up the wicked, make peace again!”  
But Benjamin stood, refusing the plea, they prepared for battle, fierce as can be.

Israel's swords four hundred thousand in hand, Benjamin's twenty-six thousand did stand.  
“Who fights first?” they asked the sky, “Judah,” Yahweh answered, “let them try.”

Israel rose at dawn's first light, to face Benjamin, prepared for the fight.  
Gibeah's men did fierce attack, twenty-two thousand Israelites fell back.

Israel's tears flowed, they cried to the Lord, “Shall we fight again?” And He gave the word.  
They faced Benjamin, again they did meet, but eighteen thousand more faced defeat.

Then Israel fasted, wept in despair, and offered sacrifices with heartfelt prayer.  
“Shall we continue, or give up the fight?” “Go on,” said Yahweh, “I'll give you the night.”

With ambush set, they moved with stealth, against Gibeah, they crept with health.  
Benjamin struck, thinking they'd won, but Israel's plan had just begun.

The ambush rose, smoke filled the air, Benjamin turned and saw despair.  
With Israel's might, Benjamin's fall, twenty-five thousand in total, that was all.

Now Benjamin fled, but Israel pursued, surrounding them, their strength renewed.  
Eighteen thousand fell, valor in vain, the rest fled, seeking shelter from pain.

To the rock of Rimmon, six hundred fled, while their cities burned, Israel's anger spread.  
In fire and fury, they left no stone, bringing justice, their message shown.
