In the hills of Ephraim lived a man named Micah,  
He told his mom, "I took your silver, now let's make it right, yah."  

His mom blessed him, "May Yahweh's favor come,"  
"I'll give the silver back, now let's get something done."  

She dedicated the silver with a plan in her head,  
For a carved image and molten, she said.  

Micah returned the cash, and she took a slice,  
Gave it to a silversmith, made idols nice.  

Micah's house of gods was filling fast,  
He crafted an ephod, built a worship that would last.  

No king in Israel, folks did what they pleased,  
Everyone living their life with ease.  

From Bethlehem Judah, came a Levite young and bold,  
Traveling to find a place to unfold.  

Micah asked, "From where do you come?"  
The Levite replied, "Bethlehem Judah's my home."  

Micah said, "Stay with me, be my priest, wear my cloth,  
Ten silver pieces yearly, food and clothing, no froth."  

The Levite agreed, content in his spot,  
Like a son to Micah, loving what he got.  

Micah made it official, the Levite his guide,  
In Micah's house, the priest would abide.  

Then Micah declared with hope so loud,  
"Yahweh's blessings are guaranteed; with a Levite, I'm proud."
