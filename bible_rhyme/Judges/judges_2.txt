An angel came from Gilgal to Bochim's gate,  
Said, "I brought you out of Egypt, to this land I create.  
I swore to your fathers, I'll keep my word true,  
But you didn't listen, now what will you do?  

Tear down their altars, make no deal with the land,  
But you turned away, didn't follow my command.  
So I won't drive them out, they'll stay by your side,  
Their gods will trap you, snare you inside."  

The people wept and cried out loud,  
In Bochim, they wept in a mourning crowd.  
Joshua sent them to claim their space,  
They served Yahweh, in their rightful place.  

Joshua lived long, then he passed on,  
Buried in Ephraim, his legacy strong.  
The next generation didn't know Yahweh's might,  
They did evil, served Baals, turned from the light.  

Abandoned their God, followed idols around,  
Provoked Yahweh's wrath, His anger unbound.  
He delivered them to raiders, their enemies' gain,  
Wherever they went, they found only pain.  

Yahweh raised judges, to save and to lead,  
But they turned from the path, did not heed.  
With the judge, Yahweh saved them from foes,  
Yet they quickly forgot, and back to sin they chose.  

When the judge died, they deepened their vice,  
Worse than their fathers, they ignored God's advice.  
Yahweh's anger burned, His words were clear,  
"This nation transgressed, and refused to hear.  

I won't drive out the nations, they'll test your ways,  
To see if you'll walk like your fathers in my gaze."  
So Yahweh left the nations, a test in disguise,  
To see if Israel would follow, or fall for the lies.
