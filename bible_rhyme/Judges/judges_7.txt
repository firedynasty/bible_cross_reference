Gideon rose early, with his crew at the spring,  
Midian's camp up north, planning a swing.  
God said, "Too many with you, can't win like this,  
Or Israel might boast, their own strength they'll miss."

"Tell the fearful and trembling to retreat,  
Mount Gilead's their path, let them beat their feet."  
Twenty-two thousand turned back, ten thousand stayed,  
God spoke again, some would still be swayed.

"Take them to the water, I'll test them for you,  
Separate them out, see what they do."  
"Those who lap with their tongue, like a dog at play,  
Set them aside, they'll go your way."

Three hundred lapped, the rest knelt down,  
God said, "With these, you'll gain renown.  
Send the others home, keep just a few,  
Midian's yours with this loyal crew."

That night God whispered, "Go down to the camp,  
I've given it to you, don't let your faith damp."  
"If you're scared, take Purah, hear what they say,  
It'll strengthen your heart for the fray."

Gideon snuck with Purah, heard a dream unfold,  
A barley loaf rolled, struck a tent so bold.  
A fellow said, "That's Gideon's might,  
God's given Midian to him, they'll take flight."

Gideon worshiped, returned to his crowd,  
"Yahweh's granted us victory," he announced loud.  
He split them in three, trumpets in hand,  
Pitchers and torches, ready to stand.

"Watch me," said Gideon, "do as I do,  
When I blow my trumpet, you blow yours too."  
"Shout 'For Yahweh and Gideon!' make it ring,  
We'll surround the camp, fear we will bring."

They reached the camp at the middle watch,  
Broke pitchers, blew trumpets, the enemy they’d botch.  
"The sword of Yahweh and Gideon!" they cried,  
Midian trembled, their courage died.

Three hundred trumpets blew, chaos took hold,  
Yahweh turned swords on each other, the story told.  
Midian fled far, toward Zererah's land,  
Israel pursued with the upper hand.

Gideon called Ephraim, "Seize the waters wide,  
Chase Midian down, let none hide."  
Oreb and Zeeb, the princes they caught,  
At Oreb's rock and Zeeb's press, victory they brought.

Heads of the princes to Gideon they brought,  
Beyond the Jordan, God's victory wrought.
