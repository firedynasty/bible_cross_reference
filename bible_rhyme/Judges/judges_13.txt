Israel's children did evil, forty years in plight,  
Philistines ruled, held them tight in their sight.  

In Zorah's land, Manoah's wife was barren, childless,  
An angel appeared, brought news so timeless:  

"You're childless now, but a son you'll conceive,  
No wine, no unclean, let this be your creed.  

No razor shall touch, a Nazirite he'll be,  
To save Israel from Philistines, set them free."  

She ran to Manoah, her heart full of awe,  
"An angelic face, the message I saw."  

Manoah prayed, "O Lord, guide us again,  
Teach us, show us, for the child you ordain."  

God heard Manoah, the angel returned,  
In the field to the woman, as Manoah yearned.  

She dashed to her husband, "The man has appeared!"  
Manoah met the angel, his questions neared.  

"Are you the one?" "I am," came the reply,  
"Let your words come true, tell us why."  

The angel said, "Let her follow my command,  
No vine, no wine, let her understand."  

Manoah offered, "A meal, stay a while,"  
The angel declined, yet spoke with a smile.  

"Offer to Yahweh, not food for me,"  
Manoah, unknowing, agreed to the decree.  

"What's your name?" Manoah did ask,  
"Incomprehensible," replied with no mask.  

A goat was prepared, offered on the stone,  
The angel amazed them, rose in flame’s zone.  

Manoah watched, both fell to the ground,  
Realized Yahweh's angel, truth profound.  

"We'll surely die, we've seen God's face,"  
But his wife assured, "We've found grace."  

A son was born, Samson his name,  
He grew strong, Yahweh's Spirit aflame.  

Between Zorah and Eshtaol, Spirit moved,  
Samson's path of destiny, God's strength proved.
