Samson saw a girl in Timnah, a Philistine delight,  
Told his folks, "Get her for me, she feels so right."  
"Can't you find someone from our own line?"  
But Samson insisted, "She's the one, she's fine."  

They didn’t know it was Yahweh's plan,  
Philistines ruling over the land, man.  
Down to Timnah with parents in tow,  
A lion roared, but Samson stole the show.  

Spirit of Yahweh, mighty and bold,  
Tore the lion with hands, a story to be told.  
He kept it quiet, didn’t say a thing,  
Met the woman, his heart took wing.  

Returned later, found bees and honey inside,  
Took some, shared it, but the source he’d hide.  
Samson threw a feast, as young men do,  
Thirty companions joined the crew.  

He challenged them with a riddle to crack,  
"Answer right, get clothes, but if not, pay me back."  
"Out of the eater, food came to be,  
Out of the strong, came sweet as can be."  

Days passed, they couldn’t get it right,  
Pressed his wife, "Get the answer tonight."  
She wept and pleaded, wore him down,  
On the seventh day, he finally came around.  

She shared the secret, they solved the rhyme,  
"What’s sweeter than honey? Strong as a lion anytime?"  
"If you hadn't teamed with my heifer,  
You'd still be blind, see the weather."  

Yahweh's Spirit filled him, rage burned fast,  
He struck thirty men, took their goods, a fierce blast.  
His anger fierce, to his father's he went,  
But his wife was given to his friend, his trust spent.
