On that day, Deborah and Barak sang a song,  
Leaders rose in Israel, and the people came along.  
"Be blessed, Yahweh! Hear, you kings, you princes too,  
I’ll sing to Yahweh, God of Israel, it's true.  

When Yahweh marched from Seir, from Edom's field he came,  
Earth trembled, skies let loose rain, quaked mountains, just the same.  
In Shamgar's days, and Jael's too, roads were empty, bare,  
Travelers took hidden paths, rulers ceased to care.  

Until I, Deborah, arose, a mother in this land,  
They sought new gods, and war came near, no shield or spear at hand.  
My heart's with Israel's leaders, they offered up their might,  
Bless Yahweh, all you riders, speak of His righteous light.  

"Awake, awake, Deborah! Sing out your mighty song!  
Barak, arise, lead captives, son of Abinoam, be strong."  
A remnant of the nobles came, Yahweh against the great,  
From Ephraim and Benjamin, and Machir, they did relate.  

Issachar stood with Deborah, and Barak at their feet,  
Why did Reuben hesitate, Gilead beyond the creek?  
Dan stayed with ships, Asher still, by the sea they chose to dwell,  
Zebulun risked their lives, Naphtali as well.  

Kings of Canaan gathered arms, at Taanach, Megiddo fought,  
No silver gained, but stars aligned, Sisera they caught.  
River Kishon swept them off, an ancient river's might,  
"Curse Meroz," the angel said, "for not joining Yahweh's fight."  

Jael, blessed among women, in her tent she did prevail,  
He asked for water, milk she gave, then with courage and a nail,  
Tent peg in her hand, she struck Sisera through his head,  
At her feet he bowed, he fell, lay where he was dead.  

Through the window Sisera's mother cried out in vain,  
"Why delay his chariot wheels, why does he not return again?"  
Her wise ladies spoke, she answered herself with hope and fear,  
"Have they not found the plunder, each man with spoils near?"  

"So let all Yahweh's enemies perish, as the sun's strength will impart,  
But those who love Him rise like dawn." Forty years of peace did start.
