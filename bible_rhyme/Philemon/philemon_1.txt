Paul, in chains for Christ, with Tim by his side,  
To Philemon, Apphia, Archippus we ride.  

"Grace and peace," I say, from Father and Lord,  
I thank God for you, my prayers never ignored.  

I hear of your love, your faith so true,  
For Jesus and saints, all the good you do.  

May your faith's fellowship, in knowledge, grow strong,  
In Christ, every good thing, where we all belong.  

Joy and comfort, in your love, we find,  
Saints' hearts refreshed, you're one of a kind.  

I could command, but for love, I plead,  
An old prisoner for Jesus, in chains, indeed.  

I beg for my child, Onesimus by name,  
Once useless, now useful, he's no longer the same.  

I'm sending him back, my heart in his hue,  
He served me well, now he serves you too.  

I won't act without your say,  
So your goodness shines in your own way.  

Perhaps he was gone, for a moment or two,  
Now a brother forever, both in flesh and in view.  

If you count me a partner, welcome him home,  
If he owes you a debt, on my account it’s known.  

"I’ll repay," I write, with my very own hand,  
Though you owe me yourself, understand.  

Give me joy, my brother, in the Lord,  
Refresh my heart, let it be restored.  

I trust your obedience, more than I ask,  
Prepare me a room, for a future task.  

Epaphras greets, in chains like me,  
Mark, Aristarchus, Demas, Luke—family.  

The grace of our Lord Jesus be with your soul,  
Amen, my friend, may His spirit make you whole.
