Jesus spoke in parables, a story to unfold,  
"The Kingdom's like a king, with a feast to behold.  

Sent servants to call, those invited to dine,  
But nobody came, they ignored the sign.  

Sent more with a message, 'Dinner's prepared,  
Cattle and fatlings, a feast to be shared!'  

Some laughed it off, to their farms and trade,  
Others grabbed his servants, a violent raid.  

The king got angry, heard what they’d done,  
Sent his armies out, judgment begun.  

Said to his servants, 'The feast is ready,  
But those invited, weren't found steady.  

Go to the highways, invite all you see,  
The bad and the good, let them all be free.'  

The hall was filled, the guests gathered 'round,  
But one man stood, in the wrong garb found.  

'Friend, how'd you enter, no wedding clothes on?'  
Speechless he stood, as the king said, 'Be gone!'  

'Bind him and cast him, out where it's dark,  
Weeping and gnashing, where hope is stark.'  

Many are called, but few are chosen,  
The Pharisees plotted, their hearts were frozen.  

They sent disciples, with Herodians in tow,  
"Teacher, you're honest, tell us what you know.  

Lawful to pay taxes, to Caesar, or not?"  
Jesus saw their trap, knew what they sought.  

"Show me the coin," he asked with care,  
"Whose image is this?" They said, "Caesar's, fair."  

"Give to Caesar what’s his, to God what’s divine."  
They marveled at his wisdom, left him, resigned.  

Sadducees came, denying the rise,  
Spoke of seven brothers, a truth in disguise.  

"Whose wife in the rise, if each had her hand?"  
Jesus replied, "You misunderstand.  

In resurrection, no marriage ties,  
Like angels in heaven, no earthly guise.  

God spoke of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob's living,  
He's God of the living, not of the dead, he's giving."  

The crowd was amazed at what they'd heard,  
Pharisees gathered, planning their word.  

A lawyer stepped up, testing his might,  
"Greatest commandment, which one is right?"  

"Love the Lord with heart, soul, mind," Jesus said,  
"Love your neighbor, keep these two in your head.  

All the law and prophets hang on these,  
Love is the greatest, the heart's own keys."  

Jesus questioned, "The Christ's lineage, say?  
David's son or Lord, how do you portray?"  

"The Lord said to my Lord, take the throne's seat,  
Until enemies fall, mere footstools at your feet.”  

"If David calls him Lord, how's he his son?"  
Silence followed, no more questions to run.
