Jesus hit the seaside, crowds grew wide,  
He hopped in a boat, left the shore behind.  

"Listen up," He said, "here's the farmer's tale,  
Seeds scattered wide, hear how they prevail.  

Some fell by the road, birds swooped down,  
Others on rocks, scorched and browned.  

Thorns choked some, but others found the good ground,  
Yielding fruit, hundredfold all around."  

"Got ears? Then hear," He made it clear,  
Disciples asked, "Why parables, dear?"  

"It's for you to know Kingdom's mystery,  
They see but don't perceive, it's history.  

Isaiah spoke of hearts hard as stone,  
Eyes shut tight, left alone."  

"Blessed are your eyes, they see and hear,  
Prophets longed for these words, far and near.  

The farmer's tale, let me speak,  
Evil snatches where understanding's weak.  

Rocks are those who hear with joy, but face the heat,  
Persecution trips them, makes them retreat."  

"Thorns are life's worries, riches that sneak,  
Choking the word, making it weak.  

Good ground hears and understands,  
Bearing fruit, as the harvest expands."  

"The Kingdom's like good seeds in a field,  
Enemy's weeds revealed, concealed.  

Wait till harvest, let them grow,  
Reapers separate, the truth to show."  

"Kingdom's like mustard seed, small and slight,  
But grows to a tree, birds take flight.  

Like yeast hidden in the meal,  
Transforms the whole, that's the deal."  

Jesus dropped parables, mysteries to unfold,  
Prophets said it'd happen, stories bold.  

Disciples wanted more, weeds explained,  
Son of Man sows good, devil's seeds profane.  

Harvest's end, angels do the deed,  
Righteous shine, wicked weep indeed.  

Kingdom's treasure hidden, a man will sell,  
The pearl of great price, he knows well.  

A dragnet cast, fish gathered tight,  
Good kept in, bad thrown from sight."  

"Understand?" He asked, they replied, "Yes, Lord."  
Scribes in the Kingdom, treasures to afford.  

Jesus taught them, hometown amazed,  
"Isn't this the carpenter's son?" they crazed.  

Offense took hold, prophets scorned near,  
Mighty works few, unbelief was clear.
