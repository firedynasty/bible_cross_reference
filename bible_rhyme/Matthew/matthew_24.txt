Jesus left the temple, the disciples in awe,  
Showed Him the buildings, He set them to jaw.  

"You see these stones? They'll all fall down,"  
Not one will stand, not in this town.  

On Olives He sat, the questions came quick,  
"When's the end, Lord? What's the trick?"  

"Be careful," He said, "many will claim,  
To be the Christ, using My name.  

Wars and rumors, they'll come your way,  
But don't be troubled, it's not the last day.  

Nations rise, kingdoms clash,  
Famines, plagues, the earth will thrash.  

These are just the birth pains' start,  
They'll deliver you up, tear you apart.  

Hated for My name, betrayed by friends,  
False prophets will rise, causing bends.  

Iniquity grows, love turns cold,  
But endurance saves, the brave and bold.  

The Kingdom's Good News will be declared,  
Then the end will come, be prepared.  

"When you see the desolation, standing tall,  
Run to the mountains, don't stall.  

Don't fetch your things, don't look back,  
Woe to the mothers, on this track.  

Pray it's not winter, not Sabbath day,  
For great suffering is on the way.  

"If days weren't shortened, none would survive,  
But for the chosen, hope will revive.  

False Christs, prophets, they'll appear,  
But don't believe, keep your vision clear.  

"As lightning flashes, east to west,  
So comes the Son, in glory dressed.  

Sun will darken, moon won't shine,  
Stars will fall, heavens' decline.  

Then the sign, the tribes will mourn,  
Son of Man, in clouds adorned.  

With trumpets sound, angels will fly,  
Gather the chosen, from sky to sky.  

"Learn from the fig, tender leaves show,  
Summer's near, soon to know.  

This generation sees these things,  
My words stand, though heaven rings.  

No one knows the hour, not even the skies,  
Only the Father, where mystery lies.  

"As in Noah's days, unaware they played,  
Till floodwaters rose, and swept all away.  

Two in the field, one will be gone,  
Grinding at the mill, one's move on.  

"Watch, for you know not when,  
The Lord will come, be vigilant then.  

If the master knew the thief's timing,  
He'd guard his home, no breaking, no climbing.  

"Be ready, in an hour not seen,  
The Son will come, like a dream.  

"Who's faithful, wise, serving the rest?  
Blessed is he, by the Lord, impressed.  

Set over all, all possessions in hand,  
But beware the evil, who misunderstand.  

Lord comes when least expected, in stealth,  
And for the wicked, there's loss of health.  

Weeping and gnashing, is where they'll find,  
The place of hypocrites, left behind."
