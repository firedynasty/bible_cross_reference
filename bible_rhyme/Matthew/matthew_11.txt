Jesus sent the twelve, then hit the road,  
Teaching and preaching, His message flowed.  

John in prison heard of Christ's great deeds,  
Sent two disciples to question His creeds.  

"Are you the One, or another's to come?"  
Jesus replied, "See what's been done:  

The blind now see, the lame stride tall,  
Lepers cleansed, the deaf hear the call,  

The dead rise up, the poor hear good news,  
Blessed is he who doesn’t refuse."  

Jesus turned to the crowd, questioning their quest,  
"In the wilderness, what did you expect?  

A reed in the wind, or a man dressed fine,  
Prophet you sought, and more you'll find.  

A messenger foretold, preparing the way,  
No one greater than John has come to stay.  

Yet even the least in Heaven's domain,  
Is greater than he, let that truth remain.  

This kingdom's been taken by force, it’s true,  
From John's days till now, it's breaking through.  

If you can hear and understand,  
John as Elijah, as was planned."  

He compared them to children, hard to please,  
Playing tunes, but none dance in the breeze.  

John abstained, and they claimed a demon,  
Christ partook, labeled glutton among men.  

Wisdom's kids will prove her right,  
For truth shines through in the darkest night.  

Then He denounced the unrepentant lands,  
For mighty works done by His hands.  

"Woe to you, Chorazin! Woe to Bethsaida!  
Tyre and Sidon would've long repented,  

In heaven’s heights, you'll find Capernaum fall,  
Like Sodom, judged beyond recall.  

Father, I thank you for hidden truths revealed,  
To babes, not wise, Your will is sealed.  

All that’s Mine, My Father gave,  
To know the Son, the Father paves.  

Come to Me, weary, burdened down,  
I’ll give you rest, release that frown.  

My yoke is easy, my burden light,  
Learn from Me, end your fight."
