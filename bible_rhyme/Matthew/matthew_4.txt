Out in the wilderness, the Spirit led him fast,  
Forty days, forty nights, hunger hit at last.  
The devil stepped up, said, "Son, make bread from stone,"  
But Jesus replied, “Man ain't fed on bread alone."  

Took him to the temple high, said, "Son, take a leap,  
Angels got your back, your feet won't meet the street."  
Jesus answered, “It's said, don’t test the Lord,  
God's in control, heed his holy word."  

On a mountain peak, the world spread wide,  
Devil said, "Bow to me, kingdoms'll be your pride."  
Jesus shot back, “Get behind me, Satan,  
Worship only God, his word I’m statin’."  

Devil left him be, angels came to serve,  
Jesus stood firm, never lost his nerve.  
He heard John's been caught, so he moved on to Galilee,  
Left Nazareth, Capernaum by the sea.  

Fulfilled Isaiah's words, in darkness light was seen,  
"Repent," he preached, "Heaven's kingdom's keen."  
Saw brothers by the sea, casting nets with might,  
"Come after me, fishers of men you'll be tonight."  

Left their nets, followed him without delay,  
Called more brothers, they came, no words to say.  
Jesus traveled Galilee, preaching Kingdom's news,  
Healing every sickness, no one did he refuse.  

From Galilee to Judea, the crowds they grew,  
The sick, the possessed, he healed them too.  
Great multitudes followed, from lands far and wide,  
His light and love spread, couldn't be denied.
