He called his crew, the twelve in line,  
Gave them power over spirits, made 'em divine.  

Simon called Peter, Andrew his kin,  
James and John, sons of Zebedee, let the mission begin.  

Philip and Bartholomew, Thomas in tow,  
Matthew the taxman, James with Alphaeus' flow.  

Lebbaeus, Thaddaeus, Simon the Zealot's pride,  
And Judas Iscariot, who later switched sides.  

Jesus sent them out with a command,  
"Don't hit the Gentiles or Samaritans land.  

Go to Israel's lost sheep, bring them the word,  
The Kingdom's near, let your voice be heard.  

Heal the sick, cleanse, and cast out the foe,  
Freely you've received, so freely let it go.  

No gold, no silver, no extra gear,  
Trust in your journey, you'll be provided here.  

In each town, find the worthy, stay while you can,  
Bless the house entering, that's part of the plan.  

If they reject you, shake off their dust,  
Judgment's gonna come, in God you trust.  

"Sheep among wolves, so wise up, see?  
Be gentle like doves, keep your mind free.  

Watch for the councils, they'll put you on trial,  
But don't stress your words, God's got your style.  

Families divided, brother against brother,  
Yet those who endure will find life like no other.  

When they chase you out, move to the next town,  
Before the Son of Man comes down.  

A student ain't above the teacher, that's the truth,  
If they diss the master, they'll probably diss the youth.  

But don't fear the hidden, it'll be revealed,  
What you hear in whispers, on rooftops, be healed.  

Fear not those who harm the flesh alone,  
Respect the one who holds soul and bone.  

Two sparrows for a coin, yet watched by God,  
Even your hairs are counted, isn't that odd?  

Confess me and I'll vouch for you up high,  
Deny me, and your chance with the Father, goodbye.  

"I didn't come for peace, but a sword to divide,  
Setting kin against kin, breaking family pride.  

Love me more than kin, take your cross, be bold,  
Lose your life for me, find treasures untold.  

Receive you, receive me, and Him above,  
A prophet's reward comes with that kind of love.  

Give a cup of water to those in need,  
Rewards in heaven for the righteous seed."
