When all the words were said and done,  
Jesus spoke, "In two days, the Passover's begun.  
The Son of Man's delivered up, crucified He'll be."  
The priests and elders plotted secretly.

In Caiaphas' court, they hatched their plan,  
"Not during the feast, or riots will span."  
In Bethany, at Simon's humble place,  
A woman with ointment poured on His face.

Disciples cried, "Why waste what's dear?"  
"Help the poor," was their collective sneer.  
Jesus replied, "Why trouble her deed?  
She prepped my burial, in this she did heed."

"Her act will echo through time and space,  
A memorial forever, in every place."  
Judas then schemed, "What will you give me?"  
Thirty silver pieces, his treachery fee.

"Prepare Passover," the disciples desired,  
"In the city," Jesus' words inspired.  
They followed His lead, prepared the feast,  
In the evening, He reclined with His priests.

"One will betray," Jesus spoke true,  
Sorrowful, each asked, "Is it I, who?"  
"The one who dips his hand with mine,"  
Judas asked, "Is it I?" Jesus' words shone like a sign.

Bread was broken, "Take, eat, it's my body,"  
The cup was shared, "For sins, this is my offering."  
"I'll not drink again till Kingdom comes," He said,  
A hymn they sang, to Olives, they were led.

"Tonight you'll stumble, the shepherd struck,"  
The flock will scatter, but hope's not stuck.  
Peter vowed, "I'll never fall,"  
Jesus said, "Three denials before the rooster's call."

In Gethsemane, Jesus prayed alone,  
"Father, let this cup pass, but let Your will be known."  
Disciples slept, eyes too weary,  
"Watch, the spirit's keen, but the flesh is dreary."

Judas arrived with a multitude near,  
"Betray with a kiss, this is clear."  
"Friend, why come?" Jesus asked on,  
Taken by force, His followers gone.

A sword was drawn, an ear struck down,  
"Put it away, or you'll wear death's crown."  
"Could call angels, legions of might,  
But prophecy fulfills on this night."

"Why come with swords as if I'm a thief?  
I taught in temples, arrests would've been brief."  
Led to Caiaphas, Peter watched from afar,  
The council sought lies, but truth was bar.

"Destroy this temple," false words did claim,  
Asked by the priest, "Are you the same?"  
Jesus said, "You said it, now you'll see,  
The Son of Man in power, clouds high and free."

Tears of garments, "Blasphemy!" they cried,  
"Worthy of death," their anger didn't hide.  
Spit and fists, He was struck and mocked,  
"Prophesy, Christ!" their cruel words rocked.

Peter outside, denied with a lie,  
"Galilean, you're one," he denied with a sigh.  
Three times, the rooster crowed,  
Remembered Jesus' words, tears flowed.
