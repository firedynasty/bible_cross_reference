From Abraham to Jesus, the line's divine,  
A heritage of kings, a sacred sign.  

Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Judah's crew,  
Judah and Tamar brought Perez and Zerah too.  

Hezron and Ram, lineage maintained,  
Amminadab, Nahshon, Salmon named.  

By Rahab, Salmon fathered Boaz bold,  
Boaz and Ruth, their story told.  

Obed to Jesse, then David the king,  
From David to Solomon, history would sing.  

Rehoboam, Abijah, Asa's reign,  
Jehoshaphat, Joram, in the line they remain.  

Uzziah and Jotham, Ahaz, the call,  
Hezekiah, Manasseh, Amon, stand tall.  

Josiah, Jechoniah at Babylon's door,  
Exile came, but the line restored.  

From Shealtiel to Zerubbabel's might,  
Abiud to Azor, the torch passed right.  

Zadok, Achim, Eliud stand,  
Eleazar, Matthan, a destined band.  

Jacob and Joseph, to Mary betrothed,  
From her, Jesus, prophecy clothed.  

Fourteen from Abraham to David's throne,  
Fourteen to exile, then Jesus shown.  

Mary, engaged but blessed by the Spirit's power,  
Joseph, righteous, faced a challenging hour.  

"Joseph, son of David," the angel proclaimed,  
"Fear not, take Mary, don't be ashamed.  

She'll bear a son, name him Jesus, the call,  
He'll save the people, redeem them all."  

Prophets spoke of a virgin's child,  
"Immanuel," God's presence compiled.  

Joseph woke, did as told, obeyed the command,  
Took Mary, awaited the promised land.  

He knew her not till the birth was done,  
And named him Jesus, the awaited one.
