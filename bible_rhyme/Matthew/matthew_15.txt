Pharisees and scribes came with questions to raise,  
"Why don't your disciples follow elder ways?"  
Jesus replied, "Traditions you hold so tight,  
But God's commandments, you twist to your delight.  

'Honor your parents,' that's God's command,  
Yet by your rules, that duty's banned.  
Isaiah spoke of you, called you hypocrite,  
Lips praise, hearts stray, in vain you submit."  

He called to the crowd, "Listen, understand,  
What goes in the mouth, that's not what's banned.  
What comes out defiles, from the heart it flows,  
Words reveal the soul, that's how it goes."  

Disciples whispered, "Pharisees took offense."  
Jesus said, "Every plant not planted will face suspense.  
Blind leading blind, both will hit the pit,  
Leave them alone, don't be caught in their writ."  

Peter asked, "Explain, we still don't see,"  
Jesus said, "It's not food, but heart's decree.  
Evil thoughts, false words, that's what defiles,  
Not eating with hands unwashed with styles."  

He traveled to Tyre, a Canaanite cried in pain,  
"Son of David, my daughter is demon's reign!"  
He stayed silent, but she begged with hope,  
"Help me, Lord, in my struggle to cope."  

"It's not right to give children's bread to dogs," He said,  
"Even dogs eat crumbs," was her faithful thread.  
"Great is your faith," Jesus then declared,  
Her daughter healed, her heart repaired.  

From there, by Galilee, Jesus took His place,  
Crowds brought the broken, seeking His grace.  
He healed them all, the multitude amazed,  
Mute spoke, blind saw, and God was praised.  

"Three days they've been here, with nothing to eat,"  
Jesus felt compassion, His love complete.  
The disciples wondered, "Where to find such bread?"  
Jesus asked, "How many loaves?" They said, "Seven," instead.  

He had them sit, took loaves and fish,  
Gave thanks, broke them, fulfilled their wish.  
All ate, were filled, leftovers in baskets,  
Four thousand men, not counting all the rest.  

Sent the crowds away, His work for now done,  
He sailed to Magdala, under the setting sun.
