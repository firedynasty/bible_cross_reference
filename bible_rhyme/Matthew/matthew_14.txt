Herod heard the news, his mind in fret,  
"John's back from the dead, that's the power he's met."  

He'd jailed John for Herodias, his brother's wife,  
John spoke out, "This ain't lawful, it's messing with life."  

Herod feared the crowd, they saw John as a prophet,  
But on his birthday, a dance made him forget.  

Herodias's girl danced, the king was pleased,  
Promised her an oath, anything she seized.  

Her mom said, "Ask for John's head on a plate."  
Herod grieved but kept his word, sealed John's fate.  

John was beheaded; his head was shown,  
Disciples took his body, buried him alone.  

They told Jesus, and he sought some space,  
Crowds followed him, seeking his grace.  

He healed the sick, his compassion flowed,  
Disciples worried, "It's late, send them down the road."  

Jesus said, "No need to let them go,  
Give them food, let's start the show."  

"Five loaves, two fish," they said in defeat,  
"Breathe life into these, let them eat."  

He blessed, he broke, his hands did share,  
Five thousand fed from baskets spare.  

He sent disciples on a boat to the other side,  
While he prayed alone, past eventide.  

Wind rose up, the sea was wild,  
Jesus walked on waves, calm and mild.  

"It's a ghost!" they cried, fear in their eyes,  
He said, "Cheer up, it's me, don't disguise."  

Peter said, "If it's you, let me walk too,"  
"Come," said Jesus, so Peter stepped true.  

Wind made him falter, fear took hold,  
"Lord, save me!" his courage cold.  

Jesus reached out, caught his hand in care,  
"Why doubt, little faith, I was there."  

Back in the boat, the storm laid down,  
They worshiped him, "Son of God, we crown."  

Reached Gennesaret, word spread wide,  
The sick were brought from far and wide.  

Touched his cloak, healing was complete,  
Faith in his presence made them whole, sweet.
