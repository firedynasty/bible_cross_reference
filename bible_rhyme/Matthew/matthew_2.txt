Born in Bethlehem, under Herod's reign,  
Wise men from the east made their claim.  

"Where's the King of the Jews?" they said,  
"We saw his star, it led ahead."  

Herod heard and felt the fear,  
All Jerusalem knew something was near.  

Chief priests, scribes, gathered, sought,  
"In Bethlehem," prophecy taught.  

Herod, in secret, called the wise,  
"Find the child, report back," he devised.  

The star led on, guiding bright,  
Till it stopped, shining its light.  

Joy overflowed, the wise men knelt,  
Mary with the child, the wonder felt.  

Gifts of gold, frankincense, myrrh they gave,  
In worship profound, their hearts did rave.  

Warned in a dream, they took a new track,  
Avoided Herod, never went back.  

An angel to Joseph, in dreams did say,  
"Rise, take the child, to Egypt away."  

By night they fled, under the stars,  
Till Herod's time passed, till all was ours.  

Herod's wrath, rage unchecked,  
Children of Bethlehem faced his wreck.  

Prophecy spoke of Rachel's tears,  
Weeping echoed through those years.  

When Herod died, Joseph dreamt again,  
"Return to Israel, free from pain."  

Back they went, but cautious, aware,  
To Galilee, Nazareth's care.  

Fulfillment came as prophets had seen,  
The child called Nazarene, where he’d been.
