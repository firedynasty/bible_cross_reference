Walking through the fields, disciples feeling famished,  
Plucking grains on Sabbath, Pharisees were astonished.  

“Look!” they said, “They break the law today.”  
Jesus answered, “David did it in his hungry day.  

He entered God’s house, ate the sacred bread,  
Only for priests, but he and his men were fed.  

Priests on Sabbath work, yet they’re guilt-free,  
And one greater than the temple you now see.  

Mercy’s what I seek, not mere sacrifice,  
For the Son of Man rules the Sabbath, precise."  

He moved on, to the synagogue he went,  
A man with a withered hand, their intent was bent.  

“Is it lawful to heal today?” they tried to trap,  
He replied, “Save a sheep on Sabbath, wouldn’t you act?  

A man’s worth more, doing good’s the way.”  
“Stretch your hand,” he said, healed him that day.  

Pharisees plotted, sought to take him down,  
Jesus stepped away, healing in every town.  

“Keep it quiet,” he said, fulfilling the prophet’s tone,  
“My Spirit’s on him, justice will be shown.  

He won’t shout loud in the crowded street,  
Till justice reigns, won’t accept defeat."  

A demon-possessed man, blind and mute,  
Jesus healed him, crowds did dispute.  

“Is this David’s son?” they began to talk,  
Pharisees claimed evil powers were his walk.  

Jesus knew their thoughts, made his stand,  
“A kingdom divided cannot withstand.  

If Satan's divided, his reign's at end,  
But by God’s Spirit, demons I send.  

Bind the strong man, take his goods away,  
With me or against me, where do you stay?”  

All sins forgiven, except against the Spirit,  
Speak against God’s breath, no pardon to inherit.  

Tree good or bad, fruit reveals the truth,  
Hearts overflow, let no one be aloof.  

Idle words matter, in judgment they count,  
Justified or condemned, your words will mount."  

“Show us a sign,” scribes and Pharisees plead,  
“Only Jonah’s sign,” said Jesus, planting seed.  

Three days in the whale, three nights too,  
Son of Man in earth, same will be true.  

Nineveh repented, will judge you today,  
Someone greater here, what more can I say?  

Queen of the South, wisdom she sought,  
Something greater here, don’t get caught.  

Unclean spirits wander, seeking a place,  
Return with more if the house stays in grace.  

Talking to the crowds, his family sought his ear,  
"Who is my family?" Jesus made it clear.  

“Who does my Father’s will, they’re kin to me,  
Brother, sister, mother—family, eternally.”
