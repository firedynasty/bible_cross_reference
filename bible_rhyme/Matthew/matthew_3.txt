In those days, John hit the scene, wild and free,  
Preaching in Judea, "Repent! The kingdom's near, you see!"  

He was the voice Isaiah foretold,  
In the wilderness, making paths bold.  

John rocked camel's hair with a leather belt,   
Locusts and honey, his meals, heartfelt.  

Crowds from all around came to see,  
Baptized in the Jordan, setting spirits free.  

But Pharisees and Sadducees came with a plan,  
John wasn't impressed, he called out their clan.  

"You brood of vipers! Who gave you the tip-off?  
Produce worthy fruit, or face the cut-off."  

"Don't lean on Abraham, thinking you're grand,  
God could raise children from stones in this land."  

"The ax is ready to chop the bad roots,  
Trees without good fruit get the boot!"  

"I baptize with water, but one greater will rise,  
With the Holy Spirit, fire in his eyes."  

"He'll winnow and cleanse, gather the wheat,  
But burn the chaff with fire oh so neat."  

Then Jesus from Galilee stepped into the scene,  
To be baptized by John in waters so clean.  

John hesitated, "I need your touch,  
Yet you come to me? This is too much!"  

Jesus replied, "Let it be so now,  
To fulfill righteousness, we'll take this vow."  

In the water, Jesus rose, heaven's gate swung wide,  
The Spirit, like a dove, came to abide.  

A voice from the heavens thundered strong,  
"This is my beloved Son, in Him I belong."
