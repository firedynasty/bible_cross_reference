When you give to the poor, keep it on the down low,  
Don't blast trumpets, let the good deeds quietly show.  

Hypocrites in the streets, seeking glory from men,  
They got their reward, but it's empty in the end.  

When you give, let your left hand be blind,  
Your Father sees in secret, His rewards you'll find.  

When you pray, don’t be like those who seek attention,  
In the corners, in the synagogues, craving man's mention.  

Go to your room, close the door tight,  
Pray to your Father who sees without sight.  

No endless babble, God's got you, He knows,  
Before you even ask, His wisdom flows.  

Pray like this: “Our Father in the sky,  
Keep Your name holy, Your Kingdom nearby.  

Your will be done, here as up there,  
Give us today what we need to share.  

Forgive our debts as we forgive too,  
Keep us from evil, in everything we do.  

For Yours is the Kingdom, power and glory,  
Forever and ever, that's the story.”  

Forgive others' wrongs, then you'll be free,  
Your Father'll forgive you, that's His decree.  

When you fast, don't show it with a gloomy face,  
Act normal, clean, full of His grace.  

Your Father sees the secret, He’ll give reward,  
Not the men who watch, with their own accord.  

Don't store treasures where moth and rust decay,  
In heaven, they last, they're there to stay.  

Where your treasure is, there’s your heart,  
So choose wisely, play your part.  

The body's lamp is the eye, let it shine bright,  
If it's evil, you'll be lost in the night.  

No man can serve two kings on the throne,  
Loving one, hating the other, you'll be torn.  

Don’t stress about life, what you’ll eat or wear,  
Life's more than these, so don’t despair.  

Look at the birds, they don’t worry or sow,  
Yet your Father feeds them, don’t you know?  

Worry can’t add a moment to your day,  
So trust in faith, let anxiety sway.  

Lilies in the fields, they grow without toil,  
Yet even Solomon can't match their royal.  

If God clothes the grass, here today, gone tomorrow,  
Won't He clothe you, dispel your sorrow?  

Seek first His Kingdom, righteousness in sight,  
Everything you need, He’ll provide in the light.  

Don't worry 'bout tomorrow, let it be,  
Each day has enough for you and me.
