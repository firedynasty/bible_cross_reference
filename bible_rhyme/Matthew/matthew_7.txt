"Don't judge others, or you'll be judged the same,  
With the measure you use, you'll play your own game.  

See the speck in your brother's eye? It's plain,  
But miss the beam in yours—that's insane.  

"Remove your own block," He said with a sigh,  
"Then help your brother, now clear in your eye."  

"Don't toss what's holy to dogs, don't be giving,  
Your pearls to pigs who'll trample and leave you grieving.  

Ask, and it's given, seek, and you'll find,  
Knock, and that door will open in kind.  

If your child needs bread, would you hand him a stone?  
If fish is his wish, would a serpent be thrown?  

If you, flawed beings, give gifts that are good,  
How much more will God, as a Father should.  

"Treat others the way you want to be treated,  
This is the law, the prophets completed.  

Enter the narrow gate, it's tight but it's right,  
The wide path leads to darkness, not light.  

Beware of false prophets, in disguise they may be,  
A wolf in sheep's clothing is not what you see.  

By their fruits, you'll know what they yield,  
Good trees bring life, corrupt trees are killed.  

Not everyone crying 'Lord' gets through heaven's gate,  
Only those who do God's will, their fate is great.  

On that day, many will plead,  
But I'll say, 'I never knew you, leave indeed.'  

"Those who hear and act are wise, like rock they stand,  
The floods, the winds won't move their land.  

But those who hear and don’t act, like sand, they fall,  
The storm takes them down, their ruin was the call."  

When Jesus spoke, the crowds were amazed,  
For He taught with authority, unlike scribes' old ways.
