Crowds gathered, mountains climbed, He took a seat,  
Disciples gathered 'round, ready for the treat.  

"Blessed are the poor in spirit, Kingdom of Heaven's theirs,  
Blessed are the mournful, comfort erases cares.  

Blessed are the gentle, they'll inherit the earth,  
Those hungry for righteousness? They'll find worth.  

Blessed are the merciful, mercy they'll obtain,  
Pure hearts will see God, His presence they'll gain.  

Peacemakers are God's kids, known far and wide,  
Persecuted for righteousness? Kingdom's on their side.  

Reproached and persecuted for my name, don't be sad,  
Rejoice, be glad, rewards in Heaven ain't half bad.  

You're the salt of the earth, don't lose your taste,  
Saltless cast out, trodden, a waste.  

You're the light of the world, city on a hill can't hide,  
Light the lamp on a stand, let it shine with pride.  

Let your light shine, show good works to men,  
Glorify your Father in Heaven, again and again.  

Don't think I came to destroy the law, no, I fulfill,  
Not one letter will pass, 'til all's accomplished, still.  

Break the least command? Least in Heaven, I say,  
Do and teach them all? Great you'll stay.  

Unless righteousness surpasses scribes' and Pharisees' call,  
No entry to the Kingdom, for you at all.  

You've heard it said, 'Don't murder, face the law's might,'  
But anger without cause brings judgment's light.  

Say 'Raca' to a brother? Council's near,  
Call him 'fool'? Fire of Gehenna, fear.  

If at the altar, you recall a brother's pain,  
Make peace first, then offer again.  

Agree with adversaries, quickly settle the score,  
Lest prison holds you, freedom no more.  

You've heard it said, 'Adultery, don't commit,'  
But lust in your heart? Guilty of it.  

If your eye offends, pluck it, better to lose a part,  
Than whole body's plunge into Gehenna's dark.  

Hand causing sin? Cut it, throw it away,  
Better one piece gone than all astray.  

Divorce with a decree, it's that you’ve heard,  
But I say immorality makes the word.  

False vows to ancient ones were said,  
But don't swear at all, keep your head.  

Heaven's God's throne, earth His footstool,  
Jerusalem's the King's city, keep it cool.  

Let your 'Yes' be 'Yes,' 'No' be 'No,'  
Anything else comes from the evil's flow.  

'An eye for an eye,' they say, justice tight,  
But turn the other cheek, don't fight.  

Give your cloak if sued for your coat,  
Go two miles when forced, that's the note.  

Give to those who ask, don't turn away,  
Borrowers receive, come what may.  

Love your neighbor, hate your enemy’s the tale,  
But love, bless, and pray, that's the trail.  

Sun rises and rain falls on good and bad,  
Love those who love you, reward's just a tad.  

Tax collectors greet friends, what's your chore?  
Be perfect as your Father, just that and more."
