Early dawn, the Mary's came to see,  
The tomb where Jesus was meant to be.  

Earth shook, angel from the sky,  
Rolled the stone and perched up high.  

Lightning flash, clothes white as snow,  
Guards fell like statues, fear on show.  

To the women, the angel spoke,  
“Don’t fear, Jesus' bonds are broke.  

He’s risen now, just as he said,  
Come and see where he once laid dead.  

Go tell his friends, he’s up and gone,  
To Galilee, where he'll move on.”  

With joy and fear, they ran fast,  
To share the news that came at last.  

On their path, Jesus appeared,  
“Rejoice!” he said, as they drew near.  

Held his feet in worship's might,  
“Don’t be afraid, share the light.  

Tell my brothers, Galilee’s the place,  
Where they'll see me, face to face.”  

Meanwhile, guards told tales untrue,  
Chiefs paid silver for their view.  

“Say disciples stole him away,  
While we slept at night, they’ll say.”  

The cash in hand, they spread the lie,  
The story lingers, never to die.  

Disciples in Galilee, mountains high,  
Bow to Jesus, yet some question why.  

“All power’s mine,” he declares aloud,  
“Heaven and earth, before this crowd.  

Go, make disciples, nations wide,  
Baptize them in the Triune tide.  

Teach them all I’ve shown and said,  
I’m with you till the ages spread.”  

With that promise, the story ends,  
Jesus' words, where hope begins.  
Amen.
