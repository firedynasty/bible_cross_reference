After six days, Jesus took the crew, Peter, James, and John's his crew,  
Up a high mountain, alone in view.  

Transformed in light, His face did blaze,  
Shining bright as the sun's fierce rays.  

Moses and Elijah, they appeared that day,  
Talking with Jesus, in a holy display.  

Peter said, “It’s good we're here, let's make three,  
Tents for You, Moses, and Elijah, let it be.”  

While he spoke, a cloud of brightness rolled,  
A voice declared from its midst and told,  

"This is my beloved Son, hear Him speak,  
In Him I'm pleased, His words you seek.”  

Terrified, the disciples fell to the ground,  
Faces down, in the fear they were found.  

Jesus touched them, His voice was kind,  
“Rise up, don’t fear, peace you’ll find.”  

Looking up, all alone was He,  
No one but Jesus, the vision to see.  

Down the mountain, with words profound,  
"Don’t speak of this till I'm risen from the ground.”  

“Why must Elijah come first?” they asked in thought,  
“Elijah’s come, but respect he got not,”  

“They did what they wanted, without delay,  
The Son of Man will suffer the same way.”  

Then they knew He spoke of John’s role,  
Understanding now filled their soul.  

A man approached, in the crowd below,  
Kneeling, he cried, “Lord, my son's in woe.  

Epileptic, he suffers, in fire and in lake,  
Your disciples tried, their hands did shake.”  

“Faithless generation!” Jesus did scold,  
“How long must I bear this, how long be bold?  

Bring him to me,” He commanded with grace,  
Jesus healed the boy, the demon displaced.  

The disciples asked, "Why couldn’t we succeed?"  
“Your unbelief, the faith you need.  

With mustard seed faith, mountains move in sight,  
But prayer and fasting bring this fight.”  

In Galilee, Jesus spoke again,  
"Son of Man will be taken by men,  

They’ll kill Him, but He’ll rise on day three.”  
Grief filled their hearts, heavy with this decree.  

In Capernaum, tax collectors came near,  
Asked Peter of Jesus, “Does He pay here?”  

“Yes, He does,” Peter replied, staying true,  
Jesus knew and asked, “What do you view?  

Kings take tribute from strangers, not their own,  
Thus, children are exempt, their freedom shown.  

But to avoid offense, go cast your line,  
A fish with a coin, a divine sign.  

Take it, pay our due, keep the peace anew.”  
In every act, wisdom shone through.
