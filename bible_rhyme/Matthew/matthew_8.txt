He stepped off the mountain, crowds at his back,  
A leper knelt, "Lord, please heal the track."  
Jesus reached out, "I want to make you clean,"  
Touched him, and leprosy was nowhere to be seen.  

"Tell no one, show the priest, offer your gift right,"  
As a testimony, let them see the light.  
In Capernaum, a centurion came in need,  
"My servant's in pain, can you perform the deed?"  

Jesus said, "I'll come and heal him now,"  
The centurion replied, "Just speak, make that vow.  
I'm under command, I know how it rolls,  
I say 'go' and they move, just like the scrolls."  

Jesus marveled, "Such faith, not found in this land,  
Many will come, but some will be banned."  
To the centurion, "Go, your faith made it true,"  
And the servant was healed, all fresh and new.  

At Peter's house, his wife’s mother lay still,  
He touched her hand, and fever had no will.  
She rose up quickly, served him on the spot,  
At night, many came, needing healing they sought.  

Demons cast out, sickness healed with his word,  
Fulfilling Isaiah’s prophecy that they heard.  
Seeing the crowds, he said, "We must depart,"  
A scribe approached, "Teacher, I’ll follow your heart."  

"The foxes have holes, birds nests to rest,  
But the Son of Man roams with no place to nest."  
Another said, "Let me bury my dead."  
"Follow me," Jesus said, "Let the dead bury the dead."  

They set sail, the sea roared loud and high,  
But Jesus slept, while the storm passed by.  
"Save us, Lord," they cried, in fear's embrace,  
"Why such little faith?" He rebuked the waves' race.  

The storm calmed, leaving marveled men in awe,  
"Even the wind and sea obey his law."  
In Gergesenes, two fierce souls met,  
Possessed by demons, a challenge they set.  

"What do you want, Son of God, at this hour?"  
The demons begged, "Send us to that swine tower."  
"Go!" he commanded, and into pigs they fled,  
Rushed down the cliff, where the waters spread.  

Witnesses ran, told the town all they'd seen,  
The city came out, begged Jesus to leave the scene.
