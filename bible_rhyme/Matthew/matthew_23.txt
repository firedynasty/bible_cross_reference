Jesus spoke to the crowd, set the scene right,  
"The scribes and Pharisees sit in Moses' seat, uptight.  

Do what they say, but not what they do,  
They lay heavy burdens, but don't help you.  

Their works are for show, their garments wide,  
They love the honor, the best seats provide.  

But don't call them 'Rabbi,' for one Teacher's true,  
One Father in heaven, and you're all crew.  

The greatest will serve, the humbled will rise,  
The exalted brought low, that's no surprise.  

"Woe to you, scribes, Pharisees, in disguise!  
You devour widows' homes, long prayers you devise.  

You lock up heaven, won't let others in,  
Make converts twice the son of sin.  

Blind guides swearing by gold, that's not the way,  
The temple's the greater, hear what I say.  

You blind fools, don't you understand?  
The altar, the gift, don't miss the command.  

Tithing mint and cumin, but justice you leave,  
Strain out the gnat, let the camel deceive.  

Clean the cup's outside, but the inside's a mess,  
Whited tombs, beauty hides distress.  

You build tombs for prophets your fathers slew,  
You claim you'd not join them, but that's not true.  

Serpents and vipers, judgment's no game,  
Righteous blood's on you, from Abel to Zechariah's name.  

Jerusalem, Jerusalem, killers of the sent,  
How I longed to gather you, but you never bent.  

Your house now desolate, till you proclaim,  
'Blessed is He who comes in the Lord's name!'"
