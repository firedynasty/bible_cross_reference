Yo, elder to Gaius, beloved in truth,  
I pray you prosper, in health and in youth.  

Joy fills my heart when the brothers testify,  
Your walk in the truth lifts me sky-high.  

No greater joy than seeing you stay true,  
Faithful in deeds, in all that you do.  

They speak of your love in the assembly crowd,  
Sending them forth, you make God proud.  

For the Name's sake, they journeyed far,  
Taking nothing, shining like a star.  

We join as workers, truth we defend,  
Together we stand, 'til the very end.  

But watch out for Diotrephes, his pride's the show,  
Rejecting our words, he puts on a low blow.  

If I come, I'll call him out, his deeds laid bare,  
Accusing with lies, he doesn't play fair.  

Don't imitate evil, chase the good instead,  
The good sees God, the evil's misled.  

Demetrius stands with truth in his call,  
Our testimony's real, known by all.  

Many words I could pen, but I choose my friend,  
To see face to face, tales to mend.  

Peace to you, as friends send their cheer,  
Greet each by name, in love sincere.
