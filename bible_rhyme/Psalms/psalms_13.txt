How long, Yahweh? Will you forget for good?  
Hide your face, like I knew you would?  

Sorrow in my soul, it's a daily grind,  
Enemies laughing, leaving me behind.  

"Look at me, Yahweh, answer my plea!"  
Give light to my eyes, don't let darkness see.  

My enemy's waiting, claiming the win,  
Rejoicing in my fall, their wicked grin.  

But I trust in your endless love, divine,  
My heart rejoices, your salvation's mine.  

I'll sing to Yahweh, for he's been kind,  
Good to my soul, peace in my mind.
