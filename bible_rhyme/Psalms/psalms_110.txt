"Sit at my right hand," Yahweh declares,  
"Enemies beneath your feet, in my care."

From Zion's heart, strength extends,  
Rule your foes, the power never ends.

Your people rise, willing and proud,  
In holy array, they stand unbowed.

Morning's birth, with youth's fresh dew,  
Strength renewed, His promise true.

Yahweh has sworn, won't change His mind,  
"Forever a priest, in Melchizedek you'll find."

The Lord's by your side, kings will fall,  
Wrathful day, His judgment calls.

Nations judged, and bodies piled high,  
The ruler crushed, beneath the sky.

He drinks from the brook, head held tall,  
In victory, He rises above it all.
