Great is Yahweh, praised high in the city's sight,  
In His holy mountain, where His glory shines bright.  

Beautiful and raised, the earth's joy to everyone,  
Mount Zion stands tall, the city of the Great One.  

God's our refuge, His strength never fails,  
The kings passed by, their courage pale.  

They saw and were stunned, dismayed and amazed,  
They fled in a hurry, their hearts all ablaze.  

Trembling gripped them, pain like birth pangs,  
The east wind blows, shattering ships with its bangs.  

As we've heard, so we've seen, God's city stands strong,  
He'll establish it forever, His love is lifelong.  

We've pondered Your kindness, Lord, in Your temple's heart,  
Your name and Your praise, to the world's every part.  

Mount Zion, rejoice! Judah's daughters, declare,  
God's judgments stand tall, His righteousness fair.  

Walk around Zion, count her towers with care,  
Notice her bulwarks, her palaces so rare.  

Tell the next generation, let them understand,  
This God is our God, forever He'll stand.  

He'll guide us always, till our final breath,  
Our eternal guide, even unto death.
