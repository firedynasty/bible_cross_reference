Give thanks to God, His Name is near,  
People speak of wonders, loud and clear.  
When the time is set, I'll judge with grace,  
The earth shakes, but I hold its place.  

To the proud I said, "Don't you boast!"  
To the wicked, "Raise no horn to toast."  
"Don't lift up high with a stubborn neck,"  
For none from the east, south, or west can check.  

God's the judge, He brings down and lifts,  
In His hand, the power shifts.  
Yahweh holds a cup with a foamy mix,  
The wicked drink deep, can't fix their fix.  

I'll declare forever, sing His praise,  
To Jacob's God, my voice I raise.  
Cut the horns of the wicked, end their spree,  
But the righteous rise, in victory.
