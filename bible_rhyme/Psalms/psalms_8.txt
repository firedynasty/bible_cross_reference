Yahweh, oh Lord, how majestic your name,  
Across the earth, your glory, it’s the same.  

From babes and infants, strength you make,  
To shut the mouths of enemies, make them quake.  

I gaze at your heavens, the work you've spun,  
The moon and stars, crafted one by one.  

Who is man that you think of his face?  
The son of man, in your caring embrace?  

A little lower than angels, you made him be,  
Crowned with glory and honor, wild and free.  

Ruler of works, the things you create,  
Under his feet, you've put them straight.  

Sheep and cattle, creatures of the land,  
Birds in the sky, by your command.  

Fish of the sea, paths wide and far,  
Under the heavens, vast as they are.  

Yahweh, oh Lord, your name we sing,  
Majestic in earth, you're the eternal King.
