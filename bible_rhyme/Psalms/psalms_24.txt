The earth is the Lord's, hear its fullness roar,  
The world and all who dwell on its shore.  
He founded it on seas, established on floods,  
Who climbs His hill? Who seeks His goods?  

With clean hands, pure heart, never lied,  
No falsehood, no deceit, no pride.  
Blessings from Yahweh, righteousness received,  
The God of salvation, in whom they believed.  

This generation seeks, faces turned to find,  
Even Jacob's line, hearts aligned.  
Lift your heads, you gates, rise high,  
Everlasting doors, let the King pass by.  

"Who is the King of glory?" they shout,  
Yahweh, strong and mighty, never doubt.  
Lift up your heads, gates, rise again,  
The King of glory will enter then.  

"Who is this King of glory?" you say,  
Yahweh of Armies, He leads the way!
