Yahweh, the judge with fire in His eyes,  
"Rise up!" He says, "the proud shall meet their demise."  

How long will the wicked reign with their might?  
They boast with their words, dance in the night.  

They break Your people, tear them down,  
Killing the widow, in sin they drown.  

"Yah won't see us," they foolishly claim,  
But God's eyes watch, He knows their game.  

He made the ear, won't He hear the cries?  
He formed the eye, sees through all lies.  

He disciplines nations, His wisdom's vast,  
Knows every thought, sees through the mask.  

Blessed is the one He teaches with care,  
Finding rest when wickedness is laid bare.  

"Who'll rise for me?" the righteous plead,  
Without Yahweh, silence would seed.  

"My foot slips!" but He catches me fast,  
His love comforts, my fears are past.  

Can wickedness stand where truth abides?  
In God's refuge, righteousness resides.  

Yahweh's a fortress, my rock so strong,  
He'll cut off the wicked, right their wrongs.  

Iniquity returns to those who defy,  
Yahweh, our God, will hear every cry.
