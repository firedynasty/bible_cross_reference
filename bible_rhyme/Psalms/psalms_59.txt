Enemies surround me, plotting in the night,  
God, lift me high, protect me, give them fright.  

From wicked schemes and bloodthirsty men,  
For no wrong I've done, they're here again.  

They gather, they wait, for my soul they crave,  
But I've done no wrong, Yahweh, I'm brave.  

"Rise up, my God, see their deceit,  
Punish the wicked, let justice be complete."  

Like dogs they howl when the evening falls,  
With mouths that spew, they prowl the city's walls.  

They think they're sly, but God, you laugh,  
Scoffing at nations, cutting their path.  

Strength is my watchword, my fortress tall,  
God's loving kindness, ensures I won't fall.  

"Don't slay them, lest my people forget,  
Scatter, bring them down, shield us from threat."  

Their mouths speak sin, their pride a snare,  
Caught in lies, let them beware.  

Consume their folly, let them face their end,  
Let all know God rules, from here to the earth's bend.  

At night they return, like dogs they roam,  
Hungry for food, homeless without a home.  

I'll sing of your strength, morning's first light,  
My refuge, my tower, in you I delight.  

To you, my strength, my praises will rise,  
God of my mercy, lifting my eyes.
