When trouble knocks, Yahweh's got your back,  
The God of Jacob lifts you up, keeps you on track.  

Help from the sanctuary, aid from Zion's peak,  
He remembers your offerings, hears you when you speak.  

He'll grant your heart’s desire, fulfill your plan,  
In salvation, we'll triumph, like a united clan.  

"May Yahweh grant your every request," we proclaim,  
In His name, we raise banners, proclaiming His fame.  

Now I know Yahweh saves His anointed with might,  
From His holy heaven, He answers with light.  

Some trust in chariots, others in horses they lean,  
But we trust in Yahweh, our God, unseen.  

They bow down, they fall, but we rise tall,  
"Save, Yahweh!" we cry, "Hear when we call!"
