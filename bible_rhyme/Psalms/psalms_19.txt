The heavens shout, "Glory!" up high and loud,  
The sky shows His handiwork, making us proud.  
Day speaks to day, night whispers to night,  
Each one sharing wisdom, a beautiful sight.  

No speech, no tongue, yet their voice rings clear,  
Traveling the world, for every ear to hear.  
For the sun, He sets a tent in the sky,  
Like a bridegroom, it rises, ready to fly.  

From one end of heaven, racing its track,  
Nothing hides from its heat, no turning back.  
God's law is perfect, restoring the soul,  
His truths make the simple wise, make us whole.  

His rules bring joy, pure light to our eyes,  
Forever they stand; in righteousness they rise.  
Desirable and sweet, more than gold and honey,  
Keeping them brings rewards, richer than money.  

"Who knows their faults?" I ask in my plea,  
"Forgive hidden errors, Lord, set me free."  
Guard me from pride, let sin not control,  
I'll be upright, blameless, whole in my soul.  

Let my words, my thoughts, please You, my defender,  
Yahweh, my rock, my eternal redeemer.
