Blessed be Yahweh, my rock so strong,  
Teaches my hands to war right where they belong.  
Loving kindness, fortress, high tower in the sky,  
Deliverer, shield, my refuge when I cry.  

"Who is man," says Yahweh, "that I care for his frame?  
His life is but a breath, like a shadow, he came."  
Part the heavens, oh Yahweh, come down with might,  
Touch the mountains, let them smoke, burn bright.  

Lightning strike and scatter them wide,  
Send your arrows, watch foes run and hide.  
Stretch your hand from above, rescue me,  
From the great waters, set me free.  

Foreign tongues speak deceit, their words lie,  
Their right hand's falsehood, reaching for the sky.  
"I'll sing a new song," I declare to my King,  
On a ten-stringed lyre, praises I bring.  

You give salvation to kings, David you save,  
Rescue me too, from the sword's deadly wave.  
Foreign hands deceitful, falsehood they boast,  
Deliverance I seek from this hostile coast.  

Sons like nurtured plants, daughters like thrones,  
Stores full of provision, wealth overflows.  
Sheep in thousands grace our fields,  
Oxen strong, heavy loads they wield.  

No breaking in, no cries in the street,  
Peace and joy where life’s complete.  
Happy are the people, in Yahweh they dwell,  
Blessings abound, hear the glad story we tell.
