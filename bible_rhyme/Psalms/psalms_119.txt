Blessed are those who walk the righteous way,  
Following Yahweh's law each day.  

Seek Him fully, keep it true,  
Do no wrong, His path pursue.  

"Obey my precepts," He commands,  
To walk steadfast, strong stands.  

"Oh, that my ways were true," I pray,  
"I'd never stray, I'd stay."  

Give thanks with a heart upright,  
Your judgments learned, a guiding light.  

"Don't forsake me," I plead sincere,  
Stay pure, follow His word dear.  

With heart and soul, I've sought His face,  
Hidden His word, I find my place.  

"Blessed are You," Yahweh, divine,  
Teach me statutes, make them mine.  

Declare His laws, my lips have done,  
His testimonies bring joy, none other won.  

Meditate, ponder on His way,  
Delight in His word, come what may.  

"Open my eyes," I ask to see,  
Wondrous things in Your law, set me free.  

A stranger here, His word I crave,  
Consume my soul, His path I pave.  

Proud are rebuked, I stay His course,  
Despite slander, His word's my source.  

Laid low, revive me, hear my plea,  
Teach me statutes, let me see.  

"My soul is weary," I confess,  
Strengthen me, grant me rest.  

Truth I've chosen, stay my guide,  
In His commandments, I abide.  

"Teach me, Yahweh," I vow to keep,  
With heart and soul, His path I sweep.  

Turn my heart, my eyes away,  
Toward His statutes, I will stay.  

Promise fulfilled, let disgrace pass,  
In His righteousness, my hope amass.  

Yahweh's kindness, let it come,  
Answer foes, His word my drum.  

"Don't snatch truth," I hope, I pray,  
Forever His law, I'll obey.  

Walk in liberty, sought His precepts wide,  
Even before kings, I'll not hide.  

"Remember your word," brings hope in night,  
His law my comfort, my heart's delight.  

"Yahweh is my portion," I proclaim,  
Obey His words, call on His name.  

My steps turned, His path I'll meet,  
Hasten to follow, swift my feet.  

Bind of wicked won't hold me down,  
Praise at midnight, His word my crown.  

Friends I find in those who fear,  
In His statutes, hold them dear.  

Before affliction, astray I'd go,  
Now His word, my heart does know.  

Your law, O Yahweh, sweeter than gold,  
Boundless wisdom, in your hold.  

Even in anguish, His law my song,  
Forever righteous, His word is strong.  

"Preserve my life," I cry in need,  
In His loving kindness, I am freed.  

His faithfulness, generations praise,  
In His precepts, my heart stays.  

Boundless love for His law, I find,  
Meditate, wisdom aligns.  

Light to my path, His word to guide,  
In His ordinances, I abide.  

"Accept my offering," my soul implores,  
Though snares surround, His law restores.  

"My shield, my hope," I trust, I claim,  
In His commandments, I remain.  

His statutes wonderful, my soul will keep,  
In His word, His promise deep.  

Cry for understanding, Yahweh, hear my plea,  
In His loving kindness, restore me.  

Near Yahweh, His truth prevails,  
Enemies fail, His word never pales.  

Consider my affliction, set me free,  
Deliverance in His word I see.  

Rejoice in His word, like treasure found,  
In His law, my heart is bound.  

With heart and soul, I call, I rise,  
Yahweh's salvation, my prize.  

Seven times a day, His praises sing,  
Peace in His law, nothing else can bring.  

"Seek Your servant," my cry sincere,  
In His commandments, I persevere.
