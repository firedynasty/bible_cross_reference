God, don’t keep silent, don't be still,  
Your enemies rise, they've got a thrill.  
Heads lifted high, they conspire with hate,  
Against your people, they plan their fate.  

"Come, let's destroy them, erase their name,  
Israel forgotten, lost to fame."  
United with one mind, they’ve formed a pact,  
Against you, God, they plan their attack.  

Edom's tents, Ishmael's kin,  
Moab's crew, Hagrites in sin.  
Gebal and Ammon, Amalek's call,  
Philistia, Tyre, they're in for the brawl.  

Assyria joins, the plot thickens like glue,  
All helping Lot’s kids, against you too.  
Do to them as you did to Midian's crew,  
Sisera, Jabin, Kishon, you knew.  

Perished at Endor, dung for the ground,  
Make their nobles fall, sound for sound.  
Oreb and Zeeb, Zebah, Zalmunna's land,  
Who sought to take what's in God’s hand.  

Turn them to tumbleweed, chaff in the blast,  
Fire in the forest, burn them fast.  
Pursue with your storm, fill them with dread,  
Confusion's cloak around their head.  

Let them be dismayed, forever in dismay,  
Confounded, perished, lost in disarray.  
That they may know, you alone, the Most High,  
Over all the earth, Yahweh, your name won't die.
