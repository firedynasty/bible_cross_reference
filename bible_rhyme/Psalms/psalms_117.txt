Praise Yahweh, every nation, raise your voice,  
Extol him, all you peoples, let’s rejoice.  

For his love and kindness, it’s so great,  
Towards us, it never does abate.  

Yahweh’s faithfulness, forever it will last,  
Praise Yah, let his name be broadcast.  

His love endures, no matter the weather,  
His faithfulness, strong and forever.  

Lift your hands, let your voices ring,  
Praise Yahweh, for he is our King.
