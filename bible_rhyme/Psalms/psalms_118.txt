Give thanks to Yahweh, he's forever good,  
Loving kindness lasts, as it should.  

Israel, shout loud, let it be known,  
His loving kindness, forever shown.  

House of Aaron, lift your voice high,  
His love endures, reaching the sky.  

All who fear Yahweh, join the song,  
His loving kindness, ever strong.  

In distress, I called, felt so small,  
Yah answered me, freed my soul's call.  

Yahweh's by my side, no fear to see,  
"What can man do?" Can't touch me.  

Yahweh's with those who aid my ride,  
Triumphant, I face those who deride.  

Better to seek Yahweh's embrace,  
Than trust in man's fleeting grace.  

In Yahweh's name, nations surround,  
With strength, their power I ground.  

Like bees they swarm, yet they fall,  
In Yahweh's name, I conquer all.  

Pushed me hard, tried to make me fall,  
But Yahweh lifted, heard my call.  

Yah is my strength, my salvation's song,  
In righteous tents, His praises belong.  

"The right hand of Yahweh, mighty in fame,  
Exalted high, victorious in name."  

I live to declare Yah's great deeds,  
Punished but saved, Yah meets my needs.  

Open the gates, let righteousness through,  
I'll enter with thanks, for Yah's love is true.  

The stone rejected, cornerstone stands,  
Yahweh's creation, crafted by His hands.  

This day Yahweh made, rejoice, be glad,  
Save us, Yahweh, blessings to be had.  

Blessed is he who in Yahweh's name came,  
From Yahweh's house, we bless His name.  

Yahweh's our God, shining bright light,  
Bind the sacrifice, honor His might.  

You're my God, I'll thank, I'll praise,  
For your goodness, all my days.  

Give thanks to Yahweh, forever He's good,  
Loving kindness endures, as it should.
