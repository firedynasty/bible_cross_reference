We've heard the tales, oh God, from days of old,  
Stories our fathers have so often told.  

You drove out nations with your powerful hand,  
While planting your people to possess the land.  

It wasn't their swords or strength that won,  
But your right hand, and favor shone like the sun.  

"You're my King," I shout, "Command victories for Jacob's fight!"  
With your power, we'll conquer, and set things right.  

I won't lean on my bow, or sword to save,  
You've rescued us from enemies, made the proud behave.  

In God we boast, all day we sing,  
Thanking your name for everything.  

But now we feel your rejection, our honor's been shamed,  
You no longer march with us, our strength's been tamed.  

Enemies turn us back, take plunder as their prize,  
You scattered us, like sheep under foreign skies.  

Sold for nothing, neighbors mock our state,  
To them, we're just a laugh, a tale of fate.  

Shame and taunts, my face burns with disgrace,  
Yet among it all, we still seek your face.  

Through jackals' haunts and death's shadowy breath,  
We've not forgotten you, even in threat of death.  

If we turned to idols, you'd know it well,  
For you search our hearts, where secrets dwell.  

For your sake, we bear each sting and slaughter,  
Like sheep led on to the deep, dark water.  

"Wake up, Lord! Why do you sleep? Rise and see,  
Don't turn away, remember us in our plea."  

Our soul's in the dust, bodies cling to earth's crust,  
Help us, redeem us, in your loving kindness we trust.
