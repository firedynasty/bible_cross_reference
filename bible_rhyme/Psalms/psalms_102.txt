Hear my prayer, Yahweh, let my cry reach you.  
Don't hide your face, I'm in distress, it's true.  

Turn your ear, answer when I call out loud,  
My days burn away like smoke, beneath a cloud.  

Bones like torches, my heart's dried out,  
Withered like grass, as I wander, doubt.  

Groans echo, my frame sticks to skin,  
Like a lonely pelican where life's grown thin.  

Watch me, I'm a sparrow on a rooftop high,  
Enemies taunt, they use my name to decry.  

Ashes for bread, tears in my cup,  
Your wrath's the reason I feel thrown up.  

Days stretch like shadows, I'm withered, grass-like,  
But You, Yahweh, are forever, never strike.  

“You’ll arise, show mercy on Zion, the time's near,  
Servants cherish her stones, hold her dust dear.”  

Nations will fear Your name from afar,  
Kings of the earth will see Your glory's star.  

Yahweh's built Zion, appearing in splendor,  
Hears the poor's prayer, finds their cries tender.  

For future folks, this is written right,  
A people yet born will praise Yah's might.  

From heaven's height, He gazed on earth,  
Heard prisoners groan, freed them with new birth.  

Declare Yahweh's name across Zion's land,  
Praise in Jerusalem will forever stand.  

Gathered peoples, kingdoms serve,  
He weakened my strength, like a bent curve.  

I plead, "My God, not in my life's prime,  
Your years stretch beyond all time."  

Laid the earth's foundation, heavens by Your hand,  
They'll perish but You'll endure, always stand.  

Old like garments, they'll be changed and worn,  
But You remain, eternal, never torn.  

Children of Your servants will continue strong,  
Their offspring established, before You they belong.
