Sing to Yahweh, a song that's brand new,  
In the saints' assembly, His praise rings true.  

Let Israel dance for the One who made,  
Children of Zion, in joy they're arrayed.  

Praise His name with dance, tambourine, and harp,  
Yahweh delights, salvation's His art.  

The humble He crowns, in glory they stand,  
Saints sing for joy, His will they command.  

High praises of God in their mouths they wield,  
A two-edged sword in their hands revealed.  

Vengeance on nations, punishments they bring,  
Kings in chains, nobles bound under wing.  

Judgment written, executed with might,  
Saints in honor, bask in His light.  

Praise Yahweh! Let His glory resound,  
In righteousness, His people are found.
