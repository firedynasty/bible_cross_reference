Yo, give it up to Yahweh, sons of might,  
Glory and strength, his name's in the light.  

Honor his name, all dressed in holy style,  
Worship Yahweh, make it all worthwhile.  

On the waters, his voice it thunders loud,  
God of glory, speaking through the cloud.  

His voice is power, full of majesty,  
Breaking cedars down, like they ain't meant to be.  

Snapping Lebanon's trees, splitting 'em clean,  
Jumping like calves, in a wild scene.  

Voice lights the sky, like a lightning bolt,  
Shakes the wilderness, feel that jolt.  

In Kadesh, he shakes the ground,  
Deer give birth, forests stripped down.  

In his temple, echoes ring, "Glory!" say the crowd,  
Above the Flood, Yahweh sits, King and proud.  

Gives strength to his people, blesses them with peace,  
Yahweh's reign forever, his power won't cease.
