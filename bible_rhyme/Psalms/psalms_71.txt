In you, Yahweh, I find my place, never let me fall from grace.  
Deliver me, rescue me in your righteous embrace.  

Turn your ear, come save me now, be my rock, my stronghold vow.  
Command my safety, you're my fortress, I bow.  

Pull me from the wicked's hand, from the cruel and unrighteous band.  
You're my hope, Yahweh, my rock on which I stand.  

From the womb, I've relied on you, you brought me forth, it's true.  
I’ll praise you forever, your wonders I view.  

To many, I'm a marvel, but you’re my refuge, my marvel’s stew.  
My mouth's filled with your praises, all day through.  

In old age, don't cast me aside, when my strength's gone, stay by my side.  
Enemies conspire, plotting wide, saying you’ve left, nowhere to hide.  

God, don't be far, hurry, be my guide.  
Let my foes be disgraced, their schemes denied.  

I’ll always hope, your praises will subside never.  
Your righteousness and salvation, I’ll speak of forever.  

With mighty acts, Lord Yahweh, I’ll recount your deeds,  
From youth, your teachings, my soul feeds.  

Old and gray, God, stay with me, till your power the young can see.  
Your righteousness reaches the heavens, who is like thee?  

You've shown us troubles, bitter and deep, but from the depths, you'll lift and keep.  
Restore my honor, comfort me in your faithful leap.  

With harp and lyre, I'll sing your praise,  
Holy One of Israel, through all my days.  

My lips shout for joy, my redeemed soul sings,  
Your righteousness all day, over everything.  

Those who sought my harm now confounded, misplaced,  
For I’ll speak of your glory, your name embraced.
