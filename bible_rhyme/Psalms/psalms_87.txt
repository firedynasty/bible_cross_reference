In the holy mountains, His foundation’s laid,  
Yahweh loves Zion, more than Jacob’s parade.  

Glorious things, the city of God hears,  
Rahab and Babylon, among those who cheer.  

Philistia, Tyre, even Ethiopia’s shown,  
"Behold," He says, "This one was born in this zone."  

Yes, Zion’s fame, “This and that were born here,”  
The Most High’s hand will make it clear.  

Yahweh counts when He writes up the names,  
"This one was born there," He proudly proclaims.  

Singers and dancers, with joy they declare,  
"All my springs are in you," everywhere.
