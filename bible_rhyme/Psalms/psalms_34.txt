I'm blessing Yahweh every day, His praise on my lips, come what may.  
My soul boasts in His holy name, the humble hear and join the acclaim.  

"Come magnify Yahweh, join with me," we lift His name, praise in unity.  
I sought Yahweh, He heard my plea, delivered me from fears, set me free.  

They look to Him, radiant in His light, their faces never shamed, shining bright.  
This poor man cried, Yahweh was near, saved him from troubles, calmed his fear.  

Yahweh's angel stands guard around, those who fear Him, safe and sound.  
"Taste and see, Yahweh is good," blessed is the one in Him who stood.  

Fear Yahweh, saints, lack won't come near, no shortage for those who hold Him dear.  
Young lions may lack, hunger in their lair, but seekers of Yahweh find plenty to share.  

"Come, listen, children," hear my speech, "I'll show you the fear of Yahweh I preach."  
Who wants life, loves many days, sees good things in Yahweh's ways?  

Keep your tongue from evil words, let no deceit from your lips be heard.  
Turn from evil, do what's right, seek peace, chase it with all your might.  

Yahweh's eyes on the righteous shine, His ears hear them when they whine.  
His face turns from those who do wrong, wipes their memory, they don't belong.  

The righteous cry, Yahweh attends, delivering them as He defends.  
He's near to those with broken hearts, mending spirits torn apart.  

Many afflictions the righteous see, but Yahweh delivers them faithfully.  
He guards their bones, none are crushed, under His care, fear is hushed.  

Evil destroys the wicked, they're done, those hating the righteous, condemned, they run.  
Yahweh redeems His servants’ souls, none who seek refuge face judgment's tolls.
