Yo, listen up, Yahweh, hear my meditation,  
I cry out loud, my King, my dedication.  

In the morning, Yahweh, my voice you’ll hear,  
I lay it all out, my hopes, my fear.  

You’re not a God who loves wicked ways,  
Evil can't stand where your presence stays.  

Arrogant ones won’t stand in your sight,  
You hate those who work wrong, who shun the light.  

Destroy the liars, the bloodthirsty you disdain,  
But in your love, I enter your domain.  

I bow before your temple in reverence and awe,  
Lead me right, Yahweh, don’t let me fall.  

Their words lack truth, hearts full of destruction,  
Their throats are graves, tongues weave seduction.  

Hold them guilty, God, by their own schemes,  
Kick them out, let them fall, for their rebellious dreams.  

But those who seek refuge, let them shout with joy,  
You defend, they rejoice, no need to be coy.  

Bless the righteous, Yahweh, give them your shield,  
With your favor surround, let their joy be revealed.
