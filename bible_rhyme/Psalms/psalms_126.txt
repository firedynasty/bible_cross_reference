When Yahweh brought us back, it was like a dream,  
Our laughter flowed, our joy supreme.  

With singing tongues, we praised His name,  
The nations saw and proclaimed His fame.  

"Yahweh has done great things for them," they cried,  
Indeed, He's blessed us far and wide.  

Restore us, Yahweh, let the blessings flow,  
Like desert streams where waters glow.  

Those who sow with tears will find delight,  
In joy they'll gather harvest bright.  

The one who weeps, with seed in hand,  
Will come back rejoicing, across the land.
