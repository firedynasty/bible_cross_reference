I'm watching my ways, keeping my tongue in check,  
Holding my peace, while the wicked wreck.  

Silent and still, though my heart's in pain,  
Inside me a fire, hard to contain.  

I spoke out loud, "Yahweh, let me see,  
Show me my end, how frail I can be."  

"Days like hand widths, my life's just a breath,  
Each man stands like a shadow, nearing death."  

"They hustle in vain, piling up their store,  
But who will gather when they're here no more?"  

"Lord, what am I waiting for? My hope's in you,  
Save me from transgressions, make my heart true."  

Mute I was, kept my lips closed tight,  
"Cause you did it, made my soul take flight."  

"Remove your scourge, this burden I can't stand,  
Struck down by the power of your mighty hand."  

"You rebuke and correct, wealth turns to dust,  
Every man's a breath, vanish they must."  

"Hear my prayer, Yahweh, heed my cry,  
Don't be silent, see these tears I cry."  

"A stranger I am, like my fathers of old,  
Spare me some strength before my story's told."
