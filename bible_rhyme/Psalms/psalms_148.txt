Praise Yah from the skies so high,  
Angels and armies, echo the cry.  

Sun and moon, stars that gleam,  
Heavens above, join the theme.  

"Let 'em praise my name," Yahweh said,  
He spoke the word, the world was spread.  

He set the rules, they'll never sway,  
Fixed them firm, night and day.  

From the earth, to the depths below,  
Sea creatures, storms that blow.  

Mountains tall, hills that rise,  
Trees with fruit, cedar skies.  

Wild beasts, livestock, birds in flight,  
All creation, day and night.  

Kings and people, princes, judges, too,  
Young and old, join the view.  

"Let 'em praise my name," Yahweh's claim,  
His name's exalted, above all fame.  

His glory’s high, beyond earth and sky,  
He lifts his people, reaching high.  

"Raise the horn, let saints proclaim,  
Israel's near, praise my name."  

Praise Yah, let the chorus ring,  
To the heavens, let praises cling.
