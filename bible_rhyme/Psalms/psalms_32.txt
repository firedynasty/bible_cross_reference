Blessed is the one who's forgiven, sin's covered tight,  
No deceit in their spirit, their path's in the light.  

Kept quiet, my bones wasted, groanin' day and night,  
God's hand heavy on me, strength sapped out of sight.  

I confessed my sins to You, I didn't hide a thing,  
I said, "Yahweh, here's my wrongs," You forgave everything.  

Let the godly pray to You while You can be found,  
When waters rise high, they won't get drowned.  

You're my hiding place, my safe retreat,  
Surround me with songs, deliverance sweet.  

"I'll teach and guide you, show you the way to go,  
With my eye on you, wisdom I'll bestow."  

Don't be stubborn like a mule, needing a bridle tight,  
Come near with understanding, walk in the light.  

Sorrows hit the wicked, but kindness wraps the just,  
Trust in Yahweh, find in Him your trust.  

Be glad in Yahweh, righteous, let your joy take flight,  
Shout for joy, upright in heart, bask in the light!
