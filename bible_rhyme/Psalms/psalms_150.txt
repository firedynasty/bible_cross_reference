Praise Yah, in His holy place,  
In the sky for His mighty grace.  

Praise His acts, mighty and grand,  
His greatness stretches across the land.  

Blow the trumpet, let it ring,  
Harp and lyre, let praises sing.  

Tambourine and dance, move your feet,  
Strings and flute make the praise complete.  

Cymbals crash, loud and strong,  
Resounding cymbals join the song.  

Let all with breath lift their voice,  
Praise Yah, let the earth rejoice!
