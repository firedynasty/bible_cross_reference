Praise Yahweh, His love don't fade,  
His kindness forever, it's here to stay.  

Who can speak of His acts so grand?  
Who can give all that praise demands?  

Blessed are those who do what's right,  
Keeping justice in their sight.  

Remember me, Lord, with favor given,  
Show me salvation, let my spirit be driven.  

We've sinned like our fathers, acted with shame,  
In Egypt's wonders, we forgot His name.  

Yet He saved them, His power displayed,  
Dried the Red Sea, a path He made.  

Enemies crushed, waters closed tight,  
His words believed, they sang in the night.  

But soon they forgot, not waiting for His say,  
In the desert, they craved, and tested His way.  

He gave their request, but souls felt lean,  
In the camp, envy of Moses was seen.  

Earth swallowed rebels, fire burned the vile,  
A calf at Horeb, they worshiped in style.  

Forgot their Savior, His wonders in hand,  
Moses stood firm, to spare the land.  

Despised the promise, murmured instead,  
Overthrown in the wild, by their own dread.  

Joined with Baal, the dead they fed,  
Provoked His anger, a plague widespread.  

Phinehas stood, judgment displayed,  
For righteousness, his action stayed.  

At Meribah's waters, they angered again,  
Moses troubled, his spirit in pain.  

Mixed with nations, idols became a snare,  
Sons and daughters, sacrificed in despair.  

Defiled by deeds, Yahweh's wrath did burn,  
Handed to nations, their enemies' turn.  

Yet He regarded their cries, His covenant true,  
Remembered His kindness, renewed them anew.  

"Save us, Yahweh, gather us near,"  
To praise Your name, we'll hold dear.  

Blessed be Yahweh, God of endless reign,  
Let all people say, "Amen," praise sustain!
