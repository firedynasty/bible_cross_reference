"Preserve me, God, I seek refuge in You,"  
My soul whispers, "Lord, all my good's from You too."  

Saints here on earth, they're my delight,  
But woes come to those who worship false light.  

Drink offerings of blood? Not my game,  
Their gods' names? I won't speak or claim.  

Yahweh's my portion, my cup, my fate,  
He made my lot secure, my life's estate.  

Pleasant lines around me, a sweet inheritance,  
I bless Yahweh, He's my life's essence.  

At night, my heart speaks, gives me insight,  
Yahweh's always before me, my guiding light.  

My heart's glad, my tongue sings out,  
My body rests safe, without a doubt.  

"You won't leave my soul in the grave,  
Corruption's path isn't mine to pave."  

Show me life's path, where joy's unmeasured,  
In Your presence, pleasures forever treasured.
