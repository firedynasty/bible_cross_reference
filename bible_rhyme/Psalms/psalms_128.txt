Blessed is the one who fears the Lord,  
Walking in His ways, in one accord.  
You’ll reap the fruits of all your toil,  
Happiness and peace, your life won't spoil.

Your wife, a vine, fruitful and fine,  
In your house, her presence divine.  
Children like olive shoots at your table,  
A scene of joy, strong and stable.

Behold the one who fears Yahweh’s might,  
Blessed with a life that feels just right.  
“May Yahweh bless you from Zion's place,  
See Jerusalem's goodness, feel its grace.”

Witness your children's children grow,  
Peace on Israel, let it flow.
