Yahweh smiled down, favor on the land,  
Restored Jacob's fortune, by His mighty hand.  

Forgiving iniquities, He covered our sin,  
Took away His wrath, let peace begin.  

"Turn us back, God, to salvation's embrace,  
Will Your anger last, on every face?"  

Revive us again, let joy resound,  
Show kindness, Yahweh, let salvation be found.  

I'll listen close, hear what God will say,  
"Peace to My people, don't turn away."  

Salvation's near to those who fear,  
Glory in the land, so pure and clear.  

Mercy and truth meet like old friends,  
Righteousness and peace, the union never ends.  

Truth springs from earth, life anew,  
Righteousness gazes from heaven's view.  

Yes, Yahweh gives good, our land will grow,  
Righteousness leads, in His steps we follow.
