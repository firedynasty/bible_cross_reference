Hear us, Shepherd, leading Joseph's flock,  
Shine from above, make the heavens rock.  

Before Ephraim, Benjamim, Manasseh's might,  
Stir up your power, bring us to light.  

"Turn us again," we cry out loud,  
"Shine your face, save us from the shroud."  

Yahweh of Armies, how long will you burn,  
Against the prayers, when will you turn?  

Fed with tears, our drink is pain,  
Neighbors mock, they think us insane.  

"Turn us again," God of Armies, we plead,  
"Shine your face, fulfill our need."  

You brought a vine from Egypt's land,  
Planted it firm with your mighty hand.  

Cleared the ground, roots dug deep,  
Mountains and shadows, promises to keep.  

Branches reached from sea to sea,  
Why break walls, let wild beasts feed free?  

Boars and beasts ravage and roam,  
Turn again, God, bring your vine home.  

Look from heaven, visit this vine,  
Your hand-planted, your divine line.  

Burned with fire, cut and torn,  
They perish away, lost and forlorn.  

Let your hand hold the man of might,  
The chosen one, bring him to light.  

We won't turn away, revive our name,  
Call on you, save us from shame.  

"Turn us again," Yahweh, we pray,  
"Shine your face, lead the way."
