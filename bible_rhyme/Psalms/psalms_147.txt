Praise Yah, it's good to sing, lift your voice to the King,  
Jerusalem's built, outcasts He'll bring,  

He heals the hearts that are broken, no lie,  
Counts all the stars, calls each by the sky,  

Mighty and great, His power and mind,  
Upholds the humble, the wicked He'll find,  

Sing to Yahweh, thank Him with song,  
Clouds in the sky, rain where it belongs,  

Grass on the mountains, food for the beasts,  
Ravens He feeds, for them He'll release,  

Strength of a horse, not His delight,  
Those who fear Him, bring Him sight,  

Jerusalem, Zion, lift your praise high,  
Strengthened your gates, children nearby,  

Peace within borders, wheat finest of all,  
His word on the earth, swiftly it calls,  

Snow like wool, frost like the ash,  
Hail like pebbles, cold with a crash,  

He sends His word, melts them away,  
Winds blow, waters flow, all in His sway,  

His word to Jacob, laws for His own,  
No other nation, His ordinances shown,  

Praise Yah, in all that you say!
