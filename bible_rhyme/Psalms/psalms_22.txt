"My God, my God, why'd you leave me so alone?  
Why you so distant from my cries and groans?"  

Daytime I call, no answer I get,  
Nighttime I'm up, no rest for me yet.  

But you’re holy, praised by Israel's song,  
Our fathers trusted, you delivered them strong.  

They cried out to you, and you came through,  
Trusted in you, never felt blue.  

But me, I'm a worm, not even a man,  
Despised by people, I'm not in their plan.  

They mock when they see, with heads they shake,  
"Trusts in Yahweh? Let Yahweh break."  

But you brought me from my mother’s womb,  
Made me trust you, gave me room.  

Don’t be far, trouble’s near, no one to aid,  
Bulls surround, like shadows they’ve laid.  

Mouths wide open, lions roar loud,  
I'm like water poured, bones disallowed.  

Heart's like wax, melting inside,  
Strength dried up, tongue's glued, can't glide.  

Dogs encircle, evildoers near,  
Hands and feet pierced, yet I won't fear.  

Bones I can count, they stare, they gawk,  
Dividing my clothes, my garments they stalk.  

Yahweh, don't be far, hurry, be quick,  
My soul from the sword, let it not stick.  

Save me from lions, wild oxen’s horns,  
I’ll declare your name, your praise adorns.  

You who fear Yahweh, lift your voice high,  
Descendants of Jacob, glorify!  

He’s not despised the afflicted's plight,  
When they cried out, heard them all right.  

In great assembly, my praise will rise,  
Humble will eat, their hearts will be wise.  

Ends of the earth will remember and turn,  
All nations to worship, their hearts will burn.  

For Yahweh’s kingdom, ruler of nations,  
Rich and poor, all generations.  

Posterity will serve, stories unfold,  
Declare his righteousness, a tale to be told.  

Future will sing, for he has done it,  
His name declared, never to quit.
