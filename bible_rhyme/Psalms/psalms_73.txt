God's been good to Israel, pure hearts in the start,  
But my footing almost slipped; envy crept in my heart.  

Saw the arrogant flourish, wicked prosper with ease,  
No struggles in their death, standing firm in their beliefs.  

Pride's a chain 'round their neck, violence their attire,  
Eyes bulging with greed, minds fueled by desire.  

They scoff, talk with malice, oppressing with might,  
Mouths in the heavens, tongues roaming the night.  

Their followers return, drink abundance like streams,  
"Does God know?" they question, mocking holy dreams.  

The wicked at ease, their riches always grow,  
I cleansed my heart in vain, felt innocence was a show.  

Plagued every day, punished with each dawn,  
If I spoke my mind, betrayals would be drawn.  

Tried to understand, but pain held me tight,  
Till in God's sanctuary, their end came to light.  

Slippery places set, they fall to destruction,  
Suddenly destroyed, swept away in eruption.  

Like dreams fading fast, God despises their schemes,  
My soul grieved, embittered, caught in silent screams.  

Ignorant, senseless, a beast in His sight,  
Yet You held my hand, guiding me to the light.  

You'll guide with Your counsel, receive me in glory,  
No one else I desire, Your love is my story.  

Flesh and heart may fail, but God's my strength and portion,  
Those far from You perish, destroyed in their notion.  

Close to God is good, Yahweh my refuge and shield,  
I'll tell of Your works, in Your presence revealed.
