Judge me, Yahweh, for I've walked strong,  
In my integrity, where I belong.  

I've trusted you, Lord, without a shake,  
"Examine me, prove me, for my heart's at stake."  

Your kindness shines right before my eyes,  
I walk in your truth, away from lies.  

No seat for me with deceitful men,  
I turn from hypocrites, again and again.  

I shun the gatherings full of evil schemes,  
Won't sit with the wicked, denying my dreams.  

I wash my hands in innocence, so pure,  
At your altar, Yahweh, my thanks I'll ensure.  

With thanksgiving, my voice will rise and tell,  
Of your wondrous deeds, where your glory dwells.  

"Don't gather my soul with sinners," I plea,  
Nor with bloodthirsty men — I'm asking you, see?  

In their hands lies wickedness, bribes in their grip,  
But as for me, it's integrity I won't let slip.  

Redeem me, Lord, show your mercy and grace,  
My foot stands firm, in your holy place.  

In the gatherings, your name I'll bless,  
Yahweh, my God, in righteousness.
