Enemies rising, they swarm and they shout,  
They say there's no help for me, no way out.  

But Yahweh, my shield, my glory, my light,  
Lifts up my head, turns my dark to bright.  

I cry out loud, from the depths I call,  
He answers from his hill, strong and tall.  

I lay down to sleep, fear takes its retreat,  
Awake anew, Yahweh keeps me on my feet.  

No fear of thousands, surrounding my place,  
"Arise, Yahweh! Save me," I shout in haste.  

Enemies struck, they've felt your bone-crack,  
Teeth of the wicked, you break and attack.  

Salvation is Yahweh's, his blessing will flow,  
On his people, his love will always show.
