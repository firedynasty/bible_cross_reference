The fool in his heart says, "God ain't around,"  
Corrupt and vile, their deeds drag 'em down.  

God looks from the sky, scanning the land,  
Seeking those who understand, who take His hand.  

But all have turned back, joined in their mess,  
Not a single one doing right, just distress.  

Do these wrongdoers not know, as they feed on my kin,  
They ignore the Almighty, they wallow in sin.  

Fear grips them tight where no fear should reign,  
Bones scattered, enemies shamed, by God’s disdain.  

"Oh, let salvation come from Zion's height!"  
When God restores, Jacob and Israel will bask in the light.
