Not to us, Yahweh, not to us at all,  
But to Your name, let the glory fall.  

Why should nations ask, "Where's their King?"  
Our God's in heaven, He runs everything.  

Their idols of silver and gold,  
Made by hands, stories of old.  

With mouths that never speak,  
Eyes that don’t take a peek.  

Ears that never hear,  
Noses that smell not near.  

Hands that never feel,  
Feet that don't make a deal.  

Those who craft them, end up the same,  
Trust in Yahweh, that's the game.  

Israel, trust in Yahweh, stand tall,  
He's your help, your shield, He’s all.  

House of Aaron, trust His might,  
He’s your help through every fight.  

Fear Yahweh, trust Him, don’t yield,  
He's your help and your shield.  

Yahweh remembers, blesses us all,  
Blessing Israel, Aaron, small and tall.  

May Yahweh increase your store,  
You and your children, evermore.  

Blessed by Yahweh, heaven and earth made,  
He gave earth to men, where we wade.  

The dead don’t praise, silent they lay,  
But we bless Yah, from now every day.  

Praise Yah! Let His glory resound,  
From this moment, forever renowned.
