God, why you seem to leave us in despair?  
Why does your anger burn while we’re in prayer?  

Remember your people, redeemed and saved,  
Mount Zion, your home, where you once stayed.  

Look at the ruins, the enemy’s game,  
They've shattered your temple, they've brought us shame.  

Roaring in the sacred halls, they bring their signs,  
Axes in hand, they cut through ancient vines.  

With hatchet and hammer, they break it all down,  
Burning your sanctuary to ashes on the ground.  

Their hearts declare, “We’ll crush them all,”  
Burning places where we used to call.  

No signs or prophets left to show the way,  
How long, O God, will the enemy stay?  

Why hold your power back, why let it be?  
Unleash your might and set us free!  

Yet you are our King, saving since old,  
Parting seas, your stories often told.  

Crushing Leviathan, your strength displayed,  
You feed the needy with what you’ve made.  

Springs and streams by your hand were born,  
Rivers dried up, and new light was sworn.  

Day and night, all yours, you made the sun,  
Earth's bounds set, seasons begun.  

Remember, God, how they mock your name,  
Let not the wild overtake your claim.  

Honor your promise, the world's a violent place,  
Let not the oppressed lose hope or face.  

Rise up, God, and plead your cause,  
Remember their mockery, their ceaseless applause.  

Hear your foes, their voices ring,  
Their cries to heaven, forever they sing.
