Overflowing heart, noble theme on my mind,  
I spit these verses for the king, words that bind.  

You're the finest of all men, grace on your lips,  
God's blessings on you, never slips.  

Sword on your thigh, mighty and true,  
Splendor and majesty, what you do.  

Ride in triumph for truth and right,  
Your hand's deeds show power and might.  

Arrows sharp, nations fall,  
Enemies struck, at your call.  

Throne eternal, God, forever it stands,  
With a scepter of fairness in your hands.  

Loved the right, hated wicked ways,  
Anointed with gladness all your days.  

Garments scent of myrrh and spice,  
Music from ivory halls, oh, so nice.  

Kings' daughters and queens in gold so grand,  
By your side they proudly stand.  

"Listen, child, and turn your ear,  
Forget your past, hold the king dear."  

King desires beauty, so give him praise,  
Lord he is, in all your ways.  

Daughter of Tyre with gifts in tow,  
Rich seek favor, they all show.  

Princess within, glorious sight,  
Clothing of gold, shining bright.  

Led to the king in garments fine,  
Virgins follow in a line.  

With joy and song, they enter the halls,  
Rejoicing echoes through these walls.  

Sons replace the fathers’ role,  
Princes of earth, destiny's scroll.  

Your name remembered, through ages passed,  
Peoples give thanks, forever to last.
