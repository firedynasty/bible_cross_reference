Don't fret about the wicked, don't envy their deeds,  
They'll be cut down like grass, like withering weeds.  

Trust in Yahweh, do good, stay on the right track,  
Dwell in the land, feel the safety, never look back.  

Delight in Yahweh, He'll grant your heart's desire,  
Commit your ways to Him, He'll lift you higher.  

He'll make your righteousness shine, bright as the sun,  
So rest in Yahweh, and let His will be done.  

Don't fret 'bout the man who's plotting his schemes,  
Cease your anger, wrath leads to evil extremes.  

Evildoers cut off, the faithful will stand,  
Wait a while and the wicked will fade from the land.  

The humble shall inherit, peace they will find,  
The wicked gnash their teeth, but Yahweh's not blind.  

"The Lord laughs," He knows their day is near,  
Their swords and bows break, their own hearts know fear.  

Better is a little with righteousness' might,  
Than abundant wickedness, fading in night.  

Yahweh knows the perfect, their days forever,  
In famine they're fed, their hope will not sever.  

The wicked perish like fields in the sun,  
Borrowers never pay back, they come undone.  

Blessed inherit the land, cursed ones fall off,  
Yahweh guides steps, He laughs when they scoff.  

Though you stumble, you're held by His hand,  
Young to old, never forsaken, as He planned.  

Gracious and lending, your offspring blessed,  
Depart from evil, in goodness rest.  

Yahweh loves justice, His saints He keeps,  
The righteous inherit, in peace their soul sleeps.  

The righteous speak wisdom, justice on their tongue,  
God's law in their heart, their steps firmly hung.  

The wicked stalk, but Yahweh won't leave,  
Wait for Yahweh, in Him believe.  

Seen the wicked in power, but they fade away,  
Mark the upright, peace fills their day.  

Transgressors destroyed, the wicked's future gone,  
Salvation from Yahweh, their hope lives on.  

Yahweh rescues, their refuge, their shield,  
In trouble, He saves them, His promise revealed.
