The Mighty One speaks, calls earth wide and far,  
From sunrise to sunset, beneath every star.  

Out of Zion, beauty’s perfection glows,  
God shines forth, in silence no more, He shows.  

Fire devours, stormy skies bring dread,  
Heaven and earth summoned, His judgment spread.  

"Gather my saints, the covenant keepers,  
A witness in sacrifice, no sleepers."  

Heaven declares His righteous stand,  
For God as judge, takes command.  

"Hear me, my people," He starts to speak,  
"Israel, your God, it’s judgment week.  

I won't rebuke for offerings you bring,  
Your sacrifices, a constant thing.  

No need for bulls, goats, not my desire,  
Every beast is mine, on hills they tire.  

Birds of the mountains, the wild I know,  
If I hunger, to you I don’t go.  

Will I eat bulls, or drink goat blood?  
Thanksgiving, your offering, a thankful flood.  

In trouble, call me, I’ll be there,  
Deliverance comes, in honor and prayer.”  

To the wicked, He speaks, “What’s your right,  
To speak my statutes, in the dead of night?  

You hate my law, you toss my word,  
With thieves, adulterers, evil has stirred.  

Your tongue crafts lies, slanders kin,  
You thought me silent, like you in sin.  

But rebuke I will, in your sight,  
You forgot God, now fear my might.  

Remember this, lest I tear you apart,  
Gratitude brings salvation's start."
