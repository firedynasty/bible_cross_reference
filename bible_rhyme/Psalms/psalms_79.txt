Nations stormed in, took your holy place low,  
Jerusalem's in ruins, heaps everywhere we go.  

Servants' bodies, food for birds in the sky,  
Saints' flesh fed to beasts while we question why.  

Blood like water, spilled all around,  
Nobody left to lay bodies in the ground.  

Neighbors mock, we face their taunt,  
Scoffing surrounds, their words they flaunt.  

"How long, Yahweh?" we cry in despair,  
Will your anger burn forever, like fire in the air?  

Pour wrath on those who know you not,  
Kingdoms ignoring, let their peace rot.  

Jacob's devoured, homeland torn apart,  
Don't hold old sins, have mercy, restart.  

Help us, O God, for your name's glory,  
Deliver, forgive, rewrite our story.  

"Where is their God?" nations tease and shout,  
Show them your vengeance for servants' blood out.  

Hear prisoners' sighs, unleash your power,  
Preserve the doomed in their darkest hour.  

Neighbors' reproach, pay them sevenfold,  
For sneering at you, Lord, let justice be bold.  

We're your people, sheep of your land,  
Forever we'll thank you, forever we’ll stand.
