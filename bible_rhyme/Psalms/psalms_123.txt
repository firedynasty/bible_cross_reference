I look to the skies, where you reside,  
Up in the heavens, can't hide my pride.  

Like a servant's gaze, on the master's hand,  
Our eyes fixed on God, waiting for His command.  

"Have mercy, Yahweh, mercy on our plight,  
We've faced too much, in this endless night."  

Our souls filled up with all their scorn,  
The proud ones laugh, leaving us torn.  

Yet we hold on, eyes steady and true,  
Waiting for mercy, from You, just You.
