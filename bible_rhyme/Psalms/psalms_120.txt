In my distress, screamed Yahweh's name,  
He answered me, eased my pain.  

"Save me now from lips that lie,  
From tongues deceitful, make them die."  

What will these slick tongues receive?  
Arrows sharp, hot coals to grieve.  

Woe to me, stuck in Meshech's land,  
Dwelling 'mong Kedar's tents, no peace at hand.  

My soul's too long in hate's embrace,  
Yearning for peace, but they choose war's race.  

I speak of peace, a calming call,  
But they battle, ready to brawl.
