Sing a new song to Yahweh, it's time to bring,  
Marvelous moves, what wonders they bring!  
His right hand's strong, His holy arm's bold,  
Worked salvation, a story retold.  

Yahweh's salvation, now made known,  
Righteousness shown where nations are grown.  
He's remembered kindness, faithful and sure,  
House of Israel, His love will endure.  

Earth's ends have seen this salvation so bright,  
Make joyful noise, shout with all your might!  
Sing for joy, let praises rise,  
Melody soaring to the skies.  

Harp in your hands, lift up your voice,  
Trumpets and horns, let's all rejoice!  
Yahweh the King, hear the joyful sound,  
Nature joins in, creation unbound.  

Let the sea roar, its fullness express,  
World and dwellers, let none suppress.  
Rivers clap hands, mountains in song,  
Joyful together, where they belong.  

Before Yahweh, let the earth dance,  
Judgment comes, in righteousness' glance.  
Fairness and truth, the people's decree,  
Equity for all, as it should be.
