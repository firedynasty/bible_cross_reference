Blessed is the man, he knows the plan,  
Doesn't walk with the wicked, he takes a stand.  
He won't chill on the path where the sinners tread,  
Or sit with the scoffers, he's ahead instead.  

His joy's in Yahweh's law, it's his delight,  
Day and night, he meditates, gets it right.  
He's like a tree by the water, roots deep,  
Bearing fruit in season, his soul to keep.  

Leaves don't wither, whatever he does thrives,  
While the wicked with their empty, fleeting lives,  
They're like chaff in the wind, blown away,  
They won't stand when judgment calls the day.  

No place for sinners among the righteous crew,  
'Cause Yahweh knows the good, the tried and true.  
The righteous walk a path that won't diminish,  
But the wicked's way is doomed to perish.
