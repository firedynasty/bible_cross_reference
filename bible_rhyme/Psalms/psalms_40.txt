I waited for Yahweh, patient and true,  
He turned, He listened, knew what to do.  

From a pit so dark, of miry clay,  
He lifted me up, set my feet to stay.  

A rock beneath where I stand so firm,  
A new song in my mouth, watch others learn.  

Many will see, will fear, and trust,  
In Yahweh's name, in Him we must.  

Blessed is the one who trusts, upright,  
No pride, no lies, in Yahweh's sight.  

Many works, Yahweh, too great to tell,  
Your thoughts for us, they excel.  

No sacrifice, You open my ears,  
Burnt offerings, none, no need for fears.  

I said, "Here, I'm written in the scroll,  
I delight in Your will, within my soul."  

Good news proclaimed, righteousness shared,  
I spoke of Your love, I showed how You cared.  

Yahweh, Your mercies, don’t hold back,  
Guide me with truth, keep me on track.  

Evils surround, my sins overflow,  
So many they are, my heart sinks low.  

"Deliver me, Yahweh, come to my aid,"  
For those who seek harm, let their plans fade.  

Shamed be they, who cry "Aha!"  
Desolation for those who go too far.  

But let seekers of Yahweh rejoice and sing,  
"Exalt Yahweh," let praises ring.  

Though poor and needy, I call Your name,  
You're my Deliverer, always the same.  

Don't delay, my God, be near,  
In You I trust, my Helper dear.
