Give thanks to Yahweh, call his name loud,  
Spread the news, make him proud amidst the crowd.  

Sing to him, praise him, tell of his deeds,  
Glory in his name, let joy plant seeds.  

Seek Yahweh's strength, seek his face for more,  
Remember his wonders, marvels galore.  

Children of Abraham, Jacob's own kin,  
Yahweh's judgments echo deep within.  

His covenant stands, word through ages stayed,  
Sworn to Abraham, to Isaac relayed.  

To Jacob confirmed, to Israel declared,  
"The land of Canaan, your lot prepared."  

Few in number, strangers they roamed,  
From nation to nation, his plan foamed.  

He guarded them, kings were put in place,  
“Don’t touch my anointed, spare their space.”  

Called a famine, food supplies were torn,  
Sent ahead Joseph, a tale reborn.  

Feet bruised in shackles, neck in chains tight,  
Till his word proved true, Yahweh's light.  

The king set him free, ruler he became,  
Lord of the house, wisdom he’d proclaim.  

Israel came to Egypt, Jacob found his stay,  
People grew strong, adversaries kept at bay.  

Hearts turned to hate, a conspiracy brewed,  
Sent Moses and Aaron, chosen and true.  

Miracles performed, wonders in sight,  
He sent darkness, turned waters to blight.  

Frogs filled the land, even kings took heed,  
Flies and lice swarmed, in their borders they breed.  

Hail fell as rain, lightning marked the night,  
Vines and fig trees shattered out of sight.  

Locusts and grasshoppers devoured the land,  
Firstborn struck down, by Yahweh's hand.  

Out they came with silver and gold,  
Not one weak, as Egypt's fear took hold.  

Cloud by day, fire by night led their track,  
Quails and heavenly bread, they did not lack.  

Waters gushed from rock, a river so wide,  
He remembered his promise, with joy they stride.  

Lands given, nations worked for their gain,  
To keep his laws, his statutes maintain.  

Praise Yahweh, let the chorus rise,  
For his deeds, his glory never dies.
