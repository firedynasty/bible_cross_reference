Yahweh's on the throne, let the people shake,  
Sitting with the cherubim, earth starts to quake.  

Great in Zion, high above the crowd,  
His name's awesome; sing it out loud.  

"He's holy!" they shout, the King loves what's right,  
Justice and fairness, shining bright.  

"Exalt Yahweh, bow at His feet,"  
Holy is He, where justice and truth meet.  

Moses and Aaron, priests in line,  
Samuel called, God gave the sign.  

In the cloud, He spoke, they held His decree,  
They followed His statutes, faithful and free.  

God answered back, forgiving their sins,  
Yet He dealt with their wrongs, justice begins.  

"Exalt Yahweh, worship the hill,"  
For our God is holy, His presence we feel.
