Day and night, I've been pleading, my cries reach out,  
Before you, Yahweh, my soul's full of doubt.  

I'm drowning in trouble, life's nearing the pit,  
Counted with the dead, no help, can't quit.  

Like those forgotten, lying in the grave,  
Cut off from your hand, no one to save.  

In the lowest pit, I'm laid, dark and deep,  
Your wrath's heavy on me, waves that never sleep.  

Friends taken away, now an abomination,  
Trapped in this grief, no escape, no salvation.  

Eyes dim with sorrow, hands stretched wide,  
"Do wonders rise with the dead?" I cried.  

"In graves, is love declared, or faithfulness known?  
Your wonders in the dark, your righteousness alone?"  

To you, Yahweh, morning prayers ascend,  
"Why reject my soul? When will this end?"  

Afflicted from youth, terror's my guide,  
Your fierce wrath surrounds, nowhere to hide.  

Like water all day, your terrors flood,  
Lover and friend, gone, into darkness they scud.
