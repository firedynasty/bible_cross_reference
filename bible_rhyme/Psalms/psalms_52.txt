Why you brag about mischief, oh mighty man?  
God's love stays strong, longer than your plan.  

Your tongue's a sharp blade, slicing with lies,  
Plotting destruction under the skies.  

Evil's your favorite, truth takes a backseat,  
Lies are the words that you love to repeat.  

But God’s gonna act, take you down, pull you out,  
Rip your roots from the land, without a doubt.  

The righteous will see, they'll fear, then they'll jeer,  
"This man forgot God, put his trust in mere gear.  

Riches and wickedness, that was his play,  
But look now, he's gone, swept away."  

But me, I'm a green olive tree,  
In God's house, that’s where I'll be.  

I'll trust in His love, forever it flows,  
Thanking Him always, His goodness shows.  

In the presence of saints, I’ll keep hope in His name,  
Forever and ever, I'll praise the same.
