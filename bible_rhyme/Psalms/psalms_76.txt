In Judah, God's known, His name's renowned,  
In Israel, His greatness is profound.  
In Salem stands His tabernacle, place divine,  
In Zion, His dwelling, where His glories shine.  

There He broke arrows, flaming and fierce,  
Smashed shields and swords, made war's tools pierce.  
Glorious and excellent, more than mountains high,  
Valiant men lie plundered, beneath His sky.   

Sleep is their blanket, they've fought their last,  
None can lift a hand, their strength has passed.  
At Jacob's God rebuke, chariots sleep deep,  
Horses too fall, into eternal keep.  

"You, even You, are to be feared,"  
Who stands in Your wrath, when anger's reared?  
Judgment from heaven, the earth fell mute,  
God rose to save, afflicted, resolute.  

Man's wrath becomes praise, Your power displayed,  
Survivors stand restrained, by Your mighty blade.  
"Make vows to Yahweh, fulfill them true,"  
Neighbors bring gifts to the fearsome view.  

He'll cut off princes' spirit, bring them low,  
Kings of earth tremble, His power they know.
