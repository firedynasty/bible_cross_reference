Praise Yah! Servants shout, His name's supreme,  
"From now till forever," that's the theme.  

From sunrise to sunset, His name's praised high,  
Above all nations, His glory fills the sky.  

Who's like Yahweh, seated on that throne,  
Yet stoops to see, where earth and heaven's shown?  

He lifts the poor from the dusty ground,  
Raises the needy till they're renowned.  

He seats them with princes, honored in His sight,  
Turns barren to joyful, her home a delight.  

Praise Yah! Let His name resound,  
From every corner, His love's unbound.
