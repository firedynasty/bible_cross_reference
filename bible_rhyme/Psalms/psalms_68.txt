Let God arise, let His foes flee fast,  
Enemies scatter, no hatred can last.  

Like smoke blown away, let them disappear,  
Wax melts in fire, wicked you should fear.  

But let the righteous rejoice and be glad,  
Sing to God, let your praises be rad.  

"Extol Him who rides on clouds high above,  
To Yah, His name, rejoice in His love."  

Father to the fatherless, defender of the weak,  
God in His holy place, His care we seek.  

He sets the lonely in families, brings hope anew,  
Prisoners sing out, rebellious get their due.  

When You marched, God, leading Your band,  
Earth trembled, skies rained at Your command.  

Plentiful rain, inheritance refreshed,  
You prepared for the poor, Your goodness blessed.  

The Lord speaks, His word goes far and wide,  
"Kings flee in terror, plunder is divided."  

While you rest by the fire's warm embrace,  
Dove wings glisten, gold feathers in place.  

When kings were scattered, snow fell on high,  
Majestic Bashan, but why jealous sigh?  

God's chosen mountain, His eternal domain,  
Chariots of thousands, His heavenly train.  

"You've ascended high, with captives in tow,  
Received gifts from men, even rebellious know."  

Blessed be the Lord, who bears our load,  
God of salvation, deliverance bestowed.  

He strikes His foes, their guilt never released,  
The Lord declares, "From depths you'll be seized."  

Crush His enemies, with victory complete,  
Dogs get their share, enemies' defeat.  

Processions seen, God leads the parade,  
Singers and minstrels, tambourines played.  

"Bless God in the gathering, bless the Lord,"  
Judah, Zebulun, Naphtali in accord.  

God commands strength, strengthen us, O King,  
Jerusalem's temple, gifts the nations bring.  

Rebuke the beast, the warriors that roar,  
Humbled and scattered, no more delight in war.  

Princes rise from Egypt, Ethiopia's hands extend,  
Sing to God, earth's kingdoms, praises never end.  

Heaven of heavens, His voice mighty and bold,  
Strength to God, His excellence unfolds.  

"Awesome God in sanctuaries, strength to His own,  
Praise be to God, His power is known."
