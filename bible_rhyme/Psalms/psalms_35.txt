Contend with me, oh Yahweh, fight my fight,  
Grab your shield and stand, make wrongs right.  

Brandish that spear, block those who pursue,  
Tell my soul, "I am salvation for you."  

Disgrace those who seek my soul's demise,  
Let them be turned, confounded by lies.  

Like chaff in the wind, let them scatter and fade,  
With angels pursuing, their plans unmade.  

They dug a pit, thinking I'd fall in,  
But their own net traps them, where they begin.  

My soul rejoices in Yahweh's embrace,  
“Who is like you?” my bones will trace.  

False witnesses rise, ask what I don't know,  
Evil for good, leaving my soul low.  

In their sickness, I wore the sack,  
Fasting and prayers, nothing lacked.  

I mourned for them like family dear,  
Yet in my pain, they gathered near.  

They attacked with joy, tore at my heart,  
Mockers with teeth, tearing me apart.  

"How long, Lord?" I cry, "Rescue my soul,  
From lions’ grip, make me whole."  

In the grand crowd, thanks I’ll declare,  
Praises among people, my voice in the air.  

Don’t let those wrong me, rejoice and cheer,  
Those who hate me, let them not sneer.  

They speak deceit, against the quiet in the land,  
Mouths wide open, with malicious demands.  

"You've seen it, Yahweh," I plead, "don't stay silent,  
Rise up, defend me, in your strength so vibrant."  

Vindicate me, Yahweh, with righteous might,  
Don’t let them think they're in the right.  

Disappointment and shame, let them wear,  
Those who rejoice at my despair.  

Let my allies shout, joyful and glad,  
“Yahweh be magnified,” in prosperity clad.  

My tongue will speak of your righteous ways,  
And sing your praises all my days.
