Why do nations rage and plot in vain?  
Kings and rulers, joining to break the chain.  

"Let's snap their bonds, cast cords away,"  
But He in heaven laughs at their play.  

The Lord will speak, His anger will rise,  
"On Zion's hill, My King now lies."  

I'll tell you the decree that was made,  
"You are my Son," Yahweh conveyed.  

"Ask me now, and nations you'll own,  
Earth's far corners, your throne alone.  

Break with iron, smash the clay,  
A potter's vessel shattered in dismay."  

Be wise, O kings, hear what’s true,  
Judges of earth, take guidance anew.  

Serve Yahweh with fear, rejoice, feel the quake,  
Kiss the Son, for your own sake.  

Lest anger burns and paths are lost,  
Blessed are those who take refuge, no cost.
