The king rejoices in your strength, oh Yahweh, he sings,  
How great is his joy in the salvation you bring.  

You gave him his heart's true desire, his lips you did hear,  
With blessings and goodness, you drew him near.  

A crown of fine gold, you placed on his head,  
Life everlasting, he asked, never to dread.  

His glory's immense through your saving grace,  
Honor and majesty, you laid in his place.  

You made him blessed, joy in your presence,  
With the Most High's kindness, he won't face resistance.  

Your hands will find enemies, those who do hate,  
A fiery furnace, their pitiful fate.  

Yahweh's wrath will swallow, the fire shall blaze,  
Their descendants erased in your powerful ways.  

They plotted, they schemed, but they couldn't succeed,  
You turned them around, their plans you impede.  

"Be exalted," we cry, "in your strength, Yahweh,  
We'll sing and we'll praise your power today."
