I was glad when they said, "Let's head to Yahweh's place!"  
Feet on Jerusalem's ground, in this sacred space.  

Jerusalem's built tight, a city compact,  
Where tribes climb up, it's a well-known fact.  

Yah's tribes rise in unity, giving thanks to His name,  
Set thrones for judgment, David's legacy's fame.  

Pray for her peace, may those who love her thrive,  
Within those walls, may prosperity arrive.  

For my brothers and friends, I declare, "Peace to you!"  
For Yahweh's house, I'll seek the good and true.
