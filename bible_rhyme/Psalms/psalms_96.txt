Sing a new song, lift your voice high,  
All the earth, let your praises fly.  

Bless His name, every single day,  
Proclaim His salvation, come what may.  

Declare His glory, tell every nation,  
His marvelous works, a grand creation.  

For Yahweh's great, greatly praised,  
Above all gods, His name is raised.  

Other gods are idols, mere stones and clods,  
But Yahweh alone made the heavens, the stars, the clods.  

Honor and majesty, right before His face,  
Strength and beauty in His holy place.  

Families of nations, give Him glory and might,  
Ascribe to Yahweh, in the day and night.  

Bring your offering, step into His courts,  
Worship Yahweh, let your heart report.  

Tremble, earth, before His holy name,  
For Yahweh reigns, His rule the same.  

Say to the nations, "Yahweh reigns supreme,"  
The world is firm, no need for a dream.  

He'll judge the peoples, with equity and right,  
Let the heavens rejoice, with all their might.  

Let the sea roar, let its fullness shout,  
Let fields dance, let trees sing out.  

Before Yahweh, for He's coming near,  
To judge the earth, His truth is clear.  

With righteousness, the world He'll see,  
Judging all with truth, so let it be.
