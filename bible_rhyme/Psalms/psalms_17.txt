Hear me, Yahweh, when I'm down on my knees,  
My words are true, no deceit in these pleas.  

Let justice flow from your holy place,  
With eyes on truth, let me see your face.  

You've tested my heart, in darkness you came,  
Found me blameless, my tongue free from shame.  

By your words I've avoided the violent way,  
Kept my feet on your path, in your light I stay.  

I call on you, God, knowing you'll reply,  
"Turn your ear to me," let my voice reach the sky.  

Show me kindness, save me with your hand,  
From those who threaten, and seek to command.  

Guard me close, like the apple of your eye,  
In the shadow of your wings, let me hide and lie.  

Enemies surround, with hearts hard as stone,  
Proud words they speak, aiming to dethrone.  

They watch us closely, ready to pounce,  
Like lions in wait, for the right moment to announce.  

"Arise, Yahweh, confront and bring them low,  
With your sword, deliver me from the foe."  

Men of the earth, with treasures in their hand,  
But you feed your loved ones with riches grand.  

As for me, with justice, your face I'll see,  
Awake in your likeness, and in that I'll be free.
