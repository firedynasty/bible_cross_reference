May God be merciful and bless us true,  
"Cause my face to shine on you."  
Let your ways be known across the land,  
Salvation spreading, like grains of sand.  

Let the people sing and raise their voice,  
"Praise me, God, let all rejoice."  
Oh, let nations dance and joy employ,  
For you judge with fairness, no decoy.  

Let the people praise you, with hearts so bright,  
"Praise me, God, in morning light."  
The earth has given, her bounty shared,  
Our God will bless, for us He cared.  

God will bless us, His love will stay,  
All ends of earth shall see His way.
