In my heart, a revelation starts, wicked ones don't fear,  
"No fear of God," they say, eyes blinded, can't steer.  

He flatters himself, can't see his own sin,  
Words deceive, no wisdom, no good within.  

On his bed, he schemes, plots that aren't right,  
Evil's his path, strays far from the light.  

But Yahweh's love stretches up to the skies,  
Faithful and true, like mountains that rise.  

Righteousness stands tall, judgments deep as the sea,  
Preserves all creatures, you and me.  

How precious, your love, God, like wings wide and strong,  
Under their shadow, the children belong.  

Satisfied and full, from your house they drink,  
River of pleasures, at the fountain's brink.  

With you, life's spring, in your light, we see,  
"Continue your love for those knowing thee."  

Keep pride's foot from crushing me down,  
Wicked hands, don't let them drive me out of town.  

The workers of iniquity, they stumble and fall,  
In the end, they rise not, heed this call.
