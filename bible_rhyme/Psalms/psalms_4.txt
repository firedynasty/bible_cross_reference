Hear me when I call, God, my righteous friend,  
Relieve my stress, let your mercy descend.  

"Men, how long will my glory fade away?  
Why chase falsehoods, why let truth decay?"  

Yahweh’s chosen, the godly, set apart,  
He hears my call, knows the beat of my heart.  

Stand in awe, don’t fall into sin,  
Search your soul deep within.  

Offer right sacrifices, trust in His might,  
Yahweh's light will guide us through the night.  

"Who will show us good?" they say with a sigh,  
"Yahweh, let your face shine, lift us high."  

Gladness fills my heart, richer than wine,  
More than their harvest, God's joy is mine.  

In peace, I'll lay me down to sleep,  
Yahweh alone, my soul to keep.
