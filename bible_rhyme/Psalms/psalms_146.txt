Praise Yah! Let's shout and sing  
While I breathe, to Yahweh, my praises bring.  

Trust not in princes, man-made schemes,  
Their spirits depart, vanish like dreams.  

Happy the one with Jacob's God at his side,  
Hope in Yahweh, a trusted guide.  

He created the heavens, earth, and seas,  
Keeps truth forever, sets captives free.  

He feeds the hungry, raises the low,  
Opens blind eyes, lets justice flow.  

Yahweh loves the righteous, strong and true,  
Lifts the fallen, supports them too.  

He preserves the stranger, protects those alone,  
Turns the wicked's path, like a rolling stone.  

Yahweh reigns eternal, no end in sight,  
Praise Yah forever, shine His light!
