David in distress, calling out so bold,  
Swore to Yahweh, his promise told:  
"I won't rest in my house or sleep in my bed,  
No slumber for me until a place for God is spread."  

In Ephrathah we heard, in Jaar fields found,  
"We'll enter His place, worship where He's crowned."  
"Rise, Yahweh, to your resting place grand,  
You and your ark, strength in your hand."  

"Clothe priests with righteousness, let saints rejoice,  
For David's sake, don't turn from your chosen voice."  
Yahweh's promise to David, sworn and true,  
"Your offspring on the throne, if my covenant they pursue."  

Zion's the chosen, God's desired land,  
"This is my home forever; here I'll stand.  
I'll bless her provisions, the poor I'll feed,  
Priests with salvation, saints with joy indeed."  

David's strength grows, a light ordained,  
Enemies shamed, but his crown remained.
