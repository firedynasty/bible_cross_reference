Yahweh's my light, my salvation's clear,  
In His strength, there's no one I fear.  

When evildoers came, thought they'd eat me whole,  
They stumbled, they fell, losing their control.  

An army may camp, my heart won't quiver,  
War might rise, but my faith won't shiver.  

One thing I ask, one thing I chase,  
To dwell in His house, to see His face.  

In trouble's day, He hides me away,  
In His secret place, He makes me stay.  

High on a rock, I'm lifted above,  
Enemies below, surrounded by love.  

I'll sing in His tent, joy will overflow,  
Praises to Yahweh, let the music go.  

"Hear me, Yahweh," when I shout out loud,  
"Have mercy on me," I cry unbowed.  

"You said, 'Seek my face,'" my heart replied,  
"Yahweh, your face, I won't let hide."  

Don't turn away, don't cast me aside,  
You've been my help, with you I'll abide.  

When family forsakes, Yahweh lifts me high,  
Above the lies, where I fly.  

Teach me your path, straight and true,  
Against my foes, lead me through.  

False witnesses rise, breathe cruelty,  
Deliver me, Yahweh, protect me truly.  

I'm confident in this, I'll surely see,  
Yahweh's goodness in the land of the free.  

Wait for Yahweh, be strong, stay true,  
Let your heart take courage, He'll see you through.
