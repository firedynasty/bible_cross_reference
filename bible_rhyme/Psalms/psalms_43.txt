"Vindicate me, God! Against the wicked I stand,  
Deliver me from deceitful men across the land.  

You're my strength, oh Lord, why feel cast aside?  
Why do I mourn with the enemy's tide?  

Send your light, your truth, to guide my way,  
Lead me to your hill, where your tents stay.  

To your altar, I'll go, my joy's complete,  
On the harp, I'll praise, your name I'll repeat.  

Why's my soul downcast? Why the inner fight?  
Hope in God! Praise Him, my Savior, my light."
