With my whole heart, I give thanks, no facade,  
Singing praises before the gods, my heart's applaud.  

Bowing low toward your holy dome,  
Thanks for your Name, truth, and loving home.  

Exalted high, your Word and Name do reign,  
When I call, you answer, strength in my soul, you sustain.  

The kings of earth will join the praise,  
Heard your words, amazed in your holy blaze.  

They'll sing of Yahweh's way, declare it grand,  
For Yahweh's glory spans across the land.  

Though high and mighty, He sees the low,  
Proud from afar, but to the humble, close He'll show.  

In trouble's midst, you revive my soul,  
Stretching your hand, making my enemies lose control.  

Your right hand saves, my path you define,  
Forever kind, your love's divine.  

Yahweh fulfills what concerns me, His plan,  
“Don't forsake the works of your hands,” is my stand.
