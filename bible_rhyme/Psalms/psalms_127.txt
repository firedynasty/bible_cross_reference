Unless Yahweh builds the house, the work's in vain,  
Labor's just a struggle, brings no gain.  

Unless Yahweh’s keeping the city tight,  
The watchman’s effort fades into the night.  

Rising early, staying up late, what's the game?  
Toiling hard, but sleep comes all the same.  

Children are his gift, a heritage divine,  
Fruit from the womb, in His design.  

Like arrows in the hand, sharp and true,  
Children of youth, strength anew.  

Happy is the one whose quiver's full,  
Facing foes at the gate, he won’t be dull.
