Praise in Zion, waiting loud, God we see  
Vows fulfilled, in you our pleas will be.  

You who hears, to you all men will arrive,  
Sins overwhelm, but you make us alive.  

Blessed the one who draws near, in your courts to stay,  
Filled with goodness of your house every day.  

Righteous deeds, awesome is your salvation's plan,  
Hope of the earth, seas, and every faroff land.  

Mountains formed by power, your strength untold,  
Roaring seas calmed, nations' turmoil controlled.  

Faraway dwellers in awe of wonders so bright,  
Morning's dawn and evening sing with delight.  

Earth you visit, watering in your grand design,  
River of God, flowing, grain you ordain divine.  

Furrows drenched, ridges leveled with showers,  
Blessed crops spring forth by your powers.  

Year crowned with bounty, carts overflow,  
Wilderness grasslands with abundance glow.  

Hills clothed in gladness, pastures with flocks abound,  
Valleys dressed in grain, joy's shout resound.  

Singing loud, they raise their joyful song,  
In your creation, we all belong.
