If it wasn't Yahweh standing by our side,  
Listen up, Israel, we gotta testify with pride.  

If it wasn't Yahweh defending our ground,  
When enemies came, trying to take us down,  

They would have swallowed us with rage so fierce,  
We'd be engulfed, no way to persevere.  

The waters would rise, pulling us deep,  
Streams overwhelming, making us weep.  

Proud waters crashing, threatening our soul,  
But Yahweh's blessing kept us whole.  

Blessed be Yahweh, didn't let us fall prey,  
Kept us safe, chased the danger away.  

Our soul flew free, like a bird in flight,  
From the hunter's snare, into the light.  

The snare broke apart, and we slipped through,  
Escaped with Yahweh, who makes all things new.  

Our help is in His mighty name,  
He made heaven, earth, and staked His claim.
