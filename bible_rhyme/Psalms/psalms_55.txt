Hear my prayer, God, don’t hide away,  
Attend and answer as I moan today.  

The enemy's voice, wickedness all around,  
Their anger and grudge keep me down.  

My heart's in pain, terrors take hold,  
Fear and trembling, horrors unfold.  

“Oh, wings of a dove, I'd fly and find rest,  
Wander far, find shelter, escape this test.”  

“Keep me safe from the wind and storm,  
Confuse them, Lord, their plans reform.”  

In the city, violence and strife reign,  
Malice, abuse, deceit, and pain.  

Not an enemy, but you, my friend,  
Shared sweet fellowship that came to an end.  

Death comes quick for those in sin's dwelling,  
But I call to God, my story I'm telling.  

Morning, noon, night, my cries reach high,  
Redemption in peace, beneath His sky.  

Forever enthroned, God hears their lies,  
His hands break the bonds of deceitful ties.  

Smooth words, but a heart for war,  
Drawn swords hidden in soft-spoken lore.  

"Cast your burden," He'll sustain you,  
Righteous won’t be moved, this is true.  

God will bring the wicked down,  
Trust in Him, His peace your crown.
