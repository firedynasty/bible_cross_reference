To You, Yahweh, I call, my rock, hear me clear,  
Don't be silent, or I'll fall into the pit I fear.  

Hear my voice when I cry, hands lifted high,  
Toward Your Holy Place, looking to the sky.  

Don't take me with the wicked, who speak of peace,  
But hold mischief in their hearts that never cease.  

Give them what they've earned, their deeds laid bare,  
Pay them back for their ways, they don't even care.  

They disrespect Your works, Your mighty hand,  
So break them down, never let them stand.  

Blessed be Yahweh, He's heard my plea,  
My strength, my shield, He's helping me.  

My heart trusts in Him, joy does abound,  
With my song, His praise goes all around.  

Yahweh is their strength, a fortress true,  
A stronghold of salvation, anointed crew.  

Save Your people, bless what You've grown,  
Be their shepherd, bear them up, and keep them Your own.
