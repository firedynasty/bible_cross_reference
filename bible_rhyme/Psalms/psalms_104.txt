Bless Yahweh, my soul, He's beyond compare,  
Clothed in honor, majesty's what He wears.  

He covers Himself with light, a shining robe,  
Stretches out the heavens, like a curtain globe.  

Lays beams in waters, clouds his chariot ride,  
Walks on wind's wings, across the sky so wide.  

Messengers like winds, His servants, flames of fire,  
Earth's foundation set firm, never to expire.  

Covered with the deep, like a cloak in the night,  
Waters once high, mountains reach their height.  

"At my rebuke they fled," the thunder's roar,  
Valleys sank down, mountains rose evermore.  

Set boundaries firm, waters can't cross the line,  
Springs in valleys flow, in mountains they twine.  

Animals drink, wild donkeys find their fill,  
Birds nest nearby, songs echo on the hill.  

Waters nourish peaks, earth fruitful with display,  
Grass grows for livestock, plants men cultivate each day.  

Wine for glad hearts, oil for faces to shine,  
Bread for strength, all works of divine design.  

Yahweh's trees watered, cedars tall and grand,  
Birds make nests, storks find homes in this land.  

Mountains for wild goats, rocks hide the badgers small,  
Moon marks the seasons, sun knows when to fall.  

Darkness turns to night, forest creatures prowl,  
Young lions roar, seeking food on the prowl.  

Sunrise sends them back to dens they rest,  
Man goes out, works hard till evening's crest.  

How many are Your works, in wisdom's might,  
Earth full with riches, in day and night.  

Great sea wide, full of creatures, small and grand,  
Ships sail, leviathan plays in the sand.  

All wait for You, their food You provide,  
Open Your hand, satisfied, they abide.  

Hide Your face, troubled they become,  
Take breath away, to dust they succumb.  

Send Your Spirit, creation anew,  
Renew the ground, life begins anew.  

Yahweh's glory endure, His works bring Him joy,  
Look, earth trembles, mountains smoke, never coy.  

Sing to Yahweh as long as I have breath,  
Praise my God till the day of my death.  

May my thoughts be sweet to Him, in His light,  
Rejoice in Yahweh, my soul takes flight.  

"Let sinners vanish, wicked be no more,"  
Bless Yahweh, my soul, praise Him evermore!
