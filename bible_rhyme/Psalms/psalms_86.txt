Hear me, Yahweh, I'm down, I plead,  
In need of your presence, your speed indeed.  
Protect my soul, for I am yours,  
Your servant who trusts, my spirit implores.  

"Mercy, Lord," I call all day,  
Lift my soul, show me the way.  
You're good, forgiving, love so wide,  
To all who reach, you open wide.  

Hear my voice, my prayer ascends,  
In trouble, I call, your help transcends.  
No god like you, none can compare,  
None do deeds like you declare.  

Nations gather to worship your fame,  
Glorifying, lifting your name.  
You're wondrous, great, the only one,  
Teach me your ways, I'm not done.  

With all my heart, I'll praise your might,  
Glorify your name, day and night.  
Your kindness saves from depths so low,  
From Sheol's grip, you've bid me go.  

The proud rise up, they seek my fall,  
But you, Lord, are my all.  
Merciful God, gracious, slow to wrath,  
Overflowing love, truth in your path.  

"Turn to me, show mercy," I plea,  
"Strengthen your servant, rescue me."  
Show me a sign, let my foes see,  
That you, Yahweh, stand with me.
