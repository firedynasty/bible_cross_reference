Deliver me, Yahweh, from the evil crew,  
Protect me from violent men, what they might do.  
Plotting mischief deep in their hearts,  
Always gearing up, ready for war to start.  

Tongues sharp as a serpent's bite,  
Viper's poison, they speak with spite.  
Yahweh, guard me from wicked hands,  
From violent schemes and their devious plans.  

The proud set traps on my way,  
Cords of a net, hoping I'll stray.  
I called to Yahweh, "You're my God, my plea,"  
Listen to my cries, Lord, hear and see.  

Yahweh, my strength, my battle shield,  
You've covered my head, my protector in the field.  
"Don't grant the wicked their desires,  
Don't let them rise, fueled by their fires."  

Let their own words bring them down,  
Burning coals on their heads, let them drown.  
Flames consume them, pits hold them tight,  
Let them never rise, lost in the night.  

The evil speaker won't rule this earth,  
Violence hunts him, his pride not worth.  
Yahweh stands for the afflicted and weak,  
Justice for the needy, His words they seek.  

Surely the righteous will give thanks,  
In Your presence, the upright ranks.
