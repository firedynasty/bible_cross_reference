Yahweh's my shepherd, won't need a thing,  
In green pastures, He lets me sing.  

Leads me to waters, calm and still,  
Restores my soul, with His will.  

Guides me right, for His name’s sake,  
In His path, no mistake I'll make.  

Through death's valley, shadows fall,  
But no fear, He's there through it all.  

"With my rod and staff, I'll comfort you,"  
His promise true, His love shines through.  

A table set, enemies all around,  
With oil, my head's anointed, profound.  

Cup overflowin', blessings outpour,  
Goodness and mercy, forevermore.  

In Yahweh's house, I'll always stay,  
With His love, all fears away.
