Clap your hands, nations, shout and rejoice,  
To God with triumph, lift your voice.  
Yahweh Most High, awesome and grand,  
A great King ruling over every land.  

He brings nations low, under our feet,  
Chooses inheritance, Jacob's seat.  
God rises up with a mighty shout,  
Trumpet sounds, His power's about.  

"Sing praises to God, lift His name high,  
Praise the King, let your voices fly!"  
King of the earth, He reigns supreme,  
Sing with understanding, fulfill the dream.  

God's on the throne, watching the lands,  
Princes and peoples join in His hands.  
For all shields, earth's might, belong to Him,  
Exalted greatly, His light won't dim!
