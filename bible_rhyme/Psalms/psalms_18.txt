I love you, Yahweh, my strength, my might,  
My rock and fortress, my guiding light.  

I call on Yahweh, praised is his name,  
Enemies fall, they're lost in his flame.  

Death's cords surrounded, fear's flood came,  
In distress, I called, whispered his name.  

He heard my voice, it reached his throne,  
Earth shook, mountains quaked, his anger shown.  

Smoke from his nostrils, fire did roar,  
He came down dark, on wings he soar.  

He made darkness hide his face,  
Thunder, hailstones, fire's embrace.  

"Yahweh thundered," his voice did declare,  
Arrows scattered foes, lightning bare.  

Waters parted, world laid bare,  
Yahweh's breath sent through the air.  

High he reached, pulled me from waves,  
Enemies crushed, Yahweh saves.  

In my crisis, he was my support,  
He led me to safety, a large resort.  

Rewarded me for my righteous ways,  
Clean hands saw brighter days.  

Kept his laws always in sight,  
Blameless, pure, walked in his light.  

With the merciful, merciful you are,  
With the perfect, perfect by far.  

Save the humble, bring pride low,  
Light my lamp, let glory show.  

Through the troop, leap the wall,  
Yahweh's word, my perfect call.  

My strength, my way, like deer I run,  
Teaching hands, battles won.  

Shield of salvation, I stand tall,  
Gentle lift, I won't fall.  

Pursue and conquer, enemies flee,  
Armed with strength, Yahweh's decree.  

Enemies turn, their cries in vain,  
Dust to wind, like streets, no gain.  

Head of nations, strangers serve,  
They hear, they obey, as I deserve.  

Yahweh lives! My rock, my salvation,  
Grateful I'll sing among every nation.  

Great deliverance to his king bestowed,  
Loving kindness to David's line flowed.
