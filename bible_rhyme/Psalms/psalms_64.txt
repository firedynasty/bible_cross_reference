Listen up, God, hear my plea,  
Keep me safe from the enemy.  

Hide me from their wicked scheme,  
From the noisy crowd with a dangerous dream.  

Sharper than swords, their tongues take aim,  
Deadly words, they're playing a game.  

They shoot the innocent, swift and sly,  
Out of nowhere, no fear in their eye.  

They hype up evil, planning in the night,  
"Who will see us? We're out of sight!"  

They plot injustice, say their plan is tight,  
But man's heart and mind aren't always right.  

God will strike with His own arrow, swift,  
And suddenly, they'll feel the rift.  

Their own words will be their fall,  
People will shake their heads at all.  

All will fear, seeing God's great deed,  
And ponder wisely, planting righteous seed.  

The righteous rejoice in Yahweh's name,  
Find refuge in Him, and praise His fame!
