Yahweh reigns, clothed in majesty's might,  
Strength surrounds Him, like day follows night.  

The world is firm, it won't be swayed,  
His throne's from the start, never decayed.  

Floods rise up, their voices loud,  
Waves crash, like thunder from a cloud.  

Over the roar of seas so grand,  
Yahweh's might forever stands.  

His laws are firm, steadfast they stay,  
Holiness wraps His house each day.  

Yahweh's power forever more,  
In His presence, we explore.
