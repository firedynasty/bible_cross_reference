Praise Yah! The man who fears Him, stands tall,  
Delights in His commands, he won't fall.  

His kids are mighty, they rule the land,  
Upright generations, blessed by His hand.  

Wealth and riches fill his home,  
His righteousness stands, never to roam.  

Light breaks the dark for the gracious, it's clear,  
Mercy and righteousness draw near.  

It’s all good for the man who lends,  
He stands firm in judgment, never bends.  

Never shaken, the righteous stand,  
Forever remembered throughout the land.  

No fear of bad news, heart's steady,  
Trusting Yahweh, always ready.  

His heart's strong, no fear when foes appear,  
He sees them crumble, conquering fear.  

He gives to the poor, shares his gain,  
Righteousness forever, honor in his name.  

The wicked see, they gnash and melt away,  
Their desires perish, vanish, decay.
