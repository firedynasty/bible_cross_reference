"God, give the king your justice, your righteous decree,  
So he can judge the people fair, let the poor be free.  

Mountains bring prosperity, hills yield righteousness,  
He saves the needy’s children, breaks the oppressor's stress.  

"They'll fear you while the sun shines, and as long as the moon,  
Throughout all generations, let your justice be the tune."  

He comes like rain on mown grass, like showers on the earth,  
In his days, peace will flourish, righteous deeds will birth.  

His dominion stretches wide, from sea to sea he'll reign,  
From the River's mighty flow to earth's farthest plain.  

Wilderness folk bow down, his foes taste the dust,  
Kings bring their tribute, their honor is a must.  

The kings of Tarshish, islands far, bring gifts in their hand,  
Sheba and Seba's rulers, before him they will stand.  

All kings shall bow before him, every nation serve,  
When the needy cry for help, his compassion won’t swerve.  

He hears the poor, delivers those without a voice,  
Redeems their lives from violence, makes the oppressed rejoice.  

Their blood is precious in his sight, their lives he’ll protect,  
Gold from Sheba they will bring, in his name they’ll reflect.  

Abundance fills the land, grain sways like Lebanon,  
Let fields flourish, thrive like grass, under the rising sun.  

His name will last forever, as long as the sun burns bright,  
Men will find their blessing in him, in his glory delight.  

"Praise Yahweh, God of Israel, who alone does wondrous things,  
Let his name be blessed forever, while all earth his glory sings!"  

Amen, amen, so it ends, the prayers of David's fame,  
The son of Jesse’s legacy, forever bless his name.
