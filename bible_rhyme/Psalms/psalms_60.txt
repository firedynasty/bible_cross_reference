God, you've rejected, you've broken us, torn,  
In anger, we plead, mend us, reborn.  

The land, it trembles, shattered and quakes,  
Fractures in need of mending, for our sakes.  

Shown us hardships, made us stagger and sway,  
Drank the wine of trials, day by day.  

Given a banner to those who still fear,  
Displayed for truth, loud and clear.  

"Save your beloved, with your right hand,  
Answer our cries, deliver this land."  

God speaks from His high, holy abode,  
"I'll triumph, divide Shechem, measure the road.  

Gilead's mine, and Manasseh too,  
Ephraim's my defense, Judah my scepter true.  

Moab's my basin, on Edom I throw,  
My sandal, triumphs over Philistia, I know."  

Who’ll lead to the strong city’s gate?  
Who guides me to Edom's fate?  

Haven't you, God, turned away your face?  
No armies march with your grace.  

Help us, God, against enemy's line,  
Man’s aid is empty, your strength divine.  

Through God, valiant deeds we'll claim,  
He treads down foes, in His mighty name.
