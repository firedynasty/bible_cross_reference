Yahweh, don’t hit me with your blazing heat,  
Or chasten me with anger, don’t bring that beat.  

Your arrows pierce, your hand weighs down,  
In my flesh, soundness can't be found.  

No health in bones, sin wraps my crown,  
A burden so heavy, it makes me drown.  

Wounds are foul, from foolish days,  
Pain bows me low, in mournful ways.  

Burning waist, no soundness to feel,  
Faint and bruised, a heart can't heal.  

Lord, you see my every plea,  
My heart's cry's not a mystery.  

Heart throbs hard, strength's run dry,  
No light left, just sightless eyes.  

Friends and lovers stand afar,  
Kin avoid me, like a scar.  

Hunters trap, with deceit they play,  
They scheme against me, every day.  

Like a deaf and mute I stand,  
Silent as the desert sand.  

Hope in you, Yahweh, I place my plea,  
“Don't let them gloat, don’t let them see.”  

Ready to fall, pain's here to stay,  
I’ll confess my sins, in a heartfelt way.  

Enemies strong, without cause they fight,  
Hating good, they shun the light.  

Don’t leave me, Yahweh, stay close by,  
Hurry, Lord, my salvation's cry.
