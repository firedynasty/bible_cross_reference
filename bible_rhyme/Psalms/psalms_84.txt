How lovely are your dwellings, God divine,  
My soul longs, faints for courts so fine.  

Heart and flesh cry out, living God I see,  
Sparrow finds a home, a nest, a sanctuary.  

Near your altars, our King and God we stand,  
Blessed are those who dwell in your land.  

Strength in you, hearts on a holy quest,  
Through Weeping Valley, they find their rest.  

Springs appear, autumn rains bring grace,  
Strength to strength, they seek your face.  

"Yahweh, hear my prayer," Jacob's God, attend,  
Shield us, look upon your anointed, friend.  

One day in your courts beats a thousand elsewhere,  
A doorkeeper in your house, nothing can compare.  

Yahweh's a sun and shield, grace and glory unfold,  
He gives all good to the blameless, bold.  

Yahweh of Armies, blessed trust in you,  
With faith so strong, our spirits renew.
