Yo, Lord, you've been our home through every generation's span,  
Before mountains were born, before the earth began.  

From everlasting to everlasting, God you stand tall,  
You turn man to dust, saying, "Return, children, heed my call."  

A thousand years in your sight are like yesterday's gone,  
Like a watch in the night, just a fleeting dawn.  

You sweep us away, while we sleep, like grass anew,  
In the morning it sprouts, by evening it's through.  

In your anger we're consumed, your wrath we face,  
Our sins laid bare before you, every secret in its place.  

Our days pass in your wrath, years end with a sigh,  
Seventy, eighty if strong, then we fly high.  

Who knows your anger's power, the fear it's due?  
Teach us to count days, gain wisdom anew.  

"Relent, Yahweh! How long? Show compassion," we pray,  
Satisfy us with your love, make us glad today.  

Make us rejoice as many days as we've seen strife,  
Let your works and glory shine in every child's life.  

Favor us, Lord, let your glory expand,  
Establish, establish the work of our hands.
