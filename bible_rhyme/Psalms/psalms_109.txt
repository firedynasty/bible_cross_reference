Yo, God of my praise, don’t stay quiet, hear it ring,  
They spit deceit, like a serpent's sting.  

Lies surround me, words filled with hate,  
Fighting for no reason, my love they negate.  

I loved, but they made me their foe,  
Yet here I am, praying, you know.  

Evil they give for good, hate for my love’s embrace,  
Put a wicked man over him, replace his place.  

"Let him be judged, let guilt be his song,  
Turn his prayers into sin, where they belong."  

"Let his days be short, his office taken away,  
His kids left wandering, begging each day."  

"Seize all his goods, let strangers plunder his toil,  
No one to show kindness, his children on desolate soil."  

"Cut off their lineage, their name erased,  
Sin remembered by Yahweh, no disgrace replaced."  

"Curse him, he loved it, let it seep in like rain,  
Like oil in his bones, like a garment of pain."  

This is Yahweh's reward for foes who've dared,  
Those who've spoken against my soul, unprepared.  

But Yahweh, deal kindly with me, your name I'll proclaim,  
Your loving kindness saves, free me from shame.  

I'm poor and needy, my heart feels torn,  
Fading like shadows, from me, strength's been shorn.  

Knees weak from fasting, thin and frail,  
They see me, mock me, my strength they curtail.  

"Help me, my God, with love's embrace,  
Show them your hand, your justice in place."  

"They curse, but you bless, let shame be their robe,  
I'll rejoice as they fall, let their shame corrode."  

I'll thank Yahweh, my voice loud and clear,  
In the crowd, his praise I'll declare sincere.  

For he stands with the needy, right there by his side,  
To save from judgment, in Yahweh, I abide.
