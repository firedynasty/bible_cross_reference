"Yahweh, don't strike me in your blazing heat,  
Don't lay your wrath on me, make my suffering retreat.  

Have mercy, Yahweh, I'm weak and worn,  
Heal these bones that feel so torn.  

My soul's in anguish, deep and strong,  
But you, Yahweh—how long, how long?  

Return to me, Yahweh, save my soul,  
For your love's sake, make me whole.  

In death there's no memory, no thanks to give,  
In Sheol's silence, who can live?  

I'm weary with my cries, my bed's a flood,  
My couch is drenched, soaked with my blood.  

My eyes grow dim from grief and fear,  
They age from enemies drawing near.  

"Depart from me, you wicked crowd," I say,  
Yahweh has heard my cries today.  

Yahweh heard my plea, took my prayer,  
My enemies will scatter in fear and despair.  

Let them be ashamed, dismayed and torn,  
They'll turn back, disgraced and worn."
