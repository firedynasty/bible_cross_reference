Yo, I call on you, Yahweh, come fast to my plea,  
Listen to my voice as I call urgently.  

Let my prayer rise like incense, sweet and bright,  
Hands lifted up like the sacrifice of night.  

"Guard my mouth," I ask, keep my lips in check,  
Don't let my heart drift to paths that wreck.  

Keep me from deeds with those who do wrong,  
Don't let me taste their sweet, seductive song.  

If the righteous strike, it's a kindness, a guide,  
Like oil on my head, a blessing I don't hide.  

Judges fall down, like rocks tumbled and tossed,  
They hear my words, for they ain't lost.  

"Our bones scattered like earth when it's torn,"  
Yet my eyes on you, Yahweh, where refuge is sworn.  

Keep me safe from traps they've set,  
From snares and plans in the dark net.  

Let the wicked tumble in their own tricky threads,  
While I walk free, past their tangled beds.
