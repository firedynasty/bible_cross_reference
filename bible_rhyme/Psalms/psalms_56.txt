Be merciful, God, the enemy's intense,  
All day they attack, I feel the suspense.  

Enemies surround, proud in their fight,  
When fear grips my heart, I lean on Your might.  

In God, I praise, trust in His word,  
What can mere flesh do, when Your voice is heard?  

Twisting my words, plotting with spite,  
Conspiring, they lurk, in shadows at night.  

"Will they escape?" I ask with a plea,  
In Your anger, bring justice, set the captives free.  

You see my tears, like raindrops in a jar,  
My wanderings counted, from near to afar.  

Enemies retreat when I call Your name,  
God's by my side, He's in my frame.  

In God, I praise, His word I hold dear,  
What can man do? I've got nothing to fear.  

Your vows are with me, offerings I bring,  
You've saved my soul, now let my spirit sing.  

You’ve kept my feet, from a slippery fall,  
I walk in Your light, answer to Your call.
