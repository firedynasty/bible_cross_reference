Listen up, people, hear what I say,  
I'll share the wisdom from back in the day.  

Stories of old, our fathers told,  
Praising Yahweh, his deeds are bold.  

He made a covenant with Jacob's line,  
Taught Israel laws, set by design.  

Kids would know, through generations past,  
"Set your hope in God," make it last.  

Ephraim's children armed for the fight,  
But turned back, didn't do what's right.  

Forgot God's wonders, deeds of renown,  
In Egypt's fields, where he came down.  

Parted the sea, waters stood tall,  
Led by cloud, fire at nightfall.  

Rocks split open, streams flowed wide,  
Rivers in desert, God did provide.  

But still they sinned, their hearts weren't true,  
"Can God provide food?" they asked anew.  

"Struck the rock, waters flowed, that's clear,  
But can he feed us in the desert here?"  

Yahweh heard and his anger rose,  
"Didn't trust me, despite what I chose."  

He opened skies, manna rained down,  
Bread of angels, spread all around.  

He sent birds, like sand in the breeze,  
Filled their camp, met their needs with ease.  

Yet with full bellies, they craved for more,  
And God's anger burned at their core.  

Some were struck down, yet they didn't learn,  
Days in vanity, years they’d burn.  

When struck, they sought him, oh so keen,  
Remembered God, their rock unseen.  

But their words were false, heart not in line,  
Yet God forgave, his mercy shined.  

He knew they were flesh, a wind that flies,  
In the desert again, they grieved his eyes.  

Forgot his power, his saving hand,  
Plagues in Egypt, through that land.  

Rivers to blood, frogs running amok,  
Flies, hail, pestilence, Egypt struck.  

Firstborn fell, a sorrowful night,  
But led his people out of fright.  

Guided them through, seas split wide,  
Enemies drowned, none could abide.  

To his holy mountain, land he gave,  
Yet still they rebelled, couldn't behave.  

Idols they made, anger they stirred,  
God's tent left, his voice unheard.  

Enemies triumphed, youth lost their song,  
But God awoke, where he belonged.  

Struck enemies back, chose his own seed,  
Judah and Zion, where he'd lead.  

David, the shepherd, called to guide,  
Led with skill, with heart open wide.
