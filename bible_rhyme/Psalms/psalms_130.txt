Out of the depths, I cried loud and clear,  
"Yahweh, hear my voice, lend an ear."  
If you kept track of sins, who could stand tall?  
But forgiveness with you, so we fear and call.  

I wait on Yahweh, my soul's in a trance,  
Hoping in His word, like a sacred dance.  
My soul longs for Him, like night waits for dawn,  
More eager than watchmen, till the light is drawn.  

Israel, put your hope in Yahweh's embrace,  
For loving kindness flows, endless in its grace.  
Abundant redemption, that's what He brings,  
He'll redeem Israel, cleansing all their sins.
