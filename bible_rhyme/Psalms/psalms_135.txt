Yo, listen up, praise Yah's name, loud and clear,  
All you servants of Yah, come stand right here.  

In the courts of God's house, lift your voice,  
Praise Yah, He's good, make a joyful noise.  

Jacob's been chosen, Israel's His own,  
Yahweh's greatness, well-known and shown.  

Above all gods, our Lord takes His stand,  
In heaven and earth, He moves His hand.  

Clouds rise up, lightning in the rain,  
Wind from His treasuries, again and again.  

Firstborn struck in Egypt, man and beast,  
Signs and wonders, Yahweh's feast.  

Mighty kings fell, nations He laid,  
Sihon and Og, by His might swayed.  

Canaan's kingdoms, land given to the bold,  
Heritage to Israel, His story told.  

Yahweh's name, forever it shall stay,  
Compassionate judge, leading the way.  

Idols of silver, mere works of man,  
Mouths don't speak; they don't understand.  

Eyes that see not, ears that can't hear,  
No breath within, their fate is clear.  

Makers and trusters, they'll be just like these,  
Empty and hollow, with no soul to please.  

House of Israel, lift Yahweh's name high,  
House of Aaron, to the sky, let praises fly.  

Levi's house, join the praise parade,  
You who fear Yahweh, in His glory wade.  

Bless Yah from Zion, where He resides,  
In Jerusalem, His presence abides.  

Praise Yah, from every corner, every place,  
Let His name be lifted, in every space.
