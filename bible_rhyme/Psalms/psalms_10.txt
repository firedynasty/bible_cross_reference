Why stand so far, Lord, when trouble's near?  
The wicked prowl, with schemes so clear.  

Boastful hearts, they flaunt their ways,  
Bless the greedy, in darkness stays.  

Prideful face, no room for God,  
Prosperous paths, on evil they trod.  

"Unshaken," they say, in their heart's deceit,  
Generations of trouble, they'll never meet.  

Cursing, deceit, oppression's his song,  
Under his tongue, mischief and wrong.  

Lurking by villages, in shadows he waits,  
Ambushing innocents, sealing their fates.  

Like a lion, he pounces, with a hidden net,  
Catching the helpless, his trap is set.  

Crushed and collapsed, they fall to the ground,  
"God's forgotten," he whispers, not making a sound.  

"Arise, Yahweh! Lift Your hand high!  
Don't forget the helpless, hear their cry."  

Why does the wicked scorn and sneer,  
Thinking, "God won't call me near"?  

But you see it all, the grief, the strife,  
Taking the victim, giving them life.  

Break the wicked, turn the tide,  
Seek out evil, make none hide.  

Yahweh's King, forever reign,  
Nations will perish, but His truth remain.  

You've heard the humble, their heart's desire,  
You'll judge the oppressed, justice will conspire.  

No more terror from earthly men,  
With Yahweh's hand, peace will begin.
