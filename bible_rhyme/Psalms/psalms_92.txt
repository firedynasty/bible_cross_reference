It's a good thing to give thanks, let praises fly high,  
To speak of your love, Most High, and never be shy.  

In the morning light, your kindness we proclaim,  
At night, your faithfulness, always the same.  

With a ten-stringed lute, and a harp in my hand,  
The melody of the lyre echoes through the land.  

You made me glad with the work of your hand,  
I triumph in your wonders, across all the land.  

Oh Yahweh, how great are your mighty works!  
Deep are your thoughts, no fool understands or lurks.  

The wicked rise like grass, flourishing, it seems,  
But forever they'll be scattered, losing their dreams.  

"But you, Yahweh, are on high, forever more,"  
Your enemies, Yahweh, shall perish, no score.  

You've lifted my horn like the wild ox strong,  
Anointed me with fresh oil, where I belong.  

I've seen my foes, heard the wicked threats they send,  
But the righteous flourish like palm trees, no end.  

Grow like cedars in Lebanon, tall and true,  
Planted in God's house, flourishing in view.  

Even in old age, their fruit will never cease,  
Full of sap, green, grounded in God's peace.  

Showing Yahweh’s upright, the rock where I stand,  
In Him, no unrighteousness, steady and grand.
