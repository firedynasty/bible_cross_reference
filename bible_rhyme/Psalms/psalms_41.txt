Blessed is the one who cares for the poor,  
Yahweh saves him when troubles roar.  

He keeps him alive, gives blessing on land,  
Enemies' will, he will withstand.  

On his sickbed, Yahweh sustains,  
Lifts him from illness, breaks the chains.  

I cry out, "Yahweh, have mercy, heal me,  
For I've sinned, let your grace set me free."  

Enemies whisper, plotting my fall,  
"When will his name vanish once and for all?"  

They visit and lie, spreading deceit,  
Gathering rumors, they repeat.  

Haters gather, they conspire and plot,  
"Evil disease, his end is his lot."  

Even my friend, one I held dear,  
Turns on me now, lifts his heel near.  

But you, Yahweh, mercy I plead,  
Raise me up to repay each deed.  

I know you delight in me, I see,  
Enemies' triumphs are not meant to be.  

Uphold me in truth, forever I stand,  
In your presence, by your hand.  

Blessed be Yahweh, from age to age,  
Amen and amen, let all engage.
