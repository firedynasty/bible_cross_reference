Mercy, God, I plea, my soul seeks your embrace,  
In the shadow of your wings, I find my safe place.  

I cry out to the Most High, with requests on my tongue,  
He sends help from heaven, where his love is sung.  

He saves me, rebukes those chasing me with might,  
Sends loving kindness and truth, like stars in the night.  

My soul's among lions, in a fiery scene,  
Men with teeth like spears, tongues sharp and keen.  

"Be exalted, God, above the heavens so wide!  
Let your glory fill the earth, like the morning tide!"  

They've set a net, my steps to confound,  
But they fall in their pit, while I stand on solid ground.  

My heart is steadfast, God, like a mountain high,  
I'll sing praises to You, let my voice reach the sky.  

"Wake up, my glory! Lute and harp, arise!  
I'll wake the dawn, let praises touch the skies."  

Thanks to you, Lord, among peoples I proclaim,  
Your loving kindness reaches the heavens, truth in your name.  

"Be exalted, God, above the heavens' expanse!  
Let your glory cover the earth, in a holy dance."
