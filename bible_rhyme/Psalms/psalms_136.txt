Give thanks to Yahweh, 'cause His goodness is tight,  
His loving kindness shines, day and night.  

To the God of gods, raise your voice,  
His love endures, let's all rejoice.  

To the Lord of lords, forever true,  
His wonders, unmatched, in all He'll do.  

He alone does great things, can't compare,  
His love's everlasting, always there.  

With wisdom, He made the heavens wide,  
His love's like an ocean, never to hide.  

Spread the earth above the waters, He laid,  
His boundless love, it never fades.  

He made great lights, the sun so bright,  
By day it's His love, ruling the height.  

Moon and stars, dance in the night,  
His endless love, always in sight.  

He struck Egypt's firstborn, set the scene,  
His love endures, through all we've seen.  

Brought Israel out, strong hand, stretched arm,  
His love's a shield, keeping from harm.  

Split the Red Sea, right down the line,  
His love held firm, divine design.  

Israel passed through, safe and sound,  
His love's a fortress, all around.  

Pharaoh's army, in the sea cast,  
His love enduring, first to last.  

Through the wilderness, He did lead,  
His love, a light, meeting every need.  

Struck down kings, mighty and great,  
His love's a victory, sealing their fate.  

Sihon and Og, kingdoms fell,  
His love the story, we always tell.  

Gave their land to Israel's hand,  
His love's inheritance, promised land.  

Remembered us low, in darkest place,  
His love's a rescue, His saving grace.  

Delivered us from every foe,  
His love, a river, forever flows.  

Feeds every creature on this earth,  
His love, the source of endless worth.  

Give thanks to the God of heaven above,  
His love's eternal, overflowing with love.
