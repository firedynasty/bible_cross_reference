In You, Yahweh, I find my place, never let me fall from grace.  
Deliver me right, make haste, be my fortress, my safe space.  

You're my rock, my fortress high, for Your name, lead and guide my way.  
Pluck me from the hidden trap, my stronghold, don't delay.  

In Your hands, my spirit stays, You redeem, Yahweh, God of truth.  
I shun falsehood and trust; in You, Yahweh, I find my proof.  

I'll rejoice, glad in Your kindness, You’ve seen my pain, known my plight.  
You set my feet on spacious ground, guard me from the foe's spite.  

"Have mercy, Yahweh," my cry beneath the weight of grief and strain,  
Life wasted in sorrow's years, strength failing, bones in pain.  

I'm scorned by foes, my neighbors' dread, they flee like shadows on the street,  
Forgotten like a broken shard, in terror's grip, I find deceit.  

But my trust in You is strong, "You're my God," I firmly say.  
Save me from my enemies' grasp, in Your hands my times stay.  

Make Your face shine on me, save me through loving grace,  
Don't let me falter, let the wicked fall from place.  

Mute the boastful lips of pride, lies spoken with disdain,  
Your goodness vast for those who seek, refuge in Your name remain.  

Hide them in Your presence, sheltered from man's plot and snare,  
Praise to Yahweh, love and kindness in a strong city's care.  

In haste, I thought I was cut off, but You heard my call, my plight.  
Love Yahweh, all you saints; He guards the faithful, gives the proud their right.  

Be strong, take heart, find courage here, all who put hope in Yahweh's light.
