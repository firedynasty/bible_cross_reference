Make a joyful noise, let the earth shout loud,  
Sing His name’s glory, lift it up, be proud.  

“Awesome are your deeds,” to God we say,  
Enemies bow down, in your power's sway.  

All earth will worship, sing to you in praise,  
They'll honor your name, in so many ways.  

Come see His works, they're truly grand,  
He split the sea, dry was the land.  

Through rivers they walked, rejoiced in Him then,  
For His rule is mighty, watching over all men.  

"Don't let the rebellious rise," He commands,  
Praise Him, let His sound fill the lands.  

He keeps us living, we won't be swayed,  
In His safe hands, we’ve stayed.  

God, you've tested, refined like silver’s glow,  
In prison, burdened, you let us know.  

Men over our heads, through fire, we trod,  
But you led us to abundance, oh God.  

Into your temple, vows I’ll fulfill,  
Burnt offerings I’ll bring, as I promised, I will.  

Rams, bulls, and goats, my gifts to you,  
In my distress, my words will stay true.  

Come hear, fear God, let me tell you all,  
He saved my soul, heard my call.  

Cried with my mouth, He heard the plea,  
Sin cherished would have turned Him from me.  

But God has listened, my prayer He heard,  
Blessed be God, who kept His word.  

Loving kindness never turned away,  
In His embrace, forever I’ll stay.
