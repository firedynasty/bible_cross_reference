In Yahweh, I find my peace, my rest,  
"Why run like a bird, leave my soul in a nest?"  

The wicked, they’re ready, they pull back their bows,  
Arrows in darkness aimed at upright souls.  

If the world's foundation starts to quake and shift,  
What can the righteous do, where’s their uplift?  

Yahweh's in his temple, high on heaven's throne,  
His eyes watch the world, every heart's known.  

He sees the righteous, but the wicked's His foe,  
Loves violence, His soul says, "No."  

On the wicked, He’ll rain down fire and coals,  
Sulfur and wind, their portion, their roles.  

For Yahweh is just, loves what is right,  
The upright shall see His face, in His light.
