Save me, God, with your power, make it right,  
Hear my prayer, Lord, listen tonight.  

Strangers rise, seeking my soul's end,  
Violent men, like a storm, they descend.  

But God’s my helper, my soul’s strong tower,  
The Lord holds me up, hour by hour.  

He’ll pay back the evil, my foes will fall,  
"Destroy them in truth," hear my call.  

With open heart, I'll bring my praise,  
Thanking Yahweh, through my days.  

Delivered from trouble, I'm standing tall,  
My eyes see triumph, over them all.
