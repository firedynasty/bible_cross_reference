God's our refuge, our strength, no disguise,  
In the midst of trouble, He's right by our side.  

Earth may change, mountains dive in the sea,  
Roaring waters, but we won't flee.  

A river flows, making God's city glad,  
Holy ground where joy's never sad.  

God's within her, she stands unshaken,  
At dawn, His help is awaken.  

Nations rage, kingdoms fall from grace,  
His voice roars, earth melts in place.  

Yahweh's with us, our fortress, our shield,  
Come see His works, the power revealed.  

Wars cease, bows break, spears shatter,  
Chariots burn, nothing else matters.  

"Be still, know I'm God," He speaks with might,  
"Exalted in nations, in all earth's sight."  

Yahweh's with us, our refuge so strong,  
In the God of Jacob, we belong.
