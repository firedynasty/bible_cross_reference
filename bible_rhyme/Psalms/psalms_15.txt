Who can chill in your holy space?  
Who walks with honor, leaves no trace?  

The one who's straight, truth on his lips,  
No gossip, no lies, no sneaky trips.  

No harm to friends, no shady deals,  
Says what he means, keeps it real.  

Sees wickedness and turns away,  
Honors those who walk Yahweh's way.  

Keeps his word, even when it stings,  
No change of heart, no backtracking.  

No loan shark play, no bribe in hand,  
Stands firm and strong, won't sink in sand.  

Those who live this way, they’ll never fall,  
Steady and true, standing tall.
