Help, Yahweh, the good are gone, they cease to exist,  
The faithful ones fade, like mist in the midst.  

Neighbors lying, heart's deceit, lips that flatter,  
Double-talkers, scheming like it don't even matter.  

Cut off those lips, those tongues that boast,  
They claim, "With words, we rule the most."  

They say, "Our lips are ours alone, who's the boss?"  
But they forget, true power ain't in the gloss.  

Yahweh hears the cries, the weak oppressed,  
"I'll rise now," He says, "give the needy rest."  

His words, pure as silver, flawless and bright,  
Refined seven times, shining in the night.  

Yahweh, you'll guard them, keep them secure,  
From this crooked world, Your protection's sure.  

The wicked circle 'round, vile things they praise,  
In such a world, keep us in Your ways.
