Shout out loud, all lands, let joy expand!  
Serve with gladness, His presence so grand.  

Know Yahweh's God, He made us, we're His own,  
We're His people, sheep in His pasture, not alone.  

Enter His gates with thanksgiving, hearts ablaze,  
Step into His courts filled with praise.  

Give thanks to Him, bless His name with delight,  
For Yahweh is good, His love's our guiding light.  

His kindness forever, faithful through the ages,  
His truth endures, written on history's pages.
