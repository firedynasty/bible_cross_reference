I'll lift you up, my God, my King,  
Praising your name, forever I'll sing.  

Each day I'll raise a brand-new song,  
Forever your praises, all day long.  

Great is Yahweh, beyond all measure,  
His greatness a vast, unsearchable treasure.  

One generation to the next will tell,  
Of mighty acts that ring like a bell.  

I'll think on your majesty, so grand,  
Your wondrous works, all you’ve planned.  

They'll speak of your power, your awesome might,  
I'll declare your greatness, shining so bright.  

The memory of your goodness will flow,  
With songs of righteousness, hearts aglow.  

Yahweh is gracious, merciful, slow to ire,  
His loving kindness, a consuming fire.  

Good to all, tender mercies abound,  
Over all his works, his love is found.  

All your works will thank you, Yahweh, loud,  
Your saints will lift you, among the crowd.  

They'll speak of glory, kingdom's power,  
Proclaiming your might in this very hour.  

Your kingdom's everlasting, forever it stands,  
Your dominion throughout all lands.  

Yahweh, faithful in word and deed,  
Upholds the fallen, meets their need.  

Eyes look to you, for food you provide,  
You open your hand, and all are satisfied.  

Righteous in ways, gracious in acts,  
Yahweh is near when truth attracts.  

He fulfills desires of those with fear,  
Hearing their cries, he'll draw near.  

Yahweh preserves all those who love,  
But destroys the wicked from above.  

My mouth will praise Yahweh's fame,  
Let all bless his holy name.
