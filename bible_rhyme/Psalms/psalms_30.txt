Raised me up, Yahweh, my foes can't cheer,  
I cried to you, God, and you brought me clear.  

You lifted my soul from the realm of the dead,  
Kept me alive, from the pit I was led.  

Sing to Yahweh, saints, his name declare,  
Anger but a moment, favor everywhere.  

Weeping may stay for the night, it's true,  
But joy in the morning, fresh and new.  

I thought in my wealth, "I'll never fall,"  
But when you hid, Yahweh, my strength felt small.  

I cried out loud, my plea to you,  
"What good am I gone? Shall dust speak true?"  

"Hear me, Yahweh, lend your ear,  
Be my helper, draw me near."  

Turned my mourning to dancing, sackcloth gone,  
Clothed me in gladness, my heart's new song.  

So my soul may sing, never cease,  
Yahweh, my thanks, forever in peace.
