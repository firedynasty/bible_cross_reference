I'll sing of love and justice, hear my voice,  
"Praises to Yahweh," I make that choice.  
Blameless living, that's my aim,  
When will you visit? I call your name.  

In my house, my heart stays pure,  
No vile thing my eyes endure.  
Faithless deeds, I won't let near,  
Perverse hearts, I steer clear.  

Secret slanders? I'll silence that sound,  
Arrogance and pride won't hang around.  
The faithful ones, they catch my sight,  
They'll dwell with me, those who do right.  

Liars and cheats, out the door they go,  
Falsehood's presence, I won't bestow.  
Morning by morning, I'll clear the land,  
Wickedness cut off by Yahweh's hand.  

In His city, justice will stand,  
The righteous will walk this promised land.
