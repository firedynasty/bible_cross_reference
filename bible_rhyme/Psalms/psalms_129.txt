From my youth, they oppressed, tried to bring me low,  
Israel, let’s say it, they came with the blow.  
But though they struck deep, plowed furrows on my back,  
They never prevailed, they didn’t stay on track.  

Yahweh's righteous hand cut the cords of the foe,  
Those who hate Zion, let them stumble back, slow.  
Like rooftop grass, withers 'fore it can grow,  
No harvest in sight, no hands to overflow.  

No passerby blesses, no words of acclaim,  
"Yahweh’s blessing," they don’t even name.  
But I stand resilient, in Yahweh's might,  
Their plans fall to nothing, lost in the night.
