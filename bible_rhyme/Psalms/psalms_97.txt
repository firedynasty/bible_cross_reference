Yahweh reigns, let the earth shout loud,  
Islands cheer, and gather the crowd.  

Clouds roll in, the darkness swirls,  
Justice and righteousness, His throne unfurls.  

Fire before Him, scorching the foes,  
Lightning strikes, the earth it knows.  

Mountains melting like wax, in His sight,  
At the Lord's presence, day turns to night.  

Heavens speak, His righteousness so bright,  
People see His glory, in the sky's light.  

Shame on idol worship, those who boast,  
"Worship Him alone," the Heavenly Host.  

Zion heard and was filled with glee,  
Judah's daughters danced joyously.  

Yahweh exalted above all the lands,  
Above all gods, His power stands.  

Love Yahweh, hate evil's shade,  
He preserves His own, in His hand they're laid.  

Light for the righteous, gladness pure,  
Heart's uprightness will endure.  

Rejoice in Yahweh, righteous crew,  
Give thanks to His Name, holy and true.
