Come on, let's sing out loud, give a shout to Yahweh's name,  
The rock of our salvation, forever the same.  

Let’s step into his presence with thanks all around,  
Lift him up with songs, make a joyful sound.  

For Yahweh is mighty, above all, he reigns,  
In his hands the earth, the mountains, the plains.  

The sea is his, he crafted every wave,  
His hands formed the land, every valley, every cave.  

So come, let's worship, let's kneel and bow,  
Before Yahweh, our Maker, let's honor him now.  

He's our God, the shepherd, and we his sheep,  
The ones in his pasture, in his care we keep.  

"Today, if you hear his voice, don't turn away,  
Don't harden your hearts like those in the old days.  

At Meribah, when they tested and tried,  
Saw my works in the desert but still denied.  

Forty years I was grieved with their ways,  
Said, 'They err in their hearts, know not my ways.'  

In my anger I swore, 'They won't find my rest,'  
A warning to heed so you'll be forever blessed."
