In the secret place, the Most High we find,  
Resting in His shadow, peace of mind.  
"I’m your refuge, fortress," Yahweh will say,  
"My God, my trust, my strength every day."  

From the fowler's snare, He'll set you free,  
Deadly pestilence? No match for He.  
Feathers for cover, under wings you’ll hide,  
His faithfulness, your shield, your guide.  

No terror by night, no arrow in flight,  
No pestilence lurking, noonday's blight.  
A thousand fall left, ten thousand to the right,  
But it won’t touch you, you’re safe in His sight.  

You’ll witness the wicked getting their due,  
But no evil or plague will come near you.  
Made Yahweh your refuge, the Most High your place,  
His angels protect you, surround you with grace.  

"Lift you up," so your foot won’t strike a stone,  
Tread on lions, cobras, their power overthrown.  
"Set your love on Me, I’ll raise you high,  
Know My name, I’ll hear your cry."  

"Call on Me, I’ll answer, through trouble I’ll guide,  
Deliver, honor you—stay by your side.  
Long life, I’ll grant, salvation you'll see,  
For you’ve placed your heart and trust in Me."
