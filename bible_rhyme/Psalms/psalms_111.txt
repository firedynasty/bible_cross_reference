Praise Yahweh with my whole heart, I'm shoutin' it out,  
In the council and congregation, hear me, no doubt.  

His works are great, pondered by those who adore,  
Honor and majesty, His righteousness forevermore.  

His wonders remembered, gracious and merciful,  
Food for the faithful, covenant always full.  

He showed His power, the nations' heritage shared,  
Truth and justice, His hands have declared.  

His precepts are sure, forever they stand,  
In truth and uprightness, by His command.  

"Redemption I've sent, My covenant's here to stay,"  
Holy and awesome, His name we obey.  

Fear Yahweh, wisdom's start, understand His ways,  
His praise endures forever, in all our days.
