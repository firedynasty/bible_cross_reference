Hear my cry, God, lend your ear,  
I'm calling out, I need you near.  

From the earth's edge, my heart's gonna shout,  
When I'm overwhelmed, you've gotta help me out.  

Lead me to the rock, higher, grand,  
A strong tower where I can stand.  

You've been my refuge, my safe place,  
Sheltered from enemies, a hiding space.  

I’ll dwell in your tent, forever stay,  
In the shelter of your wings, night and day.  

God, you heard my vows, you know my plea,  
Given me heritage, those who fear thee.  

You'll extend the king’s life, let it be known,  
Generations to come, his years will be shown.  

Enthroned forever in God's holy sight,  
"Appoint loving kindness, truth, day and night."  

So I'll sing your praises, every day anew,  
Fulfill my vows, God, all because of you.
