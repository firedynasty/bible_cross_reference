I cry with my voice, Yahweh, hear my plea,  
I ask for your mercy, come set me free.  

I pour out my troubles, lay them all bare,  
When my spirit's overwhelmed, you know where.  

On my path, hidden snares try to trap my way,  
I look to my right, no one's here, they stray.  

Refuge has fled, no one cares for my soul,  
I cried to you, Yahweh, you're my refuge, my goal.  

"You're my portion in this land of the alive,"  
Hear my cry, I'm desperate, help me survive.  

Deliver me from those who are too strong,  
Bring my soul out, where I feel I belong.  

Thanks to your name, I’ll shout loud, I’ll sing,  
The righteous will surround, your goodness they'll bring.
