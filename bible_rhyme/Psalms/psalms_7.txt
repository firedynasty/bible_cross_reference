Yahweh, my God, I seek refuge with you,  
Save me from the hunters who chase me through.  

Lest like lions they tear, leaving my soul bare,  
No one to deliver, no help anywhere.  

If wrong be in my hands, if I’ve done evil deeds,  
If peace I betrayed, sowing hostile seeds—  

Let them chase me down, let them catch me then,  
Let them grind my life, bury me again.  

Arise, Yahweh, rise, in your anger stand tall,  
Against my enemies’ rage, hear my call.  

"Awake for the judgment," you’ve commanded,  
Let the people gather, let your rule be expanded.  

Yahweh judges all, the righteous and the wrong,  
Judge me, Yahweh, let my integrity be strong.  

Let wickedness cease, let righteousness prevail,  
Righteous God sees hearts, through the veil.  

My shield is God’s, saving the upright heart,  
God judges rightly, indignation’s never far apart.  

If man repents not, God sharpens his sword,  
His bow bent, arrows flaming, all in accord.  

Conceived mischief, birthed deceit,  
Dug a pit, fell in, his own defeat.  

Trouble circles back to crown his head,  
Violence returns, by his own hand led.  

I’ll thank Yahweh, by His righteousness I’ll sing,  
Praise to Yahweh Most High, let His name ring.
