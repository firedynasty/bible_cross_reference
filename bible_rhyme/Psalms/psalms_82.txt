In the great assembly, God's got the throne,  
Judging all the gods, making His power known.  

“How long,” He asks, “will you judge all wrong,  
Showing love to the wicked, singing their song?  

Stand for the weak, protect their place,  
Uphold the poor's rights, show them grace.  

Rescue the needy, pull them from fire,  
Free them from the wicked, lift them higher."  

They wander lost, in darkness stride,  
Earth's foundations shake, no place to hide.  

"You are gods," I said, "sons of the Most High,  
Yet like men you'll fall, just like rulers die."  

Arise, O God, let justice unfurl,  
For all nations are Yours, rule the world.
