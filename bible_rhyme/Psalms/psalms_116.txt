I love Yahweh 'cause He hears my call,  
Listens to my cries when I start to fall.  

His ear to me, He turned my way,  
I'll call on Him every single day.  

Death's cords surrounded, Sheol's grip was tight,  
Trouble and sorrow, I faced that night.  

Cried out to Yahweh, "Deliver my soul!"  
He's gracious, righteous, merciful and whole.  

Yahweh preserves, lifts the low,  
Takes me from depths to the heights I know.  

"Return to rest," I tell my soul,  
He's dealt kindly, made me whole.  

Saved my soul from death's cold hand,  
Dry my tears, help me stand.  

I'll walk before Yahweh, in life I'll roam,  
His land of living, my eternal home.  

"I was afflicted," my heart believed,  
In haste I said, "All are deceived."  

What can I give for His gracious ways?  
Lift the cup of salvation, in His name I'll praise.  

Vows to Yahweh, I'll repay,  
Among His people, every day.  

Precious is the death of His saints, I see,  
Yahweh, I'm your servant, you've set me free.  

I'll offer thanks, in joy I'll proclaim,  
In Yahweh's courts, call His name.  

In His house, in Jerusalem's heart,  
Praise Yahweh! Let the praises start.
