Have mercy, God, your loving kindness is vast,  
Blot out my wrongs, let them be in the past.  

Wash me clean 'til my sins are gone,  
Scrub me deep, leave no wrongs to dwell on.  

I know my faults, they haunt my sight,  
Against you alone have I done what's not right.  

"Born in sin," I came to be,  
Yet you seek truth inside of me.  

Purify with hyssop, make me clean,  
Wash me, make me whiter than snow unseen.  

Let joy and gladness fill my bone,  
Hide your face from sins I've known.  

"Create a clean heart," I plea,  
"Renew a right spirit within me."  

"Don't cast me from your presence, let me be,  
Keep your Holy Spirit close to me."  

Restore salvation's joy, make it new,  
Uphold my spirit, ever true.  

Teach transgressors, show them your ways,  
Sinners turn back, to you they'll praise.  

Deliver me from bloodshed's guilt, I pray,  
My tongue will sing of your righteousness today.  

"Lord, open my lips," as I declare,  
My mouth sings your praise everywhere.  

You don't want sacrifice, else I'd comply,  
Burnt offerings don't satisfy.  

A broken spirit's the sacrifice you seek,  
A contrite heart you never critique.  

Do good to Zion, your pleasure fulfill,  
Build Jerusalem's walls with skilled will.  

Then you'll delight in righteous deeds,  
Bulls on your altar, meeting all needs.
