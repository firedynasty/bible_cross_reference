Brothers living in unity, oh what a sight,  
Good and pleasant, shining bright,  

Like oil on the head, flowing down so fine,  
Aaron's beard it touches, a sacred sign,  

Dripping down to robes, spreading wide,  
A blessing in every drop, can't be denied,  

Dew of Hermon, fresh and pure,  
On Zion's hills, blessings endure,  

For there Yahweh gives, life forever,  
In unity, His love, nothing can sever.
