Like a deer that's thirsty, I long for the stream,  
My soul's crying out, "God, you're my dream."  

I'm parched for the living, the one true King,  
When can I stand before Him, sing?  

Day and night, tears my only bread,  
"Where's your God?" they taunt, voices in my head.  

I remember those days, with the crowd I swayed,  
Marching to God's house, joyfully, we prayed.  

Why so down, soul? Why so blue?  
Hope in God! Praise will see you through.  

Despair grips tight, but I recall the past,  
From Jordan's land, Hermon's heights so vast.  

Waters roar, deep calls to deep,  
Waves crash over me, my tears they sweep.  

"Yahweh's love by day, His song by night,  
A prayer to my God, guiding light."  

I cry to my rock, "Why am I left alone?  
Why do I mourn, with a heart like stone?"  

Enemies mock, like swords in my bone,  
"Where's your God?" their taunt, a groan.  

Why, soul, so down? Why such fright?  
Hope in God! Praise Him through the night.
