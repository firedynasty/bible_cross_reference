My heart is steady, God, I sing with my soul,  
Wake the harp and lyre, let the dawn take its stroll.  

I'll thank you, Yahweh, across nations wide,  
Your praises I'll sing, let them never hide.  

Your love's above the heavens, a vast, endless prize,  
Your faithfulness soars, reaching up to the skies.  

"Be exalted, God, above all earth and heaven's mirth,  
Let your glory cover every inch of the earth."  

"Deliver your beloved, hear my plea,  
With your right hand, God, answer and set us free."  

God speaks from his throne, strong and grand,  
"In triumph, I'll divide the promised land.  

Gilead and Manasseh, they are mine,  
Ephraim's my helmet, Judah's my line.  

Moab's my wash pot, on Edom I throw my shoe,  
Over Philistia, my shout echoes through."  

Who's leading to the city, so fortified,  
Who guides to Edom, with strength on our side?  

"Have you turned from us, God, won't you fight?  
Without you, God, our armies aren't right."  

Help us 'gainst foes, man's aid's in vain,  
With God, we'll triumph, enemies slain.
