To Yahweh, I lift up my soul, my plea,  
"Don't let me be shamed, don't let enemies seize me."  

No one who waits on you will bear disgrace,  
But those who plot treachery, they'll lose face.  

"Show me your ways, Yahweh, teach me your path,  
Guide me in truth, clear my doubts, stave your wrath."  

"Your mercy's old, your kindness stands,  
Forget my youth's sins, remember your plans."  

Good is Yahweh, upright, no doubt,  
He shows sinners the way, shows them the route.  

The humble he guides, in justice, he'll teach,  
His covenant keepers, his truth they'll reach.  

"For your name's sake, Yahweh, pardon my wrong,  
Great's my iniquity, but to you I belong."  

Who fears Yahweh? They'll know the path,  
Their soul finds ease, their lineage laughs.  

Yahweh's friends know his covenant plan,  
Eyes on him save feet from the snare, like sand.  

"Turn to me, have mercy, I'm all alone,  
Troubles mount high, my heart's turned to stone."  

Consider my foes, they hate with fierce rage,  
"Keep my soul safe, shelter, engage."  

Integrity and truth, my life's strong tether,  
Redeem Israel, God, through all stormy weather.
