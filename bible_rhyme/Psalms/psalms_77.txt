I cry out to God, my voice reaching high,  
For Him to hear me, I raise my sigh.  

In troubled times, I stretched my hand,  
In the night, it stayed, like a steadfast band.  

I remember God, and I groan with might,  
My spirit's overwhelmed, silenced in the night.  

You keep my eyes open wide,  
Words escape as I bide my tide.  

I think of days gone, years long past,  
My song in the night, my heart holds fast.  

My spirit asks, "Will the Lord leave us be?  
Will His favor no longer be free?"  

"Has His kindness vanished, gone away?  
Do His promises fail, no longer stay?"  

"Has God forgotten to grace the earth?  
In His anger, has compassion lost its worth?"  

Then I thought, "I'll recall the days of the Most High,  
The wonders, His deeds, reaching the sky."  

Meditate on His works, consider His ways,  
In the sanctuary, His greatness stays.  

"What god is like ours, who does wonders great?  
With strength among nations, He does relate."  

Redeeming His people, His arm stretched wide,  
Sons of Jacob and Joseph, by His side.  

The waters saw You, God, they trembled, they cried,  
Clouds burst with water, as the thunder replied.  

Lightning flashed, the world in a spin,  
The earth quaked and shook from within.  

Through the sea, Your path was laid,  
Unseen footsteps, a mystery made.  

You led Your flock with a guiding hand,  
Through Moses and Aaron, across the land.
