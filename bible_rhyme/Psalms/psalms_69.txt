Save me, God, I'm drowning, neck-deep in this plight!  
I'm sinking in the mire, can't find a foothold tonight.  

Cried till I'm weary, my throat is so dry,  
My eyes search for God, I look to the sky.  

Haters surround me, countless as hair on my head,  
Enemies wrong me, want me gone, left for dead.  

God, you know my errors, nothing's hidden from your sight,  
"Let those who trust in you not be shamed by my plight."  

For your name's sake, I faced the disgrace,  
Stranger to kin, shame covered my face.  

Zeal for your house burns hot within,  
Their taunts fall on me, like an unwanted sin.  

Weeping and fasting, they mock my pain,  
Sackcloth's my fashion, I'm their song in disdain.  

But Yahweh, I pray, in your time that's just right,  
In your loving kindness, save me from this fight.  

Pull me from the mire, don't let me sink,  
From deep floods, save me, I'm on the brink.  

"Don't let waters drown, or the pit shut its gate,  
Answer me, Yahweh, your love's my only fate."  

In distress, I call, don't turn away,  
Draw near, redeem me, hear what I say.  

Reproach broke my heart, weighed with despair,  
Looked for pity but comfort was rare.  

They fed me poison, gave vinegar for thirst,  
Let their table trap them, let their plans be cursed.  

"Darken their eyes, let their backs bear the strain,  
Pour out your anger, let them feel the pain."  

Leave their homes empty, desolate lands,  
They hurt the wounded, with cruel hands.  

"Mark them with crime, blot their names out,  
Don't let them stand with the righteous crowd."  

In my pain, I call for your salvation,  
I'll praise your name, God, with pure adoration.  

It's worth more than bulls, more than horns with hooves,  
The humble see and rejoice, their faith it proves.  

God hears the needy, captive hearts he sets free,  
Heaven and earth, praise him in unity.  

For God will save Zion, build Judah anew,  
His servants' children shall dwell, and love his name true.
