Rejoice in Yahweh, righteous crew, praise flows like a river true.  
Grab the lyre, strum the strings, let the harp make the heavens sing.  

Lift a new song, play it loud, skillful notes make the heart proud.  
For Yahweh’s words are always right, faithfulness shines pure and bright.  

He loves justice, righteousness too, earth's full of kindness like morning dew.  
He spoke the heavens, stars arrayed, breathed life, and the skies were made.  

Waters gathered, seas held tight, deeps stored up, hidden from sight.  
All the earth, fear His name, stand in awe, respect His claim.  

He spoke once, it was done, His command stood firm, second to none.  
Nations’ plans, tossed away, Yahweh's counsel forever stays.  

Blessed the nation with Yahweh's might, chosen people in His light.  
From heaven, He sees all men, from His seat, He views again.  

He forms hearts, knows our deeds, sees beyond our worldly needs.  
No king saved by great armies, no strength delivers mighty pleas.  

A horse for safety, a hollow boast, power alone, an empty ghost.  
But Yahweh’s eye is sharp and keen, on those who trust, unseen.  

He saves from death, famine's call, His loving kindness over all.  
Our soul awaits, Yahweh’s our shield, in His name, our heart's revealed.  

Let kindness rain down from above, Yahweh, hold us in your love.
