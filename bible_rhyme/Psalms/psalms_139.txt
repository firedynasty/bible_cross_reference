Yahweh, you’ve searched, you know my game,  
From when I rise to when I came.  

You see my thoughts from far away,  
You trace my steps and where I lay.  

Words on my tongue, you know ‘em all,  
You hem me in, you make the call.  

This knowledge, man, it’s out of sight,  
I can’t reach up, it’s way too high.

Where can I hide from your Spirit’s flight?  
From heaven to Sheol, you’re in my sight.  

Wings of dawn, across the sea,  
Even there, your hand guides me.  

If darkness tries to take its place,  
Still, you shine light and show your face.  

In secret, knit in womb’s embrace,  
Your eyes saw me, your crafted space.  

Fearfully, wonderfully, what you made,  
Your works I know, they don’t fade.  

My frame you saw, in secret spun,  
Days written before there was one.  

How precious, God, your thoughts, the sum,  
More than sand, when morning's come.  

Kill the wicked, God, I plea,  
Bloodthirsty ones, don't let them see.  

They speak against you with wicked aim,  
Enemies take your holy name.  

I hate them, God, with perfect hate,  
Your foes and mine, they won’t relate.  

Search my heart, know what I’ve got,  
Try my thoughts, see my plot.  

See if wicked ways I tread,  
Lead me on your path instead.
