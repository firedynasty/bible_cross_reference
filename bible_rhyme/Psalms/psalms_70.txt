God, hurry up, deliver me fast,  
Come quick, Yahweh, don’t let this last.  

Those who seek my soul, let them be confused,  
Turn back in disgrace, let their plans be diffused.  

Those who mock with “Aha!” let shame be their fate,  
May they turn away, their pride deflate.  

But those who seek You, let them rejoice,  
Glad in You, let them lift their voice.  

For those who love Your salvation, they’ll say,  
“Let God be exalted!” every single day.  

I'm poor and needy, I need You near,  
You’re my help, my deliverer, make that clear.  

Yahweh, don’t delay, come through for me,  
Be my rescue, let Your power be free.
