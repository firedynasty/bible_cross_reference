Praise Yahweh, my soul, let His name be known,  
All that's within me, to His glory, I'm prone.  

Forget not His gifts, His benefits so grand,  
He forgives your sins with a healing hand.  

Redeems your life, lifts you from the pit,  
Crowns you with love, His mercy won't quit.  

Filling your desires, renewing your flight,  
Youth restored like an eagle in the sky's light.  

Yahweh acts with justice, for the oppressed He fights,  
To Moses He showed His ways, His deeds in great sights.  

"Merciful, gracious, slow to anger," He declares,  
His loving kindness abundant, forever He cares.  

Won't accuse forever, His anger won't stay,  
Not repaid for our sins, His grace leads the way.  

High as the heavens, His kindness extends,  
East from the west, our sins He suspends.  

Like a father's compassion, so is His grace,  
He knows our frame, remembers our place.  

Man's days like grass, a field flower's bloom,  
Wind passes by, it's gone, forgotten soon.  

But Yahweh's love is eternal, endless and true,  
To those who fear Him and His covenant pursue.  

His throne in the heavens, His kingdom reigns,  
Praise Him, angels mighty, who follow His claims.  

Praise Him, armies, servants of His will,  
In all His dominion, His word you fulfill.  

Praise Yahweh, all creation, in every place,  
Praise Yahweh, my soul, for His endless grace!
