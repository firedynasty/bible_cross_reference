Trust in Yahweh, you're like Mount Zion strong,  
Unmoved forever, standing firm and long.  

Mountains wrap around Jerusalem's gate,  
Yahweh surrounds us, early and late.  

Wicked's scepter won't linger or last,  
Righteous hands won't turn to the past.  

"Do good, Yahweh, to the true and pure,  
To those with hearts upright, secure."  

But those who twist their ways and stray,  
Yahweh leads them far away.  

Peace on Israel, let it be,  
A promise held eternally.
