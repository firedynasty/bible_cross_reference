Sing to God, our strength, loud and clear,  
Shout for joy, to the God we hold dear.  

Grab the tambourine, let the song take flight,  
With lyre and harp, fill the night.  

Blow the trumpet, let it ring,  
At New Moon, a feast we bring.  

A rule for Israel, God did declare,  
Appointed in Joseph, a covenant rare.  

He crossed Egypt, heard an unknown call,  
"I removed his burden, freed him all.  

You cried in trouble, I came through,  
Answered in thunder, tested you too."  

“Hear me now, my people, heed my plea,  
No strange gods should you see.  

I am Yahweh, from Egypt brought you out,  
Open wide, I'll fill, there's no doubt.  

But my people turned away,  
Listened not to what I'd say.  

I let them chase their stubborn course,  
In their own ways, they found no source.  

Oh, if only they'd hear my voice,  
Walk in my path, make that choice.  

Enemies would fall, crushed by might,  
Adversaries turned in flight.  

Haters of Yahweh would cringe and fear,  
Their punishment forever near.  

But I'd feed them wheat, finest stock,  
Satisfy with honey from the rock."
