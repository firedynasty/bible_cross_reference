I give thanks to Yahweh, with my whole heart's flame,  
Tell of all your wonders, spread your mighty name.  

Glad and rejoicing, in your presence I sing,  
"Praise to your name," echoes to heaven's king.  

Enemies turn, they stumble, they fall,  
In your presence, God, they can't stand tall.  

You've kept my cause, righteous and true,  
On your throne, judgment's what you do.  

Nations rebuked, wickedness gone,  
Their names erased, like the night's withdrawn.  

Enemies lost to endless ruin,  
Cities they crushed left in ruin's ruin.  

Yahweh reigns, forever in sight,  
Prepared for judgment, shining bright.  

Judging the world, righteous and fair,  
For all the people, justice you bear.  

A tower for the troubled, oppressed,  
A refuge in times when they're distressed.  

Trust in your name, Yahweh, we do,  
For those who seek you, you'll always be true.  

Sing praises to Yahweh, dwelling in Zion's glow,  
Declare his deeds, let the people know.  

He who avenges, remembers their plea,  
Doesn't forget the afflicted's decree.  

"Have mercy, Yahweh," I cry with pain,  
"Lift me from death's gate, break my chain.  

Show all your praise, in Zion's gate,  
Rejoicing in salvation, I celebrate."  

Nations have sunk in the pit they made,  
Caught in their net, their fate's displayed.  

Yahweh is known, judgment is clear,  
The wicked ensnared by their own fear.  

The wicked turned to Sheol's fate,  
Nations forgetting God, meet their state.  

The needy remembered, never ignored,  
Hope for the poor forever restored.  

"Arise, Yahweh!" let man not prevail,  
Judge the nations, let justice sail.  

Put fear in them; let them see,  
That they are only men—humility.
