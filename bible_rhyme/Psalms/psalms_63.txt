God, you're my God, I seek you all day,  
In this dry weary land, where I strain to stay.  

I've seen your power, felt your glory's might,  
Your love's my life, my lips praise day and night.  

I'll bless you as long as my breath stays,  
Lift my hands up high, singin' your praise.  

My soul's satisfied like a feast so grand,  
Joyful lips sing out, with you I stand.  

I think of you on my bed at night,  
In my darkest hour, you're my light.  

You've been my help, in your wings I hide,  
My soul clings close, you hold me with pride.  

Those who seek my life, they'll descend below,  
Sword's power will take them, jackals'll feast, you know.  

But the king shall rejoice, in God he'll stay,  
The truth-speakers shout, while liars fade away.
