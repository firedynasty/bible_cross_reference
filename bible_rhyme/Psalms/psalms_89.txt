I’ll sing of Yahweh's love, it won't ever fade,  
With my lips, His faithfulness I’ll cascade.  

"Love’s firm forever, I’ve set the sky,  
My faithfulness shines, way up high."  

"I made a promise to my chosen, David’s my man,  
His line's forever, His throne will stand."  

The heavens shout His wonders, Yahweh's praised,  
In the holy assembly, His name's raised.  

Who in the skies can stand near His might?  
He’s awesome in council, shines so bright.  

"Yahweh, God of Armies, who’s like you?  
Your faithfulness surrounds, strong and true."  

You rule the seas, when waves rise tall,  
You calm them down, you still them all.  

You crushed Rahab, like the slain,  
Scattered foes, in your mighty reign.  

The heavens are yours, earth’s your domain,  
North and south, you made them plain.  

Tabor and Hermon sing out loud,  
Your mighty arm, makes us proud.  

Righteousness, justice, your throne’s base,  
Loving kindness, truth, before your face.  

Blessed are those who in you delight,  
They walk in your presence, Yahweh’s light.  

"In your name, they rejoice all day,  
In righteousness, lifted in every way."  

"For you’re the glory, you are our might,  
Our strength, in your favor, shines so bright."  

"Our shield is Yahweh's, our king’s divine,  
To Israel’s Holy One, we incline."  

"You spoke in visions to the saints,  
‘I gave strength to warriors, no restraints.'"  

"Found David, my servant, anointed him well,  
My hand holds him, my strength will swell."  

"No enemy will tax, no wicked will reign,  
Adversaries crushed, those who hate, in pain."  

"My faithfulness, love will be with him strong,  
His horn exalted, all day long."  

"I’ll set his hand on seas and streams,  
He’ll call me ‘Father,’ fulfill his dreams."  

"I’ll appoint him firstborn, kingly height,  
Covenant firm, forever in sight."  

"His offspring will endure, as heavens sway,  
Throne established like sun's bright ray."  

"If children stray, they’ll face the rod,  
But my love won’t leave, faith will not nod."  

"No broken promise, no alteration,  
Sworn by my holiness, no deviation."  

"Offspring endure, like the moon’s glow,  
Throne stands firm, as heavens show."  

"But now, rejected, your wrath burns hot,  
Anointed's crown lies in the dust, forgot."  

"Hedges broken, strongholds laid low,  
Robbers pass by, neighbors’ scorn they show."  

"Enemies exalted, their joy’s on fire,  
Sword turned back, no support to inspire."  

"Splendor ended, throne thrown down,  
Days of youth cut short, covered in frown."  

"How long, Yahweh? Will you hide away?  
Burning wrath, will it hold sway?"  

"Remember our time, it’s short, a fleeting breath,  
Man faces Sheol, none escape death."  

"Where’s your love, you swore to David true?  
Reproach surrounds, in taunts, we rue."  

Blessed be Yahweh, forever declared,  
Amen, Amen, His praise shared.
