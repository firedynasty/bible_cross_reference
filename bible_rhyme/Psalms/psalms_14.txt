The fool declares with doubt and nod,  
"There ain't no God." They act all flawed.  
Corrupt deeds spread across the land,  
No good found, you understand.  

Yahweh peers from heaven's height,  
Seeking those who live it right.  
But all have strayed, turned aside,  
In corruption they abide.  

Do they know or is it fake,  
Eating people like it's cake?  
Ignoring Yahweh's mighty call,  
Do they understand at all?  

Fear grips 'em there, they shake and quake,  
For God is with the righteous wake.  
For plans of the poor you try to foil,  
But Yahweh's refuge don’t recoil.  

Oh, let Zion bring salvation's cheer,  
Yahweh restores, the day draws near.  
Jacob and Israel, joy they find,  
Rejoicing hearts and peace of mind.
