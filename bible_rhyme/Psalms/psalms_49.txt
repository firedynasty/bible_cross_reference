Listen up, people, gather 'round, hear me out,   
Both low and high, rich and poor, there's no doubt.  

My mouth speaks wisdom, my heart's got insight,   
I solve riddles on the harp, bringing truth to light.  

Why fear when evil days roll in the storm,   
When wealth can't save, it's just the norm.  

Can't redeem your brother, can't pay God's due,   
Life's redemption is costly, can't see it through.  

Wise men, fools, they all face the end,   
Leaving wealth behind, no legacy to send.  

They think their homes will stand forever,   
Naming lands, but they won't last, not ever.  

Man with riches, still like beasts that fall,   
Foolish destiny awaits, the same for all.  

Death's their shepherd, Sheol is the path they tread,   
But in the morning, the upright rise instead.  

Yet God will redeem, pull my soul from the grave,   
"He'll receive me," saving and brave.  

Don't fear when riches rise, when homes expand,   
They can't carry a thing to Sheol's land.  

While living, he blessed, received man's acclaim,   
But he'll join his fathers, lost to the flame.  

Riches without sense, can't see the truth's torch,   
Just like animals perishing on the porch.
