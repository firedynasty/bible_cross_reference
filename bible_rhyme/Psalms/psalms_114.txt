When Israel left Egypt, left those foreign lands,  
Judah was His sanctuary, Israel in His hands.  

The sea saw, and it fled, the Jordan turned away,  
Mountains skipped like rams, little hills began to sway.  

Why, sea, did you run? Why, Jordan, did you turn?  
Mountains, why skip? Little hills, why churn?  

Tremble, earth, at His presence, the God of Jacob's might,  
He turned rocks to water, made springs where none in sight.
