Yo, Yahweh, hear my plea, listen to my cry,  
In your truth and justice, lift me high.  

Don't judge me harsh, 'cause none can stand,  
In your sight, no living soul is grand.  

The enemy's chasing, got my soul on the run,  
Crushing me low, like the grave's dark sun.  

My spirit's crushed, my heart feels so dead,  
I think of old days, your works in my head.  

I reach out to you, like a land that's dry,  
My soul's parched, thirsty, reaching to the sky.  

"Hurry, Yahweh, before my spirit's gone,  
Don't hide your face, I'll be lost, withdrawn."  

"Let me hear your love, morning's first light,  
Show me the path, make my way bright."  

"Save me from foes, to you I race,  
Hide me, teach me, with your grace."  

"Lead me on the straight path, your Spirit's guide,  
Revive me, Yahweh, with you I abide."  

"In your love, cut off those who afflict,  
For I'm your servant, to you I'm strict."
