Do you speak truth, or just stay mute?  
Do you judge fair, or just pollute?  

Nah, in your heart, you plot and scheme,  
Violence flows in your wicked dream.  

From the womb, they stray off track,  
Born to lie, they don't look back.  

Like a snake's venom, they infuse,  
Deaf to charmers, they refuse.  

"Break their teeth right in their mouth," God exclaimed,  
"Shatter the lions' strength, leave them tamed."  

Let them vanish like water flows,  
Arrows blunted, no more foes.  

Like a snail that melts away,  
Never seeing the light of day.  

Before the heat touches the pot,  
He'll sweep it all, like it or not.  

The righteous cheer when justice is served,  
Feet washed in blood, the wicked unnerved.  

"There's a reward for the good," they declare,  
"God judges the earth, so beware."
