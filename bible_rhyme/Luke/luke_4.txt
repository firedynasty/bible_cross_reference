Jesus, full of Spirit, from Jordan He came,  
To the wilderness led, where the wild is untamed.  
Forty days tempted, the devil at play,  
He ate nothing, was hungry at the end of His stay.  

"If you’re the Son, turn stone into bread,"  
The devil dared, longing to see Jesus misled.  
But Jesus replied, "Man lives not on bread,  
But on God's words," with wisdom He said.  

The devil then showed Him kingdoms so vast,  
"All this authority, if you'll bow and hold fast."  
"Get behind me, Satan!" Jesus declared,  
"Worship God only, His service is shared."  

To Jerusalem's height, the devil took flight,  
"If you're the Son, from this temple take flight.  
He’ll send angels; your foot won’t be bruised,"  
But Jesus refused, "Don't tempt the Lord, it’s misused."  

The temptations done, the devil withdrew,  
Power-filled, Jesus to Galilee flew.  
Synagogues buzzed with the news He spread,  
To Nazareth He came, where the Scriptures He read.  

"The Spirit's upon me, to preach and to heal,  
Good news to the poor, broken hearts He’ll seal.  
Captives released, the blind given sight,  
The oppressed delivered, in the Lord’s light."  

He closed up the book, eyes fixed all around,  
"This day fulfilled," He shared with the crowd.  
"Isn't this Joseph’s son?" they wondered aloud,  
But Jesus knew doubt would soon grow loud.  

"No prophet’s accepted in his own town,"  
He said while the listeners turned their smiles to frowns.  
Anger arose, they moved as a crowd,  
But Jesus slipped through, unbowed and unbowed.  

To Capernaum He went, on Sabbath He taught,  
His words with authority, the people were caught.  
A man with a demon cried out in fear,  
"Have you come to destroy us? It's You we hear!"  

"Be silent, come out!" Jesus commanded with might,  
The demon fled, causing no fright.  
Amazed, they whispered, "What power He shows,  
Unclean spirits obey, wherever He goes!"  

News of Him grew, spreading wide and far,  
He entered Simon's house, where fever was the scar.  
He rebuked it, and it fled, his mother-in-law healed,  
She rose with strength, her gratitude revealed.  

At sunset, they brought the sick and the lame,  
His hands on each, none left the same.  
Demons shouted, "You’re the Christ, God’s own Son!"  
But silenced, for Jesus’ time had not yet begun.  

When day broke, to a quiet place He went,  
The crowd followed, their hearts intent.  
"I must preach God’s kingdom to other lands,"  
He said, His mission clear with divine hands.  

Preaching city to city, His voice spread wide,  
With authority and grace, always as His guide.
