There was a rich man, had a manager's scheme,  
Accusations flew, said he wasted the dream.  

Rich man called him, "What’s this I hear?  
Give me your account, your end is near."  

Manager thought, "What will I do,  
Too weak to dig, beggin' ain't my groove."  

"I know the plan, I'll make my move,  
So when I'm out, they’ll give me a room."  

Called up debtors, one by one,  
"How much you owe? Let's get it done."  

"A hundred batos," the first one said,  
"Cut it to fifty, clear your head."  

Next owes wheat, "A hundred's your share,  
Take it to eighty, let's make it fair."  

Rich man nodded, "You played it wise,  
In this world, that's no surprise."  

"Use what you got, make friends fast,  
When wealth is gone, friendships last."  

Faithful in little, faithful in all,  
Dishonest in small, you'll surely fall.  

If you can't be trusted with wealth that's wrong,  
How will you handle true riches long?  

Serve two masters? Can't be done,  
You’ll love one and hate the other one."  

Pharisees laughed, lovers of wealth,  
But God sees hearts, riches don't stealth.  

Law and prophets 'til John came through,  
Now kingdom news breaks like morning dew.  

Heaven and earth may fade away,  
But the law's strokes are here to stay.  

Divorce and marriage rules stand tight,  
Against them, there's no easy flight.  

Rich man in fine linen, all dressed to the nines,  
Lived each day in luxury, sippin' on wines.  

Lazarus begged, sores on the ground,  
Dogs licked his wounds, crumbs not found.  

Beggar died, angels carried him high,  
Rich man buried, down he did lie.  

In Hades, he looked, saw Abraham's light,  
Lazarus comforted, rich in plight.  

"Father Abraham, mercy, I plead,  
Send Lazarus, my tongue in need."  

"Son," said Abraham, "remember your life,  
You had it all, now he's free from strife."  

A gulf between us, too wide to span,  
None cross over, not even one man."  

"Send him to my family, five brothers at home,  
Warn them of torment, before they're alone."  

"They have the prophets, Moses to read,  
Let them listen and take heed."  

"If the dead rise, they'll change, I assure,"  
But Abraham said, "They won't be secure."  

If they won't hear the words that were said,  
Not even a risen man will turn their head.
