He called the twelve and gave them power,  
Over demons, disease, hour by hour.  

"Go preach the Kingdom, heal the sick,  
Take nothing with you, travel light and quick.  

No staffs, no bread, leave your wallet alone,  
Stay in houses, move on like a stone.  

If they don't receive you, shake dust from your feet,  
A testimony against them, make it complete."  

They went village to village, spreading the good news,  
Healing all the sick, sharing God's views.  

Herod heard the buzz, couldn't believe his ears,  
"John risen? Elijah here? Old prophets reappear?"  

"I beheaded John, who is this one?"  
He sought to see him, questions not done.  

The apostles came back, shared what was done,  
He withdrew to Bethsaida, away from the sun.  

The crowd followed, couldn't be swayed,  
He spoke of the Kingdom, healed and stayed.  

The day wore on, the twelve felt the weight,  
"Send them to eat, it's getting late."  

But he said, "You give them food," they exclaimed,  
"Five loaves, two fish, that's all we obtained."  

He made them sit in groups, fifty each,  
Took the bread and fish, heaven he did reach.  

Blessed, broke, and passed them around,  
All were fed, leftovers abound.  

"Who do they say I am?" he asked alone,  
"John, Elijah, prophets of old," the answers have grown.  

"But who do you say?" Peter declared,  
"The Christ of God," his faith he shared.  

He warned them, "Tell no one this sight,  
The Son must suffer, rise after night.  

Deny yourself, take up your cross,  
Follow me, whatever the cost.  

Save your life, lose it, that’s the strife,  
Lose for my sake, and you'll find life.  

What does it profit, gain the world, lose your soul?  
Ashamed of me? That takes a toll."  

Eight days later, up the mountain they went,  
Peter, John, and James, in prayer they were bent.  

His face changed, clothes dazzling bright,  
Moses and Elijah appeared in light.  

Peter awoke, wanted tents to make,  
A cloud came down, they started to shake.  

"This is my Son, listen to him,"  
With awe and silence, their eyes grew dim.  

Next day descending, a crowd did call,  
A father's plea, his son about to fall.  

"Teacher, help, my son's possessed,  
Your disciples couldn’t, I'm distressed."  

"Faithless generation, bring him here,"  
Jesus rebuked, demons disappeared.  

All amazed, yet he spoke again,  
"Son of Man will be in men's hands then."  

They argued on who was the greatest one,  
He brought a child, "See what must be done.  

Receive a child, receive me too,  
Least among you, greatest in view."  

"Someone cast out demons, not with us," John said,  
"Don’t stop him," Jesus replied, "He's not misled."  

To Jerusalem he set his face,  
Samaritans refused to give him place.  

"Call down fire?" the disciples asked,  
"No destruction, saving's my task."  

On the road, a man pledged to follow,  
"Foxes have holes, but no place I can borrow."  

"Follow me," he said to another in line,  
"Let the dead bury dead, Kingdom's your sign."  

Another wished to bid farewell at home,  
"No looking back, you're Kingdom-bound to roam."
