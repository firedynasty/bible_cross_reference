In a city lived a judge, no fear of God, no respect for man,  
A widow pleaded, "Defend me, do what you can!"  
For a while he ignored, but she wore him down,  
"She's bothering me, I'll help her before I drown."  
The Lord said, "Hear that judge's voice,  
Won't God avenge His own who cry and rejoice?"  

Two men to the temple, a Pharisee and a taxman,  
Pharisee stood tall, feeling like the righteous man,  
"Thank God I'm not like others, unrighteous or wild,  
I fast and give tithes, I'm not defiled."  
But the taxman, from a distance, beat his chest,  
"God, have mercy on me, a sinner at best."  
"This man went home justified," Jesus declared,  
"Humble yourself, and God will be there."  

Babies were brought for a gentle touch,  
Disciples rebuked, but Jesus didn't mind much,  
"Let the children come, don't block their way,  
God's Kingdom's like them," He'd say.  
"Good Teacher," a ruler asked, looking for the light,  
"What must I do to inherit eternal life?"  
"Why call me good?" Jesus replied,  
"Keep commandments, let them be your guide."  

"I've done this since youth," he claimed without shame,  
"One thing you lack," from Jesus it came.  
"Sell all you have, give to the poor,  
Treasure in heaven, that's the core."  
The ruler was sad, he loved his wealth,  
Jesus spoke of camels and the needle's stealth.  
"Who then can be saved?" the crowd began,  
"With God, all things are possible," was the plan.  

Peter said, "We've given all to follow you,"  
Jesus promised blessings, eternal life too.  
To the twelve, He spoke of Jerusalem's fate,  
Prophets' words would soon take shape.  
Delivered, mocked, killed, then rise again,  
They couldn't grasp it, the hidden plane.  

Near Jericho, a blind man by the road,  
Heard the crowd, in Jesus' name he sowed.  
"Son of David, have mercy on me!"  
Though rebuked, he shouted his plea.  
Jesus asked, "What do you want me to do?"  
"Lord, let me see, make my world anew."  
"Receive your sight, your faith's set you free,"  
He followed, glorifying God, for all to see.
