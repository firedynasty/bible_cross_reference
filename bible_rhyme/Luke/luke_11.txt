In a quiet place, his prayer was done,  
A disciple asked, "Teach us to pray, like John."  

"When you pray, say this," Jesus began,  
"Our Father in heaven, holy be your name, man.  

May your Kingdom come, your will be done,  
On earth as in heaven, let it be won.  

Give us each day, our daily bread to eat,  
Forgive our sins, as we forgive others we meet.  

Lead us not into temptation's sway,  
Deliver us from evil, we pray today."  

He went on, "Imagine at midnight's hour,  
You knock on a friend's door, with no power.  

'Friend, lend me loaves, three for me to share,  
A pal's come to visit, and my cupboard's bare.'  

Though he might say, 'I'm tucked in, door's sealed,'  
Persistence will get you the bread he'll yield.  

So ask, it'll be given, seek and you'll find,  
Knock and the door will open, keep that in mind.  

If your kid asks for bread, will you give a stone?  
Or a snake for a fish, leave him alone?  

If you, though flawed, know gifts for your kin,  
How much more will God give the Spirit within?"  

Casting out demons, a man mute now spoke,  
Crowds marveled, while some others did poke.  

"He casts by Beelzebul," they did say,  
Testing him, seeking a sign that day.  

But Jesus, aware of their inner divide,  
Said, "A kingdom split falls, it cannot abide.  

If Satan fights Satan, how can he stand?  
But if by God's finger, demons leave this land,  

Then the Kingdom of God is here with you.  
The strong man guards, but a stronger breaks through.  

Who isn't with me, surely stands against,  
Who doesn't gather, scatters by chance.  

An unclean spirit, when it leaves a man,  
Seeks rest, finds none, returns with a plan.  

Seven more join, more wicked than before,  
The man's state worsens, evil comes through the door."  

A woman shouted, "Blessed is your birth!"  
But he replied, "Blessed are those who hear God's worth."  

"This generation seeks a sign,  
But no sign shall be given, except Jonah's line.  

As Jonah was to Nineveh, a sign they knew,  
So the Son of Man is a sign to you.  

The Queen of the South came for Solomon's fame,  
One greater than Solomon stands here, no shame.  

Nineveh repented, Jonah's words they heeded,  
One greater than Jonah is here, unneeded is greed."  

A lamp on a stand shines bright,  
Not hidden but seen, giving light.  

The eye's the lamp, see well and be bright,  
If darkness clouds, your whole body's night.  

With him, a Pharisee sat to dine,  
He marveled Jesus skipped the wash line.  

"Outside you clean, but inside you're grim,  
Didn't the maker of both make them akin?  

Give from within, and you'll be clean,  
But woe to you for bypassing God's love unseen.  

Best seats and greetings you do adore,  
But justice and mercy you ignore.  

Woe to you, hidden graves so cold,  
Men walk unaware of your stories untold."  

A lawyer spoke, "Teacher, you insult our lot,"  
Jesus replied, "Woe to you, burdening a lot.  

You don't help, but weigh others down,  
You build prophets' tombs, while they frown.  

This generation will answer for the blood,  
From Abel to Zachariah, in a flood.  

You took the key of knowledge away,  
You hinder others, blocked the way."  

The scribes and Pharisees, angry they grew,  
Waiting to trap him, with accusations new.
