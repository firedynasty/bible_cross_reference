In Tiberius' reign, fifteenth year, the scene was set,  
Pilate ruled Judea, Herod Galilee's threat.  

Philip and Lysanias, regions they controlled,  
High priests Annas and Caiaphas in their roles bold.  

God's word hit John in the desert's vast expanse,  
Preaching repentance, a life-changing chance.  

"Make the way ready," Isaiah's echo,  
Valleys filled, mountains laid low.  

Paths made straight, rough ways smooth,  
Salvation for all, it's the truth to soothe.  

To the crowds, John cried, "Vipers, beware!  
Repentance is key, show fruits if you dare.  

Don't claim Abraham's line as your savior,  
God can raise sons from stones, His favor!"  

"Axes lie waiting, trees must bear good fruit,  
Or face the fire's unquenchable pursuit."  

"What must we do?" the crowd asked with yearning,  
"Share your coats, share your food," John's heart burning.  

Tax men came near, "What should we do, tell us please?"  
"Take only what's fair, don't aim to squeeze."  

Soldiers sought guidance, "What about us, our call?"  
"Don't extort, don't falsely accuse, be content with your haul."  

People wondered, "Is John the Christ?"  
But he said, "One greater comes, I'm not worth the price.  

I baptize with water, He with Spirit and flame,  
He's gathering the wheat, chaff burned in His name."  

John preached with power, he brought the good news,  
But Herod locked him away for opposing his views.  

When all were baptized, Jesus came from the crowd,  
Praying, Heaven opened, Spirit descended like a shroud.  

A voice declared, "My beloved Son, in you I delight,"  
Jesus, at thirty, began His teaching plight.  

The genealogy unfolded, tracing Jesus' line,  
From Joseph and Heli, back through the vine.  

Through David, Jesse, and Boaz too,  
All the way to Adam, Son of God, it's true.
