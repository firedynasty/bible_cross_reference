Jesus wrapped up his talk, moved to Capernaum's scene,  
A centurion's servant lay sick, barely clinging to his dream.  

He sent Jewish elders with a desperate plea,  
"Come, save my servant, he's dear to me."  

They begged Jesus, "Help, he's worth your time,  
He loves our nation, built our synagogue, prime."  

Jesus headed their way, not far from the spot,  
The centurion sent friends, with a humble thought:  

"Lord, don’t trouble yourself, I'm unworthy for sure,  
Just say the word, and my servant'll be cured."  

"I'm a man under orders with soldiers in line,  
I say, 'Go,' they go; they follow every sign."  

Jesus marveled, turned to the crowd with a cheer,  
"In all Israel, such great faith ain't here."  

Servant healed, they returned once again,  
Next stop, a city called Nain.  

At the gate, a tragic scene, a widow's son,  
Her only light, her world undone.  

Jesus saw her tears, said, "Don't cry,"  
Touched the coffin, "Young man, arise!"  

The dead man sat up, began to speak,  
Returned to his mother, the joy peaked.  

Fear gripped the crowd, they praised on high,  
"God’s visited His people," they cried.  

News spread wide, throughout Judea's land,  
John's disciples heard, took it to their man.  

John sent two, to ask Jesus a line,  
"Are you the one, or in vain our sign?"  

Jesus cured many, diseases and blights,  
Gave the blind their precious sights.  

"Tell John what you see, hear me,  
The lame walk, dead rise, and the poor hear the decree."  

"Blessed is he who stumbles not on account of me,"  
Then he spoke of John, with prophetic decree.  

"What did you seek in the wilderness spot?  
A reed blown by wind, or something you're not?"  

"A prophet you sought, yes, even more,  
John's my messenger, opening heaven's door."  

"Among those born, none greater than he,  
Yet in God's Kingdom, the least is greater, you see."  

People baptized by John declared God right,  
Pharisees and lawyers turned away from the light.  

"To what can I compare this generation's way?  
Children in markets, games they play."  

"For John fasted, you said, 'He’s mad,'  
I feast, and you call me a sinner-clad."  

"Wisdom’s children prove wisdom true,  
Actions speak loud, in all we do."  

A Pharisee invited him to dine,  
A woman with tears, brought ointment fine.  

She wept at his feet, wiped with her hair,  
Anointed with love, in a silent prayer.  

Simon judged her, thought Jesus blind,  
Jesus spoke, "Simon, hear my mind."  

"Two debtors, one owed much, another less,  
Both debts forgiven, who’ll love more, confess?"  

Simon said, "The one forgiven the most,"  
Jesus nodded, and the woman He did host.  

"You gave no water, she wept for my feet,  
No kiss from you, her love's complete."  

Her sins forgiven, for her love was vast,  
Simon watched, his judgment surpassed.  

"Your faith has saved you," He said in peace,  
"Go on your way, let your love increase."
