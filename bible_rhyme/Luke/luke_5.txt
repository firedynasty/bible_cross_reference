Crowds pressed tight, hearing God's word by the lake,  
Two boats standing empty, just nets to shake.  

He stepped in Simon's boat, "Push out a bit."  
He taught from the water, the crowd was lit.  

"Head to the deep, let your nets go wide."  
Simon said, "All night we tried, empty inside,  

But on your word, we'll cast once more."  
Nets filled with fish, more than ever before.  

They called partners, both boats near to sink,  
Simon fell to his knees, couldn't think.  

"Depart, Lord, I'm full of sin," he pled,  
But Jesus said, "From now, fish men instead."  

They left all behind, on Him they leaned,  
And followed the path, their lives convened.  

In the city, a leper came to plea,  
"Lord, make me clean, if you will," on bended knee.  

"Be clean," He said, with a touch so light,  
Leprosy vanished, healing in sight.  

"Tell none, show the priest, let Moses' word stand,"  
But news spread wide, healing by His hand.  

He withdrew to pray, yet crowds still sought,  
Teaching filled the air, Pharisees caught.  

Men carried a friend, on a cot they lay,  
Crowded, they climbed, lowered him that day.  

Seeing faith, Jesus spoke clear and true,  
"Your sins forgiven," shocked Pharisees knew.  

"Who is this?" they whispered, "Only God can,"  
Jesus, knowing their thoughts, began.  

"What's easier, forgive or to walk?"  
To the man, "Rise, take your cot, no more talk."  

He stood, amazed, glorified His name,  
Crowds in awe, "Strange things!" they proclaimed.  

Levi at the tax booth, Jesus walked by,  
"Follow me now," he left all, no goodbye.  

At Levi's feast, tax collectors abound,  
Pharisees questioned, their murmurs profound.  

"Why dine with sinners?" they asked in disdain,  
"Healthy need no doctor," came Jesus' plain.  

"I'm here for sinners, not righteous, to mend,"  
Questioned about fasting, He explained the trend.  

"Friends of the bridegroom feast while He's near,  
Days will come with fasting, when He's not here."  

A parable He gave, garments and wine,  
New and old mixed, ruin their design.  

New wine, fresh skins, preservation's art,  
Old wine preferred, new's hard to start.  

With these words, a truth He'd unfurled,  
He spoke to change, to a waiting world.
