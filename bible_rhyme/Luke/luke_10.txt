Seventy sent, in pairs they roam,  
Into each city, where He’d soon make His home.  
"The harvest is great, but workers are few,  
Pray to the Lord, He’ll send laborers through."

"Go as lambs where wolves might reside,  
Carry no purse, let My peace be your guide.  
Enter a house, say, 'Peace to this place,'  
If peace finds a home, let it embrace."

"Stay in that house, eat what they share,  
The worker's due pay, be fair and square.  
Heal the sick, say, 'God’s Kingdom's near,'  
If you’re not received, then make it clear."

Shake off the dust, leave it behind,  
"The Kingdom's come, though you were blind."  
For some, judgment's harsh, Sodom's fate will pale,  
"More tolerable for them if you fail."

"Woe to Chorazin, Bethsaida too!  
Miracles ignored, what will you do?  
Capernaum raised, but soon you'll fall,  
Rejecting My messengers, you reject all."

Seventy returned, filled with delight,  
"Even demons flee at Your name, what a sight!"  
"Satan fell like lightning," He replied,  
"You’ve power over all, with Me as your guide."

"Rejoice not in spirits that heed,  
But that heaven records your deed."  
Jesus thanked His Father above,  
For revealing truth with childlike love.

To disciples He turned, words He did impart,  
"Blessed are your eyes, seeing what others cannot.  
Prophets and kings longed for this view,  
But you see and hear—you're the chosen few."

A lawyer stood, seeking to test,  
"How to find life that's truly blessed?"  
"Love God fully, your neighbor as dear,"  
"Correct," said Jesus, "Do this without fear."

"Who is my neighbor?" came the next cue,  
Jesus told a tale to make it clear and true:  
A man robbed, beaten, left for the dead,  
Priest and Levite passed by, no tears shed.

A Samaritan came, his heart full of grace,  
Bound up the wounds, took him to a safe place.  
Paid for his care, promised more when he’d return,  
"Who was the neighbor?" "The one who showed concern."

"Go and do likewise," Jesus did say,  
As they traveled onward, into a new day.  
Martha welcomed Him, with tasks galore,  
Mary sat calmly, soaking in more.

"Martha, Martha, you're troubled, it's clear,  
One thing is needed—Mary chose it here.  
What she’s found won't be taken away,  
Sit at my feet, take time to stay."
