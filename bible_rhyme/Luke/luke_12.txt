Thousands gathered, a crowd so tight,  
He told his disciples, "Stay clear of the blight,  
The yeast of the Pharisees, hypocrisy's game,  
What’s hidden in shadows will burst into flame.  

What you whisper in rooms will shout from the top,  
In the bright light of truth, nothing will stop.  
"Fear not those who can kill, and then have no sway,  
But fear the one with the power to cast away.  

Five little sparrows, worth just two coins,  
Yet not one is forgotten in God’s vast designs.  
Your hairs, they’re counted, so don’t be dismayed,  
You’re worth more than sparrows, so don’t be afraid.  

"Confess me to men, and I’ll speak your name,  
Deny me, and the angels won’t know your fame.  
Blaspheme the Spirit, no forgiveness in sight,  
But speak against me, and you’re alright.  

Before rulers and courts, fear not what to say,  
The Spirit will guide you, word by word, way by way.”  
"Teacher," a voice rose from the crowd so loud,  
"Tell my brother to share," he tried to be proud.  

But he answered, "Man, who made me decide?  
Beware of greed, let it not guide.  
Life’s not in treasures, possessions you keep,  
A rich fool’s story echoes, his harvest so deep.  

He thought, 'I need bigger barns for my store,'  
But God said, 'Tonight, your soul’s required more.'  
What good is treasure when your life’s out of time?  
Be rich toward God, let that be your climb."  

"Don't fret for your life, food, or attire,  
Life’s more than clothing, more than your desire.  
Look at the ravens, they don’t sow or reap,  
Yet God feeds them, as you rest or sleep.  

Worry not, can it add to your height?  
If you can’t do the least, then why the fright?  
Consider the lilies, in glory they grow,  
More splendid than Solomon, they steal the show.  

If grass gets such care, bound for the flame,  
Won’t He clothe you? Why live in shame?  
Seek not what to eat, or what you’ll drink,  
Your Father knows, let Him be your link.  

Seek first His Kingdom, all needs He will meet,  
Fear not, little flock, your future’s complete.  
Sell what you have, share with the poor,  
Store treasures in heaven, where they’ll endure.  

"Keep your lamps burning, be dressed and ready,  
Like servants who watch, alert and steady.  
Blessed are they, whom the master will find,  
Watching when he knocks, with an open mind.  

If he comes late, in the night’s deep dark,  
Blessed are those still ready to embark.  
The master watches, thief knows not the hour,  
Be prepared, for the Son comes with power."  

Peter asked, "Lord, are these words ours alone?"  
"Who’s the faithful steward, wise to the bone?  
Blessed is he, serving when I arrive,  
I’ll set him over all, his deeds kept alive.  

But if he grows lazy, thinking 'There’s time,'  
His master arrives, sees his crime.  
He’ll cut him down, with the unfaithful he'll stay,  
Much given requires much, that’s the way.  

"I came to bring fire, how I wish it was lit,  
I’ll be baptized with pain, I won’t quit.  
Think I bring peace? It’s division I sow,  
Families divided, the sparks will grow.  

Look at the sky, you predict the signs,  
Yet miss the times, divine designs.  
Settle your debts before you reach court,  
Lest the judge takes your last resort.  

Pay to the last penny, no chance to flee,  
Interpret the times, and seek to be free."
