He rolled into Jericho, crowd thick as a wall,  
Zacchaeus was short, but he wanted to see it all.  

Chief tax guy, rich, up in a tree he'd race,  
"Zacchaeus, come down, I'm coming to your place."  

He climbed down quick, joy lighting up his face,  
But folks grumbled, "With a sinner, He gives grace."  

Zacchaeus stood up, pledged to make it right,  
"Half to the poor, I'll restore all I took by night."  

Jesus smiled, said, "Salvation's come today,  
Son of Abraham, the lost I came to save."  

Onward He moved, a parable He'd tell,  
"About a nobleman, hear me well.  

He handed coins, told his servants, 'Trade till I return,'  
But folks didn't want him, their disdain would burn.  

He came back king, called servants near,  
Asked, 'What'd you do while I was not here?'  

First servant said, 'Ten more I've gained,'  
'Well done, ten cities you shall claim.'  

The next said, 'Five more for your purse,'  
'Rule five cities, your rewards diverse.'  

One hid the coin, fear in his eyes,  
'Judge me not, for your ways I despise.'  

The nobleman replied, 'Why not invest?  
Take his coin, give to the best.'  

To those who have, more shall be given,  
For those without, even that's riven."  

He moved toward Jerusalem, disciples by his side,  
Sent for a colt, on it He would ride.  

"If asked why you untie, say, 'The Lord's in need,'"  
Colt came, clothes laid, He'd proceed.  

Joyful shouts, cloaks on the road they spread,  
"Blessed is the King," their praises led.  

Pharisees fretted, "Teacher, hush this noise,"  
"If silent, stones would sing," He voiced.  

He saw the city, sorrow in His eyes,  
"If you'd known peace, it wouldn't be disguised.  

Enemies will close in, not a stone left alone,  
For missing the moment, peace overthrown."  

He cleared the temple, buyers and sellers scattered,  
"'House of prayer,' not robbers' den," His voice mattered.  

Teaching daily, His words they'd savor,  
But the leaders sought to end His favor.  

Yet the crowd clung tight, to every word He spoke,  
In the temple, wisdom awoke.
