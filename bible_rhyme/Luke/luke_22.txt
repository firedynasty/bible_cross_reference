Feast of the bread, Passover's near,  
Chief priests plot, filled with fear.  

Satan whispers to Judas’ ear,  
One of the twelve, his path unclear.  

He makes a deal, with coins they cheer,  
Looks for a chance when the crowd ain't near.  

Now the day of the bread’s begun,  
Jesus sends Peter and John, "Get it done."  

"Where do we prep?" they ask with care,  
"In the city, find a man with a jar, follow him there.  

Tell the house's master, ‘The Teacher needs a room,’  
He'll show a place prepared, free from gloom."  

Peter and John found it true,  
The Passover prep, they perfectly knew.  

When the hour came, Jesus sat with his crew,  
Said, "I’ve wanted this meal before my due.  

I won’t eat again till Kingdom’s true,  
Take this cup, share it anew."  

Bread in hand, broke it with grace,  
“My body for you, keep it in place.”  

The cup after supper, the new bond’s sign,  
“My blood poured for you, covenant divine.  

Betrayer's hand with me on the table,  
Son of Man goes, but woe to the unstable."  

They questioned who it might be,  
And argued who’d be greatest, who’ll see?  

Jesus taught the kings’ way versus his,  
"Serve like the youngest, that’s what it is.  

I’m here among you serving all,  
In my Kingdom, you’ll stand tall."  

"Simon, Simon, Satan’s on the prowl,  
I prayed your faith won’t throw in the towel."  

Peter said, "Prison or death, I’m in line,"  
Jesus replied, "Denial before the rooster’s sign."  

"Remember when you lacked naught?  
Now trade cloak for a sword, don’t get caught.  

Scripture’s fulfillment must be sought,  
Counted with transgressors, it’s all been taught."  

They went to the Mount, as was his way,  
Jesus urged, "Pray, avoid temptation today."  

Alone he knelt, the night so tight,  
“Father, your will, not mine tonight.”  

Angels came, his strength renewed,  
Sweat like blood, his prayer pursued.  

He found them asleep, grief held them tight,  
“Wake up, pray, find your fight.”  

Judas approached with a betrayer's kiss,  
"Son of Man betrayed with this?"  

The crowd surged with swords and might,  
A disciple struck, cutting in the night.  

"Enough," said Jesus, "let me heal,"  
Touched the ear, made it real.  

"Why come like robbers, swords and clubs?  
In the temple, you showed no grubs."  

Seized and led to the priest’s place,  
Peter followed, hiding his face.  

By the fire’s light, the girl did claim,  
Peter denied, "I don’t know that name."  

Another pointed, "You’re one of them,"  
"Man, I’m not," Peter said again.  

A third accused, "Galilean sight,"  
"Man, I know not," denial in the night.  

The rooster crowed, the Lord’s eyes met,  
Peter remembered, tears were set.  

The guards mocked, with strikes they played,  
Blindfolded, they called, “Who struck?” they swayed.  

Morning assembly, elders drew near,  
"Are you the Christ?" they asked clear.  

“If I tell, you won’t believe,  
Son of Man at God’s hand, where I'll leave.”  

"You the Son of God?" they pressed,  
"You say it, I am," he confessed.  

“No more witness needed,” they cried,  
“From his own mouth, he supplied.”
