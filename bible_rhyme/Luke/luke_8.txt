Through cities and towns, the Good News spread,  
With the Twelve and women healed, demons fled.  

Mary Magdalene, seven spirits cast out,  
Joanna, Susanna, they served without doubt.  

Crowds gathered 'round, to hear the parable told,  
"The farmer sows seeds, some fall on the road."  

Trampled and eaten, some seeds went to waste,  
Others on rocks, no moisture to taste.  

Among the thorns, some seeds get choked,  
But the good ground's harvest, a hundredfold spoke.  

"He with ears to hear, let him hear,"  
He called out loud, making the message clear.  

His disciples asked, "What's this story mean?"  
"The seed's God's word, take heed to the scene."  

Some hear but lose it, snatched from the start,  
Others rejoice, but with no root in the heart.  

Thorns choke with worries, riches, desires,  
But the good soil bears fruit, like a fire.  

"No one lights a lamp and hides it away,"  
For secrets revealed will see the day.  

"Take care how you hear, whoever has will gain,  
Who lacks loses even what they claim."  

Family outside, waiting in the crowd,  
"Those who do God's will, they're with me now."  

In a boat with the Twelve, across the lake they go,  
But storms arise, waters overflow.  

"Master, we're dying!" they cried in fear,  
He calmed the storm, "Where's your faith here?"  

"Who commands the winds and waters to cease?"  
They wondered amazed, as the storm found peace.  

At Gadarenes' shore, a possessed man appeared,  
"Naked in tombs, by demons steered."  

"Legion's my name," many spirits inside,  
Begged not to the abyss, but in pigs to reside.  

Demons fled, pigs drowned in the lake,  
The man, now free, the townsfolk quake.  

"Go home, tell of God's deeds," Jesus bid him,  
He spread the word of what Jesus did for him.  

Back across the lake, the crowd gathered near,  
Jairus, ruler, begged with tear.  

His daughter, twelve, lay sick, near end,  
But as Jesus walked, the crowds did bend.  

A woman, bleeding twelve years long,  
Touched his cloak, made whole, healed strong.  

"Who touched me?" in the throng, He'd ask,  
Power had left Him, though hidden in task.  

She trembled, confessed in the people's view,  
"Daughter, your faith has healed you."  

But news came quick, "Your daughter's gone,"  
Yet Jesus said, "Just believe, hold on."  

Inside the house, mourners cried,  
"She's not dead, just asleep," He replied.  

He took her hand, "Child, arise!" was His call,  
Her spirit returned, amazed were they all.  

"Give her food, but tell no one this day,"  
Miracles witnessed, the power in His way.
