Rich folks were dropping gifts, making it rain,  
But a poor widow gave her all, in the smallest of coins' name.  

He said, "Hear me now, she gave more than all,  
They gave from wealth, she gave her all, standing tall."  

Talking 'bout the temple, stones shining bright,  
He warned, "Soon not one stone will be left in sight."  

"When’s it gonna happen?" they asked, seeking the sign,  
"Don't be misled by those claiming 'I’m divine.'"  

"Nation against nation, kingdom will rise,  
Earthquakes, famines, plagues, and signs from the skies."  

"They'll grab you, persecute, haul you before kings,  
But it’s your testimony that your endurance brings."  

"Don’t fret what to say, I’ll give you the word,  
Wisdom they can’t counter, truth to be heard."  

"Betrayed by family, for my name you'll stand,  
But not a hair will perish, you'll be in my hand."  

"Jerusalem’s siege—armies closing tight,  
Flee to the mountains, escape into the night."  

"Days of vengeance, prophecy fulfilled,  
Great distress upon the land, all its peace killed."  

"Signs in the heavens, fear in the air,  
Then see the Son of Man, coming with power beyond compare."  

"When you see the fig tree budding, know what's near,  
God’s Kingdom coming, be ready, have no fear."  

"This generation sees it done, heaven and earth may pass,  
But my words stand eternal, a truth that will last."  

"Stay vigilant, avoid life's heavy chain,  
Be ready for that day, like a snare it will claim."  

"Pray to escape, stand strong before the Son,"  
Every day in the temple, teaching till the day was done.  

At Olivet each night, he found peace and rest,  
Crowds gathered in the morning, eager for wisdom’s best.
