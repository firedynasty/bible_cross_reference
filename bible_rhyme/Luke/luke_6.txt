Out in the grain fields on a Sabbath day,  
The disciples plucked heads, rubbed them away.  
Pharisees questioned, "Why break the law?"  
But Jesus reminded them of David's score.  

"In God's house he ate when he was in need,  
Shared the bread meant for priests, indeed."  
"The Son of Man," He said with might,  
"Is Lord of the Sabbath, holds the right."  

Another Sabbath, in the synagogue He taught,  
A man with a withered hand, in His thoughts.  
Pharisees watched, hoping He'd slip,  
But Jesus knew their minds, wouldn't trip.  

"Stand up," He said to the man in the crowd,  
"Is it lawful to do good?" He asked aloud.  
"Stretch out your hand," and the man obeyed,  
Healed as the Pharisees' plans decayed.  

Filled with rage, they plotted and schemed,  
While Jesus on a mountain prayed and dreamed.  
In the morning, He gathered His crew,  
Twelve apostles, each one He knew.  

Simon, Peter, James, and John,  
Andrew, Philip, all drawn.  
Matthew and Thomas, James of Alphaeus too,  
Simon the Zealot, Bartholomew.  

Judas, son of James, and Iscariot's name,  
Who later would bring betrayal and shame.  
Crowds flocked to hear, to be healed and blessed,  
From Judea, Jerusalem, they sought rest.  

"Blessed are the poor," He declared with grace,  
"God's Kingdom is yours, find your place."  
"Blessed are the hungry, the weeping, the scorned,  
Rejoice, your reward in heaven is born."  

"But woe to the rich, who've had their fill,  
Woe to the laughing, who soon will chill.  
Woe to the praised, for empty words they chase,  
Like false prophets, they lose their place."  

"Love your enemies, do them good,  
Bless those who curse, as you should.  
Turn the other cheek, give your coat,  
Treat others well, from deep in your throat."  

"Judge not, and you'll walk free,  
Give, and it will come back, you'll see."  
"Blind can't guide blind, both will fall,  
See your own faults, before calling out all."  

No good tree bears rotten fruit,  
Each known by its own pursuit.  
Out of the heart, the mouth speaks clear,  
Good treasure shines, evil brings fear.  

"Why call 'Lord' and not obey?  
Listen and do, find the way."  
A house on rock withstands the stream,  
But on sand, it falls, just a dream."
