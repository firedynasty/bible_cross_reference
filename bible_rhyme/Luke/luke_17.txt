"Stumbles will come, they can't be undone,  
But woe to the one who makes others run.  
A millstone around the neck, a deep blue sea,  
Better than causing little ones to flee."

"Be careful, if your brother sins, call him out,  
If he repents, forgive, let there be no doubt.  
Seven times a day, he might fall and rise,  
But forgive him each time, that's wise."

"Increase our faith!" the apostles plead,  
The Lord said, "Faith a mustard seed can lead.  
Tell a tree, 'Move to the sea,' it'd obey,  
Faith small as this can sway."

"Servants work hard, plow and keep sheep,  
Do they sit first? No, they duties keep.  
Prepare the meal, serve first, then dine,  
They're unworthy servants, doing the line."

On his way to Jerusalem, borders he crossed,  
Samaria and Galilee, where paths were tossed.  
Ten lepers cried out, "Master, have grace!"  
"Show the priests," he said, their illness effaced.

One turned back, praised with loud voice,  
At Jesus' feet, he made a choice.  
Jesus asked, "Were ten healed, but where's the nine?  
Only this foreigner returns, divine."

"Get up, go your way, your faith made you well,"  
The Kingdom within you, no one can tell.  
Not here, not there, it's inside,  
In your heart, where faith resides."

"The days will come, you’ll long to see,  
The Son of Man, but it won't be.  
Don't chase the signs, when they say, 'Look here!'  
Like lightning, he'll appear, clear."

"As in Noah’s day, they ate, they wed,  
Till the flood came down, and all were dead.  
In Lot's time, fire rained from the sky,  
Destroyed them all, just by and by."

"When He's revealed, don't look back,  
Remember Lot’s wife, stay on track.  
Save your life, you lose it, that's the deal,  
Lose it, and true life you'll reveal."

"Two in bed, one taken, one left alone,  
Two grinding grain, one gone, one on their own."  
"Where, Lord?" they asked, longing to see,  
"Where the body's found, that's where the vultures will be."
