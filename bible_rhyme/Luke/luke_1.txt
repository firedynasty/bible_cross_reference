Yo, listen up, I'm setting the scene,  
A story of truth, things unseen.  
The days of Herod, king on the throne,  
A priest named Zacharias, not alone.  

His wife Elizabeth, pure and true,  
Righteous folks, they followed every clue.  
But no child came, they were old and gray,  
Yet Zacharias served, come what may.  

In the temple, incense he burned,  
Prayers outside, the people yearned.  
An angel appeared, the priest's heart raced,  
"Don't fear," said Gabriel, "your prayers are embraced.  

Elizabeth will bear you a son,  
Call him John, his work's just begun.  
Many will find joy, he'll be great,  
In the Spirit's power, he'll create.  

He'll prep the way, turn hearts to Him,  
Make the path straight, no longer dim."  
Zacharias asked, "How can this be?  
We're too old, you see?"  

Gabriel replied, "I stand with the Divine,  
You'll be silent, but watch Him shine."  
The crowd outside waited, time ticked away,  
He came out mute, had nothing to say.  

Elizabeth conceived, she hid in grace,  
"God's blessed me, removed disgrace."  
Then Gabriel visited a girl named Mary,  
"Rejoice, you're blessed, don't be wary.  

A son you'll bear, name him Jesus,  
His reign forever, no one to seize us."  
Mary asked, "How can this be real?"  
"The Spirit's power, that's the deal."  

Mary visited Elizabeth, joy leaped inside,  
"Blessed are you," Elizabeth cried.  
Mary sang praises, her soul magnified,  
Generations blessed, with God she allied.  

Elizabeth gave birth, joy spread around,  
On the eighth day, a name was found.  
"Not Zacharias, his name's John!"  
The people marveled, the silence gone.  

Zacharias praised, Spirit-filled he spoke,  
"Blessed is the Lord," the silence broke.  
John grew strong, wild and free,  
Preparing the way for what's to be.  

The story unfolds, prophecies told,  
God's hand in action, witness it unfold.
