The crowd rose up, took him to Pilate's place,  
Accused him of perverting the nations' face.  

"He forbids taxes, claims he's a king," they cried,  
Pilate asked, "King of the Jews?" Jesus replied.  

"You say it," he spoke, calm and clear,  
Pilate told the priests, "No charges here."  

But they pressed on, "He stirs up all the land,"  
From Galilee to here, we understand.  

Pilate caught the word "Galilee," asked the case,  
Sent him to Herod, in Jerusalem's space.  

Herod was happy, he'd longed for this day,  
He wanted miracles, wanted a display.  

Questioned with words, but Jesus stood still,  
While priests and scribes accused with a will.  

Herod mocked him, dressed him in fine clothes,  
Sent him back to Pilate, friends now, not foes.  

Pilate called them back, the rulers and the crowd,  
Examined him, said, "No guilt I've found."  

"Herod found nothing, no death-worthy crime,  
I'll chastise and release him, just give it time."  

But the crowd shouted, "Release Barabbas!"  
A rebel and murderer, causing much fuss.  

Pilate tried again, sought Jesus' release,  
But they shouted, "Crucify him, won't you cease?"  

"What has he done?" Pilate asked with a sigh,  
"I'll chastise him once more, then let him fly."  

But their voices rose, louder than before,  
Demanding crucifixion, their voices soared.  

Pilate gave in to their urgent plea,  
Released Barabbas, but Jesus was to be.  

Led him away, Simon bore the cross weight,  
Following women mourned his fate.  

Jesus turned, "Weep not for me," he said,  
"For your children, save tears instead.  

Days will come, barren wombs they'll bless,  
Mountains and hills they'll wish to address."  

Two criminals joined him on this path,  
At the Skull, they faced the aftermath.  

"Father, forgive them," Jesus cried,  
"They don't know the deed they've tried."  

Soldiers gambled for his clothes, looked on,  
Rulers scoffed, "Save yourself, chosen one!"  

A sign read, "King of the Jews," above his head,  
Mocked by soldiers, vinegar they fed.  

One thief mocked, "Save us too, if you're the Christ!"  
The other rebuked, "We've earned this plight."  

"Remember me," he asked with a plea,  
Jesus replied, "In Paradise, you'll be with me."  

Darkness fell from the sixth hour till nine,  
The temple veil tore, a divine sign.  

"Into your hands, I commit my spirit!" he cried,  
Breathed his last breath, and then he died.  

The centurion saw, "Righteous man," he declared,  
Crowds left, hearts heavy, souls bared.  

Joseph from Arimathaea, good and true,  
Asked Pilate for the body, knew what to do.  

Wrapped him in linen, laid him in stone,  
A tomb untouched, a place of his own.  

The Sabbath neared, women watched and wept,  
Prepared spices, while the commandment they kept.
