A decree from Caesar, the empire's call,  
"Go to your hometown, get enrolled, one and all."  

Joseph left Nazareth, to Bethlehem he went,  
With Mary, soon to be wed, her time was spent.  

In the town, no room, just a stable to stay,  
She gave birth to a son, wrapped him, laid him on hay.  

Shepherds in the field, watching flocks by night,  
An angel appeared, filling them with fright.  

"Fear not," he said, "I've news of joy today,  
A Savior born in David's town, laid in the hay."  

Suddenly, a chorus, angels filled the sky,  
"Praise to God on high, peace to men nearby."  

Shepherds hurried to see, and what did they find?  
Mary, Joseph, and the babe, God's promise unlined.  

They spread the word, amazed all who heard,  
Mary pondered deeply, every spoken word.  

Shepherds returned, hearts full of praise,  
For all they had seen, in those wondrous days.  

Baby's eighth day, time for his name,  
Called Jesus, as angel proclaimed.  

To Jerusalem they went, to fulfill the law,  
Offering turtledoves, in reverent awe.  

Simeon in the temple, righteous and devout,  
"Lord, I've seen salvation!" he began to shout.  

A light for the nations, glory for Israel,  
Joseph and Mary marveled at what he'd tell.  

Simeon blessed them, spoke to Mary with care,  
"This child's a sign, piercing hearts laid bare."  

Prophetess Anna, aged and wise,  
Gave thanks, spoke of redemption, to open eyes.  

Back to Nazareth, their journey complete,  
Jesus grew strong, wisdom in his seat.  

Every Passover, they made their way,  
To Jerusalem, as was custom's sway.  

At twelve, Jesus stayed, without parent's trace,  
In the temple, teaching with grace.  

They found him there, amazed at his mind,  
"Didn't you know in my Father's house, I'd find?"  

Misunderstood, yet obedient and wise,  
He grew in favor, before men's eyes.
