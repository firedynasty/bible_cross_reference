Out the sky, an angel came down with power so great,  
Lit up the earth, left no room for debate.  

With a mighty voice, he called out the fate,  
"Babylon has fallen, now demons congregate.  

Nations drunk on her sin, kings in her embrace,  
Merchants rich from her luxury, a disgrace."  

Another voice from heaven, a warning to state,  
"Come out, my people, avoid her cursed fate.  

Her sins reach the sky, God won't forget,  
Repay her double, she'll get what she set.  

She boasted strong, said mourning ain't her game,  
But in one day, fire will claim her name.  

Kings will wail as they see her smoke rise,  
Standing afar, fear in their eyes.  

'Woe, woe, Babylon, your judgment's come fast,'  
In one single hour, your riches couldn't last."  

Merchants will cry over their unsold wares,  
Gold, silver, and silks, lost in despair.  

Spices and goods, all vanished from view,  
What you lusted after, now lost to you.  

They'll stand afar, mourning in pain,  
"Great city, your riches have gone down the drain.  

In an hour, all wealth turns to dust,  
The sea folk watch, their fortunes combust."  

Dust on their heads, they cry with a roar,  
"Woe, the great city, now rich no more."  

Heaven rejoice, for justice is delivered,  
Babylon's fall, the saints' hearts are quivered.  

A mighty angel flings a stone to the sea,  
"With violence, Babylon's history will be.  

No more music, no craftsman's hand,  
The city's light extinguished, barren the land.  

Bride and groom's voices silenced in gloom,  
For merchants deceived, cast spells of doom.  

In her found the blood of the pure and the brave,  
Prophets and saints resting in their grave."
