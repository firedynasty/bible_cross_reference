The Lamb broke a seal with power and might,  
A thunderous creature called, “Come, see the sight!”  

Behold, a white horse, rider with a bow,  
Crown on his head, conquering's his show.  

The second seal opened, another voice called, “Come!”  
A red horse emerged, with peace now undone.  

Power to take peace, a great sword in his hand,  
Men turned on each other across every land.  

Third seal now broken, “Come and see!” cried the voice,  
A black horse appeared, its rider's choice.  

With balance in hand, and a voice did decree,  
“For wheat and barley, pay a denarius fee!”  

The fourth seal shattered, a creature’s call loud,  
A pale horse rode in under death's dark shroud.  

With Hades in tow, and power to kill,  
One fourth of the earth, by sword, famine, and ill.  

Fifth seal revealed souls under the altar,  
Cried, “How long, O Master, ‘til justice won't falter?”  

White robes given, “Rest yet a little more,  
Until your brothers join you, their course to explore.”  

Sixth seal opened, earth trembled and roared,  
Sun turned black, moon blood-red, chaos soared.  

Stars fell like figs from the windy shake,  
Sky rolled up, mountains and islands quake.  

Kings, princes, strong and free, in terror they stand,  
“Hide us from wrath, who can withstand?”  

The day of the Lamb’s wrath now has begun,  
Who can endure before the Holy One?
