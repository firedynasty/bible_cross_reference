Saw in the right hand, a book tight, sealed with seven seals real tight,  
A mighty angel cried with might, “Who can break the seals and show the light?”  

In heaven, earth, and beneath, no one could read, no one could reach,  
Tears I shed, no worthy one, to open the book, a task undone.  

An elder spoke with calming tone, “Don’t weep, the Lion’s on the throne.  
From Judah’s tribe, David’s root, he conquers, opens, absolute.”  

In the midst of thrones and creatures fierce, a Lamb appeared, wounds that pierce,  
Seven horns, and seven eyes, God’s Spirits sent where the earth lies.  

He took the book from the hand on high, the creatures bowed, the elders cried,  
Harps and incense, saints’ prayers rise, their melody a sacrifice.  

A new song rose, “You're worthy, Lamb, for you were slain, you took the stand,  
For every tribe, nation, and clan, we reign as priests in God’s own land.”  

Heaven’s voices, angels in throng, creatures and elders joined the song,  
“Worthy the Lamb, who overcame, power, wealth, wisdom his acclaim.”  

Every creature, land and sea, sang to the throne in harmony,  
“To him who sits, and to the Lamb, blessing, honor, glory, amen!”  

The creatures echoed, “Amen!” again, as elders worshiped, end to end.
