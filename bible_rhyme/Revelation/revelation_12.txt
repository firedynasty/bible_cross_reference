In heaven, a vision, grand and bright,  
A woman clothed with the sun's pure light.  
Moon underfoot, twelve stars on her crown,  
With child in pain, her cries resound.  

Next, a red dragon, fierce and wild,  
Seven heads, ten horns, evil compiled.  
His tail swept stars, casting them down,  
He waits to devour the newborn crown.  

She births a son, destined to reign,  
With iron rod, he'll break the chain.  
But up to God, the child ascends,  
While she retreats where God defends.  

War in the sky, Michael takes stand,  
Against the dragon and his band.  
But they're defeated, cast from heaven,  
No place left for them, peace given.  

The dragon thrown, the old serpent's fall,  
Devil and Satan, deceiver to all.  
In heaven a voice, loud and clear,  
"Salvation has come, God's Kingdom is here.  

Overcome by the Lamb's own blood,  
Their testimony stood strong in the flood.  
Rejoice, O heavens, but earth beware,  
The devil's wrath is near, so take care."  

Dragon on earth, he hunts the queen,  
But wings of eagles intervene.  
She flies to safety, away from harm,  
Protected, sheltered, from his alarm.  

Serpent spews water, a river wide,  
But the earth opens, the wave subsides.  
Dragon enraged, he turns to fight,  
Against her offspring, holding truth's light.
