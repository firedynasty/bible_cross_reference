He holds the stars in his right hand,  
He walks with golden lampstands, a command.  
"I know your toil, your perseverance too,  
You test false apostles, you see what's true.  
Endured for my name, you haven't grown weak,  
But left your first love—return, that's what I seek.  

Remember from where you fell, turn back,  
Repent, or your lampstand I'll attack.  
But you hate the Nicolaitans' deeds,  
"Whoever has ears, let them heed.  
To the overcomer, the tree of life,  
In God's paradise, free from strife.  

To Smyrna's angel, the First and Last,  
Who died and lives, his words cast:  
"I know your works, your struggles, your pain,  
But you're rich despite the disdain.  
Don't fear the suffering, prison may come,  
Stay faithful to death, the crown of life is won.  

"Whoever has ears, let them hear,  
Overcomers will taste no second fear.  

To Pergamum's angel, he speaks with a sword,  
"I know where you dwell, where evil is stored.  
Yet you hold my name, with faith you stand,  
In Antipas' days, in that harsh land.  
But Balaam's teaching's in your space,  
Idols and immorality, a disgrace.  

Repent, or my sword will wage war,  
"Whoever has ears, heed this, and more.  
To the overcomer, hidden manna I'll give,  
A white stone, a new name by which to live.  

To Thyatira, the Son of God speaks,  
With eyes of fire and brass-like feet.  
"I know your love, your service, your faith,  
But Jezebel's seduction lays waste.  
She refused to turn, her ways are wrong,  
Her followers face oppression's song.  

Search their hearts, I'll judge by deeds,  
Some don't know Satan's seeds.  
Hold firm till I come, your works secure,  
Authority over nations, for sure.  
"Whoever has ears, let them hear,  
My morning star, to the faithful, near."
