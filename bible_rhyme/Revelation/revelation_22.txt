A river flows, clear as crystal, from the throne,  
Life's water runs, giving life unknown.  

In the middle, streets and trees align,  
Twelve fruits they bear, month by month in line.  

Leaves heal nations, no curse remains,  
God's throne is here, His servants reign.  

They'll see His face, His name's on their heads,  
No need for night, by God's light they're led.  

"These words are true," the angel said to me,  
"Sent by God's spirit, prophecy to see."  

"Behold, I'm coming quick," said the Lord, 
"Blessed are those who keep this holy word."  

I, John, saw it all and fell in awe,  
But the angel stopped me, "Worship God alone," he saw.  

"Don't seal these words, the time is near,  
Let the unjust and holy keep their sphere."  

"Behold, I'm coming quick, with rewards in hand,  
Alpha, Omega, First and Last, I stand."  

Blessed are those who keep His command,  
Enter the city, by His tree stand.  

Outside are dogs, sorcerers in their lies,  
But in this city, truth never dies.  

"I, Jesus, sent my angel to proclaim,  
Root of David, Morning Star, in my name."  

The Spirit and bride shout, "Come!"  
Let the thirsty take life’s water some.  

"Don't add or take from this prophecy,"  
Or face the plagues and lose the holy tree.  

He says, "Yes, I come quickly," and we say,  
Amen! Come, Lord Jesus, without delay.  

Grace be with all who read this scroll,  
The Lord Jesus Christ saves every soul. Amen.
