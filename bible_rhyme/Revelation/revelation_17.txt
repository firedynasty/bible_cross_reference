An angel approached, with a bowl in his hand,  
"Come see the judgment, the scene I have planned.  

The great prostitute on many waters she sits,  
The kings of the earth in her web, they commit.  

Drunk on her wine, the earthlings all roam,  
He carried me off, to the wild we were flown.  

There sat a woman on a beast blazing red,  
Covered in blasphemy, many heads it spread.  

Dressed in fine purple, with gold she was bright,  
Holding a cup of earth’s sins in her sight.  

"MYSTERY," it read on her brow with pride,  
Babylon the Great, in her horror I spied.  

Drunk on saints' blood, martyrs of the Lamb,  
I gazed in wonder, she didn’t give a damn.  

The angel asked, "Why do you stare?  
I'll reveal the mystery, if you dare.  

The beast you saw, was, is not, will rise,  
From the abyss to its own demise.  

Earth's dwellers marvel, their names not found,  
In the book of life, from time's very ground.  

Seven heads are seven hills she rides,  
Seven kings fallen, one still bides.  

The beast’s the eighth, yet part of the seven,  
Headed for destruction, far from heaven.  

Ten horns are kings, no kingdoms yet,  
With the beast they rise, a bond they set.  

Warring the Lamb, but the Lamb prevails,  
Lord of lords, King of kings, He never fails.  

The waters are nations, peoples and tongues,  
Where she sits, where her dirge is sung.  

The horns and beast, they'll turn with hate,  
Strip her bare, burn her fate.  

For God's will beats in their hearts as one,  
To give the kingdom till His words are done.  

The woman you saw, that great city's might,  
Ruling kings, in her darkened light."
