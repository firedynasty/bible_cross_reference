A reed like a rod was placed in my hand,  
"Rise, measure the temple, altar, and stand.  

Leave out the court that's outside the gate,  
For the nations will tread it, that's their fate.  

Forty-two months they'll trample in scorn,  
But I'll empower my witnesses, forlorn.  

Two olive trees, two lamps stand bold,  
Prophesying in sackcloth, stories told.  

If harm's what they seek, fire will devour,  
Their words bring plagues, sky won't shower.  

When their testimony's done, the beast will rise,  
From the abyss, make war, claim their lives.  

In the great city's street, they'll lie cold,  
Sodom and Egypt, where their Lord was sold.  

For three and a half days, all will stare,  
Refusing a tomb, leaving them bare.  

The earth's dwellers rejoice, exchange gifts wide,  
Celebrating torment that these two supplied.  

But God's breath of life will enter anew,  
They'll stand on their feet, fear grips the view.  

A voice from heaven loud and clear,  
"Come up here!" and in clouds they appear.  

A great quake shakes, a tenth city falls,  
Seven thousand lost, terror enthralls.  

The second woe is gone, the third comes fast,  
The seventh angel sounds, heaven's voices cast.  

"The world's kingdom now the Lord's domain,  
Forever and ever, He shall reign!"  

The elders bow, faces to the ground,  
"Praise to You, Almighty, power profound.  

Nations raged, Your wrath unfurled,  
Time for judgment now in this world.  

Reward Your servants, prophets of light,  
The great and small, those in Your sight.  

Heaven's temple opened, the covenant's ark,  
Lightning and thunder, the earth goes dark."
