A loud voice in heaven, a shout so grand,  
“Hallelujah! Salvation and power are in God's hand.  

His judgments are true, righteous as they stand,  
Judged the great prostitute, avenged the blood on this land.”  

A second voice echoed, “Hallelujah, forever her smoke will burn,”  
Elders and creatures fell, God's praise their eternal concern.  

From the throne came a call, “Praise Him, both small and great!”  
A multitude roared, like waters and thunder, proclaiming His fate.  

“Hallelujah! The Lord Almighty reigns,  
Rejoice, give glory, for the Lamb's wedding remains.”  

The bride in fine linen, pure and bright,  
Saints’ righteous deeds, shining in the light.  

“Write this,” he said, “Blessed are those at the Lamb's feast,”  
“These are God's words, true,” he ceased.  

I bowed to worship, but he lifted me, “Don't!  
I'm a servant too, worship God alone, that's the point.”  

Heaven opened, a white horse in view,  
Faithful and True, He judges, makes war anew.  

Eyes of flame, crowns galore,  
Names unknown, mystery at the core.  

Clothed in blood, the Word of God is His name,  
Heavenly armies followed, white horses, no shame.  

From His mouth, a sword sharp and bright,  
Strikes nations, rules with iron, wrath's might.  

On His garment, thigh, a name clear and bold,  
“KING OF KINGS, LORD OF LORDS,” the story told.  

An angel in the sun, called birds to a feast,  
“Come, eat the flesh of kings, captains, and beast.”  

The beast and kings, armies amassed,  
Made war on the Rider, the final clash.  

The beast and prophet, deceived the marked,  
Thrown alive in fire, sulfur sparked.  

The rest fell by the sword, from the Rider's mouth it came,  
Birds filled with flesh, the end of their game.
