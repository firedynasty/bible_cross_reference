Up above, I looked, a door opened wide,  
A voice like a trumpet called from the sky: "Come inside,  

I'll show you what's next, things to unfold."  
In the Spirit, I moved, Heaven's secrets told.  

A throne stood there, gleaming like stone,  
Jasper and sardius, colors brightly shown.  

Around it a rainbow, an emerald glow,  
Twenty-four thrones, elders in robes of snow.  

Gold crowns adorned the elders' heads,  
From the throne, lightning and thunder spread.  

Seven lamps burned, Spirits of God in sight,  
A sea of glass, crystal-clear bright.  

Four living creatures, eyes all around,  
A lion, a calf, a man, an eagle unbound.  

Six wings they had, no rest in their flight,  
Crying, "Holy, holy, holy, day and night!  

Lord God Almighty, who was, who is, who'll be,  
Living creatures praise eternally."  

Elders fall down, casting crowns low,  
"Worthy are You, Lord, all glory You know.  

Honor and power, in Your hands they stay,  
You created all things, made by Your way!"  

With thunderous praise, Heaven's chorus rings,  
Declaring His power, the Creator of all things.
