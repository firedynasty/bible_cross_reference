An angel came down from heaven's gate,  
With a key to the abyss, a chain to seal fate.  

The dragon, old serpent, devil in disguise,  
Captured and bound, couldn't spread his lies.  

Into the abyss, cast and sealed tight,  
For a thousand years, he'd be out of sight.  

Saw thrones set high, judgment was given,  
Souls of the martyred, for Christ they'd risen.  

They reigned with Him a thousand years strong,  
A mark they refused, they stood all along.  

Rest of the dead, they waited their turn,  
First resurrection, the blessed concern.  

"Blessed and holy," in this they delight,  
Priests of God, reigning in the light.  

After a thousand years, Satan breaks free,  
Deceiving the nations, a war decree.  

Gathering forces, like sand by the sea,  
Surrounding the saints, thinking they'd flee.  

But fire from heaven came raining down,  
Devoured them all, took the devil's crown.  

The devil was tossed in the fiery lake,  
With the beast and prophet, forever to ache.  

A throne stood great, white as snow,  
Heaven and earth fled, nowhere to go.  

The dead stood tall, both great and small,  
Books of deeds opened, the judgment call.  

The sea gave its dead, Hades and Death,  
Judged by their works, each took a breath.  

Thrown into fire, death met its end,  
That lake of fire, where souls descend.  

If not in the book of life, they'd retire,  
To the endless torment of the lake of fire.
