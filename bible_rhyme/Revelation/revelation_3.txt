To the angel of Sardis, here's the word,  
Seven Spirits, seven stars, listen, be stirred.  
"I see your works, you seem alive, but you're dead,  
Wake up, hold tight to what little hasn't fled."

"Your deeds, not perfect before my eyes,  
Remember and repent, be wise.  
If you don't watch, I'll come like a thief,  
Unseen, unknown, causing disbelief."

"Some in Sardis haven't stained their dress,  
They'll walk with me, clothed in righteousness.  
He who conquers, dressed in white,  
In the book of life, his name I'll write."

Ear to hear, Spirit speaks to all,  
From Sardis, to Philly, hear the call.  
Holy and true holds David's key,  
Opens and shuts, sets the spirit free.

"I know your works, an open door,  
Holding fast to my word, you swore.  
False ones will bow before your feet,  
Knowing my love for you, complete."

"Kept my word through trial and test,  
I'll keep you safe when the world’s distressed.  
Hold on, don't lose your crown,  
Overcome, be a pillar, wear the gown."

"From heaven comes the name anew,  
Listen closely, Spirit speaks to you.  
To Laodicea, Amen's true witness,  
You're lukewarm, lacking fitness."

"Wish you were hot or cold, not bland,  
I'll spit you out, understand.  
You claim you're rich, but can't you see,  
You're wretched, poor, blind, seek clarity."

"Buy gold that's pure, garments white,  
Salve your eyes, regain your sight.  
Love reproves, so turn around,  
Open the door, let my love abound."

"To him who conquers, share my throne,  
As I with my Father, never alone.  
Ear to hear, Spirit speaks this truth,  
Listen well, heed the Spirit's booth."
