Behold the Lamb on Zion's rise,  
144,000 stand with eyes on the prize.  
Their foreheads marked, His name there displayed,  
Sound from heaven like thunder, a symphony played.  

A new song echoes, only their ears know,  
Redeemed from earth, in purity they glow.  
Virgins they are, following the Lamb's stride,  
In truth they walk, never once lied.  

An angel flies high, with Good News to share,  
"Fear the Lord, give glory, for judgment's over there.  
Worship the Creator, of sea, sky, and land!"  
Another angel cries, "Babylon's fall at hand."  

A third voice warns, "Beware the beast’s dark claim,  
Receive his mark, and suffer eternal flame.  
God's wrath is pure, in His cup of anger,  
Torment awaits, no rest, a constant clangor."  

Saints persevere, with faith in their hands,  
Keeping God's commandments, across all lands.  
A voice declares, "Blessed those who die in the Lord,  
They rest from their labors, their works their reward."  

A white cloud appears, a man with a crown,  
A sickle in hand, the harvest’s renown.  
An angel cries, "Reap, the earth is ripe!"  
The sickle swings, and humanity's stripe.  

Another angel, with a sickle sharp as ice,  
Calls from the altar, "Gather grapes of vice!"  
The vintage collected, wrath like a flood,  
Wine press outside the city, rivers of blood.
