I saw a vision, great and high,  
Seven angels, plagues, God's wrath up in the sky.  

A sea of glass, mixed with fire's glow,  
Victors over the beast, their harps in tow.  

They sang a song, Moses and the Lamb's refrain,  
"Praise the Lord Almighty, forever reign.  

Righteous and true, King of all nations,  
All will come to worship, in adoration."  

The temple gates swung open wide,  
In heaven's court, the truth can't hide.  

Seven angels, linen pure and bright,  
Golden sashes gleaming in the holy light.  

A creature gave them bowls of gold,  
Full of God's wrath, so mighty and bold.  

Smoke filled the temple, God's glory in power,  
No one entered in that solemn hour.  

Until the seven plagues would end their run,  
The mighty work of God would be done.
