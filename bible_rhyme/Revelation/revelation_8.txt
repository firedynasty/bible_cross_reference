The seventh seal opened, silence took the stage,  
Heaven held its breath, for half an hour's age.  

Seven angels stood tall, trumpets in their hand,  
Ready to sound off, by divine command.  

An angel with a censer, golden and bright,  
Stood by the altar, in heavenly light.  

Incense was given, prayers of saints rose high,  
Smoke curled to God, from the angel's hand to sky.  

He took the censer, filled it with fire,  
Cast it to earth, now things were dire.  

Thunder crashed, lightning flashed, the ground shook,  
The seven angels prepared, no second look.  

First trumpet sounded, hail and fire mixed with blood,  
One third of the earth scorched by this flood.  

Trees burned, grass gone, devastation spread,  
As the first trumpet's call left the planet red.  

The second angel blew, a mountain ablaze,  
Hurled into the sea, causing a haze.  

Waters turned to blood, creatures in the sea died,  
Ships were destroyed, waves of chaos wide.  

The third angel's blast brought a star from the sky,  
Wormwood fell, rivers and springs ran dry.  

Water turned bitter, many lives were claimed,  
By the poison they drank, the star's curse named.  

Fourth trumpet's echo struck sun, moon, and stars,  
One third darkened, cosmic lights marred.  

Day lost its shine, and night shared its fate,  
Darkness covered all, sealing their state.  

An eagle soared high, cried out loud and clear,  
"Woe to those on earth, the end draws near!"  

Three trumpets left, disaster yet to sound,  
Greater woes await, with each trumpet's pound.
