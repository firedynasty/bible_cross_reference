Out of the sky, an angel came flying, clothed with a cloud, no denying.  
Rainbow on his head, sun for a face, feet fired up, pillars in place.  

In his hand, a little book he held tight, right foot on the sea, left on land, standing might.  
He roared loud like a lion, bold and grand, the seven thunders echoed, shaking the land.  

I moved to write what the thunders had said, but a voice said, “Seal that truth, keep it in your head.”  
The angel on sea and land raised his hand high, swore by the Everlasting, who fashioned sky and land nearby.  

“No more delays,” the angel cried, "when the seventh speaks, mystery untied."  
The voice from heaven spoke to me once more, “Take the open book, here’s what’s in store.”  

I approached the angel, asked for the book, “Eat it,” he said, “bitter and sweet, take a look.”  
In my mouth, honey, sweetness it brought, but my stomach twisted, bitterness it wrought.  

They said, “You’ll prophesy far and wide, over nations, kings, in languages worldwide.”
