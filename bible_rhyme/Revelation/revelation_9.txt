The fifth angel blew his horn, a star fell from the sky,  
Key to the abyss pit was given, down below it did lie.  

He opened up the pit, smoke rose like a furnace burn,  
Sun and air darkened, thick smoke made the world churn.  

Out came locusts in the smoke, scorpions with power,  
Not to harm the grass or trees, but man in this dark hour.  

"Don’t touch the sealed," they were told, "torment those instead,  
For five months bring the pain, but leave them not dead."  

Folks sought death, but it ran, elusive like a ghost,  
Life's a prison, they cried, death denied, the ultimate host.  

Locusts looked like war horses, crowns of gold on heads,  
Faces human, hair flowing, teeth like lions, dread.  

Iron breastplates on their chests, wings loud as chariots race,  
Tails with power to sting, five months they left their trace.  

Their king, the abyss angel, Abaddon his name,  
Apollyon in Greek, but the mission’s the same.  

First woe had passed, but look, two more woes on their way,  
The sixth angel sounded, a new voice came to play.  

From the golden altar, the command loudly spoke,  
“Release the Euphrates-bound angels, now let them invoke!”  

Four angels set free for this moment in time,  
To slay a third of mankind, in a judgment so prime.  

Two hundred million horsemen, I heard them declare,  
Breastplates fiery red, blue, yellow in the air.  

Horse heads like lions, from mouths fire, smoke, and sulfur blast,  
One third of mankind fell, these plagues struck fast.  

Power in their mouths and tails, serpents with a bite,  
As devastation reigned, many lost the fight.  

Yet mankind did not repent, held tight to their hand-made ways,  
Worshipped idols dead and mute, in a haze they stayed.  

No turning from violence, magic, lust, or greed,  
Blind to the warnings, on these sins they still feed.
