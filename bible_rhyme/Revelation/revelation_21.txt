I saw a new heaven, a fresh earth too,  
The old and the sea vanished, out of view.  

A city descended, New Jerusalem's grace,  
Like a bride in beauty, took her place.  

A voice from the sky declared so clear,  
“God’s with His people, He’s dwelling here.  

Tears wiped away, death shall cease,  
No more sorrow, only peace.  

'Behold,' said the throne, 'I make things new,  
Write it down, these words are true.'”  

“I'm Alpha, Omega, Start and End,  
To the thirsty, living water I send.  

He who conquers will be my son,  
I’ll be his God, my will be done.  

But the cowards, liars, and all who deceive,  
In the burning lake they'll grieve.”  

An angel arrived, with plagues in bowls,  
“Come see the Lamb's bride,” was his goal.  

He took me high, to the mountain's view,  
Showed me the city with a light so true.  

Glory like jasper, crystal clear,  
Walls and gates, twelve angels near.  

East, north, south, and west, three gates each,  
On foundations, apostles' names did preach.  

Golden reed for measure in hand,  
City's a square, a perfect land.  

Walls of jasper, city gold like glass,  
Gem foundations, precious stones amassed.  

Gates of pearl, streets of gold so bright,  
No temple needed, God’s glory is light.  

No sun or moon, the Lamb is the lamp,  
Nations walk by light, no night’s stamp.  

Gates open wide, no night to fear,  
Only the pure and true enter here.  

No evil or lies in this place of life,  
Only those in the Lamb’s book, free from strife.
