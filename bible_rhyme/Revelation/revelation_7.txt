Yo, I saw four angels at the earth's four sides,  
Holding back the winds, so calm it rides.  

Another angel rose with the morning light,  
Seal of the living God, shining bright,  

Shouted loud to the four with might and plea,  
"Don't harm the earth, the sea, or any tree,  

Till we've sealed God's servants on their heads,"  
Marked for glory, where the spirit leads and spreads.  

Heard the number sealed, a grand design,  
One hundred forty-four thousand, perfect line.  

Tribes of Israel, twelve thousand each,  
From Judah to Benjamin, the count did reach.  

I looked again, a massive crowd, no count could tame,  
From every nation, tribe, and language, they came.  

Before the throne, in white robes they stand,  
Palm branches high, a peaceful band.  

Cried out loud, "Salvation to our God on the throne,  
And to the Lamb, His glory's shown."  

Angels, elders, creatures all around,  
Fell down and worshipped, on the ground.  

"Amen! Blessing, glory, wisdom," they proclaim,  
"Honor, power, might, forever to His name."  

An elder asked me, "Who are these and from where?"  
I said, "You know, their journey fair."  

"They came from great suffering, robes washed bright,  
In the Lamb’s blood, shining white."  

Now before the throne, serving day and night,  
Under His shelter, everything's right.  

No more hunger, thirst, or sun’s fierce heat,  
The Lamb guides them where life's waters meet.  

God wipes away their tears, renews their sight,  
In His presence, all is pure delight.
