Abijah passed, laid to rest, a chapter closed,  
Asa stepped up, and in peace, the land arose.  
Ten years of calm, no turmoil in sight,  
Asa did good, in Yahweh’s divine light.  

He tore down altars, idols, and high places,  
Broke the poles, banished unholy traces.  
"Seek Yahweh," he told Judah, "obey His command,"  
He cleared the cities, gave faith a strong stand.  

The kingdom was quiet, no battles to face,  
Yahweh gave rest, a peaceful embrace.  
"Let's build," Asa said, "walls and towers high,  
We've sought Yahweh, and his peace draws nigh."  

They built and prospered, walls standing tall,  
With Judah's three hundred thousand ready to brawl.  
Benjamite archers, shields held tight,  
All mighty men, prepared for the fight.  

Zerah the Ethiopian, a million strong,  
Chariots of war, marching along.  
In the valley of Zephathah, battle took its place,  
Asa cried to Yahweh, seeking grace:  

"Yahweh, stand with us, strong against the might,  
We rely on You, in Your name we fight.  
Don't let man prevail, show them Your hand,"  
Yahweh struck them down, couldn't withstand.  

Ethiopians fled, Asa pursued fast,  
At Gerar, they fell, couldn't outlast.  
Booty was taken, cities were struck with fear,  
Plundered them all, victory clear.  

Livestock and camels, they took in might,  
Returned to Jerusalem, after the fight.
