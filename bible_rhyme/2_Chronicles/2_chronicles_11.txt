Rehoboam rolled back to Jerusalem's gate,  
Judah and Benjamin's warriors, ready to debate.  
A hundred eighty thousand strong, swords raised to fight,  
To claim back the kingdom, to battle for the right.  

But God spoke to Shemaiah, His voice clear and true,  
"Tell Rehoboam, tell the tribes, what they must do:  
'Don't go up, don't fight your own kin today,  
Head back home, this is My way.'"  

They listened to Yahweh, His command they heeded,  
Returned from the brink, no battle was needed.  
Rehoboam settled in the city, built cities strong,  
Fortified Judah, where the brave belong.  

Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa rose high,  
Beth Zur and Soco touched the sky.  
Adullam, Gath, Mareshah, Ziph fortified,  
Adoraim, Lachish, Azekah fortified wide.  

Shields and spears in every place,  
Judah and Benjamin stood with grace.  
The priests and Levites gathered 'round,  
Left their lands, Jerusalem bound.  

Jeroboam's false priests cast them away,  
True seekers of Yahweh came to pray.  
Strengthened Judah, Rehoboam's reign,  
Three years like David's, Solomon's gain.  

Took Mahalath, David’s kin as his wife,  
With sons Jeush, Shemariah, and Zaham, new life.  
Maacah of Absalom then bore him more,  
Abijah, Attai, Ziza, Shelomith, they swore.  

Maacah loved above all, in Rehoboam's eyes,  
Eighteen wives and concubines, his family ties.  
Twenty-eight sons, daughters sixty strong,  
Abijah named chief, where he belonged.  

Dispersed his sons, in Judah they roamed,  
Food in abundance, seeking wives for home.  
Wise in his dealings, a kingdom well spread,  
Rehoboam’s legacy, a path well tread.
