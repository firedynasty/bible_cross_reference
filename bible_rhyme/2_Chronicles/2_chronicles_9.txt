Queen of Sheba heard Solomon's name,  
Came to test him, play the wisdom game.  

Caravan so grand, spices and gold,  
Precious stones in hand, stories to be told.  

Questions she had, from her heart they came,  
Solomon answered, his wisdom like a flame.  

Saw his house, the food on his table,  
Servants all around, Solomon was able.  

Witnessed his ascent to Yahweh's place,  
No spirit left in her, awed by his grace.  

She said, "In my land, your fame had spread,  
But seeing it now, those words were underfed.  

Your wisdom surpasses what I once heard,  
Your happy people, blessed by your word.  

Praise to Yahweh, who set you high,  
To rule with justice, under God's sky."  

Gifts she brought, gold, spices never seen,  
Solomon received like a king, serene.  

Servants brought treasures from distant lands,  
Algum trees, precious stones, in their hands.  

Terraces built, instruments made,  
In Judah's land, a spectacle displayed.  

Solomon gave her all she desired,  
Off she went, her heart inspired.  

Gold flowed in, more than ever before,  
Kings and traders brought wealth to his door.  

Bucklers and shields crafted from gold,  
In the Forest of Lebanon, a sight to behold.  

Ivory throne, gold overlay bright,  
Lions stood guard, a royal delight.  

Drinking vessels, gold in every cup,  
Silver was common, gold filled them up.  

Ships to Tarshish, treasures they bore,  
Gold, silver, peacocks, and more.  

Riches and wisdom, unmatched on earth,  
Kings sought his presence, a testament to his worth.  

Tributes brought, silver and gold,  
Every year a story told.  

Four thousand stalls, horses in line,  
Chariots, horsemen, all design.  

Ruled over kings, his domain was vast,  
Silver like stones, cedar trees amassed.  

Horses from Egypt, lands far and wide,  
Solomon's reign, a majestic ride.  

Forty years, Jerusalem's crown,  
In David's city, they laid him down.  

Rehoboam his son took the throne,  
Solomon slept, his legacy known.
