Sennacherib marched, Judah in his sight,  
Hezekiah saw the threat, preparing for the fight.  
Princes and warriors, gathered for a plan,  
Stop the waters, leave the enemy in a jam.  

"Why should Assyria find water to drink?"  
Hezekiah built the walls, made the city a strong link.  
Weapons crafted, shields in store,  
Captains appointed, warriors ready for war.  

"Be strong, be bold, don’t fear his might,  
For Yahweh’s with us, in this fight.  
He's got flesh, but we’ve got the divine,  
Yahweh fights our battles, we'll be fine."  

Sennacherib sent words, mockery and scorn,  
"Trust in your God, to be left forlorn?  
Hezekiah deceives, I’ll starve you all,  
No god has stopped me, watch your kingdom fall."  

Taunts and letters, aimed to sow fear,  
But Hezekiah and Isaiah, in prayer sincere.  
Yahweh sent an angel, struck down the foe,  
Sennacherib fled, with shame in tow.  

Back in his land, in his god's own place,  
His sons rose up, ended his disgrace.  
Yahweh saved Judah, gifts flowed in,  
Hezekiah praised, from lands he'd win.  

Though he prospered, pride caused wrath to rise,  
Hezekiah humbled, in time grew wise.  
Riches and honor, in abundance shown,  
Yet Babylon’s questions left him alone.  

Hezekiah passed, honored in death,  
Manasseh his son, took his final breath.  
Acts of a king, in the books they remain,  
A life of trust, through triumph and pain.
