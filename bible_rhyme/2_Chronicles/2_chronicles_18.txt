Jehoshaphat got riches, honor piled high,  
Linked with Ahab, an alliance on the sly.  

Years later, down to Samaria he went,  
Ahab’s sheep and cattle, a feast was well-spent.  

Ahab asked, "With me to Ramoth Gilead, will you go?"  
Jehoshaphat replied, "Your people are mine, let's show."  

But Jehoshaphat asked, "Seek Yahweh’s word first,  
Before we dive into battle, before things get worse."  

Ahab gathered prophets, four hundred strong,  
"Shall we fight or hold back?" they sang the same song.  

"Go up," they said, "God will give you the win."  
But Jehoshaphat questioned, "Is there no other within?"  

Ahab sighed, "One man, Micaiah by name,  
But he speaks only evil, never fortune or fame."  

"Don’t say that," Jehoshaphat urged, "Bring him here,  
Let’s hear what he says, whether it’s bad or clear."  

Thrones were set, kings royal in robes,  
Prophets shouted their hopeful probes.  

Zedekiah made iron horns, bold and grand,  
"Push the Syrians," he declared, "with Yahweh’s own hand."  

Prophets echoed, "Gilead’s yours to claim,  
Yahweh’s deliverance is the name of the game."  

The messenger to Micaiah said, "Speak like the rest,  
Make your words good, at the king’s request."  

Micaiah responded, "Yahweh’s truth I’ll relay,  
Whatever my God says, that will I say."  

Before the king, Micaiah did stand,  
"Shall we go to Gilead, take the land?"  

He answered, "Prosper, victory's in sight,"  
But the king pressed for truth, "Speak Yahweh’s light."  

Micaiah revealed, "Like scattered sheep they’ll be,  
No shepherd in sight, let them return home free."  

Ahab to Jehoshaphat, "See, he speaks ill,  
No good from him, just words meant to chill."  

Micaiah continued, "Hear Yahweh’s decree,  
Yahweh on His throne, the host heavenly.  

Who will entice Ahab, make him fall?  
A spirit came forward, offering his call.  

'I’ll be a lying spirit in their voice,'  
Yahweh said, 'Go, you’ve made your choice.'"  

"These prophets lie, Yahweh’s spoken your fate,"  
Zedekiah struck him, "Where’s Yahweh in debate?"  

Micaiah replied, "You’ll see on that day,  
When you hide in a room, Yahweh’s spirit you’ll weigh."  

Ahab ordered, "Take him back, keep him confined,  
Bread and water of affliction, till peace I find."  

"If peace returns," Micaiah declared bold,  
"Yahweh’s not spoken, heed my words told."  

Together the kings went to Ramoth Gilead’s ground,  
Ahab in disguise, with robes Jehoshaphat was crowned.  

Syrian chariot captains targeting the king,  
Jehoshaphat cried out, God’s help did bring.  

Realizing their mistake, they turned back,  
A random arrow hit Ahab, attacked his track.  

"Driver, turn, take me away, I’m struck so deep,"  
In his chariot, he propped up, till evening sleep.  

The battle raged on, Ahab held till the end,  
With sunset’s fall, his life did suspend.
