Solomon's throne, yeah, it's firmly in place,  
God's with him, giving him that amazing grace.  

He spoke to Israel, leaders all around,  
Captains, judges, princes, where they could be found.  

To Gibeon they marched, a high place so grand,  
God's Tent of Meeting, built by Moses' own hand.  

David brought the Ark to a tent he prepared,  
In Jerusalem, with reverence, it was aired.  

Before the bronze altar, Bezalel's creation,  
Solomon sought wisdom, seeking God's elation.  

He offered a thousand burnt offerings that night,  
And God appeared, His presence, pure light.  

"Ask what you want," the Lord did say,  
Solomon replied in a humble way.  

"You loved my father, and made me king,  
Let your promise stand, like a bird on the wing.  

Grant me wisdom to lead this multitude,  
For who can judge them with gratitude?"  

God replied, "You asked wise and true,  
Not for riches or revenge, but wisdom for you.  

So wisdom and knowledge I now do grant,  
Plus riches and honor, beyond any past chant."  

Solomon returned, back to his city,  
Reigned over Israel, strong and gritty.  

Chariots and horsemen, he gathered with might,  
Silver like stones, cedars in sight.  

Horses from Egypt and Kue were bought,  
Merchants dealing, in riches they sought.  

Chariots and horses, to Hittites and Syrians sold,  
Solomon's wisdom and wealth, a story told.
