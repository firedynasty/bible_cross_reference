Solomon rose, with a plan in his mind,  
A house for Yahweh’s name and kingdom combined.  
Seventy thousand strong to carry the load,  
Eighty thousand stone cutters, down the mountain road.  

Three thousand six hundred to keep the plan tight,  
Solomon wrote to Huram, seeking his might.  
"As you helped my father with cedars so fine,  
Help me build a house, in God’s grand design.  

For Yahweh, my God, incense will rise,  
Showbread and offerings, up to the skies.  
Morning and night, Sabbaths and feasts,  
An ordinance of Israel, never to cease.  

This house is for Yahweh, majestic and grand,  
Yet who can contain Him, with heaven in hand?  
Send me a craftsman skilled in the arts,  
Gold, silver, and purple, to play their parts.  

Cedar and cypress, Lebanon’s pride,  
My servants with yours, side by side.  
Timber in abundance, for a temple so great,  
And in return, wheat, barley, wine on each plate."  

Huram replied, with words on a scroll,  
"Yahweh loves His people; He’s made you whole.  
Blessed be Yahweh, the God of the skies,  
Who gave David a son, so wise and wise.  

I send a craftsman, skilled and true,  
In every craft, he’ll work for you.  
Son of Dan’s daughter, Tyre his home,  
With skills to engrave, in wood or stone.  

Send the wheat, barley, oil, and wine,  
We’ll cut the wood, send it down the line.  
Rafts on the sea, from Lebanon to Joppa,  
Then it’s yours to carry, to Jerusalem proper."  

Solomon counted, the foreigners vast,  
One fifty-three thousand six hundred amassed.  
Seventy thousand to bear, eighty thousand to cut,  
Three thousand six hundred overseers, the work to strut.
