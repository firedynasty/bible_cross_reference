Hezekiah took the throne at twenty-five,  
In Jerusalem, he ruled, kept the faith alive.  

First year, first month, he swung Yahweh's doors wide,  
With priests and Levites, he stood by their side.  

"Listen up, Levites, sanctify your space,  
Cleanse Yahweh's house, no time to waste.  

Our fathers failed, turned their backs in shame,  
Shut the doors, let out the flame.  

Because of them, wrath came to our land,  
Now rise, be ministers, by His command."  

Levites stood up, Mahath and Joel too,  
Along with their brothers, they knew what to do.  

Sanctified themselves, followed the king's decree,  
To cleanse Yahweh's house, set His temple free.  

Priests went deep, found filth to expel,  
Levites carried it out, to the Kidron well.  

They started the month, worked eight days straight,  
By the sixteenth day, Yahweh's house was great.  

"Yahweh's house is clean," to the king they said,  
"Every vessel restored, once left for dead."  

Hezekiah rose early, gathered the crew,  
Bulls, rams, lambs, and goats, offerings made new.  

Killed the bulls, sprinkled blood on the stone,  
Rams and lambs followed, altar's call was known.  

Goats for sin, the priests laid their hands,  
Blood spilled on the altar, for all the land.  

Levites took their place, cymbals in hand,  
With harps and trumpets, by the king's command.  

Burnt offering began, Yahweh's song on high,  
Worship soared, lifted to the sky.  

When offerings were done, they bowed low,  
Hezekiah commanded praises to flow.  

"Now come near," said the king with zeal,  
"Bring sacrifices, thank offerings real."  

Seventy bulls, a hundred rams they gave,  
Two hundred lambs, for Yahweh, brave.  

Cattle, sheep, more than they could handle,  
Levites stepped up, lit the worship candle.  

Priests few, Levites showed their heart,  
Burnt offerings in abundance, they played their part.  

The service set, all in Yahweh's plan,  
Hezekiah rejoiced, for what God began.  

The people cheered, for what swiftly came,  
Hezekiah's reign, glorifying Yahweh's name.
