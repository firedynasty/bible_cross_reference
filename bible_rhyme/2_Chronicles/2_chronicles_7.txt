Solomon prayed, and fire hit the ground,  
Burnt offering consumed, God's glory all around.  

Priests couldn't enter, the house was ablaze,  
God's presence filled the place, a glorious haze.  

Israel saw the fire, they hit the floor,  
Faces down, they worshiped, "Forevermore!"  

King Solomon stepped up with his crew,  
Twenty-two thousand cattle, sheep too.  

With music and trumpets, the Levites played,  
"Praise the Lord!" with instruments made.  

The altar couldn't hold all the sacrifice heat,  
So he made the court holy, where offerings meet.  

Seven days of feasting, all gathered 'round,  
From Hamath to Egypt, joy did abound.  

Eighth day, solemn assembly, they stayed,  
Then rejoiced back home, goodness displayed.  

Solomon finished, God's house complete,  
His heart fulfilled with every beat.  

God came at night, spoke clear and bright,  
"I've chosen this place, it's precious in my sight."  

"If drought hits, or locusts swarm your land,  
Turn to me, humble, and take your stand."  

"Hear you I will, forgive, and heal,  
My eyes and ears open, your prayers I feel."  

"Walk like David, keep my ways true,  
And I'll keep your throne, always for you."  

"But turn away, and worship wrong,  
I'll uproot you and make your end long."  

"High house will fall, a shocking sight,  
People will ask, 'Why this plight?'"  

"Answer will come, they left God's hand,  
Chased other gods, lost their land."
