In Jerusalem, the youngest son took the throne,  
Ahaziah was king; the oldest were gone, overthrown.  
Just forty-two when his reign began,  
One year in power, according to the plan.  

His mom Athaliah, daughter of Omri's line,  
Counseled him in ways less than divine.  
He walked in Ahab's path, against God's sight,  
His father's death led him to wrong, not right.  

Followed his counsel, went to battle with Joram,  
Against Hazael, king of Syria, they fought hard and long.  
Joram was wounded, needed healing in Jezreel,  
While Azariah came to visit, to see how he'd heal.  

God planned Ahaziah's fall, a destined end,  
At Joram's side, with Jehu he did contend.  
Jehu, God's anointed, aimed to cut Ahab's tree,  
Judah's princes and Ahaziah's kin met their decree.  

Ahaziah hid in Samaria, but Jehu found his place,  
Brought him out, killed him, but with respect to save face.  
“Son of Jehoshaphat, who sought God’s grace.”  
The kingdom slipped from his family's embrace.  

Athaliah saw her son’s fate,  
Rose in fury to eliminate the state's heir's slate.  
But Jehoshabeath took Joash, hid him away,  
With a nurse in a bedroom, keeping death at bay.  

For six years Joash was hidden in God's own space,  
While Athaliah ruled, unaware of his place.  
The tale ends with a boy protected from the foe,  
In the house of God, where true safety does grow.
