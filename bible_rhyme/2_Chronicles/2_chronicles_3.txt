Solomon starts to build, on Mount Moriah high,  
Where Yahweh met David, the place set to the sky.  

Second month, fourth year, he kicked off his reign,  
Measured out the foundation, plans were insane.  

Sixty long, twenty wide, God's house takes shape,  
With a porch in front, gold within, no escape.  

A ceiling of cypress, gold overlay bright,  
Palm trees and chains, a glorious sight.  

He adorned with stones, precious, beauty untold,  
From Parvaim the gold, bold stories unfold.  

Gold on beams, walls, doors, cherubim engraved,  
Most holy place sparkles, treasures not saved.  

Twenty by twenty, fine gold laid thick,  
Six hundred talents, nails of fifty, slick.  

Carved cherubim, golden they soar,  
Wingspan twenty cubits, touching the floor.  

Each wing five cubits, to walls they reach,  
Fierce faces forward, sacred they teach.  

Veil of blue, purple, crimson so pure,  
Cherubim adorned, majesty sure.  

Pillars stand tall, thirty-five to the sky,  
Chains and pomegranates, a sight to apply.  

Jachin on the right, Boaz on the left side,  
Solomon's temple, where God's glory abide.
