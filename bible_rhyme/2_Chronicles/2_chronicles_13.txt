Year eighteen of Jeroboam's reign, Abijah took the throne,  
In Jerusalem, three years he reigned, not alone.  
His momma Micaiah, from Uriel's line,  
War ignited with Jeroboam, a battle divine.  

Abijah gathered four hundred thousand strong,  
Jeroboam had double, mighty men, couldn't go wrong.  
Up on Mount Zemaraim, Abijah stood tall,  
"Hear me, Jeroboam, and Israel, heed my call:  

Didn't Yahweh give David's line the kingdom to hold?  
Yet Jeroboam rebelled, his heart was bold.  
Trash gathered round him, when Rehoboam was weak,  
Now you challenge Yahweh's kingdom, your hearts you must seek.  

You flaunt golden calves, your gods falsely made,  
Chased out Yahweh's priests, traditions betrayed.  
But we serve Yahweh, we hold firm and true,  
Our priests burn offerings, both morning and dew.  

God is with us, priests sound trumpets loud,  
Don't fight Yahweh, your pride is your shroud."  
But Jeroboam had tricks, an ambush set behind,  
Judah turned and saw, fear gripped their mind.  

They cried to Yahweh, trumpets blared,  
Judah shouted, and God struck, enemies snared.  
Israel fled, defeated, God delivered the win,  
Five hundred thousand fell, Judah's strength from within.  

Judah prevailed, Yahweh was their guide,  
Abijah chased Jeroboam, took cities far and wide.  
Jeroboam never regained, struck down, he died,  
Abijah grew mighty, with family by his side.  

Fourteen wives, children galore,  
Power and might, his legacy bore.  
His story's penned by the prophet's hand,  
Abijah's reign, a tale of a land.
