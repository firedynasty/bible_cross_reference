Jehoshaphat stepped up, took the throne with might,  
Fortified his cities, prepped them for a fight.  
God was with him, he walked David's trail,  
Shunned the Baals, he wouldn't fail.  

Sought his father's God, commandments on track,  
Didn't follow Israel, no turning back.  
Yahweh gave him strength, his kingdom stood tall,  
Judah brought tribute, riches flowed like a waterfall.  

His heart soared high, in Yahweh's name,  
Tore down high places, ended false fame.  
In the third year, sent princes to teach,  
With Levites, priests, knowledge they'd preach.  

Through Judah they roamed, law book in hand,  
Taught the people, Yahweh's command.  
Fear of God fell on lands around,  
No war with Jehoshaphat, peace profound.  

Philistines brought gifts, silver paid the dues,  
Arabians with flocks, rams and goats in queues.  
Jehoshaphat grew great, built up his store,  
Fortresses, cities, men of war galore.  

Counted his warriors, mighty men of pride,  
Adnah with three hundred thousand by his side.  
Jehohanan next, two hundred eighty strong,  
Amasiah followed, in Yahweh he belonged.  

From Benjamin came Eliada, bow and shield in hand,  
Two hundred thousand ready, a fierce war band.  
Jehozabad next, one hundred eighty ready for strife,  
Waiting on the king, prepared for life.  

In fortified cities, his forces spread wide,  
Jehoshaphat's rule, righteous and dignified.  
A kingdom established, by God led in peace,  
In Judah's borders, prosperity wouldn't cease.
