Amaziah took the throne at twenty-five,  
In Jerusalem, he ruled, kept the kingdom alive.  

He did what was right, but his heart had a flaw,  
Yet he followed the path laid down by the law.  

Avenging his father, he punished the crew,  
But spared all the children as the law said to do.  

He gathered Judah, soldiers handpicked,  
Three hundred thousand with spears and shields, equipped.  

Hired warriors from Israel, a hundred thousand strong,  
Paid them silver, but was it right or wrong?  

A man of God came, spoke truth to the king,  
"Don't take Israel's army, they won't help you win.  

God's got the power to help or take down,  
Trust in Him, and you’ll wear the crown."  

Amaziah worried, "What about the silver I spent?"  
But God’s prophet said, "Much more can be sent."  

So he sent Israel back, they left with a scowl,  
Their anger burned fierce, like a dog on the prowl.  

Amaziah led Judah to the Valley of Salt,  
Struck ten thousand Seir, halted their assault.  

Ten thousand alive were carried to rock,  
Thrown down, shattered, their bones did knock.  

But the army sent home wasn't done,  
They raided Judah, plundered, won.  

Amaziah returned with spoils of war,  
But bowed to false gods, Yahweh abhorred.  

Yahweh sent a prophet with a burning word,  
"Why worship gods that didn't save their herd?"  

Amaziah dismissed him, "Stop or be struck!"  
Prophet said, "God’s judgment, you'll self-destruct."  

He challenged Joash, king of Israel's might,  
"Face me now, let's have a fight."  

Joash replied, "You're a thistle, I'm the cedar,  
Stay home or fall, don't be so eager."  

But Amaziah wouldn't heed,  
Pride drove him to mislead.  

They met at Beth Shemesh, face to face,  
Judah defeated, lost the race.  

Joash captured him, tore down the wall,  
Took treasure, made Jerusalem small.  

Amaziah lived on, fifteen more years,  
The record of his reign, written through tears.  

Once he turned from God, a plot was laid,  
In Lachish he died, his escape plan frayed.  

Brought back on horses, laid him to rest,  
With his fathers, his story confessed.
