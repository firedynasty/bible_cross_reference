Josiah set the scene, Passover in full swing,  
Fourteenth day of the month, praises they would sing.  

He placed the priests in their rightful place,  
Encouraged them to serve with steadfast grace.  

To the Levites he spoke, in words so clear,  
"Put the ark in Solomon's house, have no fear.  

No longer a burden on your shoulders to bear,  
Now serve Yahweh, His word declare."  

"Prepare by your houses, your divisions stand tall,  
As David and Solomon wrote, heed their call.  

Stand in the holy place, each tribe in line,  
For the Levites to serve, everything's fine."  

"Kill the Passover, sanctify, prepare,  
For Yahweh's word through Moses we share."  

Josiah gave lambs, young goats, thirty thousand strong,  
Three thousand bulls, to the people they belong.  

Princes offered freely, to priests, Levites too,  
With livestock and cattle, their offerings they grew.  

With Conaniah and brothers, Levites were fed,  
Five thousand small livestock, five hundred head.  

The service was ready, priests took their spot,  
And Levites divided in lines that never forgot.  

They killed the Passover, sprinkled blood with care,  
Levites skinned, prepared, and offerings they’d share.  

Roasted the Passover, boiled pots of holy meat,  
Quickly served to the people, everything complete.  

Priests worked, offerings burned, into the night,  
Levites prepared more, keeping it right.  

Singers of Asaph sang in their place,  
Gatekeepers stood firm, never left their space.  

All for Yahweh was done in a day,  
Offering burnt offerings, in their own way.  

Children of Israel kept the feast of seven days,  
None like this Passover, Josiah's ways.  

No king kept Passover with such a grand show,  
In the eighteenth year, Josiah let it flow.  

Then Egypt’s king Neco rose for a fight,  
Josiah went out, ready to face the night.  

Neco sent word, "This fight’s not with you,  
God’s commanded me, heed and be true."  

Josiah ignored, went in disguise,  
In Megiddo’s valley, arrows did fly.  

"Take me away," the wounded king cried,  
In his chariot, to Jerusalem he died.  

Mourned by all Judah, they lamented in song,  
Jeremiah wept, their lamentations were long.  

Josiah's deeds, from start to end,  
Written in books, remembered by friend.
