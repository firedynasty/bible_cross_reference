Jehoshaphat laid down with his forebears in David's town,  
Jehoram took the throne, wore the crown, no frown.  

Brothers on the side, sons of Jehoshaphat's line,  
Azariah, Jehiel, Zechariah, all in their prime.  

Their father gave them gifts, silver and gold,  
Fortified cities in Judah, stories retold.  

But Jehoram got the kingdom, firstborn's right claim,  
When he rose to power, he played a deadly game.  

Strengthened his throne, but his heart was stone cold,  
Killed his brothers, the princes, for power to hold.  

Jehoram at thirty-two, began his eight-year reign,  
Walked like Ahab's house, brought evil and pain.  

Married Ahab’s daughter, led astray in God's sight,  
But Yahweh spared David's house, a promise of light.  

Edom broke free, declared a king of their own,  
Jehoram struck by night, but his power wasn’t shown.  

Edom and Libnah both turned away,  
For Jehoram had left Yahweh astray.  

He built high places, made Judah fall,  
A letter from Elijah, God’s warning call:  

"Yahweh says, ‘You walked the wrong track,  
Not like your fathers, now Yahweh strikes back."  

A plague on your people, a sickness for you,  
Day by day, your bowels will fall through.  

Philistines and Arabians, stirred up to invade,  
Took his sons, his wives, all Jehoram had made.  

Yahweh struck Jehoram with pain so severe,  
Two years later, his death was near.  

His bowels fell out, his sickness was grave,  
No burning for him, no kingly grave.  

Thirty-two when he started, eight years he stayed,  
Departed without love, in a tomb unlaid.
