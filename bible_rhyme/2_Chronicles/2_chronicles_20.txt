Enemies gathered, ready to fight,  
Jehoshaphat heard, and fear took flight.  

"From beyond the sea, they come with might,  
In Hazazon Tamar, they plan their strike."  

Alarmed, he sought Yahweh, called for fast,  
Judah united, prayers amassed.  

In the sacred court, Jehoshaphat stood,  
“Yahweh in heaven, above all that’s good.  

Didn't You clear this land for us, Your own?  
A home for Abraham's children, Your throne.  

When evil strikes, we cry and You save,  
Now they come to steal what You gave.  

Judge them, O God, they're vast and strong,  
We look to You, and we belong."  

Judah stood still, families gathered tight,  
Yahweh's Spirit spoke, brought them light.  

“Fear not this multitude, hear My plan,  
The battle's Mine, not for man's hand.  

Tomorrow, set out, stand and see,  
Yahweh's salvation, victory free."  

Jehoshaphat bowed, all Judah praised,  
Levites sang loud, voices raised.  

Early next day, to Tekoa they marched,  
Jehoshaphat spoke, faith firmly parched.  

"Believe in Yahweh, prophets proclaim,  
Establish your steps, prosper with His name."  

Singers led with songs of grace,  
Praise rang out, filled the space.  

As music soared, chaos ensued,  
Ambushers struck, foes were subdued.  

Ammon and Moab turned on Seir,  
Destruction raged, none left to fear.  

Judah arrived, the enemy lay still,  
Riches abandoned, treasures to fill.  

For three days they gathered, so profound,  
In Beracah Valley, blessings resound.  

Back to Jerusalem, joy in their hearts,  
Yahweh's victory, a new start.  

Fear spread through lands, God's name renowned,  
Jehoshaphat reigned, peace all around.  

He walked the way his father had paved,  
But high places remained, hearts unbraved.  

His deeds recorded, written in lore,  
But joining with wicked, brought destruction's door.  

Ships wrecked in the sea, plans fell apart,  
"Because of this alliance," Yahweh's word did impart.
