Joash took the throne at just age seven,  
Forty years in Jerusalem, looking to heaven.  
His mama Zibiah, from Beersheba's lane,  
Joash did right, with Jehoiada's reign.  

Jehoiada found him wives, two for his kin,  
With sons and daughters, his legacy did begin.  
Joash set his mind, God's house to restore,  
Called priests and Levites, for funds to explore.  

"Go to Judah's towns, collect the dues,  
Repair God's house, no time to lose."  
But the Levites delayed, the king wasn't pleased,  
He asked Jehoiada, "Why was the task ceased?"  

Athaliah's lot had done wicked deeds,  
Breaking God's house, serving their needs.  
The king ordered a chest outside the gate,  
Proclaimed through the land, "Bring Moses' rate."  

Princes and people, joy in their heart,  
Filled up the chest, doing their part.  
Day by day, coin overflowed,  
Scribes and priests, the treasure bestowed.  

Masons and carpenters, hired for the task,  
Iron and bronze, they rebuilt fast.  
God's house restored, standing tall and bright,  
A testament of faith, a glorious sight.  

When it was finished, with funds still to spare,  
Vessels of gold and silver, they'd prepare.  
Offerings burned, God's praise and might,  
All through Jehoiada's days, a sacred light.  

Jehoiada grew old, full of days he'd see,  
At one hundred thirty, he passed peacefully.  
Buried with kings, for the good he had done,  
His deeds for God, second to none.  

But after his death, the story took a turn,  
Princes swayed Joash, God's house they did spurn.  
Asherah poles and idols they revered,  
Wrath fell on Judah, for they didn't hear.  

Prophets came to call them back to faith,  
Yet they ignored, continuing their wraith.  
Zechariah stood, the Spirit in his say,  
"Why forsake God? He'll turn you away."  

But they stoned him, on the king's decree,  
Joash forgot Jehoiada's loyalty.  
"May Yahweh see, and repay your way,"  
Zechariah's words lingered that day.  

Syrians arrived, with a tiny band,  
Judah's great army fell, by God's hand.  
Judgment on Joash, forsaking the Lord,  
His own servants struck, by the sword.  

In his sickbed, Joash met his end,  
Buried in David's town, not with royal friends.  
Zabad and Jehozabad sealed his fate,  
Amaziah his son, the throne did await.
