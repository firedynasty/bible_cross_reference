In Judah, young Uzziah, just sixteen,  
Was crowned as king, stepping on the scene.  
He rebuilt Eloth, brought it back strong,  
After his father's reign, he came along.  

For fifty-two years, he held the throne,  
With Jechiliah from Jerusalem, not alone.  
Did right by God, like his father taught,  
With Zechariah's guidance, wisdom he sought.  

He waged war on Philistines, walls came down,  
Gath, Jabneh, Ashdod, he took that crown.  
Cities he built, lands he claimed,  
With God's help, his strength was famed.  

Ammonites paid tribute, his name spread wide,  
From Judah to Egypt, power amplified.  
Towers in Jerusalem, strong and high,  
With cisterns and farms reaching to the sky.  

His army vast, counted and arrayed,  
Three hundred thousand strong, ready and made.  
Weapons prepared, devices on the walls,  
His fame soared high, as strength recalls.  

But pride came creeping when his power grew,  
Into the temple, incense he drew.  
Azariah and priests said, "No, you can't stay,  
It's for Aaron's sons to burn and pray."  

Angry, he stood with censer in hand,  
Leprosy struck by God's command.  
Priests pushed him out, as his fate was sealed,  
A separate house, his end revealed.  

Uzziah lived as a leper, cut off and alone,  
Jotham, his son, took on the throne.  
Isaiah wrote of his deeds, first and last,  
He was buried with kings, but his leprosy cast.  

Though he was great, his fall was deep,  
In the field of kings, he now sleeps.
