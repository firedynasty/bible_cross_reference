Josiah, just eight, took the throne with might,  
Reigned thirty-one years, doing what was right.  
Walked David's path, didn't stray left or right,  
In his eighth year, sought God with all his might.  

At twelve, he purged altars, idols, and poles,  
Smashed them to dust, scattered on graves, took souls.  
Burned priests' bones, cleansed Judah through and through,  
Cities felt the change, as Josiah's vision grew.  

In year eighteen, fixing God's house was the plan,  
Sent Shaphan and crew, to repair what they can.  
Gathered funds from Judah and Israel's hands,  
Paid workers and builders to restore God's lands.  

Faithful men worked, Levites oversaw,  
Musicians, scribes, and guards, all in awe.  
Hilkiah found the law, Moses had penned,  
Passed to Shaphan, to the king it would send.  

Shaphan read aloud, king tore his robe in grief,  
"Go seek Yahweh," he said, "find relief.  
Our fathers strayed, God's wrath is near,  
We must act now, make intentions clear."  

To Huldah the prophetess, they ran quick,  
"Yahweh says, curses will stick.  
Forsaken, provoked, his anger won't dim,  
Wrath on this place, a fate so grim."  

"But Josiah, tender heart, you will find peace,  
Gathered to your fathers, your days will cease.  
Your eyes spared from the evil I'll bring,  
Words returned to the king, a hopeful ring."  

King gathered elders, all of Judah's lot,  
At Yahweh's house, the covenant he sought.  
Read to the people, great and small,  
Renewed their pledge to God's law, stood tall.  

Cleared abominations, served Yahweh alone,  
In Josiah's days, God's love was known.  
Never departed from the path he laid,  
All his days, Yahweh's word was obeyed.
