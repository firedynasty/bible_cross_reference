Solomon spoke with a powerful tone,  
"Yahweh dwells where the shadows are grown.  
But I've built a house for You to stay,  
A place for Your presence every day."

He turned to bless Israel's crowd,  
His voice was clear, his words were loud.  
"Bless Yahweh, the God of our land,  
Fulfilling His promise by His own hand."

He chose no city, no land before,  
Until He called Jerusalem, His name to store.  
David was chosen, his heart set on fire,  
To build for Yahweh, his deepest desire.

But Yahweh said, "David, your heart is true,  
But not you, your son will build it anew."  
Now Solomon stands, he's on the throne,  
Building Yahweh's house, stone by stone.

The Ark is placed, covenant kept tight,  
In this house of worship, shining bright.  
Solomon knelt, hands spread wide,  
Prayed to Yahweh, none beside.

"Yahweh, Israel's God," He cried,  
"In heaven and earth, none can hide.  
You've kept your word to my father, King,  
Fulfill Your promise, let blessings spring."

"Will God dwell in houses man-made?  
Heaven can't hold You, nor our humble shade.  
Yet hear our cries, our daily plea,  
Be with us, God, eternally."

"If we sin, or face our foe,  
Return to You, blessings flow.  
If skies are dry, famine strikes,  
Turn to You, hearts ignite."

"Foreigners too, from lands afar,  
Will pray to this house, under your star.  
Hear their call, show them your might,  
Let them know your name, shining bright."

"When battles rage, hear our prayer,  
Guide us, Yahweh, everywhere.  
If captive taken, hearts contrite,  
Bring them back, into Your light."

He closed with words, so deep and true,  
"Yahweh, rise, strength anew.  
Let priests be clothed, saints rejoice,  
Hear our prayers, in Your voice."

Remember David, servant dear,  
Yahweh, in Your love, keep him near.  
Solomon's prayer reached the skies,  
And blessings rained from on high.
