In Asa's reign, year thirty-six, Baasha came quick,  
Built Ramah up strong, tried to seal the trick.  

Asa pulled treasures, silver and gold,  
Sent to Ben Hadad, with a story told.  

"Make a treaty, like our fathers once knew,  
I've sent you riches, now do what you do.  

Break with Baasha, make him retreat,  
Set my people free, let Judah feel peace."  

Ben Hadad heard, sent captains with might,  
They struck down cities, put Baasha to flight.  

Baasha stopped building, his plans fell apart,  
Asa took Judah, gave them a new start.  

With Ramah's stones and timber so fine,  
He built Geba and Mizpah, made them his line.  

Then came Hanani, seer of the Lord,  
Spoke to Asa, delivered God's word:  

"Trusted a king, not Yahweh's own hand,  
Now Syria's army escapes from your land.  

Remember the Ethiopians, their chariots and might?  
Yahweh delivered when your heart was right.  

Now wars will follow, cause you've been a fool,  
Yahweh's strength seeks hearts that are true."  

Asa in anger, put the seer away,  
Oppressed his people, his heart led astray.  

His tale’s recorded, in the kings' great book,  
The lessons of Asa, if you take a look.  

In year thirty-nine, his feet fell ill,  
Ignored Yahweh’s power, sought physicians’ skill.  

Forty-one years and Asa was gone,  
In his own tomb, with a great fire shone.  

Laid with sweet odors, spices to burn,  
Judah's king, with lessons to learn.
