Solomon, the work's complete, the temple's done,  
He brought the treasures in, one by one.  

Gold, silver, vessels, all that David saved,  
Placed in God's house, where they'd be engraved.  

Called the elders, tribes, the leaders to the scene,  
To bring up Yahweh's ark, where it had been.  

Men of Israel gathered, the seventh month feast,  
Elders arrived, Levites with the ark in the least.  

Carried the ark, the Tent, holy vessels they did,  
Priests with them, nothing hidden, nothing hid.  

Before the ark, Solomon and Israel stood,  
Sacrifices uncountable, as best as they could.  

The ark placed in the holy, under cherubim's wings,  
Wings spread wide, covering holy things.  

Poles so long, ends seen in the sacred space,  
But not outside, still in their place.  

Inside the ark, the tablets alone,  
Moses' covenant, etched in stone.  

Priests came out, sanctified, divisions gone,  
Levites singing, with music drawn.  

Singers and priests, in fine linen dressed,  
Trumpets and voices, praising with zest.  

"For He is good, His love forever," they sang out loud,  
The house filled with a cloud, Yahweh's presence proud.  

So thick the cloud, priests couldn't stand,  
Yahweh's glory filled the land.
