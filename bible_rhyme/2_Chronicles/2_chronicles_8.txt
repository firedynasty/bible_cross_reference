After twenty long years, Solomon built with might,  
Yahweh's house and his palace shining bright.  

He took the cities Huram gave, made them homes,  
For Israel's children, under the sky's domes.  

To Hamath Zobah Solomon went, and he won,  
He built Tadmor in the wild under the sun.  

Beth Horon, both high and low, he fortified strong,  
Walls, gates, and bars, where they belong.  

Baalath and storage cities, chariots' pride,  
In Jerusalem, Lebanon, Solomon didn’t hide.  

People left from Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites in sway,  
Became forced labor, working day by day.  

But Israel's sons were warriors bold,  
Captains and rulers, their stories told.  

Two hundred fifty officers in command,  
Chiefs of Solomon's mighty land.  

Pharaoh's daughter brought to her new home,  
"Not in David's house," Solomon said, alone.  

Burnt offerings to Yahweh, flames reaching high,  
Day by day, offerings never dry.  

On Sabbaths, new moons, feasts in line,  
Unleavened bread, weeks, booths divine.  

He set priests and Levites, each in their role,  
Doorkeepers stationed, guarding the whole.  

From David's commands they did not stray,  
Solomon's work finished, Yahweh's house to stay.  

To Ezion Geber and Eloth he traveled wide,  
Huram sent ships, with wisdom as their guide.  

Four hundred fifty talents of gold in hand,  
To Solomon's kingdom, wealth and riches stand.
