They took Jehoahaz, Josiah's own kin,  
Made him king in Jerusalem, let the reign begin.  
But just three months, then Egypt came,  
Removed him, fined them, changed the game.  

Eliakim, his brother, took the throne,  
Named Jehoiakim, but not his own.  
He reigned eleven years, did what was wrong,  
In God's sight, where he didn't belong.  

Nebuchadnezzar came, put him in chains,  
Took him to Babylon with Yahweh's remains.  
The vessels of God’s house, they were taken too,  
Placed in Babylon's temple, where no light shone through.  

Jehoiakim's story, written in the book,  
Jehoiachin, his son, a new king took.  
Eight years old, he reigned for a short while,  
Did evil in God's sight, a wayward style.  

Nebuchadnezzar returned, took him away,  
With treasures from God’s house, no time to sway.  
Zedekiah, his brother, took the crown,  
Reigned eleven years, but brought God frown.  

Rebelled against Nebuchadnezzar, broke his vow,  
Hardened his heart, wouldn’t bow.  
The priests and people polluted God's space,  
Mocked his messengers, turned from grace.  

God's wrath rose, no remedy found,  
The king of Chaldeans brought them down.  
He killed the young, in the house so grand,  
Compassion lost, they fell to his hand.  

God’s house burned, Jerusalem's wall fell,  
Palaces aflame, a story to tell.  
Those who escaped, taken to serve,  
In Babylon, they got what they deserve.  

Seventy years, the land lay still,  
Sabbaths kept, by God’s will.  
Cyrus of Persia, his heart stirred right,  
Proclaimed aloud, put his words to light:  

“Yahweh, the God of heaven, gave me the lands,  
To build him a house, with my own hands.  
Whoever is among you, rise and go,  
To Jerusalem, let your faith show.”
