Hezekiah sent out the call to the land,  
To Judah and Israel, with letters in hand.  
“Come to Yahweh's house and keep the feast,  
Join us in Jerusalem, to say the least.”  

Counsel was taken, the king had a plan,  
Passover in the second month for every clan.  
Priests not ready, people not there,  
But the king and assembly thought it fair.  

From Beersheba to Dan, the word went wide,  
"Come to Yahweh, do not hide.  
Too long we've strayed from the ancient way,  
Now in great numbers, come and pray."  

The couriers traveled with their decree,  
"Turn to Yahweh, and be free."  
"Don't be like your fathers, lost and stiff,  
Yield to Yahweh, let your spirits lift."

"Turn back to Yahweh, He's gracious and kind,  
Return to His mercy, peace you will find."  
Though some mocked and laughed with disdain,  
From Asher and Manasseh, some came again.  

God's hand moved Judah, one heart, one mind,  
To follow the king's word, no one left behind.  
A great assembly for the unleavened bread,  
All altars cleared, Kidron swallowed their dread.  

Passover was killed on the fourteenth day,  
Levites and priests in their ordered array.  
Though many unclean, Levites took their part,  
Hezekiah's prayer showed his heart.  

"May Yahweh pardon those who seek His face,  
Even if unclean, grant them grace."  
Yahweh heard Hezekiah, healing ensued,  
Feast of gladness, hearts renewed.  

Seven days of singing and praise,  
Levites and priests in joyful phase.  
Peace offerings made, confession done,  
Seven more days, the feast went on.  

Hezekiah gave bulls and sheep,  
The people’s joy was true and deep.  
In Judah and Israel, voices raised,  
In Jerusalem, Yahweh was praised.  

Since Solomon's days, no joy like this,  
Levitical priests blessed with heavenly bliss.  
Their prayers reached up to the throne,  
In God's holy place, they were known.
