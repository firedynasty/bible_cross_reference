Jehoshaphat returned home, peace in his stride,  
Came to Jerusalem, where he'd abide.  

Jehu the seer came forth to speak,  
"Helping the wicked, why do you seek?  

Wrath from Yahweh's on your trail,  
But good you did, you will prevail.  

You tore down idols, set your heart right,  
Now seek God and walk in His light."  

In Jerusalem, Jehoshaphat stayed,  
From Beersheba to Ephraim, he paved the way.  

Bringing people back to the Lord,  
To the God their fathers adored.  

Judges he placed in cities strong,  
"Judge not for man, but for Yahweh all along.  

Feel His fear, let it be clear,  
For Yahweh shows no favor here.  

No bribes, no tricks, just pure decree,  
In His name, deliver justly."  

In Jerusalem, Levites he set,  
To judge and solve disputes that met.  

"Do this in fear, with heart that's true,  
Any conflict, let righteousness ensue.  

Warn them well, for Yahweh's sake,  
Avoid His wrath, the right path take.  

Amariah's chief, in Yahweh's sphere,  
Zebadiah leads Judah's affairs here.  

Levites assist, be strong and good,  
May Yahweh's presence be understood."
