All Israel gathered, the scene was set,  
They broke down the pillars, no idol was left.  
Asherah poles and high places they tore,  
In Judah, Benjamin, Ephraim, and more.  

Hezekiah stood tall, made the priests their divide,  
For offerings and praise, in Yahweh's courts they'd abide.  
The king's portion given, burnt offerings laid,  
Morning and evening, on Sabbaths displayed.  

Jerusalem's folks were commanded to share,  
Gifts for the priests, so Yahweh's law they'd bear.  
With first fruits in plenty, the people complied,  
Grain, wine, and oil, their fields magnified.  

Cattle and sheep, tithes were piled high,  
In the third month they started, by the seventh they'd fly.  
Hezekiah saw the heaps, blessed Yahweh's name,  
Asked the priests about the abundance that came.  

Azariah spoke, “Yahweh's blessings overflow,  
We’ve more than enough, from His hand it does show.”  
Rooms in Yahweh’s house, Hezekiah had made,  
Offerings and tithes, in faith were laid.  

Conaniah led, with Shimei his kin,  
Overseers named, keeping watch within.  
Kore at the gate, handled the free will,  
Eden and friends, their roles did fulfill.  

For those in the house, from age three and above,  
Priests and Levites served, with duty and love.  
With all generations, they sanctified their way,  
With trust in their office, in holiness they'd stay.  

Sons of Aaron, in pasture lands wide,  
Gave portions to priests, in every divide.  
Hezekiah's deeds, righteous before Yahweh,  
With heart full of zeal, he prospered each day.
