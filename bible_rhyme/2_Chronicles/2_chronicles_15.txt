The Spirit of God hit Azariah bright,  
He went to Asa, spoke truth, shed light.  
"Hear me, Asa, Judah, Benjamin too,  
Yahweh's with you, as long as you're true.  

Seek Him, find Him, but leave and He's gone,  
Once Israel was lost, the true God withdrawn.  
Without law and priest, they wandered in pain,  
But in their distress, they called His name again.  

No peace in the land, nation in strife,  
Troubles spread wide, danger was rife.  
Be strong, don't slack, keep hands on the task,  
Your work shall be blessed, that's all I ask."  

Asa heard these words, courage he gained,  
Cleared Judah and Benji, where idols reigned.  
He renewed God's altar, the sacred site,  
Gathered the tribes, to set things right.  

From Ephraim, Manasseh, folks came in droves,  
Jerusalem gathered, as loyalty rose.  
Sacrificed cattle and sheep, a grand display,  
In the fifteenth year, of Asa's sway.  

Into covenant they went, heart and soul in the game,  
Seek Yahweh or face death, equal was the claim.  
Swore aloud, trumpets, shouting in the air,  
All Judah rejoiced, for God answered prayer.  

Asa dethroned his mom, for her idol's blight,  
Cut, ground, and burned it, by Kidron's light.  
Israel's high places yet stood, true,  
But Asa's heart was strong, his days through.  

Brought silver and gold, to God's holy site,  
Peace reigned 'til his thirty-fifth year, no fight.  
They sought with desire, with all they possessed,  
And Yahweh gave them peace, in His rest.
