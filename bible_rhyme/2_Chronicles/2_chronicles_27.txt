Jotham was young, just twenty-five when he took the throne,  
Sixteen years he reigned, Jerusalem was his zone.  

His mom Jerushah, Zadok's daughter by name,  
Kept him steady on the path of righteous fame.  

He did what's right in Yahweh's eyes, followed Uzziah's stride,  
But stayed out the temple; the people's corruption he couldn't hide.  

Built the upper gate, Yahweh's house got some love,  
Fortified the wall, Ophel's heights soared above.  

In Judah's hills, cities rose and spread,  
Fortresses and towers in the forests he led.  

Fought Ammon's king, came out on top,  
Got silver, wheat, barley, their tribute wouldn't stop.  

Mighty Jotham, for his ways were just,  
Before Yahweh, his faith was a must.  

His wars, his ways, all written in the scroll,  
King of Israel and Judah, his story they told.  

Reigned from twenty-five, sixteen years strong,  
Laid to rest in David's city, where he belongs.  

Ahaz, his son, took the king's place,  
Jotham's legacy, a steady pace.
