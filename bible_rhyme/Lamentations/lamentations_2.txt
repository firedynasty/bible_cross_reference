The Lord's anger's clouding Zion's sky,  
Beauty of Israel cast down from high.  

Dwellings of Jacob swallowed up, no pity,  
Judah's strongholds thrown down, they're gritty.  

Horns of Israel cut off in His rage,  
Fire consumes Jacob, sets the stage.  

Bow bent like a foe, hand as adversary,  
Zion's tents face wrath, it's scary.  

Enemy-like, the Lord swallowed Israel whole,  
Palaces gone, grief takes its toll.  

Like a garden, His tabernacle's torn,  
Assembly place destroyed, left forlorn.  

Altars cast off, His sanctuary despised,  
Enemies make noise where solemn assembly lies.  

Zion's walls doomed by Yahweh's plan,  
Ramparts weep as He stretches His hand.  

Gates sunk low, kings and princes stray,  
Prophets find no vision, no way.  

Elders sit silent, dust on their crown,  
Virgins hang heads, spirits down.  

Eyes weep tears, hearts in trouble deep,  
Children faint, in the streets they weep.  

"Where's the grain and wine?" they cry,  
Mothers hold them as they die.  

What can I say, daughter of Jerusalem?  
Your breach is vast, who can heal them?  

False visions from your prophets' sight,  
Banishment, no iniquity in light.  

Passersby clap in scorn and hiss,  
"Is this the city's joy and bliss?"  

Enemies mock, mouths open wide,  
"Swallowed her up, with pride."  

Yahweh's plan fulfilled, ancient word,  
Enemies rejoice, His voice heard.  

Heart cries out, Zion's wall in tears,  
Day and night, through all fears.  

Arise and cry in night’s quiet,  
Lift hands for children, soul’s diet.  

"Look, Yahweh, see what You've done!  
Mothers eat children, priests undone."  

Youth and old lie slain in streets,  
Virgins and young men meet defeats.  

Days of anger, terrors called in,  
No escape, consumed by sin.
