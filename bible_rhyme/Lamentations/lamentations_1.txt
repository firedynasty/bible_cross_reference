The city sits alone, once teeming with people wide,  
Now like a widow, her grandeur, she can't hide.  
Once a princess, now a slave, she weeps at night,  
With tears on her cheeks, no lover in sight.  

Judah in chains, driven by pain, no place to find rest,  
Lost among nations, pursued, distressed.  
Zion's roads mourn, no assembly to see,  
Desolate gates, priests sighing, bitter she be.  

Adversaries rise; enemies take charge,  
For Yahweh's wrath has come, sins at large.  
Children captive, majesty gone, like deer they flee,  
Princes powerless, no pasture to see.  

Jerusalem remembers old days of joy,  
Mocked now by foes, her honor destroyed.  
Sinned grievously, now unclean and despised,  
Nakedness revealed, with backward eyes.  

"See, Yahweh, my suffering, my vast affliction,"  
The enemy's hand on all, a bitter conviction.  
Her people sigh, trade treasures for bread,  
"Look, Yahweh," she cries, "I'm despised and dead."  

"To those passing by, see my sorrow's weight,  
Afflicted by Yahweh's fierce, burning hate.  
Fire in my bones, a net for my feet,  
Turned back and faint, no strength to meet."  

"The yoke tied tight, my transgressions bound,  
My strength fails, I'm delivered down.  
Mighty men crushed, Judah pressed like wine,  
For these tears, no comfort is mine."  

Zion spreads hands, no comfort found,  
Jacob's foes gather, Jerusalem's unclean ground.  
"Yahweh is righteous, I've rebelled, see my pain,  
Virgins and young men, captive again."  

"Called for lovers, deceived, left alone,  
Priests and elders, their spirits have flown.  
Yahweh, I'm distressed, my heart churns with dread,  
Sword outside, death at home, my spirit dead."  

Enemies hear, they're glad at my plight,  
But Yahweh, your day will make all right.  
Bring their wickedness, as you've done to me,  
Sighs many, heart faint, let justice be.
