Yahweh, see what's come our way,  
Strangers walk where we used to stay.  

Our homes gone, our lands no more,  
We’re orphans, mothers feel the widow's score.  

Pay for water, wood sold at a cost,  
Pursued by foes, all hope seems lost.  

Sent to Egypt and Assyria's land,  
Just to fill our mouths, we gave our hand.  

Fathers sinned, they're gone, we're left,  
Bearing burdens, feeling bereft.  

Servants rule, we're trapped, you see,  
No one's here to set us free.  

Bread comes with life on the line,  
Sword in the wild, danger all the time.  

Our skin's dark from famine's heat,  
Zion's women face defeat.  

Judah's virgins met with shame,  
Princes hanged, no honor to claim.  

Young men grind, children strain,  
Elders' wisdom, now in vain.  

Joy's gone, dances turned to tears,  
Our crown has fallen through these years.  

Hearts are faint, eyes grow dim,  
Zion desolate, foxes on a whim.  

Yahweh, forever you remain,  
On an eternal throne, you reign.  

"Why forget us, leave us long?  
Turn us back where we belong."  

Bring us back, renew our days,  
Yet your anger still delays.
