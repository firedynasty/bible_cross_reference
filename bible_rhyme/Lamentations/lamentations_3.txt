I've seen affliction, felt the wrath like a rod,  
Led into darkness where there ain't no God.  

Turned his hand against me, day and night,  
Made my skin old, broke my bones tight.  

Surrounded by bitterness, trapped in a wall,  
Chains so heavy, no way to call.  

My prayers blocked, paths turned crooked,  
A bear lying in wait, my soul shook it.  

He pulled me apart, left me desolate,  
Bent his bow, aimed with deliberate fate.  

Arrows hit deep, filled with bitter strife,  
Became a joke to my people, song of my life.  

Fed on wormwood, teeth crushed with gravel,  
Covered in ashes, my peace unraveled.  

"My strength's gone," I cry in despair,  
"Expectation lost, Yahweh, are you there?"  

Still I recall hope, buried in my soul,  
Yahweh's kindness keeps me whole.  

New every morning, faithfulness grand,  
"My portion," says my soul, "I'll stand."  

Yahweh's good to those who wait and seek,  
Hope and salvation for the humble and meek.  

Bear the yoke young, sit in silence and wait,  
Mouth in the dust, hope for a better fate.  

For grief may come, but compassion's near,  
The Lord's love outweighs the fear.  

He doesn't crush or subvert the right,  
Commands every word, brings dark and light.  

So why complain, when sins we bear?  
Turn to Yahweh, lift hands in prayer.  

"We've rebelled," we admit, sins unfold,  
Yet anger surrounds, our stories told.  

Covered with clouds, prayers can't pass,  
Enemies mock, destruction's amassed.  

Tears flow down, won't cease or end,  
Till Yahweh sees, from heaven descend.  

Chased like a bird, thrown in a pit,  
Waters rise, "I'm cut off," I admit.  

Called Yahweh's name from the lowest place,  
"You heard my cry," and showed your grace.  

"Don't be afraid," you said, came near,  
Redeemed my life, wiped away fear.  

Yahweh sees all, the vengeance they plan,  
He'll judge them rightly, work of their hand.  

A heart turned hard, curse at their door,  
Pursue in anger till they're no more.
