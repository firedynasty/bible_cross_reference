Gold's lost its shine, the purest ain't the same,  
Stones from the sanctuary scattered like a game.  

Sons of Zion once like gold, now just clay,  
Crafted by hands, molded every day.  

Even jackals nurse their young with care,  
But my people turned cold, nothing left to share.  

Babies' tongues stick, thirst holds them tight,  
Kids beg for bread, but there's none in sight.  

Once feasted on delicacies, now roam the streets,  
From royal robes to dunghills, fate repeats.  

Zion's sin's heavier than Sodom's fall,  
No hands touched her, yet wrecked she stood tall.  

Nobles once pure, whiter than snow,  
Now dark as coal, their identity they forgo.  

Skin like sticks, to their bones they cling,  
Hunger's grip a deadly, silent sting.  

Sword's death better than starvation's clutch,  
Fields fruitless, they suffer much.  

Mothers in despair, boil their own child,  
Destruction's feast, horror runs wild.  

Yahweh's wrath has burned the ground,  
Zion's base devoured, no mercy found.  

No king believed the enemy'd breach,  
Jerusalem's gates the foe did reach.  

Prophets sinned, priests spilled blood,  
Blind in streets, in a guilty flood.  

“Unclean!” they shout, “Go away, flee!”  
Exiled, not welcome, homeless to be.  

Yahweh's anger scattered them wide,  
Priests and elders, no respect, denied.  

Eyes search in vain for a nation's aid,  
Hunted steps, dreams fade.  

Pursuers faster than eagles in flight,  
Mountains or wilderness, nowhere's right.  

Breath of life, Yahweh's own, ensnared,  
Thought they'd thrive, but they're impaired.  

Edom rejoice now, your turn will come,  
Drink the cup, reveal what you've done.  

Zion's punishment reached its end,  
No more exile, Edom's sin will rend.
