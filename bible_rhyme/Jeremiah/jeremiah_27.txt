In Jehoiakim's reign, a word came through,  
Yahweh spoke to Jeremiah, here's what to do:  
"Make bonds, make bars, put them on your neck,  
Send them to the kings — show them respect.  

To Edom and Moab, Ammon, and Tyre,  
Sidon too, with messengers — don't tire.  
Tell their masters this command from above,  
Yahweh of Armies, the God they should love.  

'I made the earth, men, animals with power,  
Gave it as I saw fit, in the right hour.  
Now Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon's mighty king,  
Holds these lands — to him the nations bring.  

All will serve him, son, and grandson,  
Till his time's up and his own land's gone.  
But if a nation won't bow under his yoke,  
They'll face sword, famine, pestilence — no joke.  

Don't heed your prophets, dreamers, or those,  
Who say "Don't serve Babylon" — they're false, God knows.  
They're lying, pulling you from your land,  
I drive you out — it's Yahweh's hand.  

But nations serving Babylon, that's the key,  
They'll stay on their land, live peacefully.  
Zedekiah, listen to these words I bring,  
Serve Babylon's king, find life in this thing.  

Why die by sword, famine, pestilence,  
When Yahweh’s words show the consequence?  
Don't buy the lies of prophets that deceive,  
I didn't send them, don't be naive.  

I spoke to priests and the people too,  
Yahweh says, "Don't let false words fool you.  
The vessels won't be back from Babylon fast,  
Their prophets lie — that tale won't last.  

Serve Babylon, live — don't let the city fall,  
If they speak truth, let them to Yahweh call.  
The vessels in His house will stay away,  
Till Yahweh visits them one fine day.  

Nebuchadnezzar didn't take 'em all,  
When Jeconiah and nobles heard the call.  
Yahweh of Armies says, vessels will go,  
To Babylon, till His visit — this you should know.  

Then they'll return, restored to this place,  
Yahweh will bring them back, in His grace."
