In Jehoiakim’s reign, Yahweh’s word came,  
To Judah's cities, loud and clear, no shame.  

“Stand in my house, deliver my decree,  
Speak every word, don’t omit, hear Me.  

Maybe they’ll listen, turn from their wrong ways,  
I might hold back the wrath that for years it weighs.”  

“Tell them,” Yahweh declared, “listen up,  
Walk in my law, don’t interrupt.  

Heed my prophets, I send them to you,  
Rise early, sending, but you refuse.  

If not, this house becomes Shiloh’s fate,  
This city a curse, destruction’s gate.”  

Jeremiah spoke, the priests heard it all,  
They seized him quick, in Yahweh’s hall.  

“You’ll die,” they cried, “for your prophecy,  
Calling this house Shiloh, city's desolate sea.”  

Princes from the king’s house heard the scene,  
Sat by the gate, where the crowd had been.  

Priests and prophets shouted, “He should die,  
He’s spoken against us, why, oh why?”  

Jeremiah rose, his voice was strong,  
“Yahweh sent me to right this wrong.  

Change your ways, listen to Yahweh’s call,  
He’ll relent from evil, no downfall.”  

“Do as you will, I’m in your hand,  
But shed my blood, you’ll curse this land.”  

Princes and people, they took a pause,  
“This man speaks truth, he breaks no laws.”  

Elders stood up, their words rang through,  
“Micah spoke too, and Hezekiah knew.  

Zion’s fields plowed, Jerusalem in heaps,  
Yet they didn’t kill him, Yahweh keeps.”  

Another prophet, Uriah by name,  
Spoke like Jeremiah, met the same game.  

Jehoiakim sought his life, Uriah fled,  
But from Egypt taken, soon he was dead.  

Jeremiah spared by Ahikam’s hand,  
Protected from death, in Yahweh’s land.
