"Woe to Nebo!" Yahweh's shout rings loud,  
Kiriathaim's taken, Misgab's lost its proud.  

Moab's glory's vanished, Heshbon plots its fall,  
"Let's cut her off," they scheme, heed Madmen's call.  

A cry from Horonaim, destruction's near,  
Moab's kids weep, their wails fill the air.  

Up Luhith they climb, tears never cease,  
Down Horonaim's path, cries of no peace.  

"Flee for your lives, like junipers wild,"  
Trust in your works, but treasures defiled.  

Chemosh's bound, his priests led away,  
The destroyer comes, no city will stay.  

Give wings to Moab, let her fly fast,  
Desolation claims her, none there to last.  

"Cursed is he," Yahweh warns with wrath,  
Who shirks his duty, who holds back his path.  

Moab's been easy, settled since youth,  
But change is coming, this is the truth.  

"Days will come," Yahweh sends pourers to Moab,  
Breaking and emptying, no pride to grab.  

Moab shamed like Israel once felt,  
Valiant talk's cheap, when faced with what's dealt.  

"The calamity's close," Yahweh's decree,  
Mourn for Moab, broken staff, bent knee.  

Dibon's glory falls, sit in despair,  
The destroyer comes, your strength laid bare.  

Aroer's watcher asks those who flee,  
"What happened here? What did they see?"  

Moab's broken, wail by the Arnon's stream,  
Judgment hits plain country, no city's dream.  

Moab's horn cut, his arm's now weak,  
Yahweh declares his pride's at its peak.  

Moab's pride known, "I know his boast,"  
Yahweh laments, Moab's place is toast.  

Cry for Moab, Kir Heres too,  
Sibmah’s vine, your joy is through.  

No joy, no wine, presses stand still,  
Moab's lamentation echoes the hill.  

Pipes mourn Moab, from head to beard,  
Sackcloth worn, Moab's fate is feared.  

Yahweh breaks Moab, like a vessel smashed,  
Moab's shame's a tale all will have hashed.  

"Eagle flies," declares Yahweh bold,  
Moab's strongholds fall, hearts grow cold.  

Moab destroyed, pride led to this,  
Terror, pit, snare—none can miss.  

"Who flees terror finds the pit," Yahweh warns,  
Snare awaits all, in this year of scorns.  

Fire from Heshbon, Sihon's flame,  
Moab devoured, head bowed in shame.  

"Woe, Moab, Chemosh's folk are undone,"  
Sons and daughters in chains, nowhere to run.  

But Yahweh promises, "Days will change,"  
Moab's future's written, though now's strange.
