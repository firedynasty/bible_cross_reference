Hear the word Yahweh drops, house of Israel, stand tall,  
"Don't follow the nations, don't fear their sky signs at all.  
The customs they chase are empty and vain,  
Cutting trees from the forest, craftsmen's work with the grain.  

They deck with silver and gold, fasten with nails,  
But those idols, like palm trees, motionless tales.  
Don't fear 'em, they can't do harm or good,  
There's no one like Yahweh, understood?  

You're great, Yahweh, your name's got might,  
Who would dare not fear you, King in the light?  
The wise men of nations, in their royal plan,  
Stand foolish and brutish, just wood in their hand.  

They craft with silver from Tarshish, gold from Uphaz,  
Blue and purple clothes, their skill's just a jazz.  
But Yahweh's true, the living King,  
At his wrath, earth shakes, nations can't cling.  

"You shall tell them: 'Gods not making the skies,  
Will perish from earth, under heavens, they lie.'"  
He's made earth with power, world with wisdom laid,  
He speaks, waters roar, vapors from the earth made.  

Lightning for rain, wind from his hoard,  
Men brutish, goldsmiths aboard.  
Images fail, no breath, no life,  
Just vanity and delusion in strife.  

Jacob's portion isn't like these,  
Maker of all, tribe of his ease.  
"Gather your wares," Yahweh calls with might,  
"Sling out the land’s folk, distress 'em tight."  

"Woe is me," the cry's intense,  
"My wound is deep, and I bear the expense.  
Tent destroyed, cords broken, children gone,  
No one to pitch my tent, to carry on."  

Shepherds foolish, no inquiry made,  
Flocks scattered, prosperity delayed.  
News from the north, a commotion's sound,  
Judah's cities to jackals, desolation found.  

"Yahweh, correct me, but gentle, be mild,  
Not in anger, don't waste your child.  
Pour wrath on nations, unknown to your name,  
They've devoured Jacob, left only shame."
