Jeremiah, chains released in the land,  
Nebuzaradan set him free, gave him a stand.  
"Yahweh spoke this doom, here it's done,  
'Cause you sinned, now the deed’s begun.  

Today you're free, the chains are gone,  
Choose your path, where you belong.  
Come to Babylon, I'll care for you,  
Or stay here, where you feel it’s true."  

While he lingered, he heard the call,  
"Go back to Gedaliah, stand tall.  
Live among those left behind,  
Or choose the path you want to find."  

Food and a gift, the captain gave,  
Jeremiah, now no longer a slave.  
He went to Mizpah, with Gedaliah stayed,  
Where Judah’s remnant had obeyed.  

The captains heard, from the fields they came,  
Gedaliah's rule, now his name.  
Men, women, children, left in the land,  
Not taken captive, under Babylon’s hand.  

Gedaliah swore, "Don’t fear the Chaldeans,  
Serve them well, live with reason.  
I'll be at Mizpah, for the Chaldean sight,  
Gather your fruits, in vessels tight."  

Jews from Moab, Ammon, and Edom’s shores,  
Heard Judah had a remnant once more.  
Back they came, with wine in hand,  
To Gedaliah in Judah's land.  

But Johanan warned, "Baalis sends strife,  
Ishmael's coming to take your life."  
Gedaliah, calm, didn't believe the threat,  
"False words, Ishmael's not a threat yet."  

In secret, Johanan tried again,  
"Let me stop Ishmael, save us men.  
Why risk your life, let us live,  
Prevent the chaos that he might give."  

But Gedaliah shook his head, said,  
"You speak falsely, let it be dead."  
The tension rose, but he stood unmoved,  
In Mizpah, where the remnant proved.
