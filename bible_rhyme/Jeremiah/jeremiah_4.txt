"If you return, Israel," Yahweh's decree,  
"Put away your sins, come back to me.  
Swear truth and justice, righteousness too,  
In me, the nations will find what's true."

"Break up your ground, don't sow with thorns,  
Circumcise your hearts, my wrath warns.  
A trumpet in Judah, let the cry ring,  
Head for the cities, let safety cling."

"A lion emerges, destruction rides,  
Your land laid waste, where no one hides.  
Sackcloth and lament, Yahweh's anger fierce,  
The king's heart fails, priests' shock will pierce."

"Ah, Lord Yahweh!" I cried in dismay,  
"Peace you promised, but swords betray."  
A hot wind scorches, judgment draws near,  
Swift as eagles, chariots appear.

"Jerusalem, cleanse from wickedness,  
How long will evil in your heart rest?  
From Dan a voice, a cry from the hills,  
Watchers surround, with hostile wills."

"Your deeds bring this pain, bitter the taste,  
Destruction comes, nothing left to waste.  
Foolish people, skillful in sin,  
No knowledge of good, nowhere to begin."

"I saw the earth, void and bare,  
The mountains trembled, shadows in the air.  
Cities fallen, Yahweh's wrath descends,  
Yet a full end, he suspends."

"The land mourns, the skies turn black,  
I've spoken, no turning back.  
Cities flee from horsemen's might,  
Forsaken thickets, no soul in sight."

"Adorned in vain, lovers despise,  
Amidst desolation, hear Zion's cries.  
A voice like a mother in labor's pain,  
'Woe is me!' is the mournful refrain."
