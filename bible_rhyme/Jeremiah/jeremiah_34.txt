Yo, Jeremiah's got a word from Yahweh, it's dire,  
The king of Babylon's here, gonna set the city on fire.  

"Zedekiah, listen up," Yahweh’s message is clear,  
"You can't escape Babylon, your fate’s drawing near."  

"You’ll see the king face to face, eye to eye,  
But you'll go to Babylon, you won’t die by the sword, you'll die with a sigh."  

"They'll mourn you like kings who’ve passed before,  
Burning incense, they’ll cry out, 'Ah Lord!' evermore."  

Jeremiah spoke it, while battles raged on,  
Babylon’s army strong, and the cities nearly gone.  

Then a covenant made, freedom proclaimed,  
Servants set free, but the people felt chained.  

They turned and took back what they had let go,  
Yahweh's anger flared, his words began to flow:  

"I freed your fathers from Egypt's land,  
Said every seventh year, release them, that was my command."  

"You did right for a moment, then profaned my name,  
Brought back your servants, played this wicked game."  

"Now hear me out, you’ve ignored my decree,  
So I’ll proclaim a freedom — to sword, plague, and misery."  

"Enemies closing in, bodies left for the birds,  
Zedekiah and his princes, captured, you heard."  

"I’ll bring Babylon back, make this city a blaze,  
Judah’s cities desolate, no one left to praise."
