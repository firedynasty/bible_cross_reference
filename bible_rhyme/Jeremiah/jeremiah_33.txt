Locked up tight, but Yahweh spoke again,  
To Jeremiah in the guard's domain.  

"Yahweh's my name, I form and I make,  
Call to me, great things I'll awake.  

The city houses, broken down with might,  
Men fight the Chaldeans, fallen in the night.  

But behold, health and cure I'll bring,  
Peace and truth, like a mighty spring.  

Judah and Israel, I'll restore their place,  
Cleanse their sins, and grant them grace.  

The city's name, a joy to behold,  
Nations will tremble at the story told.  

Voices of joy, bride and groom they'll say,  
Thanks to Yahweh, His love will stay.  

Flocks will roam once more in peace,  
In Judah's cities, new life will increase.  

Behold the days, a Branch will rise,  
Justice and righteousness in David's eyes.  

Judah saved, Jerusalem safe and sound,  
Yahweh our righteousness, all around.  

David's throne will never stand bare,  
Priests will offer, a continual prayer.  

If day and night can break apart,  
Then my covenant too will depart.  

David's line, countless like the sand,  
Priests my ministers, they'll expand.  

Don't say Yahweh's cast off chosen kin,  
For their captivity I'll turn, mercy will begin."
