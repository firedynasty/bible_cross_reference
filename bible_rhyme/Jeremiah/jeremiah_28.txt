In the reign of Zedekiah, the scene was set,  
Hananiah spoke with a claim you won't forget.  
“In Yahweh’s house,” he said to the crowd,  
“‘I’ll break the yoke of Babylon!’” he vowed.  

“In two years’ time, vessels will return,  
From Nebuchadnezzar’s grasp, watch them burn.  
Jeconiah and captives will come back too,  
The yoke of Babylon, I’ll break through.”  

Jeremiah stood, his voice rang clear,  
“Amen, may Yahweh make it happen here.  
But listen now, to the word I proclaim,  
A prophet’s truth is known by the peace he can claim.”  

Hananiah took the bar from Jeremiah’s neck,  
“Yahweh will break Babylon’s yoke,” he said in check.  
Jeremiah left, the moment intense,  
But Yahweh’s voice came, making perfect sense.  

“Hananiah, your work is flawed,  
You break wooden bars, but here's the rod.  
Iron yokes on nations, Nebuchadnezzar’s reign,  
The animals too, under his domain.”  

Jeremiah warned, his words did fly,  
“Hananiah, false prophet, you lead with a lie.  
Yahweh declares, your time is done,  
In this very year, your life's undone.”  

In the seventh month, Hananiah fell,  
A prophet's life ended, with truth to tell.
