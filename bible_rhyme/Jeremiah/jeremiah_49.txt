"Has Israel no sons, no heir?" God does declare,  
"Why’s Gad in Malcam’s care, cities laid bare?"  

"Days will come," Yahweh’s voice thunders high,  
"Rabbah’s alarm will sound, flames touch the sky."  

"Wail, Heshbon, Ai's ruins tell the tale,  
Rabbah's daughters in sackcloth, run and wail."  

Malcam's downfall, priests and princes caught,  
Why boast in valleys? Your treasures are naught.  

"From all around, terror I’ll bring," says the Lord,  
Driven out with no refuge, no accord.  

"But one day, Ammon's fate will turn," God’s decree,  
Captivity ends, a future set free.  

For Edom, Yahweh questions Teman’s wise,  
"Has wisdom vanished, counsel in disguise?"  

"Dwell deep, Dedan, flee calamity’s reach,  
Esau’s judgment comes, lessons to teach."  

Gleaners leave grapes, thieves take their fill,  
But Esau’s secrets laid bare, silent and still.  

"Your children I’ll preserve," Yahweh does say,  
"Trust Me, widows, on this fateful day."  

"Drink from the cup, punishment’s a must,  
Bozrah’s desolation, all cities to dust."  

A call to nations, "Gather, rise to fight,  
Edom’s pride deceived by the mountain’s height."  

"Edom shall astonish, just like before,  
No man shall dwell there, rooted to the core."  

"Up like a lion from Jordan’s pride,  
I’ll make them flee, My power won’t hide."  

"Who’s like Me?" Yahweh asks with might,  
"Who can challenge, in day or night?"  

Yahweh’s counsel speaks, Teman’s desolate call,  
Little ones dragged away, earth trembles with their fall.  

Eagles fly against Bozrah, mighty hearts will shake,  
Edom’s warriors feel the pangs they cannot fake.  

Damascus hears news, fears melt and flow,  
City of praise now trembling below.  

Men fall silent, war's sound fades away,  
Yahweh’s fire devours, judgment’s sway.  

"Kedar and Hazor, rise up and destroy,  
Nebuchadnezzar’s plan, without joy."  

Tents and flocks taken, terror on all sides,  
Flee and wander, travelers in the tides.  

Hazor becomes a jackal's home, desolate,  
No man will dwell, sealed by fate.  

To Elam, Yahweh’s word came down,  
"I’ll break your might, scatter you around."  

Four winds scatter, outcasts spread wide,  
Enemies pursue, Elam cannot hide.  

"My throne in Elam, kings and princes fall,"  
But hope remains, I’ll restore them all.
