Jeremiah got the word, Judah was in his sight,  
Fourth year of Jehoiakim, Babylon's first flight.  
For twenty-three years, I've spoken day by day,  
But y'all ain't listening, just turn away.

Prophets sent early, God's voice in their stride,  
“Turn from your evil, in the land abide.  
Don't chase other gods, don't provoke my wrath,  
Then I'll keep you safe, stay on my path.”

But you ignored, turned your ear away,  
Provoking anger, bringing judgment your way.  
Yahweh speaks, "You heard not my cries,  
So Babylon's comin', it's time to rise."

Nebuchadnezzar, my servant, will come,  
Turn your land to shock, make you feel numb.  
No joy or laughter, bride's voice will cease,  
Mirth turned to mourning, no light, no peace.

Seventy years, Babylon holds sway,  
Then I'll bring judgment, they'll repay.  
Desolation to the land, Chaldeans fall,  
Great kings rise, and Babylon crawls.

"Take this cup, wrath’s bitter drink,  
Nations will stagger, they'll fall, they'll sink.  
Sword in hand, make them reel,  
Jerusalem, Judah, all will feel."

Kings and lands, from Egypt to Uz,  
Philistines, Ammon, each has a buzz.  
The north, the south, all will drink,  
God’s wrath poured out, no time to think.

"Drink deep, feel the sword's might,  
Fall and rise no more, in the night.  
Yahweh calls, no nation unscathed,  
Sword on earth, His judgment engraved."

Proclaim His roar, His mighty shout,  
Judgment's here, no doubt about.  
From end to end, His controversy flows,  
Wicked to sword, His justice shows.

Evil storm, nation to nation,  
Yahweh's fury, no hesitation.  
Slain on earth, no one cries,  
On the ground, life's demise.

Shepherds wail, leaders weep,  
No escape, judgment's deep.  
Yahweh roars from His place,  
Land astonished, His fierce face.
