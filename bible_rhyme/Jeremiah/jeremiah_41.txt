Seventh month rolls in, Ishmael's on the scene,  
Royal blood, ten men, a deadly team.  
They met in Mizpah, broke bread with Gedaliah,  
Then Ishmael rose up, dealt death with fire.  

With swords they struck, Gedaliah fell,  
Governor no more, by Babylon's spell.  
Jews and Chaldeans, they met their fate,  
Ishmael's wrath, they couldn't escape.  

Two days passed, no news had spread,  
Eighty men came, mourning the dead.  
From Shechem, Shiloh, with offerings in hand,  
Ishmael met them, tears in the sand.  

He lured them in, "Come see Gedaliah," he said,  
But into the pit, those men were led.  
Yet ten men pleaded, "Spare us, we pray,  
We've hidden treasures, just let us stay."  

He spared their lives, but the pit was filled,  
With bodies of those whom Ishmael killed.  
He took captives, the king's daughters bound,  
Off to Ammon, they were outward bound.  

But Johanan heard, and gathered his band,  
To fight Ishmael, to take a stand.  
At Gibeon's waters, the clash was near,  
People rejoiced when Johanan appeared.  

Captives turned back, glad to be free,  
Yet Ishmael escaped, with a menacing spree.  
Johanan gathered the remnants, moved on,  
To dwell near Bethlehem, before the dawn.  

Fear of Chaldeans drove them to flee,  
To Egypt they aimed, seeking to be free.  
For Ishmael had killed, and the land was torn,  
Babylon's governor, now forlorn.
