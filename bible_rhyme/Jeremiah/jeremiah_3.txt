"If a man sends away his wife, then she leaves,  
Takes another man and returns, is it right to believe?"  
But you’ve roamed with many a lover, still come back to me,  
Says Yahweh, "Lift up your eyes, tell me what do you see?  

You’ve waited on roadsides like those in the desert, unseen,  
You've stained the land with ways that are unclean.  
So the rain is withheld, skies refuse to pour,  
Yet your forehead’s bold, you feel no shame anymore.  

Will you not cry out and call me 'Father of my youth?'  
Will anger last forever? Is that the truth?"  
You've spoken and acted, did evil your own way,  
Yahweh says, "Israel's strayed, what can I say?  

She climbed every high mountain, under each green tree,  
Played the harlot there, wasn't faithful to me.  
I thought she'd return, but she stayed on her track,  
Judah watched her sister, yet didn't turn back.  

Israel's betrayal led to divorce, a certificate I gave,  
Yet Judah, she wandered, no fear did she save.  
Played lightly with stones and wood, it's understood,  
Her heart wasn't true, though she thought it should.  

"Backsliding Israel's more righteous," Yahweh declares,  
"Go north, say, 'Return,' for I care.  
I won’t stay angry, merciful I will be,  
Just admit your wrongs, come back to me."  

"Return, lost children," Yahweh does proclaim,  
"I’m your husband, come back, don’t be the same.  
From cities and families, I’ll gather you near,  
Bring you to Zion, where you’ll have no fear.  

Shepherds will guide with wisdom to share,  
When you multiply, my covenant you’ll no longer bear.  
Jerusalem, Yahweh’s Throne, all nations will come,  
No more stubborn hearts, just a unified hum.  

Judah and Israel, together they'll stand,  
Out of the north to the promised land.  
I long to place you among sons, give you lands of delight,  
“You’ll call me Father, follow me right."  

But like a wife who leaves, Israel’s broken the pact,  
Weeping on heights, for Yahweh they lacked.  
"Return," says Yahweh, "and I’ll heal your ways,  
We acknowledge, you're our God, through all our days.  

Help from the hills, and mountains, all in vain,  
Salvation's in Yahweh, remember His name.  
Our shame has eaten the work of our hands,  
With flocks, herds, daughters, and sons across the lands.  

In shame we'll lie, with confusion in place,  
We’ve sinned from youth, turned from God's face."
