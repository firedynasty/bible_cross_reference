"Woe to the shepherds," Yahweh's voice is clear,  
"They scatter my sheep, no visit, nowhere near.  

You've driven them away, left them all alone,  
Now I'll visit your evil, make it known.  

I'll gather my flock from lands far and wide,  
Bring 'em back to their folds, let 'em thrive inside.  

New shepherds will rise, feed them right and true,  
Fear and dismay? Nah, they'll never pursue.  

"Behold the days," Yahweh says with might,  
"A Branch from David will rise, set it right.  

Judah saved, Israel at peace, he'll reign,  
Yahweh our Righteousness, his holy name.  

The days are coming, no longer will be said,  
'Yahweh brought Israel from Egypt,' instead,  

'He led them from the north, every scattered clan,  
To dwell in their land, held by Yahweh's hand.'  

Prophets, oh prophets, my heart is broke,  
Bones shake like a man who just awoke.  

The land's full of curse, wilderness dried,  
Prophets and priests, in profane paths they stride.  

Slippery darkness, they'll fall, not rise,  
Evil on them, Yahweh's justice flies.  

Samaria's prophets, folly seen,  
Prophesied by Baal, they've been mean.  

In Jerusalem's prophets, adultery, lies,  
Evildoers they strengthen, no one denies.  

"Like Sodom, like Gomorrah," Yahweh cries out,  
Wormwood and poison, their fate devout.  

"Don't heed their words," Yahweh warns,  
They speak visions from hearts that scorn.  

"Who stood in my council?" Yahweh asks,  
To hear my word, to take on the tasks?  

Yahweh's storm, a whirlwind will break,  
On the wicked's head, wrath he won't forsake.  

Prophets ran, but I never sent,  
They spoke, but from me they're absent.  

"Am I not near?" Yahweh demands,  
"Filling heaven and earth by my hands.  

I've heard their lies, 'A dream! A dream!' they said,  
Forget my name, by dreams they're led.  

The dreamer tells dreams, my word's the truth,  
"Isn't my word like fire?" Yahweh's proof.  

Against false prophets, Yahweh stands,  
They steal my words, without my commands.  

Lying dreams, they cause deceit,  
I never sent them, their words are weak.  

When asked for Yahweh's message, here's your cue,  
"What message?" Yahweh says, "I cast off you."  

Prophet, priest, people who claim my word,  
Punishment on them, justice deferred.  

From your mouths, no more will my message flow,  
You've twisted my words, turned them into show.  

The message from Yahweh, forget it for good,  
Perpetual shame, misunderstood."
