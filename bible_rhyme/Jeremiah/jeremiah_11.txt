Jeremiah heard a word from Yahweh, clear and bright,  
"Speak to Judah, to Jerusalem, in the pale moonlight.  

Tell them Yahweh says, 'Cursed is the one who won't hear,  
The words of this covenant, so perfectly clear.  

I led your fathers from Egypt, from the furnace so hot,  
Said, "Obey my voice, do what I taught."  

Be my people, and I'll be your God, see,  
I swore to your fathers, a land of milk and honey.'  

I said, 'Amen, Yahweh,' echoing the call,  
In Judah's cities, Jerusalem's streets, I told them all.  

'Listen to this covenant, do what is right,  
I warned your fathers from morning till night.  

But they turned away, in their own hearts' desire,  
I brought the covenant's words, and now they're under fire.'  

Yahweh said, 'A conspiracy in Judah unfolds,  
They chase other gods, breaking promises of old.  

I'm bringing evil, no escape from the dread,  
They'll call to me, but I'll turn my head.  

To their gods they will cry, who won't save them at all,  
A god for each city, to Baal they fall.'  

So don't pray for them, no cries, no pleas,  
I won't hear them, down on their knees.  

What has my beloved done, in my house so defiled?  
Lewdness abounds, holy flesh exiled.  

I called you a green olive tree, beauty in sight,  
But a roar kindled fire, branches broken in the night.  

Yahweh of Armies, planted you strong,  
Now brings evil, because they did wrong.  

He showed me their doings, secrets they planned,  
I was like a lamb, led by the hand.  

They said, 'Destroy the tree, his name erase,'  
But Yahweh of Armies, sees and keeps pace.  

Yahweh says, 'Men of Anathoth, seek your life,  
Say, "Don't prophesy, or meet the knife."  

But Yahweh says, 'Punishment will come their way,  
Young men by the sword, no more light of day.  

No remnant left, no sons or daughters to mourn,  
The year of their visitation, wrath will be born.'"
