Jeremiah's the son of Hilkiah, priest in Anathoth's land,  
Yahweh's word to him, on Josiah's command.  

In the days of Jehoiakim, the king's reign spanned,  
Till Zedekiah's rule, and Jerusalem's stand.  

Yahweh speaks to me, "Before birth, you were mine,  
A prophet to nations, your role's divine."  

I said, "Lord Yahweh, I'm just a youth,  
Can't speak for you, that's the truth."  

But Yahweh replied, "Don't say you're just a kid,  
You'll go where I send, do as you're bid.  

Don't fear them, for I'm by your side,  
To rescue you, my words will guide."  

He touched my mouth, His words in my core,  
"Over nations, kingdoms, you'll uproot, restore.  

Uproot and tear down, destroy, overthrow,  
Build and plant, make my kingdom grow."  

"Jeremiah, what do you see?" came the divine call,  
"I see an almond branch, standing tall."  

"Seen well," He said, "My word will not fall,  
I watch over it, fulfilling it all."  

A boiling pot, tipping from the north,  
"Evil's coming forth, from that direction it'll start.  

All kingdoms will come to Jerusalem's gate,  
Against Judah's cities, they seal their fate.  

Judgments for wickedness, they left me behind,  
Worshiping false gods, their own hands' designs.  

So gird your waist, stand tall and declare,  
Don't fear the faces of those who stare.  

I've made you a city, strong like iron and bronze,  
Against kings, princes, priests, and lands beyond.  

They'll fight you, but won't succeed,  
For I am with you, my word is your creed."
