Jeremiah writes from the city's core,  
To exiles far, behind Babylon's door.  

He sends the word, to captives bound tight,  
Saying, "Live your lives, don't fight the night.  

Build your homes and gardens, plant seeds in the ground,  
Grow your families, let your joy resound.  

Seek the city's peace, and pray for its good,  
In its welfare, find your own livelihood.  

Don't trust the false dreams, or prophets who lie,  
I didn't send them," says Yahweh, "they defy.  

After seventy years, I'll bring you back home,  
With peace and hope, no more to roam.  

Call on me, I'll hear, I'll be found by you,  
With all your heart, seek, find a future true.  

I'll end your captivity, gather from the lands,  
Bring you back to where your freedom stands."  

But for those who remain in the city alone,  
Yahweh's wrath comes, through sword and stone.  

Rotten figs they'll be, scattered and lost,  
For ignoring my prophets, this is their cost.  

Ahab and Zedekiah, liars in my name,  
To Babylon's fire, they'll be put to shame.  

Shemaiah's words, false letters he sends,  
Punishment awaits, for him and his friends.  

The captivity's lengthy, but do not despair,  
In the ground of your trial, sprout hope everywhere.  

For Yahweh's plans are good, though long may it seem,  
In the end, you'll awaken from this distant dream.
