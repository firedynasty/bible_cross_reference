Run the streets of Jerusalem, look and seek,  
Find a just man, truth they speak.  
If you do, pardon I'll grant,  
Though they swear by Me, it's just a chant.  

"Yahweh sees truth," but they've gone astray,  
Struck, ungrieved, turned away.  
Faces harder than stone, they refuse to learn,  
To the great men, now I must turn.  

But even the wise have broken the yoke,  
The bonds they burst, their promises broke.  
A lion, a wolf, a leopard on the prowl,  
Transgressions many, sin on the prowl.  

"How can I pardon?" Yahweh cries,  
Children forsake, believe in lies.  
Fed and full, yet they stray,  
Neighing for lust, night and day.  

"Shall I not punish?" Yahweh demands,  
"Go tear the walls, obey My commands.  
Israel, Judah, treacherous they’ve been,  
Denied Yahweh, in deceit they spin."  

Prophets like wind, words are gone,  
With fire and wood, they'll be undone.  
A nation comes, mighty and vast,  
Language unknown, like shadows they cast.  

Eating harvests, daughters' bread,  
Beating cities, leaving dread.  
"But even then," Yahweh's grace,  
"I won't end you, just leave a trace."  

When questioned why these things you face,  
"Foreign gods you've chased, now displaced."  
Hear this, foolish, without sight,  
Eyes and ears closed tight.  

"Don't you fear Me?" Yahweh asks,  
Sea bounds I set, waves in tasks.  
Yet hearts are hard, revolting they've gone,  
Forgotten the rain, the harvest's dawn.  

Iniquities turn blessings away,  
Wicked men trap, lead astray.  
Rich in deceit, fat and bright,  
Neglect the needy, ignore the plight.  

"Shall I not punish?" Yahweh declares,  
Horrible deeds, nobody cares.  
Prophets lie, priests mislead,  
And My people love the deceit they feed.  

So what will you do when the end is near?  
With truth ignored, in trembling fear?
