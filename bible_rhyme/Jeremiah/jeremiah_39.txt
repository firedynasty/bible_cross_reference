In Zedekiah's ninth year, Babylon marched with might,  
Nebuchadnezzar's army came, set Jerusalem in their sight.  

By the eleventh year, the city wall was breached,  
The Babylonian princes entered, the city's heart they reached.  

Zedekiah saw them come, he and his soldiers fled,  
Through the garden's gate by night, to Arabah they sped.  

Chaldeans gave chase, caught him in Jericho's field,  
To Nebuchadnezzar they brought him, his fate was sealed.  

Sons of Zedekiah fell before his eyes,  
Nobles of Judah, too, faced their demise.  

His eyes were put out, bound in chains so tight,  
To Babylon they led him, out of Judah's sight.  

Chaldeans burned houses, Jerusalem's walls came down,  
Nebuzaradan took captives, led them to Babylon's town.  

Yet some poor folk stayed, vineyards and fields they gained,  
A new life in the land, though much had changed.  

Nebuchadnezzar spoke of Jeremiah, a command he gave,  
"Protect him, do no harm, as he wishes, behave."  

Jeremiah was freed, Gedaliah took him in,  
Among the people now, a new chapter would begin.  

Yahweh spoke to Jeremiah, words to Ebedmelech brought,  
“Destruction's coming, but you, fear not.”  

“I'll save you that day," Yahweh's promise clear,  
"You'll escape with your life, for in Me you hold dear."
