"At that time," Yahweh spoke with might,  
"I'll be Israel's God, in their hearts, alight."  
Survivors in the wild, they found my grace,  
Even when they wandered in a restless place.  

"I loved you long, with love that won't fade,  
With kindness I drew you, though you've strayed."  
"I'll build you again, with tambourines in hand,  
Dance with joy across this promised land."  

"Vineyards on Samaria's hills you'll plant anew,  
Watchmen call, ‘To Zion, let's go, me and you!’"  
"Sing for Jacob, shout for nations to hear,  
'Save your people, Yahweh, bring them near.'"  

From the north and farthest land, they'll form a throng,  
The blind, the lame, together they belong.  
With tears they'll come, led by rivers' gentle flow,  
"For I am their Father, I will make it so."  

"Declare my word to distant shores,  
'He scattered, He'll gather, like a shepherd restores.'"  
Ransomed from the stronger hand, now redeemed,  
To Zion they sing, to Yahweh's goodness streamed.  

Like a watered garden, their souls will thrive,  
Young and old dance, mourning can't survive.  
"I'll turn their sorrow to joy," Yahweh decrees,  
"Satisfy the priests, my goodness will please."  

But Rachel weeps, her children are no more,  
"Hold your tears," Yahweh whispers, "I'll restore."  
"They'll return from the enemy's grasp," He declares,  
"There's hope in the end, renewal in their lairs."  

Ephraim's grief, like an untrained calf's cry,  
"Turn me, Yahweh, for I have gone awry."  
Confounded by shame, yet remembered dear,  
"My heart yearns for mercy, I'll draw him near."  

"Set up signs, guide your heart back home,  
Turn again, Israel, no longer roam."  
"A new thing's created, a woman will surround,  
Judah's speech will bless, as they are unbound."  

The weary, replenished, find rest at last,  
Yahweh's watch over them, unbroken, steadfast.  
"The days approach," Yahweh says with grace,  
"To sow Israel and Judah in this sacred space."  

Their sour grapes no longer the children's fate,  
Each bears their iniquity, their own weight.  
"A new covenant I'll write on their hearts within,  
They'll know me, from least to greatest, cleansed of sin."  

The sun and moon obey Yahweh's command,  
"If these depart, Israel won't stand,"  
Yet Yahweh's promise holds the nation tight,  
"Forever they remain in my sight."  

"The city's built, from Hananel's tower to the gate,  
Holy to Yahweh, in peace they'll wait."  
The land, once marred by death and decay,  
Is Yahweh's forever, unthrown and to stay."
