"At that time," Yahweh declared with force,  
Bones of kings and priests, they'll take their course.  

Out from graves, spread beneath the sky,  
Worshipped the sun, moon, stars on high.  

Not gathered, not buried, like dung they’ll lie,  
Death's preferred, life denied, they sigh.  

"Do men fall and not rise up once more?  
Why does Jerusalem backslide, ignore?"  

Clinging to deceit, they refuse to hear,  
No repentance, no echo of fear.  

Like horses rushed to battle they fly,  
The crane knows the time, but my people ask why.  

"We are wise," they claim, but it's a tale,  
The scribes' false pen, a deceitful trail.  

Wise men dismayed, trapped in their plight,  
Rejected Yahweh, wisdom's taken flight.  

Wives to others, fields will change hand,  
From least to greatest, greed fills the land.  

"Peace, peace," they say, but it’s nothing but lies,  
No shame in sin, no blush, no cries.  

"I will consume," Yahweh's decree,  
No figs, no grapes, bare the vine and tree.  

"Why sit still? Assemble, take the silent road,  
For Yahweh has given us a bitter load."  

Expected peace, but only dismay,  
Horses from Dan, the land to betray.  

"I send serpents to bite and to sting,"  
In sorrow, my heart’s a broken thing.  

From distant lands, a cry rings out,  
"Is Yahweh in Zion?" They shout.  

Provoked to anger with idols and pride,  
"Harvest has passed, salvation denied."  

For Zion's daughter, my heart does mourn,  
Where’s the balm in Gilead for hearts torn?
