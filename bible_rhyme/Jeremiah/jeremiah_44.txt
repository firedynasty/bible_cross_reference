Jeremiah got the word, to Egypt's land he came,  
Speaking to the Jews, hear this message, feel the flame.  

"Yahweh of Armies speaks, you've seen the ruin,  
Jerusalem's left empty, cities of Judah in ruin.  

All 'cause of wicked deeds, incense to gods unknown,  
Provoking anger, my wrath has overthrown.  

I sent prophets early, saying 'Don’t dare do this thing,'  
Yet you turned deaf ears, worshiping false gods like a king.  

So my wrath poured out, in Judah's streets they fell,  
Wasted and desolate, in a sorrowful, silent shell.  

Now Yahweh speaks again, why harm your own soul?  
Cutting off your kin, leaving not one whole.  

Burning incense in Egypt, provoking with your hands,  
To become a curse, be scattered across lands.  

Have you forgotten the sins of your fathers and kings?  
They walked not in my laws, disregarding all things.  

So now I face you, cutting off all of Judah's might,  
Those who sought Egypt to live, consumed in the night.  

By sword, famine, pestilence, they'll fall,  
A horror, curse, reproach — they won't stand tall.  

No remnant will return, except the ones who flee,  
Yet all will see whose word will truly be.  

The people answered Jeremiah, they wouldn’t heed the call,  
"We’ll do what we want, burning incense, offerings tall.  

Back then life was sweet, food plenty, all was well,  
Now without our worship, sword and famine fell."  

Jeremiah replied, "You think Yahweh forgot your actions?  
He remembers your incense, saw your transactions.  

That's why desolation and curses now reign,  
You didn’t listen to His voice, hence the pain."  

Jeremiah said further, "Hear Yahweh’s strong decree,  
You've declared your vows, now fulfill them, let it be.  

Yet hear Yahweh’s word, your fate is clear,  
His name will no longer be spoken here.  

Watched for evil, not good, by sword and famine consumed,  
Few will escape, back to Judah, where doom loomed.  

This is your sign; Yahweh’s words will take their place,  
Pharaoh Hophra’s fate sealed, given to enemies who chase."
