Yahweh showed a vision, two baskets in view,  
After Babylon's king had taken Judah's crew.  

One basket had figs that were ripe and sweet,  
While the other held figs that no one could eat.  

Yahweh asked, "Jeremiah, what do you see?"  
I saw good figs and bad figs, clear as can be.  

Then the word of Yahweh came to me,  
"Like these good figs, Judah’s captives I'll set free.  

I'll watch over them, bring them back to this land,  
I'll build them up strong, with my own hand.  

"I'll give them hearts that know I am Yahweh,  
They'll be my people, and walk in my way.  

But those bad figs, they're beyond repair,  
Zedekiah and his kind won't find my care.  

"To every kingdom, they'll be tossed around,  
A curse, a taunt, wherever they're found.  

I'll send sword, famine, pestilence in my wrath,  
Till they're wiped from the land, down to the last."  

That's the vision, clear and bold,  
The fate of Judah, plainly told.
