Judah's sin, carved with an iron pen,  
Etched deep in hearts, on altars again.  
Children remember those high hills, green,  
Where poles of Asherah are always seen.  

"My mountain, your treasures, plundered wide,  
For sins all across borders, they can't hide.  
You'll leave the heritage I gave, it's true,  
Serve enemies, in lands you never knew.  

Cursed is the man who trusts in flesh alone,  
Like a desert bush, parched to the bone.  
Blessed is the soul who trusts in Me,  
Like a tree by the water, roots spread free.  

He won't fear drought, his leaves stay green,  
In his fruitfulness, abundance is seen.  
The heart's deceitful, who can understand?  
I search the heart, and judge by every hand."  

Riches gained not right, like eggs not laid,  
Soon leave the fool, where fake riches fade.  
A throne on high from the start's our claim,  
Yahweh, our hope, forsake Him, feel shame.  

"Heal me, Yahweh," I cry, "and make me whole,  
Save me, for You are praise in my soul.  
They ask, 'Where's Yahweh's word?' Let them see,  
I follow, not rushing from Your decree.  

Be my refuge when days turn vile,  
Disappoint them, who seek to defile.  
Don't let me stumble; let them fall,  
Bring double destruction on them all."  

Yahweh said, "Stand at the gate, and call,  
Tell kings, Judah, hear Me all.  
No burdens on Sabbath, keep it pure,  
Just as I commanded before, for sure.  

Yet they stiffened necks, refused the word,  
If you listen, your voices will be heard.  
Kings will come, on David's throne they'll ride,  
From near and far, they'll come inside.  

Burnt offerings, sacrifice, they'll bring,  
Praises rise, to Yahweh, they'll sing.  
But if you won't listen, if you disobey,  
Fire will burn, consume the gates that day."
