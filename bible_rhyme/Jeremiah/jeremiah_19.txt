Yahweh spoke, "Go buy a potter's clay,  
With elders by your side, hear what I say.  

To Hinnom's valley, follow the gate,  
Proclaim my words, don't hesitate.  

"Listen up, Judah's kings, Jerusalem's crew,  
Yahweh of Armies has a message for you:  

I'll bring a sound that'll make ears ring,  
On this land, chaos I'll bring.  

They abandoned me, defiled this space,  
Offered incense, brought disgrace.  

Baal's altars built high in the air,  
Children burned, they didn't care.  

I never spoke or thought this plan,  
Now this valley's fate, understand:  

Soon it'll be 'Slaughter,' not what you know,  
Judah and Jerusalem's counsel will go.  

By the sword, they'll meet their doom,  
Birds and beasts will feast in gloom.  

People aghast, they'll hiss and stare,  
Plagues will leave them in despair.  

In siege, they'll eat their kin,  
Desperation thick, can't win."  

Break the pot, shatter it on the ground,  
Say, "Yahweh's wrath will resound.  

This city's broken, no mending it back,  
Topheth's their grave, under the attack.  

Houses defiled, incense on the dome,  
Poured drinks to gods, far from home."  

Jeremiah left, to the court he went,  
Spoke Yahweh's words, his message sent.  

"On this city, every curse will fall,  
For they stiffened their necks, ignored my call."
