Yo, listen up, this is a tale of Babylon’s fall,  
Jeremiah's words, Yahweh's call.  

"Shout it out to the nations, don’t keep it inside,  
Babylon's been taken, her idols can’t hide.  

From the north comes a force, making her land desolate,  
Man and beast flee, leave her to fate."  

"In that time," says Yahweh, "Israel returns,  
Weeping, seeking, their hearts will yearn.  

They’ll come together, a covenant they'll find,  
Never forgotten, forever aligned.  

My people like lost sheep, stray and roam,  
But now they remember where they belong, their home."  

"Flee from Babylon, get out now,  
‘Cause nations are coming to bring her down.  

Arrows fly like mighty men,  
Chaldea a prey, won’t rise again."  

"You laughed and plundered, danced in the sun,  
But now your downfall has begun.  

Desolate and dry, a wilderness so,  
Wrath of Yahweh, none will dwell, none will grow."  

"Surround her now, archers take aim,  
Spare no arrows, for she bears shame.  

Her walls crumble, vengeance is due,  
As Babylon did, do to her too."  

"Israel, once hunted, now the lions are gone,  
Assyria's devoured, Babylon’s bones undone.  

But Yahweh says: 'I’ll bring you back,  
To pastures green, no more attack.'"  

In those days, no sin found,  
Remnant forgiven, grace abounds.  

"Merathaim, Pekod, destruction’s tune,  
Battle cries echo, Babylon’s doom."  

Hammer of the earth, broken apart,  
Caught in a snare, unaware from the start.  

Yahweh opened his armory, weapons in sight,  
Against Chaldea, it's a brewing fight.  

"Archers gather, surround with might,  
Babylon’s pride meets darkest night.  

Her warriors fall, silence in the street,  
No one to lift the proud from defeat."  

Yahweh of Armies, Israel’s strong,  
To give them rest, right every wrong.  

"Sword on Chaldeans, princes in dread,  
Boasters turned fools, their strength fled.  

Drought on waters, idols can’t save,  
Desert creatures in a city made grave."  

"Like Sodom and Gomorrah, nothing will stand,  
No man will dwell, barren the land."  

From the north, mighty nations rise,  
No mercy in their hearts, only battle cries.  

King of Babylon hears, his strength fades away,  
Fear grips him, like labor pains sway.  

"Enemy like a lion, from Jordan they roam,  
Stronghold to dust, making it their home.  

Hear Yahweh’s counsel, against Babylon’s land,  
Desolation awaits, by Yahweh’s hand."  

At Babylon’s fall, the earth shakes wide,  
A cry rises up, heard worldwide.
