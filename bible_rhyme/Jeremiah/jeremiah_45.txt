In the fourth year of Jehoiakim's reign,  
Jeremiah spoke words that Baruch did gain.  

"Yahweh, God of Israel, has this to say,  
To you, Baruch, listen, don't stray.  

You cried out, 'Woe, for sorrow adds to my pain,  
I'm weary, groaning, seeking rest in vain.'  

Tell him this, Yahweh's message is clear,  
'What I've built, I'll break, what I've planted, I shear.  

Do you seek greatness, things for yourself?  
Don't chase them, for I'll bring trouble to all else.'  

Says Yahweh, 'But here's the deal,  
Your life I'll protect, it's the real seal.'"
