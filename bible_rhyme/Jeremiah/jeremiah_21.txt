Jeremiah got a word from Yahweh, you know,  
Zedekiah sent Pashhur and Zephaniah to go.  
"Ask Yahweh for us," they said, with fear in their eyes,  
"King Nebuchadnezzar's coming, bringing war and cries."  

Jeremiah said, "Listen, tell Zedekiah this,  
Yahweh's got a message, nothing you can dismiss:  
‘I’ll turn back your weapons, what you hold in your hand,  
The Chaldeans are coming, they're gonna take a stand.  

I’ll fight against you, with anger and might,  
Striking down man and beast, a terrible sight.  
Pestilence will sweep through, everyone will fall,  
Nobody's safe behind these city walls.  

After that,’ says Yahweh, ‘Zedekiah will be caught,  
Delivered to the king of Babylon, what a plot!  
No mercy will be shown, swords will gleam,  
Against those left standing by the enemy team.’"  

"Tell the people this," Yahweh's words began,  
"Two paths lie ahead, for every woman and man:  
Stay in the city, die from sword, famine, pest,  
Leave to the Chaldeans, and live, that’s the test.  

My face is set against this city in wrath,  
It'll burn in the fire, as a witness of my path."  

To the house of Judah, Yahweh's words so clear,  
"Justice and deliverance, every morning, hear.  
Or my wrath will blaze, a fire from your deeds,  
Punishment will come, like flames in the reeds.  

I’m against you, O valley, O rock of the plain,  
You think you're untouchable, but it’s all in vain.  
I’ll punish you by what you’ve done, hear my word,  
Kindling a fire, devouring all you’ve heard."
