Yo, Yahweh sends a message, clear and tight,  
To the king of Judah, gotta get it right.  
"Listen up, hear what I say,  
Justice and righteousness, do it today.  

Save the oppressed, make it your quest,  
No wrong, no violence, put it to rest.  
Treat the foreigner, widow, and child right,  
Shed no innocent blood, don’t start a fight.  

If you do this, kings will roll in style,  
Chariots, horses, mile after mile.  
But if you don't hear, let me make it plain,  
This house, desolation, nothing will remain."  

Judah's house, you're precious like Gilead,  
But soon, deserted, you'll be sad.  
Destroyers with weapons, they’ll come fast,  
Your cedars cut, into the fire cast.  

Nations passing by, they’ll wonder why,  
"Why did Yahweh let this great city die?"  
They’ll answer back, "Abandoned the covenant,  
Worshiped false gods, that’s the judgment."  

Don't cry for the dead, but weep for the lost,  
Never returning, they’ve paid the cost.  
Shallum's gone, he won't come back,  
In foreign lands, his life's on track.  

"Woe to builders of unrighteous walls,  
Who cheat their neighbors, in justice falls.  
Build wide houses, fancy and grand,  
But justice and mercy, they don't understand."  

Your father's ways were right and just,  
He knew Yahweh, did what he must.  
But your eyes are greedy, shedding blood,  
Oppression and violence, it's in your flood.  

Jehoiakim, they won’t weep for you,  
Buried like a donkey, beyond the blue.  
Cry out from Lebanon, Bashan, and Abarim,  
All your allies gone, your future grim.  

In prosperity, you turned away,  
Refused to listen, day by day.  
Now shame and disgrace, they’ll come to you,  
Your wicked ways, they’re coming through.  

Coniah, if a ring on my hand you were,  
Still I'd pluck you, let me be clear.  
Into Babylon's hands, you will fall,  
Not your land, but there’s your call.  

Into exile, with your mother too,  
In strange lands, your life’s anew.  
Earth, hear Yahweh, loud and bold,  
Coniah's line, no more to hold.  

No offspring will prosper, no throne to see,  
Ruling in Judah, it will not be."
