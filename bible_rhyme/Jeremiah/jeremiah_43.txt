Jeremiah spoke God's truth, every word he said,  
Azariah and Johanan shook their heads, "It's all in your head.  

Jeremiah, you're lying, God didn't send you here,  
Saying 'Don't go to Egypt'—that's not what we hear.  

Baruch's turned you against us, to hand us to Chaldeans,  
To take us captive, that's his dark scheming.  

But they ignored the voice of Yahweh, their God,  
Johanan and his men on a path that was flawed.  

They gathered Judah's remnant, men and women in tow,  
All the people left in Judah, they had to go.  

With Jeremiah and Baruch, they made their way,  
To Egypt's land—they didn't obey.  

Down to Tahpanhes, they arrived with haste,  
Ignoring God's word, they sealed their fate.  

Then Yahweh spoke to Jeremiah, clear and loud,  
"Take these stones, hide them in the crowd.  

In Pharaoh's brickwork, where all can see,  
Say, 'Here Nebuchadnezzar's throne will be.'  

The king of Babylon, my servant, he'll reign,  
His throne on these stones, it's all preordained.  

Egypt will shatter, death and captivity,  
To the sword, the fire—it's their destiny.  

He'll burn their gods' houses, take them away,  
Arrayed like a shepherd, he'll leave in the day.  

Beth Shemesh's pillars, they'll break and burn,  
Egypt's gods' houses, to ashes they'll turn."
