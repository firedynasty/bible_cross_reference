The word came down to Jeremiah's ear,  
About nations far and nations near.  

"Prepare your shield, get ready to fight,  
Mount the horses, stand up with might.  

Polish those spears, put on the mail,  
Why do I see them dismayed and pale?  

Mighty ones beaten, fleeing in fear,  
Terror's around, nothing is clear."  

"Don't let the swift escape the fray,  
By Euphrates' river, they fall today.  

Who rises like the Nile, high and wide?  
Egypt's waters surge like a pride.  

'I’ll cover the earth,' Egypt boasts of power,  
'I'll destroy cities, this is my hour.'  

Charge, you horses! Chariots, rage!  
Mighty men, with strength engage.  

For Yahweh's day, a vengeance to claim,  
The sword drinks blood, ends their game.  

Go to Gilead, take balm for the pain,  
But healing escapes, all efforts in vain.  

Nations hear cries, shame spreads wide,  
Mighty men stumble, side by side."  

Yahweh spoke, a word to declare,  
"Babylon strikes, Egypt beware.  

Shout in Egypt, Migdol, Memphis, hear,  
‘Prepare for the sword, it's drawing near.’  

Strong ones fall, they don't stand tall,  
Yahweh's push makes them all crawl.  

‘Rise, return to our birthland,’ they say,  
Pharaoh's just noise, wasted the day.  

'As I live,' Yahweh declares with might,  
Tabor and Carmel, he comes to fight.  

Egypt, beautiful heifer, now desolate,  
From the north rides your fate.  

Hired men like calves, fled away,  
On calamity's day, they can't stay.  

Sound of a serpent, armies march on,  
Axes like woodcutters, Egypt's gone.  

Yahweh says, 'I punish all in sight,  
Amon, Pharaoh, all in my might.  

Into Babylon's hand, I’ll give them all,  
Yet Egypt will rise, as old days call.  

But Jacob, don’t fear, my servant true,  
I’ll save you from lands, make you new.  

Quiet and calm, Jacob returns free,  
No fear to be, for I am with thee.  

I’ll end all nations, where you’ve roamed,  
But not you, Jacob, you’ll come home."
