Yo, listen up, here's a tale from the land,  
Before Pharaoh's strike, Jeremiah takes a stand.  

"Behold, waters rise, coming down from the north,  
Overflowing the land, pouring forth with great wrath.  

Men will cry out, wails will fill the air,  
At the sound of hoofbeats, bringing despair.  

Rushing chariots, wheels rumbling loud,  
Fathers too weak, not turning around.  

The day is coming to end the Philistine's fight,  
Cutting off Tyre, leaving Sidon's helpers in fright.  

Yahweh declares the Philistine's fate,  
From Caphtor's isle, there's no escape.  

Gaza's gone bald, Ashkelon's no more,  
How long will your mourning cut to the core?  

"Yahweh's sword, when will you cease?  
Return to the scabbard, find your peace."  

But how can it rest, when Yahweh's command,  
Points against Ashkelon, the seashore's sand?  

The sword is appointed, its path is set,  
No quiet will come till the goal is met.
