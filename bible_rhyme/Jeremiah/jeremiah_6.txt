"Flee for safety, Benjamins, run out of town,  
Blow that trumpet loud, let the warning go around.  

Evil's coming from the north, destruction's in its eyes,  
The delicate daughter of Zion, she cries and sighs.  

Shepherds and their flocks will camp on her ground,  
They’ll pitch tents around her, a siege all around.  

"Prepare for war! Get up, it's noon, and we're late,  
Shadows grow long, but we won't hesitate."  

"By night we’ll rise, destroy her grand halls,  
Her palaces will crumble, her pride will fall."  

Yahweh of Armies says, "Cut down the trees,  
Build a mound 'gainst the city, bring her to her knees.  

Oppression flows like water from a well,  
Violence within her, a never-ending tale to tell."  

"Jerusalem, heed, or be desolate, alone,  
An uninhabited land, where no seeds are sown."  

"They'll glean Israel like grapes off the vine,  
Hand to basket, not leaving a sign."  

"To whom can I speak, who’ll hear my word?  
Their ears are blocked, my voice unheard."  

"I'm weary from holding wrath inside,  
Pour it on the young, there’s nowhere to hide."  

"Houses and fields will be given away,  
For my hand stretches over them today."  

"From least to greatest, they're greedy for gain,  
Prophets and priests, they lie without shame.  

Crying 'Peace, peace!' when there ain't none to find,  
Ashamed? Not at all, they're out of their mind."  

"Stand by the roads, ask for paths of old,  
Find the good way, let your soul be consoled."  

"Listen to the trumpet, watchmen call aloud,  
But they cover their ears, defiant and proud."  

"Listen up nations, hear what’s said,  
Evil's coming, it’s what their thoughts bred."  

"Frankincense and cane, your offerings fall flat,  
I won't accept them, what's up with that?"  

"Stumbling blocks set before father and son,  
Neighbors and friends, all will be undone."  

"A people rise from the north, a nation stirred,  
Cruel and fierce, their war cries heard.  

With bow and spear, they ride like the sea,  
Against you, daughter of Zion, no chance to flee."  

"Hands grow weak, we're gripped by fear,  
Anguish like labor, the danger’s near."  

"Don't go to the fields, stay off the way,  
Enemy swords and terror every day."  

"Put on sackcloth, roll in the ash,  
Cry for the loss, like losing your last."  

"I’ve made you a tester, a fortress to see,  
Their rebellious ways, their slanderous spree."  

"Bellows blow, the lead's in the fire,  
In vain they refine, wickedness won't tire."  

"Rejected silver, that's what they'll be,  
For Yahweh's rejected their deceit and greed."
