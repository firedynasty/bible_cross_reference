In the tenth year of King Zedekiah's reign,  
Jeremiah locked up, prophecy brought him pain.  

Babylon’s army was at Jerusalem’s door,  
Zedekiah asked, "Why spread these tales of war?"  

"Yahweh speaks," Jeremiah did proclaim,  
"This city's Babylon's prize, it's not to remain.  

Zedekiah won't escape, Babylon's king he'll meet,  
Face to face, his eyes he'll greet.  

"They'll take the king to Babylon until I visit," declares Yahweh,  
"Fight all you want, but victory won't stay."  

Jeremiah recounted, "Yahweh's word to me came,  
Hanamel's field in Anathoth, 'twas part of the game.  

'Buy the field,' he said, ‘it's redemption time.’  
When Hanamel approached, I knew it was divine.  

Seventeen shekels weighed, the deal sealed and signed,  
Witnesses gathered, the legal deed aligned.  

Baruch held the deed, in an earthen vessel stored,  
Houses and fields will rise again, declared the Lord."  

Jeremiah prayed, "Ah Lord Yahweh, great is your might,  
You made the heavens and earth, nothing's beyond your sight.  

Loving and kind, to thousands you show,  
Repaying the fathers for deeds long ago.  

Signs in Egypt, your name's renowned,  
You delivered your people with miracles profound.  

You gave them a land, milk and honey it flows,  
Yet they disobeyed, calamity now shows.  

Siege ramps rise, Chaldeans take the city by sword,  
Yet you say, ‘Buy the field,’ oh Lord."  

Then Yahweh spoke once more,  
"Is anything too hard, I'm the God of all, for sure.  

The city to Chaldeans and Nebuchadnezzar will fall,  
They'll burn it down, despite cries and calls.  

From youth, evil in my sight they've done,  
Provoked my anger, now they're on the run.  

But I'll gather them back from lands far and wide,  
They'll dwell safely, with me they'll abide.  

My people they'll be, I'll be their God,  
One heart, one way, they'll fear, they'll laud.  

An everlasting covenant, I’ll not turn away,  
With joy I'll plant them, they’ll forever stay.  

Fields will be bought, deeds signed anew,  
For I will reverse their captivity," declares Yahweh—the truth.
