Pashhur heard Jeremiah, prophet's voice loud and clear,  
But instead of listening, he struck him, put him near.  

In the stocks at the gate, Jeremiah was confined,  
Next day, Pashhur released him, but a new name he'd find.  

Jeremiah spoke, "Your name ain't Pashhur no more,  
It's Magormissabib, 'cause terror's what's in store."  

"Yahweh declares, 'Terror to you and your friends,  
By the sword's cruel edge, Judah's storied line ends.  

In Babylon, they'll carry treasures away,  
And there you'll end, in a foreign land stay.  

All your riches and gains, precious things so fine,  
Into enemy hands, each will now align.  

You and your house, in captivity bound,  
In Babylon's dust, your resting place found.'"  

Jeremiah cried, "Yahweh, you pulled me in tight,  
Stronger than me, you prevail in this fight.  

I've become a laughingstock, mocked every day,  
Crying 'Violence and destruction!' they say.  

His word's in my heart like a fire in my bones,  
I can't hold it in, despite all the groans.  

'Terror on every side!' they whisper and plot,  
Waiting for me to stumble, revenge they sought.  

But Yahweh's with me, mighty and true,  
Persecutors stumble, their shame will accrue.  

Yahweh of Armies, you test deep inside,  
Let me see your vengeance, in you I confide.  

Sing to Yahweh! Praise His mighty hand,  
Saved the needy soul from the evildoers' band.  

Cursed be the day that I first drew breath,  
The birth that brought joy now wishes for death.  

Cursed the man who brought news, 'A boy's born today!'  
In ruined cities, let him ever stay.  

Why, oh why, did I emerge from the womb?  
To face toil and sorrow, a future of gloom."
