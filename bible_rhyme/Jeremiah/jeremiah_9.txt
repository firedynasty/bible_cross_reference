Oh, my head's a waterfall, my eyes a spring of tears,  
Weep night and day for my people's fallen peers.  

Wish I could find a place way out in the wild,  
Leave behind the treacherous, every deceitful child.  

"They bend their tongues like bows, shooting lies,"  
"They grow strong, not for truth, but for evil's ties," Yahweh cries.  

"Watch your neighbor, trust no brother at all,  
They'll supplant and slander, watch you fall."  

Friends lie, truth's a rare thing they speak,  
Lies roll off their tongues, makes them weak.  

"In deceit, they live, refusing to know me,"  
Through treachery, they refuse to see," says He.  

So Yahweh speaks, "I'll melt and test their ways,  
How should I deal with these deceitful days?  

Their tongues are arrows, deadly deceit they spread,  
Speak peace, but in their hearts, ambush lies ahead.  

Shouldn't I punish them?" Yahweh asks with might,  
"Shouldn't My soul find vengeance, make it right?  

I'll weep for the mountains, lament the scorched land,  
The birds and beasts gone, wilderness unmanned.  

Jerusalem, I'll make heaps, jackals their home,  
Judah's cities desolate, where no one will roam."  

Who can understand, who's wise to declare?  
The land's burnt up, like a wilderness, bare.  

Yahweh says, "They've left my laws behind,  
Walked in stubbornness, Baals in their mind."  

"Behold, I'll feed them wormwood's bitter drink,  
Poisoned water, scatter them, making them rethink.  

Call the mourning women, let their cries rise high,  
Teach daughters lamentation, let tears never dry."  

Death's crawling in windows, invading palaces grand,  
Cutting off children, young men in the land.  

Yahweh's word, hear it loud and clear,  
"Dead bodies like dung, no one will come near."  

"Don't the wise boast in wisdom, or the mighty in might,  
Or the rich in riches, for I am the light."  

"Glory in knowing Me, kindness and justice I seek,  
Righteousness on earth, it's my delight," Yahweh speaks.  

"Behold, days will come," Yahweh says with power,  
"I'll punish the circumcised only in outward hour.  

Egypt, Judah, Edom, Ammon, Moab will learn,  
For all uncircumcised in heart, my wrath will burn."
