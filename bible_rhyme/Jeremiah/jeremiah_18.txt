Yahweh spoke, "Jeremiah, rise and go,  
To the potter's house, where my words will flow."  
Down I went, saw the potter at his wheel,  
Crafting clay vessels, his skill was real.  

The vessel marred, in his hands it lay,  
But he molded anew, in his own way.  
Then Yahweh spoke, "House of Israel, see,  
Just like this potter, you are clay to me.  

I can break or build, pluck or plant,  
Turn from evil, or face what you can't.  
But if you disobey, turn from the light,  
The good I planned will vanish from sight.  

Tell Judah and Jerusalem my plan,  
"Return from your ways, change while you can."  
But they say, ‘It's useless, we’ll follow our heart,’  
Each stubbornly walking their own evil part.  

Yahweh questions, “Among nations, who’s seen  
Such betrayal, such a terrible scene?  
Snow in Lebanon doesn’t fail to fall,  
Yet my people forget me, they heed no call.  

They burn incense to gods that are fake,  
Walk twisted paths, ignoring what's at stake.  
Their land will be ruined, a hissing sound,  
Passersby shaking their heads all around.  

Scattered by the east wind, enemies near,  
I’ll turn my back, calamity’s fear.”  
They plot against me, "Let's strike him, they say,  
Jeremiah's words, let's keep them at bay."

"Yahweh, hear me, they scheme my demise,  
I stood for them, tried to open their eyes.  
Yet they’ve repaid me with a pit of despair,  
Now let their cries echo in the air.  

Let their children face famine, and swords take their men,  
Their plots against me bring judgment again.  
Don’t forgive their sins, let them face your might,  
Deal with them, Yahweh, in your anger's sight."
