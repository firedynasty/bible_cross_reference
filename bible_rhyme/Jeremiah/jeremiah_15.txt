"Even Moses and Samuel can't change my mind,   
This people must go, leave them all behind.  

When they ask, 'Where do we go?' you'll reply,   
'Yahweh says: Death, sword, famine, or captivity's sky.'  

Four kinds I appoint," Yahweh declares,   
"Sword to kill, dogs to tear, birds and beasts everywhere.  

Tossed among kingdoms for Manasseh's crime,   
Jerusalem's fate sealed in his wicked time.  

Who will weep for you, who will care?   
You've rejected me, you've got despair.  

I've stretched my hand, now you're destroyed,   
My compassion's gone, I'm annoyed.  

Winnowed at the gates, bereaved of kin,   
Destruction's here, won't turn from sin.  

Widows multiplied like sand by the sea,   
Noonday destroyer, terror suddenly.  

Seven's mother weak, her spirit departed,   
Sun sets early, dreams thwarted, disheartened.  

Woe is me, born into strife,   
Cursed though I lent neither debt nor life.  

But Yahweh says, "I'll make you strong,   
Enemies will beg when things go wrong.  

Can iron be broken, iron and bronze?   
Your treasures plundered, sins' response.  

Enemies will take you to lands unknown,   
My fire burns, my anger's grown."  

Remember me, Yahweh, don't forget,   
Patiently I wait, don't leave me yet.  

Your words are joy, they fill my heart,   
I'm called by your name, set apart.  

I didn't join in joy, alone I stayed,   
Filled with your anger, indignation's blade.  

Why's my pain unending, wound unhealed?   
Will you be like a brook, waters concealed?  

Yahweh says, "Return, I bring you back,   
Separate the precious, stay on track.  

I'll make you a wall, bronze fortified,   
They'll fight but won't conquer, I'm by your side.  

Deliverance from wicked hands,   
Redeemed from terrors, Yahweh stands."
