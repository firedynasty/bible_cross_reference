Yahweh spoke, and it was clear to me,  
"Don't marry here or let your children be."  
For those born and raised in this land's embrace,  
Will meet grim fates, with no memorial trace.  

"They'll die without tears, unburied they'll lay,  
Like waste on the ground, swept away.  
By sword and famine they'll find their doom,  
Feast for the birds, 'neath the sky's wide room."  

Yahweh said, "Don't mourn or wail,  
I've pulled back my peace, my love will fail.  
Great and small will perish here,  
No one will mourn or shed a tear.  

No bread of comfort, no solace to find,  
For mother or father, they're left behind.  
Don't join the feasts, don't eat or drink,  
Stay away from joy, let your heart sink."  

Yahweh of Armies declares with might,  
"I'll silence joy from day to night.  
Bride and groom's voices will fade away,  
In your sight, in your day."  

When they ask, "Why such doom and gloom?"  
Tell them, "For forsaking me, they sealed their doom.  
Chasing gods not of me, they strayed,  
Ignoring my law, my voice betrayed."  

"You've done worse than those who came before,  
Following evil hearts, you shut the door.  
I'll cast you to lands unknown,  
There you'll serve gods made of stone."  

But Yahweh says, "One day will come,  
When talk of Egypt's deliverance is done.  
Instead, they'll speak of the north's escape,  
And return to the land that was once their shape."  

"I'll send fishermen to gather you back,  
Hunters will find you from every crack.  
My eyes see all, nothing's concealed,  
Their sins will be doubled, their fate revealed."  

Yahweh, my strength, my refuge you are,  
Nations will come from lands afar.  
"Our fathers' lies we'll now disown,  
Crafted gods of wood and stone."  

"Behold, I’ll make them see,  
My power, my hand, my identity.  
Then they'll know my name,  
Yahweh's the same."
