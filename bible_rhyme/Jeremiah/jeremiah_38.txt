Shephatiah and the crew, they heard the news,  
Jeremiah spoke truth, and it shook their views.  
"Yahweh says, stay here and face your doom,  
But head to the Chaldeans, and escape the gloom."

"This city's handed over," Yahweh declared,  
To Babylon's army, the king prepared.  
The princes said, "Let's put this man down,  
He's causing fear, spreading panic in town."

King Zedekiah sighed, "He's in your hand,  
I can't fight you, I can't command."  
Into the dungeon, they let Jeremiah fall,  
No water, just mire, he sank with it all.

Ebedmelech the Ethiopian heard the plight,  
Went to the king, said, "This isn't right.  
Jeremiah's cast in, with famine and dread,  
Without rescue, soon he'll be dead."

The king ordered, "Take thirty men, go quick,  
Lift him from the dungeon, don't let him stick."  
With rags and cords, they pulled him high,  
Jeremiah's safe, no longer to die.

Zedekiah called for Jeremiah's word,  
"Tell me the truth, let it be heard."  
But Jeremiah feared, "Will you take my life?  
You won't listen, just cause more strife."

Yet Zedekiah swore, "As Yahweh lives,  
Your life, I won't give."  
Jeremiah spoke, "The Lord's decree:  
Go to Babylon's prince, your life will be free."

"But if not, this city will burn,  
Chaldeans win, and you'll have no turn."  
Zedekiah feared the defectors' scorn,  
But Jeremiah urged him, "Obey, be reborn."

"Refuse, and ruin's what Yahweh showed,  
Your house will crumble, down that road."  
Zedekiah pleaded, "Let no one know,  
If they ask, tell them where you wish to go."

The princes came, asked their things,  
Jeremiah spoke as advised by the king.  
He stayed in the guard, his patience vast,  
Until Jerusalem's fall came at last.
