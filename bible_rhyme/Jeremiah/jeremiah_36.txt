In Jehoiakim's fourth year, a word came clear,  
"Jeremiah, write my words for all to hear.  

On a scroll, inscribe what I’ve said in the past,  
'Bout Israel, Judah, nations, warnings vast.  

Maybe Judah will heed, turn from sin's dark thread,  
If they change their ways, I’ll forgive them,’ God said.  

Jeremiah called Baruch, his scribe with skill,  
Baruch penned God’s words, Jeremiah's voice to fill.  

Jeremiah instructed, "I’m bound, can't enter there,  
So you read Yahweh’s words, let the people be aware.  

On the fast day, read aloud in Yahweh's holy space,  
Maybe they’ll seek mercy, return to God’s grace."  

Baruch followed through, did just as he was told,  
In Yahweh's house, the words of wrath were bold.  

In the fifth year, ninth month, a fast was declared,  
Judah’s people gathered, God’s message shared.  

Baruch stood and read in the house up high,  
Gemariah's room, beneath the clear blue sky.  

Micaiah heard the words, urgency in his stride,  
To the king’s house he went, the news couldn’t hide.  

Princes gathered round, scribes and noble men,  
Micaiah shared the words, told them what and when.  

They called for Baruch, "Bring the scroll you read,  
Let us hear these words that make hearts dread."  

In their presence, Baruch read each line,  
Fear sparked in their eyes, a message so divine.  

"We’ll tell the king," they vowed, trembling with fright,  
"Baruch, how did you write?" they asked that night.  

"Jeremiah spoke, I wrote it down," Baruch replied,  
With ink upon the scroll, he couldn't hide.  

"Go, hide yourselves," the princes then implored,  
"Stay out of sight, don’t be ignored."  

They took the scroll to the king’s winter den,  
But kept it aside, told the king again.  

The king sent Jehudi, "Bring the scroll, let’s see,"  
He read before the king, beside the blazing heat.  

As Jehudi read, the king with knife in hand,  
Cut the scroll, fed it to the fire as planned.  

No fear in their hearts, no garments torn,  
Despite the warnings, contempt was born.  

Elnathan pleaded, "Please, don't burn this scroll,"  
The king wouldn’t listen, stubborn his soul.  

He ordered arrests, Jeremiah and Baruch,  
But Yahweh hid them, their capture forsook.  

Then God spoke again, once the scroll burned away,  
"Write once more, Jeremiah, what I have to say.  

Tell of Jehoiakim’s fate, for burning My word,  
No son on his throne, his end shall be heard.  

Punishment will come, for servants and kin,  
The evil declared, for ignoring their sin."  

Jeremiah took another scroll, Baruch wrote anew,  
The same words of warning, and more God did imbue.
