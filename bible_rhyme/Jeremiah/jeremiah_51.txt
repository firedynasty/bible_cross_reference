"Look around," Yahweh declares, "Babylon's got a storm ahead,  
Raising winds, strangers come, leaving her land empty, dead.  

Bend your bows, archers, aim, destroy her young and strong,  
Every street sees the fall, Chaldeans' spirits gone.  

Israel's not forgotten, no, nor Judah left all alone,  
Though they stumble in guilt, Yahweh still calls them His own.  

"Escape now, flee fast, save your life from Babylon's sin,  
Yahweh’s vengeance falls, her payback will begin.  

Once a golden cup, now nations drunk and mad,  
Suddenly she’s fallen, heal her if you can, feel sad.  

"We tried to save her, but her wounds are too deep,  
Judgment rises to the skies, leave her, don’t weep.  

"Sharpen arrows, hold your shields, the Medes stand tall,  
Yahweh's purpose is vengeance, Babylon's great fall.  

"Set up a standard, guard the walls tight,  
Watchmen alert, ambush ready, Babylon’s long night.  

Your waters rich, treasures vast, end now you must face,  
Yahweh swore to fill you with cries, in each desolate place.  

"He made the earth with power, heavens stretched wide,  
Roaring skies, lightning, wind from His side.  

"Fools in the land worship images of stone,  
They hold no breath, their hopes are alone.  

"Jacob's portion, Yahweh’s name, breaks nations in hand,  
Battle axe of justice, laying waste to every land.  

"With you I'll shatter kingdoms, chariots too,  
Older and younger, all will break with you.  

"Babylon, your mountains burn, desolate you’ll be,  
No stone, no foundation, only Yahweh’s decree.  

"Sound the trumpet, nations prepare,  
Ararat, Minni, join Ashkenaz there.  

The land shakes, Yahweh's plans hold fast,  
Mighty men fail, their strength doesn't last.  

Fire in her dwellings, runners bear the news,  
Babylon seized, her warriors confused.  

"Zion cries out, Babylon devoured,  
Nebuchadnezzar's wreckage, hope turned sour.  

Vengeance is Yahweh's, drying seas, fountains dry,  
Jackals dwell in heaps, under Yahweh’s watchful eye.  

"Lions roar, then fall to sleep,  
Their feast made drunk, eternal rest they keep.  

Babylon’s waves engulf, her cities dry and bare,  
Judgment falls on Bel, nations no longer care.  

"Go from her midst, my people, flee,  
Fear not the news, trust in me.  

"Her towers high, but destroyers rise,  
Babylon cries as Yahweh's judgment flies.  

Princes, wise men, and mighty fall,  
Drunken sleep consumes them all.  

"Wide walls of Babylon crash, gates burn down,  
Nations toil for vanity, Yahweh's the only crown.  

Jeremiah wrote the woes, to Babylon they’d come,  
A stone to the book, in the Euphrates it’s done.  

"Babylon sinks, never to rise again,  
Evil brought upon her, weary till the end."
