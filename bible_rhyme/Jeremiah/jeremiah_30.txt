Listen up, Jeremiah, hear what Yahweh’s sayin’,  
"Write it all down, every word I'm relayin'.  

"The days will come when I’ll flip the scene,  
Bring back my people to where they’ve been.  

Fear and trembling, voices of dread,  
But in the end, they'll rise from the dead.  

Why do I see men holding their waist?  
Faces pale, like they've been chased.  

A day unlike any, Jacob's in trouble,  
But he’ll be saved, make that hope double.  

"On that day, I’ll break your chains,  
No more strangers with their reigns.  

Serve Yahweh, and David will rise,  
Don't fear, Jacob, no more cries.  

I'll save you from afar, your kids too,  
Bring you back home, safe and true.  

I'm with you, says Yahweh, your guide,  
Nations fall, but you'll abide.  

Your hurt's deep, wounds severe,  
Nobody to help, no cure near.  

Lovers forgot you, left you alone,  
Your sins piled up, like a heavy stone.  

Why weep over pain that won't go?  
For your wrongs, the punishment flows.  

Those who devour will be devoured,  
Adversaries captured, no more empowered.  

Health I’ll restore, heal those wounds,  
Zion the outcast, now renewed.  

"I’ll bring back Jacob's tents, show compassion,  
City rebuilt, in glorious fashion.  

Thankful songs from voices raised,  
Multiply them, be amazed.  

Kids like before, strong congregation,  
Oppressors punished, no hesitation.  

A prince from them, a ruler so bold,  
Draws near to me, hearts of gold.  

"You’ll be my people, I’ll be your God,  
Watch Yahweh’s storm, striking hard.  

His wrath won’t rest till it’s done,  
Understand it all when it's begun."
