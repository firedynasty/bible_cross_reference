Yahweh's word came down, Jeremiah in sight,  
Judah mourns, gates weep in the night.  

On the ground they sit, dressed in black,  
Crying out, Jerusalem's lacking slack.  

Nobles send kids to seek the water's flow,  
Cisterns empty, heads hung low.  

Ground cracked, no rain in sight,  
Plowmen's hope takes flight.  

Doe in the field, leaves young behind,  
No grass grows, the earth's unkind.  

Wild donkeys stand on barren height,  
Pant like jackals, eyes lose light.  

"Our sins scream out, but for your name's grace,  
Savior of Israel, don't turn your face."  

"Why appear like a stranger to us,  
A traveler who won't stay, causing fuss?"  

Yahweh in the midst, we're called by His name,  
"Don't leave us," we plead, "it's not the same."  

Yahweh says, "They love to wander,  
Feet unrestrained, hearts grow fonder.  

I won't accept them, their iniquity's clear,  
Punishment comes, it's drawing near."  

"Don't pray for their good, I won't hear,  
Fasting or offering, none'll draw me near."  

Sword, famine, pestilence, Yahweh's decree,  
False prophets speak lies, misleading spree.  

"Visions they claim, I did not send,  
Deception in hearts, false messages blend."  

Prophets, people alike, streets will fill,  
No burial for them, wickedness spill.  

"I'll let tears flow, night and day,  
Virgin daughter broken, hope in dismay."  

Fields filled with slain, cities with grief,  
Prophet and priest walk in disbelief.  

"Utter rejection? Zion's wounds won't heal,  
Hope for peace vanishes, truth is revealed."  

"Acknowledge our sins, Yahweh, we see,  
Don't break your covenant, set us free.  

No nation's idols bring rain from the sky,  
Yahweh our God, hear our cry."  

Therefore we wait for what Yahweh can bring,  
He alone makes life through everything.
