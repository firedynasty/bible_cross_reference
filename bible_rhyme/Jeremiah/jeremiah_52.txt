Zedekiah's reign began at twenty-one, but his deeds were wrong,  
Did evil in Yahweh’s sight, like Jehoiakim's song.  

God’s anger flared, Jerusalem faced His decree,  
Zedekiah rebelled against Babylon, seeking to be free.  

Ninth year, tenth month, tenth day, Babylon came to play,  
Nebuchadnezzar’s army encamped, made Jerusalem their prey.  

The city besieged, held tight until year eleven,  
Famine hit hard, bread less than a raven.  

A breach in the walls, men of war took flight,  
Through the garden gate they slipped, vanishing into night.  

The Chaldeans pursued, caught Zedekiah in Jericho's plain,  
His troops scattered, only fear and pain.  

Captured and brought before Babylon’s king,  
Judgment pronounced, what suffering did bring.  

Zedekiah's sons slain before his very eyes,  
Princes of Judah met their demise.  

His eyes put out, bound and led away,  
To Babylon’s prison, there he’d stay.  

Fifth month, tenth day, Nebuzaradan arrived,  
Jerusalem’s houses burned, none survived.  

Yahweh’s house in flames, kings’ houses consumed,  
Chaldeans broke walls, all hope was doomed.  

Captives taken, poorest remained to toil the land,  
Vineyard keepers and farmers, by Babylon's command.  

Bronze pillars, bases, and sea, they took it all,  
From Yahweh’s house, treasures did fall.  

Vessels of gold and silver, every piece they seized,  
The spoons, bowls, and stands, not a single one appeased.  

Pillars tall and strong, pomegranates adorned with care,  
Eighteen cubits high, bronze capitals rare.  

Chief priests and officials, Nebuzaradan took away,  
To Riblah they were brought, where judgment held sway.  

Judah’s people carried off, four thousand six hundred gone,  
Captivity spread, Jerusalem’s dawn forlorn.  

Jehoiachin released, kindness in Babylon’s land,  
Changed his garments, with honor did stand.  

Daily allowance he received, his life restored,  
Set above kings, by Babylon adored.  

All his days he ate, a portion granted till the end,  
A tale of captivity, but also of a friend.
