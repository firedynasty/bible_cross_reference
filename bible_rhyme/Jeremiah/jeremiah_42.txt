Captains and people, from least to the great,  
Came to Jeremiah, with worries so weight.  
"Pray to Yahweh for this remnant, so few,  
Show us the path, the things we should do."  

Jeremiah replied, "I hear your plea,  
I'll pray to Yahweh, wait and see.  
Whatever He speaks, I'll tell you straight,  
Not a word will I keep, no delay, no wait."  

They swore to obey, come good or bad,  
Yahweh’s witness, in this they were glad.  
"Whether it’s tough, or makes us feel free,  
We'll follow Yahweh, so well it will be."  

Ten days passed, Yahweh spoke at last,  
Jeremiah called them, no time to contrast.  
"Yahweh says, stay in this land, I'll build,  
Not tear you down, your hopes fulfilled."  

"Don’t fear Babylon, I’ll save you,  
I'll grant mercy, help you through.  
Return to your land, don't break the plan,  
Stay in this place, where you began."  

"But if you say, 'To Egypt we go,'  
No war, no sound of trumpet, no woe.  
Hear Yahweh's word, Judah’s remnant,  
In Egypt, death awaits, your fears sent."  

"Sword and famine will chase you down,  
In Egypt's land, you’ll wear a frown.  
For Yahweh’s wrath will outpour,  
As Jerusalem saw, you'll see it more."  

"Don’t go to Egypt," Yahweh declared,  
"You’ve been warned, you should have cared.  
Deceit against your souls you’ve shown,  
Prayed for a word, yet truth's unknown."  

"I’ve told you today, but you've not heard,  
Yahweh's commands, not a single word.  
You'll die by sword, famine, pestilence near,  
In the place you desire, where you steer."
