Word to Jeremiah, came straight from Yahweh,  
"Go to Rechabites, bring them, hear what they say.  

Invite them in, offer wine in Yahweh’s house,  
Test their resolve, see if they stick to their vows."  

So I gathered Jaazaniah, the whole Rechab crew,  
Led them to the room, where the sons of Hanan grew.  

Bowls full of wine, I set before their eyes,  
Said, "Drink up, enjoy, don’t be shy."  

But they replied, "No wine will touch our lips,  
Jonadab, our father, gave us these tips:  

'No wine, no fields, no homes will we claim,  
In tents we dwell, nomads in name.'"  

Obeyed Jonadab, their father’s command,  
No wine, no homes, just tents on this land.  

But Babylon’s king brought fear to the land,  
They said, "To Jerusalem, take a stand."  

Yahweh spoke again, His words to share,  
"Judah, listen up, do you even care?  

Jonadab’s words, his sons they obey,  
But you ignore Me, day after day.  

Prophets I sent, calling you back,  
Turn from your ways, but you’ve kept off track.  

Jonadab’s sons, they honor and heed,  
Yet you, My people, won’t listen, won’t lead."  

"So watch out, Judah, hear the alarm,  
Evil comes your way, see the harm.  

Rechabites stand firm, obey and stay true,  
And for this loyalty, here’s what I’ll do:  

Their line will remain, forever they’ll stand,  
Before Me, Yahweh, their legacy planned."
