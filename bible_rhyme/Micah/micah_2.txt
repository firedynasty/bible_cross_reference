Woe to the schemers, plotting evil in bed,  
When morning comes, they act out what's in their head.  

They covet fields, they take what they see,  
Oppressing a man, his home, and his legacy.  

Yahweh speaks: "Behold, disaster's in line,  
You'll bow your necks in this evil time."  

A lament will rise, a doleful cry,  
"We're ruined! Our fields, taken by and by."  

No one to divide the land in Yahweh's space,  
"Don't prophesy," they say, "no disgrace will we face."  

House of Jacob, say it clear:  
"Is Yahweh angry? Do my words bring fear?"  

But my people rise as an enemy,  
Stripping robes from passersby in their spree.  

Driving women from homes, taking blessings from the young,  
Arise, depart! This land's corruption is sung.  

False prophets speak of wine and dreams,  
They're the voices this people deems.  

"I'll gather Jacob, every last one,  
Like sheep in Bozrah, when the day is done."  

The way breaker leads, they break through the gate,  
Their king before them, with Yahweh innate.
