Listen up now, leaders of Jacob's crew,  
Isn't justice what you're supposed to pursue?  
But you love what's evil, despise what's right,  
Tearing off skin, devouring with might.  

You eat my people like they're meat for your pot,  
Break their bones, leave them to rot.  
You’ll cry to Yahweh, but he’ll turn away,  
Hide his face, 'cause you led astray.  

Prophets lie, lead my people blind,  
For a bite to eat, they proclaim, "All's fine!"  
But if you don’t feed them, they’ll wage war,  
Darkness falls, visions no more.  

"The seers are lost, the diviners confused,  
Lips sealed tight, no answer for the abused."  
But me, I'm filled with Yahweh’s might,  
To declare Jacob's sins, and bring truth to light.  

Hear this, rulers, who twist the laws,  
Build Zion with blood, ignoring flaws.  
Judges take bribes, priests speak for a fee,  
Prophets lie for cash, claiming, "Safe are we."  

But Zion will be plowed, Jerusalem a heap,  
The temple's mount a forest, dark and deep.
