Misery's mine, picking fruit in vain,  
No grapes on the vine, just empty pain.  

The godly vanish, none upright in sight,  
Hunting each other, they fight through the night.  

Hands on evil, bribes rule the land,  
Rulers conspire, their wicked plans stand.  

Best of them's a brier, thorns in their soul,  
Watchmen's day comes, confusion takes its toll.  

"Don't trust your neighbor, don't lean on a friend,  
Mind your words, even with those you depend."  

Sons against fathers, daughters against mothers,  
Enemies at home, even among brothers.  

But as for me, I look to Yahweh above,  
Wait for salvation, His light and His love.  

"Don't gloat, my foe, for I'll rise when I fall,  
In darkness, Yahweh shines, His light on us all."  

I'll bear His wrath, for I've sinned 'neath His view,  
Till He pleads my case, His justice rings true.  

Enemies see, their shame covers their face,  
Trod like street mire, they fall from their place.  

Walls will rise, boundaries extend wide,  
From Assyria to Egypt, God's people abide.  

Lands desolate, fruits of deeds take their toll,  
Shepherd Your flock, guide each wandering soul.  

"As in Egypt's days, My wonders I'll show,  
Nations will quake, their strength laid low."  

Dust they'll lick, serpents bent in dread,  
Trembling before Yahweh, their fear widespread.  

Who pardons like You, passes over our wrong,  
Loving kindness His delight, His anger not long.  

He'll tread sins down, cast them far away,  
Truth to Jacob, mercy to stay.  

Sworn to our fathers, from ancient times heard,  
Compassion flows, faithful to His word.
