Troops are gathering, the siege is on,  
Israel's judge gets struck, the battle's begun.  

But Bethlehem Ephrathah, you're small but grand,  
A ruler comes forth, ancient, destined to stand.  

He'll leave them till the labor brings forth birth,  
Then brothers will return, reclaim their worth.  

He'll stand and shepherd with Yahweh's might,  
In His name's majesty, they'll live in the light.  

Peace he'll bring when Assyria invades,  
Seven shepherds rise, eight leaders in spades.  

They'll wield the sword in Assyria's lands,  
Deliverance at the borders, wielding their hands.  

Jacob's remnant, like dew on grass,  
Silent blessings, no need to ask.  

Like lions in forests, fierce and bold,  
Through nations they move, strong and untold.  

"Your hand will rise, adversaries fall,  
Enemies gone, hear Yahweh's call:  

'I’ll cut your horses, chariots too,  
Destroy your cities, all you knew.  

No more witchcraft, no soothsayers here,  
Images and pillars, I’ll make them disappear.  

Uproot your poles, tear down your town,  
Vengeance on nations, my wrath comes down.'"
