"Rise up," He says, "take your place,  
Let mountains and hills hear your case.  
Listen up, ancient stones and earth,  
Yahweh's got a word, a rebirth.  

People, what have I done to you?  
How did I burden, what did I do?  
I took you from Egypt's chains,  
Through Moses, Aaron, Miriam's reign.  

Recall Balak's wicked plot,  
And Balaam's words, the lesson taught.  
From Shittim to Gilgal, see,  
The righteous acts of Yahweh, free.  

How should I stand before the Lord?  
With calves, or gifts I can't afford?  
Thousands of rams, rivers of oil,  
Firstborn for sins that spoil?  

He's shown what’s good, plain to see,  
Act justly, love mercy, walk humbly.  

Yahweh calls, hear wisdom’s song,  
"Listen to the rod that keeps you strong.  
Do you hide treasures cursed and sly?  
Or measure with scales that lie?  

Rich with violence, deceit on their lips,  
Their words are twisted, truth just slips.  
So I've struck you down, pain's my tool,  
Desolation comes, sin's cruel.  

You'll eat, but hunger stays,  
Store up, but it decays.  
Sow the seeds, but reap won't come,  
Olives pressed, yet oil's numb.  

You follow Omri and Ahab's way,  
A path to ruin, in dismay.  
Hissing whispers of disgrace,  
Bearing reproach, you lose face."
