Micah got the word, in the days of the kings,  
For Samaria and Jerusalem, truth it brings.  

"Listen, all you people, hear the earth proclaim,  
Yahweh from His temple, calling out your name."  

Look, Yahweh's stepping down from His holy place,  
Treads on the high mountains, earth shakes in His face.  

Mountains melt like wax, valleys split and part,  
Like water pouring down, nature plays its part.  

"For the sins of Jacob, Samaria's the cause,  
Judah's high places, breaking all My laws."  

"Samaria, I'll turn you to rubble and stone,  
Vines will claim your ground, foundations overthrown."  

"Idols crushed to pieces, temple gifts in flame,  
Prostitution’s wage, returns from whence it came."  

So I lament, I wail, stripped and bare, I cry,  
Howl like jackals, moan as night owls fly.  

Her wounds are deep, incurable the pain,  
From her gates to Judah, trouble's in the lane.  

"Don't speak it in Gath, don't shed a tear,  
In Beth Ophrah, dust rolls with fear."  

Shaphir's folks walk in shame, Zaanan won't appear,  
Beth Ezel's cry rings out, no help is near.  

Maroth waits in hope, but evil descends,  
From Yahweh's gate, Jerusalem it bends.  

"Lachish, harness swift steeds, you're the sin's start,  
Israel's transgressions found, tearing it apart."  

"Gifts to Moresheth Gath, deceit in Achzib's place,  
Mareshah, I’ll send glory, Adullam brace."  

"Shave your heads in mourning, hair falls to the floor,  
Mourn for your children, captive evermore."
