In the days ahead, Yahweh's mountain will rise,  
Above all peaks, reaching high to the skies.  

Nations will say, "Let's climb, let's seek,  
To God's house we'll go, his wisdom we'll speak."  

From Zion the law, from Jerusalem his word,  
He'll judge the peoples, his voice will be heard.  

Swords into plowshares, spears to pruning hooks,  
No more war, just peace in the books.  

Under the vine, under the fig tree they’ll stay,  
No fear in their hearts, Yahweh’s spoken today.  

Others may follow gods of their own,  
But we'll walk with Yahweh, we're never alone.  

"In that day," Yahweh declares with might,  
"I'll gather the lame, bring them to the light.  

Though they were scattered, they’ll come back strong,  
Yahweh will reign forever, where they belong."  

To the tower, the flock, Zion's hill so high,  
The kingdom will return, as time goes by.  

Why do you cry, is your king now gone?  
Is pain gripping you, like a laboring dawn?  

Endure the pain, like birth pangs anew,  
You'll leave the city, to fields you'll pursue.  

In Babylon's land, rescue is near,  
Yahweh will redeem you from all you fear.  

Nations gather, eyes set to defile,  
But they don't know Yahweh, his plans are worthwhile.  

"Arise and thresh, daughter of Zion, make haste,  
With iron horns and bronze hoofs, no time to waste.  

Break the nations, their wealth I'll claim,  
For Yahweh, Lord of earth, in his holy name."
