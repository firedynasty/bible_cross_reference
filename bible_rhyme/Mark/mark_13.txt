Out the temple, they gazed at stones so grand,  
"Teacher, look at these buildings, all across the land!"  
Jesus replied, "These stones won't stand,  
Not one on another, they'll be thrown by hand."  

On the Mount of Olives, sitting in peace,  
Peter, James, John, and Andrew seeking release,  
"Tell us, when will this all unfold?  
What's the sign of the end, when stories are told?"  

Jesus warned, "Be cautious, don't stray,  
Many will come, saying, 'I'm the way!'  
You'll hear of wars, the rumors of fights,  
But that's not the end, just the dimming of lights."  

"Nation 'gainst nation, kingdoms will clash,  
Earthquakes and famines, a troublesome crash.  
These are the birth pains, so be aware,  
For councils will call you, they'll drag you there."  

"You'll stand beaten, rulers at your side,  
For my sake, before kings, your truth will abide.  
The Good News goes to every nation,  
So don't worry 'bout your oration."  

"When brought to trial, don't rehearse your plea,  
The Spirit speaks, not you, you'll see.  
Families torn, brother betrays brother,  
Children rise 'gainst father and mother."  

"Hated for my name, yet those who endure,  
Will find salvation, their hearts pure.  
See the abomination, in the wrong place,  
Run to the mountains, make haste, no trace."  

"Don't descend from rooftops, don't turn back,  
Pray your escape isn't in winter's track.  
Oppression will be like none before,  
God shortens the days, or none would endure."  

"False christs and prophets, with signs in sight,  
Even the chosen might lose their light.  
Stay alert, I've warned you clear,  
The sun and moon will disappear."  

"Stars will fall, the sky will shake,  
Then comes the Son, with glory at stake.  
Angels will gather, the chosen from afar,  
From earth's ends to heaven's spar."  

"The fig tree speaks, it shows the way,  
Tender branches, summer's at bay.  
When these signs come, know it's near,  
This generation will see it clear."  

"Heaven and earth might fade away,  
But my words, they forever stay.  
Of that day, none can know,  
Only the Father, no angel, no show."  

"Stay awake, alert, and pray,  
The time's unknown, don't delay.  
Like a man who travels, leaves his home,  
Servants watch, not alone."  

"Watch, for you don’t know when he’ll come back,  
Evening, midnight, the rooster's track.  
Lest he finds you fast asleep,  
Stay vigilant, and watch, keep."
