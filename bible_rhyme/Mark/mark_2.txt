Back in Capernaum, the buzz was real,  
The house was packed, no room to steal.  
Four friends arrived with a man on a mat,  
But through the crowd? Nah, no chance of that.  

So up they climbed, the roof they tore,  
Lowered their friend right to the floor.  
Jesus saw their faith, the love they gave,  
"Son, your sins are forgiven, you're saved."  

Scribes were puzzled, hearts full of doubt,  
"Who can forgive? What's this about?"  
Jesus knew their thoughts, their silent fight,  
"Which is easier, to heal or forgive tonight?"  

"But know this," he said, with authority clear,  
"The Son of Man can forgive right here."  
To the man on the mat, he turned his gaze,  
"Get up, take your mat, walk away amazed."  

He stood up quick, mat in his hand,  
The crowd was shocked, like, "A miracle's at hand!"  
They praised God, awe in their eyes,  
"We've never seen wonders that defy!"  

By the sea, Jesus walked, the crowd would grow,  
Saw Levi at the booth, called him to go.  
"Follow me," he said, and Levi complied,  
Left his post, walked right by his side.  

At Levi's table, Jesus reclined,  
With sinners and tax men, all intertwined.  
Pharisees whispered, puzzled and sore,  
"Why eat with them? What for?"  

Jesus heard, replied with care,  
"The sick need a doctor, not those who fare.  
I came for sinners, not those who think they're right,  
To call them back, bring them to the light."  

Questions on fasting, they came his way,  
"Why don't your disciples fast today?"  
"The groom's here now, let the party last,  
But the time will come when they will fast."  

"New cloth on old, it won't stay,  
New wine in old skins, bursts away.  
Fresh for fresh, new for new,  
New ways for the things I do."  

Through the fields on Sabbath they trod,  
Plucking grain, the Pharisees thought it odd.  
"Why break the law?" they asked, annoyed,  
Rules of Sabbath, they wished to avoid.  

Jesus spoke, bringing insight,  
"Remember David in his plight?  
Ate the bread meant for priests alone,  
When hunger hit, broke tradition's stone."  

"The Sabbath's made for man, a gift, a nod.  
The Son of Man is Lord, even over Sabbath's sod."  
With those words, he captured the day,  
A new understanding, a new way.
