By the seaside, he taught, crowds so immense,  
He got in a boat, gave the sea his sense.  

"Listen up now, a farmer went to sow,  
Seeds hit the road, devoured by birds, you know.  

Some hit the rocks, sprang up without deep trace,  
Sun beat 'em down, no roots, they lost the race.  

Others hit thorns, choked out, no fruit would show,  
Yet some found good ground, and oh how they'd grow!  

Thirty, sixty, a hundred-fold they shine,  
Got ears? Then hear, let the truth align."  

Alone with his crew, questions they did bring,  
He spoke of mysteries, truth was the thing.  

"The farmer sows word, the road ones don't see,  
Satan snatches it quick, like the birds, you see.  

Rocky ones hear with joy, but shallow they stay,  
Persecution hits, their faith fades away.  

Thorny ground hears, but the world chokes it cold,  
Riches and desires, the spirit gets sold.  

Good ground hears and bears fruit, it's understood,  
Thirty, sixty, a hundred, like they should."  

He asked, "Does a lamp hide under a bed?  
It's meant for a stand, to shine light instead.  

What you hear, take heed, measure it well,  
What you give, you get, I tell you, hear and dwell.  

Kingdom's like seed, cast on earth, watch it grow,  
Man sleeps and wakes, how it sprouts, he don't know.  

First the blade, then the ear, grain full-grown,  
When ripe, it's harvest time, reap what's sown.  

God's Kingdom's a mustard seed, tiny in the land,  
Sown, it grows mightier than you understand.  

Branches spread wide, birds dwell in its shade,  
With parables he taught, truth was displayed.  

Crossing the sea, evening had drawn near,  
In the boat, with small boats, they held him dear.  

A storm hit, waves crashed, filled up their ride,  
Jesus slept, they woke him, fear spread wide.  

"Teacher, don't you care we're going down?" they cried,  
He rose, rebuked the wind, "Peace, be still," he replied.  

The storm ceased, calm took over the scene,  
"Why fear?" he asked, "No faith in what's been?"  

Amazed they whispered, "Who is this, can you see?  
Even the wind and sea bow to his decree."
