"Some here won't taste death," Jesus did declare,  
"Till God's Kingdom comes with power, beyond compare."

Six days passed, Jesus took Peter, James, and John,  
Up the mountain high, where the light of heaven shone.

He transformed before them, clothes glistening bright,  
Whiter than snow, stunning in their sight.

Elijah and Moses appeared, talking with Him there,  
Peter, overwhelmed, offered tents with great care.

A cloud overshadowed, and a voice did say,  
"This is my beloved Son. Listen to Him today."

Suddenly, they saw Jesus standing alone,  
Descending the mountain, secrets to postpone.

"Tell no one till the Son of Man rises from the dead,"  
Confused by His words, questions filled their head.

"Why must Elijah come first?" they sought to understand,  
He spoke of sufferings, part of the greater plan.

Upon return, a crowd surrounded the scene,  
Scribes questioned the disciples, tempers keen.

A man cried out, “My son, spirit-bound and mute,  
Throws him down, foaming, despite the route.”

Jesus replied, "Unbelieving generation, bring him to me,"  
The spirit convulsed the boy, wild for all to see.

"How long has this been?" Jesus asked with care,  
“Since childhood,” the father, desperate, did share.

"If you can do anything, help us, I plead,"  
Jesus said, "If you believe, all things you’ll achieve."

The father cried, "I believe, help my unbelief!"  
The spirit was rebuked, leaving behind relief.

The boy seemed lifeless, whispers of death spread,  
But Jesus lifted him, alive, as He said.

"Why couldn't we cast it out?" the disciples asked,  
"Prayer and fasting," Jesus unmasked.

Through Galilee, they quietly advanced,  
Jesus taught His fate, the disciples in a trance.

In Capernaum, He asked, "What were you disputing on the way?"  
Silent, they pondered who was greatest, in disarray.

"If you want to be first, be last and serve all,"  
He embraced a child, teaching love's call.

"Receive this child, you receive me," Jesus explained,  
"And the One who sent me, through love unchained."

John mentioned one casting out demons, not in their crew,  
"Don't stop him," Jesus said, "He's working for you."

"Who’s not against us is with us," He made clear,  
"Even a cup of water given will be rewarded, sincere."

"Cause a little one to stumble? Better to drown,"  
In Gehenna's fire, with worm death can't crown.

"Cut off hand or foot if they lead astray,  
Better maimed than lose life, cast away."

"Eye that causes sin? Pluck it out, be wise,  
Enter God’s Kingdom, with one good eye."

"Salt will flavor sacrifice, and life’s fire, too,  
Keep your saltiness, and peace pursue."
