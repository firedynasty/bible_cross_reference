Early in the morn, priests and scribes convened,  
They bound up Jesus, a plot they had schemed.  
To Pilate they brought him, the council's demand,  
Questions were asked, the stakes in their hands.  

"Are you King of the Jews?" Pilate inquired,  
"So you say," Jesus calmly inspired.  
Chief priests accused him, words sharp and many,  
Pilate was baffled by Jesus’ silence, uncanny.  

At feast time, a prisoner would go free,  
Barabbas, a rebel of high degree.  
Crowds shouted loud, their voices grew strong,  
For tradition to release one, Pilate played along.  

"Release the King of Jews?" Pilate did press,  
But envy and schemes, the priests did confess.  
"Release Barabbas!" the crowd did scream,  
Pilate, confused by the scene extreme.  

"What should I do with this King of Jews?"  
"Crucify him!" came the harsh reviews.  
"Why, what evil?" Pilate tried to debate,  
"Crucify him!" their anger, too great.  

To please the crowd, Barabbas went free,  
Jesus was flogged, marked for the tree.  
Soldiers took Jesus, mocked him with scorn,  
Clothed him in purple, a crown of thorn.  

"Hail, King of the Jews," they cruelly said,  
Struck him, spat on him, while bowing their head.  
They stripped off the robe, back in his clothes,  
Led him to Golgotha, where the cross arose.  

Simon of Cyrene carried the cross’s weight,  
To Golgotha, the place of a skull, they did await.  
With wine and myrrh, they offered a taste,  
But Jesus refused, his fate to embrace.  

Crucified at the hour when shadows grew long,  
"THE KING OF THE JEWS," inscribed above wrong.  
Two robbers beside, one on each hand,  
Scripture fulfilled, just as it was planned.  

Passersby mocked with heads held high,  
"Destroy the temple? Save yourself, and fly!"  
Chief priests and scribes echoed with jeers,  
"Come down, O King, if you are sincere."  

Darkness fell, the sixth hour had come,  
At the ninth, Jesus cried, "Why am I undone?"  
"Eloi, Eloi," his voice rang out,  
"God, why forsake me?" in desperate shout.  

Some thought Elijah might hear his plea,  
Vinegar was offered to see if he'd be free.  
With a final loud cry, his spirit was let go,  
The temple veil split, top to low.  

The centurion, moved, his eyes opened wide,  
"Truly, this man was the Son," he testified.  
Women watched from afar, faithful and true,  
Mary Magdalene, Mary, and Salome too.  

As evening fell, in came Joseph bold,  
For Jesus’ body, a tomb of rock to hold.  
Pilate confirmed, then Jesus was laid,  
In linen wrapped, while the stone was displayed.  

Mary Magdalene and mother Mary stayed,  
Watching the tomb where Jesus was laid.
