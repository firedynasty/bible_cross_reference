Crowd's gathered tight, nothing to eat,  
Jesus called his crew, his words were sweet.  
"Three days they've stayed, no food in sight,  
Send them home now, they'll faint from the plight."  

The disciples asked, "Where's bread to be found,  
In this deserted place, no food around?"  
Jesus asked, "How many loaves do we hold?"  
"Seven," they replied, the story told.  

He sat them down on the ground so bare,  
Took those loaves, gave thanks with care.  
He broke, he blessed, passed the loaves around,  
Fed four thousand till full, nothing profound.  

Picked up the leftovers, baskets were seven,  
Sent the crowd away, toward their heaven.  
Into the boat, to Dalmanutha they sail,  
Pharisees came, seeking signs, made him frail.  

He sighed deep, and said with a frown,  
"This generation seeks a sign, none will be found."  
Left them standing, moved to the other shore,  
With just one loaf, disciples pondered more.  

"Beware the yeast," he warned them in haste,  
Of Pharisees and Herod, don’t let it waste.  
They whispered, "It's 'cause bread we forgot,"  
Jesus knew their thoughts, questioned the lot.  

"Why speak of bread, don’t you yet see?  
Hearts still hardened, with no clarity?"  
"Remember five loaves? Fed thousands then,  
Twelve baskets left, and now again."  

"Seven loaves for four thousand fed,  
Seven baskets, full pieces of bread."  
"Still don't get it?" he asked, with a nod,  
Onto Bethsaida, where he touched a man odd.  

Blind and helpless, Jesus took his hand,  
Led him away, spat, and touched by brand.  
"See anything?" he asked, the man peered wide,  
"Men like trees," he replied, eyes open wide.  

Once more he touched, vision became clear,  
"Don’t tell the village," Jesus whispered near.  
To Caesarea Philippi, the questions came,  
"Who am I?" he asked, the answers the same.  

"John the Baptizer, Elijah, or a prophet,  
But who do you say?" Peter spoke it.  
"The Christ," he said, a secret to keep,  
"Don't tell anyone," the silence deep.  

He taught them then of suffering and pain,  
Rejected and killed, but rise again.  
Peter rebuked, but Jesus turned,  
"Get behind me, Satan, your mind's unlearned."  

"Whoever follows, take up the cross,  
Lose your life for me, no loss.  
Gain the world, yet lose your soul,  
What will you give for life's whole?"  

"Don’t be ashamed in this sinful age,  
Son of Man's glory will be the wage."
