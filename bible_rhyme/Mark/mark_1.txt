Yo, it starts with the Good News, the Son of God proclaimed,  
Prophets said, "A messenger's coming, prepare the way named.”  

John’s in the wild with his camel hair on,  
Preaching forgiveness, baptizing till dawn.  

From Judea and Jerusalem, folks came in a wave,  
Down to the Jordan, sins confessed, souls to save.  

John's like, "After me, a mightier one will rise,  
I’m not worthy to untie his sandal ties."  

"I baptize with water, his Spirit’s divine,  
Get ready for a baptism of a different kind."  

Then Jesus from Nazareth came down to the stream,  
Baptized by John, fulfilling the dream.  

The sky cracked open, Spirit like a dove,  
Voice from above, "My Son, my love.”  

Spirit sent him, wilderness bound,  
Forty days tempted with no one around.  

After John's arrest, Jesus stepped in,  
Preaching God's Kingdom, where the change begins.  

"The time’s come, believe in the news,  
Repent, for God's Kingdom's yours to choose."  

By Galilee’s sea, he saw two fishin' bros,  
Simon and Andrew, casting their net low.  

"Follow me, fishers of men you'll be,"  
Left their nets, followed him, set free.  

Saw James and John with their nets to mend,  
He called, they left their father, followed him to the end.  

In Capernaum, on Sabbath he taught,  
Amazed the crowd, with authority he brought.  

In the synagogue, a spirit cried out,  
"Jesus the Nazarene, what’s this about?"  

"Be quiet, come out!" Jesus commanded strong,  
Spirit screamed, then was gone.  

Crowd amazed, "What’s this we see?  
Even spirits obey his decree."  

News spread wide, Galilee shook,  
Everywhere they talked about this new book.  

Out of the synagogue, to Simon’s place,  
His mother-in-law sick, fever set the pace.  

Took her hand, lifted her high,  
Fever fled, she served, no lie.  

As sun set, they gathered outside,  
The sick, the possessed, with hope in their eyes.  

He healed the masses, demons were cast,  
Silent they stayed, knowing who’d passed.  

Early next morn, to pray alone,  
Disciples searched, found him on his own.  

"Everyone seeks," they said, he replied,  
"Let's move on, more towns to stride."  

Preached in synagogues, healed, cast out dread,  
His fame spread like fire, wherever he tread.  

A leper came, bowed with a plea,  
"If you want, make me clean, set me free."  

With compassion, Jesus reached and spoke,  
"Be clean," and the chains of sickness broke.  

Warned the leper, "Tell no one, show the priest,"  
But news spread fast, from west to east.  

Deserted spots became the meeting ground,  
Everywhere, people came, hope unbound.
