Listen up now, let me tell you a tale,  
A man planted a vineyard, set it up without fail.  
He built a hedge, dug a pit, a tower tall he made,  
Rented it to farmers, in another land he stayed.  

Harvest time came, sent a servant for his share,  
But the farmers beat him, sent him back without a care.  
So he sent another, stones flew, head was bruised,  
Rejected once more, treated shamefully and abused.  

He sent yet another, and they killed him outright,  
Many servants met the same dark plight.  
Last, he sent his beloved son, thinking they'd respect,  
But they plotted, "Kill him, his inheritance we'll collect."  

They took him, killed him, cast him out of sight,  
"What will the owner do?" Destroy those farmers, set it right.  
"The stone builders rejected, now the cornerstone,"  
Marvelous is God's work, in it truth is shown.  

Pharisees and Herodians, with traps they came,  
Questions about taxes, to catch him in shame.  
Jesus knew their schemes, called for a coin,  
"Whose image is this?" The answer in joint.  

"Render to Caesar what is Caesar's,” he declared,  
"And to God what is God's," they marveled, truth he shared.  

Sadducees questioned, no resurrection they said,  
Seven brothers one wife, each one dead.  
"In resurrection, who’s the wife?" they asked in jest,  
Jesus replied, "In heaven, no marriage, just rest."  

"God of the living, not the dead," he declared,  
Their understanding flawed, truth laid bare.  

A scribe came forth, asked about the greatest command,  
"Hear, O Israel, the Lord is one, love with all you can.  
Love your neighbor as yourself, no commandment more,"  
The scribe agreed, Jesus said, "God's Kingdom's at your door."  

No one dared to question, his wisdom was profound,  
He taught them in the temple, truth made the rounds.  
"Beware the scribes, who love status, not the deed,  
Widows' houses they devour, greater condemnation their need."  

A poor widow gave all she possessed,  
Two small coins, more than the rest.  
"She gave from poverty, not abundance or pride,"  
Her sacrifice seen, in it God's heart resides.
