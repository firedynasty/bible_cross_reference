Two days 'til Passover, plotting in the night,  
Chief priests and scribes, scheming out of sight.  
"Not during the feast, can't start a fight,"  
Bethany, in Simon's house, under candlelight.  

A woman came with a jar of precious nard,  
Broke it, poured it, her love unscarred.  
Some folks grumbled, "What a costly waste,  
Sell it, feed the poor, that's more taste."  

But Jesus said, "Leave her be, she's done right,  
The poor you'll have always, but I'm out of sight.  
She's anointed me for what's ahead,  
Her story will be told, wherever it's spread."  

Judas slipped away, to the priests he ran,  
"What's it worth to take down this man?"  
They promised silver, he planned his move,  
Looking for the moment, quick and smooth.  

First day of the feast, disciples ask,  
"Where do we prep? We’re up for the task."  
Jesus sent two, with a plan well laid,  
"Find a man with water, follow, don’t be swayed.  

Ask for the room, upper and spread,  
Prepare the feast, the way I’ve said."  
Evening came, they gathered tight,  
Jesus declared, "One will betray tonight."  

Sorrow hit them, "Not I," they said,  
"One who dips with me," the words spread.  
Son of Man goes, as written and known,  
Woe to the betrayer, better not been born.  

Bread was broken, "This is my body, take and eat,"  
Cup passed around, "My blood, new covenant, sweet.  
I’ll drink new in God’s realm," he proclaimed,  
A hymn sung, to Olives, they aimed.  

"Tonight you'll stumble, scattered like sheep,  
But after this, in Galilee, we’ll meet."  
Peter vowed, "I’ll stand by you, never deny,"  
Jesus foresaw, "Three times, before the rooster’s cry."  

Gethsemane's garden, "Sit while I pray,"  
Took Peter, James, John, troubled in dismay.  
"My soul sorrowful, watch with me," he pled,  
Fell to the ground, "Father, this cup, if it can, be shed."  

Back he came, found them asleep,  
"One hour, couldn't you keep?  
The spirit's willing, flesh is weak,"  
Again he prayed, the same words he'd speak.  

Third time, "Sleep on, the hour is here,  
The Son of Man's betrayal, now near.  
Rise, let’s go, the betrayer's at hand,"  
Judas with a crowd, ready to stand.  

A kiss from Judas, "Rabbi," was said,  
Hands laid on him, seized in dread.  
A sword was drawn, an ear was shed,  
Jesus, calm, "With swords like I'm a threat?"  

"Daily I taught, you did not seize,  
Scriptures fulfilled, as you please."  
Disciples fled, left him alone,  
A young man followed, linen gone, he'd flown.  

Dragged to the high priests, the council's call,  
Peter followed, from shadowed wall.  
False words spoken, none could stick,  
"Destroy the temple, in three days rebuild it quick."  

High priest asked, "Are you the Blessed's son?"  
Jesus replied, "I am, you'll see the One,  
At Power's right hand, on clouds I’ll ride."  
Tears of rage, "Blasphemy!" was cried.  

Spit and fists, mockery and scorn,  
Peter out in the court, feeling torn.  
A maid said, "You know the Nazarene!"  
"Not me," Peter said, trying to stay unseen.  

Rooster crowed, a second chance denied,  
"You’re Galilean," they pressed, he lied.  
Curses, "I don't know him," he swore,  
Rooster crowed twice, he recalled more.  

Jesus' words hit, his heart bruised and bent,  
Peter wept bitter tears, in deep lament.
