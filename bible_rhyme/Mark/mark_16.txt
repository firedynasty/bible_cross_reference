Sabbath past, Mary, Mary, and Salome in the mix,  
Bought spices to anoint, love in the fix.  
Early morning light, first day of the week,  
To the tomb they went, where the sun did peek.  

"Who'll roll the stone?" Big and bold it stood,  
But rolled away it was, just like it should.  
Entered the tomb, on the right side they see,  
A young man in white, amazed they be.  

"Don't be amazed, you seek Jesus, the Nazarene,  
Crucified, now risen, no longer here seen.  
Look at the place where they laid him to rest,  
Go tell the disciples, and Peter, no jest."  

Fear and awe, they fled from the place,  
Said nothing to none, fear in the race.  
Risen early, Jesus to Mary appeared,  
From her, seven demons he once cleared.  

She told the mourning, though doubt did persist,  
Alive and seen, yet faith they missed.  
Revealed again on a country road,  
Two more saw, but disbelief flowed.  

To the eleven, as they gathered tight,  
He rebuked their hearts, for lack of sight.  
"Go to the world, preach Good News to all,  
Believe and be saved, heed the call.  

Signs will follow those who believe,  
In my name, new languages they’ll weave.  
Serpents they’ll take, poison won’t harm,  
Heal the sick, bring them calm."  

Ascended to heaven, he sat on the throne,  
Right hand of God, in glory alone.  
Preaching they went, with signs in line,  
The Lord with them, word divine. Amen, it's time.
