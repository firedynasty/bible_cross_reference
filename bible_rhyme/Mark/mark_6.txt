Jesus rolled to his hometown crew,  
Disciples by his side, they knew what to do.  

On Sabbath, he hit the synagogue stage,  
Crowds were stunned, their minds engaged.  

"Where’s this man's wisdom? His power's profound,  
Isn't he the carpenter, from this very town?"  

"A prophet's only disrespected at home,"  
Jesus said, feeling the hearts like stone.  

He laid hands on a few, the sick got healed,  
But their doubt was something he deeply felt.  

Called the twelve, sent 'em out in pairs,  
Gave 'em power over spirits, showed he cares.  

"No bread, no cash, just a staff in hand,  
Wear your sandals, by my command."  

"Stay in homes that welcome you well,  
If not, shake off dust, leave with a tale to tell."  

They preached repentance, cast demons out,  
Anointed and healed, spreading faith without doubt.  

Herod heard the buzz, fear in his eyes,  
"John's risen," he said, spreading surprise.  

Others claimed Elijah, a prophet returned,  
Herod felt haunted by the lesson he'd learned.  

Arrested John for Herodias' grudge,  
Against God's law, a verdict couldn’t budge.  

Herod feared John, in his words found delight,  
Kept him safe, though his wife took slight.  

Herod's birthday bash, a setting grand,  
Herodias' daughter danced, got him in a jam.  

"Ask what you want, half my kingdom's yours,"  
She asked her mom, who settled old sores.  

"John's head on a platter," the daughter said,  
Herod felt trapped by the oath he'd spread.  

Soldier dispatched, John lost his life,  
Brought the head, fulfilling the strife.  

Disciples mourned, laid John to rest,  
Jesus' crew returned, sharing their quest.  

"Come rest with me," Jesus said with care,  
So many, they couldn't find time to share.  

They sailed away, but the crowd still ran,  
Finding Jesus, the compassionate man.  

Sheep without shepherds, he taught them well,  
But as the day waned, hunger began to swell.  

"Send them off for food," disciples pled,  
"You feed them," Jesus calmly said.  

"Five loaves, two fish," they replied,  
Jesus blessed them, then multiplied.  

Groups sat on grass, hundreds and fifties,  
He fed five thousand, filled their bellies.  

Twelve baskets collected, fragments galore,  
Then sent the disciples to the other shore.  

Jesus prayed alone, as night grew deep,  
Saw them struggle on the waves, no sleep.  

Walking on water, he meant to pass by,  
Ghostly fear struck, their spirits high.  

"Cheer up! It's me, don't fear the sea,"  
Wind calmed, hearts amazed, but hard to see.  

Landed at Gennesaret, crowds quickly spread,  
Bringing the sick to touch his thread.  

Villages, cities, country towns,  
Touched his garment, healed all around.
