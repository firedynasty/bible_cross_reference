King’s wrath cooled, he recalled Vashti’s scene,  
Servants said, “Seek virgins to make a new queen.”  

“Gather them from provinces wide,  
To Susa’s citadel, let them reside.”  

The plan pleased the king, officers spread,  
With Hegai in charge, beauty rituals led.  

Mordecai, a Jew, in Susa he stayed,  
With Esther, his kin, whom he had raised.  

Esther was fair, with beauty so bright,  
Orphaned young, she shone like light.  

King’s decree echoed through halls,  
Maidens gathered, within Susa’s walls.  

Esther taken to the king’s domain,  
Hegai saw her, his favor she’d gain.  

Cosmetics, food, and seven maids too,  
To the best place in the house she flew.  

“Keep your people unknown,” Mordecai said,  
As he paced every day, anxious, full of dread.  

Each woman’s turn, a twelve-month prepare,  
Sweet scents and oils filled the air.  

To the king’s house, they’d go at night,  
Return to wait in morning light.  

Esther’s moment, she sought no excess,  
Hegai’s advice, nothing more, nothing less.  

Taken to the king in Tebeth’s chill,  
His heart she captured, with grace and will.  

Crown on her head, queen she became,  
Feast declared, in Esther’s name.  

Mordecai lingered at the gate,  
Listening, watching, early to late.  

Two eunuchs plotted against the throne,  
Mordecai heard, he wasn’t alone.  

Told Esther, who told the king,  
Plot unraveled, justice did ring.  

Both were hanged, truth revealed,  
Chronicles penned, their fate was sealed.
