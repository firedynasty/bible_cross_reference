Esther's banquet's on with the king and Haman in sight,  
The king leaned in, asking Esther, "What's your wish tonight?  
Tell me your request, queen, don't hesitate to speak,  
Even half my kingdom, if that's what you seek."  

Esther replied, "If I've found favor in your eyes,  
Spare my life and my people's, before all hope dies.  
We're sold to be destroyed, slain, left to expire,  
If just for slaves, I'd keep quiet, but the stakes are higher."  

The king, now tense, asked, "Who's behind this wicked plot?"  
Esther pointed, "It's Haman, the enemy, the rot!"  
Fear gripped Haman, the king stormed out with fire,  
As Haman begged Esther, for his life's desire.  

When the king returned, Haman fell where Esther lay,  
The king shouted, "Will he assault my queen today?"  
Guards moved quick, covered Haman’s face in shame,  
Harbonah spoke up, calling out Haman's name.  

"Behold, the gallows, fifty cubits in the sky,  
Built for Mordecai, who spoke truth, not a lie."  
The king ordered, "Hang him on what he made,"  
So Haman hung high, and the king's wrath swayed.  

Justice saw its day, the plot was reversed,  
The king's anger cooled, Haman's curse was cursed.
