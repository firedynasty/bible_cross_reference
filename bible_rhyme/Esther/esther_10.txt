King Ahasuerus, he raised the stakes,  
Tribute on the lands, across the sea breaks.  

Power and might, his acts well-spun,  
Mordecai's greatness, second to none.  

The chronicles hold the deeds so grand,  
In Media and Persia, his rule did stand.  

Mordecai the Jew, right at the throne,  
Great among his people, never alone.  

Beloved by many, his heart was true,  
For his people's good, he always knew.  

He spoke of peace, with wisdom's glance,  
For all his kin, he took a stance.
