Mordecai learned what had gone down, tore his clothes, sackcloth draped 'round,  
In the city he cried, a wail so loud, grieving in the midst of the crowd.  

To the king's gate he approached, but no sackcloth allowed inside,  
Across provinces, mourning spread wide, with fasting, tears, in sackcloth they cried.  

Esther heard the news, her heart heavy with the weight,  
Sent clothes to Mordecai, but he refused to change his state.  

Hathach, the eunuch, went to meet Mordecai,  
Found him by the gate, heard his desperate cry.  

Mordecai shared the scheme, Haman's wicked plan,  
Coin to destroy Jews, a shadow over the land.  

He handed the decree, urged Esther to plead,  
To the king for mercy, for the Jews in need.  

Hathach brought the message back, Esther listened with care,  
"Approach without a call means death, I'm aware."  

Esther's words returned to Mordecai's ear,  
He responded with a message, crystal clear:  

"Don't think you'll escape when trouble's near,  
Help will come, but your family may disappear.  

Perhaps you were made queen for a time like this,  
To stand up, take courage, don't dismiss."  

Esther replied with a bold, steady breath,  
"Gather all the Jews, fast 'til there's nothing left.  

Three days, nights, no food or drink for me,  
I'll go to the king, risk it all, if need be."  

Mordecai obeyed, spread the word all around,  
In unity they fasted, hope in silence found.
