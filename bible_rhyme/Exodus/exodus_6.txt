Yahweh said to Moses, "Watch what I'll do,  
Pharaoh's gonna let them go, it's true.  

I am Yahweh, the one who came,  
To Abraham, Isaac, Jacob — they knew my name.  

I made a deal, a promise to keep,  
Canaan's land is where they'll leap.  

I heard their cries, the groan and moan,  
Egypt's chains — they'll soon be gone.  

Tell them, 'I am Yahweh, I'll bring you out,  
From Egyptian chains, you'll leave without a doubt.  

I'll redeem you with an arm stretched wide,  
Judgment's coming, nowhere to hide.  

You'll be my people, I'll be your God,  
Remember, I'm Yahweh, against all odds.  

I'll lead you to the land I swore,  
To Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and more.'"  

Moses spoke, but they didn't trust,  
Their spirits crushed, hearts full of dust.  

Yahweh urged him, "Go to the Pharaoh,  
Tell him, 'Let my people go.'"  

Moses worried, "They won't hear me,  
Pharaoh won't listen, can't you see?"  

But Yahweh commanded Moses and Aaron,  
"Speak to Pharaoh, freedom's the narrative you're sharing."  

Listed were the clans, the sons of each tribe,  
Reuben, Simeon, Levi — all described.  

Heads of families, listed by name,  
Generations recounted, their heritage claim.  

Amram wed Jochebed, and they had two,  
Aaron and Moses, leaders—so true.  

To Pharaoh, Moses and Aaron were sent,  
With Yahweh's words, on a mission they went.  

Yahweh's voice, "Speak what I say,"  
Moses replied, "Pharaoh won't obey."  

Yet Moses and Aaron stood tall and brave,  
For the children of Israel, they fought to save.
