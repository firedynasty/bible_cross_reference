Moses on a mountain, tablets to renew,  
Yahweh's words to write, for the people true.  
"Chisel them like the first, be ready at dawn,  
Come to Sinai's top, where my glory's drawn.  

Alone you must come, no one else can see,  
Not even the flocks should be grazing near me."  
Moses rose early with two tablets of stone,  
To the peak of Sinai, where he'd meet God alone.  

Yahweh in the cloud, His presence came near,  
Proclaiming His name, His voice we could hear.  
"Merciful and gracious, slow to anger," He said,  
"Lovingkindness and truth, from God they are spread.  

Forgiving the sin, but justice is sure,  
From fathers to sons, the guilt will endure."  
Moses bowed low, worshiped, and then,  
"Have mercy," he asked, "on this stiff-necked men.  

Make us yours, forgive our sin,  
Let your presence with us begin."  
"I'll make a covenant, marvels I'll show,  
Nations before you, their strength I'll throw.  

Break down their altars, shatter their stone,  
Worship no other, worship me alone."  
"Keep the feasts, remember the days,  
Rest on the seventh, in plowing and blaze.  

Bring me your first, the fruits of the ground,  
My covenant stands, in these words found."  
Moses on the mountain, forty nights and days,  
The covenant written, in God's holy ways.  

When Moses came down, his face full of light,  
The people saw glory, shining so bright.  
He shared God's commands, with veiled face,  
But before Yahweh, the veil had no place.  

The people stood back, but they came near,  
To hear the commandments, their hearts to steer.  
Moses' face shining, the veil would return,  
Until once more to God, his face would turn.
