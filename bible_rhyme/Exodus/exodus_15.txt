Moses and Israel sang loud and clear,  
"Praise Yahweh, for His triumph is near.  

He tossed horse and rider into the sea,  
Yah is my strength, my song, my decree.  

My God, I’ll praise, my father's God I own,  
Yahweh the warrior, His name is known.  

Pharaoh's chariots sunk deep in the tide,  
Captains gone where the Red Sea's waves collide.  

The depths covered them, stone-like they fell,  
Yahweh's hand shattered them, no one could tell.  

Your power is great, Yahweh, unmatched and bright,  
Wrath like fire, stubble burns in the night.  

With the blast of your nostrils, waters piled high,  
Floods stood like walls, a miracle nigh.  

The enemy boasted, 'I'll chase them, I'll win,  
Divide the spoils, my blade will spin.'  

But Your wind blew, and the sea swallowed them,  
Sank like lead, while waves overwhelmed the men.  

"Who is like You, Yahweh, among the gods?  
Glorious, holy, wonder-working nods.  

Your hand stretched, and the earth opened wide,  
Swallowed them whole, no place for them to hide.  

In kindness, You lead those redeemed by Your might,  
Guide them to Your holy place, out of the night.  

The nations hear and tremble with fear,  
Philistia's hearts quake, Edom's chiefs weep tears.  

Moab's warriors tremble, Canaanites melt,  
Terror grips them, the power they've felt.  

Your people pass through, Your arm holds them still,  
To the mountain, Your promise, Your dwelling, You will.  

Yahweh reigns forever, a truth declared,  
Pharaoh's army drowned, but Israel spared.  

Miriam led with tambourines and dance,  
Singing of Yahweh's glorious stance.  

Three days in Shur, with no water to find,  
At Marah, the water was bitterly unkind.  

People murmured, "What shall we drink?"  
Moses cried to Yahweh, standing on the brink.  

Yahweh showed a tree, thrown in, waters turned sweet,  
There He tested them, His statutes they’d meet.  

"Heal and protect, if My words you heed,  
Keep My commands, and you will be freed."  

To Elim they came, springs and palms in sight,  
There they camped by waters, restful in the night.
