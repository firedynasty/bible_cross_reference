Yahweh spoke to Moses, here's the decree,  
"Sanctify each firstborn, they belong to Me. 

Remember this day, outta Egypt you came,  
No leavened bread, just remember the name. 

Today you leave, it's the month of Abib,  
A land flowing with honey is what you'll receive. 

Seven days of unleavened bread you gotta eat,  
And on the seventh day, Yahweh’s feast you'll meet. 

No leaven around, not within your borders,  
Remember this act, keep it in good orders. 

Tell your son, 'It's what Yahweh did for me,  
When I came out of Egypt, setting me free.' 

A sign on your hand, a memory between your eyes,  
Yahweh’s law in your mouth, under these skies. 

Keep this ordinance, from year to year,  
Yahweh's promises, loud and clear. 

When Yahweh brings you into the land,  
The firstborn’s mine, you understand? 

Redeem each donkey with a lamb, or neck-break,  
The firstborn of man, redemption'll take. 

If your son asks, 'What does this mean?'  
Tell him, 'Yahweh's strength set us free, unseen.' 

Pharaoh was stubborn, in Egypt he stayed,  
So Yahweh took firstborns, all debts repaid. 

A sign on your hand, symbols in your sight,  
Yahweh's mighty hand led us into the light. 

When Pharaoh let them go, God had a plan,  
Not through Philistine lands, not in that span. 

God led through the wilderness, by the Red Sea shore,  
Israel walked armed, out of Egypt, nevermore. 

Moses took Joseph's bones, as he had sworn,  
"God's visit is true, He shall transform."

From Succoth to Etham, on the wilderness edge,  
Yahweh led with cloud and fire, a divine pledge. 

A pillar by day, a fire by night,  
Guiding their steps, with heavenly light. 

The pillar never left, stood strong and bold,  
A constant guide for the stories told.
