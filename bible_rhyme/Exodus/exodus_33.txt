Yahweh spoke to Moses, "Time to depart,  
You and your people, make a new start.  
To the land I promised, flowin' milk and honey,  
An angel leads, but I'll keep my distance, sonny.

You're a stiff-necked people, and I'd consume,  
So strip off your jewels, don't meet your doom."  
The news hit hard, they mourned in tears,  
Jewels abandoned, facing their fears.

Moses pitched the Tent, far from the crowd,  
Seeking Yahweh, where no one's allowed.  
People stood up as Moses went in,  
The cloud descended, where the talks begin.

Face to face, like friends, they spoke,  
Moses turned back, as the people awoke.  
Joshua stayed, never left the Tent,  
Moses asked Yahweh, "Show me what you meant.

You said, 'I know you, you've found my favor,'  
Show me your way, let me savor."  
"My presence goes with you," Yahweh did rest,  
"Without you, we can't leave, won't pass the test."

"How will they know? We're your chosen crew,"  
Yahweh replied, "I'll go with you too.  
You've found favor, I know your name,  
Ask what you will, I'll do the same."

"Show me your glory," Moses did plea,  
"My goodness will pass, but you can't see me.  
I'll be gracious, show mercy where I choose,  
My face unseen, it'll mean your lose."

"Stand on the rock, here's where you'll stay,  
My glory passes, my hand will sway.  
I'll cover you first, then the vision will lack,  
Remove my hand, you'll see my back."
