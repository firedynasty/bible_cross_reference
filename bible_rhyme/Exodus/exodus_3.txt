Moses tending sheep, on the wilderness roam,  
Led them to Horeb, God’s mountain, his new home.  

A bush on fire, yet it didn’t consume,  
Moses said, “Check it out, what's with this bloom?”  

God called out, “Moses!” from the bush's core,  
“Here I am,” Moses answered, ready to explore.  

“Take off your sandals, this ground is divine,  
I’m the God of your fathers, your family line.”  

Seeing God, Moses hid his face in fear,  
Yahweh spoke of the cries, the troubles He’d hear.  

“I’ve seen their pain, heard their cries, truly know,  
I’ll bring them to a land where milk and honey flow.  

To Canaan, where the Jebusites and Hittites dwell,  
Now go to Pharaoh, break your people’s spell.”  

Moses asked, “Who am I to take this stand?”  
God replied, “I’ll be with you, this is my command."  

“When you’re free, worship here, on this holy site,  
Tell them 'I AM has sent you' to ignite."  

“Gather the elders, tell them all you see,  
'Yahweh has come, plans for you to be free.'”  

Moses pondered how to prove his voice strong,  
God said, “Say I AM, my name all along.”  

“Tell the king, ‘Let us journey, three days to be,  
To sacrifice to Yahweh, let us be free.’”  

The king’s a tough hand, won’t let you go,  
But I’ll strike Egypt, and then he will know.  

You’ll leave with treasures, jewels galore,  
Plundering Egypt, like nothing before.”
