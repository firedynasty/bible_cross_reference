With blue, purple, scarlet thread, the garments they made,  
For the holy place, finely crafted, as Yahweh bade.  

Ephod of gold, colors that shine,  
Woven with skill, a work divine.  

Gold plates beaten, cut into wire,  
Mixed with fabrics to inspire.  

Shoulder straps joined at two ends,  
A woven band, where skill and gold blend.  

Onyx stones set in gold to shine,  
Names engraved, a memorial sign.  

Breastplate crafted, square and bold,  
Gold, blue, purple like stories told.  

Double its span, rows of stones set tight,  
Ruby, turquoise, sapphire, a color delight.  

Chains of gold, braided and strong,  
Rings and settings where they belong.  

On ephod’s front, chains do cling,  
Bound with blue, like a king's ring.  

Robe woven all in blue,  
Opening bound, work they knew.  

Pomegranates and bells interlace,  
A rhythmic dance, in holy place.  

Tunics fine, for Aaron’s sons,  
Linen headbands when service runs.  

Holy crown of gold inscribed,  
"HOLY TO YAHWEH," it described.  

Tabernacle work now complete,  
Every piece brought to Moses’ feet.  

Tent and furniture, all arrayed,  
As Yahweh commanded, each part laid.  

Moses saw, and to them he said,  
“You've done as Yahweh's words were led.”  

And with a blessing, he closed the day,  
As they followed what Yahweh did say.
