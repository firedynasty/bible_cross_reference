Ordinances set, here's what to know,  
A Hebrew servant's time, six years to show.  
Year seven comes, he walks out free,  
Alone or with his family, as it should be.  

If a master's gifts bring family ties,  
He goes alone if kids arise.  
But if he loves them, chooses to stay,  
To the doorpost they go, an awl in play.  

A daughter sold, not the same plan,  
If he’s deceitful, redeeming’s at hand.  
Married to his son, treat her right,  
Food, clothing, rights, don’t slight.  

Kill a man, death’s the fate,  
Unintentional though, God’s plan’s straight.  
Scheming murder, take him from the altar,  
To die, no chance for him to falter.  

Hit mom or dad, or curse, it’s death,  
Kidnap and sell? Face the last breath.  
Men quarrel, stones or fists in the fray,  
If walking again, debt he must pay.  

Servant struck, dies from the rod,  
Punishment comes, but wait for the nod.  
Pregnant woman hurt? Fine in demand,  
Harm follows? Life for life, God’s command.  

Eye or tooth struck, servant goes free,  
Bull’s rage known, owner’s penalty.  
Open pit traps, pay the cost,  
Divide the dead, nothing’s lost.  

Bull injures bull, split the price,  
Known danger? Pay for the vice.  
These are the rules, the laws to heed,  
Justice served for every deed.
