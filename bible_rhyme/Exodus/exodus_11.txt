God spoke to Moses with a plan so bold,  
"One more plague, and Pharaoh will fold.  

Tell the people, speak in their ears,  
Ask your neighbors for treasures, calm their fears.  

I'll give you favor in the Egyptians' sight,  
Moses stands mighty, in the land like light."  

Moses declared, "This is Yahweh's call,  
'At midnight, death will visit all.  

The firstborn shall perish, from throne to ground,  
Even the beasts, death will surround.  

A cry will rise, Egypt will wail,  
But for Israel, peace will prevail.  

Not a dog will bark, or tongue will sway,  
Know Yahweh sees the night and day.  

Pharaoh's servants will bow to me,  
'Leave us, depart!' will be their plea."  

Moses left with anger hot,  
God said, "Pharaoh's heart will not  

Hear your plea, hard it'll stay,  
So my wonders will light the day."  

Moses and Aaron, wonders displayed,  
Pharaoh's heart, though, never swayed.
