Yahweh spoke to Moses, His chosen one,  
"Behold, Bezalel, skilled and wise as the sun.  

Filled with My Spirit, to craft and to build,  
Gold, silver, bronze, with his hands he's skilled.  

Stones he will cut, wood he will carve,  
All kinds of workmanship his mind will starve.  

Oholiab too, from Dan’s great line,  
With wisdom in hearts, for My plan divine.  

The Tent of Meeting, with each sacred piece,  
The ark, the mercy seat, their works won't cease.  

Tables, lampstands, incense to arise,  
Garments for priests, holy in their eyes.  

Anointing oil, sweet incense to smell,  
According to My commands they'll do well."  

Yahweh continued, His voice strong,  
"Tell Israel of the Sabbath, where they belong.  

A sign between us, through your line it shall stay,  
I am Yahweh, who sanctifies in this way.  

Keep it holy, set apart and blessed,  
Profane it, you'll face eternal rest.  

Six days for labor, the seventh you rest,  
Who works on Sabbath, won't be blessed.  

A covenant perpetual, for generations to hold,  
In six days I made all, on the seventh, I'm told,  

I rested, refreshed, a sign to see,  
Between Me and Israel, eternally."  

When words were finished on Sinai’s peak,  
Moses received tablets, God's words to speak.  
Stone tablets written with finger divine,  
A covenant of stone, for all of time.
