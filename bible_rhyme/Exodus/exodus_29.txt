"Here's the way to make them holy," said with a true voice,  
"One bull, two rams, without defect, that's the choice.  
Unleavened bread, cakes, and wafers with oil,  
Fine wheat flour, all set for the toil.  

Put it all in one basket, neat and grand,  
Bring it with the bull and rams in your hand.  
Bring Aaron and his sons to the Tent’s door,  
Wash them with water, pure to the core.  

Dress Aaron with tunic, ephod, and band,  
Crown on the turban, as the plan's been planned.  
Pour oil on his head, anoint with grace,  
Bring his sons, dress them for their place.  

Bind belts, headbands, the priesthood’s theirs,  
Consecrate them with careful prayers.  
Bring the bull before the Tent now,  
Aaron and his sons will lay hands, they know how.  

Slaughter the bull at the Tent’s door,  
Blood on the altar's horns, pour it on the floor.  
Burn the fat and kidneys on the fire,  
The rest of the bull is burned outside, higher.  

Take one ram, lay your hands on its head,  
Kill it and sprinkle the blood, just as was said.  
Cut the ram, wash it, head and feet,  
Burn it whole on the altar, smoke rising sweet.  

Take the other ram, hands on its crown,  
Kill it, blood on ears, thumbs, toes, pass it down.  
Sprinkle blood and oil on Aaron’s vest,  
He, his sons, their garments, now all are blessed.  

Fat of the ram, bread from the basket, wave in hand,  
Burn it on the altar, by Yahweh’s command.  
Wave the breast, it’s for you, take your share,  
An eternal portion, a holy affair.  

Seven days they wear the sacred attire,  
Consecrate the altar, cleanse with fire.  
Offer lambs day by day, morning and night,  
With flour, oil, and wine, a pleasing sight.  

At the Tent of Meeting, I'll be there,  
Speak to you, children, the place made fair.  
I’ll dwell among you, be your God,  
From Egypt, you were freed, upon this holy sod."
