Jethro heard what God had done, how He freed His chosen one,  
Moses' wife and sons came back, her father led the pack. 

Gershom and Eliezer, their names told tales of past,  
"God was my help," said Moses, with stories that would last.  

Jethro came to Moses' camp, at the Mountain of God, they stand,  
"I'm here with your wife and sons," he said, a family reunion planned.  

Moses met him, bowed and kissed, tales of Yahweh they exchanged,  
Of Pharaoh's fall, the trials faced, and how God rearranged.  

Jethro rejoiced, "Blessed be Yahweh for His mighty hand,  
Deliverance from Pharaoh's grip, to the Promised Land."  

He offered up a burnt offering, sacrifices to the Lord,  
Aaron and the elders came, shared the meal, their hearts in accord.  

Next day, Moses judged the crowd, from sunrise till the set,  
Jethro saw and questioned him, "Why alone you fret?"  

Moses said, "They come to me, to know God's will and way,  
I judge their matters, big and small, every single day."  

Jethro gave wise counsel, "This burden's heavy, not just fine,  
You'll wear out, and so will they, it's time to realign."  

"Listen now, I'll give advice, teach them God's own laws,  
Find able men who fear the Lord, let them share this cause."  

"Rulers of thousands, hundreds, fifties, tens will ease the strain,  
Let them handle smaller things, and lighten up your pain."  

"If you do this, God commands, you'll endure and find some peace,  
The people will go home content, and all your stress will cease."  

Moses took Jethro's words to heart, set his plan in motion,  
Chose strong leaders, shared the load, with wisdom and devotion.  

They judged the people day by day, left the hardest for the chief,  
Jethro then went on his way, his counsel brought relief.
