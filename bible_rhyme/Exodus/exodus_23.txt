Don't spread lies, don't join with the wicked crew,  
In court, keep justice, don't let the lies break through.  

Your enemy's ox lost, bring it back, don't stall,  
Help the donkey that's burdened, even if you don't like him at all.  

Justice for the poor, in court, it must stay,  
Avoid false charges, let innocence light the way.  

No bribes, they blind the eye, twist what's right,  
Don't oppress the outsider, remember Egypt's night.  

For six years work the land, the seventh let it rest,  
For the poor and beasts, that year is best.  

Six days of labor, then a day of rest,  
Refresh the weary, let the tired be blessed.  

Observe my words, don't speak of other gods,  
Keep your feasts as I said, in your holy squads.  

Unleavened bread for seven days, as I command,  
Harvest and ingather, the fruits of your land.  

Three times a year, before Yahweh appear,  
Bring your first fruits, get your blessings clear.  

"An angel leads you, hear his voice,  
Obey, and I’ll make your enemies no choice."  

Drive out the nations, but not all at once,  
Little by little, to avoid nature's response.  

Borders wide, from sea to river wide,  
No deals with their gods, let none abide.  

Serve Yahweh alone, and blessings will flow,  
Your enemy's terror, God's might will show.  

Don't let them dwell, don't let them in,  
Serve their gods, and you'll fall to sin.
