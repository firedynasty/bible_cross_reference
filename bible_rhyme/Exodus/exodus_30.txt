Make an altar, acacia wood's the call,  
Square and tall, gold over it all.  

"Overlay it pure, with gold so bright,  
Molding around, shining in the light.  

"Two golden rings, beneath the frame,  
Poles of acacia, gold just the same.  

"Place it before the veil, by the sacred seat,  
Where I'm gonna meet you, where we will meet.  

"Every morning, Aaron burns the spice,  
Tending lamps, making it precise.  

"Evening comes, the incense burns again,  
A sweet scent rising, like a holy refrain.  

"No strange incense, nor offering here,  
Only atonement, once a year.  

"Blood on the horns, for the sin's release,  
It’s sacred to Yahweh, it won't cease."  

Then God spoke, with a census plan,  
"Each man's ransom, show where you stand.  

"Half a shekel, sanctuary's rate,  
Each soul's offering, don't debate.  

"Rich and poor, equal, face to face,  
To make atonement, for your soul's place.  

"Use that silver, for the Tent's affair,  
Memorial for Israel, in Yahweh's care."  

"Make a bronze basin, for washing clean,  
Set between tent and altar seen.  

"Aaron and sons, wash hands and feet,  
Before they come near, or their lives will deplete.  

"Statute forever, through generations,  
Purity required for holy ministrations."  

Then more words from God’s own voice,  
"Gather fine spices, but make the right choice.  

"Liquid myrrh, and cinnamon sweet,  
Cane and cassia, for the mix complete.  

"A holy oil, anointing all,  
Tent and altar, large or small.  

"Make it holy, set apart,  
Nothing like it, in your heart.  

"Anoint Aaron, sons sanctify,  
They serve as priests, pure and high.  

"To Israel, this oil is mine,  
No imitations, none entwine.  

"Compounds like this, for common use,  
Cut off that one, there's no excuse."  

"Take sweet spices, frankincense pure,  
Create incense, holy and sure.  

"Seasoned fine, pure, and holy made,  
In the Tent of Meeting, let it be laid.  

"This incense sacred, none for your pleasure,  
For Yahweh alone, it’s my treasure.  

"Whoever makes it for himself to smell,  
Cut him off, there he shall dwell."
