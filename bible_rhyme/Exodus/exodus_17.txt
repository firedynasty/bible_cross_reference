In the wilderness of Sin, they moved on Yah's command,  
Arrived at Rephidim, but found no water on the land.  

Thirsty and angry, they quarreled with Moses, "We need a drink!"  
Moses asked, "Why test Yahweh? Why push us to the brink?"  

With parched lips they cried, "Why leave Egypt for thirst's curse?  
To kill us and our children, our livestock in this dry verse?"  

Moses turned to Yahweh and cried out in despair,   
"They're ready to stone me, with no answer, I fear."  

Yahweh said, "Walk on, take the elders and your rod,  
The one that struck the Nile, go forward and trust in God."  

"On Horeb's rock, I'll stand," Yahweh did declare,  
"Strike it, water will flow, quenching their despair."  

Moses struck the rock, and out came the stream,  
He called the place Massah, where doubts intervened.  

But then came Amalek to fight them hand to hand,  
Moses told Joshua, "Choose men, make a stand."  

"Tomorrow on the hill, with God's rod in my grip,  
I'll raise my hands to heaven, for victory not to slip."  

As Joshua fought below, with courage in his soul,  
Moses, Aaron, and Hur climbed the hill to control.  

When his hands were raised, Israel took the fight,  
When they fell, Amalek gained the might.  

Moses' arms grew weary, so they found him a stone,  
Aaron and Hur held his hands, not letting him fight alone.  

Steady hands till sunset, they stood strong in line,  
Joshua's sword cut down Amalek, their victory divine.  

Yahweh spoke to Moses, "Write this story down,  
Amalek's memory, I'll erase from the ground."  

Moses built an altar, "Yahweh our Banner" he named,  
"Yah has sworn, Amalek's war remains untamed."
