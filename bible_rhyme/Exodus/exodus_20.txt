God spoke to the crowd, laying down the line,  
"I am Yahweh, your God, the one who freed you just in time.  

No other gods before me, keep your worship pure,  
No idols, no images, let your faith be sure.  

Don't bow, don't serve them, I'm a jealous type,  
Punishing fathers' sins till the fourth, but to the loved, kind ripe.  

Don't misuse my name, it's sacred, keep it tight,  
Respect the Sabbath day, rest in holy light.  

Six days you labor, but the seventh's for rest,  
Everyone's included, give your servants your best.  

For in six days, heaven, earth, sea were made,  
On the seventh, I rested, so the Sabbath I laid.  

Honor your parents, so your days will stretch long,  
In the land I give, keep your spirit strong.  

No murder, no adultery, don't steal or lie,  
Don't covet what's your neighbor's, let desires pass by.  

The people saw thunder, lightning, mountain smoke,  
Trembling from the power, they felt the divine yoke.  

They said to Moses, "Speak, let it be your voice,  
But don't let God speak, or we won't have a choice."  

Moses calmed them, "Don't fear, it's a test,  
So you'll hold his fear, and live your life blessed."  

People kept their distance, Moses stepped near,  
Into thick darkness, where God's words were clear.  

Yahweh spoke to Moses, "Tell them what you’ve seen,  
No gods of silver or gold, let the worship stay clean.  

Build me an altar of earth, offer peace and fire,  
In places I name, my blessings will inspire.  

If stone you choose, use no tool that pollutes,  
Keep the altar simple, no steps that reveal the roots."
