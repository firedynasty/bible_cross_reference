Moses gathered the crowd, he had words to convey,  
"This is what Yahweh commands, follow His way.  

Work six days, but take the seventh to rest,  
A holy Sabbath for Yahweh, it's truly blessed.  

No fire in your homes on that Sabbath day,  
Break this rule, and there's a price to pay."  

Then Moses declared to the congregation wide,  
"Yahweh's command, let it be your guide.  

Bring an offering, from hearts that are free,  
Gold, silver, bronze—bring it willingly.  

Blue, purple, scarlet, fine linen, goats' hair,  
Rams' skins dyed red, sea cow hides, acacia wood to share.  

Oil for the light, spices sweet and rare,  
Onyx stones for the ephod, breastplate with care.  

Wise-hearted folks, come and make what's planned,  
The tabernacle, the coverings, all by Yahweh's command.  

The ark and its poles, the mercy seat of gold,  
The veil, the table with vessels, the stories of old.  

Lampstand for light, the altar's incense sweet,  
Doors of the tabernacle, every detail neat.  

Altar of burnt offering, bronze grating and base,  
Court hangings, gate screens, pins in their place.  

Garments for Aaron, the priest's holy attire,  
All these are needed, as Yahweh did require."  

The people left, their hearts moved and inspired,  
Men and women came, with gifts they were fired.  

With brooches, earrings, rings, and gold,  
All who had treasures, their offerings told.  

Blue, purple, scarlet, fine linen brought near,  
Goats' hair spun by women with wisdom clear.  

Rulers brought stones, for ephod and breastplate,  
Spices and oil, their offerings were great.  

Israel brought offerings, their heartstrings played,  
For all that Yahweh had Moses say.  

"Behold," said Moses, "Yahweh's chosen by name,  
Bezalel, son of Uri, steps into fame.  

Filled with God's Spirit, wisdom and skill,  
To craft in gold, silver, with craftsmanship's thrill.  

Stones to be set, wood carved with grace,  
Bezalel and Oholiab, teaching their place.  

Wisdom of heart, for every artful hand,  
To weave, embroider, create as planned."
