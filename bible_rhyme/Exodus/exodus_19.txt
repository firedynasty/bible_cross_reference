Third month, on the day they left Egypt's land,  
Israel hit Sinai, camped in the desert sand.  
Moses climbed up to meet God on the peak,  
Yahweh's voice boomed, mighty and unique.  

"Tell Jacob's house, tell Israel's crew,  
You saw what I did, carried on eagle's wings, brought you.  
Obey my voice, keep my pact tight,  
You'll be my treasure, in my holy light."  

Moses spoke Yahweh's words, called the elders near,  
People replied, "What He spoke, we'll do, loud and clear."  
Yahweh said, "I'll come in a cloud thick and gray,  
So they hear me speak and believe every day."  

"Go, sanctify them, let garments be clean,  
Be ready by the third day, my power will be seen.  
Set bounds on the mount, don't touch or climb,  
For touching the mountain is death's chime."  

Moses came down, told the people the plan,  
"Be pure, wash up, don't touch woman or man."  
On the third morning, thunder roared, lightning flashed,  
A trumpet's blast, in the camp their hearts thrashed.  

Moses led them out to meet God face to face,  
Mount Sinai quaked, smoke filled the place.  
Trumpet grew louder, Moses spoke out strong,  
God's voice answered, like a powerful song.  

Yahweh called Moses up, but warned him tight,  
"Tell the people, don't come near my sight.  
Even priests must cleanse, or face my flame,  
Keep them back, hold them in my name."  

Moses replied, "Boundaries are set, none can breach,"  
Yahweh said, "Bring Aaron, but the rest must not reach."  
Moses descended, shared Yahweh's command,  
Ensuring God's word was spread through the land.
