Moses and Aaron, they got a plan,  
Yahweh spoke, and they took their stand.  
"Moses, you're like God to Pharaoh's eyes,  
Aaron, your brother, will prophesy the skies.  

Speak all I command, let Aaron relay,  
Tell Pharaoh, 'Let my people walk away.'  
Yet I'll harden his heart, show signs and wonders too,  
In Egypt's land, my power will break through.  

But Pharaoh won't listen, his heart's gonna freeze,  
I'll bring Israel out, my people will be free.  
"Egypt will know I'm Yahweh when my hand goes wide,  
Bringing Israel out with judgments beside."  

Moses and Aaron, they did just that,  
Old but bold, no time for chat.  
Yahweh instructed, "When Pharaoh wants a show,  
Tell Aaron to cast his rod, let the serpent grow."  

Into Pharaoh's court, Aaron threw it down,  
A rod to a serpent, right there in town.  
Pharaoh's magicians did the same trick,  
But Aaron's serpent swallowed theirs, quick.  

Pharaoh's heart was stone, didn't heed the call,  
Yahweh said, "He's stubborn, won't budge at all.  
Meet him by the river, where he stands tall,  
Tell him Yahweh's command, the final call.  

'Let my people go, to serve me in the wild,'  
Yet you haven't listened, Pharaoh, don't be beguiled.  
Strike the river with the rod in your hand,  
Turn waters to blood across this land.  

Fish will perish, river foul and red,  
Egyptians will loathe to drink, they'll dread."  
Moses obeyed, Aaron stretched wide,  
Struck the waters, red tides won't hide.  

Fish belly-up, river now foul,  
Egyptians thirsty, water makes them scowl.  
Magicians mimicked, but Pharaoh stayed hard,  
Turned away, didn't take it to heart.  

Egyptians dug wells, searching for a sip,  
Seven days passed since Yahweh's mighty grip.
