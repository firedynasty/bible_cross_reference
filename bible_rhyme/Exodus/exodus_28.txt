"Bring Aaron and his sons to serve me, yo,  
In the priest's office, let glory show.  
Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, Ithamar, they’ll stand,  
In holy garments, made by wise hands.  

“Speak to the wise, with wisdom filled,  
To craft the garments, as I have willed.  
A breastplate, ephod, robe shining bright,  
Turban, sash, in gold, blue, and scarlet light.  

Gold threads woven fine, purple and red,  
Holy garments for the priesthood ahead.  
Ephod with straps, joined like a chain,  
Gold and linen, skillfully made, again and again.  

Two onyx stones, names engraved bold,  
Six on each, in birth order told.  
Set in gold, on shoulders they’ll stay,  
Memorial stones for Israel, day by day.  

Chains of gold, braided and pure,  
On the ephod for Aaron, to serve secure.  
Breastplate of judgment, skillfully wrought,  
In gold and linen, its purpose taught.  

Four stone rows on the breastplate shine,  
Ruby, sapphire, emerald align.  
Twelve tribes’ names, engraved in array,  
On Aaron’s heart, always to stay.  

Blue lace binds, ephod and breastplate unite,  
For Aaron before Yahweh, shining bright.  
Urim and Thummim, in judgment they guide,  
On Aaron’s heart, they’ll never hide.  

“Robe of blue, with a hole in the top,  
Woven strong, so it won’t tear or flop.  
Pomegranates and golden bells around the hem,  
Sounding when Aaron enters, a holy gem.  

A plate of pure gold, ‘HOLY TO YAHWEH,’  
On Aaron’s forehead, always to stay.  
A tunic of linen, a turban so fine,  
For glory and beauty, divine design.  

Anoint, consecrate, make them stand firm,  
In the priest’s office, for all time to affirm.  
Linen pants to cover, from waist to thigh,  
A lasting statute, under Yahweh’s eye."
