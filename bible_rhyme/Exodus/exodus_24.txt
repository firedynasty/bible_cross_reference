"Come up to Yahweh," He spoke so clear,  
Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, draw near.  
Seventy elders, but keep your distance,  
Moses alone will enter God's presence.  

Moses shared Yahweh's word, the people's choice,  
"We'll do all Yahweh's words," they said in one voice.  
Moses wrote down every word Yahweh declared,  
At dawn, he built an altar, twelve pillars prepared.  

Young men brought offerings, peace and burn,  
Half the blood in basins, half on the altar, discern.  
He read the covenant, the people vowed again,  
"We'll do what Yahweh says," they proclaimed, amen.  

Moses sprinkled blood, "This covenant's sign,"  
By Yahweh's word, this bond we define.  
Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu ascended the height,  
Seventy elders saw God, beneath His feet, sapphire light.  

No hand on nobles, they saw God, they dined,  
Yahweh said to Moses, "Stone tablets you'll find.  
Come to the mountain, learn law and command,  
Teach them to people, as I have planned."  

Moses with Joshua climbed God's domain,  
Told the elders, "Wait, we'll return again.  
Aaron and Hur will judge disputes wide,  
While upon God's mountain, I'll abide."  

Clouds covered Sinai, glory did bestow,  
Six days it lingered, then Yahweh's call did flow.  
To the people, His glory, like fire it blazed,  
Moses in the cloud, forty days did he praise.
