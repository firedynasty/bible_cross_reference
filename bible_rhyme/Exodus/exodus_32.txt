Moses was delayed, and the people grew wild,  
"Make us gods," they said, voices defiled.  
Aaron nodded, "Bring me your gold,"  
Earrings, rings, treasures untold.  

They brought him the gold, from ears it came,  
He molded a calf, they shouted its name.  
"These are your gods," they cried with glee,  
"That brought you out of Egypt, set you free."  

Aaron built an altar, "A feast we’ll raise,"  
Early they gathered, with offerings and praise.  
God saw from the mountain, spoke to Moses in flame,  
"Your people have turned, corrupted my name."  

"They’ve made a calf, worshiped and bowed,  
Said it's their god, voices loud and proud.  
I see they're stiff-necked, wrath burns hot,  
Leave me alone, consume them on the spot."  

But Moses pleaded, "God, calm your fire,  
Remember your promise, your holy desire.  
Abraham, Isaac, Israel you swore,  
Their children would grow, like stars evermore."  

God relented, his anger subdued,  
Moses descended, with God's tablets imbued.  
Joshua heard noise, thought war was near,  
But Moses knew it was dancing and cheer.  

He saw the calf, and his anger did spark,  
Threw down the tablets, shattered in the ark.  
Burned the calf, scattered it on the stream,  
Made Israel drink, to erase the dream.  

"What have you done?" Moses asked with a glare,  
Aaron shrugged, "You know their evil stare.  
They gave me gold, I threw it in flame,  
Out came this calf, I'm not to blame."  

Moses called, "Who stands with Yahweh, arise!"  
Levites gathered, swords ready, eyes wise.  
"Go through the camp," was the divine command,  
Three thousand fell by their hand.  

"Consecrate yourselves," Moses announced,  
"On Yahweh's side, your loyalty pronounced."  
Next day Moses climbed, to plead once more,  
"For this great sin, mercy implore."  

"Oh, they've sinned, made gods of gold,  
Forgive them, blot me out if I’m too bold."  
God replied, "Each sinner I'll judge,  
Lead them on, my angel won’t budge."  

God struck the people, a lesson told,  
For sins with the calf, their actions bold.
