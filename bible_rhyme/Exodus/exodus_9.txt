Yahweh told Moses, "Go to Pharaoh once more,  
Tell him Yahweh's words, let my people out the door.  

If you hold them back, the toll will be grim,  
Livestock hit by plague, from hoof to brim.  

In Israel's land, their herds will stay alive,  
Watch the distinction, see how they thrive."  

Yahweh set the time, "Tomorrow it's done,"  
And Egyptians' livestock fell, one by one.  

Pharaoh checked, Israel's herds were fine,  
But his heart was stubborn, wouldn't toe the line.  

Then Yahweh said, "Moses, Aaron, take ash,  
Sprinkle it high, let it spread in a flash.  

Boils and blisters on beast and man,  
Covering Egypt, a widespread span."  

Magicians couldn't stand, boils on their skin,  
Yet Pharaoh's heart was hard, wouldn't let them win.  

Yahweh told Moses, "Rise early, go back,   
Tell Pharaoh again, it's time to pack.  

Plagues on your heart, on your people and land,  
Know my power, by my hand I stand.  

Tomorrow's hail, fierce and grand,  
Bring livestock inside, heed the command."  

Some feared Yahweh, brought herds inside,  
Others left them out, ignoring the tide.  

Moses stretched his rod, skyward it went,  
Hail came down, thunder and lightning sent.  

Egypt was struck, fields laid low,  
In Goshen no hail, just peace and glow.  

Pharaoh called Moses, said, "I've sinned, I know,  
Yahweh is right, let your people go."  

Moses replied, "I'll pray for the storm to cease,  
But you and your folks, lack of fear won't increase."  

Flax and barley struck, wheat left alone,  
Moses prayed, the storm was gone.  

Pharaoh saw peace, yet hardened his heart,  
Refused to let Israel make a new start.
