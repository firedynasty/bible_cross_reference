In Egypt's land, Yahweh spoke to the pair,  
"This month is the start, so begin to prepare.  

On the tenth day, each house gets a lamb,  
Too small? Share with neighbors, that's the plan.  

No defects allowed, a male, one year old,  
From sheep or goats, as I have told.  

Keep it 'til the fourteenth, then at dawn's break,  
Kill it at evening, all Israel will partake.  

Blood on doorposts, lintel too, my command,  
In those homes, tonight, you shall stand.  

Roast the lamb, eat with bitter herbs by the fire,  
Unleavened bread, do not tire.  

Don't eat raw, nor boiled, roast it whole,  
Eat fast, staff in hand, for this night is the goal.  

"It's Yahweh’s Passover," said with might,  
"I’ll strike Egypt’s firstborn, man and beast that night.  

Blood on your doors is a sign you’re secure,  
When I pass, no plague will come, be sure.  

This day you'll remember, a feast forever,  
Through generations, you shall sever.  

Seven days, unleavened bread’s your meal,  
Remove the yeast, or you’ll face the seal.  

First and seventh days, holy convocations hold,  
Only food prep, no work, as I’ve told.  

The day I led armies out, remember this,  
Celebrate it by ordinance, none shall miss.  

From fourteenth evening to twenty-first stay,  
Eat no yeast, keep it away.  

"Now Moses called the elders, gave them the word,  
"Draw lambs for the Passover, as you’ve heard.  

Hyssop in blood, strike the door,  
Stay inside 'til morning, go out no more.  

Yahweh will pass, sparing homes marked in red,  
Destroyer won’t enter, see the blood instead.  

Observe this rite, you and your sons, it’s clear,  
When in promised lands, keep it dear.  

When asked by your children, tell them the tale,  
Yahweh’s Passover, when Egypt’s gods did fail."  

People bowed, worshiped in awe,  
Did as commanded, no flaw.  

At midnight, Yahweh struck with might,  
From Pharaoh’s palace to dungeon’s night.  

Great cries arose, death visited all,  
Pharaoh summoned Moses, gave the call.  

"Get out," he said, "Go serve your God,  
Take flocks, herds, leave our sod!"  

Egyptians urged, fearing death’s dark door,  
Israel took dough, couldn’t wait anymore.  

Asked for jewels, silver and gold in hand,  
Yahweh’s favor, they plundered the land.  

From Rameses to Succoth, they fled,  
Six hundred thousand, with children they led.  

Unleavened cakes they baked on the road,  
No time for bread, in haste they strode.  

Four hundred thirty years came to an end,  
Yahweh’s armies left, freedom to defend.  

A night to observe, for Yahweh’s grace,  
Brought out of Egypt, a remembered place.  

Yahweh spoke again, firm and clear,  
"No foreigner eats the Passover here.  

Circumcised servants may partake,  
Foreigners and hired hands must forsake.  

One house, one meal, no bone shall break,  
All Israel shall partake.  

A stranger who wishes to keep and partake,  
Must first be circumcised, for Yahweh's sake.  

One law for native and foreign, I decree,  
All Israel followed, set free."  

That day Yahweh led with a mighty hand,  
Israel's children out of Egypt's land.
