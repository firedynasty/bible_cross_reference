"First day, first month, raise it tall,"  
The tabernacle's up, where He'll call.  

Ark inside, wrapped in the veil,  
Table next, order prevail.  

"Bring the lamp, let it shine bright,  
Set the incense, make it right."  

Altar's there, near the tent door,  
Basin's filled, water's pour.  

"Anoint it all, make it holy,  
Every piece, sanctify solely."  

Aaron's sons, wash them clean,  
Holy garments, priestly scene.  

"Anoint them, serve in this space,  
Everlasting priesthood, their place."  

Moses acted as commanded, all was done,  
Tabernacle raised under the sun.  

Sockets laid, boards set wide,  
Pillars high, coverings tied.  

Ark inside, mercy seat on top,  
Veil set up, there won't be a stop.  

Table and bread, on the north side,  
Lamp stand south, light won't hide.  

Golden altar, incense burns sweet,  
Screen at the door, the work's complete.  

Offerings given, as Moses was told,  
Basin there, water cold.  

Hands and feet washed, pure and clean,  
Priests prepared, seen and unseen.  

Cloud descends, glory fills that space,  
Moses steps back, can't show his face.  

When cloud lifted, they moved ahead,  
But stayed put, if it still spread.  

By day a cloud, by night fire's glow,  
Israel watched, ready to go.
