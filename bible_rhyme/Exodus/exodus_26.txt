Craft the tabernacle, curtains wide and tall,  
Fine linen, blue, purple, scarlet, cherubs on the wall.  

Ten curtains long, each twenty-eight cubits stand,  
Four cubits wide, they fit the plan.  

Five curtains together, coupled side by side,  
Loops of blue on the edge, where they abide.  

Fifty loops here, fifty loops there,  
Golden clasps unite them, floating in the air.  

Make the goats' hair curtains, eleven in line,  
Thirty cubits each, four wide, all divine.  

Five and six, a double of the sixth,  
Loops on edges, nothing missed.  

Clasps of bronze, tents unite as one,  
Overhangs drape when the work's done.  

Rams' skins dyed red, sea cow hides on top,  
Acacia wood boards, upright they prop.  

Ten cubits high, each board stands,  
With two tenons, for steady hands.  

Twenty boards south, sockets silver bright,  
Two per board, holding tight.  

North side the same, west with six and two,  
Corners doubled, whole and true.  

Eight boards total, sockets plenty made,  
Acacia bars, this tabernacle's parade.  

Five bars here, five bars there,  
Middle bar runs through, everywhere.  

Overlay in gold, rings shining bold,  
Set it up just as you were told.  

A veil of blue, purple, scarlet twined,  
With cherubim by skill designed.  

Four pillars hold it, hooks of gold they bear,  
Separate the holy from most holy, a sacred pair.  

The mercy seat on the ark, in the most holy place,  
Table outside the veil, lamp stand keeps its grace.  

Screen the door with color, blue and purple bright,  
Five gold pillars, bronze sockets, all in sight.
