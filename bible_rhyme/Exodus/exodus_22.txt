If a man steals an ox, or a sheep, makes the sale,  
He owes five oxen, four sheep, a costly tale.  

Break into a house, and if he falls,  
No guilt for blood if within the walls.  

But if by morning he's caught in crime,  
He's guilty and must pay in time.  

If he’s got nothing, he'll be sold,  
To pay his debt, as laws unfold.  

If the stolen beast he still retains,  
Double the debt, that’s how it remains.  

If you let your beast stray to graze,  
Pay back with fields of the finest praise.  

Fire in the field, blazing, wild, and broad,  
The one who sparked it pays to God.  

Give your neighbor goods, they get nicked,  
Find the thief, pay double quick.  

If no thief, the owner swears,  
Before God, to settle affairs.  

In trespass matters, ox or sheep,  
To God they go, the verdict to keep.  

If you lend a beast and it's injured or dead,  
If the owner’s not there, pay what's said.  

But if the owner stands by its side,  
No need for payment, just abide.  

Entice a virgin, you must pay,  
A dowry for marriage in the proper way.  

Sorceress or beast with lust,  
Judgment comes, return to dust.  

Sacrifice to gods that aren’t Yahweh,  
Utter destruction, the law’s way.  

Treat strangers well, remember your plight,  
In Egypt, you were strangers in the night.  

Widows or orphans, don’t oppress or wrong,  
Cry to God, His wrath comes strong.  

Lend to the poor without a fee,  
Don’t charge interest, let them be.  

If you take a cloak for a debt unpaid,  
Return it by sunset, don’t be swayed.  

He cries to Me, I'll hear the plea,  
For I am gracious, hear and see.  

Don’t blaspheme God, respect the throne,  
Don’t curse leaders, it’s in the tone.  

Offer your first, both crop and son,  
Seven days with mother, then the duty’s done.  

Holy men, no torn meat to eat,  
Cast it to dogs, walk in God’s beat.
