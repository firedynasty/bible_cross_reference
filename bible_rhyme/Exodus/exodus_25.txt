Moses heard Yahweh's voice, so clear,  
"Tell Israel to bring offerings, draw near.  

Gold, silver, bronze, the list goes on,  
Scarlet, linen, with the finest yarns drawn.  

Rams' skins, acacia, oil for light,  
Spices sweet, onyx stones shining bright.  

A sanctuary's what they will create,  
For there I’ll dwell, with them relate.  

Show them the plan, from furniture to tent,  
Build it exactly, every detail meant.  

An ark of wood, two and a half cubits long,  
Overlaid with gold, both inside and strong.  

Four gold rings, placed on its feet,  
Poles of acacia, gold complete.  

Carry it carefully, covenant placed within,  
A mercy seat of pure gold, where it begins.  

Two cherubim, with wings spread wide,  
On either end, looking side by side.  

"Here I'll meet, from the seat I’ll speak,  
To guide them all, the words I’ll seek.  

A table of wood, gold over it laid,  
With rings for poles, craftsmanship displayed.  

Its dishes, spoons, and bowls of gold,  
Bread of presence always foretold.  

A lamp stand of gold, with branches six,  
Almond blossom cups, crafted with a fix.  

Seven lamps shall shine, brightly in view,  
All made as shown, in the pattern true."  

From the mountaintop came the grand design,  
For Israel’s journey, the sacred sign.
