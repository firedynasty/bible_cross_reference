Solomon loved women from every land,  
Though God warned Israel, "Don't take their hand.  

"They'll turn your heart to other gods," He'd say,  
But Solomon loved them anyway.  

Seven hundred wives, concubines a ton,  
They turned his heart, and Yahweh said, "You're done."  

He chased after Ashtoreth and Milcom’s call,  
Didn't follow Yahweh, did what was appall.  

Built high places for Chemosh and Molech's game,  
For his foreign wives, he bowed to their flame.  

Yahweh was angry, His heart filled with pain,  
For Solomon forgot God's word again.  

"Didn't I appear to you once, then twice?  
Commanded you to follow, but you paid the price.  

"For this, I'll tear the kingdom from your hand,  
Give it to your servant, like shifting sand.  

"Won't do it in your day, for David's sake,  
But your son's reign will surely break.  

"One tribe for David, my servant's light,  
For Jerusalem, my chosen in the night."  

Adversaries rose, Hadad came to fight,  
Once a child in Egypt, returned with might.  

Rezon the rebel gathered a crew,  
Hated Israel, made trouble anew.  

Jeroboam stood, a mighty man of fame,  
Solomon saw his strength, knew his name.  

Ahijah the prophet tore his robe in two,  
Gave ten pieces, "These tribes are for you."  

Yahweh's words echoed, "You'll reign, if you heed,  
Walk in my ways, and succeed.  

"I'll build you a house, steadfast and true,  
Afflict David's line, but not forever, too."  

Solomon sought Jeroboam’s end in sight,  
But he fled to Egypt, stayed out of the fight.  

Solomon's deeds, his wisdom full of praise,  
Written in the book of his days.  

Forty years he reigned, then took his rest,  
Rehoboam his son, continued the quest.
