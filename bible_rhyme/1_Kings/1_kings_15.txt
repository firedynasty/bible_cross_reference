In the eighteenth year of Jeroboam's time,  
Abijam reigned Judah, his mountain to climb.  
Three years in Jerusalem, he held his throne,  
Maacah's son, but his sins were his own.  

Not like David, his heart wasn't right,  
Yet for David's sake, God held the light.  
A lamp in the city, a son to succeed,  
Jerusalem's strength, a legacy indeed.  

War raged on between Rehoboam's line,  
The chronicles tell of Abijam's time.  
He slept with his fathers, in peace he lay,  
Asa his son took the throne that day.  

In Jeroboam's twentieth, Asa ruled strong,  
Forty-one years in a city where kings belong.  
He did what was right in the sight of the Lord,  
Cleared the idols, broke the sinful cord.  

Maacah removed, her image he burned,  
At Kidron's brook, her power spurned.  
Though high places stood, Asa's heart was true,  
Silver and gold to the temple he drew.  

War with Baasha, Israel's king,  
Ramah built up, a siege it would bring.  
Asa sought help, sent gifts of gold,  
To Ben Hadad in Syria, alliances bold.  

"Break with Baasha," Asa's decree,  
Ben Hadad struck cities, setting Asa free.  
Ramah abandoned, its stones Asa claimed,  
Building up Judah, his strength proclaimed.  

The chronicles tell of Asa's feats,  
But old age brought pain to his feet.  
Asa slept with his fathers, his reign complete,  
Jehoshaphat rose, Judah's new beat.  

Nadab ruled Israel, Asa's second year,  
But his evil deeds brought Baasha near.  
Baasha struck him down, claiming the crown,  
Jeroboam's house, he tore it down.  

Baasha's reign, like Jeroboam's sin,  
Provoked God's anger from deep within.  
The chronicles hold their deeds and fights,  
Through Asa and Baasha, enduring nights.
