David was old, couldn't get warm, wrapped in clothes, in a storm.  
"Find a young virgin," his servants said, "to keep the king from feeling dead."  

They searched far and wide, in Israel's land, found Abishag to lend a hand.  
Though she served him well, they didn't share a bed, just warmth from her stead.  

Adonijah, son of Haggith, rose up with pride, "I'll be king," he cried.  
Chariots, horsemen, fifty by his side, but David hadn't yet replied.  

Handsome like Absalom, he gathered allies, Joab and Abiathar joined his rise.  
But Zadok, Benaiah, Nathan stayed away; they weren't part of Adonijah's play.  

Adonijah held a feast, the stone of Zoheleth near,  
Yet left out Nathan, Solomon, his intentions clear.  

Nathan spoke to Bathsheba, "You heard the scene? Adonijah reigns, with David evergreen."  
"Tell the king of his promise to make Solomon great, while I confirm it, sealing his fate."  

Bathsheba approached, bowed low to the king, reminding him of the promise she'd bring.  
"Adonijah reigns, but Solomon's your choice, O king, speak with your voice."  

While she spoke, Nathan entered the room, confirming the scheme, Adonijah's assumed.  
David called Bathsheba back, swore by God's name, "Solomon will reign, it's not just a claim."  

"Call Zadok, Nathan, Benaiah," David said, "Anoint Solomon, crown his head."  
"Ride my mule to Gihon, let the trumpet sound, 'Long live Solomon!' let joy abound."  

Benaiah responded, "Amen, let it be, may Solomon's reign be greater than thee."  
So they took the mule, down to Gihon they went, Zadok anointed, and the trumpet was sent.  

The earth shook with joy, pipes played their tune, as Solomon's reign was made opportune.  
Adonijah and guests, hearing the noise, asked what caused such uproar to poise.  

Jonathan arrived with the news, "David made Solomon king, no time to lose."  
All were afraid, Adonijah feared, he clung to the altar, hoping to be spared.  

Solomon heard and assured his kin, "If he's worthy, no harm will begin."  
Adonijah bowed, respectful of the throne, and Solomon sent him, peace overthrown.
