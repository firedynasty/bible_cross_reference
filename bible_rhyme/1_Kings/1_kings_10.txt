Queen of Sheba heard of Solomon's fame,  
Came with questions, testing his name.  
Caravan of spices, gold, and stones,  
In Jerusalem, she laid out her tones.  

Solomon answered, nothing stayed hidden,  
His wisdom revealed, her doubts were overridden.  
She saw his house, his table spread wide,  
Servants in splendor, her wonder magnified.  

"No more spirit," in awe she cried,  
"Reports I heard, and truth wasn't denied.  
Not half was told, your wisdom's grand,  
Happy are those who in your court stand.  

Blessed be Yahweh, who set you high,  
To rule with justice, under the sky."  
She gave him gold, spices in great measure,  
Never again such spices, a rare treasure.  

Hiram's fleet brought almug trees,  
For the house and the king's harmonies.  
Solomon granted her every desire,  
To her own land, she did retire.  

Gold flowed in, six sixty-six talents a year,  
From traders, merchants, governors near.  
Bucklers and shields of beaten gold,  
In the Forest of Lebanon, a sight to behold.  

A throne of ivory, gold overlaid,  
With lions beside, a grand parade.  
Twelve lions on steps, never seen before,  
Solomon's kingdom, a legend and lore.  

Drinking vessels of gold, silver held no sway,  
Riches and wisdom, his every day.  
Fleet of Tarshish, treasures did bring,  
Solomon's name, the earth did sing.  

Chariots and horsemen, cities filled,  
Silver like stones, Jerusalem thrilled.  
Horses from Egypt, merchants did trade,  
To kings afar, deals they made.
