Solomon called the elders, they all came to the scene,  
In Jerusalem, for the ark, it was time to convene.  
The whole of Israel gathered in the seventh month,  
To bring the ark of Yahweh's covenant upfront.  

Priests and Levites lifted the ark up high,  
With holy vessels from the Tent, reaching for the sky.  
Sheep and cattle sacrificed, uncountable, immense,  
Before the holy ark, making Israel's presence intense.  

The priests set the ark in the sanctuary, its sacred dome,  
Under the wings of cherubim, the ark found its home.  
Long poles peeking from the holy space, yet unseen,  
Only the tablets of the covenant, kept pristine.  

As priests exited, a cloud filled Yahweh's abode,  
His glory so dense, they couldn't bear the load.  
Solomon spoke, "Yahweh dwells in darkness thick,  
I've built Your house forever, where Your light will stick."  

He turned to Israel, blessed them, and they stood,  
"Praise Yahweh," he declared, "for keeping promises good."  
From Egypt to now, no city was chosen,  
But David was made king, the divine plan not frozen.  

David dreamed of a house for Yahweh's name,  
But God chose his son to bring that dream to fame.  
"Yahweh's word is true," Solomon declared with pride,  
"I built this house, where His covenant does reside."  

Hands raised to heaven, Solomon began to pray,  
Acknowledging Yahweh's greatness every single day.  
"Keep promises to David, let his line hold strong,  
If his children walk right and avoid the wrong."  

"Can heaven itself contain God?" Solomon mused,  
Yet he asked for God’s ears, towards their prayers fused.  
"When people sin and face the enemy's might,  
If they turn here and pray, make their wrongs right."  

"If the skies hold back rain, famine strikes the land,  
Hear their prayers, teach them, let them understand.  
Foreigners too, who come to honor Your fame,  
Hear them from heaven, so they know Your name."  

"If captives repent, far from home they stay,  
Hear their cries and maintain their way.  
For they are Yours, Your treasured claim,  
Brought out of Egypt, by Your mighty name."  

Solomon finished, rose from his knees,  
Blessed the assembly, as they felt at ease.  
"Yahweh's words never fail, He's with us this day,  
May our hearts follow Him, in His statutes, we’ll stay."  

Sacrifices offered, in abundance they came,  
Dedicating Yahweh’s house, all in His name.  
Fourteen days of feast, joy spread wide and far,  
The people went home, with gladness in their hearts.
