Solomon ruled, the king of all the land,  
Princes and scribes stood at his command.  
Azariah, Elihoreph, Ahijah too,  
Jehoshaphat kept records, made them true.  

Benaiah led armies, strength in his hands,  
Zadok and Abiathar, priests in the lands.  
Azariah, Nathan's son, kept the officers straight,  
Zabud, the king’s friend, held a powerful fate.  

Ahishar ran the household with care,  
Adoniram made workers' burdens fair.  
Twelve officers served, bringing food in line,  
Each month one provided, the king's table to shine.  

From Ephraim to Jokmeam, names unfold,  
Ben Hur, Ben Deker, stories told.  
Ben Hesed, Ben Abinadab, held their ground,  
Baana, in Beth Shean, his duties renowned.  

Ben Geber in Ramoth Gilead staked his claim,  
Ahinadab in Mahanaim held a similar aim.  
Ahimaaz took Solomon's daughter as wife,  
In Naphtali he lived a prosperous life.  

Solomon’s rule stretched far and wide,  
From riverbanks to where the Philistines abide.  
Tribute flowed in, his kingdom secure,  
With wisdom and peace, his reign was pure.  

Daily provisions—a feast arrayed,  
Cattle and sheep, in abundance displayed.  
Dominion stretched from Tiphsah to Gaza's reach,  
His wisdom like sand on the seashore's beach.  

Forty thousand horses, chariots in rows,  
Twelve thousand riders, ready to go.  
Officers made sure the table stayed full,  
Barley and straw, the horses' pull.  

God gave him wisdom, like none before,  
He spoke proverbs and songs, a thousand more.  
From cedars to hyssop, he knew them all,  
People came from nations to hear his call.  

Solomon, wise king, his fame spread wide,  
Kings sent their envoys to sit by his side.
