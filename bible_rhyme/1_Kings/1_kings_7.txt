Solomon built a house, took thirteen years,  
Finished his crib, shed no tears.  

A Forest of Lebanon, cedar and stone,  
Pillars and beams, like a king on a throne.  

Covered in cedar, beams fifteen per row,  
Windows aligned, let the breezes flow.  

Porch with pillars, fifty by thirty,  
A throne of judgment, never dirty.  

His own pad and Pharaoh’s daughter's too,  
Cedar and costly stones shaping the view.  

Foundation laid with mighty stones,  
Ten cubits, eight cubits—each one honed.  

Cedar beams and costly fair,  
Three courses of stones lined with care.  

Summoned was Hiram, bronze in his hand,  
Skilled, wise, a worker so grand.  

Bronze pillars rose, eighteen cubits tall,  
Caps of five cubits, gracing them all.  

Checker work, chains, around they twine,  
Lily work capitals, oh so fine.  

Pillars named Jachin and Boaz in place,  
Lily tops finished with style and grace.  

A sea of bronze, ten cubits wide,  
Twelve oxen beneath, as if they guide.  

Thick as a hand, brim like a cup,  
Held two thousand baths, filled right up.  

Ten bases made, four cubits in length,  
Panels with lions showing their strength.  

Wheels of bronze, like chariots they rolled,  
Solid as stone, their story told.  

Each base with cherubim, palm trees, and more,  
A uniform casting, a measured score.  

Ten basins of bronze, forty baths each,  
Set side by side within easy reach.  

Hiram molded pots, shovels, and basins,  
Bronze work for the Lord’s holy places.  

Two pillars, bowls, networks, ornate,  
Four hundred pomegranates to decorate.  

Bases, basins, sea with twelve oxen,  
All burnished bronze, polished like toxin.  

Cast in the Jordan's clay grounds,  
So many vessels, their weight unbound.  

Golden altar and table for bread,  
Lamp stands shining, glowing ahead.  

Gold cups, snuffers, basins, and more,  
For the inner house of the Lord to adore.  

Finished was all Solomon's glorious task,  
Treasures of David in Yahweh's house, at last.
