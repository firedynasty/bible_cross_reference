Solomon made a deal with Egypt’s great king,  
Took Pharaoh’s daughter, as part of the ring.  

Brought her to the city, till his house was done,  
With Yahweh’s house and the wall, all under the sun.  

But folks kept sacrificing on the high hills,  
No house for Yahweh yet, to cure their ills.  

Solomon loved Yahweh, walked David’s path right,  
Except he burned incense in high places at night.  

To Gibeon he went, a high place so grand,  
A thousand burnt offerings he made by hand.  

In a dream at Gibeon, Yahweh came near,  
"Ask what you want, make your wish clear."  

Solomon spoke of his father’s kind heart,  
How he walked with God, right from the start.  

"You made me king, but I’m young and new,  
I need wisdom to judge, help me see through."  

Pleased with his wish, God said with delight,  
"You didn’t ask for riches, nor power to fight.  

I’ll give you wisdom, none before or to come,  
You’ll be unmatched, your reign won’t be undone.  

Riches and honor, I’ll give beyond measure,  
Walk in my ways, and lengthen your treasure."  

Solomon awoke, it was just a dream,  
Back to Jerusalem, to the ark’s gleam.  

Two women came forward, both with a tale,  
Each claimed the living child, as their travail.  

One woman said, "We lived in one place,  
Her baby died, now she wants to erase."  

The other argued, "No, the living one's mine!"  
They bickered and fought, a tangled line.  

"Bring me a sword," the king declared,  
"Cut the child in two, if justice be shared."  

The true mother cried, "Oh give her the child!  
Let him live, I can’t see him defiled."  

The king then spoke, "Give her the son,  
For her heart shows truth, she’s the one."  

All of Israel heard, saw wisdom’s crown,  
God’s justice in Solomon, renown spread ‘round.
