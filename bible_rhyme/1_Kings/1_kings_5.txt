Hiram, king of Tyre, sent word to his friend,  
He heard Solomon's reign had come to commence.  
Loved David, he did, with a loyal heart,  
Sent messengers swiftly, playing his part.  

Solomon spoke, sent a message back,  
"My father David, he faced attack,  
Couldn't build a house for Yahweh's name,  
Too many wars, too much to claim.  

But now God's given me peace and rest,  
No enemies here, no threats to test.  
I plan to build that house so grand,  
Just like Yahweh promised, by His hand.  

I need your cedar from Lebanon's tall,  
My servants will help, just give the call.  
Your Sidonians, the best with the trees,  
No one cuts timber with such ease."  

Hiram heard Solomon and joy did rise,  
"Blessed be Yahweh, wise son under skies.  
I'll do your will, send cedar and more,  
Cypress rafts to your designated shore.  

In return, bring food, my household's need,  
We'll trade in peace, in thought and deed."  
So timber flowed to Solomon's land,  
And food and oil went by Solomon's hand.  

Yahweh gave Solomon wisdom deep,  
Peace with Hiram, a treaty to keep.  
A levy of men, thirty thousand strong,  
To Lebanon they went, a month not long.  

Seventy thousand bore burdens great,  
Eighty thousand stone cutters, fate.  
The leaders, three thousand and three hundred more,  
Ruling over laborers, building Yahweh's floor.  

They cut large stones, precious and rare,  
To lay the foundation with utmost care.  
Builders from Solomon, Hiram, and Gebal,  
Together they worked, to raise that hall.
