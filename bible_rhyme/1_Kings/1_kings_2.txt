David felt his time, he knew he'd soon die,  
Called Solomon close, gave him wisdom to fly.  

"I'm headed down the path, like all do someday,  
Be strong, be a man, in every way.  

Walk in God's law, His statutes, His plan,  
Do this, you'll prosper, wherever you stand.  

His word to me will surely come true,  
If your heart stays faithful, none can undo."  

"But Joab, you know, his hands stained red,  
Abner, Amasa — both struck dead.  

Use your wisdom, let not his gray head  
Find peace in Sheol, his deeds must be read.  

Show kindness to Barzillai's kin,  
They sheltered me when Absalom sinned.  

And Shimei, who cursed me bad,  
I swore no sword, but you know what's had."  

David laid to rest, with fathers he sleeps,  
Forty years he reigned, his legacy deep.  

Solomon took the throne, firm and tight,  
Peace in the land, shining bright.  

Adonijah came, a request in mind,  
To Bathsheba he spoke, "Be kind.  

That kingdom was once mine, but turned away,  
Now just one favor, hear what I say.  

Ask Solomon for Abishag's hand,  
A small request, not a demand."  

Bathsheba to Solomon, the throne she faced,  
Asked her son, in a mother's grace.  

Solomon replied, his voice firm and grand,  
"Why ask for Abishag, the kingdom's next stand?  

Adonijah's words, against his own life,  
Today he shall meet the edge of a knife."  

Benaiah sent forth, swift and true,  
Adonijah fell, his end, he knew.  

Abiathar spared, sent to his fields,  
The prophecy fulfilled, Yahweh's word revealed.  

Joab fled to the altar, gripping tight,  
Benaiah came, in the Tent's holy light.  

"Come out!" he called, Joab refused,  
So there he fell, justice not confused.  

Shimei warned, stay within the city,  
For crossing the brook, there'd be no pity.  

But when his slaves fled, he gave chase,  
Solomon heard, called him to face.  

"Why break your vow, when clear it was told?"  
Shimei fell, his tale now old.  

The kingdom stood firm, Solomon's strong hand,  
Peace and order across the land.
