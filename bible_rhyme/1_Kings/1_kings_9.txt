Solomon finished building with grand design, Yahweh’s house and the king’s combined.  
Yahweh appeared once more, as he'd done before, in a moment divine, at Gibeon’s door.  

He said, “I've heard your prayer, to set my name there.  
This house is made holy, with my eyes and heart, I’ll forever care.”  

“Walk like David did, with integrity and might,  
Keep my statutes and commandments in your sight.”  

“I’ll establish your throne, your kingdom will never fall,  
As David was promised, so it shall be for all.”  

“But turn away from me, and serve gods not true,  
I’ll cut Israel off, and cast away this view.”  

“Though the house stands tall, strangers will scorn and laugh,  
They’ll wonder why this land suffers Yahweh’s wrath.”  

“They’ll say, ‘Because they left Yahweh, who freed their kin,  
Embraced other gods, so this destruction came in.’”  

Twenty years passed by, two houses Solomon made,  
With cedar, cypress, and gold, by Hiram's aid.  

Solomon gifted Hiram cities of Galilee,  
Hiram didn’t like them, “Cabul” they’d be.  

With gold he sent, one hundred twenty fold,  
For all the work and the stories told.  

Pharaoh’s forces took Gezer by fire and sword,  
A wedding gift to Solomon's bride, Egypt's accord.  

Solomon built up cities and storage space,  
For horses and chariots, across the place.  

People left from old tribes, not Israel’s sons,  
Made bondservants still, from battles not won.  

Israel's children weren’t servants, they were warriors brave,  
Chiefs over labor, in the work they gave.  

Pharaoh’s daughter moved to a house built anew,  
Solomon constructed Millo, as he was due.  

He offered sacrifices on Yahweh’s altar,  
Three times a year, his faith didn’t falter.  

Solomon built a fleet by the Red Sea’s shore,  
Hiram’s sailors joined, with knowledge galore.  

They traveled to Ophir, fetching gold so bright,  
Four hundred twenty talents, a treasure in sight.
