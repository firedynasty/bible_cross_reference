Yahweh's word came strong, to Jehu it was sent,  
"From dust I raised you up, as Israel's prince you were meant.  
But you walked like Jeroboam, made my people stray,  
Provoked my anger, so your house I'll sweep away.  

Dogs will feast on those in the city who fall,  
Birds will devour the rest beyond the wall."  
Baasha's deeds and might, in chronicles they write,  
He slept where his fathers lay, Elah took the right.  

Yahweh spoke again to Jehu, prophet's call,  
Baasha's house of sin, destined to fall.  
In Asa's twenty-sixth year, Elah took the throne,  
For two years he reigned, but wasn't left alone.  

Zimri, captain bold, conspired in Tirzah's night,  
Elah drank and stumbled, Zimri seized the fight.  
He struck him down in Asa's twenty-seventh year,  
Claimed the throne for himself, but brought more fear.  

Zimri wiped Baasha's kin, just like Yahweh said,  
For sins they laid on Israel, in vanities they led.  
Elah's record, deeds all noted down,  
In chronicles of Israel's kings, where stories abound.  

Zimri's rule short-lived, just seven days in fame,  
Omri rose with Israel's might, laid claim to the name.  
Tirzah besieged, Zimri saw the fall,  
Into fire he went, his sins recalled.  

Israel's hearts divided, Omri or Tibni's way,  
Omri's strength prevailed, Tibni's end in dismay.  
Omri reigned in Samaria, twelve years' span,  
Built the city up, Samaria began.  

But he walked in Jeroboam's path, evil in Yahweh's sight,  
Provoked the Lord's anger, with vanities of night.  
Chronicles hold his might, his deeds in view,  
Omri slept with fathers, Ahab's reign was due.  

Ahab took the throne, wickedness increased,  
Married Jezebel, Baal worship's feast.  
Built altars high, God's anger he did stir,  
More than kings before him, his sins did incur.  

In those days Hiel built Jericho again,  
At the cost of sons, his heart knew pain.  
Fulfilled the word spoken long ago,  
By Joshua's mouth, Jericho's woe.
