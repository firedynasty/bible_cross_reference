Yo, four hundred and eighty years after Egypt's release,  
In Solomon's fourth year, he started the piece.  
Month of Ziv, the second one, time to begin,  
Yahweh's house rising, where His glory would spin.  

Sixty cubits long, twenty wide, thirty high,  
Solomon built it to kiss the sky.  
The porch was twenty cubits long, ten wide,  
Fixed lattice windows to let light inside.  

Against the walls, floors wrapped around,  
Temple and sanctuary, side rooms were found.  
Five, six, seven cubits, each floor grew,  
Beams offset, not to pierce through.  

Quarry stones, no hammer's sound,  
Silent construction, sacred ground.  
Winding stairs, middle and third,  
Cedar beams, Yahweh's word heard:  

"Walk in my statutes, keep my decree,  
I promise to dwell and never flee."  
So Solomon built, finished the task,  
Cedar boards inside, walls unmasked.  

Cedar carvings, buds, flowers in bloom,  
All hidden stone, gold within the room.  
Ark set in the sanctuary, most holy space,  
Twenty cubits square, gold-covered place.  

Cherubim of olive wood, wings spread wide,  
Touching walls, golden wings side by side.  
Every wall carved, cherubim, palms, flowers,  
Gold-covered floors, inside and outside towers.  

Olive wood doors, carved, overlaid with gold,  
Cherubim and palms, stories told.  
Inner court, stone and cedar beams laid,  
Seven years in Bul, the house then displayed.
