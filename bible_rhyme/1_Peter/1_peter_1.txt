Yo, Peter's speaking to the chosen crew,  
Living as strangers, but God's got you.  

Grace and peace, may it multiply,  
Sanctified in Spirit, reach for the sky.  

Blessed be God, Father of our Lord,  
Born again with hope, through Jesus restored.  

An inheritance pure, eternal, won't fade,  
Protected by God's power, that's how it's made.  

Rejoice now, though trials be your test,  
Faith like gold, refined, you’re blessed.  

In Christ unseen, you still believe,  
With joy unspeakable, your soul receives.  

Prophets searched, sought the grace,  
Predicted Christ's pain, then the glory's embrace.  

It’s revealed to you, for your sake,  
Angels watch closely, eager to partake.  

Prepare your minds, with sober heart,  
Hope in grace, let the action start.  

Be holy, as He's holy, in behavior pure,  
The call is clear, let holiness endure.  

"Be holy," He said, for I am holy,  
Live with reverence, keep it low-key.  

Redeemed not with gold, but with Christ's blood,  
From futile ways, rise above the flood.  

Foreknown before the world began,  
Revealed for you, the divine plan.  

Believe in God, who raised Him high,  
That faith and hope in God never die.  

Purified souls, obey the truth,  
Love each other, with heart and youth.  

Born again, incorruptible seed,  
The Word of God, our eternal creed.  

"All flesh is grass," it withers away,  
But God's Word endures, come what may.  

The Good News preached, forever true,  
This is the Word, delivered to you.
