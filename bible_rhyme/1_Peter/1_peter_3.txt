Ladies, align with your husbands' grace,  
Win them over, no need to embrace debate face-to-face.  

Let your true beauty shine from within,  
A gentle spirit's worth more than gold or a pin.  

Like the holy women of old, pure and true,  
Sarah called Abraham "lord," that's your cue.  

Men, live with your wives, honor them right,  
Together in grace, keep your prayers in sight.  

Be compassionate, loving, like brothers, it's clear,  
Bless those who curse you, hold them dear.  

"He who loves life, keep your tongue free from deceit,  
Turn from evil, do good, let peace be your beat."  

The Lord's eyes watch the righteous, His ears hear,  
But against the wicked, His face draws near.  

Fear not what they fear, be troubled no more,  
In your heart, sanctify the Lord.  

Be ready to answer, with humility and grace,  
Keep a good conscience, let the world see your faith's trace.  

Suffering for good? If it's God's will, stay strong,  
Christ suffered too, showing what's right from wrong.  

He preached to spirits once locked down,  
Eight saved through water, while the world did drown.  

Baptism's the symbol, not just a cleansing show,  
A good conscience through Jesus, risen, we know.  

He's at God's right hand, with powers that bow,  
Angels and authorities, all subject now.
