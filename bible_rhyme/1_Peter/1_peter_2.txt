Put away wickedness, deceit, hypocrisy too,  
Like newborn babies, crave the Word that's true.  

Taste the Lord's grace, come to Him, alive,  
Rejected by men, but chosen to thrive.  

Living stones, He builds a house that's spiritual,  
A holy priesthood, with sacrifices acceptable.  

Scripture says, "In Zion, a stone I lay,  
Chosen, precious, believe and won't sway."  

For believers, honor stands clear,  
But disobedient ones, the stone they fear.  

"A rejected stone, now cornerstone," they say,  
A stumbling block, an offense in their way.  

You're a chosen race, a priesthood royal,  
A holy nation, in darkness no more embroiled.  

Once not a people, now God's own,  
Mercy found, in His light you're shown.  

Beloved, as strangers, abstain from flesh's fight,  
Live good among nations, make wrongs right.  

Submit to authority for the Lord's sake,  
Kings and governors, justice they make.  

God's will is silence to fools through good deeds,  
Use freedom right, be bondservants indeed.  

Honor all, love brethren, fear God as King,  
Servants, respect all masters, endure the sting.  

Commendable is suffering with conscience clear,  
Not for sin, but doing good, persevere.  

You were called, as Christ also bore,  
Suffering, He set an example to explore.  

No sin, no deceit in His mouth found,  
When cursed, He didn't turn it around.  

He bore our sins, on that tree so grand,  
Died to sin, live righteous, healed by His hand.  

You were like lost sheep, astray,  
Now to the Shepherd, your souls stay.
