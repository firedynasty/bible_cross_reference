Pentecost hit, they gathered as one,  
A mighty wind from the sky, like a race begun.  

Flames like tongues sat on each of their heads,  
Filled with the Spirit, speaking languages spread.  

In Jerusalem, from nations they came,  
Hearing their languages, amazed by the same.  

"Galileans speaking our native tones,  
Parthians, Medes, and Elamites' zones.  

From Judea, Asia, Egypt's land,  
Rome, Crete, Arabia, all understand."  

Some were amazed, others mocked with disdain,  
"They're filled with new wine," they claimed in vain.  

Peter rose with the eleven, his voice loud and clear,  
"Men of Judea, let my words ring in your ear.  

They're not drunk as you say, it's just morning light,  
But what the prophet Joel spoke is in sight:  

'In the last days, God will pour Spirit on all,  
Sons and daughters will prophesy, visions will call.  

Wonders above, signs down below,  
Sun darkens, moon's blood will show.  

Who calls on the Lord's name will be saved,  
Through Jesus, mighty wonders God gave.  

You crucified Him, but God raised Him high,  
Death couldn't hold Him, He lives, can't die.  

David spoke of Him, foresaw resurrection's might,  
His flesh didn't decay, now in God's sight.  

Exalted on high, Spirit poured from above,  
This is what you see, proof of His love.  

Jesus, whom you crucified, Lord and Christ He stands,  
Israel, know this, by God's own hands."  

The crowd was pierced, hearts deeply moved,  
"Brothers, what do we do?" they asked, approved.  

"Repent, be baptized in Jesus' name,  
For forgiveness, the Spirit's gift you claim.  

This promise for you and your children to hold,  
For all far off, as the Lord foretold."  

Peter preached, "Save from this twisted age,"  
Three thousand baptized, a new chapter's page.  

Together they learned, breaking bread in accord,  
In fellowship, in prayer, praising the Lord.  

Fear and wonders spread through their days,  
Sharing and caring in generous ways.  

Daily in the temple, gladness in heart,  
The Lord added to them, a new life to start.
