Prophets and teachers in Antioch's scene,  
Barnabas, Simeon, and Saul so keen.  

Fasting and praying, the Spirit spoke,  
"Separate these two for the task, that's no joke."  

Hands laid, prayers strong, off they go,  
Sent by the Spirit, His will to show.  

To Seleucia, then Cyprus's shore,  
Preaching in synagogues, God's word to explore.  

In Paphos they met a sorcerer sly,  
Bar Jesus, false prophet, spinning his lie.  

Sergius Paulus, the proconsul wise,  
Called for Saul and Barnabas, truth to realize.  

Elymas the sorcerer tried to mislead,  
But Paul, filled with Spirit, took the lead.  

"You son of the devil, stop your charade,  
The Lord's hand against you, sight will fade."  

Darkness fell, he stumbled around,  
The proconsul believed, truth he found.  

From Paphos they sailed, onward they went,  
To Perga, then Antioch, on mission bent.  

In the synagogue, Paul took his stand,  
"Listen, O Israel, hear God's grand plan.  

Our fathers He chose, by His might they were led,  
Forty years in the desert, they followed, they tread.  

He gave them judges, then a king they sought,  
Saul first, then David, a man of God's thought.  

From David's line came salvation's plan,  
Through Jesus, the promise to every man.  

John preached repentance, paving the way,  
"One greater is coming," he would say.  

Jerusalem rulers, blind to the truth,  
Condemned the innocent, God's own proof.  

They laid Him in a tomb, lifeless and cold,  
But God raised Him up, as prophets foretold.  

Witnessed by many, alive He stood,  
The promise fulfilled, God's word is good.  

Through Jesus, forgiveness proclaimed,  
The law surpassed, by faith you're named.  

Beware, prophets warned, don't scoff or ignore,  
A work He declares, believe and explore.  

Gentiles rejoiced, glad in their heart,  
God's word spreading, no force could part.  

Jews stirred up strife, envy burned bright,  
But Paul and Barnabas spoke truth and light.  

"First to you, now to the Gentiles we turn,  
A light to the nations, salvation's concern."  

Persecution came, but they moved on,  
Joy in their hearts, Spirit strong.  

Dust off their feet, to Iconium they went,  
With joy and the Spirit, their lives were spent.
