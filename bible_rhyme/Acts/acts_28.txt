We landed on Malta, the island's name we learned,  
The locals showed kindness, as the fire burned.  
Paul gathered sticks, laid them on the flame,  
A viper struck his hand, but he stayed the same.  

The natives whispered, "This man must be cursed,  
Escaped the sea, but Justice will do its worst."  
Paul shook the snake off into the blazing fire,  
He felt no harm, watching eyes filled with desire.  

They thought he'd swell, maybe drop down dead,  
But when nothing happened, "He's a god," they said.  
Nearby lands owned by Publius, the chief so grand,  
Welcomed us warmly, in his house we did stand.  

Publius's father lay sick, fever gripping tight,  
Paul prayed, laid hands, and restored his light.  
Then all the sick came, and Paul healed each one,  
They honored us well for the good deeds done.  

After three months, we sailed on from that shore,  
In Alexandria's ship, known by "The Twin Brothers" lore.  
Touched Syracuse, stayed for three days,  
Circled to Rhegium, where the south wind plays.  

Reached Puteoli, found brothers, stayed seven days,  
Then onward to Rome, where courage found its ways.  
Brothers greeted us at the Appian Market's call,  
Paul thanked God, heart strengthened, standing tall.  

In Rome, handed to the guard, the prisoners were,  
But Paul stayed with a soldier, no chains to deter.  
Three days later, the Jewish leaders he called,  
Explained his case, how to Caesar he was hauled.  

"No letters against you," the leaders said,  
"But we want to hear about this sect so widespread."  
Paul testified from morning to night,  
Some believed, some left, their minds in a fight.  

"The Spirit spoke through Isaiah," Paul declared,  
"Hearing but not understanding, hearts hardly cared.  
God's salvation is sent, to nations they'll receive,  
They will listen, their hearts won't deceive."  

Two years Paul stayed, in his rented place,  
Preaching boldly of Christ, sharing grace.  
God's Kingdom proclaimed, with no restraint,  
Paul's courage unwavering, a steadfast saint.
