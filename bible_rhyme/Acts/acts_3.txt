Peter and John, the temple's hour was near,  
The ninth hour, to pray, they drew near.  

A man lame from birth, placed each day,  
By the Beautiful Gate, where he'd lay.  

He saw them coming, their presence felt,  
Asked for gifts, longing for help.  

Peter fixed his gaze, with John's embrace,  
"Look at us," he said, face to face.  

The man hoped for coins, a generous token,  
But Peter's words, a miracle was spoken:  

"No silver or gold, I've none to give,  
But in Jesus' name, rise and live!"  

He took his hand, lifted him high,  
Strength filled his feet, he reached for the sky.  

Leaping with joy, he walked inside,  
Praising God loudly, with every stride.  

The people saw him, they recognized,  
The beggar now healed, open-eyed.  

Amazed and filled with wonder they stood,  
At Solomon’s porch, the crowd now good.  

Peter spoke, "Why are you amazed?  
Not by our power, this miracle raised.  

The God of our fathers, Abraham's claim,  
Glorified Jesus, whom you did shame.  

You denied the Righteous, chose a thief,  
Killed the Life Prince, but God brought relief.  

Raised Him from death, we stand here today,  
Faith in His name made him this way.  

Brothers, you acted in ignorance, unaware,  
But God's plan, the prophets did declare.  

"Repent and turn, let sins fade away,  
Times of refreshing will come your way.  

God will send Jesus, ordained, to restore,  
Prophets told of this time before.  

Moses said, a prophet will arise,  
Listen to him, the wise recognize.  

Reject him, you'll face destruction's fate,  
But blessings through Abraham, don't hesitate.  

God raised Jesus, sent Him to bless,  
Turn from wickedness, find righteousness."
