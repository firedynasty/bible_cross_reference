Paul came to Derbe and Lystra, found Timothy there,  
A disciple with a Jewish mom, but his dad was elsewhere.

The folks at Lystra spoke well of this young man,  
So Paul took him along, part of his plan.

For the Jews' sake, he had Timothy circumcised,  
For his Greek father was known, couldn't disguise.

They traveled city to city, sharing decrees,  
Strengthening churches, faith spreading with ease.

Through Phrygia and Galatia, they tried to preach,  
But the Holy Spirit said, "Asia's out of reach."

In Mysia they aimed for Bithynia in sight,  
But again, the Spirit blocked their flight.

Down to Troas, where Paul saw a vision clear,  
A Macedonian man pleading, “Come over here.”

They set sail, knowing their mission was true,  
To Macedonia, preaching the news, breaking through.

In Philippi, a Roman colony, they stayed,  
On Sabbath, by the river, women gathered and prayed.

Lydia, a merchant in purple, heard Paul's part,  
The Lord opened her ears, touched her heart.

Baptized with her house, she begged them, pleading,  
"If I've been faithful, come stay, I'm needing."

As they went to prayer, a girl with a spirit came by,  
Crying, "These men show salvation on high!"

Days of this, till Paul, annoyed, turned around,  
"In Jesus' name, come out!" And the spirit unbound.

Her masters saw profit vanish in the air,  
Seized Paul and Silas, dragged them in despair.

Before the rulers, accusations loud and clear,  
"These Jews disturb our city, customs we won't adhere."

Crowd in fury, magistrates stripped them down,  
Beaten with rods, tossed in jail, in the center of town.

In the stocks, they prayed and sang through night’s dark,  
An earthquake hit, breaking chains, making its mark.

The jailer woke, saw doors open wide,  
Drew his sword, ready to die, terrified inside.

But Paul shouted, "Don’t harm, we’re all here!"  
Lights called, he trembled, feeling fear.

"What must I do to be saved?" he cried, broken,  
"Believe in Jesus, and salvation’s awoken."

They shared the Lord’s word with him and his kin,  
He washed their wounds, baptized, free from sin.

Rejoicing in faith, he set food at the table,  
Morning came, and the jailer was able.

The magistrates sent, "Let those men go free,"  
But Paul said, "Not secretly, come set us free!"

Hearing they were Romans, the rulers were in fright,  
Came and begged them, asking them to take flight.

Released from prison, they went to Lydia’s place,  
Encouraged the brothers, then left with grace.
