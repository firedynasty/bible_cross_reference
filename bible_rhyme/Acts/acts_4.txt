The priests and the captain came, feeling the heat,  
Sadducees were mad 'cause of what they teach on the street.  

They grabbed Peter and John, put 'em in a cell,  
But five thousand heard and believed, that's swell.  

Next day in Jerusalem, rulers gathered 'round,  
Annas, Caiaphas, and kin, ready to throw down.  

They stood Peter in the center, asked him with disdain,  
"By what power or name did you heal and proclaim?"  

Filled with Spirit, Peter said, standing tall,  
"If a cripple is healed, it's by Jesus, y'all.  

In the name of Christ, whom you crucified,  
God raised Him up, and in Him, this man’s revived.  

The stone you builders disregarded, now it’s key,  
No other name can save, take it from me."  

They saw the boldness, marveled at the scene,  
Unlearned men with Jesus, they made the connection clean.  

The healed man stood there, silent proof in sight,  
Council conferred, seeking to dim the light.  

"We can't deny the miracle that's been done,  
But let's stop the spread, keep the people from this one."  

They warned them, "No more talk, no Jesus name speak,"  
But Peter and John replied, "We can’t be weak.  

Judge for yourselves, is it God or you we should heed?  
We’ll testify to what we saw, sowing God's seed."  

Threats made again, but they let them go,  
The people praised God for the miracle show.  

Back to their people, the apostles relayed,  
The threats from the leaders, and strong they prayed:  

"O Lord, Creator of the earth and the sea,  
Why do nations rage, plotting against Thee?"  

Herod and Pilate, gathered in this plot,  
Against Jesus, Your anointed, they sought.  

"See their threats, Lord, grant us boldness to proclaim,  
Stretch Your hand, heal, glorify Your Servant’s name."  

Place shaking, Spirit filled, they spoke with might,  
The believers united, hearts and souls held tight.  

No one claimed a thing as solely their own,  
Shared all they had, love and grace were shown.  

Apostles testified with power, in the Lord they basked,  
No one among them lacked, for all needs they asked.  

Barnabas sold his field, laid the money down,  
At the apostles' feet, in the Spirit's renown.
