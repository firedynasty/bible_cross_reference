Paul faced Agrippa, ready to defend,  
His hand stretched out, the truth to send.  

"Happy to speak, O King, to you today,  
Concerning accusations the Jews relay.  

You know their customs, you know their lore,  
So hear me patiently, it's you I implore.  

From youth in Jerusalem, my path I walked,  
A Pharisee's life, where devotion stalked.  

Now judged for hope, the promise divine,  
Our twelve tribes have sought it, through time.  

Why doubt God can raise the dead?  
With proof and promise being widespread.  

I fought the name of Jesus with zeal,  
Persecuted believers with misguided deal.  

In synagogues, I pushed them to blaspheme,  
Chased them to cities, rage in my scheme.  

On Damascus road, with power and might,  
A heavenly light blazed, brighter than bright.  

We fell down, sky's voice did awake,  
'Saul, Saul, why this path you take?'  

'Who are you, Lord?' I inquired,  
'I'm Jesus, whom you've conspired.  

But rise, stand strong, my message be clear,  
To witness to all, from far and near.  

Gentiles and kin, open their eyes,  
From darkness to light, let them rise.  

I obeyed, King, that vision so grand,  
Declared repentance, throughout the land.  

For this, the Jews seized me in fury,  
But God's help steadied my jury.  

Prophets and Moses, I preach their claim,  
Christ's suffering, resurrection, the flame.  

Festus called me mad, bold with disdain,  
'Not crazy,' I said, 'truth remains plain.  

King knows these truths, not from the dark,  
Publicly spoken, all left a mark.  

Do you believe, O King, the prophets true?  
I know you do, their words come through.'  

Agrippa queried, 'Trying to convert with might?'  
Paul wished all were like him, but without chains tight.  

The king rose, with Bernice and crew,  
Decided Paul did nothing death could ensue.  

Agrippa to Festus, with a sigh so deep,  
'If not for Caesar, this man we'd release to keep.'"
