The high priest asked, "Is this the truth you know?"  
Stephen stood up, "Brothers, let me show.  

God spoke to Abraham in a distant land,  
Said, 'Leave your kin, follow my command.'  

He left the Chaldaeans, settled in Haran,  
Till God moved him to this promised land.  

No inheritance given, not even a place,  
Promised it for his seed by God's grace.  

God said his offspring would face tribulation,  
Enslaved and mistreated for generations.  

'I’ll judge that nation,' declared the Lord,  
'Then they'll serve me, by my word.'  

The covenant of circumcision, Abraham received,  
Isaac, then Jacob, through whom we believed.  

The patriarchs, in jealousy, sold Joseph away,  
But God stayed with him every day.  

Joseph rose high in Pharaoh’s land,  
Then famine struck, as God had planned.  

Jacob heard of grain, sent his sons to seek,  
Joseph revealed himself, not weak.  

Brought his family, seventy-five strong,  
In Egypt, they settled, it didn’t take long.  

But soon a king rose who knew not the past,  
Oppressed our fathers, made life hard and fast.  

Moses was born, a child of great light,  
Raised by Pharaoh’s daughter, trained for the fight.  

At forty, he sought his kin, saw their pain,  
Struck an Egyptian, justice sought to claim.  

His people pushed him, 'Who made you the judge?'  
Moses fled to Midian, where he'd find God's nudge.  

Forty years passed, in Sinai’s wild land,  
God's flame in the bush, His voice took a stand.  

'I am the God of your fathers,' He declared,  
'Releasing my people, to Egypt you’ll be spared.'  

This Moses, once refused and scorned,  
God sent back, His power adorned.  

He led them out, with signs and wonders so grand,  
Through sea and desert, by divine hand.  

Yet they turned back, hearts to Egypt’s ways,  
Made a calf, rejoiced in their own praise.  

God turned them over, idols they would choose,  
A warning, His presence they might lose.  

The tabernacle Moses built by God’s design,  
Carried through Joshua's time, a sign divine.  

Solomon built a house, but God’s not contained,  
“Heaven’s my throne, earth’s where I’ve reigned.  

You stiff-necked people, resist till the end,  
As your fathers did, so again you offend.  

The prophets they killed, and now Him you betray,  
The Righteous One you slew, you turned away.”  

When they heard these words, their hearts were torn,  
They gnashed their teeth, full of scorn.  

Stephen, full of Spirit, gazed up high,  
Saw God’s glory, Jesus by His side.  

“I see the heavens open wide,” he cried,  
The Son of Man at God’s right side.  

They rushed at him, stopped their ears from the sound,  
Dragged him out, stoned him to the ground.  

At Saul’s feet, their garments were laid,  
As Stephen prayed, his peace displayed.  

“Lord Jesus, my spirit receive,”  
He knelt, forgiving those who didn’t believe.  

He cried, “Lord, this sin don’t hold against them!”  
Then fell asleep, his soul to God did commend.
