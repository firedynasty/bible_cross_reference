Paul faced the council, standing tall and true,  
"Brothers, my conscience is clear before God and you."  
Ananias ordered a strike on Paul's face,  
Paul shot back, "God'll hit you in disgrace!"  

"Whitewashed wall, do you judge by the law's might,  
Yet command a strike that ain't right?"  
"Do you speak against the high priest?" they cried,  
Paul replied, "I didn't know, I abide.  

For it is written, 'Speak no ill of the chief,  
That's the word, no room for disbelief.'"  
Paul saw the divide, Pharisees from Sadducees,  
He shouted out, "On resurrection, judge me, please!"  

A clash arose, the crowd was torn,  
Pharisees believed, but Sadducees scorned.  
Scribes of the Pharisees stood to say,  
"Let him be, if an angel showed the way."  

Commotion stirred, the officer feared,  
Paul might be harmed; he intervened and steered.  
In the barracks, Paul found peace that night,  
The Lord said, "Cheer up, you'll testify in Rome's light."  

By day, a plot thickened, forty men swore,  
Not to eat or drink till Paul's no more.  
They planned with leaders, a deadly snare,  
"We'll strike him down, with no time to spare."  

Paul's nephew heard and ran to the cell,  
Told Paul the plot, told him well.  
Paul called a centurion, "Take this lad,  
He's got something urgent, and it's bad."  

The officer listened, hand in hand,  
"What's your news? Help me understand."  
"They're lying in wait, more than forty,  
Don't trust their words, they're ready to be naughty."  

Officer warned, "Don't spill a word,  
Keep this secret, let nothing be heard."  
He called for soldiers, two hundred strong,  
With horsemen and spears, to move Paul along.  

To Felix, he'd write, the truth set to sail,  
"Paul's a Roman, saved him from their wail."  
Soldiers moved by night with skill,  
To Antipatris, they climbed the hill.  

Caesarea reached, the letter was shown,  
Felix read, and his tone was grown.  
"What province?" he asked, from Cilicia you hail,  
"When accusers come, your case will prevail."  

Paul was held in Herod's halls,  
Awaiting justice, within those walls.
