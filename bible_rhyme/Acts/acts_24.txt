After five days, Ananias came with his crew,  
Elders and Tertullus, they'd something to do.  

Informing the governor, with Paul in their sights,  
Tertullus spoke, setting the stage for the fights.  

"By your great foresight, peace we embrace,  
Prosperity flows, thanks to your grace."  

"But let's not delay, hear what we say,  
This man’s a plague, incites in a big way."  

A ringleader of Nazarenes, causing much dread,  
He tried to profane the temple, that's what he said.  

The Jews joined the attack, confirming the charge,  
Paul stood up, ready to barge.  

"Felix, for years you've judged this land,  
I make my defense, with confidence at hand."  

"Twelve days since I went to Jerusalem's heart,  
No disputes, no crowds, I played no part."  

"They’ve got no proof, just empty claims,  
But I follow the Way, with no shame."  

"Believing the law and prophets, I confess,  
Hope in resurrection, the just and unjust, I profess."  

"My conscience is clear, before God, before men,  
Gifts for the needy, that’s where I've been."  

"They found me purified, without a riot,  
Those from Asia should speak, if they stay quiet."  

"Unless it’s this, the resurrection theme,  
For that, I’m judged, it seems."  

Felix knew the Way, but pushed pause,  
"Wait for Lysias, then decide on the cause."  

Paul kept in custody but had his space,  
Friends could visit, he wasn't erased.  

Felix with Drusilla, called Paul near,  
He spoke of faith, Felix quaked in fear.  

"Not now, Paul, I'll call you in time,"  
Hoping for money, his thoughts not in line.  

Two years later, Felix stepped away,  
Festus took over, but Paul stayed.
