Herod was oppressing, James was slain,  
The Jews were pleased, so he seized Peter, again.  

Locked up tight in a prison's hold,  
Four squads of soldiers, mighty and bold.  

The church was praying, night and day,  
For Peter's freedom, they did stay.  

That night, Herod planned a show,  
But an angel came with a glorious glow.  

“Stand up quick!” the angel did declare,  
Chains fell off, into the air.  

“Get dressed, put on your sandals tight,”   
“Follow me, into the night.”  

Past guards, through the iron gate,  
Opened by itself, no need to wait.  

Peter thought he was in a dream,  
The angel vanished, like a beam.  

Awake he was, in the city so wide,  
“The Lord has delivered me!” he cried.  

To Mary's house, he made his way,  
Where prayers were said, night and day.  

Rhoda heard his voice, in joy she ran,  
“Peter's at the gate!” was her loud plan.  

“You're crazy!” they said, but she was true,  
Peter kept knocking, until they knew.  

Amazed they were, at his tale of release,  
“Tell James and the brothers, let there be peace.”  

Herod searched, but Peter was gone,  
The guards faced wrath, with the breaking dawn.  

Herod's anger grew, with Tyre and Sidon,  
They sought for peace, with Blastus as the one.  

In royal robes, Herod took his place,  
“The voice of a god!” they said, in haste.  

Struck down by an angel, worms took hold,  
For glory to God, he failed to uphold.  

But God's word grew, spread far and wide,  
Barnabas and Saul, with Mark, by their side.
