Paul hit Ephesus, disciples he found,  
"Did the Spirit come when belief was around?"  
"We haven't heard of the Holy Ghost,"  
Paul asked, "In which baptism do you boast?"  

"John's," they answered, "that's what we know,"  
Paul said, "John spoke of the one who'd show.  
Baptized in Jesus, now you're in,  
Holy Spirit coming, let Him begin."  

Hands laid, Spirit flowed, tongues and prophecy,  
Twelve men strong, now hear and see.  
Paul hit the synagogue, three months strong,  
Bold words of the Kingdom, where they belong.  

Some hearts hardened, speaking ill of the Way,  
Paul took the disciples, taught every day.  
Two years gone, in Asia all heard,  
Jews and Greeks received the Word.  

Miracles flew from Paul's hand,  
Healing the sick across the land.  
Some exorcists tried to imitate,  
Called Jesus' name, but met their fate.  

"Jesus I know, Paul too,  
But who are you?" the spirit threw.  
They fled wounded, story spread wide,  
Fear and reverence on every side.  

Believers came, confessing deeds,  
Burned their books, destroyed their creeds.  
Fifty thousand silver's worth gone,  
The Lord's Word mighty, shone like dawn.  

Paul planned, Spirit-led, to roam,  
“Macedonia, Jerusalem, then to Rome.”  
Timothy and Erastus sent ahead,  
Paul stayed back, Asia's work not dead.  

A stir rose up, Demetrius' concern,  
Silver shrines, a trade they yearn.  
"Paul's turned people from our hand-made gods,  
Artemis' temple, her majesty nods."  

Ephesus angered, shouts of praise,  
"Great is Artemis," in a roaring blaze.  
City in a frenzy, theater's call,  
Gaius, Aristarchus, caught in the brawl.  

Paul held back, friends' plea made,  
Assembly wild in the theater's shade.  
Confusion reigned, no clear track,  
Alexander tried, but crowd fought back.  

Two-hour chant, a deafening cry,  
"Great is Artemis," filled the sky.  
Town clerk calmed, "Ephesus knows,  
Keeper of Artemis, image from Zeus shows."  

"Quiet down, don't act in haste,  
These men have no temple waste.  
Courts are open, seek your due,  
Don't create a riot without a clue."  

Dismissed the crowd, with peace in sight,  
Ephesus calmed, from their riotous night.
