I wrote a book to tell the tale, Theophilus, hear me well,  
Of all the acts and words of Christ, until to heaven he did dwell.  

He showed himself alive, they saw, for forty days he taught,  
Of God's Kingdom and its ways, by many proofs he brought.  

Gathered 'round, he gave command, “Don’t leave Jerusalem's gates,  
Wait for the Father's promised gift, the Holy Spirit awaits.  

John baptized with water — true, but Spirit’s coming soon,  
You'll be soaked in power from above, baptized under the moon.”  

"Lord, restore the kingdom now?" the disciples begged to know,  
“It’s not for you to see the times — the Father runs this show.  

But power’s yours when Spirit comes, to witness far and wide,  
Jerusalem to the ends of Earth, my word you won't hide.”  

He spoke, then risen up he went, a cloud took him away,  
The disciples gazed into the sky, lost in that bright display.  

Two men in white stood by and asked, “Why gaze into the blue?  
This Jesus will return again, the way he left your view.”  

From Olivet they traveled back, Jerusalem the place,  
In the upper room they stayed, prayers filling every space.  

Peter stood among them all, a crowd of hundred twenty,  
“Brothers, the words of David spoke of Judas’ empty plenty.  

He led the ones who took our Lord, now fallen, burst apart,  
His field of blood, in local tongue, a cursed and barren part.  

The Psalms declare his place be void, his office someone take,  
From John’s baptism till now, a witness we must make.”  

Two they named, Barsabbas called Justus, and Matthias too,  
They prayed, “Lord, you know every heart, reveal what we must do.  

Pick the one to fill the role from which Judas did fall,  
They cast their lots, Matthias won, an apostle with them all.
