Some dudes from Judea came down to say,  
"Without Moses' cut, you can't be saved today."  
Paul and Barnabas, they wouldn't agree,  
So they headed to Jerusalem to see how things should be.  

Through Phoenicia and Samaria, spreading the news,  
About Gentile conversion, with joy as their muse.  
In Jerusalem, they shared what God had done,  
Pharisees chimed in, "Circumcise everyone."  

The apostles and elders gathered to chat,  
Peter stood up, put an end to that:  
"God chose me to bring the Good News,  
He gave the Spirit, what's there to lose?  

No distinction between them and us,  
He cleansed by faith, in Him we trust.  
Why test God with a burden so great,  
Saved by grace, let’s not complicate."  

Silence fell, then Paul and Barnabas spoke,  
Of wonders God did, the Spirit awoke.  
James replied, "Listen, this is the plan,  
God’s building up nations, His mighty hand.  

Don’t trouble Gentiles who turn to the Lord,  
Just ask them to follow this simple accord:  
No idols, no blood, no strangled meat,  
Stay pure, and faith's complete."  

The apostles agreed, sent a letter to share,  
With Judas and Silas, they took special care.  
To Antioch they went, delivered the word,  
The brothers rejoiced at what they heard.  

Judas and Silas, prophets with might,  
Strengthened the brothers, spirits took flight.  
Paul and Barnabas stayed on to teach,  
Sharing God's word, the truth they’d preach.  

Paul soon said, "Let’s check on the crew,  
Every city we visited, where our faith grew."  
Barnabas wanted John Mark on the scene,  
But Paul disagreed, if you know what I mean.  

A sharp disagreement, they parted ways,  
Barnabas took Mark and sailed the waves.  
Paul grabbed Silas, off they did roam,  
Strengthening believers, far from home.
