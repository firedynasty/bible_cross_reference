In Caesarea lived a man named Cornelius, bold,  
A centurion of the Italian Regiment, devout and controlled.  
Feared God with his family, gave gifts as planned,  
He prayed to God, a generous hand.  

At the ninth hour, an angel appeared in a vision,  
"Cornelius!" called out with precision.  
Startled, he asked, "What is it, Lord?"  
"Your prayers and gifts, they've reached God adored.  

"Send men to Joppa, fetch Simon called Peter,  
He's with a tanner, by the seaside, a caretaker."  
When the angel left, Cornelius called his crew,  
Explained it all, sent them off to pursue.  

Next day, nearing the city, Peter went to pray,  
Hungry and waiting, he fell into a trance, they say.  
Heaven opened, a sheet came descending,  
Animals and birds, a vision unending.  

"Rise, Peter, kill and eat!" said the voice from above,  
But Peter refused, "Not so, Lord, I keep kosher love."  
"What God cleansed, don't call unclean," came the reply,  
Three times it happened, the sheet rose to the sky.  

While Peter puzzled over what it all meant,  
Cornelius' men arrived, their inquiry they sent.  
The Spirit spoke, "Three men seek, arise,  
Go with them now, no doubt or surprise."  

Peter met them and asked their intent,  
"Cornelius, a righteous man, you were sent."  
He invited them in, showed them hospitality,  
Next day, they traveled with Peter's locality.  

In Caesarea, Cornelius gathered his kin,  
When Peter arrived, Cornelius welcomed him in.  
Fell to his feet, but Peter said, "Stand tall,  
I'm just a man, after all."  

Peter spoke, "As a Jew, I shouldn't be here,  
But God showed me, no man should I fear.  
I ask you now, why did you send?"  
Cornelius shared the vision, how it did extend.  

"Four days ago, as I fasted and prayed,  
An angel spoke, and this is what he relayed:  
Send for Peter in Joppa, by the sea,  
He'll talk, and God's will you'll see."  

Peter spoke, "In every nation, God sees the right,  
No favoritism, just those living in light.  
Jesus of Nazareth, filled with Spirit and power,  
Healing and doing good, even at the darkest hour.  

"We witnessed his works, in Judea and Jerusalem's land,  
Killed on a tree, by God's command.  
Raised on the third day, revealed to a few,  
Ate and drank with us, as we knew.  

"He’s the Judge of living and dead,  
Prophets spoke, through him, sins are shed."  
As Peter spoke, the Spirit fell on each,  
Amazement stirred, all began to preach.  

"Can anyone stop their baptismal flight?  
They have the Spirit, just like us, in sight."  
Baptized in Christ’s name, they were,  
Peter stayed there, the message to confer.
