In those days, the disciples grew, complaints began to rise,  
The Hellenists said the Hebrews left their widows to compromise.  

The twelve gathered all and made their voices loud,  
"It's not right to leave God's word for serving the crowd.  

Choose seven men, filled with spirit and wisdom's might,  
To handle this task, and make sure it's done right.  

We'll stay in prayer, keep preaching the word,  
That's our mission, in service to the Lord."  

The crowd agreed, pleased with the plan,  
They chose Stephen, a faithful, Spirit-filled man.  

Philip, Prochorus, Nicanor, and Timon too,  
Parmenas and Nicolaus, proselyte anew.  

The apostles prayed, laid hands on their heads,  
The word of God spread, with new paths it treads.  

In Jerusalem, disciples multiplied,  
Even priests joined, faith amplified.  

Stephen, with power, showed wonders and signs,  
But some from synagogues crossed the lines.  

They debated but couldn't overcome his wisdom or spirit's flame,  
So they whispered lies, trying to ruin his name.  

"We heard him speak against Moses and God," they swore,  
Riling up the elders, they seized him at the door.  

Before the council, false witnesses stood,  
Accusing him of plans to destroy the understood.  

Claiming Jesus would change the law's face,  
But Stephen, like an angel, met their gaze.
