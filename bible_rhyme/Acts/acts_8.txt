Saul gave the nod to Stephen's demise,  
Persecution hit hard, scattered 'neath the skies.  

Devout men grieved, buried Stephen with tears,  
But Saul stormed the houses, spreading more fears.  

The scattered ones preached, the Word they shared,  
Philip hit Samaria, bold and prepared.  

Crowds listened close, saw signs he displayed,  
Demons fled loud, the lame were remade.  

Joy filled the city, the air was bright,  
But Simon the sorcerer stepped in the light.  

He amazed them long, they called him great,  
But Philip's message would change their fate.  

Believing in Christ, they were baptized anew,  
Even Simon joined in, amazed by what's true.  

Jerusalem's apostles heard Samaria's news,  
Sent Peter and John, Spirit to infuse.  

Hands were laid, the Spirit came,  
Simon offered cash, seeking power, not fame.  

Peter rebuked him, "Your heart's not right,  
Repent and pray, escape this plight."  

Simon replied, "Pray none of this be,"  
Peter’s words shook him, set Simon free.  

They preached in villages, on their way back,  
An angel spoke to Philip, a new path to track.  

He met an Ethiopian, reading on his ride,  
Philip joined him, heard the scriptures inside.  

"Do you understand?" Philip asked with care,  
The eunuch needed guidance, a truth to share.  

From Isaiah's scroll, Philip spoke of the Lamb,  
Jesus' story unfolded, the eternal plan.  

Water appeared, the eunuch cried, "Why wait?  
Baptize me now, before it's too late!"  

Both went down, water held the scene,  
Spirit caught Philip, gone, like a dream.  

The eunuch rejoiced, his heart set free,  
Philip preached on, from town to sea.
