Saul's breathing threats, with hate in his chest,  
Seeks the high priest, on a mission, no rest.  

With letters in hand for Damascus' gate,  
To bind up the followers, seal their fate.  

But on that road, a flash from the sky,  
Saul hit the ground, heard a voice up high:  

"Saul, why persecute me?" came the call,  
"Who are you, Lord?" Saul asked in thrall.  

"I'm Jesus, whom you chase and fight,  
Get up, go to the city, wait for your light."  

His men stood speechless, heard but unseen,  
Saul opened his eyes, found the world had turned mean.  

Blind for three days, no food, no sip,  
Led to Damascus, feeling the grip.  

Ananias in vision heard the Lord's call,  
"Go to Straight Street, seek Saul of Tarsus, tall.  

He's praying, he's seen you lay hands to restore,  
He'll regain his sight, walk through a new door."  

"Lord," said Ananias, fear in his tone,  
"This Saul is known for breaking bones."  

But the Lord replied, "He’s my chosen name,  
To speak before nations, in suffering gain."  

Ananias went, found the house,  
Laid hands, brought light back to Saul's darkened house.  

"Sight’s yours, Holy Spirit's gift," he declared,  
Scales fell away, Saul was prepared.  

He ate, found strength, stayed with the crew,  
Preached Christ in synagogues, a gospel new.  

People amazed, "Isn't this the foe?  
He bound the believers, now he's aglow?"  

Strength in Saul grew, proving Christ’s the key,  
Jews planned to kill him, but Saul would flee.  

Disciples by night, lowered him with care,  
In a basket through the wall, escaping the snare.  

In Jerusalem, he faced fear and doubt,  
Till Barnabas vouched, "He's changed, no doubt."  

Bold in Jesus' name, Saul spoke true,  
Hellenists plotted, but escape he knew.  

The church found peace in Judea’s land,  
Walking in fear, comfort at hand.  

Peter traveled, met Aeneas in bed,  
"Jesus heals you," and Aeneas rose, instead.  

Lydda and Sharon saw, turned to the Lord,  
Miracles unfolding, through Jesus' word.  

In Joppa, Tabitha, kind with her skill,  
Fell sick and died, but hope lived still.  

Peter arrived, widows wept and showed,  
The garments she made, acts of love bestowed.  

Peter prayed, "Tabitha, rise!"  
She opened her eyes, life in disguise.  

Raised her up, called the saints, they saw,  
News spread wide, faith and awe.  

Peter stayed with Simon, a tanner by trade,  
Miracles of life, the gospel conveyed.
