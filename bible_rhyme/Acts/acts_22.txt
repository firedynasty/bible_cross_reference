Brothers, fathers, lean in close, hear my defense I'm 'bout to disclose.  
Speaking Hebrew, the crowd fell still, listening to my tale with quiet thrill.  

"A Jew from Tarsus, I was raised here, under Gamaliel's watchful sphere.  
Zealous for God, like you today, I pursued this path, come what may.  

I hunted followers of the Way, binding them up, night and day.  
High priest and elders all know my deeds, letters in hand for Damascus' needs.  

On my journey near Damascus noon, a blazing light turned day from gloom.  
I hit the ground and heard Him call, 'Saul, Saul, why do you persecute at all?'  

'Who are you, Lord?' I asked in fright. He said, 'I'm Jesus, in your sight.'  
My companions saw the light's glare, but His voice they didn't share.  

I asked, 'What should I do, my Lord?' He replied, 'To Damascus, walk forward.'  
Blind from the light that shone so bright, led by hand, I reached the city's height.  

Ananias, devout and true, met me there, knew what to do.  
'Brother Saul, receive your sight,' he said, restoring my vision right.  

God chose me for this role, to hear and see, deep in my soul.  
'Witness to all, do not delay, rise up baptized, wash sin away.'  

Back in Jerusalem, in prayer's deep trance, He warned, 'Flee, no second chance.  
They won't heed your testimony, so depart for lands unknown, my homie.'  

'But Lord,' I pleaded, 'they know my crime, Stephen's blood, my consent at the time.'  
'Go,' He commanded, 'to the Gentiles, you're sent.' 

They listened keen til that last line was spent.  
Then rage ignited, voices soared, 'Rid the earth of this man,' they roared.  

Dust filled the air, they cast cloaks aside, officer grabbed me, to scourge and decide.  
But I spoke up, 'A Roman am I, no lawful scourge you can apply.'  

The centurion paused, went to his boss, 'Careful, this man's Roman, not a loss.'  
The commander asked, 'Is this true?' 'Yes,' I replied, my words drew.  

'A costly buy,' he said of his claim, 'But I was born Roman,' I declared my name.  
Fear struck them all, they stepped away, not daring to touch me another day.  

Next day came, seeking truth profound, freed from bonds, before council, I was bound."
