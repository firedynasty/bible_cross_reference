After the uproar ceased, Paul gathered his crew,  
Gave them a hug, said "Farewell," and off he flew.  
Through Macedonia, encouraging them all,  
Then into Greece, where he answered the call.  

Three months he spent, but trouble was near,  
The Jews plotted hard, so he steered clear.  
Back through Macedonia, he chose his way,  
With Sopater, Aristarchus, and others on display.  

They went ahead, waiting for us at Troas,  
We sailed from Philippi, fast as we could pass.  
Five days on the sea, then seven days at rest,  
Paul spoke his heart out, giving them his best.  

In the upper room, the lights were bright,  
Eutychus in the window, caught in the night.  
Sleep took him down from the third-floor height,  
Paul embraced him, "He's alive!" he said outright.  

Up they went, breaking bread and talking 'til dawn,  
The boy was alive, their sorrow all gone.  
We sailed to Assos to meet Paul on the way,  
Then onward to Mitylene, sailing day by day.  

Past Ephesus, Paul was on a mission,  
Jerusalem called him, with a Pentecost vision.  
He summoned the elders, from Ephesus they came,  
He spoke of his journey, in the Lord's holy name.  

"From the first day I set foot on this ground,  
With humility and tears, my faith did resound.  
I taught you the truth, both in public and home,  
To Jews and Greeks, God's grace I've shown.  

Bound in the Spirit, Jerusalem’s my aim,  
The Spirit warns me, bonds and trials the same.  
But I won't stop, I’ll finish my race,  
Testify to the Good News of God’s grace."  

"You'll see my face no more, this is my parting word,  
But I've spoken the truth, the whole counsel you've heard.  
Guard the flock, for wolves will come,  
From your own ranks, they’ll try to numb.  

For three years straight, with tears I taught,  
Now to God’s word of grace, you’re brought.  
I sought no treasure, my hands met the need,  
Remember, it's more blessed to give than to greed."  

He knelt down and prayed, tears flowed like rain,  
They hugged and wept, feeling the pain.  
Sorrowful most for words that were said,  
They'd see him no more, as Paul turned ahead.
