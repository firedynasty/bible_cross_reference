Yo, Jude's the name, servant of Christ, brother of James,  
I'm writing to the called, kept for His fame.  

Mercy, peace, love, multiplied your way,  
Gotta talk 'bout faith, can't delay.  

Sneaky men slipped in, acting all sly,  
Turning grace into shame, God's truth they deny.  

Remember Egypt, God saved His own,  
But unbelief brought ruin, shown.  

Angels left their place, now they're bound,  
Judgment's coming, darkness profound.  

Sodom and Gomorrah, fire was their fate,  
Immorality led 'em to that fiery gate.  

Dreamers defile, despise authority's might,  
Slander the stars, think they're right.  

Michael faced the devil, Moses' body was the fight,  
Said, "The Lord rebukes you," not with spite.  

These folks talk evil 'bout what they don't know,  
Like dumb beasts, to destruction they go.  

Woe to them, like Cain's way and Balaam's error,  
Korah's rebellion, they met their horror.  

Hidden reefs at your feast, shepherds feeding alone,  
Clouds without water, trees twice dead, overthrown.  

Waves of shame, wandering stars astray,  
Darkness reserved for their judgment day.  

Enoch spoke, seventh from Adam's line,  
"The Lord comes with holy ones, judgment's sign."  

Ungodly deeds and words, they'll face the call,  
Judgment falls on sinners, one and all.  

Murmurers, proud mouths, lust-driven quest,  
Respecting persons for gain, they fail the test.  

But you, beloved, hold the apostolic word,  
Mockers will come, but don't be deterred.  

Build on holy faith, in the Spirit pray,  
Keep in God's love, mercy leads the way.  

Some need compassion, make that division,  
Others snatch from fire, with precision.  

Now to Him who can keep you from a fall,  
Present you with joy before His glory tall.  

To God our Savior, wise and grand,  
Be glory, power, forever in His hand. Amen, understand?
