Paul, an apostle called by God's command,  
To Timothy, my true child, with grace from His hand.  
"Stay in Ephesus," I urged, "hold the line,  
Stop those teaching false doctrines, out of line.  

Don't chase myths or endless family trees,  
Focus on God's truth, that sets the spirit free.  
The goal is love from a heart that's pure,  
A good conscience, faith that's sincere and sure.  

Some have strayed, now they talk in vain,  
Want to be teachers, but know not the chain.  
The law is good when used the right way,  
For the lawless, not for those who obey.  

For the sinners, the profane, the lies unfold,  
For the actions contrary to sound doctrine told.  
The glory of God entrusted to me,  
My thanks to Christ, who set me free.  

I was once lost, blaspheming, astray,  
But His mercy found me, showed a new way.  
Grace abounded with faith and love so fine,  
Christ came to save sinners, this truth is mine.  

I was the worst, yet mercy I received,  
To show His patience, for all to believe.  
To the King eternal, immortal, wise,  
Be glory forever, beyond the skies.  

Timothy, hold fast to this word I send,  
Prophecies spoken, fight till the end.  
Keep faith, good conscience, don't let it wreck,  
Hymenaeus and Alexander, gone off deck.  

Delivered to learn, not to blaspheme,  
Follow this path, fulfill the dream."
