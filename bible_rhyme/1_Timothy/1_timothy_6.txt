Bonded servants under the yoke, hear the call,  
Honor your masters, so God's name stands tall.  

If your master's a believer, don't despise,  
Serve them well, for they're beloved allies.  

Teach these lessons, spread the word,  
Stay true to the teachings of our Lord.  

If someone brings a different creed,  
Arguing, disputing, fueled by greed,  

They're empty, lost, just causing fights,  
From chaos comes envy, evil bites.  

Withdraw from those who seek to gain,  
Godliness with contentment's the real gain.  

Nothing we brought, nothing we'll take,  
Food and clothes are enough, make no mistake.  

But chasing riches leads to a snare,  
Desires that drown, leaving despair.  

Love of money roots all kinds of vice,  
Some stray from faith, paying a high price.  

But you, man of God, rise and pursue,  
Righteousness, love, and faith in all you do.  

Fight the fight of faith, hold tight,  
To eternal life, your call, your light.  

Before the God who gives life, I command,  
And the Christ who took the stand,  

Keep the commandment pure and true,  
Till Christ returns, shining through.  

He's the King of kings, Lord of lords,  
Dwelling in light, beyond our words.  

Those rich in this world, don't be proud,  
Set your hope on God, not riches loud.  

Do good, be rich in deeds to share,  
Laying a foundation of eternal care.  

Timothy, guard what's given to you,  
Turn from chatter, hold faith true.  

Some claim knowledge, but faith they miss,  
Grace be with you, in every bliss. Amen.
