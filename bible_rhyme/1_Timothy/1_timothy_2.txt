First things first, let's lift up a prayer,  
For kings and leaders, everywhere.  

Petitions and thanks, for every soul,  
To live in peace, that's the goal.  

God sees it good, desires the same,  
All saved, knowing truth by name.  

One God, one bridge, between us stands,  
Christ Jesus, with outstretched hands.  

Gave Himself, the ransom paid,  
His testimony, never swayed.  

Here I am, apostle, preacher true,  
Gentiles taught, faith and truth in view.  

Men, lift hands, without a doubt,  
Pray in peace, let love shout out.  

Women, dress with modest care,  
Works of good, more precious than hair.  

Quiet learning, in submission found,  
Order set, as truth is crowned.  

Adam first, then came Eve,  
Deception led to disbelieve.  

Yet through the pain, salvation's seed,  
In faith and love, they will succeed.
