If you wanna lead, it's a noble quest,  
But gotta be blameless, passing every test.  

One wife, temperate, steady, never wild,  
Wise, modest, hospitable, teaching like a child.  

Not hitting the bottle, nor quick to fight,  
Gentle and calm, not greedy for the night.  

Run your home right, kids in line,  
'Cause if you can't do that, how can you hold the divine?  

Not a newbie, or pride will make him fall,  
Like the devil's trap, snaring them all.  

A good rep outside, that's a must,  
To avoid shame, and keep God's trust.  

Servants too, gotta walk the line,  
Not double-tongued, not addicted to wine.  

Hold the faith pure, heart and mind,  
Test them first, see what you find.  

Wives, same deal, reverent, true,  
Faithful and temperate in all they do.  

One wife, kids in hand, home's gotta be tight,  
Serve well, earn boldness in the faith's light.  

I'm writing to guide you, hoping to come soon,  
But if I'm delayed, here's the tune:  

How to act in God's house, where truth's the root,  
Where God is living, foundation's absolute.  

Mystery of faith, no debate,  
God in flesh, spirit justified, great.  

Seen by angels, preached wide and far,  
Believed worldwide, glory's star.
