The Spirit speaks clear, in the future some stray,  
Chasing spirits of darkness, led far away.  

Lies from the mouths of men, hypocrisy burns,  
For marriage they ban, against food they turn.  

God made these gifts to be received with thanks,  
Every creature is good, no need for pranks.  

"Sanctified by prayer, by God's word it's done,"  
Teach the brothers, and shine like the sun.  

Reject old wives' tales, and nonsense that's foul,  
Train in godliness, let it be your prowl.  

Exercise gives some gain, but godliness reigns,  
Promising life now, and beyond our chains.  

Faithful and true, this saying holds strong,  
For we labor and strive, where all men belong.  

Trust in the living God, our Savior and King,  
Teach these truths, let your voice ring.  

Don't let youth be despised, be an example in deed,  
In word, life, love, and spirit, plant the seed.  

Till I come back, focus on reading and teaching,  
Neglect not your gift, with hands it was reaching.  

Be diligent, fully give yourself to the call,  
Pay attention, save yourself and save them all.
