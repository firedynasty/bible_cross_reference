Yahweh spoke a word, Hosea heard the call,  
In the days of kings, Judah and Israel stand tall.  

He told Hosea, "Take a wife of the street,  
For this land's unfaithful, with deceitful feet."  

So Hosea found Gomer, Diblaim's own,  
She bore him a son, in their home he's grown.  

"Name him Jezreel," Yahweh did declare,  
"For the house of Jehu's blood, I will lay bare."  

"In the valley, Israel's bow will break,  
The kingdom's reign, soon I will take."  

Gomer conceived again, a daughter she bore,  
"Call her Lo-Ruhamah, mercy's closed door."  

"I'll have mercy on Judah, they'll be saved by Me,  
Not by sword or battle, by divine decree."  

Weaned was Lo-Ruhamah, then came a son,  
"Name him Lo-Ammi, for our union's undone."  

Yet Israel's children will fill the sea's sand,  
Where 'not My people' stood, 'sons of God' will stand.  

Judah and Israel, together they'll be,  
One head to lead, in unity they'll see.  

For great is the day, Jezreel will shine,  
Yahweh's word fulfilled, His design divine.
