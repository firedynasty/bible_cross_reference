Israel, return to Yahweh, don't delay,  
You've stumbled in sin, let His mercy display.  

Bring words of repentance, come back to His side,  
"Forgive all our wrongs, in Your goodness abide."  

Assyria won't save us, no horses we'll ride,  
No more idols made from our hands as our guide.  

"In You, the fatherless find mercy and grace,"  
Said God with love, "I’ll heal their disgrace."  

"I'll be like the dew, make Israel shine bright,  
Blossom like lilies, roots strong in their might."  

Branches will spread, beauty like the olive tree,  
Fragrance like Lebanon, rich and free.  

Men will dwell in His shade, grains will revive,  
Blossom like vines, in His fragrance they thrive.  

Ephraim, idols are done, I care for you now,  
I’m a green cypress, your fruit I endow.  

Who is wise, who understands this decree?  
Yahweh's ways are right, walk them righteously.  

The just stride forward, but rebels will fall,  
In His path, find wisdom, His call to us all.
