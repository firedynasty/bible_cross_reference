Yahweh spoke, “Go love her again,  
She's with another, but love won't end.  

Like I love Israel, though they stray,  
Chasing other gods, in their own way.”  

So I bought her back, silver and grain,  
Fifteen pieces, paid to ease the pain.  

I told her, “With me, you'll stay,  
No more tricks, no other way.  

No more playing roles of old,  
I'll be faithful, honest, bold.”  

Israel will go days, empty and bare,  
No king, no prince, no worship to share.  

No sacred stones, or idols to claim,  
They'll wait in silence, hearts aflame.  

But they'll return, trembling in the night,  
Seeking Yahweh, their God, the shining light.  

With David their king, they'll come to see,  
Yahweh's blessings, the final decree.
