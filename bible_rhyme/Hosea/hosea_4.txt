Listen up, Israel, hear Yahweh’s charge,  
"Inhabitants of the land, your sins are large.  

No truth, no goodness, no knowledge in sight,  
Cursing, lying, murder take flight.  

Stealing, adultery, breaking the line,  
Bloodshed on bloodshed, time after time.  

The land will mourn, its dwellers waste away,  
Creatures of the field, birds of the sky, decay.  

Fish of the sea will die, and none shall accuse,  
Your people like those priests who refuse.  

Stumble by day, prophet stumble by night,  
“I’ll destroy your mother, for wrongs not made right.  

Destroyed for lack of the knowledge you spurned,  
Rejected you’ll be, for my law you’ve unlearned.  

Multiplied, yet sin grew unchecked,  
Glory to shame, what did you expect?  

Feeding on sins, with hearts set on wrong,  
People and priest, where do you belong?  

Eat but not filled, play but not grow,  
Abandon Yahweh, and reap what you sow.  

Prostitution and wine take wisdom away,  
Consulting wood idols, led astray.  

High places for sacrifice, incense burns,  
Under the shade where infidelity churns.  

Daughters unpunished, for men do the same,  
Sacrifices with shame, none are to blame.  

Israel, though astray, let Judah remain,  
Avoid Gilgal, Beth Aven, don’t profane.  

Stubborn as a heifer, Israel’s plight,  
Yahweh can’t feed them in meadows of light.  

Ephraim to idols, let him be,  
Rulers in shame, loving infamy.  

The wind wraps tight, disappointment’s their end,  
Sacrifices in vain, no heart to mend."
