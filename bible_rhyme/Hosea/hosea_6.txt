"Let's return to Yahweh, torn and bruised,  
He'll bind us up, healing our wounds.

Two days gone, and He'll revive our breath,  
On the third, we'll rise, conquering death.

"Let's know Yahweh, press on, don't fail,  
Like the sun rising, His presence prevails.

He'll come like rain, springing forth the earth,  
Bringing life, bringing worth.

Ephraim, Judah, what's next for you?  
Your love fades like morning dew.

Prophets cut you, words struck like a flash,  
Judgment swift, a lightning crash.

"I desire mercy, not hollow sacrifice,  
Know Me, not just offerings, at any price.

Like Adam, you broke the bond,  
Unfaithful actions, too far gone.

Gilead, your iniquity's clear,  
Stained with blood, not to be revered.

Robbers ambush, priests do the same,  
On Shechem's path, they bring shame.

In Israel's house, horrors unfold,  
Ephraim's acts, stories retold.

Judah, your harvest's yet to be seen,  
When I restore fortunes, clean and pristine."
