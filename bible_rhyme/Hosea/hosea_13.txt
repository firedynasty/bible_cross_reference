Ephraim spoke, the world would shake,  
But in Baal's guilt, sealed his fate, he'd break.  

Crafted idols from silver, they made them by hand,  
"Human sacrifice," they whispered, kissed calves on command.  

But like morning mist, they'll fade and be gone,  
Chaff in the whirlwind, smoke at dawn.  

"I'm Yahweh, your God, from Egypt I led,  
Acknowledge no other; let that be said."  

In deserts I knew you, where drought was king,  
They ate and were filled, then forgot everything.  

Now I'm a lion, lurking in wait,  
A leopard on paths, sealing their fate.  

Like a bear without cubs, with fury I'll tear,  
A lioness devours, a wild beast's glare.  

Destroyed, Israel, rejecting my aid,  
"Where is your king?" I asked, you prayed.  

I gave you leaders in anger's storm,  
Took them back when wrath took form.  

Ephraim's guilt is stacking high,  
Sorrows like labor, oh how they cry.  

Yet I'll ransom from Sheol's grasp,  
"Death, where's your plague?" I ask.  

Compassion hidden, no mercy's light,  
East wind's coming, drying springs tight.  

Samaria's guilt, rebellion defined,  
Sword will fall, their fate entwined.
