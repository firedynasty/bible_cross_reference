"Listen up, priests, hear the call!  
Israel, king’s house, it's judgment for all.  

You've been a snare at Mizpah, a net at Tabor’s gates,  
Rebels deep in slaughter, but I discipline these fates.  

I know Ephraim, you can't hide from me,  
Ephraim’s played false, Israel’s not free.  

Their deeds keep them from turning back,  
Spirit of betrayal, there's no Yahweh track.  

Pride blinds Israel, they stumble and fall,  
Ephraim and Judah, both caught in the thrall.  

They seek Yahweh with flocks, with their herds,  
But He’s withdrawn, leaving them unheard.  

Unfaithful to Yahweh, bearing children untrue,  
The new moon will consume them, fields devoured, too.  

"Blow the cornet in Gibeah, trumpet in Ramah loud!  
Battle cries in Beth Aven, Benjamin, stand proud!  

Ephraim to ruins on rebuke’s heavy day,  
Among tribes of Israel, my truths will stay.  

Judah’s princes move the landmarks, it’s true,  
My wrath floods over them like water, breaking through.  

Ephraim oppressed, crushed by judgment’s chase,  
Chasing idols, it's a losing race.  

To Ephraim, I’m like a moth, to Judah, decay,  
When Ephraim’s sick, Judah’s wounds display.  

To Assyria, Ephraim runs, king Jareb sought,  
But no healing here, no cure will be bought.  

I’ll be a lion to Ephraim, tearing them apart,  
To Judah like a young lion, a fierce new start.  

I’ll carry off, none to rescue or save,  
Return to my place, until they seek and behave.  

In affliction, they will seek my face,  
Earnestly searching, returning to grace."
