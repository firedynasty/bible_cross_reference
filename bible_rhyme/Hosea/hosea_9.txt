Israel, don't celebrate like the rest,  
Unfaithful to God, you failed the test.  

You love the wages of a lover's deceit,  
At every threshing floor, where grain and wine meet.  

Threshing floor's empty, wine press runs dry,  
New wine fails, and you ask why.  

No place in Yahweh's land to dwell,  
Ephraim to Egypt, unclean food to sell.  

No wine offerings, no joy to bring,  
Sacrifices like mourners' bread, a polluted thing.  

"What happens on feast days?" you may ask,  
Gone, destruction's grip, a heavy task.  

Egypt gathers, Memphis lays to rest,  
Nettles and thorns in tents infest.  

Days of reckoning, judgment's near,  
Prophets called fools, insanity feared.  

Ephraim's paths a snare, hostility in the air,  
Corruption deep like Gibeah's fare.  

Found Israel like grapes in a wild scene,  
Fathers like figs, ripe and green.  

But they turned to Baal Peor's shame,  
Abominable acts, they earned that name.  

Ephraim's glory flies like a bird,  
No birth, no growth, no future heard.  

Children raised, but grief's the way,  
Woe's the cry when God walks away.  

Ephraim once like Tyre, so fair,  
But murderer's hand awaits them there.  

"What to give, Yahweh?" they cry,  
A barren womb, dry breasts, is the reply.  

"Wickedness in Gilgal, where I despise,  
Out of my house, no more ties.  

Ephraim struck, roots dry, fruit's no more,  
Even the born, loved ones, gone from the core."  

God will cast them, because they turned away,  
Wanderers among nations, they shall stray.
