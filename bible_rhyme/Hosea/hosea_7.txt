When I’d heal Israel, their sins are exposed,  
Ephraim’s iniquity, Samaria's woes.  
Falsehoods they commit, thieves slipping inside,  
Robbers on the prowl, nowhere to hide.  

They don’t stop to think, I see all their deeds,  
Their own actions trap them, they won’t be freed.  
Making kings glad with their wicked, sly lies,  
Princes rejoice while their truth slowly dies.  

Adulterers all, like ovens they burn,  
Baker stops stirring, waits for dough to turn.  
Princes got drunk when the king had his day,  
Joined with the mockers, they led him astray.  

Hearts like hot ovens, plotting while they sleep,  
Baker snoozes on, but the fire runs deep.  
Flames devour judges, kings fallen low,  
None among them calls my name, it shows.  

Ephraim blends with nations, pancake half-baked,  
Strangers take strength, and he's not awake.  
Gray hairs are sprouting, he doesn’t see it,  
Pride testifies, but to me, they won’t submit.  

“Ephraim’s a dove, easily deceived,  
Calls Egypt, Assyria, by lies they’re grieved.  
I’ll spread my net, like birds they will fall,  
Chastise them strong, echo through their hall.  

Woe to them now, they’ve wandered away,  
Destruction comes quick, for their heart’s astray.  
Though I’d redeem, they speak lies in my face,  
Howl on their beds, but turn from my grace.  

They gather for grain, new wine, they crave,  
Turn from me, though I taught them to be brave.  
Return they do, but not to the Most High,  
Faulty bows they are, in rage they will die.  

Princes fall by the sword, tongues full of rage,  
Egypt will mock them, trapped in their cage."
