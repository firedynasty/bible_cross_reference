Blow the trumpet, hear it loud and clear,  
Like an eagle over the house, drawing near.  

They broke my covenant, rebelled in my sight,  
Yet they cry to me, claiming they're upright.  

Israel's cast aside the good they knew,  
Enemies chase them, relentless, in pursuit.  

Kings they appoint, but not by my hand,  
Princes arise, I don't sanction these plans.  

Silver and gold turned into false gods,  
Idols they've made, cast off, cut the odds.  

"Samaria, toss that calf away!"  
My anger burns, they lead astray.  

Crafted by hands, no God in sight,  
That calf of Samaria's doomed to plight.  

Sow the wind, the whirlwind they reap,  
No grain left standing, no harvest to keep.  

Israel's swallowed, nations make him small,  
Like a wild donkey, alone he falls.  

To Assyria they've gone, lonely, astray,  
Ephraim, with lovers, pays betrayal's wage.  

But I'll gather them back, though they're sold far and wide,  
Oppression will wither, won't long abide.  

Ephraim's altars for sin, a multiplying shame,  
My laws written, now viewed as strange.  

They sacrifice meat, but Yahweh turns away,  
He'll remember their sins, make them repay.  

Back to Egypt, they'll face what they dread,  
Israel's forgotten, palaces instead.  

Judah's cities, fortified and grand,  
But fire will come, devouring the land.
