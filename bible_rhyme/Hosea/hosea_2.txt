"Brothers, you're my people!" Sisters, hear my call,  
"Contend with your mother, she’s not mine at all."  

She needs to stop her ways, put down that disguise,  
Or I'll lay her bare, like the day she first cries.  

No mercy for her kids, born of deceit,  
Their mother went astray, in shame she's complete.  

"Chasing lovers for bread, water, flax, and wine,  
But I'll block her path, her way won't be fine."  

She'll chase her dreams, but never them she'll find,  
Then she'll think, "Back to my first love, I was blind."  

She forgot it was me, who gave the grain and gold,  
Now I'll take it back, leave her out in the cold.  

Exposing her shame, in front of her crew,  
No one will save, nothing they can do.  

Her feasts, her moons, her Sabbaths will cease,  
Her vines and figs, become wild with beasts.  

"Forgetting me, for Baals she did crave,"  
Says Yahweh, "In the wild, I’ll her enslave."  

"I’ll speak soft, give vineyards for hope,  
She’ll answer like youth, no longer she'll mope."  

"In that day," Yahweh says, "call me your own,  
Names of Baals gone, you'll forget all they've shown."  

"A covenant with creatures, peace will reign,  
Bow, sword, and battles will no longer strain."  

"Betrothed to me, forever we'll stand,  
In justice, kindness, compassion so grand."  

Faithful you’ll be, you'll know Yahweh's name,  
Heavens and earth will respond to my claim.  

Grain, wine, and oil, Jezreel will agree,  
Mercy for those unloved, my people you'll be."  

"They'll call me God, and I'll say, "You're mine,"  
A promise fulfilled, in love we'll entwine."
