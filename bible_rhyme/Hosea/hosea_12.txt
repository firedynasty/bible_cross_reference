Ephraim chases the wind, running wild with deceit,  
Deals with Assyria, sends oil Egypt's street.  

God's got beef with Judah, Jacob's ways he surveys,  
He'll repay him for deeds, in those old twisted days.  

Grabbed his brother's heel, even in the womb's space,  
Grew up strong, wrestled God face to face.  

Fought with the angel, and he shed some tears,  
Pleaded at Bethel, where God calmed his fears.  

Yahweh's the name, the God of renown,  
"Turn to your God, let justice wear the crown."  

A merchant cheats, with scales that deceive,  
Ephraim boasts, "Riches I achieve!"  

"But I'm Yahweh, from Egypt I led,  
In tents you'll dwell, like the old days," He said.  

Prophets spoke visions, words made clear,  
But Gilead’s wicked, their altar heaps drear.  

Jacob ran to Aram, worked for a bride,  
Israel preserved by prophets who guide.  

Ephraim provoked, his anger won't fade,  
His blood remains, for his contempt he's repaid.
