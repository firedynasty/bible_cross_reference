Israel thrives, a lush vine indeed,  
His fruit multiplies, feeds every need.  
With plenty of fruit, altars are raised,  
Prospered land, and stones are praised.  

Heart divided, guilt will be found,  
Altars fall, stones hit the ground.  
Now they say, "No king to claim,  
No fear of Yahweh, what's in a name?"  

Promises made, covenants fake,  
Judgment rises like weeds in a wake.  
Samaria's folks, in terror they cry,  
Mourn for the calves, their glory's gone by.  

To Assyria, a gift it'll go,  
Ephraim's shame begins to show.  
Samaria's king drifts like a twig,  
Sinful heights, destruction big.  

Thorn and thistle on altars sprout,  
Mountains hide us, hills block us out.  
"Israel, you've sinned since Gibeah's days,"  
Battle doesn't stop, it stays and stays.  

"I'll chastise with nations at hand,  
Bound to transgress, they'll understand.  
Ephraim's yoked, though thresher she loves,  
Judah will plow, Jacob's clods shove.  

Sow righteousness, kindness you'll reap,  
Break your ground, Yahweh you seek.  
You’ve sown wickedness, reaped deceit,  
Trusted in men, now face defeat.  

Battle roars, fortresses fall,  
Beth Arbel's day, echoes all.  
Bethel's fate for wickedness shown,  
At dawn, Israel's king dethroned."
