When Israel was young, I loved him, called him outta Egypt's land,  
But they turned away, sacrificed to idols made by hand.

I taught Ephraim to walk, took him by the arm,  
He didn’t know I healed him, kept him from harm.

Drew him with love, lifted burdens from his neck,  
Bent down low, fed him, kept his life in check.

No return to Egypt—Assyria’s their king,  
They refused to repent, let the sword swing.

The bars will fall, cities laid to waste,  
Turned from me, called, but I won’t be chased.

“How can I let you go, Ephraim? Israel, how can I hand over?  
Like Admah, Zeboiim, my heart’s in turmoil, like a storm, it hovers.

Won’t unleash my anger, won’t destroy my own,  
For I am God, not man, I stand alone.

They’ll follow Yahweh, roar like a lion in the night,  
Children trembling from the west, seeking the light.

From Egypt like birds, out of Assyria they’ll flee,  
“I’ll settle them in houses,” says Yahweh, eternally.

Ephraim wraps me in lies, Israel in deceit,  
Judah strays, unfaithful, makes the cycle repeat.
