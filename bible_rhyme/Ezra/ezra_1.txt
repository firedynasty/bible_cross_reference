In Persia's land, Cyrus took the throne,  
God stirred his heart, he wasn't alone.  

"Yahweh's word through Jeremiah's call,  
Now fulfilled, let the nations hear it all."  

Cyrus declared, "The God of heaven's decree,  
Build Him a house in Jerusalem, let it be free."  

"Who among you belongs to His clan,  
Rise up, and build, do all that you can."  

"Those who remain, lend a hand,  
With silver, with gold, all at His command."  

Judah and Benjamin's leaders rose,  
Priests and Levites, in spirit they chose.  

Neighbors gave gifts, strengthened their way,  
Silver and gold, for the new day.  

Cyrus brought out what Nebuchadnezzar stole,  
Yahweh’s vessels, once hidden in the fold.  

Mithredath counted, to Sheshbazzar's hand,  
Gold and silver to rebuild the land.  

Thirty gold platters, a thousand silver more,  
Twenty-nine knives, and bowls by the score.  

Five thousand four hundred vessels in all,  
Sheshbazzar carried them back, heeded the call.  

Captives from Babylon to Jerusalem went,  
To rebuild God's house—their hearts content.
