The adversaries heard the temple was on the rise,  
Judah and Benjamin building, aiming for the skies.  

They came to Zerubbabel, heads of the kin,  
Saying, "Let us join, we've worshipped where you've been."  

But Zerubbabel and Jeshua, they stood their ground,  
"Your help we don't need, by the king's command, we're bound."  

So the locals, they schemed, tried to weaken the hand,  
Hiring counselors, frustrating the plan.  

Through Cyrus to Darius, they caused delay,  
Penning accusations in a crafty play.  

In Ahasuerus' reign, they wrote with no shame,  
Saying, "Jerusalem’s rise will tarnish the king's name."  

To Artaxerxes, they sent words in a Syrian script,  
Claimed, "This city builds bad, a power they'll equip."  

"These Jews from you, they're rebellious and bold,  
They finish the walls, their defiance will unfold."  

"We eat the palace salt, can't watch the king's disgrace,  
Search the records, see the rebels' past erase."  

"If they finish, you'll lose beyond the river's span,  
Your grip on this land, will slip from your hand."  

Artaxerxes heard, and with a decree,  
Ordered the work to stop, until further from me.  

"Make sure the damage doesn't grow,  
Let no city rise until I say so."  

The letter read, they rushed with force,  
To stop the building, they took their course.  

God's house paused, the work ceased,  
Until Darius, the king released.
