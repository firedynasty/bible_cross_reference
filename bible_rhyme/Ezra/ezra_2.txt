They rose from captivity, a journey to start,  
From Babylon's grip, they were set apart.  
Led by Zerubbabel, they walked the line,  
Returning to the cities, a sign of the divine.  

Children of Parosh, two thousand plus,  
Shephatiah's clan, three hundred a must.  
Arah’s seven hundred, nearly eight,  
Pahathmoab's thousands, carrying fate.  

Elam, Zattu, Zaccai, they come,  
Bani, Bebai, marching like a drum.  
Azgad, Adonikam, numerals in the mix,  
Bigvai, Adin, Ater, in fixed.  

Bethlehem’s children, a hundred strong,  
Netophah’s men, not left for long.  
Anathoth's group, counted right,  
Azmaveth’s few, they take flight.  

Jericho, Lod, Hadid, Ono,  
Gathering their numbers, ready to go.  
A mighty assembly, rising like dawn,  
One by one, they journey on.  

Priests and Levites, singers in tune,  
Gatekeepers stand, 'neath the moon.  
Temple servants, Solomon's line,  
Working together, a purpose divine.  

"Don't eat," said the gov'nor, "until the time,  
The priest stands with Urim and Thummim, in their prime."  
Forty-two thousand, their numbers complete,  
With servants and singers, the sound was sweet.  

Horses, mules, camels, donkeys in stride,  
A community together, a unified tide.  
Heads of households offered their wealth,  
For the house of God, they gave with stealth.  

Gold and silver to the treasury pour,  
Priests and Levites, now restored.  
In their cities, they found a home,  
All Israel together, no longer to roam.
