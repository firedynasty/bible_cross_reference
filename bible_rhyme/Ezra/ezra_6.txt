Darius the king made a decree, search the Babylon vault,  
Found a scroll in Achmetha's palace, held in Media's halt.  

Cyrus once decreed, "Build God's house, make it tall,  
Sixty cubits high and wide, its foundations won't fall.  

Three layers of stone, new timber in the mix,  
From the king's own treasury, let's fix up these bricks.  

Return the gold and silver Nebuchadnezzar stole,  
Back to Jerusalem's temple, place each in its role.  

Tattenai and crew, stay away, let the Jews build,  
Let the elders and the governor see this prophecy fulfilled.  

From the king's goods, let expenses flow,  
For bulls, rams, and lambs, as offerings to show.  

Wheat, salt, wine, and oil, give it day by day,  
So the priests can pray for the king, keep obstacles away.  

If you alter this decree, your house becomes a heap,  
Pulled beams for a gallows, punishment runs deep.  

Let no king nor people touch this divine place,  
Darius' word stands strong, steady in its pace."  

Tattenai obeyed, with diligence they moved,  
The elders of Jews built, by the prophets approved.  

Finished on Adar's third day, in Darius' sixth year,  
Israel's children rejoiced, their dedication clear.  

One hundred bulls, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs they brought,  
Twelve goats as a sin offering, by Moses' law were taught.  

Priests and Levites in order, serving God's plan,  
Captivity's children kept Passover, as only they can.  

Purified, they slaughtered for their brothers and kin,  
Those who sought Yahweh joined in, leaving filth and sin.  

Seven days of unleavened bread, joy filled the air,  
God turned Assyria's heart, to aid them with care.
