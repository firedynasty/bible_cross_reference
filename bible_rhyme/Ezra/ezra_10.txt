Ezra wept before God's place, a sorrowful sight,  
Men, women, kids gathered, tears flowing all night.  
Shecaniah spoke up, "We've made a wrong move,  
Married foreign women, against God's groove.  
Yet hope remains, for Israel's not done,  
Let's make a deal with God, get the wrongs undone."

"Put away the wives, the children they bore,  
Follow the law, hear God's mighty roar.  
Stand up, be brave, this task is yours,  
We'll follow you, Ezra, for the cause."  
Ezra rose, made them all swear true,  
Priests, Levites, Israel, they all knew.

Ezra left God's house, no food or drink,  
Mourning the sins, he sat to think.  
A call went out through all the land,  
"Gather in three days, or lose what you hand.  
From Judah and Benjamin, they came with haste,  
In front of God's house, in the rain, they faced."

Ezra stood tall, "You've trespassed, it's clear,  
Separate from foreign wives, do not fear.  
Confess to Yahweh, make his pleasure your goal,  
Leave behind the wrong, cleanse your soul."  
The assembly cried, "We'll do as you said,  
But this takes time, the rain we dread."

"Let the leaders appoint, in cities they stay,  
Until God's fierce wrath is turned away."  
Yet Jonathan and Jahzeiah stood opposed,  
With Meshullam and Shabbethai, their case they closed.  
The children of captivity took it to heart,  
With Ezra's lead, they all played their part.

Month by month, they examined each case,  
Sons of priests found guilty, they embraced grace.  
They offered a ram, their guilt to atone,  
Step by step, they returned to their own.  
Names were listed, from tribes far and wide,  
Commitment to change, they did not hide.

All who married foreign, names were read,  
They put away their wives, on their knees they pled.  
Foreign wives, children, their ties were cut,  
To reconcile with God, the door was shut.  
In the end, they faced their wrongs,  
Israel's hope in repentance, forever strong.
