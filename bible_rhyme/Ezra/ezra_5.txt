Prophets Haggai and Zechariah, they spoke real bold,  
To the Jews in Judah, God's story retold.  

Zerubbabel and Jeshua, they rose to the call,  
Building God's house, with the prophets standing tall.  

Then Tattenai came, asking, "Who gave you the right,  
To build up this house and finish it tight?"  

They wanted names, the builders to find,  
But God's watchful eye, left them in a bind.  

The elders didn't stop, they knew their cause,  
Till King Darius would give his applause.  

A letter was sent, to the king in command,  
"To Darius, peace, from the river's land.  

We saw the great work, stones laid with care,  
The house of God rising, in Judah's fair air.  

We asked the elders, 'Who gave you this decree?'  
They replied, 'Servants of God, that's who we be.  

The house once built by a king so grand,  
Destroyed by Nebuchadnezzar's hand.  

Cyrus then decreed, in his first year's reign,  
To build it anew, in God's holy name.  

The vessels of gold taken away,  
Cyrus returned them, for God's display.  

Sheshbazzar came, foundations to lay,  
The work continues, still on the way.  

Now, dear king, let it be known,  
Search your records and let truth be shown.  

See if Cyrus decreed this plan,  
And send us your word as only you can."
