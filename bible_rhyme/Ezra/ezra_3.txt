When the seventh month arrived, they gathered in the land,  
As one in Jerusalem, united they stand.  

Jeshua and his crew, with Zerubbabel in line,  
Built an altar for offerings, a task divine.  

Despite the fear from neighbors all around,  
They set the altar firm, on holy ground.  

Morning and evening, the offerings rise,  
Obeying Moses’ law, under the skies.  

Feast of booths they kept, as the scripture required,  
With daily offerings, they never tired.  

The new moons and set feasts, offerings flowed,  
From the first of the month, the devotion showed.  

But the temple's foundation remained untouched,  
While masons and carpenters were richly clutched.  

To Sidon and Tyre, for cedar they paid,  
Following King Cyrus’ grant, the plans were laid.  

In year two, month two, the work began to bloom,  
Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the Levites made room.  

Oversight given to those twenty and above,  
Working on God's house, guided by love.  

Jeshua and crew, with sons by their side,  
Directed the craftsmen with unified pride.  

When the temple's foundation was finally placed,  
The priests and Levites sang and embraced.  

With trumpets and cymbals, they praised Yahweh,  
"For His loving kindness endures," they'd say.  

The people shouted, with a joy that was grand,  
As the temple's foundation, firm did stand.  

Yet old men wept, for the first house they had known,  
Joy mixed with tears, in the cries that were shown.  

Shouts of joy and weeping intertwined,  
The sound carried far, in Israel's mind.
