In the days of Artaxerxes, king's reign was wide,  
Ezra, son of Seraiah, had the law as his guide.  

From Aaron's line, a priestly crew,  
Ezra rose, his mission true.  

From Babylon he made his start,  
A scribe with wisdom, set apart.  

The king said, "Ezra, go, take your crew,  
To Jerusalem, and do what you do."  

With priests and Levites by his side,  
They journeyed forth, their faith as their guide.  

"Take silver and gold, offerings bold,  
To honor the God whose stories are told."  

Artaxerxes' decree was clear and grand,  
"Support Ezra, God's scribe, across the land."  

"Need supplies? The king will provide,  
For God's house, let nothing be denied."  

"No taxes for priests, the singers, the gate,  
Their service to God, we celebrate."  

Ezra taught, with wisdom in hand,  
Judges to guide, in Yahweh's command.  

"Whoever disobeys, let justice be swift,  
In law and in truth, let no one drift."  

Praise to Yahweh, who moved the king's heart,  
To beautify His house, set apart.  

Ezra felt strength, by God's hand was led,  
Gathered Israel's leaders as ahead they tread.
