Princes came near, they had something to say,  
“Israel's folks have gone astray.  

Priests and Levites, they're mixed with the rest,  
Canaanites and Hittites, they've failed the test.  

Taking daughters, giving sons, that's the story,  
Princes themselves have led in this folly.”  

When I heard, I tore my robe, pulled my hair with dread,  
Sat down motionless, feeling dead.  

Those who feared God gathered 'round,  
In silence, we sat, no sound.  

At the evening offering, I rose from my shame,  
Torn clothes and spirit, calling God's name.  

Said, "My God, ashamed, I can barely lift my face,  
Our guilt's like a flood, filling every space.  

From our fathers' days, we've been guilty and weak,  
Kings and priests, all laid low, can't even speak.  

Yet a little grace, a remnant you gave,  
In our bondage, you reached out to save.  

Though we're bondservants, you showed us love,  
Persian kings see us blessed from above.  

You revived us, repaired your house,  
Built walls in Judah and Jerusalem, no more doubts.  

Now what can we say, God, what can we do?  
We've forsaken commands, forgotten you.  

The land's unclean, filled with wrong,  
Don't mix with them, stay strong.  

In spite of our sins, you punished less,  
Yet we stray, breaking commandments, making a mess.  

Would your anger consume, leave no trace?  
Stand before you, guilty, seeking grace.  

God of Israel, righteous and true,  
We stand in guilt, but our remnant's from you."
