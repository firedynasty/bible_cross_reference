Gather 'round, let me tell you a tale,  
Of folks who left Babylon, set to sail.  

In Artaxerxes’ reign, they left the land,  
Led by the heads, a strong, mighty band.  

From Phinehas, Gershom took his place,  
Daniel from Ithamar joined the race.  

David's line, it was Hattush who came,  
Shecaniah and Parosh, in the genealogy game.  

Zechariah rolled with a hundred fifty men,  
Pahathmoab’s crew, two hundred, again.  

Shecaniah's son brought three hundred strong,  
Adin's line came with fifty along.  

Elam's group, seventy, marching tall,  
Shephatiah's eighty ready for the call.  

From Joab, two eighteen in the pack,  
Shelomith's one sixty had their back.  

Bebai’s Zechariah brought twenty-eight,  
Azgad's Johanan had a hundred ten in state.  

Adonikam’s crew, last on the list,  
With Eliphelet, Jeuel, and Shemaiah, none missed.  

Bigvai’s boys, seventy more to go,  
Gathered by the river Ahava’s flow.  

For three days, encamped, we did wait,  
No Levites found, so we changed our fate.  

Called for Eliezer, Ariel, Elnathan, too,  
A team of chiefs, teachers, knew what to do.  

Sent them to Iddo, at Casiphia, to say,  
“We need ministers, for God's house, come our way.”  

God's good hand brought us what we sought,  
Sherebiah and brothers, Levites they brought.  

With Hashabiah, Jeshaiah, from Merari’s line,  
Two hundred twenty temple servants, all fine.  

Called for a fast, by the river’s side,  
To seek God’s way, in Him to abide.  

Ashamed to ask the king for soldiers strong,  
Told the king, “God’s hand guides us along.”  

We fasted, we prayed, our God did bless,  
Twelve priests chosen, to manage the rest.  

Silver, gold, vessels, weighed and pure,  
For God’s house, offerings to endure.  

“Holy to Yahweh,” I told them straight,  
“Guard these gifts, till in Jerusalem’s gate.”  

Priests and Levites took the precious load,  
To Jerusalem, along the winding road.  

Departed from Ahava, God’s hand was sure,  
Delivered us safe, from bandits secure.  

Arrived in Jerusalem, three days rest,  
On the fourth, counted treasures, all were blessed.  

Burnt offerings given to God of Israel,  
Bulls, rams, lambs, goats, as the numbers tell.  

Delivered king’s commissions, far and wide,  
Support for the people, with God’s house allied.
