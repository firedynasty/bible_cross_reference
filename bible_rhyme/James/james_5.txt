Yo, rich folks, hear the sound, your miseries abound,  
Your wealth's decayed, your clothes with moths are found.  

Gold and silver corrode, eating through your flesh,  
Saved up treasure now a burden, laid to rest.  

Laborers' cries echo, fields they mowed in pain,  
"Lord of Armies" hears them, not one cry in vain.  

Lived in luxury, your heart in slaughter's feast,  
Condemned the righteous, who wouldn't resist in the least.  

"Be patient, brothers, the Lord's on His way,"  
Like a farmer waits for rain, both night and day.  

Hold your hearts steady, the end is near,  
Don't grumble, judge stands at the door, don't fear.  

Prophets endured, spoke in His holy name,  
Job's patience shines, His compassion's no game.  

Above all, brothers, let your word be true,  
"Yes" or "no," let them see the real you.  

Suffering here? Then pray, don't delay,  
Cheerful hearts, sing out, make the darkness sway.  

If you're sick, call the elders, let them pray,  
Faith's prayer heals, sins forgiven, washed away.  

Confess to each other, let your prayers be strong,  
The righteous man’s prayer, a powerful song.  

Elijah prayed, skies withheld their rain,  
Prayed once more, earth's fruit returned again.  

If one strays from truth, guide him back on track,  
Save a soul from death, cover sins, no lack.
