Yo, James to the twelve tribes, listen up, it's clear,  
Joy in trials, my brothers, though temptations appear.  

Testing your faith, it builds the endurance you need,  
Let it work perfectly, so you'll be complete indeed.  

Lacking wisdom? Ask God, He gives without a doubt,  
"Ask in faith, no wavering," that's what it's all about.  

Doubt makes you like waves, tossed in the sea,  
Unstable, double-minded, don't expect to receive.  

Humble brother, find glory in your elevated state,  
Rich man, know your humbling fate, it's the flower's fate.  

Sun rises, scorches grass, beauty fades away,  
Riches will vanish, pursuits won't stay.  

Blessed is the one who endures the test's strife,  
Once approved, he receives the crown of life.  

"Don't say God tempts," 'cause He doesn't play that game,  
Tempted by your own lust, you're the one to blame.  

Lust conceives sin, sin full-grown leads to death,  
Don't be fooled, my brothers, heed this with every breath.  

Every good gift, perfect gift, comes straight from above,  
From the Father of lights, constant in His love.  

He birthed us by truth, first fruits of creation,  
Swift to hear, slow to speak, with patience in relation.  

Human anger won't bring out God's righteousness,  
So ditch all wickedness, humbly receive His blessed.  

Be doers, not hearers, don't deceive your own mind,  
Look in the law of freedom, let it guide and bind.  

If you think you're religious, yet your tongue's unrestrained,  
Your heart's deceived, your faith's worthless, it’s plain.  

Pure religion? Visit orphans, widows in their distress,  
Stay unstained by the world, that's the Godly impress.
