Why wars among you, fights that churn?  
Pleasures inside you, make your heart burn.  

You lust and can't get, you murder, you covet,  
But what you want, you still can't shove it.  

You fight and you war, but you lack and you ask,  
Yet empty motives, make empty the task.  

"Friendship with the world?" That's against God's way,  
Want the world's embrace? Enemy, you'll stay.  

The Spirit within, yearns with a jealous cry,  
But more grace is given, from God on high.  

"God resists the proud, grace to the humble, He'll provide,"  
Submit to God, and watch the devil slide.  

Come close to God, He'll meet you there,  
Clean your hands, purify, lay your hearts bare.  

Weep and mourn, turn joy to gloom,  
Humble in His sight, exalted you'll bloom.  

Don't speak against brothers, don't judge,  
One Lawgiver judges, don't hold a grudge.  

Boasting of tomorrow, your plans detailed,  
Your life's a vapor, soon it is veiled.  

"If the Lord wills," is what you should say,  
Boasting is evil, let good lead the way.  

Know to do good, yet still you sin,  
By failing the good, you can't truly win.
