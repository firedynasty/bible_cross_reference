Let not many teach, my brothers, take heed,  
Heavier judgment's what teachers receive indeed.  

We all stumble, in many ways we stray,  
Perfect's the one whose words never betray.  

Bits into horses’ mouths, they obey and steer,  
Ships with small rudders, mighty paths they clear.  

The tongue's a small thing, yet it boasts so wide,  
A spark ignites forests, the blaze won't hide.  

Tongue's a fire, a world of iniquity it breeds,  
Sets life ablaze, from Gehenna it feeds.  

Beasts are tamed by mankind's hand,  
But the tongue? No one can command.  

Restless evil, poison it spreads,  
Blesses God, curses men instead.  

From one mouth, both blessings and curse?  
Brothers, these things should not be rehearsed.  

Does a spring flow both bitter and sweet?  
Can figs grow from olives, or grapes on the street?  

Who's wise? Let actions show their might,  
Gentle deeds in wisdom's light.  

Bitter jealousy and selfish ambition,  
Don't boast or stray from truth's mission.  

Earthly wisdom, demonic and sly,  
Where jealousy reigns, chaos will lie.  

Wisdom from above is pure, brings peace,  
Full of mercy, good fruits increase.  

Righteousness sown by peacemakers' hand,  
In peace it grows, across the land.
