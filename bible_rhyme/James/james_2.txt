Brothers, don't hold faith in Christ with no clarity,  
Show no favoritism, avoid that disparity.  

A man walks in with gold, dressed so fine,  
Another's clothes are filthy, yet you draw a line.  

Tell the rich, "Sit here, in a good place, my friend,"  
To the poor, "Stand there," this message you send.  

Aren't you judging with thoughts that are grim?  
God chose the poor to be rich in Him.  

You've dishonored the poor, but the rich drag you in,  
They blaspheme the name, committing the sin.  

"Love your neighbor as yourself," the royal decree,  
Show no partiality, let all people be free.  

Break one part of the law, you've broken it all,  
"Do not murder, don't commit adultery," hear the call.  

Speak and act under freedom's rule,  
Judgment's harsh if mercy's not your tool.  

Mercy wins over judgment, it's plain to see,  
Faith without works, what's the good it could be?  

If a brother lacks food, and you say, "Be well,"  
Yet offer no help, your faith's just a shell.  

Faith without action, dead as a bone,  
Show me your works, let your faith be shown.  

You believe God is one, even demons believe,  
But faith without deeds, don't let it deceive.  

Abraham showed faith when Isaac he laid,  
Faith and works together, righteousness displayed.  

"Abraham believed," the Scripture declared,  
Friend of God, his faith was shared.  

So by works, not by faith alone,  
Justified actions, let it be known.  

Rahab welcomed the spies, sent them another way,  
Faith and works combined, on that day.  

Just as the body needs the spirit to live ahead,  
Faith without works is surely dead.
