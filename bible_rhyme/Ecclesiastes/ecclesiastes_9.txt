I laid it down, straight to my heart,  
Righteous and wise, their works play a part.  
In God's hand, they’re held tight,  
Love or hate, who can see that light?  

Same event hits all, righteous or wicked,  
Good and clean, or those unpicked.  
Sacrificer or not, they face the same fate,  
Good and sinner both meet their date.  

Under the sun, one evil remains,  
Madness in hearts, breaking the chains.  
Alive, there's hope, even for the least,  
A living dog, better than a dead beast.  

The living know their end's in sight,  
The dead, forgotten, out of the light.  
Love and hate, gone from their mind,  
No more share in what they left behind.  

So go, eat your bread, drink with delight,  
God’s accepted your work in His sight.  
Wear white, keep your head anointed,  
Live joyfully, love appointed.  

Whatever you find, do it strong and right,  
For in Sheol, there’s no work, no light.  
I saw races not won by the swift,  
Battles not claimed by the strong in the lift.  

Time and chance, they trip us all,  
Like fish in a net, or birds that fall.  
Wisdom I saw, shining so bright,  
A poor man's wisdom saved through the night.  

Though wise words are quiet, they’re better than shouts,  
Better wisdom than war, no doubts.  
Yet one wrong, destroys much that's good,  
In the silence, wisdom should.
