Remember your Creator while you're still young,  
Before hard times hit and life becomes unsung.  

Before the sun fades and the moon's light goes dim,  
When stars lose their sparkle, clouds roll in on a whim.  

When protectors shake and the strong bend low,  
When work grinds to a halt, and time moves slow.  

Doors closed in the street, sound of grinding fades,  
Birdsong wakes the sleeper, music shrouds in shades.  

Fear climbs heights, terror waits on the road,  
Almond blossoms bloom, heavy is the load.  

Desire dwindles, everyone heads home,  
Mourners fill the streets where the shadows roam.  

Before silver cords snap, golden bowls break,  
Pitchers shatter at springs, wheels no longer take.  

Dust returns to dust, the spirit goes back,  
To God who gave life, the eternal track.  

"Vanity of vanities," the Preacher declared,  
"All is vanity," in this world we shared.  

Though wise, the Preacher taught with care,  
Sorting proverbs, truth beyond compare.  

His words sharp, like well-driven nails,  
From the shepherd's hand, wisdom prevails.  

Books unending, knowledge wears you thin,  
But listen, my son: let your learning begin.  

Fear God, keep commandments, it's the whole man's claim,  
For every deed will meet judgment's flame.  

Good or evil, all will be revealed,  
In God's sight, nothing stays concealed.
