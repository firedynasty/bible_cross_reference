There's a season for all, a time to live and die,  
Time to plant seeds and then reach for the sky.  
A time to break, a time to mend,  
A time to tear down, a time to build again.  

Time to cry, let the tears flow,  
Time to laugh, let the good times show.  
Dance in the sunlight, mourn in the night,  
Gather your stones, or toss them from sight.  

Embrace your loved ones, then let go,  
Seek what's lost, or let it blow.  
Keep it close, or throw it away,  
Silence your mouth, or have your say.  

Love's got a time, hate's got one too,  
War might come, but peace can break through.  
What's the point of all this toil?  
I've seen the burdens, felt the soil.  

God's made it all beautiful, right on time,  
Placed eternity in hearts, yet left us blind.  
Rejoice and do good, as long as you live,  
Eat, drink, enjoy—the gifts He gives.  

What God does stands forever, unchanged,  
Nothing added, nothing rearranged.  
Time's been and gone, and here it comes again,  
Justice tarnished, even where it should reign.  

I thought, "God will judge the good and bad,  
Every work, every purpose that we've ever had."  
In my heart I said, we're tested like beasts,  
All share breath, all life will cease.  

Dust to dust, we all return,  
Who can tell where spirits yearn?  
So I saw, better to find joy in your toil,  
For who knows what the future will spoil?
