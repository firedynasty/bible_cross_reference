Dead flies in perfume make it stink bad,  
A little folly outweighs wisdom you had.  

A wise heart's on the right, fool's on the left side,  
Walking the road, the fool's foolishness can't hide.  

If the ruler's spirit rises, stay calm, hold your space,  
Gentleness can lay offenses to rest in their place.  

I've seen an evil under the sun,   
Errors from rulers that can't be undone.  

Folly high on a throne, while the rich sit low,  
Servants on horses, where princes should go.  

Dig a pit, you might fall in, that's the risk you take,  
Break a wall, a snake could bite, it's a chance to make.  

Carve a stone, get hurt by it, a cut that may sting,  
Split wood, face danger, it's a hazardous thing.  

A blunt ax takes more strength, sharpen it well,  
Skill brings success, that's the story to tell.  

If a snake bites before it's charmed, no profit's won,  
A charmer's tongue's useless if the work ain't done.  

A wise man speaks with grace, his words are a gift,  
A fool's swallowed by his lips, can't get a lift.  

Foolish words start simple, end with madness,  
He multiplies words, can't see future sadness.  

Fools' work wearies them, don't know the way to go,  
Lost in their talk, no city they know.  

Woe to the land with a child as king,  
Princes feast in the morning, what chaos they bring.  

Happy the land with a noble king's son,  
Princes eat for strength, not drunken fun.  

Sloth lets the roof sink, idle hands let it leak,  
A feast for laughter, wine for joy, money speaks.  

Don't curse the king, not even in thought,  
A bird might hear you, and tell what you wrought.
