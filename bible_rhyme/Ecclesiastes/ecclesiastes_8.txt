Who’s like the wise, who sees it clear?  
A wise man’s face shines, loses fear.  

"Keep the king's command," I say,  
You swore it to God, don’t stray.  

Don’t rush out, don’t do wrong,  
The king’s word is law, it’s strong.  

His command keeps you from harm,  
The wise know time’s gentle charm.  

There’s a time for each endeavor,  
Though man’s burden’s heavy forever.  

He can’t foresee what’s ahead,  
No one knows what will be said.  

No power over spirit’s flight,  
Can’t control death’s final night.  

War gives no sweet release,  
Wickedness won’t bring peace.  

I’ve seen the wicked laid low,  
From holy to forgotten, they go.  

Evil punished slow, hearts grow cold,  
Men set on sin, bold and old.  

Sinners live long, crimes in scores,  
But those who fear God, He restores.  

The wicked, days shadowed, brief,  
They lack God’s fear, find no relief.  

Righteous suffer, wicked enjoy,  
Vanity plays with man, like a toy.  

So I say, find joy in your days,  
Eat, drink, work in God’s ways.  

I sought wisdom day and night,  
The labor of earth was in my sight.  

God's work is vast, hidden from man,  
Though he seeks, finds whatever he can.  

The wise think they know the plan,  
But God’s work, they can’t understand.
