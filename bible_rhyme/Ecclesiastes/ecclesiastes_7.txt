A name that rings true is better than scent,  
And death's sweet release over birth's first event.  

Better to mourn where the hearts can see,  
Than feast in the house of false ecstasy.  

Sorrow's face, though it may seem rough,  
Shapes the heart, makes it good enough.  

The wise dwell where mourning is found,  
Fools dance where laughter's the sound.  

Hear the rebuke from the wise, take the cue,  
Not the fool's song that's empty and untrue.  

Like crackling thorns beneath a pot,  
Foolish laughter's all for naught.  

Extortion makes the wise man fall,  
A bribe takes understanding's call.  

Better an end than to start with pride,  
Patience is better with nothing to hide.  

Quick to anger, avoid that trap,  
It rests with fools, it's a clumsy wrap.  

Don't question the past with a mind so tight,  
It's not wise to say, "The past was right."  

Wisdom, like wealth, offers defense,  
But knowledge preserves with common sense.  

Consider God's work, who's straightens His ways?  
Crooked or straight, it's His mighty gaze.  

Joy in good times, consider in pain,  
Both sides He made, like sunshine and rain.  

Seen it all in my vain days,  
Righteous perish, wicked live in their ways.  

Don't overdo righteousness or wisdom's role,  
Why destroy yourself or lose control?  

Avoid wickedness, don't play the fool,  
Why die too soon, breaking the rule?  

Hold on to this, but don't let go of that,  
Fear God and rise from where you're at.  

Wisdom's strength is tenfold in the wise,  
More than rulers beneath the skies.  

No one on earth is perfectly just,  
All have sinned, betrayed their trust.  

Don't heed each word that's spoken around,  
Lest you hear curses where none should sound.  

Knowing you've cursed just as much,  
In your heart, you've felt that touch.  

Tried wisdom, sought its grace,  
But it drifted to a distant place.  

Deep and far, wisdom hides its face,  
The scheme of things, a complex space.  

Turned and sought to know what's true,  
Wickedness is folly, madness too.  

A woman's snares, more bitter than death,  
Traps like chains, stealing breath.  

"Behold I've found this," the Preacher said,  
A thousand men, not a woman in thread.  

God made man upright, pure and clean,  
Yet they search for schemes unseen.
