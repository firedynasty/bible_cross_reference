Under the sun, a burden so heavy to see,  
Riches and honor, yet no power to be free.  

A man has it all, but can't take a bite,  
An outsider feasts, in the dead of night.  

Vanity calls, an evil disease,  
A man with no joy, no soul at ease.  

Father a hundred, live long and proud,  
But an empty soul, no burial shroud.  

"Better the child who never saw light,  
Than a man with years, yet no delight."  

Born in vanity, departs in the dark,  
Never seen the sun, no lasting mark.  

Thousand years lived, but no joy found,  
Doesn't all end in the same ground?  

Working for food, the appetite unfed,  
Wise or foolish, still chasing bread.  

"Eyes see the real, desires stray wide,  
It's vanity, wind-chasing, no place to hide."  

Names given long ago, man known true,  
Can't fight the mighty, it's nothing new.  

"Words spin vanity, what does it gain?  
Life's a shadow, all the same."  

Who knows what's good in life's brief span?  
Who can tell what follows for any man?
