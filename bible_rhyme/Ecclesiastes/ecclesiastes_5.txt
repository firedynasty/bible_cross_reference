Step with care, you approach God's place,  
Listen close, don’t rush to state your case.  
Keep your words few, let them be wise,  
God’s in heaven, where your thoughts rise.  

Dreams come with worry, words with a fool,  
Vow to God, follow through, that's the rule.  
Better not to vow, than vow and forget,  
Don't let your mouth lead to regret.  

Don’t cry mistake to the messenger’s face,  
Why provoke God, and wreck your place?  
In dreams and talk, vanity is found,  
But fear God, let reverence abound.  

See the poor oppressed, justice stolen,  
Don’t be shocked, hierarchy’s holdin’.  
The earth’s for all, from king to field,  
To the worker, its bounty shall yield.  

Love silver? You’ll never have enough,  
Chase abundance, find vanity tough.  
More goods, more mouths to feed,  
What’s left for the owner’s need?  

Labor’s sleep is sweet, rich man’s not,  
Wealth guards harm, riches rot.  
Riches lost, a son with none,  
Naked he came, naked he’s gone.  

Toil for the wind, what gain is there?  
Dark days, frustration, sickness, despair.  
What’s good is simple, eat, drink, rejoice,  
In work’s fruits, make the choice.  

Riches and joy, gifts from above,  
God fills your heart with love.  
Forget past days, let joy’s song start,  
God keeps your soul with joy in your heart.
