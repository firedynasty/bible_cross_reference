Cast your bread on waters wide, it’ll come back, just wait and see,  
Give a portion, share with seven, maybe even eight, let it be.  

Clouds full of rain drop down to ground, trees fall and stay where they land,  
Watch the winds, you'll never sow, look at clouds, and harvest? Can't.  

You don’t know wind’s secret path, or how bones grow within the womb,  
God’s work, a mystery all around, in the light and in the gloom.  

Morning, sow your seed in faith, evening, don’t hold back your hand,  
Which will prosper? You don’t know, both could flourish on the land.  

Sweet's the light, the sun's a joy, eyes delight in morning’s glow,  
Live your years with smiles and cheer, but remember, darkness will show.  

Rejoice in youth, let your heart be glad, follow the ways you choose to know,  
But remember, before the Lord, for all your actions, you’ll stand and show.  

Remove sorrow, cast off evil, youth and dawn are fleeting days,  
Life's a vapor, like the mist, remember always, God’s wise ways.
