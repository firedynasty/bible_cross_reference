Surrounded by witnesses, a crowd so great,  
Let's shed the weight, all the sins that wait.  
Run with perseverance, the race is on,  
Eyes on Jesus, who endured, pain gone.  

He faced the cross, despised the shame,  
Now at God's right hand, He reigns the same.  
Consider His path, don't grow weary in soul,  
You've not yet bled, striving for the goal.  

"Don't take lightly the Lord's discipline,  
He loves, He chastises, that's how you begin."  
If you're without correction, check your place,  
A father's love disciplines, a sign of grace.  

Our fathers chastened, we showed respect,  
How much more to God, our lives reflect?  
Their ways were short, His for our gain,  
Holiness shared, through joy and pain.  

Discipline feels heavy, not a happy scene,  
But yields righteousness, a fruit unseen.  
Lift up those hands, strengthen weak knees,  
Make straight paths, seek healing, please.  

Pursue peace and holy ground,  
Without it, no man's Lord-bound.  
Watch for grace, avoid bitter roots,  
Don't let sin's trap take its fruits.  

Esau sold his birthright for a meal,  
Cried for blessing, found no appeal.  
We approach no fiery, trembling mount,  
But Zion's joy, where angels count.  

A heavenly city, God's presence pure,  
Jesus, mediator, His covenant sure.  
Don't refuse Him who speaks from above,  
Earth shook then, now hear His love.  

"Yet once more," He'll shake the skies,  
Things unshaken will arise.  
A Kingdom sure, let's serve, embrace,  
For our God, a consuming fire's grace.
