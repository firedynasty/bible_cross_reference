Listen close, don't let this drift away,  
The word's been spoken, it's here to stay.  

Angels brought the message, firm and right,  
Every wrong got its due, shining bright.  

Escape we can't, if we neglect,  
A salvation so great, we must respect.  

First spoken by the Lord, it came,  
Confirmed by those who heard His name.  

God showed up with signs and might,  
Wonders and gifts, His power in sight.  

World to come, not angels' domain,  
But for us, He laid out the plane.  

"What is man?" a question profound,  
"Son of man," why care, why surround?  

Made just lower than angels' height,  
Crowned in glory, in honor's light.  

All things under his feet, placed to stand,  
Yet not all seen under his command.  

But see, it’s Him, just below the sky,  
Jesus, crowned for death, glory's prize.  

For whom and through whom all things remain,  
Bringing many to glory, through the pain.  

He who sanctifies and those sanctified,  
One family bond, no shame to hide.  

"I’ll declare Your name, brothers in sight,  
Sing Your praise, in the midst of the night."  

"I’ll trust in Him," "Here I am," declared,  
With children God gave, He wasn't scared.  

Children of flesh, blood they share,  
He joined the same, the burden to bear.  

Through death, He broke the devil's chain,  
Delivered us from fear and pain.  

Not angels' aid, but Abraham's seed,  
He helps us all in our time of need.  

Made like His brothers, in every way,  
A high priest merciful, faithful to stay.  

Tempted like us, He suffered too,  
Now He helps us, sees us through.
