So listen up, let's fear, don't miss the rest,  
A promise made, don't come up short, give it your best.  

Good news preached, but some missed the flow,  
Heard the word but without faith, they couldn't grow.  

We who believe, we enter that rest,  
"As I swore in my wrath, they failed the test."  

From the world's dawn, the work complete,  
God rested on the seventh, from all His feat.  

"Won't enter my rest," He said it again,  
Some missed out, disobedient men.  

But a new day defined, today is the call,  
"Listen up, don't harden your hearts, y'all."  

Joshua's rest wasn't the final say,  
There's a Sabbath for God's people, another day.  

Enter the rest, work no more,  
Like God did, settle the score.  

Be diligent, don't fall from grace,  
Disobedience won't win this race.  

God's word is alive, sharp as a blade,  
Pierces deep, thoughts and intents weighed.  

No creature hidden, all seen clear,  
Before His eyes, account we must fear.  

A high priest above, Jesus, the Son,  
Hold on tight, confession's begun.  

He's felt our pain, tempted like we,  
Yet without sin, pure as can be.  

So come with boldness, don't hesitate,  
Mercy and grace, in times of need, await.
