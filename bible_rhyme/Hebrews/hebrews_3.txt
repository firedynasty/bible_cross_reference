Listen up, holy brothers, called from above,  
Consider Jesus, Apostle and Priest, we're talking love.  
Faithful to His mission, appointed by the One,  
Like Moses in the house, but Jesus outshone.  

More glory to Christ, the builder, you see,  
Every house needs a maker, but God built you and me.  
Moses served the house, faithful, a servant's role,  
But Christ is the Son, with authority whole.  

We're His house if we hold on tight,  
Our hope and confession, shining bright.  
The Spirit's speaking, "Today hear His voice,  
Don’t harden your hearts, you have a choice.  

Like in the wilderness, tested and tried,  
For forty years, they saw God by their side.  
Yet they strayed in their hearts, didn’t know His way,  
So God said, 'No rest for you, not today.'"

Beware, brothers, don’t let unbelief creep,  
Stay close to God, don’t fall asleep.  
Encourage each other, keep the faith alive,  
Hold fast to the end, together we’ll thrive.  

"Today hear His voice, don’t rebel and stray,"  
Learn from the past, don’t throw it away.  
Who rebelled when they heard, back in the day?  
Those from Egypt led by Moses on the way.  

God was displeased, for forty years they roamed,  
Their bodies fell, never reaching home.  
He swore in His wrath, they’d not find rest,  
Disobedient hearts, they failed the test.  

Unbelief blocked the promise, no entry within,  
So hold firm your faith, don’t let doubt win.
