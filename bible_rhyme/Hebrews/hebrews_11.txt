Faith is the substance of what's hoped for, unseen proof,  
Ancients got their witness, that’s the truth.  

By faith, we see God spoke, the universe framed,  
Visible made from the unseen, His word proclaimed.  

Abel's offering by faith, a better gift than Cain,  
Though gone, he still speaks, righteous in his fame.  

Enoch walked with faith, never tasted death's breath,  
God took him, pleasing before his final step.  

Without faith, pleasing God is out of reach,  
"Believe in Me, seek Me," He does teach.  

Noah warned of unseen rain, built that ark,  
By faith, saved his house, lit the world with a spark.  

Abraham packed up, called to unknown land,  
By faith, he wandered, trusting in God’s plan.  

Lived in tents with Isaac and Jacob, heirs of grace,  
Sought a city with foundations, God’s dwelling place.  

Sarah believed, bore a child past her prime,  
Stars and sand from her, faith defied time.  

They died in faith, promises still afar,  
Embraced them from a distance, traveled like a star.  

Seeking a homeland, not of earth’s domain,  
Longing for heaven, God’s city without stain.  

Abraham tested, offered his son,  
"Through Isaac," God said, "your lineage will run."  

Believed God could raise him, from death to life again,  
Received him back, in faith’s refrain.  

Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, each blessed with foresight,  
By faith, they passed on promises, through dark to light.  

Moses hidden, parents unafraid,  
By faith, he chose the people’s pain, not riches displayed.  

Left Egypt, unshaken by king's fierce rage,  
Saw the invisible, endured through every stage.  

Kept the Passover, sprinkled blood,  
The firstborn saved and spared the flood.  

By faith, they crossed the Red Sea's floor,  
Egyptians swallowed, faith’s power soared.  

Jericho’s walls fell, encircled with praise,  
Rahab saved by peace in her days.  

What more can I say? Heroes of old,  
Gideon, Samson, and others bold.  

By faith, kingdoms fell, fires quenched,  
Lions silenced, swords unclenched.  

Weak made strong, victories won,  
Women saw their dead risen, battles not done.  

Others endured mockings, chains, and pain,  
Stoned, sawn apart, yet faith remained.  

Wanderers of the world, earth not worthy of their feet,  
In deserts, mountains, caves, their faith complete.  

All bore witness through faith alone,  
Yet the promise awaited, through us, would be shown.
