Let's move beyond the start, press on to perfection,  
Not laying old foundations, but seeking resurrection.  

Not stuck in basics, let's aim for the sky,  
God willing, we'll do it, reach high and fly.  

Once enlightened, tasted gifts so sweet,  
Shared in the Spirit, felt that divine beat.  

But if you turn away, it's a shameful game,  
Crucifying Him again, it's an open shame.  

The land drinks rain and yields its crops,  
But if it bears thorns, it's cursed, it stops.  

But we believe in better things for you,  
Salvation's fruits, actions true.  

God won't forget your works of love,  
Serving the saints, showing faith above.  

Stay diligent, hold on to hope's end,  
Imitate those who faith did lend.  

God swore to Abraham, no greater could He find,  
"Blessing I will bless, multiplying in kind."  

Abraham endured, and the promise he obtained,  
Men swear by greater, God's oath remains.  

Two immutable things, God's truth won't fail,  
Hope as our anchor, through trial and gale.  

Jesus, our forerunner, in glory does dwell,  
A high priest forever, after Melchizedek's spell.
