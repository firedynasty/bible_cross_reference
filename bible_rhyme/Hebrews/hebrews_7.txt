Melchizedek, king of Salem, priest on high,  
Met Abraham when kings had to die.  
He blessed him there, and Abraham shared,  
A tenth of the spoils, because he cared.  

Righteous king, bringing peace so profound,  
Without mom or dad, no lineage found.  
Eternal priest, like the Son of God,  
Forever standing where few have trod.  

Consider the man, so great and revered,  
Even Abraham gave, without fear.  
Levi's sons took tithes, law in their hand,  
Yet to Melchizedek, Abraham would stand.  

The lesser blessed by the greater's decree,  
A priest who lives eternally.  
Through Abraham, even Levi paid,  
When Melchizedek was met, their bond was made.  

If perfection came through the Levite's call,  
Why need a priest not from Aaron’s hall?  
The priesthood shifted, laws rearranged,  
A new order through Melchizedek was exchanged.  

From Judah's tribe our Lord appears,  
No priests before, yet now he clears.  
Like Melchizedek, he rises anew,  
Not by fleshly command, but life that’s true.  

"You are a priest forever," the words declare,  
A better hope through which we share.  
With an oath, he made it known,  
A priest forever, unchanging throne.  

Jesus brings a covenant better than the past,  
Lives forever, a priest meant to last.  
He saves to the utmost, those who draw near,  
Always living to intercede without fear.  

Such a high priest, holy and right,  
No daily sacrifices, once was his might.  
Law named priests with mortal flaws,  
But the oath named a Son, perfect without pause.
