The law's a shadow, just a trace,  
Not the real thing, can't take sin's place.  

Year after year, the same old show,  
Sacrifices that can't make you whole.  

If they worked, they'd stop the grind,  
No more guilt, no sin on the mind.  

But in each offering, sins reappear,  
Blood of goats can't wipe the slate clear.  

He comes to the world, hear Him say,  
"Sacrifices? No, there's a better way.  

A body prepared, I'll do Your will,  
Written in the scroll, it's My purpose to fulfill."  

"Whole burnt offerings? Not My delight,  
I've come to do what's right in Your sight."  

He takes the old, makes the new,  
Through His body, sanctifies you.  

Priests stand daily, same old plea,  
But His sacrifice sets us free.  

He offered once, then took His seat,  
Waiting for foes beneath His feet.  

The Spirit speaks, a promise to keep,  
"My laws in hearts, my words deep."  

"I’ll forget their sins, wipe them away,  
No more offerings, a new day."  

Boldly enter, through the veil torn,  
Blood of Jesus, we're reborn.  

A great priest stands, guiding each way,  
Hearts pure, sins' stains washed away.  

Hold to hope, unwavering creed,  
He is faithful, meets every need.  

Stir each other to love and act,  
Gather together, don't turn back.  

If you sin knowing, judgment's near,  
A fire awaits, a fate to fear.  

Reject the Son, face the cost,  
Spurn grace, see all that's lost.  

"Vengeance is mine," says the Lord,  
Fear His judgment, not ignored.  

Remember struggles, trials and fight,  
Endured through faith, into the light.  

You stood with me when chains confined,  
Joy in loss, eternal treasure to find.  

Keep your boldness, it's worth the cost,  
Do God's will, the promise not lost.  

"In a little while, He's on His way,  
The righteous live by faith each day."  

We're not those who shrink in despair,  
We stand in faith, in His care.
