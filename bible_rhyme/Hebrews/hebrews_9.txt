First covenant had rules, divine service was the aim,  
An earthly sanctuary set the scene for this holy game.  

A tabernacle prepared, with lamp stand and table in place,  
Showbread sat in the Holy Place, a sacred space.  

Beyond the second veil, the Holy of Holies was found,  
A golden altar, ark of the covenant, glory all around.  

Golden pot of manna, Aaron's rod that bloomed,  
Tablets of covenant, cherubim that loomed.  

Priests went in continually, the first tabernacle was their zone,  
The high priest alone to the second, once a year, blood his tone.  

Holy Spirit signaled, the Holy Place was hidden still,  
First tabernacle standing, awaiting a new will.  

Gifts and sacrifices, fleshly laws they bring,  
Couldn't perfect the worshiper, just a temporary thing.  

Christ came as High Priest of good things yet to come,  
A perfect tabernacle, not with human hands was it from.  

Through his own blood, not goats or calves,  
Entered the Holy Place, eternal redemption, the path he halves.  

Blood of goats and bulls, ashes of heifer cleansed for flesh's sake,  
But Christ's blood, through eternal Spirit, conscience it would awake.  

A new covenant Christ mediates, redemption for the past,  
Eternal inheritance promised, a legacy to last.  

A will needs a death, for its power to arise,  
First covenant dedicated, blood was its prize.  

Moses spoke commands, blood was sprinkled wide,  
“This is the covenant's blood,” he testified with pride.  

Tabernacle, vessels, all cleansed by blood's intent,  
Without its shedding, no remission, no ascent.  

Copies of heavenly things cleansed, but heaven needs more,  
Christ entered heaven itself, opened the divine door.  

Not to offer often, like the priests of old,  
Once for all, his sacrifice, sin's story retold.  

Men die once, then comes the judgment scene,  
Christ bore sins once, salvation for those keen.  

Await his second coming, without sin he'll appear,  
For those eagerly waiting, salvation drawing near.
