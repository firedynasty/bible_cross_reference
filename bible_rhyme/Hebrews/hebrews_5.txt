High priests chosen from men, for God’s grand plan,  
Offering gifts and sacrifices, that's how it began.  

Gentle with the lost, they understand the plight,  
‘Cause they're wrapped in weakness, just like the night.  

They sacrifice for sins, theirs and the people's too,  
No self-promotion here, it's God’s call through and through.  

Like Aaron, they’re chosen, can’t just decide,  
Christ didn’t seek the glory, God took him aside.  

“You’re my Son,” the Father declared with might,  
“A priest forever, Melchizedek’s light.”  

In flesh he prayed, tears fell like rain,  
To the one who could save, through struggles and pain.  

Though God's own Son, learned through the stride,  
Perfected by suffering, eternal life he provides.  

Called by God, a priest on high,  
Melchizedek’s order, never to die.  

We got much to say, but it's hard to break through,  
‘Cause your ears have grown dull, like an old shoe.  

By now you should teach, share the divine code,  
But still needing milk, not the solid food mode.  

Milk’s for the unlearned, the baby in the fold,  
Solid food's for the mature, discerning pure gold.
