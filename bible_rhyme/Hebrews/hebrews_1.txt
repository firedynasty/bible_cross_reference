In the past, God spoke through the prophet's might,  
In various ways, both day and night.  
But now in these days, a new voice has begun,  
God speaks to us through His very own Son.  

He made Him heir of all creation,  
Through whom He crafted every nation.  
The Son shines bright with glory unfurled,  
Reflecting God's image to the world.  

He holds it together with a powerful word,  
Purified our sins, with His voice we heard.  
Sat down right beside the Majesty on high,  
Better than angels, you can't deny.  

Which angel was told, "You're my Son today?"  
"Father and Son," in a divine array.  
Firstborn enters and the angels sing,  
"Let all of heaven worship the King."  

Angels move like the winds, a fiery blaze,  
But to the Son, God speaks in praise:  
"Your throne's eternal, justice your rod,  
You've loved what's right; anointed by God."  

"You laid the earth, with hands wide-spread,  
Heavens crafted, like a quilt, as it's said.  
They'll age and fade, like clothes they'll fold,  
But you stay the same, your years won't grow old."  

Which angel hears, "Sit beside my throne,  
Till your foes are the mat beneath your bone?"  
They're just spirits sent to serve the call,  
Helping those who'll inherit it all.
