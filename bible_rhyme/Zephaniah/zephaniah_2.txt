Gather round, you nation with no shame,  
Before the day chaffs away, before Yahweh’s flame.  

"Seek me now," He calls, "those humble and true,  
Seek righteousness, seek humility too."  

Gaza forsaken, Ashkelon laid bare,  
Ashdod at noon, Ekron torn in the air.  

Woe to the coast, Cherethites hear the sound,  
Yahweh's word's against you, soon no one around.  

Pastures and flocks will claim the sea's edge,  
Judah's remnant will rest, God will keep his pledge.  

He’s heard Moab's scorn, Ammon’s insult loud,  
Once mighty borders, now nettles in a shroud.  

As Sodom was, so Moab shall be,  
Ammon as Gomorrah, laid waste by decree.  

Yahweh’s wrath will famish the gods of the land,  
All shores will know Him, and bow to His hand.  

You Cushites too will face my sword's might,  
Assyria falls, Nineveh’s night.  

Animals rest where cities once stood,  
Calls echo through windows, in the cedar wood.  

The joyous city, once careless and bold,  
Now desolate and barren, the stories told.  

"I am, no other," were her prideful boasts,  
Now she’s a ruin, with ghosts as her hosts.
