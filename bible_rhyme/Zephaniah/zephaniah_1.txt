In the days of Josiah, a word from the Lord,  
To Zephaniah it came, sharp as a sword.  
"Everything's swept from the earth's face," He declared,  
"Man and beast, sky and sea, nothing will be spared.  

Birds and fish, rubble, and wickedness too,  
All will be cut off, this warning's for you.  
Against Judah, My hand will extend,  
Their idols and priests will meet their end.  

Those who worship on rooftops the stars,  
Who mix My name with Malcam's scars.  
Turned back from Me, they didn't seek,  
The day is near; it's not for the meek.  

Be still in My presence, the sacred is near,  
I've prepared a feast, the chosen appear.  
Princes and kings will face My command,  
Foreign clothes won't save the land.  

Leaping thresholds, with violence and lies,  
Punishment's coming, drowned in cries.  
From fish gate to hills, the cry will resound,  
Canaan's people undone, silver unbound.  

I'll search Jerusalem, lamps in hand,  
Punish the complacent, who've misunderstood My stand.  
Their houses will rot, wealth turned to dust,  
Building and planting denied, in their trust.  

The day of the Lord is racing near,  
Mighty men weep, overcome by fear.  
Wrath, distress, day of gloom and despair,  
Trumpets sound, fortified walls tear.  

Men will stumble, blind by their sin,  
Blood like dust, flesh discarded in the din.  
Gold and silver won't buy salvation's release,  
Fire of jealousy ends the land’s peace."
