Woe to the city, rebellious and stained,  
Ignored the voice, her soul remained unchained.  

Princes like lions, judges like wolves at night,  
Devouring all, leaving none in sight.  

Arrogant prophets, priests defiling law,  
Injustice reigns without a flaw.  

"Fear me," I said, "Receive correction true,"  
But they woke early to corrupt anew.  

"Wait for me," says Yahweh, "when I rise to the fight,  
Gather the nations, pour out my might.  

Purify lips, call my name together,  
People serving me, through stormy weather.  

Beyond the rivers, offerings will bring,  
No more shame in the songs you sing."  

The proud removed, the humble will remain,  
Taking refuge in Yahweh's name.  

No more lies, no deceit will be found,  
Peaceful pastures, fear unbound.  

Sing, daughter Zion, shout with all your heart,  
Yahweh's presence never to depart.  

"Don't be afraid," the message is clear,  
Yahweh's might always near.  

He’ll rejoice over you, calm in his embrace,  
Singing love, removing disgrace.  

"I'll deal with your foes, gather the lost,  
Give them praise, no longer at cost.  

I'll restore fortunes, bring you honor and fame,"  
Says Yahweh, with power in his name.
