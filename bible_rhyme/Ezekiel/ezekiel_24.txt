In the ninth year, tenth month, tenth day,  
Yahweh spoke, here's what He had to say:  

"Son of man, write this day, make it known,  
The king of Babylon's near, Jerusalem's own.  

Tell the rebels a story—parables unfold,  
‘The Lord Yahweh says, put the cauldron on bold.  

Fill it with water, gather choice meat,  
Thighs, shoulders, bones, make it a feast.  

Boil it well, let the fire ignite,  
Watch the city rust in the heat of the night.  

Woe to the city, bloody through and through,  
Rust in the cauldron, here's what you do:  

Piece by piece, take it apart,  
No dice, no luck, just a broken heart.  

Blood on the rock, left uncovered,  
Vengeance comes, justice discovered.  

Heap the wood, make the fire great,  
Boil the broth; let the bones incinerate.  

Empty the cauldron, let the bronze blaze,  
Filth melts away, consumed in the blaze.  

For your filth's deep, lewdness has reigned,  
Cleansed you before, but no cleansing remained.  

My wrath will rest, I won't spare,  
"I, Yahweh, have spoken," declares the air.  

Then Yahweh spoke again, a word so cold,  
"Son of man, I'll take your heart's hold.  

The desire of your eyes, with a single stroke,  
No mourning, no tears, no weeping cloak.  

Sigh in silence, no cries aloud,  
Wear your headdress, sandals unbowed.  

No mourner's bread, keep your lips bare,  
Mourn not for the dead, let them stare."  

I did as commanded, the morning came,  
That evening, my wife gone, the same.  

People asked, "Tell us, what does this show?"  
I said, "Yahweh's word, you need to know.  

He'll profane His sanctuary, pride of your place,  
Sons, daughters fall by the sword's cruel embrace.  

Do as I've done, no mourning, no cries,  
Turban on head, sandals in ties.  

Ezekiel's your sign, a witness to see,  
When this happens, Yahweh's majesty.  

Son of man, when strength they lose,  
Joy of glory, hearts confused,  

Those who escape will hear the word,  
Your mouth will open, speak undeterred.  

You'll be their sign, truth to show,  
Then they'll know Yahweh in the flow."
