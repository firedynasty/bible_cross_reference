Yo, listen up, here's a tale to be told,  
About a lioness bold, with cubs in her hold.  

She raised her young strong, among lions she sat,  
One grew to hunt, a lion in combat.  

He roared in the land, devouring men,  
Till nations caught wind and trapped him in the fen.  

Into Egypt he went, in chains and in pain,  
A prisoner bound, no longer to reign.  

But mama lioness wasn't done, she tried once more,  
Another cub grew fierce, prowling the forest floor.  

He roamed with might, cities quaked at his roar,  
Till the nations attacked, they couldn't ignore.  

They caught him in nets, dragged to Babylon's den,  
Locked in a cage, never to roar again.  

"Your mother, a vine, by the waters she lay,  
Branches reaching high, in the light of the day."  

Strong were her branches, rulers' scepters they bore,  
But fury plucked her up, cast down to the floor.  

The east wind blew, and her fruits did dry,  
Her strong arms broke, under a fiery sky.  

Now she's planted in a wilderness, barren and dry,  
No more scepters, her time passed by.  

This lament, hear it loud, hear it clear,  
A song of loss, of hope turned to fear.
