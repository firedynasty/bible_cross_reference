Yahweh spoke, "Son of man, face the land of Magog,  
Gog's the prince, hear this dialogue.  

'The Lord Yahweh says: "I'm against you, Gog,  
I'll turn you around, like a dog on a log.  

Hooks in your jaws, I’ll bring you out,  
Your army of riders, hear them shout.  

Persia and Cush, Put in the crowd,  
Gomer and hordes, all feeling proud.  

Prepare yourself, gather your crew,  
Be on guard, watch what you do.  

Years will pass, and you'll take a stand,  
In Israel's mountains, a reclaimed land.  

You'll rise like a storm, covering skies,  
Hordes of people, in disguise.  

The Lord Yahweh says: "Plans you'll devise,  
Evil intent, in your own eyes.  

You’ll target the villages, peaceful and wide,  
No walls, no bars, nowhere to hide.  

You'll go for the plunder, livestock and gold,  
Nations will ask, ‘What do you hold?’  

Son of man, tell Gog what's so,  
When Israel's safe, will you not know?  

From the north, you’ll come with might,  
A great army, ready to fight.  

Against my people, like a cloud you'll spread,  
But in those days, they'll grow more steadfast.  

In prophets' words, I did foretell,  
A day when against Israel, you’d rebel.  

When you arrive, my wrath will flare,  
All will tremble in the air.  

Fish in the sea, birds in the sky,  
Everything living will wonder why.  

Mountains will fall, every wall will break,  
In my presence, the earth will quake.  

Swords will rise, brother against brother,  
Judgment will come, like no other.  

Pestilence and blood, rain and fire,  
I’ll show the nations what I require.  

Magnify my name, sanctify my fame,  
And all will know Yahweh's reign, my claim."
