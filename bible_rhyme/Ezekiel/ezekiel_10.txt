In the sky, above the cherubim high,  
A throne like sapphire caught the eye.  

He spoke to the man, linen-clad and bold,  
"Go between the wheels, take the coals, be cold.  

Scatter them over the city," He said,  
He moved as I watched, no fear, no dread.  

Cherubim stood on the house's right side,  
The cloud filled the court, nowhere to hide.  

Yahweh's glory rose, lighting the scene,  
Court filled with brightness, pure and clean.  

Wings of cherubim roared loud and clear,  
Like God Almighty's voice, all could hear.  

He commanded the man to take fire from the wheels,  
He stood by a wheel; cherubim hand seals the deals.  

The cherub reached out, took fire with care,  
Hand to linen man, who carried the flare.  

Four wheels appeared, next to each face,  
Wheels like beryl, moved with grace.  

Wheels within wheels, no turning in place,  
Followed where the head looked, steady pace.  

Bodies and wings, eyes all around,  
Even the wheels, eyes did surround.  

Called "whirling wheels," they were heard,  
Faces of cherub, man, lion, and bird.  

Cherubim rose, creatures alive,  
Seen by the river, Chebar's drive.  

Wheels beside as cherubim soared,  
Lifted wings, spirit adored.  

Yahweh's glory left the threshold,  
Stood over them, story retold.  

Cherubim lifted, wings spread wide,  
At the east gate, God's glory their guide.  

These were the creatures I'd seen before,  
Under Israel's God, by the river shore.  

Each had four faces, four wings to fly,  
Hands beneath wings, reaching the sky.  

Faces familiar from the river's face,  
They moved straight ahead, keeping pace.
