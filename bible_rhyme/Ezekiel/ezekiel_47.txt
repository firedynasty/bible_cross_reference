Back at the door, waters flowed from the east,  
Right side of the house, south of the altar, at least.  

He took me round, through the north gate we went,  
Waters flowed right, on a journey they were sent.  

Measured east a thousand, water to my feet,  
Another thousand steps, up to my knees they'd meet.  

Another thousand measured, now to my waist,  
Then a river too deep, couldn't be faced.  

"Son of man, have you seen?" He asked me loud,  
To the riverbank back, with trees it was endowed.  

"The waters flow east, heal the sea as they go,  
Life abounds where the river's waters flow."  

Fishermen gather, from En Gedi to En Eglaim's shore,  
With nets they'll catch, fish like never before.  

But the marshes stay salty, untouched by the stream,  
Trees grow on banks, with leaves that gleam.  

"Leaves won't wither, fruits won't fail," He said,  
"New fruit each month, by the riverbed."  

"These borders divide the land for each tribe,  
Joseph gets two portions," the words Yahweh inscribed.  

"North from the great sea, all the way to Zedad,  
Damascus, Hamath, the boundaries are set, not just a fad."  

"The east by the Jordan, to the east sea it flows,  
To the south Tamar, Meriboth Kadesh it shows."  

The west by the great sea, Hamath in view,  
Divide the land for all, Israel's tribes, and the new.  

Aliens among you, treated as kin,  
Inherit the land, where their lives begin.  

"In every tribe where a stranger resides,  
There you shall grant them a place to abide," Yahweh decides.
