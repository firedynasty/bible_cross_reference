Yahweh spoke, a vision, a plan,  
"Son of man, face Ammon, prophesy, understand.  
Tell them, hear Yahweh's decree,  
'Cause you mocked my sanctuary, you'll see.  

When Israel fell, when Judah was chained,  
You laughed, you cheered, but now you'll be pained.  
I'll hand you to the east as their prize,  
They'll camp in your lands, make your home their skies.  

Rabbah, a stable, camels will roam,  
Ammon, a pasture, not your own home.  
Then you'll know, I am Yahweh, no doubt,  
For your mocking, you'll be cast out.  

You clapped, you stomped, with contempt in your soul,  
Rejoiced at Israel's fall, but now pay the toll.  
Stretched my hand, you'll be plundered and gone,  
Cut from the nations, perish, withdrawn.  

Moab and Seir, you said, "Like the rest,"  
Mocked Judah's fall, now face Yahweh's test.  
I'll open Moab, let the east take hold,  
Ammon's memory gone, as prophecy told.  

Judgment on Moab, justice comes near,  
Then you'll know Yahweh in every tear.  
Edom offended, vengeance was claimed,  
Now Yahweh's wrath, you'll be maimed.  

Teman to Dedan, desolate land,  
Israel's hands will fulfill my command.  
Vengeance on Edom, with my wrath they'll contend,  
Then they'll know Yahweh, the beginning and the end.  

Philistines sought revenge, with soul's disdain,  
Eternal hostility, now feel the pain.  
Cherethites cut off, coasts laid bare,  
Vengeance will strike, Yahweh's justice everywhere.  

Wrathful rebukes, my vengeance will call,  
Then you'll know Yahweh, once and for all."
