Elders of Israel sat down, solemn and still,  
Yahweh's word came, His message to fulfill.  
"Son of man, these folks got idols deep inside,  
Should I let them ask, let them confide?"  

"Tell 'em, every man who clings to stone and wood,  
Who seeks the prophet, misunderstood.  
In their hearts, they've wandered, strayed so far,  
Estranged from me, like a distant star."  

"Return, O Israel, from idols turn away,  
Face me again, don't go astray.  
Anyone who separates, seeks his own path,  
I'll answer him with a fierce wrath."  

"I'll set my face against him, a tale to tell,  
A sign among the people, where he fell.  
Prophets deceived, their words will betray,  
For I, Yahweh, will lead them astray."  

"Iniquity they bear, iniquity shared,  
That they stray no more, be my people prepared.  
When a land sins, and my hand I stretch wide,  
Famine, drought, no place to hide."  

"Noah, Daniel, Job, righteous trio,  
Save only themselves, no sons to show.  
Wild beasts ravage, desolation reigns,  
Yet they alone escape the chains."  

"If I call the sword, the blade must fall,  
Men, beasts, all, heed the call.  
Pestilence strikes, wrath pours out,  
Only the righteous find the drought."  

"Four judgments on Jerusalem, I will send,  
Sword, famine, beasts, pestilence without end.  
But a remnant will remain, sons, daughters too,  
They'll come to you, you'll see what they do."  

"They'll comfort you with their ways,  
Proving I didn't act without cause, in all my days."
