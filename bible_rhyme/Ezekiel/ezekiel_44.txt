Back to the gate, east side, it's shut,  
Yahweh said, "Closed tight, none shall strut.  
No man enters here, for I’ve walked through,  
The God of Israel, this path's true."

The prince will sit, have bread, a royal feat,  
Enters by the porch, exits on repeat.  
North gate now, before the holy scene,  
Yahweh's glory shines, I'm down, I'm keen.

"Son of man, listen, watch with your eyes,  
The laws and the ways, where the entrance lies.  
Tell the rebellious clan, enough with sin,  
Foreigners brought in, broke the covenant within."

Yahweh says, "Uncircumcised shall not defile,  
My sanctuary's sacred, keep it worthwhile.  
Levites strayed, went idol way,  
They bear the cost, they led astray."

Ministers they’ll be, not priests to me,  
Oversight of gates, sacrifices they'll see.  
Yet shame they'll bear for idols they showed,  
A duty's theirs, but from holiness they're roaded.

"Sons of Zadok, priests from the line,  
Stayed true, to minister, to offer what's mine.  
Linen garments they wear, no wool to sweat,  
Service pure in my court, no regret."

Linen turbans on heads, trousers on waist,  
Holy garments off, when outer court’s faced.  
Hair kept neat, not long, not bare,  
Entering sober, no wine to impair.

These priests teach the holy divide,  
Judges in disputes, Sabbath sanctified.  
No dead shall they touch, except family near,  
Cleanse, wait seven days, no fear.

Inheritance is Me, for them, not land,  
First fruits and offerings, all in their hand.  
No self-dead meat shall they eat,  
My priests, my calls, they complete.
