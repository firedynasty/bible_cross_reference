Yahweh's hand was on me, took me out His way,  
In the middle of a valley, full of bones, they lay.  

He led me around, bones piled high and dry,  
"Can these bones live?" was His question, I replied, "You know, Most High."  

"Prophesy to these bones, hear Yahweh's word,  
Breath will enter, flesh and skin will be stirred."  

I spoke as commanded, the ground shook beneath,  
Bones came together, no longer in grief.  

Sinews and flesh, skin wrapped them tight,  
But breath was absent, they needed life.  

"Prophesy to the wind, son of man, do it now,  
Tell the breath to enter, these slain to avow."  

I spoke to the wind, and life they received,  
They stood like an army, alive and relieved.  

"These bones are Israel, lost and cut off," they said,  
"Hope’s dried up, we're as good as dead."  

Yahweh spoke again, "I’ll open your graves,  
Bring you out, to the land of your waves."  

Spirit in you, alive you'll be,  
Yahweh's word fulfilled, you'll see.  

Take one stick for Judah, for Israel stand,  
Another for Joseph, join them in your hand.  

"When folks ask, 'What does it mean?'  
Say, 'Yahweh will gather, make them one team.'"  

"I’ll take them from nations, to their own land,  
One king, no division, united they'll stand."  

"No idols, no sin, they'll be cleansed,  
My people, their God, no need for amends."  

"David will reign, one shepherd they'll heed,  
Walking my laws, fulfilling the creed."  

"In Jacob's land, their place will be,  
Children forever, dwelling in peace with me."  

"A covenant of peace, everlasting and true,  
My sanctuary among them, I'll dwell too."  

"Nations will know, Yahweh sanctifies,  
My presence with Israel, no more goodbyes."
