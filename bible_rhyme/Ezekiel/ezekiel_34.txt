Yahweh's words hit me like a storm,  
"Son of man, speak, bring the norm.  

Tell those shepherds, hear what I say,  
Woe to those who lead astray.  

Feeding themselves while sheep they neglect,  
Eating the fat, the wool they collect.  

They kill the fatlings, yet the sheep are unfed,  
Not healing the sick, no care for the dead.  

Broken they leave, scattered away,  
Ruling with force, leading astray.  

My flock's scattered, mountains they climb,  
No shepherds in sight, not even one time.  

Listen up, shepherds, Yahweh declares,  
"My sheep are prey, caught unaware.  

Shepherds fed themselves, not my sheep,  
Now hear my word, listen deep.  

I'm against you, shepherds, your time is done,  
Rescue my flock, every last one.  

I'll search my sheep, bring them back home,  
From dark to light, no more to roam.  

I'll gather them up, to Israel's land,  
Feed them on mountains, by Yahweh's hand.  

Good pastures await, they will lie down,  
In justice and peace, they'll wear no frown.  

Seek the lost, heal the weak,  
Strengthen the sick, the future we seek.  

I'll judge between sheep, the strong and the lean,  
Clean pastures and waters, no more unclean.  

Pushers and prodders, your time's at an end,  
My flock is safe, on me they depend.  

One shepherd I'll set, David's line,  
He'll feed my sheep, in pastures divine.  

Yahweh's their God, David their prince,  
These words are spoken, let them convince.  

A covenant of peace, showers of blessing,  
Fields full of fruit, no fears distressing.  

Bars of yoke broken, slavery no more,  
Secure in their land, Yahweh they adore.  

They'll know I'm with them, forever near,  
My people, my sheep, have nothing to fear.  

You’re the sheep of my pasture, men in my care,  
I am your God, forever there."
