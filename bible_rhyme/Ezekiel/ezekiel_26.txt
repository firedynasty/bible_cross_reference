In the eleventh year, first day of the scene,  
God's word came clear, like a dream.  
"Son of man, hear Tyre's boastful say,  
'Aha, Jerusalem's broken, she's ours today!'

But listen up, Tyre, the Lord Yahweh's near,  
"Behold, I'm against you, feel the fear.  
Like waves from the sea, nations will rise,  
They'll crash your walls, tear down your skies.

Scrape off your dust, make you a bare rock,  
In the sea you'll be, where fishermen flock.  
For I've spoken," Yahweh said,  
"Plundered, you'll be, nations will tread.

Nebuchadnezzar's coming, king of kings,  
From the north, with armies, he brings.  
Your daughters in fields meet the sword,  
Against you, he'll build forts, and horde.

Engines will batter, axes will break,  
Walls will crumble, towers will shake.  
His horses' dust will cloud your sight,  
Chariots roar, walls trembling in fright.

Hoofbeats will echo in your streets loud,  
People fall, pillars hit the ground.  
Riches plundered, homes torn down,  
Stones, timber, dust in waters drown.

Songs will cease, harps won't play,  
Bare rock you'll be, nets where you'll lay.  
"Never rebuilt," Yahweh's decree,  
"Mark my words, that's how it'll be."

Islands will tremble at your sound,  
Princes of the sea will come down.  
Clothed in trembling, they'll sit,  
Astonished by Tyre's pitiful split.

They'll lament, "How you've fallen, renowned sea town,  
Once strong, now laid down.  
Islands shiver at your departure,  
Dismayed at Tyre's shattered future."

"When I make you desolate, cities not found,  
Deep waters cover, no more around.  
Into the pit, with old you'll go,  
In desolate places, you'll dwell below.

A terror you'll be, no more seen,  
Sought but missing, like a faded dream."  
Thus spoke Yahweh, His word's a bind,  
Tyre's end, no more will you find.
