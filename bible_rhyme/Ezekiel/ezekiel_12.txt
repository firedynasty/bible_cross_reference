In the midst of rebels, the message was clear,  
"Son of man, listen, and lend me your ear.  
Eyes they have, yet sight’s out of reach,  
Ears they own, but they don’t heed my speech.  

Pack up your stuff, let them see you leave,  
In broad daylight, let them watch and perceive.  
It's a sign to a house that never complies,  
Carry it out and let them realize.  

Dig through the wall, let the rubble fall,  
In their view, carry it out, take it all.  
Cover your face, don’t see the land,  
For you're a sign, as I’ve planned.  

I did what He said, moved in the day,  
Dug through the wall, took my things away.  
Darkness my cloak, on my shoulder's weight,  
In their sight, all of this I create.  

Morning came, Yahweh spoke anew,  
"Rebellious Israel questions, ‘What do you do?’  
Say to them, ‘This is a burden true,  
The prince, the people, it concerns you.’  

‘Like me, they’ll leave, exiled and gone,  
Through the wall and into the dawn.  
The prince will carry, face covered tight,  
He won’t see the land, but he'll face the night.  

I’ll spread my net, caught in my snare,  
To Babylon bound, yet not seeing there.  
Scattered in winds, their bands will go,  
Chased by the sword, they’ll surely know.  

Then they’ll know I am Yahweh indeed,  
Few will escape, from sword, famine freed.  
They’ll declare their ways in foreign zones,  
And know I’m Yahweh, on my throne."  

"Son of man, eat with trembling hands,  
Drink your water, fear in the lands.  
Tell them their bread will be eaten in fear,  
For violence and waste are drawing near.  

Cities once full, desolate and bare,  
Then they’ll know I’m Yahweh, everywhere."  

Yahweh said, "What’s this saying they’ve got,  
‘Days prolonged, visions all for naught’?  
Tell them ‘Yahweh says it’ll cease,  
Prophecies won’t delay, none for lease.  

Visions will come, false words won’t last,  
In your days, rebellious, the word’s cast.  
I’ll speak it, perform it, no more wait,  
What I declare will find its fate.’  

Again Yahweh spoke, "They say, ‘It’s far,  
Visions for days away, like a distant star.’  
Tell them, ‘No more delays, it’s time to act,  
My words fulfilled, that is a fact.’"
