"Son of man, take a bite, eat this scroll,  
Speak to Israel, let my words unfold."  
Mouth wide open, the scroll tastes sweet,  
Honey-like flavor, prophecy complete.  

He said, "Go to Israel, speak my word,  
Not to strangers' tongues, but to those who've heard.  
They won't listen, their hearts are stone,  
But I've made your face like a hardened bone."  

"Diamond forehead, harder than flint, stand strong,  
Fear not their stares, don't be led wrong.  
Receive my words, let them fill your heart,  
Go to your people, play your part."  

The Spirit lifted, and I heard a rush,  
"Bless Yahweh's glory," in a mighty hush.  
Wings and wheels, the sound so profound,  
Lifted me up, took me from the ground.  

In bitterness, with spirit’s heat inside,  
Yahweh's hand on me, my guide.  
To the captives by Chebar, I came,  
Sat overwhelmed, seven days the same.  

After days passed, Yahweh spoke again,  
"Watchman of Israel, hear my command then.  
Warn the wicked, 'You will surely die,'  
If you don't, their blood's on you, oh my."  

"Warn the righteous, keep them from sin,  
If they listen, their life they'll win.  
Your soul's delivered, your duty fulfilled,  
Even if warning them gets you killed."  

Yahweh's hand spoke, "Meet me on the plain,  
I'll talk to you there, explain my aim."  
I rose, I went, His glory stood,  
Like by Chebar, there I understood.  

The Spirit entered, set me on my feet,  
He spoke with me, words concrete.  
"Shut in your house, they'll tie you down,  
Mute your words, but don't you frown."  

"When I speak, your mouth I'll free,  
Say, 'This is Yahweh's decree.'  
If they hear or refuse, let it be known,  
To a rebellious house, truth is thrown."
