Yo, check it, these are the tribes' names laid down,  
From the north end, Hethlon to Hamath town.  

Hazar Enan stands as the border wide,  
With Damascus and Hamath at its northern side.  

Dan steps up, from east to west it rolls,  
Then comes Asher, owning similar goals.  

Naphtali's next, with a spread wide and grand,  
Manasseh follows, holding his land.  

Ephraim and Reuben, side by side they stand,  
Judah too, claiming his piece of the land.  

An offering's next, twenty-five thousand reeds,  
Sanctuary in the center, fulfilling holy needs.  

God declares, "The offering's for Me,  
Twenty-five thousand long, ten thousand wide, you see."  

For the priests, sons of Zadok, who've stayed true and strong,  
Kept My word, while others went wrong.  

Levites claim their share, a holy pact,  
Not to sell or trade, it's a sacred act.  

Five thousand left for the city’s plan,  
For homes, pastures, where the city stands.  

Measurements clear, four-five hundred each side,  
Pastures stretch two-fifty wide.  

The rest supports the city's labor,  
From all tribes, their work the flavor.  

Square of offerings twenty-five by twenty-five,  
Holy and pure, keeping the spirit alive.  

For the prince, more land by east and west,  
Near Judah and Benjamin's border, put to the test.  

Benjamin, Simeon, Issachar in line,  
Zebulun and Gad’s borders they define.  

From east to west each tribe’s claim,  
Tamar to the sea, they stake their name.  

"This land you'll divide, by lot you shall share,  
Among Israel's tribes, in portions so fair,"  

Says Yahweh, the Lord, His command is clear.  
City gates named for tribes, to honor and revere.  

On north side, gates for Reuben, Judah, Levi,  
East side for Joseph, Benjamin, Dan's ally.  

South side hosts Simeon, Issachar, Zebulun,  
West holds Gad, Asher, Naphtali won.  

Eighteen thousand reeds wrap around the town,  
And the name of the city? "Yahweh is there," renowned.
