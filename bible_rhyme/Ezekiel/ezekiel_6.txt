Yahweh spoke, gave me a task to do,  
"Son of man, face Israel's mountains, it's true.  

Prophesy to them, let my words unfold,  
To mountains and valleys, my story is told.  

'Behold! See the sword that I bring,  
Your high places fall, your idols will sting.  

Altars desolate, incense altars broken,  
Your slain before idols, that's my token.  

Bones scattered round your sacred space,  
Cities laid waste, a desolate place.  

Slain will fall, know my name,  
Yahweh I am, never in vain.  

Yet a remnant left, some escape the sword,  
Scattered nations, they'll recall my word.  

Broken by hearts that strayed,  
Eyes chasing idols, my love betrayed.  

They'll loathe their ways, see the wrongs they've done,  
They'll know I am Yahweh, the only one.  

Lord Yahweh says, 'Clap and stomp, cry "Alas!"  
Evil abominations, Israel's past.  

Fall by sword, famine, pestilence's breath,  
Accomplishing wrath, amid their death.  

Know I'm Yahweh, when slain are laid,  
Under trees, on hills, where idols are made.  

Stretch my hand, desolation's spread,  
From wilderness to Diblah, where hope is dead.  

Then, at last, they'll know my name,  
Yahweh I am, forever the same.'"
