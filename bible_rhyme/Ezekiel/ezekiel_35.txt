Yo, Yahweh spoke, told the prophet what to say,  
"Turn to Mount Seir, I'm comin' your way.  

The Lord says, 'Yo, Seir, I'm against you today,  
Stretchin' my hand, makin' ruin your stay.  

Your cities laid waste, desolation's the goal,  
Then you'll know I'm Yahweh, the one in control.  

Your hate for my people, that's where you went wrong,  
In their darkest hour, you handed 'em along.  

So listen up now, as I live and breathe,  
Blood's gonna chase you, you won't find reprieve.  

I'll make Seir a shock, a deserted land,  
Cutting off the travelers, none understand.  

Mountains filled with the fallen, swords in the hills,  
Valleys and streams, the silence that kills.  

A desolation forever, your cities no more,  
Then you'll know I'm Yahweh, the one they adore.  

You claimed you’d take what wasn’t yours,  
Two nations, two lands, you thought yours for sure.  

But I say, 'As I live,' your anger's been seen,  
Your envy and hate, a reckoning's keen.  

You'll know I'm Yahweh when my judgments come through,  
I've heard what you've said, those insults you threw.  

'Devour the desolate,' you thought you'd won,  
But I heard it all, every boast, every one.  

When the earth sings, in joy and delight,  
You'll be the desolate, lost in the night.  

Rejoiced at their fall, now yours will be worse,  
Then all will know Yahweh, the one with the verse."
