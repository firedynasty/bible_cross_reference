"Yo, son of man, prophesy, make it clear,  
The Lord Yahweh speaks, so listen near.  

"Behold, I'm against you, Gog from the north,  
Turn you around, lead you forth.  

Onto Israel's mountains you'll be led,  
Bows and arrows will fall, your power's dead.  

On those hills, you and your horde will lay,  
Birds and beasts will feast today.  

"You'll fall in the open space,  
For I've spoken it with grace."  

A fire on Magog, islands too,  
Know I'm Yahweh, it's true.  

"My name in Israel will shine,  
No more profane, it's divine.  

Behold, it's coming, I've said,  
The day is near, it's not misread.  

"In Israel's cities, folks will burn,  
Weapons for fires, year-long turn.  

No need for wood, the weapons will serve,  
Robbers robbed, justice preserved.  

"Gog's grave in Israel will be made,  
In the east, where passers stayed.  

Bury them for months, cleanse the land,  
My glory, my command.  

Workers set apart to roam,  
To bury bones, make them known.  

Hamon Gog, a valley's name,  
Cleanse the land, erase the shame.  

"Birds and beasts, gather 'round,  
A sacrifice on the mountains found.  

Eat the mighty, drink deep,  
Of princes' blood, the land to sweep.  

"Filled at my table, feast of might,  
With horses, chariots, men of fight.  

"My glory shown, my judgment clear,  
Nations will see my hand, fear.  

Israel will know, I'm their God,  
From this day, forward, in awe.  

Captured for sin, hid my face,  
Delivered them, now they're embraced.  

Mercy flows, Jacob's fate reversed,  
For my holy name, I'm immersed.  

In their land, safe they'll stay,  
Enemies gone, fear away.  

Gathered back, sanctified bright,  
Captives no more, in my sight.  

My Spirit poured, face revealed,  
Israel, I'm with you, the deal's sealed."
