Yahweh spoke, said it clear, "Son of man, hear my plea,  
Judge this bloody city, expose her infamy.  
A town shedding blood, idols crafted in hate,  
Guilty of her actions, her own end she'll create."

"Shamed by the nations, mocked far and near,  
Infamous city, full of chaos and fear.  
Princes shed blood, power corrupt and wild,  
Parents disrespected, orphans and widows reviled."

"You've scorned my holy things, my Sabbaths profaned,  
Slander and lewdness in mountains, unrestrained.  
Abominations abound, neighbor's wife defiled,  
Daughter-in-law shamed, sister made reviled."

"Bribes for blood, interest taken with greed,  
Oppressed your neighbors, forgot my creed.  
I've struck my hand at your dishonest gain,  
Can you endure the judgment, face the pain?"

"Scattered you'll be, across nations wide,  
Profaned and filthy, no place to hide.  
Then you'll know, Yahweh's name you'll see,  
For I've spoken, it's my decree."

"Israel's become dross, like metals in flame,  
Bronze, tin, iron, in the furnace of shame.  
Gathered in wrath, melted by fire,  
You'll feel my anger, my burning desire."

"Prophets conspire, like lions roar,  
Devouring souls, treasures they store.  
Priests violate law, desecrate the sacred line,  
Make no distinction, between holy and unclean sign."

"Princes like wolves, prey they pursue,  
Shedding blood, dishonest gain their view.  
Prophets false visions, whitewashed lies they tell,  
Claiming Yahweh's word, when silence fell."

"Opression runs rampant, robbery is rife,  
Poor and needy suffer, foreigner faces strife.  
Sought a man to stand, the gap to bridge,  
Found none among them, so wrath is my pledge."

"My indignation poured, the fire's fierce track,  
Their ways on their heads, no turning back."
