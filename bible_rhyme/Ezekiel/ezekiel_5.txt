"Son of man, grab a sword, sharp as a blade,  
Use it like a razor, your head and beard invade.  
Weigh and divide that hair, piece by piece,  
A third you'll burn in the city's heart, the fire won't cease.  

Another third, strike it with your sword in full force,  
The last third, scatter it to the wind's course.  
Bind a few strands in your robe, tight and neat,  
Then toss them in the fire, let the flames complete.  

The Lord Yahweh declares, 'This is Jerusalem's fate,  
Placed among nations, but rebels at a higher rate.  
Ignored my laws, more than those nations near,  
Rejected my statutes, refused to adhere.'  

'So I'm against you,' Yahweh firmly says,  
'Judgments will rain down, in astonishing ways.  
What I will do, never done before,  
Because of your sins, abominations I abhor.  

Fathers and sons will eat one another,  
Judgments scattered, no refuge to recover.  
Your detestable acts have defiled my place,  
My eye won't spare; I'll diminish your space.  

A third of you will perish by pestilence's claw,  
Another third by the sword, no mercy, no law.  
The last third will scatter with the wind as it goes,  
With a sword chasing after, until my wrath slows.  

My anger will rest, my wrath will cease,  
Then you'll know Yahweh's words bring no peace.  
I'll make you a desolation, a tale for the lands,  
A reproach and taunt, seen by all who pass, stands.  

My judgments are fierce, like arrows they fly,  
Famine's the tool to make your strength die.  
Evil beasts, pestilence, blood through your street,  
The sword will descend, my words are complete.'"
