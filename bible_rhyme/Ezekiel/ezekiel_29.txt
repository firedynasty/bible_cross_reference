In the tenth year, tenth month, day twelve came the word,  
God spoke to me, His voice clearly heard.  

"Son of man, face Pharaoh, king of the Nile,  
Prophesy against him and Egypt in style.  

The Lord Yahweh speaks, Pharaoh, hear me now,  
I’m against you, great monster, time to take a bow.  

You said ‘My river, I made it my zone,’  
But I'll hook your jaws, drag you from your throne.  

Fish of your rivers will cling to your scales,  
I'll pull you out, where desolation prevails.  

Cast into the wild, you and your aquatic crew,  
Left in the open, no gathering for you.  

Food for the beasts, birds in the sky so blue,  
Egypt will know Yahweh, His power so true.  

You were a reed to Israel, snapped in their hand,  
Broke and tore them when they took a stand.  

Yahweh says, hear my sword, feel its might,  
Man and beast cut off, Egypt’s future not bright.  

Desolation spreads, your land laid bare,  
From Seveneh to Ethiopia, none will dare.  

Forty years of waste, cities in despair,  
Scattered Egyptians, wandering everywhere.  

After forty years, I'll gather you once more,  
Return to Pathros, to the roots you bore.  

A kingdom lowly, no longer ruling the scene,  
Diminished and humble, never to be keen.  

No more the crutch for Israel’s quest,  
Know I am Yahweh, your ultimate test."  

In the twenty-seventh year, first month, first day,  
Yahweh's word came, more truths to convey.  

"Nebuchadnezzar, Babylon’s king,  
Labored against Tyre, but lacking the bling.  

No wages for him, no reward from the fight,  
So Egypt I'll give him, to set things right.  

Loot and plunder, his army’s due,  
They worked for me,” Yahweh’s words so true.  

"In that day, for Israel, a horn will rise,  
Your mouth will open, no more disguise.  

Then they'll know Yahweh, the Lord of the skies."
