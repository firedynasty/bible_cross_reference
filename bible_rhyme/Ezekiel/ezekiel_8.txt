In the sixth year, sixth month, fifth day, in the house I sat,  
Judah's elders with me, then God's hand fell flat.  

Behold, fire's likeness, waist down was a blaze,  
Upward a brightness, like metal ablaze.  

A hand reached out, grabbed a lock of my hair,  
The Spirit lifted me, earth and sky in the air.  

Visions took me to Jerusalem's gate,  
Image of jealousy, provoking God's fate.  

God's glory shone, Israel's God in sight,  
"Son of man," He said, "look north, take flight."  

I lifted my eyes, saw the jealousy stand,  
"Do you see, Son of man, the abominations at hand?  

Israel's sins make Me leave My own place,  
But more abominations you'll soon face."  

He led me to the court, a hole in the wall,  
"Dig through, Son of man, uncover it all."  

I dug and found a door, beyond it lay,  
Wicked abominations, night to day.  

Creeping things and idols lined the walls,  
Seventy elders with censers in the halls.  

The incense rose, cloud of a sinful shroud,  
"In the dark, they say, 'Yahweh's not around.'"  

"More abominations yet you will see,"  
To the north gate, where women cried in plea.  

"Weeping for Tammuz, have you seen this, my son?  
Greater abominations are still not done."  

Inner court, twenty-five men stood,  
Back to the temple, sun worship they would.  

"Is it light to Judah, these sins they repeat,  
Filled with violence, their actions indiscreet?  

They provoke Me to wrath, the branch to their nose,  
I will not spare them, My voice won’t close.  

Though they cry loud, My ears will not hear,  
For the acts they've committed, My judgment is near."
