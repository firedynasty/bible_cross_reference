In the twenty-fifth year, captivity's scene,  
New year, tenth day, visions keen.  
Yahweh's hand took me far and wide,  
To a high mount, where visions abide.  

In God's vision, to Israel's land,  
A city framed, high did stand.  
There stood a man, like bronze he shone,  
With flax and reed, he wasn't alone.  

He said, "Son of man, look and see,  
Hear with your ears, these visions be.  
You've been brought for what I show,  
Tell Israel all you know."  

A wall around, measured with care,  
Reed in hand, the man was there.  
He measured gates, steps that lead,  
Thresholds wide, all with that reed.  

To the eastward gate he went,  
Thresholds measured, a reed's extent.  
Lodges lined, each one the same,  
Porch and posts, in precise frame.  

Gate width ten, length thirteen,  
Borders, lodges, a measured scene.  
Roofs to roofs, door against door,  
Posts sixty tall, court around the floor.  

Fifty cubits, gate to porch,  
Windows shut, palm trees torch.  
Outer court, rooms in line,  
Thirty rooms, pavements fine.  

From gate to court, a hundred span,  
East and north, the measure ran.  
North gate measured, just like before,  
Posts, arches, palm trees galore.  

Seven steps up, arches grand,  
Gate to gate, a hundred span.  
Southward now, another gate,  
Measured posts, arches great.  

Windows round, arches wide,  
Palm trees carved on either side.  
Steps up seven, inner court near,  
Gate to gate, measurements clear.  

Inner court by south we go,  
Lodges, posts, arches show.  
Windows round, arches neat,  
Fifty long, twenty-five feet.  

Arches faced the outer way,  
Palm trees marked where they lay.  
East gate next, same design,  
Windows, arches all align.  

To the north, the pattern bold,  
Windows, arches, palm trees told.  
Room by posts, offerings to start,  
Tables set for sacred art.  

Four tables each side stand,  
Where offerings laid by hand.  
Hooks inside, offerings laid,  
Instruments down, meat displayed.  

Singers' rooms by inner gate,  
North and south, they situate.  
He said, "Priests' rooms, duty hold,  
Sons of Zadok, pure and bold."  

Court measured square, altar seen,  
Porch measured, pillars keen.  
Steps led up, pillars two,  
A solemn scene, in view.
