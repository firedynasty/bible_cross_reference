Yahweh spoke, and I heard Him declare,  
"Son of man, tell Israel, I'm everywhere.  

An end has come, on every corner it lands,  
My anger's unleashed, in judgment it stands.  

Your ways come back, your sins on display,  
No mercy from me, you'll know me this day.  

'Evil's unique, behold, it arrives,  
Your doom is here, it catches your lives.  

The time is now, no joy on the hills,  
My wrath is coming, to judge all your ills.  

No pity from me, your ways bring your fall,  
You'll know it's Yahweh, above it all.  

See the day approach, doom's rod has grown,  
Violence blooms, no wealth to be shown.  

Time's here, the day draws near,  
Buyers and sellers, in fear and tears.  

The trumpet sounds, ready or not,  
My wrath is on you, like a blistering shot.  

Sword outside, famine within,  
Field or city, it's where you’ll begin.  

The mountain's refuge, like moaning doves,  
Hands weak, knees tremble, forsaking loves.  

Sackcloth and horror, heads bald, shame,  
Silver in streets, gold is a stain.  

These won't save you, when my wrath descends,  
Your idol's wealth, iniquity bends.  

Foreigners plunder, your treasures defiled,  
Turn from you, my face exiled.  

Chains made ready, the land's full of crime,  
Nations invade, in your pride's decline.  

Destruction comes, peace can't be found,  
Rumors rise, mischief compounds.  

Prophet's vision lost, priest's law is gone,  
King and prince in mourning, forlorn.  

I'll judge by your ways, your own choices revealed,  
Then you'll know Yahweh, as the truth is unsealed."
