"Son of man," He said, "stand up, feet firm and true,  
I'll speak with you now, this message is due."

The Spirit swept in as His voice filled the air,  
Lifted me upright, His words clear and rare.

"Son of man," He continued, "to Israel you go,  
To a nation of rebels, they need to know."

"From fathers to sons, they've turned away,  
Yet today I send you to show them My way."

"Stubborn and hard, these children of mine,  
Say, 'This is Yahweh's word, straight and divine.'"

"Listen or not, let them choose their stance,  
But they'll know a prophet took this chance."

"Don't fear their words, nor thorns that sting,  
Even scorpions can't break the message I bring."

"Speak My words, whether they hear or deny,  
For they are rebellious, but you must try."

"But you, son of man, heed My decree,  
Don't be like them, stay true to Me."

"Open your mouth, eat what I give,  
In this truth, you'll surely live."

A hand reached out, a scroll unrolled,  
Inside and out, lamentations were told.
