He led me to the gate, to the east it faced,  
The glory of God came, the earth was embraced.  

A voice like waters, His presence so bright,  
Fell on my face in the dazzling light.  

Through the eastern gate, His glory did ride,  
The Spirit took me, brought me inside.  

The house was filled, His glory displayed,  
A voice from the house, these words were conveyed:  

"Son of man, this is my throne's place,  
Where I'll dwell forever with Israel's race."  

No more shall they defile my name,  
With idols and kings, bringing me shame.  

"Son of man, show Israel their form,  
Measure the house, let them reform.  

If shame fills their hearts, reveal every space,  
All ordinances and laws they must embrace."  

"This house on the mountain, holy it stands,  
These are its laws, by God's commands."  

Measurements given, for altar's design,  
Cubits precise, God's plan aligns.  

"Son of man, these are altar's commands,  
Offerings made by priestly hands.  

A bull for sin, blood on horns to spread,  
Cleansed and atoned, by these rites led.  

Burn the bull in a sacred place,  
Second day's goat, a cleansing embrace.  

A bull and a ram, without defect,  
On the altar, salt they project.  

Seven days, consecrate and purify,  
Eighth day, offerings rise to the sky.  

Then peace will reign, and you'll be received,"  
Says the Lord Yahweh, who is to be believed.
