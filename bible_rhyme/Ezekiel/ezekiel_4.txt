"Son of man, grab a tile, make it real,  
Draw the city, Jerusalem, make it feel.  
Lay siege, build forts, raise up a mound,  
Camps and rams, surround it all around.  

Take an iron pan, set it like a wall,  
Face the city, this siege will stand tall.  
Lie on your side, for Israel bear the weight,  
Three hundred ninety days, their sins on your plate.  

"When that’s done, switch sides, Judah's turn to bear,  
Forty days for forty years, their story laid bare.  
Face the siege, arm out, prophesy strong,  
With ropes I’ll hold you till the days are long.  

"Gather wheat, barley, beans, lentils all in one,  
Make bread, day by day, till the time is done.  
Eat by weight, twenty shekels per day,  
Drink by measure, water rationed all the way.  

Make barley cakes, bake them on what man expels,  
Yahweh declared, 'Unclean bread among the nations dwells.'  
I cried, 'Lord Yahweh, I’ve kept myself clean,  
No filthy meat has ever come between!'  

Yahweh said, 'Use cow dung instead of man’s,  
Prepare your bread, as My judgment stands.  
In Jerusalem, bread will break, fear will rise,  
Water scarce, they’ll mourn in their own disguise.  

In their iniquity, lacking bread and water,  
Dismay will rule, as they falter and totter.'"
