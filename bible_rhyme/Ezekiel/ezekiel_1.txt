In the thirtieth year, fourth month, fifth day,  
By the river Chebar, visions came my way.  

In Jehoiachin’s fifth year of captivity's test,  
Yahweh's word came; His presence impressed.  

A stormy wind from the north, clouds flashed bright,  
Glowing metal from fire, a powerful sight.  

From its center emerged four living things,  
Human form, with faces and wings.  

Four faces each, four wings they displayed,  
Feet straight and strong, like bronze, finely made.  

Hands beneath wings, each face held a plan,  
Lion, ox, eagle, and the face of a man.  

Wings outstretched, two touched, two concealed,  
They moved straight ahead, their path revealed.  

Like burning coals, torches blazing in line,  
Lightning flashed, creatures moved with a sign.  

Beside each face, a wheel on the ground,  
Wheels within wheels, beryl-like, profound.  

High rims, dreadful, eyes all around,  
With the spirit, they moved, no turning found.  

Above their heads, an expanse crystal clear,  
Wings straightened, like waters they cheer.  

A voice above, wings lowered in might,  
A sapphire throne, a man bathed in light.  

Glowing metal, fire's glow bright and wide,  
Rainbow-like glory encircles his side.  

In Yahweh’s presence, I fell to the floor,  
He spoke, His voice, a thunderous roar.
