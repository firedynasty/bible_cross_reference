Yahweh's word hit me like a thunderclap,  
"Son of man, tell the prince of Tyre this rap:  

'Your heart's puffed up, you claim you're a god,  
Sitting in the seas, acting like you’re Lord.  

But you're a man, not divine in any way,  
You think you're wise; Daniel ain't got your sway.  

With wisdom and wit, you stacked silver and gold,  
Trading brought riches, your pride's uncontrolled.  

So listen up, here's what Yahweh's gonna do,  
Bringing strangers, nations terrible and true.  

They'll draw their swords, cut through your wisdom's glow,  
Down to the pit, your brightness will go.  

Will you declare to your slayer, 'I'm a god'?  
You're a man, he'll see through that façade.  

Uncircumcised death, by a foreigner’s hand,  
'Cause Yahweh's spoken, now do you understand?  

Yahweh's voice echoed in my ear once more,  
"Son of man, let this lamentation soar:  

'You were the seal of wisdom, beauty's prime,  
In Eden's garden, adorned for a time.  

Ruby and topaz, emerald and stone,  
Gold tambourines, pipes in you alone.  

Anointed cherub, holy mountain's tread,  
Perfect till unrighteousness in you spread.  

Trading's abundance filled you with might,  
Sinned, now cast down from the mountain's height.  

Your beauty's pride twisted wisdom's path,  
Before kings laid down, bearing Yahweh's wrath.  

Fire from within you, devoured your frame,  
Now ashes on earth, all witness your shame.  

Astonished the people, a terror's your name,  
Existence snuffed out, no more in the game.'  

Yahweh spoke, "Son of man, face Sidon's land,  
Prophesy against them, as I command:  

'Behold, Sidon, Yahweh's against thee,  
My glory you'll see, then you'll know it's Me.  

Pestilence and blood will flood your streets,  
Swords on all sides, your defeat repeats.  

No more thorns for Israel, no briers in their land,  
They'll know I'm Yahweh, My word will stand.  

When Israel's gathered from scattered nations wide,  
Sanctified in them, in their land they'll abide.  

Securely they'll live, vineyards and homes,  
Judgments executed, no more to roam.  

Then they'll know I am Yahweh, their God supreme,  
In their own land, living the dream."
