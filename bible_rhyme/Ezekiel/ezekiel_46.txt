The Lord Yahweh speaks, hear what He says,  
East gate's closed on the workweek days.  

But Sabbath breaks, the gate swings wide,  
New moon too, let the prince inside.  

Prince enters through the porch, stands by,  
Priests prepare offerings, watch him comply.  

Worship at the gate's edge, then he leaves,  
Gate stays open 'til the moonlight weaves.  

The people gather, they all come near,  
Sabbaths and new moons, they revere.  

Six lambs flawless, a ram without blame,  
The prince offers up in Yahweh's name.  

Ephah for the ram, lambs get what he can,  
Hin of oil blends with this holy plan.  

New moon's bull, lambs, and ram the same,  
All must be perfect, no spot, no shame.  

He prepares the meal like before,  
With oil and ephah, offerings galore.  

Prince enters the porch, exits the same,  
His path is set, no need to reclaim.  

When the people come, feast days bright,  
Enter north, exit south in Yahweh's sight.  

Prince joins them, goes out with the flow,  
In and out with the people's show.  

Feast meals, ephah for bull and ram,  
As he is able, the offering's plan.  

At free will offerings, the east gate's ajar,  
For the prince to give, like Sabbath, not far.  

Daily a lamb, perfect, year old,  
Morning by morning, this tale is retold.  

Meal offerings, oil, and flour blend,  
Each morning's gift, no end.  

The prince has gifts, he passes them down,  
To sons, it's inheritance, no frown.  

But if to a servant, just for a year,  
Returns to the prince, this is clear.  

Don't take from the people, his own he'll give,  
Keep their possessions, ensure they live.  

He led me to rooms where priests reside,  
North-facing chambers on the side.  

"Here priests boil offerings, bake the meal,  
Keep them holy, out of the public's zeal."  

He showed me corners, courts enclosed,  
Forty by thirty, their purpose exposed.  

Walls surround, boilers underneath,  
Ministers cook for the people beneath.  

"These boiling houses," he said to me,  
"Where sacrifices for people shall be."
