Two sisters, Oholah and Oholibah, named,  
Played games in Egypt, where their youth was tamed.  
They were mine, yet they strayed, chasing desire,  
Lusting for Assyrians, setting hearts on fire.  

Oholah, the elder, chased her Assyrian dreams,  
Men in blue, on horses, a sight that gleams.  
She gave herself away, defiled by their hands,  
Never left her past in those foreign lands.  

"Thus I gave her to her lovers, those she adored,  
They stripped her bare, left her with a sword.  
Her children taken, her life laid to waste,  
Her story a warning, in haste, they faced."  

Yet Oholibah saw, and deeper she fell,  
After Assyrian lords, she lusted as well.  
Images on walls, Chaldeans with pride,  
She sent word, and they came to her side.  

In the bed of love, polluted she became,  
Her soul turned cold, her heart aflame.  
She uncovered herself, her past she'd revive,  
Her youthful passions, in Egypt she'd strive.  

God said, "Oholibah, your lovers will rise,  
From Babylon, Assyria, see their eyes.  
They'll come with weapons, fury in their hand,  
Judgment passed by their command."  

"Jealousy and rage, they'll strip you bare,  
Your children lost, your jewels rare.  
Your lewdness will cease, no more Egypt's fame,  
Forget your past, no more of that game."  

Oholibah, you'll drink from your sister's cup,  
A deep, bitter draught, won't let up.  
"Desolation and sorrow, you'll tear your own breast,  
For you've forgotten me, failed my test."  

God spoke, "Shall you judge these sisters, man?  
Adultery and blood, in their own hands ran.  
Defiled my house, on my Sabbath they tread,  
Children passed through fire, to idols misled."  

"They called for men, from afar they came,  
Painted eyes, adorned, seeking fame.  
Drunkards and fools, they entertained in bed,  
Even on sacred ground, they boldly tread."  

"Righteous men will judge, adulteresses condemned,  
For blood on their hands, to destruction they're penned.  
A mob will rise, stones and swords will fall,  
Homes burned to ashes, they'll face their call."  

"In this, lewdness will cease, a lesson taught,  
Women will learn, to sin they ought not.  
You'll bear your idols' sins, all will see,  
Then you'll know, I am Yahweh, let it be."
