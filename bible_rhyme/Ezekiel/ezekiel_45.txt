When you divide the land, an offering's the plan,  
A holy portion long and wide, Yahweh's command.  

Five hundred by five hundred, a square most neat,  
With fifty cubits around for pasture complete.  

Measure out the land, sanctuary inside,  
For priests close to Yahweh, where they’ll reside.  

For Levites, twenty-five by ten in length,  
Housing them with rooms, giving them strength.  

The city's five by twenty-five, side by side,  
For Israel's house, where they all abide.  

For the prince, land stretches east to west,  
No more oppression, give the tribes their rest.  

"Princes, take heed," Yahweh's voice rings,  
"Do justice, righteousness, fair dealings it brings."  

Equal measures, ephah, bath, and shekel,  
Ajust weight, no more injustice, let fairness settle.  

Offer a sixth of ephah from a homer of wheat,  
Barley, oil, a lamb from well-watered retreat.  

For offerings of peace, atonement you’ll make,  
"All people contribute for the prince's sake."  

The prince will provide, feasts he'll maintain,  
Sin and meal offerings, in Yahweh's name.  

"In the first month, cleanse the sanctuary pure,  
Young bull's blood on doorposts, atonement sure."  

Celebrate Passover, unleavened bread for seven,  
For the sin offering, a bull to be given.  

Seven days, bulls and rams, no defect found,  
Daily offerings, peace in their sound.  

In the seventh month, do it again,  
Sin and meal offerings, to Yahweh, amen.
