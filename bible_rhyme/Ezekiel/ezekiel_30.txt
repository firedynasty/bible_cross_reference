Yahweh spoke, "Son of man, hear my say,  
Prophesy now, cry 'Alas for the day!'  

The day of Yahweh, clouds will cover the sky,  
Nations will tremble, hear Egypt's cry.  

A sword's gonna drop, Ethiopia in pain,  
Egypt's multitude gone, her foundations slain.  

Ethiopia, Put, Lud, all fall by the sword,  
Chaos in the land, no peace can be restored.  

Yahweh declares, "Egypt's pride will fall,  
From Seveneh's tower, hear the desolate call.  

Amidst the wasted places, cities will be bare,  
They'll know it's Yahweh when the flames flare.  

Messengers in ships, Ethiopians will fear,  
Anguish like Egypt’s, the reckoning is here."  

Yahweh says, "I'll make Egypt's multitude cease,  
Nebuchadnezzar's hand won't bring them peace.  

His people, fierce, will strike with their might,  
Egypt's rivers dry, vanquished in the fight.  

Idols destroyed, Memphis' images cease,  
Egypt's princes gone, fear will not decrease.  

Pathros in ruins, Zoan set ablaze,  
Judgments on No, wrath in a fiery haze.  

Sin's stronghold shattered, No's multitude gone,  
Memphis in daylight faces foes head-on.  

Young men fall, taken captive and chained,  
Pride of Egypt, in darkness remained.  

Judgments on Egypt, they'll know my name,  
Yahweh, the Lord, through chaos and flame."  

Eleventh year, first month, seventh day,  
Yahweh spoke again, listen to what He'd say.  

"Pharaoh's arm broken, no bandage to bind,  
Sword in his hand, now left behind.  

I'm against Pharaoh, his strong arm I'll break,  
Egyptians scattered, in foreign lands they'll wake.  

King of Babylon, I'll strengthen his hand,  
Pharaoh's groan before him, as he loses his stand.  

Babylon's arms held high, Pharaoh's will fall,  
Then know I am Yahweh, the one above all.  

Egypt dispersed, scattered wide and low,  
They'll know I'm Yahweh, as my power I'll show."
