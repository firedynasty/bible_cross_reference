Got a word from Yahweh, listen close,  
"What's this proverb y'all boast?  

Fathers eat sour grapes, kids' teeth get sore,  
Not in my land, not anymore.  

Every soul is mine, father and son,  
The one who sins? That's the one who's done.  

If a man stays right, lawful in sight,  
Doesn't feast on mountains, avoids idols' light.  

No neighbor's wife did he defile,  
No robbery, just offers a smile.  

Feeds the hungry, clothes those in need,  
No interest taken, from iniquity freed.  

Walks in my ways, keeps my decree,  
That man will live, surely, you'll see.  

But if his son turns ruthless and cold,  
Sheds blood, robs, and breaks the mold,  

Eats at shrines, defiles, lifts his eyes,  
To idols of stone, he lives in lies.  

This son shall perish, blood on his head,  
Abominations done, he's surely dead.  

Now if this wicked man has a son,  
Sees the father's sins and decides to run,  

Avoids the shrines, keeps the law tight,  
Doesn't follow darkness, walks in the light,  

Feeds the hungry, avoids the loan's snare,  
He won't die for dad's sins, life's fair.  

Father may die in his own cruel way,  
But the son's got life, he's here to stay.  

"Why doesn't the son bear the father's sin?"  
When he's living right, why drag him in?  

Sins are personal, soul by soul,  
Righteous or wicked, each one whole.  

But if the wicked man turns and repents,  
Follows my statutes, to righteousness he consents.  

His past's forgotten, nothing to fear,  
In his new path, he'll find life here.  

"Do I want the wicked dead?" Yahweh asks,  
"Turn and live, that's the task."  

But if the righteous switches to wrong,  
And starts singing the wicked song,  

His past good deeds won't count, he’ll fall,  
In his iniquity, he'll lose it all.  

"Unfair!" you say, "Your ways aren't straight!"  
Israel, check your paths, yours hold the weight.  

A righteous man turns evil, he dies in sin,  
A wicked man turns good, his life's a win.  

House of Israel, hear my call,  
I'll judge you, every one of y'all.  

Turn from sin, make a new start,  
Get a new spirit, a fresh heart.  

I find no joy in death's cold sting,  
Turn back to me, and live, my offspring."
