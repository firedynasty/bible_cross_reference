The Spirit lifted me up, brought me east to the gate,  
Twenty-five men stand there, princes with their fate.  

"Son of man," He said, "these men plot iniquity,  
Their counsel's wicked, poisoned the city."  

"They say, 'No need for homes, we're safe in this heat,  
This is the cauldron, we are the meat.'"  

"Prophesy, son of man, against their deceit."  
Yahweh's Spirit on me, His words I repeat:  

"You've filled the streets with the slain, no one alive,  
This cauldron's no safe haven, you won't survive."  

"You feared the sword, and the sword you shall meet,  
Judgment awaits, strangers will sweep you off your feet."  

"This is not your cauldron, you're not the feast,  
You'll know I'm Yahweh when your rebellion's ceased."  

Pelatiah fell dead, I cried out in dismay,  
"Will Israel's remnant vanish, Lord, I pray?"  

Yahweh spoke again: "Your brothers are cast aside,  
Jerusalem's claim, 'This land's our pride.'"  

"But tell them: 'Though scattered wide, I'll be your place,  
A sanctuary afar, for a time, in grace.'"  

"I'll gather you back, from lands far and near,  
Return you to Israel, cleanse what's dear."  

"A new heart I'll give, flesh where stone once lay,  
Walk my statutes, be my people, come what may."  

"Yet those clinging to abominations and vile ways,  
Their deeds will judge them, end of days."  

The cherubim rose, wings and wheels in sync,  
The glory of Yahweh, on the city's brink.  

The Spirit took me back, across the land wide,  
To captives in Chaldea, with vision as my guide.  

I spoke to them all, what Yahweh had shown,  
His message of judgment and mercy, they'd own.
