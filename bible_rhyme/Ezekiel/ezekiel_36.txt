Son of man, hear Yahweh's decree,  
To the mountains of Israel, listen closely to me.  

"The enemy taunts, claiming what’s mine,  
Those ancient high places, they think they’ll define."  

They left you desolate, made you a prey,  
Now you're a tale on every tongue, they say.  

Mountains and valleys, every hill and stream,  
You've been mocked by nations, but here's Yahweh's dream.  

"My jealousy burns against those that surround,  
Who claim my land with spite, bringing it down."  

To Israel’s land, I now prophesy,  
"The nations around you will face their own cry."  

"But you, oh mountains, your branches will sprout,  
Israel's return is what it’s all about."  

"I'll multiply people, rebuild every place,  
The desolate cities will have a new face."  

Men and beasts will thrive, fruitful and grand,  
Better than ever, you'll know Yahweh’s hand.  

"You won't hear the shame, the reproach anymore,  
Your people will prosper, unlike before."  

Yahweh says, "Israel defiled its land,  
With blood and idols, they ignored my command."  

So I scattered them wide, judged by their ways,  
Profaning my name, in those distant days.  

"But not for your sake do I act," Yahweh declares,  
"For my holy name, I’ll answer their prayers."  

"I'll gather you back, cleanse every stain,  
Give you new hearts, new spirit to reign."  

"My statutes you’ll keep, my laws you’ll own,  
You’ll be my people; I’ll be your God alone."  

No more famine, fruit will abound,  
The nations will see how my power's profound.  

"You’ll recall your past, and how far you fell,  
But know I cleanse you, to break every spell."  

Desolate lands will be like Eden's lush scene,  
Ruined cities built up, so green and serene.  

Nations will know Yahweh restored it all,  
Flocks of people will answer my call.  

When Israel inquires, I’ll fulfill their hope,  
Flocks like Jerusalem’s, with blessings to cope.  

And then, all will know, I am Yahweh, the Lord,  
The desolate revived, by my mighty word.
