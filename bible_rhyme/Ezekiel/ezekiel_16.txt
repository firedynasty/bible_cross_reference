Yo, listen up, Yahweh's word's in play,  
"Son of man, to Jerusalem, let's convey.  

Your roots in Canaan, your birth in the land,  
Amorite dad, Hittite mom, understand.  

You were born, no cord was snipped,  
No water washed you, no blankets gripped.  

No eye showed you a hint of love,  
Cast in the field, no cover above.  

I passed by, saw you in blood,  
Said, 'Live in that crimson flood.'  

I made you grow, beauty displayed,  
But you were bare, no clothes arrayed.  

Time for love, I spread my cloak,  
Covenant made, our bond bespoke.  

I washed you clean, anointed with oil,  
Embroidery and silk, I dressed you in royal.  

Jewels and gold adorned your form,  
You were stunning, took the world by storm.  

But you trusted beauty, played the fool,  
Prostituted fame, broke every rule.  

Made shrines with garments, colors bright,  
Worshipped objects, lost in sight.  

Gave bread and oil to idols, oh why?  
Sacrificed sons and daughters, made Yahweh cry.  

Forgot your youth, naked and bare,  
Wallowing in blood, without a care.  

"Woe to you!"—Yahweh's refrain,  
Built lofty places, but brought only pain.  

Egyptians, Assyrians, you entertained,  
With merchants, ever unsatisfied, remained.  

Weak heart, impudent in your way,  
Not even scorned pay, for shame shall stay.  

A wife unfaithful, adultery rife,  
Giving gifts to lovers, courting strife.  

"Therefore, hear Yahweh's decree,"  
Your filthiness exposed for all to see.  

Lovers gathered, nakedness shown,  
Judgment like women with broken vows known.  

Stripped and stoned, wrath and fire,  
No more prostitution, your ways retire.  

In your shame, remember the past,  
When Yahweh's love was meant to last.  

Yet, Yahweh's covenant never fades,  
Promised to restore, though choices made.  

Remember your shame, sisters to embrace,  
Yahweh's forgiveness, his eternal grace.  

When all sins are forgiven, and shame's undone,  
And you know Yahweh, the lasting One."
