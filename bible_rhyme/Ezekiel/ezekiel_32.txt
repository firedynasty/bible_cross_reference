In the twelfth year, twelfth month, first day came the word,  
"Son of man, lament for Pharaoh, let him be heard.  
Like a young lion, king of the seas,  
Stirred rivers and waters, left them diseased.  

The Lord Yahweh’s words, 'I’ll cast out my net,  
With nations aplenty, they’ll pull you yet.  
On dry land you’ll lie, in the open field,  
Birds and beasts on your flesh will feast and be filled.  

Your body's height fills the valleys, your blood flows wide,  
Mountains drenched, rivers thick, your presence won't hide.  
I’ll extinguish your light, darken the sky,  
Sun covered in clouds, moon won’t shine by and by.  

Hearts of many will tremble in fear,  
Nations you knew not will see your downfall near.  
Kings will quake, swords in their sight,  
As Egypt’s pride falls in the dead of night.  

The sword of Babylon, mighty and strong,  
Will cut down your numbers, right all your wrongs.  
Ruthless they come, the multitude’s pride,  
No more will man's foot trouble the tide.  

Waters run clear, and rivers like oil,  
Desolate Egypt, laid waste in the soil.  
Then they will know who I am, Yahweh speaks,  
Lamentation rises, for Egypt’s weaks.  

On the fifteenth day, again Yahweh’s word came,  
“Wail for Egypt, cast them down in shame.  
Beauty unmatched, yet down you go,  
With those in the pit, where shadows grow.  

Laid with the uncircumcised, slain by the sword,  
Drawn to Sheol, with the mighty’s accord.  
Asshur and her company, graves surround,  
Slain by the sword, terror profound.  

Elam’s multitude, graves in their wake,  
Uncircumcised fallen, shame they take.  
Meshech and Tubal, all in a ring,  
Graves circled tight, none hear the king.  

Edom and her princes, laid in their might,  
Slain by the sword, lost in the night.  
Princes of the north, Sidonians too,  
Lie in shame, with the terror they drew.  

Pharaoh sees and finds comfort at last,  
With his multitude slain, his terror past.  
In the land of the living, his terror spread wide,  
Yet laid with the uncircumcised, Pharaoh's pride."
