Yahweh spoke, a message to share,  
"Son of man, hear and beware.  

Tell those prophets, who claim in vain,  
They speak from the heart, but it’s all a game.  

'Woe to these fools,' Yahweh declares,  
They’ve seen nothing, but act like they care.  

Israel's prophets, like foxes they play,  
Not building walls, just leading astray.  

Claiming visions, false words they say,  
But Yahweh ain’t sent them anyway.  

'You’ve seen false sights,' Yahweh calls it out,  
'Spoken lies, sowing doubt.'  

'So hear this now,' Yahweh’s decree,  
'Because you lie, I’m coming for thee.'  

'My hand’s against those who pretend,  
Not in my council, without a friend.  

You won’t enter Israel's land,  
Then you’ll know it’s Yahweh’s hand.'  

'You promise peace where none will dwell,  
Whitewash walls, they’ll all fell.'  

'Rain will pour, hail will fall,  
Where’s your plaster, when down goes the wall?'  

'With storm and wrath, I’ll tear it apart,  
Expose foundations, revealing the heart.'  

'The wall’s no more, nor those who built,  
False prophets, with peace, stained with guilt.'  

Turn to the women, prophesy bold,  
Speaking lies, for barley and gold.  

'Woe to those with pillows, kerchiefs made,  
Hunting souls, in deception’s trade.'  

Yahweh's against your deceitful ways,  
Ripping away your soul-catching plays.  

'Freeing those that you ensnared,  
My people will know that I’ve cared.'  

'No more lies, no more false sights,  
I’ll save my people, set things right.'  

Yahweh's words, through truth they flow,  
Now you’ll know, He rules the show."
