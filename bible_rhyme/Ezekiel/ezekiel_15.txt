Yahweh spoke to me right there,  
"Son of man, see that vine, thin as air.  

What's it more than a tree so grand?  
Can you make a tool with that in your hand?  

Toss it in the fire, watch it burn,  
Ends devoured, the middle can't return.  

When it was whole, it had no use,  
Now it's burned, what's left to produce?"  

So the Lord Yahweh makes it clear,  
Like that vine, Jerusalem's near.  

"I'll turn my face, set against their land,  
They'll escape the fire, but can't withstand.  

Then you'll know I'm Yahweh's might,  
When my face turns, dark as night.  

I'll leave the land barren, empty and sore,  
Because their trespass, it can't be ignored," Yahweh swore.
