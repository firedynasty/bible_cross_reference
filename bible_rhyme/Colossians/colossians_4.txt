Masters, treat your servants fair, give them what they're due,  
Remember you got a Master in heaven watchin' you.  

Stay strong in prayer, keep watch and give thanks,  
Pray for us too, so God opens word's ranks.  

To share Christ's mystery, though in chains I'm bound,  
Speaking clear, so the truth is profound.  

Walk wise with outsiders, make the most of your time,  
Let speech be with grace, seasoned just fine.  

Tychicus will tell you all things that I face,  
He's a beloved brother, reliable in God's grace.  

He comes with Onesimus, faithful and true,  
They'll share all that's happening, bringing comfort to you.  

Aristarchus, my cellmate, sends greetings your way,  
With Mark, Barnabas' cousin, who you should obey.  

And Jesus called Justus, they work by my side,  
For God's Kingdom, through them I've been fortified.  

Epaphras, your own, sends his prayers with zeal,  
For you to stand complete in God's perfect will.  

I vouch for his passion, for you and Laodicea's land,  
And those in Hierapolis, he's got a fervent hand.  

Luke the doctor and Demas say hi,  
Greet Laodicea's brothers and Nymphas nearby.  

When this letter is read, share with Laodicea's crew,  
And read their letter, it's for you too.  

Tell Archippus, “Stay true to the task God gave,  
Fulfill it with heart, be bold and brave.”  

This is my sign-off, Paul writes by hand,  
Remember my chains, with grace take a stand. Amen, my friend.
