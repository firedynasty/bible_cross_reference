Paul and Tim, apostles on this ride,  
Writing to the saints, with Christ at their side.  

"Grace and peace," we send from the Lord above,  
We thank God always, praying with love.  

Heard of your faith, and how it grows,  
Love for the saints, and it surely shows.  

Hope laid up for you, stored in heaven's space,  
Word of truth, the Good News you embrace.  

It's spreading worldwide, bearing fruit and more,  
Since you heard it first, to its truth you swore.  

Epaphras taught you, servant, loyal and true,  
He told us of the love the Spirit's given you.  

Since the first we heard, we never stopped to pray,  
For wisdom and knowledge to guide your way.  

Walk worthily, pleasing the Lord's sight,  
Bear fruit in good works, grow in His light.  

Strength and power from His glorious might,  
Endurance with joy, from morning till night.  

Thank the Father, for He made us part,  
He delivered us from darkness, gave us a new start.  

Kingdom of the Son, in whom we're redeemed,  
Forgiveness of sins, more than we dreamed.  

He's the image, the invisible God's own,  
Firstborn of creation, by Him all's known.  

In heaven and earth, seen and unseen,  
Thrones and powers, all things in between.  

He's before all things, holding it together,  
Head of the body, leading us forever.  

Fullness pleased to dwell, reconciled through His blood,  
Making peace on the cross, with a redemptive flood.  

Once alienated, enemies in the mind,  
Now reconciled through His death, a gift so kind.  

Continue in faith, grounded and steadfast,  
Hope of the Good News, eternal and vast.  

Paul, a servant, proclaiming this truth,  
Rejoicing in sufferings, for you, the proof.  

A mystery revealed to the saints in light,  
Christ in you, the hope, shining bright.  

We proclaim, teaching wisdom to every man,  
Presenting each perfect, as best we can.  

Laboring hard, striving with might,  
His power works in me, burning so bright.
