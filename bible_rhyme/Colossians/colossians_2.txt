Listen up, I gotta tell you, I'm fightin' for y'all,  
For Laodicea and those who haven't seen me at all.  

May your hearts find comfort, knit in love tight,  
Gain riches of truth, in God’s mystery delight.  

In Him are treasures, wisdom, knowledge concealed,  
I speak so you're not by sweet words misled or appealed.  

Though I'm not there, in spirit I'm near,  
Rejoicing in your faith, steadfast and clear.  

As you received Christ, walk strong in His way,  
Rooted, built up, let thanksgiving display.  

Don't let no one deceive you with worldly tricks,  
Philosophies vain, pulling from Christ's mix.  

In Him, the fullness of Deity dwells,  
In Him, you are complete, over powers He excels.  

Circumcised not by hands, flesh's sins cast aside,  
In baptism buried, in faith with Him you rise.  

You were once dead in your sins, now alive with His grace,  
Forgiven, He nailed your past to its place.  

Stripped powers, principalities made a show,  
Triumph openly, His victory we know.  

Let no judgment come from food, drink, or days,  
These are shadows, Christ’s body displays.  

Don’t lose your prize to false humility and deceit,  
Puffed-up minds, not holding the Head's seat.  

From Him, the body grows, knit and supplied,  
If with Christ you died, why in the world abide?  

"Don't handle, nor taste, nor touch" they say,  
Yet such commands perish, just doctrines of men’s way.  

They seem wise, with humility and strictness they dress,  
But against the flesh, they hold no real success.
