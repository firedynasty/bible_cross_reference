Raised with Christ, seek the heights, where He's chillin' at God's right,  
Mind on the heavenly, leave earthly things out of sight.  

You’ve died, now your life’s with Christ, tucked away,  
When Christ shines, you'll shine too, in glory's display.  

Kill off those desires, earthly and wild,  
Like greed and lust, they're idolatry, beguiled.  

Once you walked this path, in those shoes you stood,  
But now anger and slander, gotta be put away for good.  

Don't lie, truth’s your new attire,  
The old man’s gone, now reach higher.  

Renewed in the image, created anew,  
No Greek or Jew, just Christ in you.  

Chosen and holy, put compassion on your sleeve,  
With kindness and humility, believe and achieve.  

Forgive as Christ forgave, let love be the tie,  
Peace of God in your hearts, lift your praise to the sky.  

Rich words of Christ dwell within,  
Teach and sing with joy, let the grace begin.  

In word or deed, aim your sights,  
Do all in Jesus' name, giving thanks, day and night.  

Wives, respect, as is fitting and right,  
Husbands, love without bitterness, keep love in sight.  

Children, obey, bring pleasure to the Lord,  
Fathers, don’t provoke, let encouragement be your chord.  

Servants, obey, not just when seen,  
Work for the Lord, let your heart be keen.  

Work for Christ, not for man's acclaim,  
The reward's from the Lord, not merely in name.  

Wrong won't escape, justice will reign,  
No favoritism here, fair is the chain.
