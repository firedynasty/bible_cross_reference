Athaliah saw her son's fate, gone, left her alone,  
She rose up fierce, to claim the throne.

Jehosheba stepped in with a clever plan,  
Hid young Joash, saved the future of the clan.

Six years they kept him safe in God’s sacred space,  
While Athaliah ruled, a dark grim case.

Seventh year arrived, Jehoiada made his move,  
Called the captains, laid down the groove.

"This is the plan," he said, "you gotta be bold,  
Keep the king guarded, let no one uncontrolled."

A third at the king’s house, a third at the gate,  
Watch over the palace, seal his fate.

"Surround the king, weapons ready in hand,  
Anyone who intrudes, won’t leave this land."

Captains obeyed, they followed the priest’s command,  
Spears and shields from David’s hand.

The guards took their place, every man in line,  
Ready to protect the royal sign.

Out came the prince, crowned and anointed,  
"Long live the king!" the people rejoiced, appointed.

Athaliah heard the noise, rushed to the scene,  
Saw the king by the pillar, trumpets serene.

"Treason!" she cried, her clothes she tore,  
Jehoiada said, "Take her out, we want no more."

"Don’t kill her here," the priest commanded the crew,  
They led her out, her reign was through.

A covenant made, the people and king,  
To serve Yahweh, this was the thing.

Baal’s house was broken, altars all down,  
Mattan the priest slain, no longer to frown.

They brought the king down, to the throne’s rightful place,  
The city rejoiced, a new dawn embraced.

Peace in the land, Athaliah’s rule undone,  
Jehoash on the throne, at just seven begun.
