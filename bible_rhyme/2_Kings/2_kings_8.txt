Elisha told the woman, "Get up, leave your home,  
Take your family, find a place where you can roam.  
For Yahweh decreed a famine, seven years it'll stay,  
Pack your things, head out without delay."

She listened, she moved, to the Philistines she went,  
Seven years passed, then back home she was sent.  
When she returned, her land she sought to claim,  
To the king she went, spoke her story, named her aim.

The king was chatting with Gehazi, the prophet's aide,  
Said, "Tell me ‘bout Elisha, all the miracles he made."  
Gehazi spoke of life restored, then lo and behold,  
The woman appeared, her story untold.

Gehazi exclaimed, "This is her, the very one,  
Whose son Elisha raised, the deed he’s done."  
The king heard, and justice he decreed,  
"Restore her land, all its fruits, fulfill her need."

Meanwhile, in Damascus, Benhadad lay weak,  
He heard Elisha came, and his guidance did seek.  
"Take gifts," said the king to his servant Hazael,  
"Ask the man of God if I will be well."

Hazael met Elisha with gifts galore,  
Forty camels burdened with treasures to implore.  
Elisha said, "Tell him he’ll recover and mend,  
But Yahweh shows me his life will soon end."

The prophet wept, his gaze locked in pain,  
Hazael asked, "Why these tears that fall like rain?"  
Elisha replied, "I see the horrors you'll bring,  
Fire, sword, and sorrow to Israel's offspring."

Hazael protested, "How can this be my role?"  
Elisha spoke true, "You'll be king, that's your goal."  
Hazael returned, said the king would survive,  
Yet the next day, his master wasn’t alive.

A cloth soaked in water ended the king's reign,  
Hazael took the throne, his power unchained.  
In Judah, Jehoram rose as king to reign,  
He followed Ahab’s ways, bringing shame.

Yet Yahweh spared Judah for David’s sake,  
A promise of a light that evil couldn't break.  
Edom revolted, Judah's grasp it slipped,  
Joram struck by night, but the bonds were ripped.

Joram's deeds were written, but he found his rest,  
In David's city, with his fathers, he was blessed.  
Ahaziah, his son, took the throne, young and bold,  
Following Ahab’s path, his deeds were cold.

He joined with Joram, against Syria, to fight,  
At Ramoth Gilead, the Syrians struck with might.  
Joram wounded, Ahaziah by his side,  
Seeking healing in Jezreel, their fates collide.
