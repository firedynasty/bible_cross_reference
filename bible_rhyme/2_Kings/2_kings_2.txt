Elijah knew his time was near, a whirlwind ride to heaven's sphere,  
With Elisha at his side, from Gilgal they took the stride.  

"Stay here," Elijah said, "for Yahweh calls me ahead."  
Elisha vowed, "I won't leave, as Yahweh lives, that's my creed."  

Prophets in Bethel warned Elisha, "Today your master leaves ya."  
He nodded, "I know, keep it low," as they continued the flow.  

Again Elijah spoke, "To Jericho, Yahweh beckons me to go."  
Elisha replied, "I'm with you, through and through, you know it's true."  

Jericho's prophets said, "Elijah's leaving, your leader's head."  
"Yes, I know," Elisha replied, "but let's keep this on the quiet side."  

"To the Jordan I must go," Elijah said, but Elisha wouldn't let go.  
Together they walked, prophets watched from afar, their faith locked.  

Elijah's cloak smacked the river wide, and dry ground appeared side by side.  
On the other bank, Elijah turned, "Ask me now, before I'm spurned."  

Elisha asked for a double share, of the spirit that Elijah's care.  
"It's a hard request," Elijah confessed, "but if you see me, you'll be blessed."  

As they walked, fire from the sky, chariots and horses, Elijah flew high.  
"My father!" Elisha cried, torn robes in despair, as Elijah was in the air.  

He picked the mantle, a legacy's sign, struck the waters, "Where's Yahweh divine?"  
The river split again, Elisha crossed, prophets saw the spirit wasn't lost.  

"The spirit of Elijah's on Elisha," they bowed down, respect they show.  
"Send men to find him," they said, but Elisha declined, "He's gone ahead."  

Pressed by their plea, he relented, "Go see," but three days passed without decree.  
"I told you not to go," Elisha said, as they returned with weary tread.  

The city's water was bad, the land, a desolate span.  
Elisha called for a jar, with salt, to heal it near and far.  

"Yahweh says, 'These waters are healed,'" Elisha declared, and the curse was repealed.  
To Bethel he went, mocked by youth, "Go up, baldy!" was their uncouth.  

He cursed them in Yahweh's name, and bears came, their wrath untamed.  
Elisha continued, from Carmel to Samaria, led by Yahweh's aria.
