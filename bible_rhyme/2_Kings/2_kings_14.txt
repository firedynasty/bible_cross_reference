Amaziah steps up to take the reign,  
Twenty-five young, in Judah's domain.  

He did right in Yahweh's sight,  
But not like David, his light wasn't as bright.  

High places remained, incense burned high,  
Kingdom secure, but he wouldn't let that lie.  

Servants who killed his father faced his wrath,  
But spared the children, followed Moses' path.  

Ten thousand Edomites in the Valley of Salt,  
He took Sela, but his pride would halt.  

Sent word to Jehoash, "Let's face each other, man,"  
Jehoash replied with a wise man's plan.  

"Thistle to cedar, don’t be hasty, stay wise,  
Enjoy your glory, don’t risk your demise."  

Amaziah wouldn't heed, the battle was set,  
Beth Shemesh met, but Judah's defeat was met.  

Jehoash broke walls, took gold and more,  
Yahweh’s house emptied, he evened the score.  

Jehoash laid down, his battles complete,  
Jeroboam rose up, taking the king's seat.  

Amaziah lived on, years passed by,  
But in Jerusalem, they plotted, he couldn’t fly.  

Fled to Lachish, but found no reprieve,  
Caught and killed, his city left to grieve.  

Azariah, young at sixteen, took the throne,  
Built up Elath, made Judah his own.  

Jeroboam in Israel reigned forty-one,  
Evil deeds carried, from father to son.  

Borders restored from Hamath's gate wide,  
Yahweh's word by Jonah's side.  

Israel in pain, bitter and alone,  
Yahweh saved them, His mercy was shown.  

Jeroboam's might in records laid,  
Passed on, Zechariah the next they made.
