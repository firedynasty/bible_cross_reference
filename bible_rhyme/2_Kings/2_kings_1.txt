Moab's rebellion hit the scene, after Ahab's death, they weren't keen.  
Ahaziah stumbled, through the lattice he fell, sick in Samaria, feeling unwell.  
He sent his messengers on a quest, to inquire of Baal Zebub, was his health blessed?  
But Yahweh's angel whispered to the Tishbite,  
"Go meet those messengers, set the story right."  

"Why seek Ekron's god when Israel's is near?"  
"Yahweh declares, 'You won't leave your bed, death is here.'"  
Elijah told the messengers, then left them behind,  
Back to the king, with words that were divine.  

The king asked, "Why back so fast?"  
They spoke of a man with a message to cast,  
"Yahweh's words were firm and real,  
There's no escape from the fate you feel."  

"What kind of man?" was the king's demand.  
"A hairy man, leather belt in hand."  
"That's Elijah," the king huffed with a frown,  
Sent his captain with fifty, to bring Elijah down.  

"Man of God, the king calls you, come on down!"  
Elijah replied, "Let fire rain, consume this town!"  
And fire roared down, burned them to ash,  
The king sent more, but they met the same crash.  

A third captain came, his knees hit the ground,  
"Man of God, spare us, let life abound!"  
Fire had toasted those who came before,  
"Please spare us, let us live once more."  

Angel of Yahweh said, "Elijah, go,  
Don't be afraid, with this man flow."  
Elijah went down, to the king he did speak,  
"You sought Baal Zebub, your fate is bleak."  

"You'll not rise, but surely die,"  
And as Yahweh spoke, Ahaziah's time ran dry.  
Jehoram took the throne, his reign would begin,  
Ahaziah's tale ended, written in chronicles within.
