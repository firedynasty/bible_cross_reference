Nebuchadnezzar came, Babylon's might,  
Jehoiakim served three years, then took flight in the night.  

Yahweh sent bands—Chaldeans, Syrians too,  
Moabites, Ammonites, came for Judah's undo.  

It was Yahweh's command, Judah's time was done,  
Manasseh's sins, innocent blood—Judah's spirit overrun.  

Jehoiakim passed, laid with his kin,  
Jehoiachin rose, a new reign to begin.  

Egypt's king stayed put, Babylon owned the land,  
From Egypt's brook to Euphrates, all under his hand.  

Jehoiachin at eighteen took the throne,  
Three months he reigned, but evil was sown.  

Nebuchadnezzar's servants came, city under siege,  
The king himself arrived, fulfilling the league.  

Jehoiachin went out, mother, servants, and all,  
Captured by Babylon, Judah's king did fall.  

Treasures taken, Yahweh's house stripped bare,  
Solomon's gold vessels, gone without a care.  

Jerusalem's elite, ten thousand captives bound,  
Craftsmen and smiths, none left to surround.  

To Babylon they went, from Jerusalem's heart,  
Jehoiachin, his kin, from the land did depart.  

Nebuchadnezzar set Mattaniah on the throne,  
Zedekiah his name, a king yet unknown.  

Zedekiah at twenty-one, reigned eleven years long,  
But Yahweh's sight, evil remained strong.  

Judah's fate, Yahweh's anger cast them out,  
Zedekiah's rebellion, Babylon's clout.
