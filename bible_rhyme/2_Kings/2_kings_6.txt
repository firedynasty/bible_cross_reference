The prophets to Elisha, "Our place is too tight,  
Let's head to the Jordan, build something right."  
"Go," he replied, "and take me along,"  
To the Jordan they went, their axes swung strong.  

As one chopped a tree, the ax head took flight,  
Into the water, out of sight.  
"Master, it's borrowed!" the man did wail,  
Elisha asked, "Where?" and then did prevail.  

He cut a stick, threw it in, made iron float,  
"Take it," he said, and the man did tote.  
Syria's king plotted war's cunning plan,  
"Here we will camp," he told his clan.  

Elisha warned Israel, "Avoid this place,"  
The king of Israel moved with haste.  
The Syrian king was troubled, "Who’s the spy?"  
"No, Elisha hears what you speak and reply."  

"Find him," the king commanded, "in Dothan he stays,"  
With horses and chariots, they came by night’s haze.  
Elisha's servant saw the army, "What shall we do?"  
"Fear not," said Elisha, "we’ve more in view."  

"Yahweh, open his eyes," Elisha did pray,  
He saw hills with fiery chariots array.  
Elisha prayed again, "Blind them, oh Lord,"  
Struck with blindness, they knew not their ford.  

"This is not the city," Elisha led them away,  
To Samaria's heart, then prayed, "Lord, let them see."  
The king of Israel asked, "Shall I strike them down?"  
"No," Elisha said, "let mercy wear the crown."  

They feasted and drank, then sent them in peace,  
Syria’s raids on Israel ceased.  
Yet Benhadad besieged Samaria's gate,  
With famine so harsh, no food on the plate.  

A woman cried out, "Help, oh king, relieve!"  
He replied, "If God won't help, what can I achieve?"  
She spoke of a pact, two sons to consume,  
Her tale of despair made the king’s heart fume.  

He tore his clothes, sackcloth revealed,  
Vowed Elisha's fate would be sealed.  
With elders near, Elisha sat calm,  
A messenger sent with threats so strong.  

"This evil is from Yahweh," the king’s cry,  
"Why should I wait?" with a heavy sigh.  
Elisha stood firm, faith in God's plan,  
Amidst chaos and threats, a resolute man.
