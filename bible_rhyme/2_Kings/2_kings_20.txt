Hezekiah was sick, lying in bed,  
Isaiah came with words that filled with dread.  

"Yahweh says, 'Get your house in line,  
Your time is up, end of the line.'"  

Hezekiah turned, face to the wall,   
Prayed to Yahweh, in tears he'd fall.  

"Remember how I've walked with you,  
In truth and heart, I've been true."  

Bitter tears flowed as he spoke,  
But Yahweh's word to Isaiah broke.  

"Tell Hezekiah, I've heard his plea,  
I've seen his tears, now listen to Me.  

I'll heal, on the third day rise,  
Fifteen more years, a gift to your eyes.  

Assyria's threat, I'll push away,  
For My sake and David's, this city's okay."  

Isaiah then said, "Bring the figs here,"  
On the boil they laid, recovery near.  

Hezekiah asked, "What sign will be,  
That Yahweh will heal and I'll be free?"  

Isaiah responded, "Shadow's the sign,  
Forward ten steps, or back in time?"  

Hezekiah chose, "Backward it should sway,  
Let the shadow move the opposite way."  

Isaiah called out, Yahweh did align,  
Shadow stepped back, a miracle in time.  

Babylon’s king sent gifts and cheer,  
Hezekiah healed, they came to hear.  

Hezekiah showed all his prized delight,  
Nothing in his palace stayed out of sight.  

Isaiah asked, "What did they see?"  
Hezekiah said, "All, nothing hid from me."  

Isaiah warned of days coming near,  
All taken to Babylon, nothing left here.  

"Your sons will serve in Babylon's hall,"  
Hezekiah accepted, seeing peace for all.  

Acts of Hezekiah, written in the book,  
He slept with his fathers, Manasseh took.
