Elisha called a young prophet near, he said, "Buckle up, it's time to hear.  
Take this oil in your hand, get ready to ride, to Ramoth Gilead where Jehu must abide.  

Find him quick, pull him aside, to a secret room, where secrets confide.  
Pour the oil on his head with this decree: 'Yahweh anoints you, king you shall be.'  

Then open the door, and flee real fast, don't wait around, let the moment pass."  
The young man took off, to Ramoth he went, to find Jehu, on a mission he's sent.  

The captains sat, Jehu among, "I've a message for you," the young man sung.  
Jehu rose up, into the house he walked, the oil poured out, as the prophet talked.  

"Yahweh says, 'You're the king today, over Israel, hear what I say.  
Strike Ahab's house for all they've done, Jezebel's blood will be avenged, one by one.  

Ahab's line will all fall down, like Jeroboam's, they'll lose their crown.  
Jezebel's fate, by dogs she'll be torn, in Jezreel's land, forlorn and scorned.'"  

The prophet took off, the message laid, Jehu stood up, no time to fade.  
"Is all well?" the others asked, "What did that madman say?" Jehu unmasked.  

"He said, 'Yahweh anoints me king,'" the truth spilled madly, they shouted, the trumpets rang loudly.  
"Jehu is king!" the captains proclaimed, destiny's path was forever changed.  

Jehu conspired, no time to delay, Joram in Jezreel, healing away.  
"If you’re with me, let no one escape, to tell the tale, to change our fate."  

He rode in his chariot, to Jezreel he sped, Joram waited, unaware of dread.  
Watchman spotted Jehu's fierce ride, "A company comes!" Joram replied.  

"Send a horseman, ask, 'Is it peace?'" Jehu replied, "Fall in line, cease."  
No peace, no turning, they joined his force, the watchman reported the furious course.  

Joram and Ahaziah rode out to meet, Jehu stood waiting, on Naboth’s street.  
"Is it peace, Jehu?" Joram inquired, "No peace," Jehu's voice flared, inspired.  

Joram fled, "Treason!" he cried, Jehu's arrow, through his heart it did glide.  
"Bidkar, toss him on Naboth's land, Yahweh's words fulfilled, as planned."  

Ahaziah fled, but Jehu pursued, struck him down, his reign subdued.  
Back to Jerusalem, to rest in peace, buried with fathers, his life released.  

Jezebel heard, painted her face, looked out the window, her final place.  
"Come in peace, Zimri?" she taunted sly, Jehu looked up, his answer nigh.  

"Who stands with me?" he boldly said, eunuchs answered, no words misread.  
"Throw her down!" and down she fell, horses trampled, her story they’d tell.  

Jehu ate and drank, then ordered to bury, but only bones remained, a truth so scary.  
"This is Yahweh's word," Jehu declared, Jezebel's end, Elijah had shared.  

Her body's like dung, on Jezreel’s earth lain, no one to say, "This is Jezebel," again.
