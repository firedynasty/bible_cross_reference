In the ninth year, tenth month, tenth day,  
Nebuchadnezzar and his army made their way,  
Jerusalem surrounded, forts all around,  
Until the eleventh year, the city was bound.  

By the ninth day of month four, famine hit hard,  
No bread left, the situation was scarred.  
A breach in the city, warriors slipped through,  
By night, through the garden, the king went too.  

But on Jericho's plains, the Chaldeans closed in,  
The king captured, his army lost in the wind.  
To Riblah, Zedekiah was brought,  
His sons slain, his sight lost, Babylon caught.  

Fifth month, seventh day, Nebuzaradan came,  
Jerusalem burned, in fire and flame.  
Houses great and small, Yahweh's house too,  
Walls broken down by the Chaldean crew.  

The people taken captive, the rest left behind,  
To work the fields, the poorest resigned.  
Bronze pillars shattered, vessels taken away,  
Gold and silver, carried without delay.  

Seraiah, Zephaniah, chiefs in the fray,  
Taken to Riblah, where judgment held sway.  
Sixty men of the city, all brought near,  
In Hamath they died, Judah shed tears.  

Gedaliah made governor, but fear stayed strong,  
Ishmael struck him down, it didn’t take long.  
To Egypt fled the people, great and small,  
Afraid of the Chaldeans, fearing the fall.  

In the thirty-seventh year, Jehoiachin’s fate,  
Evilmerodach lifted him from prison’s gate.  
Spoke kindly, gave him a throne high,  
Changed his garments, bread supply.  

Daily allowance, for the rest of his days,  
A king redeemed, in Babylon he stays.  
Judah's tale, of exile and pain,  
Of kings fallen, and the Babylonian reign.
