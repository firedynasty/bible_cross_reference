Josiah was just eight, when he took his seat,  
Reigned in Jerusalem, made his rule complete.  
His mother Jedidah, her name we relate,  
From Bozkath she came, Adaiah's fate.

Did right in Yahweh's sight, walked David's line,  
Left or right, he didn't stray, stayed in the right design.  
Eighteenth year, he sent Shaphan, wise and fast,  
"Go to Yahweh's house, set the task."

"Count up the money the keepers took,  
Deliver to workmen, to repair the book.  
Give to the carpenters, builders, and stone,  
Faithful they work, let no fault be shown."

Hilkiah the priest to Shaphan did say,  
"Found the law's book, in Yahweh's way."  
Shaphan to the king brought the word,  
About money handled, and the book he heard.

When the king heard, he tore his robe,  
Deep was his sorrow, his heart probe.  
He sent for Hilkiah, Ahikam, and more,  
"Seek Yahweh's word, what’s in store?"

Great is the wrath, it's kindled high,  
For our fathers' deafness to this cry.  
To Huldah the prophetess, they went to hear,  
In the second quarter, her home was near.

"Yahweh says, 'Tell the man who sent,  
Evil will come, anger won’t relent.  
They’ve forsaken me, incense to others burned,  
Wrath will not quench, it’s what they've earned.'"

"But to Judah's king, who sent for divine,  
Yahweh says, 'For your humble sign,  
Your heart was tender, tears and torn clothes,  
I’ve heard your cries, Yahweh knows.'"

"In peace you'll rest, no evil to see,  
Gathered to your fathers, in calm you'll be."  
Message delivered, the king heard it plain,  
Peace was promised, amidst the pain.
