In the twenty-third year, Joash on Judah's throne,  
Jehoahaz began his reign, not alone.  
Seventeen years in Samaria, he stayed,  
But Jeroboam's sins, he never strayed.  

Evil in Yahweh's sight, he did all wrong,  
God's anger burned hot, it didn't take long.  
Into Hazael's hand, they fell deep,  
Syrians oppressed them, made them weep.  

Jehoahaz prayed, begged for a plan,  
Yahweh listened, heard the cry of man.  
He sent a savior, Israel was free,  
Back to their tents, like it used to be.  

Still, they clung to Jeroboam's sin,  
Asherah poles, they lingered within.  
Fifty horsemen left, chariots few,  
Like dust in threshing, the Syrians slew.  

Written are the deeds of Jehoahaz's might,  
In chronicles of kings, they’re out of sight.  
He slept with fathers, buried in Samaria ground,  
Joash took the throne, a new king was crowned.  

In Judah’s thirty-seventh year,  
Joash's son, Jehoash, appeared near.  
Sixteen years, walked in Jeroboam's sin,  
The evil ways, he was caught within.  

Acts of Joash, battles and fights,  
Chronicles hold those, under history's lights.  
He slept with kings, buried in Samaria's land,  
Jeroboam took the throne, in Israel, he would stand.  

Elisha fell sick, Joash by his side,  
Weeping for the chariots’ ride.  
Elisha spoke, "Take bow and arrows in hand,"  
King obeyed, followed the command.  

"Open the window, shoot east," was the cue,  
"Yahweh's victory will come through.  
Strike Syria, victory will shine,  
Consume them at Aphek, all in due time."  

"Strike the ground," Elisha said plain,  
King struck thrice, but stopped again.  
"You should've struck more, five or six at least,  
Now only three times will Syria's power cease."  

Elisha passed, buried and gone,  
Yet his power lived on and on.  
A dead man thrown, touched his bones,  
Revived, stood up, life once more his own.  

Hazael oppressed, but Yahweh was kind,  
Covenant with forefathers kept in mind.  
Benhadad reigned when Hazael died,  
Jehoash struck cities, three times he tried.  

Regained the cities, Israel's pride restored,  
A tale of kings and battles, in the Lord.
