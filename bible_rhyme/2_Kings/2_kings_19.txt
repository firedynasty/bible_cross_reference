Hezekiah heard the news, tore his robe, and cried,  
In sackcloth he went where Yahweh resides.  

Sent Eliakim, Shebna, elders to Isaiah's dome,  
Covered in sackcloth, seeking a word from home.  

"Hezekiah says, 'Trouble’s here, no strength to give birth,  
Maybe Yahweh heard the blasphemer's mirth.  

Lift up your prayers for the remnant still here.'"  
So they went to Isaiah, with hearts full of fear.  

Isaiah replied, "Tell your king this from Yahweh,  
'Don't fear the Assyrian words that sway.  

I'll put a spirit in him, news he'll hear,  
Back to his land, by the sword, he'll disappear.'"

Rabshakeh found the king at Libnah, in fight,  
He heard Ethiopia came with might.  

Sent word to Hezekiah, "Don't be deceived,  
Jerusalem’s fall is what we’ve achieved.  

The gods of the nations fell one by one,  
Will yours be different when we're done?"  

Hezekiah read the letter, laid it before the Lord,  
Prayed for deliverance from the Assyrian horde.  

"Yahweh, you're the God enthroned above,  
Creator of heaven and earth, show your love.  

Hear Sennacherib's words, defying your name,  
Save us, so all know you're the same."  

Yahweh spoke through Isaiah, a message so clear,  
"Sennacherib’s pride, I’ve heard, and his sneer.  

Zion despises, shakes her head at you,  
You've defied the Holy One in all you do.  

I’ll turn you back, put my hook in your nose,  
The way that you came, that’s how it goes."  

A sign for Hezekiah, that year’s yield will grow,  
And Judah’s remnant will again flow.  

Assyria won't breach, not a single arrow fly,  
"I’ll defend this city," Yahweh's reply.  

That night, Yahweh's angel struck with might,  
185,000 fell in a single night.  

Sennacherib retreated, back to Nineveh he fled,  
In his own temple, by his sons, he bled.  

Esarhaddon reigned, as the story shifts,  
In Yahweh’s power, Jerusalem lifts.
