In Jeroboam's twenty-seventh year reign,  
Azariah of Judah took the king's domain.  
Just sixteen when he took the throne,  
Reigned fifty-two years, never alone.  

In Yahweh's sight, he did what was right,  
But high places stayed, burning incense at night.  
Struck by Yahweh, a leper, he lived apart,  
Jotham his son took over the king's part.  

Acts of Azariah, the chronicles tell,  
He slept with his fathers, his story to dwell.  
Zechariah in Israel, just six months to rule,  
Evil in Yahweh's sight, played the same old fool.  

Shallum struck Zechariah, took the throne quick,  
Chronicles hold the story, if you want the pick.  
Yahweh's word to Jehu, "Fourth generation's reign,"  
Shallum ruled a month, and Menahem brought pain.  

Menahem struck Shallum, new king's claim,  
Acts of Shallum, in chronicles, stay the same.  
He attacked Tiphsah, left no stone unturned,  
Reigned ten years, and evil he learned.  

Pul from Assyria came with might,  
Menahem's silver made his kingdom tight.  
He taxed the wealthy, paid the Assyrian king,  
And Pul turned away, didn't do a thing.  

Menahem's deeds, in chronicles they lie,  
He slept with his fathers, his time went by.  
Pekahiah, his son, took the reigns for a time,  
Evil like Jeroboam, walked the same line.  

Pekah conspired, with fifty men strong,  
Killed Pekahiah, reign didn't last long.  
Chronicles hold Pekahiah's tale,  
Pekah took over, his rule set sail.  

Twenty years for Pekah, in Israel's land,  
Evil in Yahweh's eyes, sin at hand.  
Tiglath Pileser took cities away,  
Carried captives to Assyria, in a dark day.  

Hoshea struck Pekah, took the throne's space,  
Jotham began his reign in Judah's place.  
He did what's right, like his father before,  
High places stayed, incense burned more.  

Acts of Jotham, the chronicles display,  
Rezin and Pekah came Judah's way.  
Jotham slept with his fathers, his rule did cease,  
And Ahaz his son took the throne in peace.
