The king sent word, all elders came near,  
Judah and Jerusalem gathered to hear.  

In Yahweh's house, the king took his stand,  
With priests and prophets and people at hand.  

He read aloud the covenant's code,  
The words from Yahweh's house, in their abode.  

By the pillar, the king took a vow,  
"To walk with Yahweh in heart and soul now."  

The people agreed, the covenant sealed,  
A promise to keep what was revealed.  

"Bring out the vessels made for Baal,"  
The king commanded, removing them all.  

Burned them outside in Kidron's field,  
To Bethel the ashes, forever sealed.  

Idolatrous priests, he cast aside,  
Judah's high places, now denied.  

He crushed the altars, beat them to dust,  
A cleansing act, deemed fair and just.  

The Asherah poles, he broke them too,  
On common graves, the dust he threw.  

From Geba to Beersheba's stretch,  
He tore down high places, those of wretch.  

Topheth defiled, no child would face,  
The fire of Molech, erased with haste.  

The sun’s horses he took away,  
Chariots of fire burned that day.  

Altars of Ahaz, broken and gone,  
Manasseh's works to dust were drawn.  

Solomon's places, where idols were praised,  
Smashed and scattered, no trace erased.  

At Bethel's high place, his anger burned,  
Tombs were opened, bones overturned.  

"What’s this monument I see?" he inquired,  
"Prophet's grave," the people replied, inspired.  

"Let him rest," Josiah decreed,  
Bones untouched, his legacy freed.  

Samaria's places, he did dethrone,  
Priests and altars, all overthrown.  

"Keep the Passover," the king declared,  
A celebration, none compared.  

No spirit or wizard, none would remain,  
Josiah purged them, cleansing the land's stain.  

A king like no other, heart full and true,  
In God’s eyes, his deeds shone through.  

Yet Yahweh’s wrath wouldn't relent,  
For Judah’s sins, his anger still sent.  

"I’ll cast off Judah, my chosen site,  
Just as Israel, removed from my sight."  

Josiah's acts, written in the scroll,  
A life recorded, in chronicles whole.  

Pharaoh Necoh rose, Josiah opposed,  
At Megiddo's field, his life was closed.  

Carried by servants, in a chariot of death,  
To Jerusalem, he took his last breath.  

Jehoahaz reigned, but only three months’ span,  
Did evil in Yahweh’s sight, like his clan.  

Bound by Necoh, a tribute laid bare,  
Silver and gold, a burden to bear.  

Eliakim, renamed Jehoiakim's reign,  
In Judah he ruled, under Egypt's chain.  

Like those before, evil he did,  
According to the deeds his fathers hid.
