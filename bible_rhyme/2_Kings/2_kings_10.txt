Seventy sons in Samaria's town,  
Jehu sent letters, trying to take the crown.  

"Pick the best of Ahab's son, let him stand,  
Fight for the throne, take command of the land."  

Fear shook the rulers, they were scared to death,  
"Two kings fell, how can we stand against his breath?"  

"We're your servants, Jehu, do what you see right,  
We won't make a king, we won't put up a fight."  

Jehu wrote again, "If you're on my side,  
Bring me the heads, and let my word be your guide."  

They took the sons, seventy slain,  
Heads in baskets, sent with disdain.  

"Lay them by the gate," Jehu said with a call,  
In the morning, he faced the people, standing tall.  

"Righteous you are, I conspired against one,  
But who took these lives, under the sun?"  

"Yahweh's word stands, not a thing will fall,  
Ahab's house crumbles, just like the call."  

Jehu struck all who remained in the land,  
Left none from Ahab's house to make a stand.  

On his journey, he met brothers on the way,  
"Who are you?" "Ahaziah's kin," they did say.  

"Take them alive!" Forty-two met their fate,  
At the pit, Jehu sealed their bitter state.  

Jehonadab met him, heart to heart they spoke,  
"Come with me, see my zeal, it's no joke."  

In Samaria, he struck with fierce hand,  
Fulfilled Yahweh's word, as was planned.  

"Ahab served Baal just a bit," Jehu declared with might,  
"But Jehu's service will be a terrible sight."  

"Gather Baal's prophets, none should evade,  
A sacrifice is coming, a deadly charade."  

He played them with craft, a plan in his mind,  
"Sanctify a gathering, Baal's worshipers, confined."  

The house was packed, from end to end it was tight,  
Jehu's trick was set, ready for the night.  

"Robes for all!" he ordered with flair,  
To mark Baal's followers, unaware.  

Inside they went, with offerings to burn,  
Jehu's men waited, ready for their turn.  

"Go in, kill them, let none escape,"  
Swords flashed, sealing Baal's fate.  

Pillars torn down, Baal's house laid waste,  
Jehu's zeal for Yahweh, no time to waste.  

Yet sins of Jeroboam, Jehu couldn’t leave,  
Golden calves in Bethel, still there to deceive.  

"Well done, Jehu," Yahweh's voice rang clear,  
"Your sons on Israel’s throne, four generations to steer."  

But Jehu strayed, didn't walk Yahweh's way,  
His heart led Israel into sinful sway.  

Yahweh began to trim Israel's domain,  
Hazael struck, bringing border pain.  

Jehu's deeds, his might, were written down,  
Twenty-eight years he ruled in Samaria's crown.  

Laid to rest with his fathers, Jehoahaz took the reins,  
Jehu's legacy of blood, still in the people's veins.
