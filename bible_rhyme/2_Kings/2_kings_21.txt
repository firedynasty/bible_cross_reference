Manasseh was twelve when he took the throne,  
Fifty-five years he reigned, but not alone.  
He did evil in Yahweh’s sight,  
Rebuilt high places, brought darkness, no light.  

He raised Baal altars, Asherah the same,  
Worshiped the skies, all in Yahweh’s name.  
In Yahweh's house, altars he made,  
“Jerusalem's mine,” Yahweh had once said.  

Through fire his son passed, sorcery he sought,  
Spoke with spirits, wizards he brought.  
Engraved image of Asherah he placed,  
In the house Yahweh chose, His name erased.  

Yahweh warned, but they wouldn't heed,  
Manasseh's influence, evil's seed.  
Prophets spoke, Yahweh's voice clear,  
“For his abominations, judgment is near.  

Evil so great, ears will ring,  
Jerusalem wiped clean, no king.  
Remnant cast off, enemies will seize,  
For provoking My wrath, won't find peace.”  

Blood flowed innocent, streets ran red,  
Manasseh's sins piled high, dread.  
When he passed, his deeds were known,  
A book of kings, where they're shown.  

Amon, his son, two years he'd reign,  
Like his father, evil again.  
Idols he served, Yahweh he spurned,  
In his own house, betrayed and burned.  

People rose up, avenged their king,  
Josiah crowned, a new hope to bring.  
In the garden, Amon lay,  
Josiah reigned, a brighter day.
