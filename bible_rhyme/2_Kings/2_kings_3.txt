Jehoram took the throne, son of Ahab's might,  
Eighteenth year of Jehoshaphat, he reigned in sight.  
Twelve years he ruled, doing evil, it's true,  
But he tore down Baal's pillar, a different view.  

Still clung to Jeroboam’s sin, wouldn’t depart,  
The legacy of Israel's wayward heart.  
Mesha the Moabite had sheep to spare,  
A hundred thousand lambs, a king's share.  

But when Ahab died, Mesha turned,  
Against Israel's rule, his heart burned.  
Jehoram mustered all Israel, no time to slack,  
Called to Jehoshaphat, planning their track.  

"The king of Moab's rebelled, join me in fight?"  
Jehoshaphat said, "I am with you, all right."  
"Which way shall we march?" Jehoram did say,  
"The wilderness of Edom," was the chosen way.  

Three kings marching, seven days, yet dry,  
No water for the armies, the livestock nearby.  
The king of Israel cried, "Yahweh’s plan is grim,  
To deliver us three to Moab's hand within."  

Jehoshaphat asked, "A prophet, can he be found?"  
"Elisha, who served Elijah, he's around."  
The kings went to Elisha, their hopes pinned,  
He said, "Go to your folks’ prophets, I’m disinclined."  

But for Jehoshaphat, Elisha would heed,  
"Bring a musician," for Yahweh’s word to seed.  
"Yahweh says, 'Dig trenches, make them deep,  
No wind or rain, but water you’ll reap.'"

"This is easy for Yahweh, Moab's yours to claim,  
Strike every city, and shatter their flame."  
As morning came, water flowed in sight,  
A miracle in the valley, clear and bright.  

Moab saw the water, red as blood,  
"Kings are dead," they thought, "we’ll plunder the flood!"  
But Israel rose and struck them down,  
Moab fled, Israel took the crown.  

Every city fell, each land filled with stones,  
Springs were stopped, trees cut to bones.  
King of Moab, desperate, sacrifice his kin,  
Great wrath rose, Israel went home, within.
