Israel stumbled, broke the rule, took what's devoted, not so cool,  
Achan grabbed, Yahweh's wrath, burned hot, that's the aftermath.  

Joshua sent spies to Ai's gate, "Check it out, don't hesitate."  
They said, "Just a few thousand will do, no need for everyone to go too."  

But three thousand men went, tried to fight,  
They fled, Ai chased, hearts melted, lost the light.  

Joshua tore his clothes, fell to the ground,  
Dust on heads, elders all around.  

He cried, "Lord, why bring us here just to lose,  
Amorites will hear, how will we choose?  

Canaanites will mock, our name erased,  
What of Your name, what's to be faced?"  

Yahweh spoke, "Get up, why on your face?  
Israel sinned, broken covenant, a disgrace.  

Stolen goods hidden, can't stand and fight,  
Destroy what's taken, make it right.  

Sanctify, prepare, tomorrow's the test,  
Tribes come near, find the one detest.”  

Morning came, tribes gathered by,  
Judah chosen, no need to lie.  

Family by family, down to the core,  
Achan stood guilty, couldn't ignore.  

Joshua said, "Tell me, son, your act,  
Give glory to Yahweh, state the fact."  

Achan confessed, "I took the gold,  
Robe so fine, silver in the fold.  

Hidden it lies beneath my tent,  
Coveted treasures, now I repent."  

Messengers ran, found it below,  
Brought to Joshua, for all to know.  

Achan's fate, set in stone,  
Troubled Israel, now alone.  

Stones flew, fire burned,  
Valley of Achor, lesson learned.  

Heap of stones, marks the place,  
Yahweh's anger, turned from disgrace.
