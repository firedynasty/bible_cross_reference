God spoke to Josh, “Tell the crew,  
Set up the cities like Moses told you.  

A place for those who didn't mean to harm,  
A refuge from the one with an avenging arm.  

Run to the gate, tell your tale,  
The city's elders won't let justice fail.  

Come inside, find a place to stay,  
Live in safety, day by day.  

If chased by blood, don't be afraid,  
The city's your shelter, justice's shade.  

Stay till the trial, till the high priest's day,  
Then return home, come what may.”  

Kedesh in Naphtali's hill was made,  
Shechem and Hebron, where boundaries laid.  

Across the Jordan, Jericho way,  
Bezer, Ramoth, and Golan stay.  

These cities stand for all, even the stranger,  
A haven from the wrath, away from danger.  

Till they stand before the crowd's discerning,  
These cities grant refuge for the returning.
