Yahweh said to Josh, "Don't be afraid, don't be dismayed,  
Take your warriors, rise up, and make Ai afraid.  

I've given their king, their city, and land,  
Do what you did to Jericho, with my command.  

Take the goods, the livestock, all for your crew,  
Set an ambush, that's what you'll do."  

Joshua got up, warriors in the night,  
Thirty thousand men ready to fight.  

He told them, "Lie in wait, be tight,  
When they come out, we'll make it right.  

We'll act like we're beat, flee and run,  
They'll chase us, thinking they've already won.  

Then you'll rise, take the city in your hand,  
Yahweh'll deliver it, as he planned.  

Seize the city, set it on fire,  
Obey His word, fulfill His desire."  

Joshua sent the men; they took their place,  
Between Bethel and Ai, in silence, no trace.  

Joshua stayed with the crowd that night,  
Rose early, ready for the fight.  

Encamped on Ai's north side they lay,  
Five thousand hidden, prepared for the fray.  

City's king saw them, rose with pride,  
With all his men, rushed outside.  

Joshua's crew pretended defeat,  
Fled to the wilderness in quick retreat.  

All Ai's men chased, city left bare,  
No soul behind, none would dare.  

Yahweh spoke, "Stretch your javelin high,  
For I give you Ai, victory's nigh."  

Joshua raised his hand, the ambush ran,  
Into the city, like a well-laid plan.  

They set the city ablaze, flames reached the sky,  
Ai's men turned, but nowhere to fly.  

Smoke rose, Israel saw their chance,  
Turned back, with swords to advance.  

Captured Ai's king, ended his reign,  
Struck down foes, left none to remain.  

Joshua's hand stayed firm with might,  
Till Ai was destroyed, day turned to night.  

Livestock, goods, they took as their own,  
As Yahweh had ordered, it was known.  

Ai burned, a heap, a desolate scene,  
King hanged till sundown, a fate so mean.  

Joshua built an altar on Mount Ebal,  
Following Moses' law, he stood tall.  

Offered peace, and burnt offerings high,  
Wrote Moses' law for every eye.  

Israel stood, from all around,  
Before the ark, as blessings abound.  

He read the law, blessings and curse,  
Every word heard, none would disperse.  

In front of all, with women and young,  
Foreigners too, Yahweh's praises sung.
