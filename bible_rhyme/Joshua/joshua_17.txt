Manasseh's lot, firstborn son of the scene,  
Machir got Gilead, war's keen machine.  

For the rest of Manasseh, land spread wide,  
Abiezer, Helek, the family pride.  

Zelophehad's daughters, no sons to claim,  
Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, Tirzah by name.  

To Eleazar and Joshua, they made their plea,  
"Yahweh said inheritance, give it to me."  

Ten parts for Manasseh, Gilead and Bashan too,  
Beyond the Jordan, the daughters' right came through.  

Borders stretched from Asher to Michmethath's way,  
Tappuah part of Ephraim, Manasseh's sway.  

Brook of Kanah, the boundaries played,  
North to the sea, where each tribe laid.  

Three heights in Issachar, Asher's scene,  
Beth Shean, Ibleam, Dor's in-between.  

But Canaanites stayed, they couldn't drive them out,  
Israel grew strong, made them work without a doubt.  

Children of Joseph asked, “Why just one lot,  
We're numerous, blessed, you see what we've got?”  

Joshua replied, “Clear the forest, make your claim,  
Hill country too tight, Perizzites' land your aim.”  

They said, “Iron chariots Canaanites wield,  
Valley dwellers strong, hard to make them yield.”  

Joshua spoke firm, “You're a power, carry might,  
Not just one lot, the hill country’s your right.  

Cut the forest, drive them out, take your stand,  
Even iron chariots can't stop your command.”
