Joshua called the tribes, gathered them near,  
In Shechem they stood, with their leaders clear.  

He said, "Listen up, this is what God speaks,  
Your fathers served gods across the river's peaks.  

I took Abraham, brought him through Canaan's land,  
Multiplied his seed, made a great stand.  

Isaac was born, then Jacob and Esau too,  
To Esau, Mount Seir, but Egypt Jacob knew.  

I sent Moses and Aaron, plagued Egypt's might,  
Freed your fathers, led them through the night.  

At the Red Sea's edge, Egypt gave chase,  
But Yahweh brought darkness, covered them in grace.  

Through wilderness vast, you wandered many days,  
Then to Amorite land, I showed my ways.  

Balak rose up, sought Balaam's curse,  
But I made blessings, quenched his thirst.  

Crossed the Jordan, reached Jericho's walls,  
Defeated the nations, heeded their calls.  

Hornets drove out kings, not by your hand,  
Gave you cities, fertile is the land.  

So fear Yahweh, serve Him alone,  
Choose now your god, make it known.  

But me and my house, Yahweh we serve,  
Said Joshua with might, gave the nerve.  

The people declared, "We'll serve Yahweh too,  
He freed us from bondage, brought us through."  

Joshua warned, "He's holy, jealous and just,  
Forsake Him and suffer, turn not from trust."  

"We will serve Yahweh," the people did say,  
"Witnesses we are, we'll walk His way."  

"Put away those gods, incline your heart,"  
The people agreed, "We'll never part."  

Joshua made covenant, wrote down the law,  
Under the oak, a stone heard it all.  

"This stone's a witness, don't deny,"  
Joshua spoke, then bid goodbye.  

He lived to one hundred ten, then died,  
Buried in his land, where his ancestors reside.  

Israel served Yahweh, through each elder's day,  
Remembered His deeds, His mighty display.  

Joseph's bones rested in purchased ground,  
In Shechem they stayed, inheritance found.  

Eleazar too, laid to rest on the hill,  
In Ephraim's heart, fulfilling God's will.
