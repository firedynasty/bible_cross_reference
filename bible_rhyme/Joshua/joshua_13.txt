Joshua was old, the years had passed,  
But land remained, vast and vast.  

"You're old," God said, "Yet much remains,  
Philistine lands, in their domains.  

From Shihor near Egypt, north to Ekron,  
The five Philistine lords upon the dawn.  

Canaanite south to Sidonian turf,  
Amorite borders on this earth.  

Lebanon's heights toward sunrise light,  
From Baal Gad to Hamath's sight.  

Sidonians there, in hill country dwell,  
But I'll drive them out; hear the bell.  

So divide the land, for Israel's gain,  
Nine tribes, Manasseh's half, remain.  

Reubenites, Gadites, got their share,  
Beyond Jordan, eastward, they'd declare.  

Aroer stood on the Arnon's line,  
Medeba's plain, cities aligned.  

Sihon's cities, Heshbon's reign,  
Ammon's border, with Gilead's claim.  

Mount Hermon, Bashan to Salecah's peak,  
Og's kingdom, Moses did seek.  

Yet some stayed, Geshurites, Maacathites,  
In Israel's heart, still in sight.  

Levi's tribe, no land to claim,  
God's offerings, their eternal flame.  

Reuben's borders, valley to plain,  
Heshbon's strength, Sihon's slain.  

Balaam fell by Israel's sword,  
Reuben's land, Jordan adored.  

Gad's domain, Jazer's cities wide,  
Ammon's edge, near Rabbah's side.  

From Heshbon, to Ramath's ground,  
In valleys deep, their borders found.  

Gad's inheritance, clear as day,  
Cities and villages along the way.  

Manasseh's half, beyond the tide,  
Mahanaim to Bashan's pride.  

Sixty cities of Jair's embrace,  
Machir's line, claimed their place.  

Moses gave on Moab's plain,  
Beyond Jordan, east terrain.  

But Levi's tribe, no land to see,  
Yahweh's words, their legacy.
