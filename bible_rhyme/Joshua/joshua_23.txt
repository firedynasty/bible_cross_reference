Yo, listen up, Israel, here's the scene,  
Joshua's old now, but his spirit's keen.  

Yahweh gave rest, enemies all around,  
Joshua calls the elders, brings 'em to the ground.  

"I'm old," he says, "you've seen the fights,  
Yahweh fought for you, brought you the rights.  

Nations remain, but they're yours to claim,  
From Jordan to the sea, in Yahweh's name.  

He'll drive them out, clear your sight,  
Possess the land, it's your God-given right.  

Be brave, keep the law, don't stray or bend,  
Follow the book, don't swerve or pretend.  

Don't mix with nations, don't say their gods' names,  
Hold fast to Yahweh, keep playing His games.  

He's driven out nations, strong and great,  
No man stands before you, that's your fate.  

One of you shall chase a thousand away,  
'Cause Yahweh fights, as He did yesterday.  

So love Yahweh, keep His commands tight,  
But don't turn back, don't lose the fight.  

If you mix and mingle, marry and blend,  
Yahweh's blessings will come to an end.  

They'll be traps, thorns in your eyes,  
Till you perish from the land, under dark skies.  

I'm going the way of all the earth,  
But remember Yahweh's promises from birth.  

Every good word, not one has failed,  
But disobey, and evil will be unveiled.  

Break the covenant, serve other gods,  
Yahweh's anger strikes, against all odds.  

You’ll perish quick from the good land,  
So heed these words from Yahweh's hand."
