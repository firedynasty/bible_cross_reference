Yo, the tribes of Israel gathered strong in Shiloh's light,  
Set up the Tent of Meeting, victory in their sight.  
The land before them, calm and subdued,  
Seven tribes left, waiting, waiting for their due.  

Joshua stepped up, called the people to the test,  
"How long will you wait? Time to possess!  
Yahweh your God, He gave you this land,  
Pick three men per tribe, let them walk this sand.  

They'll map out the portions, sevenfold divide,  
Judah in the south, Joseph takes the north side.  
Survey the land, bring back the vision,  
I'll cast lots here, decide with precision.  

Levites needn't worry, their portion's divine,  
Priesthood of Yahweh, their sacred line.  
Gad, Reuben, on Jordan's east remain,  
With Manasseh’s half, for Moses did ordain."  

Men arose, with purpose they went,  
Through the land they journeyed, with survey intent.  
Back at Shiloh, they laid out the plan,  
Joshua cast lots before Yahweh's hand.  

The tribe of Benjamin's lot fell between,  
Judah and Joseph, the borders unseen.  
From Jordan's north, to Jericho's rise,  
Through the hills westward, under Bethlehem skies.  

To Luz, to Ataroth, the border made its way,  
Down past Beth Horon, to Kiriath Jearim's sway.  
Springs of Nephtoah, marked the southern line,  
Through valleys and hills, the border did wind.  

Ended at Jordan, on the east it flowed,  
Benjamin's inheritance, their land bestowed.  
Cities of Benjamin, named with pride,  
Jericho, Beth Hoglah, villages far and wide.  

Twelve cities plus fourteen more,  
A land rich in history, tales of yore.  
This was Benjamin’s lot, by family claim,  
A piece of the promise, in Yahweh's name.
