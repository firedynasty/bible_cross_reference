In the land of Canaan, tribes took their stand,  
Divided by lot, as Yahweh planned.  
Joshua and priests, they held the line,  
Nine tribes and a half, all doing fine.  

East of the Jordan, Moses gave the rest,  
Levites got cities, in Yahweh's behest.  
Joseph's sons, two tribes strong,  
No land for Levites, but their towns belong.  

Caleb stepped up, bold in Gilgal's scene,  
Reminds Joshua of what Yahweh had once been.  
"Forty years then, I spied the land,  
Brought truth to Moses, as Yahweh planned.  

My brothers' doubts made hearts all wane,  
But I followed God, through joy and pain.  
Moses promised me land where I tread,  
For my loyalty, my children were fed."  

"Now look at me, eighty-five and alive,  
Strong as in youth, ready to strive.  
Give me that hill where the giants stay,  
With Yahweh's help, I'll drive them away."  

Joshua blessed him, Hebron was his prize,  
Caleb took it, eyes on the skies.  
Hebron, once Kiriath Arba's name,  
Rest from war, as Caleb's claim.
