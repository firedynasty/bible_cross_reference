Joshua sent his spies from Shittim's place,  
"Check the land, Jericho, see every face."  
They slid into Rahab's house to rest,  
A secret spot, a hidden nest.  

The king heard news, his heart in a bind,  
"Israelite spies? I need them to find!"  
He sent word to Rahab, "Hand them over,  
They came here to spy, uncover, discover."  

But Rahab was smart, she hid them well,  
She spun a tale the king couldn't quell,  
"Men came by, yes, but where they went,  
I don't know, chase fast, be diligent."  

Up on the roof, under flax they laid,  
While pursuers chased, the gates firmly stayed.  
She whispered to them under the night,  
"Yahweh's with you, a fearsome sight."  

"We heard of the sea that split so wide,  
The Amorite kings you cast aside.  
Our hearts have melted, spirits fled,  
'Cause your God rules above our heads."  

"Swear to me by Yahweh's might,  
Show kindness back, protect our light,  
Save my family, keep us alive,  
When Jericho falls, let us survive."  

"Our life for yours," the spies did vow,  
"When Yahweh gives us this town,  
We'll keep our promise, deal true with you,  
But keep this secret, speak only what's due."  

Down by the cord, they slipped through the wall,  
"Hide in the mountains, 'til pursuers fall."  
The spies agreed, "Here's what to do,  
Tie the scarlet thread, make your mark true."  

"When we come back, stay in your space,  
Anyone outside, they're not in a safe place.  
Keep silent, and we'll hold our word,  
Tell one soul, and the deal's disturbed."  

"Let it be as you have said," she sighed,  
The scarlet thread in the window tied.  
Three days passed, hidden from the chase,  
Then they returned to Joshua's place.  

"Yahweh's given us this land," they declared,  
"The people tremble, their spirits bared."  
The spies' report, a promise grand,  
The land was theirs, by God's hand.
