Jabin heard the news, and he called his crew,  
Jobab, Shimron, Achshaph's kings, too.  
North, south, east, west, he rallied the might,  
Canaanites, Amorites, set for the fight.  

Armies like sand, horses, and chariots came,  
To Merom's waters, they marched without shame.  
Yahweh spoke to Joshua, "Don't you fear,  
Tomorrow's their end, I'll make it clear."  

Joshua struck at Merom, a sudden attack,  
With Yahweh's hand, they chased them back.  
Sidon to Mizpah, they ran them down,  
No one left standing, took the crown.  

Horses hamstrung, chariots aflame,  
Hazor fell, its king met the same.  
No soul was spared, no breath remained,  
Hazor burned, its rule was drained.  

Cities captured, kings laid low,  
Joshua followed Yahweh's flow.  
Hazor alone felt the fire's embrace,  
Israel kept cities in their place.  

Plunder taken, livestock claimed,  
Every foe with the sword was named.  
Joshua did just as Moses said,  
Left not a single word unread.  

He took the land from hill to sea,  
Goshen, Arabah, and all that be.  
Kings were struck, put to their rest,  
With Yahweh's aid, they passed the test.  

No city made peace, none dared to plea,  
Yahweh's plan, hearts hardened, you see.  
Anakim cut from Israel's soil,  
Gaza and Gath, their last coil.  

Joshua took it all, as promised before,  
Divided the land, tribes galore.  
At last, the land had a moment's pause,  
Israel rested from the wars' harsh laws.
