Yo, listen up, these are the kings they faced,  
Israel's children moved in, took their place.  

Beyond the Jordan, where the sun does rise,  
From Arnon's valley up to Hermon's skies.  

Sihon the Amorite, in Heshbon he stayed,  
Aroer to Jabbok, his border was laid.  

Chinneroth eastward, to the Salt Sea's shore,  
Beth Jeshimoth and Pisgah, they claimed more.  

Og of Bashan, a remnant strong and might,  
In Ashtaroth and Edrei, he'd hold his fight.  

Hermon to Salecah, Bashan's lands he led,  
Geshurites and Maacathites near his bed.  

Moses, Yahweh’s servant, struck them down,  
Gave Reubenites, Gadites, half-Manasseh their crown.  

Then Joshua led, west of the Jordan's flow,  
From Baal Gad to Mount Halak, on they'd go.  

Hill and lowland, Arabah and south,  
Hittite, Amorite, they'd clear them out.  

Canaanite, Perizzite met their fate,  
Hivite, Jebusite couldn't escape.  

Jericho's king, Ai near Bethel's space,  
Each king they faced, none could keep their place.  

Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth's fall,  
Lachish, Eglon, Gezer, they took all.  

Debir and Geder, Hormah did bow,  
Arad and Libnah, in defeat they vow.  

Adullam, Makkedah, Bethel's end,  
Tappuah, Hepher, the list extends.  

Aphek, Lassharon, Madon, Hazor's done,  
Shimron Meron, Achshaph, one by one.  

Taanach, Megiddo, Kedesh, Kings fell,  
Jokneam of Carmel's story to tell.  

Dor and Goiim, Gilgal's power fled,  
Tirzah, thirty-one kings now lay dead.  

Israel's tribes claimed what was theirs by right,  
United, they stood, in Yahweh's might.
