Jericho's gates were locked up tight, no one's goin' in or out,  
Israel camped outside those walls, no doubt, God's got a plan, a route.  

The Lord told Joshua, "Jericho's yours, with its king and might,  
March 'round the city for six days, warriors ready to fight."  

"Seven priests with trumpets, rams' horns in hand,  
On the seventh day, seven laps, like planned."  

"When you hear that trumpet blast, shout loud with all your soul,  
And those walls will crumble down, you'll take control."  

Joshua called the priests, "Lift the ark, let's move,  
Seven trumpets lead the way, it's time to groove."  

"Advance!" he said, "March 'round, armed and bold,  
Wait for my command to shout, let the story unfold."  

Day by day, they circled once, silent and slow,  
Until the seventh day, they marched seven times, ready to blow.  

When the horns blew long, Joshua yelled, "Shout out loud,  
For Yahweh's given us this town, let’s make Him proud!"  

The city's devoted, all's for Yahweh, except Rahab's crew,  
She hid our spies, she'll survive, along with her family too.  

"Silver and gold, the Lord's treasure they’ll be,  
But take nothing else, or cursed you will see."  

The people roared, the trumpets blared, the walls fell flat,  
The army charged in, victory in their hat.  

They destroyed it all, man and beast alike,  
Israel took Jericho with power and might.  

Joshua sent spies to Rahab's place, as he swore,  
Saved her and her kin, brought them through the door.  

They burned the city, left nothing standing tall,  
Gold and silver in Yahweh’s house, they took it all.  

Rahab lived in Israel, her story's no bluff,  
For she protected those spies, proved courage enough.  

Joshua spoke a curse, "Rebuild Jericho, you'll pay,  
Firstborn for the foundation, youngest for the gates that day."  

So Yahweh was with Joshua, his fame spread wide,  
Israel knew their leader, with God by his side.
