Moses is gone, Joshua steps to the line,  
Yahweh speaks, "It's your time to shine.  

"Moses is dead, now rise and lead,  
Cross the Jordan, fulfill the need.  

"Every place your foot will tread,  
I've given you, just like I said.  

"From Lebanon to Euphrates wide,  
To the Great Sea where the sun slides.  

"None can stand, all your days,  
As with Moses, I'll guide your ways.  

"Be strong, be brave, lead this crowd,  
To the land promised, loud and proud.  

"Observe the law, don’t turn aside,  
In success and prosperity, you'll abide.  

"Meditate on this day and night,  
Keep it close, do what’s right.  

"I've commanded, don’t show fear,  
For Yahweh your God is always near."  

Joshua commands, with a voice so clear,  
"Prepare yourselves, the time is near.  

"In three days, the Jordan we’ll cross,  
Possess the land, gain not loss."  

To Reuben, Gad, Manasseh he spoke,  
"Moses' words, no idle joke.  

"Your families stay on this side,  
But you must cross, with brothers ride.  

"Help them rest as you were blessed,  
Then your land will be possessed."  

The people replied, ready to go,  
"Where you lead, we will follow.  

"As we listened to Moses, so to you,  
May Yahweh be with you in all you do.  

"Rebellion will face death's toll,  
Be strong, be brave, that’s our goal."
