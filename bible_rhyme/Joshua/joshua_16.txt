From Jordan at Jericho, where the waters flow,  
The lot for Joseph's children began to grow.  
Through the wilderness, rising to Bethel's door,  
It shot out from Bethel, reaching Luz and more.  

Past the Archites to Ataroth it went,  
Down west to the Japhletites, paths were bent.  
To Beth Horon lower, on to Gezer's bay,  
And ended at the sea, where the waters lay.  

Manasseh and Ephraim claimed their land,  
Line by line, border drawn by God's hand.  
Ephraim's edge stretched from Ataroth Addar,  
To Beth Horon upper, not too far.  

North to Michmethath, then east it turned,  
Taanath Shiloh passed, where the sun burned.  
Down from Janoah, through Ataroth's site,  
To Naarah, Jericho, Jordan's might.  

From Tappuah west to Kanah's brook,  
To the sea's end, their inheritance they took.  
Cities in the midst of Manasseh's share,  
For Ephraim's tribe, their dwellings there.  

Yet Canaanites in Gezer still held sway,  
Servants in Ephraim, there they stay.  
The land's not cleared, but life goes on,  
This is Ephraim's tale, its border drawn.
