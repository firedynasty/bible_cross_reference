Joshua called the tribes, gathered round,  
"Reubenites, Gadites, listen to the sound.  

You’ve kept the commands, followed the path,  
Stayed with your brothers through the aftermath.  

Now Yahweh’s given rest, just as He said,  
Return to your tents, the land ahead.  

But heed this word, follow the law,  
Love Yahweh with all, never withdraw."  

He blessed them, sent them on their way,  
To their tents they returned, no delay.  

Manasseh split, some to Bashan's land,  
Others by the Jordan, as Moses planned.  

Joshua spoke, sent them home with wealth,  
"Share the spoil, keep your brothers in health."  

Reuben and Gad, traveled away,  
Crossed the Jordan without delay.  

Built an altar by the river's side,  
A mighty sight, impossible to hide.  

But Israel heard, and their hearts were stirred,  
"An altar, they say? Prepare for war!"  

Phinehas and leaders sent to find,  
The truth of the altar, peace in mind.  

"What’s this you’ve done? Turned away?  
Built your own altar, led astray?  

Remember Peor’s sin and Achan’s fall,  
Rebel today, and anger calls."  

But Reuben and Gad, responded clear,  
"Yahweh knows, let Israel hear.  

Not in rebellion, nor to defy,  
But as a witness, for days gone by.  

If future children question our place,  
This altar's proof of Yahweh’s grace.  

Not for sacrifice, not to replace,  
But a sign of unity, face to face."  

Phinehas heard, and joy did rise,  
"Yahweh's among us, no disguise."  

Returned to Israel, spread the good word,  
Peace restored, no more war, no sword.  

Named the altar, “A Witness,” they said,  
That Yahweh is God, to Him they’ve wed.
