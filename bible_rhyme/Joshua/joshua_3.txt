Joshua rose at dawn, ready to lead the way,  
From Shittim to Jordan, where they'd camp and stay.  

Three days passed, the officers took their stride,  
"In sight of God's ark, you'll follow and abide.  

Keep your distance, a space you must hold true,  
This path is new, it's fresh, it's made for you."  

Joshua to the crowd, "Sanctify tonight,  
Tomorrow Yahweh's wonders, a wondrous sight."  

To priests, he called out, "Lift the ark, go ahead,  
Cross before the people, take the path we tread."  

Yahweh spoke to Josh, "I'll lift you high, you'll see,  
With Israel watching, like Moses, you'll be.  

Tell the priests at the brink, 'Stand in Jordan's flow,'  
Watch the waters stop, let the story grow."  

Josh gathered Israel, "Hear what God has planned,  
He'll clear the way, drive enemies from the land.  

The living God leads, the ark goes before,  
Twelve men from the tribes, for what's in store."  

"When priestly feet touch Jordan's rolling tide,  
Waters will halt, the path will open wide."  

Tents left behind, the people made their quest,  
Priests at the water's edge, stood the test.  

Water rising high, like a wall so steep,  
Jordan parted wide, in one mighty heap.  

The priests stood firm, dry ground 'neath their feet,  
Israel crossed over, their victory complete.
