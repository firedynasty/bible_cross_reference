When the nation crossed the Jordan, standing tall,  
Yahweh spoke to Joshua, gave him a call:  

"Take twelve men, one from each tribe,  
And have them lift stones from where the waters divide.  

Place them where you camp tonight,  
A reminder of this powerful sight."

Joshua gathered the chosen twelve,  
Said, "Step into the river, where the priests dwell.  

Each carry a stone on your strong shoulder,  
A tale to tell when your children get older.  

When they ask, 'What’s the story behind these stones?'  
Tell them how the Jordan's waters turned to bone.  

With Yahweh’s ark, the waters did part,  
And these stones will forever be close to our heart."  

The people obeyed and brought stones to the shore,  
A testament to God's mighty roar.  

Joshua placed stones in the river's flow,  
Where the priests with the ark stood, long ago.  

The priests held their ground, their task was clear,  
Until all God commanded was brought near.  

Forty thousand soldiers, armed and ready,  
Crossed the river, their hearts steady.  

On that day, Joshua's name grew great,  
Feared like Moses, by Israel's state.  

Yahweh spoke again, clear as day,  
"Tell the priests it's time to walk away."  

Joshua echoed, "Priests, come forth from the tide,"  
And as they did, the waters returned wide.  

Camped in Gilgal, east of Jericho’s gate,  
The twelve stones stood, their story innate.  

Joshua said, "When asked by your kin,  
'What do these stones mean?' Let the story begin.  

Yahweh dried up Jordan, just like the Red Sea,  
So the whole world knows, His might you will see.  

Remember His power, remember His hand,  
Fear Yahweh your God, in this promised land."
