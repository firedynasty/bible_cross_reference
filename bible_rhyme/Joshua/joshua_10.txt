Yo, listen up, 'bout Adoni-Zedek's fear,  
Joshua took Ai, made it disappear.  
Just like he did to Jericho, no doubt,  
And Gibeon's peace deal made the kings shout.  

Gibeon was big, like a royal scene,  
Greater than Ai, its men were mean.  
Adoni-Zedek called his crew,  
Five kings, plotting what they would do.  

"Come help me strike Gibeon down,  
They made peace, don't let them wear the crown."  
So the kings of the Amorites gathered their might,  
Set their sights on Gibeon, ready to fight.  

Gibeon's men, scared, sent word to Josh,  
"Don't leave us now, come help us squash,  
These five kings rising from the hills,  
Come quick with your mighty skills."  

Joshua marched with his crew all night,  
Ready to fight, ready to fight.  
And Yahweh said, "Don't fear their band,  
I've delivered them into your hand."  

Joshua struck fast, took them by surprise,  
Yahweh confused 'em, left 'em in cries.  
Hailstones rained, more died from the sky,  
Than by the sword, those Amorites would die.  

Then Joshua spoke, "Sun, stand still,  
Moon, halt in Aijalon, until we kill."  
The sun paused, the moon delayed,  
A day like no other, Yahweh's aid displayed.  

Five kings fled, hid in a cave,  
But Joshua found them, none he would save.  
"Roll stones, guard them tight," Joshua spoke,  
But first, strike your enemies, make 'em choke.  

Joshua's crew finished the fight,  
Then returned to Makkedah, all was right.  
"Open the cave, bring out those kings,  
Put your feet on their necks, see what it brings."  

Joshua said, "Don’t fear, be brave and bold,  
Yahweh fights for us, His power we hold."  
The kings were struck, hung on trees,  
Til sunset, then thrown in their stone-made freeze.  

Joshua conquered, city by city,  
With Yahweh's hand, no room for pity.  
Libnah, Lachish, Eglon fell,  
Hebron, Debir, Joshua's story to tell.  

From Kadesh Barnea to Gaza's gate,  
Joshua struck, sealing their fate.  
Yahweh fought, gave them the land,  
Joshua returned to Gilgal, God's plan was grand.
