The kings heard tales, their hearts did quail,  
No spirit left, their courage frail.  

Water dried, the Jordan crossed,  
Fear filled their souls, a line was tossed.  

Yahweh said, "Joshua, grab those knives,  
Circumcise again, renew their lives."  

Joshua moved, with flint in hand,  
At foreskin hill, he took a stand.  

Those who left Egypt, warriors brave,  
Died in the desert, none to save.  

In the wild they roamed, forty years, long and wide,  
Till the old warriors faded, the promise denied.  

Their children rose, a new generation’s birth,  
Joshua circumcised them, claimed their worth.  

When all was done, healing came fast,  
The camp settled down, pain didn’t last.  

Yahweh spoke, "Reproach of Egypt's gone away,"  
Gilgal's the name, a new dawn in display.  

In Gilgal’s embrace, they camped, they ate,  
Passover kept, aligned with fate.  

Unleavened bread, grain from the ground,  
Manna ceased, new food they found.  

Joshua near Jericho, eyes lifted high,  
A man with a sword, ready to try.  

"Are you friend or foe?" Joshua asked bold,  
"Commander of Yahweh, truth's been told."  

Joshua bowed low, face to earth,  
"What does my lord say, what's this worth?"  

"Remove your sandals, this ground is holy,"  
Joshua obeyed, approached it slowly.
