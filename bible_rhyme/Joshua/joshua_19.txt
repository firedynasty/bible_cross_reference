Simeon in the middle, Judah's share was wide,  
Beersheba, Moladah, villages they provide.  

Cities on cities, thirteen strong and true,  
Ziklag, Hormah, all the lands they drew.  

Judah shared their portion, giving Simeon space,  
Inheritance they got, in the heart of the place.  

Zebulun's lot next, boundaries to Sarid flow,  
West to Maralah, Jokneam did they know.  

East to the sunrise, Chisloth Tabor they gained,  
Daberath and Japhia, all that land they claimed.  

Hannathon to the north, valley Iphtah El,  
Twelve cities in their grasp, every village as well.  

Issachar’s turn, their border Jezreel bound,  
Chesulloth, Shunem, cities all around.  

Sixteen cities strong, by Jordan's end they stand,  
Issachar’s families, with their village land.  

Asher was next, Helkath, Hali they own,  
Beten, Achshaph, to Carmel it was shown.  

Sidon's great walls, Ramah to Tyre's might,  
Twenty-two cities, all in Asher’s sight.  

Naphtali followed, from Heleph did they start,  
Zaanannim’s oak, cities played their part.  

Nineteen cities gained, on Jordan's banks they rest,  
Naphtali's families, in the land they possessed.  

Dan's borders stretched, Zorah to Eshtaol,  
They fought for Leshem, called it Dan their goal.  

Each tribe got their share, cities, lands, and might,  
Joshua, too, got his, by the Lord's own right.  

In Shiloh they gathered, borders drawn with care,  
Before the Tent of Meeting, every tribe took their share.
