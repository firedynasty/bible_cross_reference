Paul, servant of God, with a message to share,  
Chosen by faith, with a truth that's rare.  

In hope of life eternal, God can't deceive,  
Promised before time, in this truth we believe.  

God's word revealed in His own perfect time,  
To Titus, my child, with a faith that’s prime.  

"Grace and peace," from God the Savior divine,  
I left you in Crete with a duty in line.  

Set things in order, elders appoint,  
In every city, make strong this joint.  

Blameless leaders, one wife they keep,  
Children believe, no trouble runs deep.  

God's stewards blameless, not chasing gain,  
Hospitable, holy, sober in brain.  

Hold the word faithful, doctrine to teach,  
Exhort and convict those out of reach.  

Unruly men, vain talkers abound,  
Mouths must be stopped, their teachings unsound.  

"Cretans are liars," the prophet once said,  
Reprove them sharp, to truth be led.  

Pure hearts find purity in all they see,  
While defiled minds lack purity.  

They claim to know God, but actions deny,  
Unfit for good, as deeds testify.
