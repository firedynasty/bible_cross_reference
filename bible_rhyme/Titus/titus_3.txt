Listen up, obey the rules, submit to the power,  
Ready for good deeds every minute, every hour.  

Speak no evil, stay calm, be kind,  
Show humility, leave contention behind.  

Once we were foolish, lost in the night,  
Serving desires, with envy and spite.  

But then came kindness, love from above,  
God's mercy saved, His Spirit like a dove.  

"Not by your deeds," He said with grace,  
Renewed by the Spirit, in His embrace.  

Jesus poured out, His love so bright,  
Now we're heirs to eternal light.  

This truth is strong, affirm it bold,  
Those who believe, let good works unfold.  

Avoid silly disputes, they lead to waste,  
Foolish arguments are a bitter taste.  

Warn the divisive once, then twice,  
They're self-condemned, caught in their vice.  

Artemas or Tychicus, I'll send your way,  
Join me in Nicopolis, where I'll stay.  

Send Zenas and Apollos, without delay,  
Support them well, don't block their way.  

Teach our people, fruitful to be,  
Grace be with you, from them and me.  

Greetings to all, united in faith's call,  
Grace be with you, blessings on y'all. Amen, y'all.
