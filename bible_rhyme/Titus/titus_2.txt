Speak the truth, sound and clear, let it shine,  
Older men, be wise, keep it fine.  

Be steady, temperate, with faith that inspires,  
In love and perseverance, never tire.  

Older women, reverent, live with grace,  
No slander, no wine, goodness embrace.  

Train young wives to love, to keep the home,  
To cherish their children, never roam.  

Be sober and chaste, kind in their way,  
Following the word, no dismay.  

Young men, be sound, focused on the goal,  
Show with your deeds, let integrity roll.  

In teaching, be solid, serious, and true,  
So opponents are shamed, nothing to spew.  

Servants, be loyal, pleasing, and right,  
No stealing, just fidelity, keep it tight.  

Adorn God's doctrine with actions so bright,  
His grace appeared, bringing salvation's light.  

Deny the world’s lusts, live righteously here,  
Seek the blessed hope, let the future be clear.  

Jesus, the Savior, gave all for our gain,  
Redeemed us from sin, pure we remain.  

Speak with authority, let it be known,  
With courage and truth, make it your own.
