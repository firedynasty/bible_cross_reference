Rose of Sharon, lily's grace,  
In the valleys, love finds its place.  

Among thorns, my love shines bright,  
Apple tree stands, a pure delight.  

In his shadow, I find rest,  
His fruit's sweetness, I am blessed.  

Banquet hall, love's banner flies,  
"Strengthen me," my heart cries.  

Left hand cradles, right hand holds,  
Love's embrace, as time unfolds.  

"Daughters of Jerusalem, hear my plea,  
Don't stir love 'til it’s ready to be."  

Oh, hear his voice, my love's approach,  
Leaping mountains, hills encroach.  

Like a young deer, swift and near,  
At the window, he appears.  

"Rise up, my love, come with me,  
Winter's gone, set us free.  

Flowers bloom, songs abound,  
Turtledove's voice, sweet sound.  

Figs ripen, vines in bloom,  
Fragrance fills every room."  

"Dove in rock, come out, be seen,  
Your sweet voice, face serene.  

Catch the foxes, the vineyard’s foe,  
For our blossoms, let love grow."  

My beloved's mine, and I am his,  
Among the lilies, love is bliss.  

'Til day cools and shadows part,  
Turn, my love, follow your heart.
