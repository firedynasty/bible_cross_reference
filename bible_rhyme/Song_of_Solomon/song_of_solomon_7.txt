Your feet in sandals, oh so neat,  
A prince's daughter can't be beat.  

Thighs rounded, jewels so grand,  
Crafted by a skillful hand.  

Your body's like a goblet round,  
No mixed wine lacking, profound.  

Waist like wheat, lilies surround,  
Beauty in every pound.  

Breasts like fawns, twin roe in grace,  
In this garden, you find your place.  

Your neck's an ivory tower high,  
Eyes like pools where waters lie.  

Nose like Lebanon's tower strong,  
Watching Damascus all day long.  

Head like Carmel standing tall,  
Hair in purple, where kings fall.  

How beautiful, how pleasant, love,  
Delights from earth and above.  

Stature like a palm tree wide,  
Breasts like fruit on every side.  

"I'll climb the palm, hold its fruit tight,  
Breasts like clusters, for my delight."  

Breath's like apples, mouth like wine,  
For my beloved, smooth and fine.  

"I'm my beloved's, his desire is mine,"  
In this love, our hearts align.  

"Come, my love, let's fieldward stray,  
In the villages, we'll stay."  

To vineyards early, let's retreat,  
See the buds, the blossoms sweet.  

Pomegranates in flower bloom,  
"I'll give you love, dispel the gloom."  

Mandrakes fragrant, fruits galore,  
New and old, at every door.  

Stored up treasures, love devout,  
For you, my beloved, no doubt.
