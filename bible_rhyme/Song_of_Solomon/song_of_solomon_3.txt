In the night I lay, seeking the one my soul adores,  
Looked and searched, but found closed doors.  

Jumped from bed, hit the streets wide,  
Seeking my love in the city's stride.  

Watchmen found me, they saw me roam,  
"Seen my love?" I asked as I wandered alone.  

Barely passed them, my heart took flight,  
Found my love, held him tight.  

Brought him home, wouldn't let go,  
To my mother's room, where love would grow.  

"Daughters of Jerusalem, hear me clear,"  
"Don't wake love before it draws near."  

Who's this rising from the wild,  
Pillars of smoke, smellin' sweet and mild?  

Look, it's Solomon's ride in style,  
Sixty warriors strong, marching in file.  

Swords at the ready, experts in fight,  
Guardians against the fears of night.  

Solomon's chariot, crafted with care,  
Wood of Lebanon, silver flare.  

Gold base, purple seat, love inlaid,  
Jerusalem's daughters, their craft displayed.  

Step out, daughters of Zion, see him crowned,  
On his wedding day, pure joy abound.
