You're stunning, my love, pure delight I proclaim,  
Your eyes like doves, framed by your veil, a gentle flame.  
Hair cascading like goats on Gilead's grand slopes,  
Teeth like fresh-shorn flocks, no missing hopes.  

Lips of scarlet thread, your mouth speaks grace,  
Temples like pomegranate, behind the veil, in place.  
Your neck, a tower of David, shields hang and sway,  
Strength of mighty men, in every way.  

Breasts like twin fawns, grazing lilies so fair,  
'Til shadows flee, myrrh and frankincense in the air.  
You're flawless, my love, perfect in every view,  
"Come from Lebanon, my bride, I'll be with you."  

Ravished my heart with a glance and a chain,  
Your love, oh my bride, like sweet wine, never plain.  
Lips drip honeycomb, sweetness flows deep,  
Garments smell of Lebanon, in them I steep.  

Locked garden, sealed fountain, pure and bright,  
Orchards of pomegranates, in the soft moonlight.  
Spikenard, saffron, cinnamon's embrace,  
With myrrh and aloes, spices fill the space.  

Fountain of gardens, living waters flow,  
From Lebanon's streams, where the best spices grow.  
"Awake north wind, south breeze, make my garden known,  
Let my beloved taste, where precious fruits have grown."
