Where's your beloved gone, fair woman, where's he turned?  
We'll seek him with you, find him where he yearned.  

He's down in the garden, beds of spice,  
Pasturing flocks, gathering lilies, oh so nice.  

"I'm my beloved's, and he is mine,"  
He browses among lilies, where they intertwine.  

You're beautiful, my love, like Tirzah's grace,  
Lovely as Jerusalem, with an awesome face.  

"Turn your eyes away, they've overcome my soul,"  
Your hair's like Gilead's goats, on the gentle roll.  

Your teeth are like ewes, fresh from the stream,  
Every one's got twins, perfect as they gleam.  

Your temples, a piece of pomegranate's hue,  
Behind your veil, they shine through.  

Sixty queens, eighty concubines in line,  
Virgins without number, yet you're divine.  

"My dove, my perfect one, you're unique,"  
Your mother's only, her favorite chic.  

Daughters call you blessed, queens praise you high,  
Who is she, like morning, moon, sun in the sky?  

I went to the nut grove, green valley's expanse,  
Watching vines bud, pomegranates dance.  

Without knowing, desire drove my heart,  
With royal chariots, ready to depart.  

Return, return, oh Shulammite, so rare,  
Why gaze at you, like Mahanaim's dance, fair?
