Oh, that you were like my brother, my kin,  
I'd kiss you outside, no shame, no sin.  

Lead you home, where my mother imparts,  
Pour you spiced wine, juice from my heart.  

His left hand supports under my head,  
Right arm embracing, love's comfort spread.  

"Daughters of Jerusalem, heed my plea,  
Don't wake love till it desires to be free."  

Who emerges from the wild, leaning close,  
Under apple trees, where love first grows?  

"Set me as a seal on your heart, so tight,  
For love’s strong as death, a fierce, burning light."  

Many waters can't quench a love so grand,  
Floods can't drown it; it will withstand.  

A sister, not grown, what shall we do?  
Build her a silver tower, strong and true.  

"I am a wall, my form towers high,  
In his eyes, peace, where love does lie."  

Solomon's vineyard leased at great worth,  
Shekels for fruit, a measure of earth.  

"My vineyard's mine, yet for you a share,  
Silver for keepers, fruit they bear."  

"You who dwell in gardens, friends near and clear,  
Let your voice echo, sweet in my ear."  

"Come away, my love, like a gazelle's race,  
Bound on spice mountains, swift in grace."
