Solomon's song, a tale of love's delight,  
"Your kiss is sweeter than wine," she recites.  

Your oils, your scent, finer than gold,  
Your name's like perfume, a story retold.  

"Take me away, let's run with haste,  
Into the king's rooms, where joy's embraced."  

Dark but lovely, she stands with pride,  
"Like Kedar's tents, no need to hide."  

"Don't gaze at my skin, sun-kissed and warm,  
My brothers' wrath became my norm."  

"Where's your flock at noon?" she calls,  
"Why should I veiled wander these walls?"  

He speaks, "Oh beauty, follow the way,  
By shepherds' tents, let your young goats play."  

"You're a steed in Pharaoh's chariot ride,  
Cheeks adorned, jewels on your side."  

"Gold earrings we'll craft, silver inlay,  
While the king dines, my perfume's display."  

"My beloved's myrrh, so close, so near,  
Henna blossoms from En Gedi appear."  

"Behold, you're beautiful, dove-like eyes,  
And our verdant couch, where love lies."  

"See our home's beams, cedar strong,  
Firs are our rafters, where we belong."
