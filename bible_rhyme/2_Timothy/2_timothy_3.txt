Know this: in the last days, the struggle's real,  
People loving themselves, and cash they steal.  

Boastful and loud, arrogant in stride,  
Disobedient, unholy, they don't abide.  

No love naturally, won't forgive a slight,  
Slandering, fierce, and avoiding the light.  

Traitors, headstrong, pleasure their crave,  
Fake godliness, but empty ways they pave.  

"Turn away from these," the wise one say,  
Creeping folks lead others astray.  

Always learning, truth they can't find,  
Corrupted are they, with a rejected mind.  

Jannes and Jambres stood against the great,  
Their folly exposed, couldn't change their fate.  

But you've followed my path, my faith, my aim,  
Through trials and towns, I stayed the same.  

Persecuted, enduring, yet I stood tall,  
The Lord delivered me from them all.  

Godly living brings persecution's weight,  
While evil deceivers meet a worse fate.  

Stick to what you've learned, hold it tight,  
From childhood, Scriptures guided you right.  

God-breathed words, teaching and wise,  
Correct and instruct, open your eyes.  

Equipped for good, each person complete,  
Righteous paths laid down at your feet.
