Listen up, my child, get strong in that grace,  
The kind found in Christ, run this faithful race.  

What you’ve heard from me, shared with many around,  
Pass it on to faithful ones, let their teaching abound.  

Endure like a soldier, steadfast and bold,  
No tangled affairs, just the mission you've been told.  

An athlete runs right, the crown he’ll gain,  
Compete by the rules, don’t train in vain.  

The farmer works hard, gets the first taste,  
Consider these words, don’t let wisdom go to waste.  

Remember Christ risen, descendant of Dave,  
For this Good News, I suffer and brave.  

In chains for a cause, but God’s word is free,  
Endure for the chosen, salvation’s the key.  

“If we died with Him, we’ll live anew,  
Endure and reign, be faithful and true.  

Deny Him now, He’ll deny you back,  
Faithless we are, but His love won’t lack.”  

Remind them all, in the Lord’s sight,  
Don’t argue on words, it’s a pointless fight.  

Show diligence, be approved by the Lord,  
Properly handle the Truth, it’s His word.  

Avoid empty talk, it’s ungodly and vain,  
Words that spread like gangrene, causing pain.  

Yet God’s foundation, firm and strong,  
“The Lord knows His own,” now sing that song.  

In a great house, vessels gold and clay,  
Some for honor, some lead astray.  

Purge yourself from bad, be a vessel of pride,  
Sanctified and ready, with the Master abide.  

Flee youthful lusts, pursue what’s pure,  
Righteousness, faith, love, and peace endure.  

Refuse foolishness, causing strife,  
The Lord’s servant gentle, with patience in life.  

Teach and correct in gentle ways,  
Perhaps they’ll find truth and mend their ways.  

Escape the snare, find freedom and grace,  
Caught no more, they’ll find their place.
