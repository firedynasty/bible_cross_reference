Before God and Christ, who'll judge living, dead,  
Preach the word, be ready, keep your head.  

Reprove and rebuke, with patience teach,  
When ears itch for lies, truth's out of reach.  

Stay sober, face hardship, evangelist's call,  
Fulfill your ministry, stand tall.  

I'm offered up now, departure's near,  
Fought the fight, kept faith without fear.  

A crown awaits, righteousness bestows,  
Not just for me, but all He knows.  

Come quickly, Demas left for the world's delight,  
Only Luke’s here, bring Mark, he’s right.  

Sent Tychicus to Ephesus, don’t delay,  
Bring my cloak, my books, on your way.  

Alexander did me wrong, let God repay,  
Be cautious, he opposed what I say.  

When I first stood, no one by my side,  
Forgive them, with the Lord I abide.  

Strength He gave, message proclaimed,  
From the lion's mouth, I wasn't maimed.  

He’ll save me from evil, Heaven awaits,  
To Him be glory, forever great.  

Greet Prisca, Aquila, Onesiphorus's kin,  
Erastus stayed, Trophimus is sick within.  

Come before winter, greetings flow,  
Jesus with your spirit, grace and glow. Amen, you know.
