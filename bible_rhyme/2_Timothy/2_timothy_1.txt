Paul, an apostle, through God's own hand,  
To Timothy, my child, in a promised land.  
Grace, mercy, and peace, from the Father above,  
From Christ Jesus, our Lord, sent with love.  

I thank God, like those who came before,  
With a conscience clear, I remember more.  
Day and night, you're in my prayer,  
Longing to see you, in joy and care.  

Your faith, sincere, like Lois and Eunice instilled,  
A legacy of belief, a promise fulfilled.  
So fan the spark, the gift inside you,  
By my hands, it came to guide you.  

"God gave no spirit of fear," hear it clear,  
"But power, love, self-control" we hold dear.  
Don't be ashamed of Christ's testimony,  
Nor of my chains, endure in harmony.  

God saved, called us with a holy decree,  
Not by works, but grace, eternally.  
In the light of Christ, death met its end,  
Life and immortality, a message to send.  

Preacher, apostle, to Gentiles I go,  
Suffering yet strong, for the truth I know.  
"I trust Him, guard my belief,  
Till that day, protect from grief."  

Hold fast to words, sound and true,  
Faith and love in Christ, shine through.  
Guard the treasure with Spirit's force,  
In His dwelling, find your course.  

Asia turned away, left me alone,  
Phygelus, Hermogenes, seeds they've sown.  
Onesiphorus, may mercy seek his door,  
He refreshed my soul, found me once more.  

In Rome, he sought me, found my chain,  
May mercy meet him, in Jesus' name.  
In Ephesus, his service you know,  
A light in darkness, his deeds did show.
