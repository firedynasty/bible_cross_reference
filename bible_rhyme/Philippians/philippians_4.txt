Brothers, sisters, listen, here’s the plan,  
Stand firm in the Lord, be strong, take a stand.  

Euodia and Syntyche, think as one,  
True partner, help them get it done.  

They worked with me spreading the Good News,  
Names in the book of life, they've paid their dues.  

Rejoice in the Lord, I say it again,  
"Rejoice!" let it echo, shout it, my friend.  

Let your gentleness to all be shown,  
The Lord is near, you’re not alone.  

Don’t be anxious, drop your fear,  
Pray with thanks, let God hear.  

God's peace surpassing all you see,  
Will guard your heart, your mind set free.  

Think on things that are true and right,  
Pure and lovely, keep them in sight.  

What you've learned and heard, do these things,  
God of peace, His presence brings.  

I rejoice greatly, your thought revived,  
Not because of lack, but content I’ve survived.  

Humbled or abounding, I know it well,  
Filled or hungry, in all I dwell.  

"I can do all things through Christ," my creed,  
In your affliction, you planted a seed.  

Philippians, you stood by my side,  
From the start, you were my guide.  

Even in Thessalonica, you met my needs,  
No gift I seek, but your growing deeds.  

I've got all things, abound in grace,  
Epaphroditus brought your embrace.  

A sweet aroma, a sacrifice pleasing,  
God will supply, in glory unceasing.  

To our God and Father, glory forever,  
Greetings from all, united, never severed.  

Grace of the Lord, with you all,  
Amen, my friends, hear the call.
