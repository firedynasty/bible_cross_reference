Yo, brothers, rejoice, find joy in the Lord's embrace,  
I write this again, for your safety, not a waste of space.  

Beware the dogs, the workers of evil schemes,  
False moves, false talk, watch out for their themes.  

We're the true sign, worship in Spirit's name,  
Rejoice in Christ, no fleshly game.  

I could boast about flesh, if anyone can,  
Circumcised, Israelite, Hebrew, a pure-bred man.  

Pharisee by the law, zeal led me to chase,  
Blameless by the law, but I found a higher place.  

What I gained, I count as loss,  
For knowing Christ, it's worth the cost.  

Suffered loss, but it's nothing, just refuse,  
To gain Christ, the path I choose.  

Found in him, not my own right or flaw,  
But faith in Christ, righteousness from God’s law.  

To know him, feel resurrection's power,  
Share in his sufferings, conform in the darkest hour.  

Not there yet, but pressing on,  
For what Christ claimed, I reach, never gone.  

Forgetting the past, eyes on the prize,  
The high calling in Christ, where my purpose lies.  

Perfect ones, think like this, stay in line,  
If you wander, God will reveal in time.  

Let’s walk in unity, same rule, same mind,  
Be imitators of me, examples you’ll find.  

Many walk wrong, enemies of Christ's cross,  
I weep for them, their end, their loss.  

Their god is the belly, their glory’s in shame,  
Earthly thoughts, forgetting Heaven's name.  

Our home is heaven, where we wait and see,  
For Christ to change us, to set us free.  

He'll shape our humble shell, make it shine,  
Conformed to his glory, by power divine.
