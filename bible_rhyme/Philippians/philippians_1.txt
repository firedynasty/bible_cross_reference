Paul and Timothy, servants of the Lord,  
Writing to Philippi's saints in one accord.  
"Grace and peace," we send from above,  
"From God and Christ, with all our love."  

I thank my God every time you're in sight,  
Always joyful in prayer, morning to night.  
From day one, we’ve been partners in the Good News,  
And I’m confident He’ll finish the work He’ll use.  

Even in chains, you’re all in my heart,  
Sharing in grace, never apart.  
God’s my witness, I long for you all,  
In Christ’s tender mercy, stand tall.  

I pray your love grows, in wisdom and sight,  
Approve what's excellent, walk in the light.  
Filled with righteousness, fruits on display,  
For God’s glory and praise every day.  

Know this, brothers, my trials and chains,  
Spread the Good News, like unbroken chains.  
Even the guards see my bonds are for Christ,  
Inspiring boldness, no fear, they’ve sliced.  

Some preach from envy, some from affection,  
But Christ proclaimed is the only direction.  
Does it matter, pretentious or pure?  
Christ is preached, that’s what I ensure.  

I’ll be saved through your prayers and the Spirit,  
Christ magnified, alive or in death, I’m near it.  
To live is Christ, to die is gain,  
But staying with you, I’ll sustain.  

I’m torn, yet I’ll stay for your joy in faith,  
Your rejoicing in Christ will never abate.  
So live worthy of the Good News, stand firm,  
In one spirit, like a flame, let it burn.  

Don’t fear opponents, proof of their end,  
Your salvation’s from God, your friend.  
Granted to believe and sometimes suffer,  
But in Christ's conflict, you’ll grow tougher.
