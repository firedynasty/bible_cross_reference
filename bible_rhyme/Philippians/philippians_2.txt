If there's encouragement in Christ, if love's real,  
If the Spirit's fellowship, you can feel,  

Make my joy full, be of one mind,  
Love each other, leave rivalry behind.  

Don't act conceited, be humble and true,  
Think of others, not just you.  

Have the mindset Christ displayed,  
Though God in form, as a servant He stayed.  

He humbled Himself, to death He went,  
The cross was His obedient intent.  

God exalted Him, a name to proclaim,  
At Jesus' name, knees bend, tongues exclaim.  

Work out your salvation with trembling fear,  
For God works in you, His pleasure's near.  

Do all things without a grumble or fight,  
Blameless children, shining bright.  

Hold the word of life, be my reason to boast,  
Run not in vain, let God be the host.  

Poured out for your faith, I find joy,  
Rejoice with me, let no sadness destroy.  

Timothy I'll send, to see how you fare,  
He’s like-minded, genuinely cares.  

Others seek their own, but not he,  
Proof of his service, as you can see.  

Epaphroditus, my brother, I send,  
For he longed for you, your friend till the end.  

He was sick, nearly dead, mercy God showed,  
Now he returns, let your joy explode.  

Receive him with joy, honor his name,  
He risked his life, for Christ he came.
