Brothers, listen up, no need for a scroll,  
The Lord's day comes like thieves on patrol.  

When they shout, "Peace and safety!" in the night,  
Destruction hits fast, like birth pains in sight.  

But you're not in darkness, overtaken by fear,  
You're children of light, the day shining clear.  

So stay awake, let’s watch, stay sober,  
Don't sleep through life like those in night’s cover.  

For night is for sleep and for drunken haze,  
But we’re of the day, in faith’s bright blaze.  

Strap on the breastplate, faith and love in motion,  
Wear hope's helmet, salvation’s devotion.  

God called us for salvation, not wrath’s bitter taste,  
Through Jesus who died, our lives won't go to waste.  

Encourage each other, build up, stand tall,  
Respect those who guide, in love honor them all.  

Stay at peace, brothers, in all you do,  
Admonish the wild, support the true.  

Don’t return evil, choose good over hate,  
Always rejoice, pray without debate.  

In all things give thanks, God's will is the deal,  
Keep the Spirit alive, let gratitude heal.  

Test all, hold the good, shun every evil,  
May God’s peace sanctify, keep you primeval.  

He calls you faithful, faithful He’ll be,  
Brothers, pray for us, keep unity.  

Greet each other with love, a holy embrace,  
Let this letter be read, the whole holy place.  

The grace of the Lord, always with you,  
Amen, brothers, in all that you do.
