Couldn't stand it no more, had to leave Athens alone,  
Sent Timothy, our brother, to keep you strong as stone.  

He's there to lift you up, comfort your faith in the fight,  
So no one shakes or falters in the trials of this night.  

You know we're meant for this, it's our appointed way,  
Told you we'd face afflictions, like we did in those days.  

I had to know your faith, couldn't take the tempter's game,  
Feared our work would crumble, all our efforts in vain.  

Then Timothy returned, brought the news of your love,  
You remembered us fondly, sending joy from above.  

Brothers, in our chaos, your faith was our light,  
For now we truly live if you stand in His sight.  

What thanks can we give to God for your grace,  
Praying night and day to see you face to face.  

May God and our Lord guide us back your way,  
Increase in love, abound more every day.  

He'll make your hearts blameless, holy in His name,  
Till Jesus comes with saints, in glory and acclaim.
