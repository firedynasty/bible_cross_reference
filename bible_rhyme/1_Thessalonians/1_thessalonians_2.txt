Yo, brothers, listen up, we came with a mission,  
It wasn’t in vain, we spoke with precision.  

In Philippi, we faced shame and pain,  
But in God's strength, we spoke again.  

Our words weren't lies, no tricks, no game,  
God's truth we shared, with no shame.  

Not pleasing men, but God who sees,  
He tests our hearts, knows our pleas.  

No empty flattery or greedy gain,  
God's our witness, we're not in vain.  

Didn't seek glory, didn’t claim a high throne,  
Gentle like a mother, love deeply shown.  

Sharing not just words, but our soul,  
For you, brothers, made our hearts whole.  

You remember our toil, night and day,  
To bring God's news your way.  

We stood blameless, holy, right,  
As witnesses, in God's sight.  

Like fathers, we urged you all,  
To walk worthy of God's call.  

Thank God without end, you took His Word,  
Not as man's voice, but truth you heard.  

You imitated those before, who faced pain,  
Just like Judea, you stood, same strain.  

From those who silenced, drove us afar,  
Their actions don't please God, they've gone too far.  

But brothers, though apart for a while,  
Our hearts with you, through every trial.  

We tried so hard, to see your face,  
Though Satan blocked, we're still in the race.  

What's our joy, our crown, our gleam?  
It's you, brothers, our shining dream.  

Before the Lord, at His great coming,  
You are our joy, keep the love running.
