Reuben's sons, Israel's firstborn line,  
Lost their birthright, Joseph's time to shine.  
Judah led, his brothers praised,  
But Joseph's place, the birthright stayed.  

Hanoch, Pallu, Hezron, Carmi there,  
Joel's sons, a lineage to declare.  
Shemaiah, Gog, Shimei, and more,  
Micah, Reaiah, Baal to explore.  

Beerah, the prince, by Assyria taken,  
Reubenite chief, his lineage shaken.  
Jeiel and Zechariah, listed in the lore,  
Bela of Azaz, they roamed Gilead's shore.  

To Bashan's land, Gad's sons came,  
Joel, Shapham, and others in name.  
Michael, Meshullam, Sheba in line,  
Seven sons, Abihail's family sign.  

In pastures of Sharon, they spread wide,  
Listed in Jotham and Jeroboam's pride.  
Reuben, Gad, and half-Manasseh stand,  
Forty-four thousand, warriors of the land.  

War with the Hagrites, Jetur and Nodab,  
Helped by God, their victory nabbed.  
Livestock taken, camels and sheep,  
God answered their cry, faith to keep.  

Manasseh's sons, from Bashan they grew,  
Epher and Ishi, warriors true.  
Yet they turned from God, idols in hand,  
Trespassed and lost, by Assyria's command.  

God stirred Pul, Assyria's might,  
Reubenites, Gadites, all in flight.  
Taken to Halah, Habor's flow,  
Their fate sealed, in exile they go.
