David's old, full of days, passed the crown,  
Made Solomon king, brought the leaders around.  

Priests and Levites, all gathered in place,  
Counted the Levites in this sacred space.  

Thirty-eight thousand, from thirty years on,  
Twenty-four thousand for work that must be done.  

"Six thousand judges, officers in line,  
Four thousand doorkeepers, guarding what's divine."  

"Four thousand praised with instruments of praise,  
In divisions by Levi's sons, set in ways."  

Gershon, Kohath, Merari, divided by name,  
Ladan and Shimei in Gershon's claim.  

Jehiel, Zetham, Joel, Shimei's line,  
Shelomoth, Haziel, Haran, they all align.  

Jahath was chief, Zizah was next,  
Jeush and Beriah, fewer sons perplexed.  

Amram, Izhar, Hebron, Uzziel's lot,  
Aaron and Moses, in Amram's plot.  

Aaron set apart, sanctified the most high,  
Burned incense, blessed, to Yahweh testified.  

Moses' sons, Levi's tribe they embrace,  
Gershom and Eliezer, in their rightful place.  

Rehabiah's many, Eliezer's line,  
Izhar's son Shelomith, with Hebron's four in line.  

Micah, Isshiah, Uzziel's sons known,  
Mahli and Mushi, Merari's own.  

Eleazar's daughters wed Kish's sons,  
Mahli, Eder, Jeremoth, Mushi’s ones.  

Levi's clans counted, from twenty years on,  
For Yahweh’s service, with strength and brawn.  

David declared, "Yahweh gives rest,  
In Jerusalem, His dwelling's blessed."  

No more to bear the tabernacle’s weight,  
For Levi's sons, a different fate.  

Serving Aaron’s sons, in holy place,  
Purifying all things, with steadfast grace.  

Morning and evening, Yahweh they praise,  
On Sabbaths, new moons, in feast day displays.  

Keeping the Tent, the holy duty they bear,  
In service to Yahweh, with reverent care.
