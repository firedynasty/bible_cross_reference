David stood up, addressed the throng,  
"Solomon's young, the work is long;  
Chosen by God, this task is grand,  
The palace built by Yahweh's hand.  

Prepared with might, I've gathered gold,  
Silver and bronze, stories told,  
Iron, wood, gems of every hue,  
Marble stones, more than a few.  

From my treasure, gifts I bring,  
Gold and silver for Yahweh’s King.  
Who will offer? Who will give?  
Let's consecrate, let's truly live."  

Princes and captains came with zeal,  
Offered their treasures, the love was real.  
Gold, silver, bronze, iron too,  
Donations from many, freely they flew.  

Precious stones to Jehiel they gave,  
Rejoicing hearts, the people brave;  
With pure hearts, they offered here,  
David danced, joy was near.  

David blessed Yahweh, lifted voice,  
"Our God is great, in Him we rejoice!  
Yours is the power, the glory, the might,  
Riches and honor, day and night.  

All we give is yours to own,  
From your hand, blessings flown.  
We're strangers here, shadows pass,  
Our days like whispers, fleeting, fast.  

God examines hearts, loves the true,  
In uprightness, offerings accrue.  
Let the people keep this fire,  
Hearts prepared, never tire.  

To Solomon, grant wisdom's grace,  
To keep your laws, build this place."  
David turned to the crowd and said,  
"Praise Yahweh," as they bowed their head.  

Sacrifices offered, fires burned bright,  
They celebrated with joy that night.  
Solomon crowned once again,  
With Zadok priest, it marked the reign.  

Solomon sat as king on the throne,  
He ruled the land, not alone;  
Princes and warriors bowed in awe,  
Yahweh's majesty, all saw.  

David's days, forty years long,  
Seven in Hebron, where he belonged.  
Thirty-three in Jerusalem’s light,  
He died rich, full, in Yahweh's sight.  

Recorded deeds, written well,  
Samuel, Nathan, Gad did tell.  
David's story, his reign, his might,  
Over Israel, kingdoms in sight.
