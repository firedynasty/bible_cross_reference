David rose up, Philistines under his boot,  
Took Gath and towns, gave them the recruit.  
Moab fell, became servants, tribute they bring,  
David's victories echoed, like a lion he'd sing.  

Hadadezer of Zobah, David came to defeat,  
By Euphrates he marched, made his enemies retreat.  
Chariots and horsemen, he took them all,  
Hamstrung their steeds, made their power fall.  

Syrians of Damascus, they came to aid,  
But David struck down theirs in a fierce cascade.  
Garrisons placed, Syrians served under his hand,  
Yahweh gave him victory, across the land.  

Gold shields from Hadadezer, to Jerusalem they went,  
Tibhath and Cun's bronze, to Solomon was sent.  
Tou of Hamath, heard David's might,  
Sent Hadoram with gifts, acknowledging the fight.  

Gold, silver, bronze from every land,  
David dedicated to Yahweh, by divine command.  
Edomites in the Valley met Abishai's blade,  
Eighteen thousand struck, their resistance decayed.  

Edomites served, David's reign spread wide,  
Justice and righteousness, his people's guide.  
Joab led the army, Jehoshaphat kept the score,  
Zadok and Abimelech, the priests they bore.  

Shavsha the scribe, Benaiah stood tall,  
Cherethites and Pelethites, at David's call.  
His sons chief officials, the kingdom in peace,  
David's reign, a testament, to war's fierce release.
