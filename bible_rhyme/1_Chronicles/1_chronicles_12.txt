David's in Ziklag, on the run, on the mend,  
Hiding from Saul, but with strong men to defend.  

They're brothers with bows, ambidextrous might,  
Flinging stones, shooting arrows, ready for a fight.  

Ahiezer, Joash, from Gibeah's land,  
Jeziel, Pelet, fierce, with weapons in hand.  

Ishmaiah the leader, thirty men strong,  
Jeremiah, Jahaziel, marching along.  

From Gad came warriors, faces like lions,  
Swift as gazelles, on mountains they're flyin'.  

Ezer the chief, Obadiah the next,  
Mighty men of valor, never perplexed.  

Through swollen Jordan, they charged on through,  
Scattering foes, east and west they flew.  

David asked those who came, "Are you friend or foe?  
If you're here for peace, then let us grow."  

Amasai spoke, "We are yours, son of Jesse,  
Peace to you, your God helps plenty."  

Manasseh joined in, with Philistines they came,  
But they turned Saul's way, risking their name.  

Day by day, warriors gathered 'round,  
An army like God's, they stood their ground.  

To Hebron they came, Judah's men in line,  
Turning Saul's kingdom, by Yahweh's design.  

Judah, Simeon, Levi, standing tall,  
Benjamin, Ephraim, came at David's call.  

Zebulun, Naphtali, ready for war's parade,  
Danites, Asher, their battle array displayed.  

Reuben, Gad, and Manasseh, with instruments of fight,  
Came to make David king with hearts aligned right.  

Three days of feasting, they gathered 'round,  
Provisions abundant, joy in the land profound.  

Bread, wine, cattle, gifts they bring,  
To celebrate David, Israel's king.
