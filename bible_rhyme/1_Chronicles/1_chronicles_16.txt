They brought God's ark, set it center stage,  
In David's tent, amidst holy sage.  
Burnt offerings flared, peace offerings too,  
David blessed the people, God's name shining through.  

Bread, meat, and raisins to each man and wife,  
A feast for all, a moment of life.  
Levites were called, their roles they knew,  
To praise, to thank, for all God would do.  

Asaph with cymbals, Zechariah nearby,  
Strings and harps played, the music soared high.  
Benaiah, Jahaziel, trumpets in hand,  
Sounding before the covenant's stand.  

David commanded thanks on that day,  
"Give thanks to Yahweh," he'd say.  
"Sing praises, tell of His marvelous deeds,  
Seek His strength, fulfill your needs."  

Remember His wonders, Israel's kin,  
His covenant spoken, time and again.  
Promised to Abraham, Isaac too,  
Jacob's inheritance, every word true.  

"From few to many, I led you all,  
Protected by my hand, no harm would befall."  
"Touch not my anointed, my prophets are mine,"  
Reproved the kings, a promise divine.  

"Sing to Yahweh, all earth's expanse,  
Declare His glory, let nations dance.  
Great is Yahweh, above all gods revered,  
He made the heavens, His majesty cleared."  

"Honor and strength, before Him stand,  
Bring offerings, worship, with open hand.  
Let earth and heaven rejoice and sing,  
For Yahweh reigns, let praises ring!"  

"Save us, O God, gather us near,  
In Your name's triumph, quiet all fear."  
Blessed be Yahweh, eternal His name,  
"Save us," they cried, "forever the same."  

The people cried "Amen," praise would not cease,  
Asaph and brothers, their duties in peace.  
Day by day, they ministered there,  
In Yahweh's presence, in constant prayer.  

Morning and evening, offerings raised,  
In holy array, His name be praised.  
David returned, his house to bless,  
In Yahweh's love, they found their rest.
