When time came for kings to march in line,  
Joab led the army, crossed the borderline.  

He struck the land of Ammon, made it fall,  
But David stayed in Jerusalem's hall.  

Joab took Rabbah, brought it to its knees,  
While David sat back, enjoying the breeze.  

Off came the crown from the Ammonite king,  
A talent of gold, a heavy, fine thing.  

With jewels that shimmered, it adorned his head,  
And riches from the city, he then spread.  

He brought out the people, tools in hand,  
Axes, saws—he made a stand.  

To every Ammonite city, he did just the same,  
Then back to Jerusalem, he made his fame.  

War rose with Philistines at Gezer’s gate,  
Sibbecai, strong, sealed Sippai's fate.  

Elhanan stood firm in battle’s roar,  
Took down Lahmi, Goliath’s kin once more.  

At Gath, a giant with fingers and toes that amazed,  
Jonathan, David’s kin, left him dazed.  

Those born to the giant fell by David’s crew,  
By his servants' hands, they got their due.
