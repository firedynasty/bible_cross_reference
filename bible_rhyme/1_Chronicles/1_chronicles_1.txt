Listen up, gather 'round, here's the family line,  
From Adam to Abraham, let's keep it in rhyme.  

Adam had Seth, then came Enosh and Kenan,  
Mahalalel, Jared, Enoch, Methuselah, and Lamech in the plan.  

Noah came next with sons Shem, Ham, and Japheth,  
Japheth's sons Gomer, Magog, and Tubal, no less.  

Gomer had Ashkenaz, Diphath, Togarmah,  
Javan’s kids were Elishah, Kittim, and Rodanim’s drama.  

Ham had Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan,  
Cush's son Nimrod became mighty in the land.  

Mizraim begot Ludim and Anamim,  
Philistines from Casluhim, oh, what a scene!  

Canaan fathered Sidon, Heth, and more,  
Jebusites, Amorites, they all came before.  

Shem had Elam, Asshur, Arpachshad, Lud, and Aram,  
Arpachshad's line led to Eber and Peleg, oh man!  

Eber had Peleg, earth's division, that's key,  
Joktan his brother, with sons like Sheba, you see.  

Shem's line goes on, leading to Abraham,  
Isaac and Ishmael, his sons in the clan.  

Ishmael's sons: Nebaioth, Kedar, Mibsam,  
Adbeel, Dumah, Massa, Hadad, Tema, it ran.  

Abraham’s concubine Keturah bore Zimran, Medan,  
Their line branched to Midian, Ishbak, and Shuah, understand?  

Isaac fathered Esau and Israel,  
Esau had Eliphaz, Reuel, and a tale to tell.  

Eliphaz’s sons: Teman, Omar, Zephi, Kenaz,  
Reuel begot Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, like jazz.  

Kings of Edom reigned before Israel's day,  
Bela, Jobab, Husham, Hadad, they held sway.  

Kings came and went, each in their place,  
Till Hadad reigned last, then Edom’s chiefs took space.  

Chiefs Timna, Aliah, Jetheth, and more,  
Leading Edom’s tribes in the days of yore.  

The family tree spans wide and deep,  
From Adam to nations, this history we keep.
