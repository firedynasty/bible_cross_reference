David sat at home, cozy in cedar's embrace,  
But the ark of Yahweh stayed in a tented space.  
He spoke to Nathan, prophet and friend,  
“I’m in a house, while God’s ark’s got no end.”  

Nathan said, “Do what's in your heart's lane,  
For God’s with you, no need to restrain.”  
But that night, God’s word came to Nathan in sight,  
“Tell David, ‘It’s not you who'll build my house bright.'”  

“I’ve roamed in tents since Israel’s birth,  
From tent to tent, I moved on this earth.  
Did I ask any judge, ‘Build me a house of cedar?’  
Never did I demand such from any leader.”  

“Tell David, ‘I took you from sheep’s pen,  
Raised you up high, above other men.  
With you, I defeated your foes in the land,  
Made your name great, with a strong, mighty hand.’”  

“I’ll plant a place for Israel to stand,  
No more will wickedness trouble this land.  
I’ll subdue enemies, build you a house of might,  
Your son will rule, his kingdom in sight.”  

“He’ll build me a house, his throne will not end,  
I’ll be his father, him I’ll defend.  
My love won’t depart, as it once did before,  
In my kingdom, his throne will endure evermore.”  

Nathan shared these words with the king that night,  
David pondered, in Yahweh's sight.  
“Who am I, God, that you've brought me so far?  
You've lifted my house, like a bright shining star.”  

“You’ve honored your servant, in ways so grand,  
Respected me, lifted me by your hand.  
Yahweh, none like you, no other god compares,  
You redeemed a people, showed them you care.”  

“Israel’s your own, forever to keep,  
Yahweh, you became their God, a promise deep.  
Let your word be firm, as you have proclaimed,  
Let the house of David be forever famed.”  

“Blessed is your servant, with courage, I pray,  
Your promises stand firm, never to sway.  
Yahweh, you’ve blessed, your will, let it be,  
For your blessing remains through eternity.”
