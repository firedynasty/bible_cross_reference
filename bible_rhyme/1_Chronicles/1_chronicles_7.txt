Issachar’s sons roll out, four strong as they soar,  
Tola, Puah, Jashub, Shimron, warriors galore.  
Tola's boys, mighty men in every generation,  
Twenty-two thousand six hundred, David's foundation.  

Uzzi’s boy Izrahiah, five chiefs in line,  
Michael, Obadiah, Joel, Isshiah, they shine.  
With thirty-six thousand, ready for war,  
Brothers in valor, eighty-seven thousand, more.  

Benjamin's sons—Bela, Becher, Jediael—three,  
Bela's sons, five leaders, valorous as can be.  
Ezbon, Uzzi, Uzziel, Jerimoth, Iri stand tall,  
Twenty-two thousand thirty-four, they heed the call.  

Becher's sons count nine, heads of their clan,  
Zemirah to Alemeth, each a mighty man.  
Genealogy listed, twenty thousand strong,  
Ready for battle, where they belong.  

Jediael's next, with sons to the brim,  
Bilhan's boys—Jeush, Benjamin, and kin.  
Seventeen thousand two hundred, fit for the fight,  
Shuppim, Huppim, Ir, Hushim, ready day and night.  

Naphtali's sons—Jahziel, Guni, Jezer, Shallum's line,  
Through Bilhah they descend, warriors who shine.  
Manasseh's sons, Asriel leads the path,  
Machir’s kin, Gilead’s aftermath.  

Machir wed into Huppim, family ties tight,  
Zelophehad's daughters, a unique sight.  
Peresh and Sheresh, born to Maacah’s side,  
Ulam and Rakem, the family's pride.  

Ephraim’s clan, Shuthelah and heirs,  
But tragedy came, as the men of Gath dared.  
Ephraim mourned, comfort from his kin,  
Named his son Beriah, trouble within.  

Sheerah, Ephraim's daughter, builder of lands,  
Beth Horon and Uzzen, built by her hands.  
Line after line, through generations they go,  
Joshua at the end, his legacy aglow.  

Asher’s sons, Imnah, Ishvah, they lead,  
Followed by Ishvi and Beriah, they succeed.  
Twenty-six thousand strong, for service in war,  
Mighty men of valor, Asher's pride in store.
