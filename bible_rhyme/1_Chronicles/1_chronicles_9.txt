Israel's tale, written down in the king's own books,  
Judah's captive to Babylon for disobedient looks.  

First to settle back, in their own land they reside,  
Israel, priests, Levites, temple servants by their side.  

In Jerusalem, children of Judah took their place,  
Benjamin, Ephraim, Manasseh joined the race.  

Uthai, Asaiah, sons of Perez, they stood tall,  
Jeuel and brothers, six hundred ninety in all.  

Benjamin's kin, Sallu and crew, they had the claim,  
Nine hundred fifty-six, heads of households they became.  

Priests like Jedaiah, Jehoiarib, Jachin, bold,  
Azariah, Adaiah, in God's house they took hold.  

Heads of houses, one thousand seven sixty they were,  
Able men serving in God's house without a slur.  

Levites came forth, Shemaiah led the line,  
With Bakbakkar, Heresh, in service, they did shine.  

Gatekeepers like Shallum, at the king's gate they'd stand,  
Over camp, keepers of entry, they had the command.  

Zechariah, Phinehas, in times past they ruled,  
With Yahweh's presence, their leadership fueled.  

Two hundred twelve, chosen to guard and to serve,  
Every seven days, their posts they wouldn't swerve.  

Four chief gatekeepers, Levites with trust in hand,  
Over rooms, over treasuries, in God's house they'd stand.  

In charge of vessels, by count they'd take and give,  
Some over furniture, spices, by faith they lived.  

Mattithiah baked, Kohathites showed the bread,  
Singers of the Levites, in their rooms they spread.  

Jeiel in Gibeon with his family close by,  
Saul's line through Kish, in that lineage lies.  

Father of Jonathan, Saul's legacy strong,  
Micah and sons, in the family throng.  

Generations recorded, Azel's six sons named,  
Their stories, their legacy, in history acclaimed.
