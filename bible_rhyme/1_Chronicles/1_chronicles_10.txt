The Philistines charged, Israel took flight,  
On Mount Gilboa, they fell in the night.  

Chased after Saul, his sons in the fray,  
Jonathan, Abinadab, Malchishua lay.  

The battle was fierce, the arrows flew,  
Saul felt the pain, he knew what to do.  

"Draw your sword," was Saul's grave request,  
But fear stopped his bearer, couldn't protest.  

With his own blade, Saul took his fall,  
His bearer followed, ended it all.  

Saul and sons, all in one breath,  
Together they faced the shadow of death.  

Israel saw, and they left their land,  
Philistines moved in, took command.  

Next day they stripped the slain in pride,  
Saul and sons on the mountain side.  

Armor to idols, head to Dagon’s place,  
Philistines spread news, a victorious case.  

Jabesh Gilead heard of the shame,  
Valiant men rose to reclaim the name.  

Took the bones, buried them deep,  
Under an oak, their memory to keep.  

Saul fell for not heeding divine word,  
For seeking spirits when Yahweh he spurned.  

So Yahweh judged, David was crowned,  
The son of Jesse, new king renowned.
