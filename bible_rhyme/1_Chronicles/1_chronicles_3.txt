David's sons were born in Hebron, a grand sight,  
Amnon first, then Daniel, their future was bright.  

Absalom came third, Maacah's prideful heir,  
Adonijah fourth, Haggith's child with flair.  

Shephatiah fifth, Abital's delight,  
Ithream sixth, by Eglah, shining light.  

Six sons in Hebron, where David's throne lay,  
Seven years and six months, he ruled day by day.  

In Jerusalem, his dynasty grew,  
Shimea, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon too.  

Ibhar, Elishama, Eliphelet came next,  
Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia, the family complexed.  

Elishama, Eliada, Eliphelet again,  
Sons of David, the legacy they extend.  

Tamar, their sister, in the royal line,  
While Solomon's seed would rise and shine.  

Rehoboam, Abijah, Asa, in line,  
Jehoshaphat, Joram, their futures entwine.  

Ahaziah, Joash, Amaziah in place,  
Azariah, Jotham, each took their space.  

Ahaz and Hezekiah, Manasseh's reign,  
Amon, then Josiah, continued the chain.  

Josiah's sons, Johanan to start,  
Jehoiakim, Zedekiah, Shallum took part.  

Jehoiakim's children, Jeconiah, Zedekiah next,  
Jeconiah the captive, with history vexed.  

Shealtiel his first, Malchiram, more came,  
Pedaiah, Shenazzar, Jekamiah by name.  

Hoshama, Nedabiah, sons in their prime,  
Pedaiah fathered Zerubbabel, Shimei in time.  

Zerubbabel's line, Meshullam, Hananiah the claim,  
Shelomith their sister, in their family's name.  

Hashubah, Ohel, five sons, they grew,  
Berechiah, Hasadiah, Jushab Hesed too.  

Hananiah's sons, Pelatiah, Jeshaiah's line,  
Rephaiah, Arnan, Obadiah, Shecaniah in kind.  

Shemaiah, the son of Shecaniah's feat,  
Hattush, Igal, Bariah, Neariah, Shaphat repeat.  

Neariah's three, Elioenai, Hizkiah, Azrikam's plan,  
Elioenai's seven, Hodaviah, Eliashib in the clan.  

Pelaiah, Akkub, Johanan in the fold,  
Delaiah and Anani, their stories told.
