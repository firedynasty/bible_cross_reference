Satan rose up, had a plan in his hand,  
Moved David to count all the people in the land.  
David told Joab and princes, "Go far and wide,  
Count from Beersheba to Dan, all of Israel's pride."  

Joab said, "May Yahweh's people grow great,  
But why count them, King? Don't cause guilt on their fate."  
Yet the king's word stood, Joab went on his quest,  
Across all the land, then back to his nest.  

Joab returned, the census was done,  
One million one hundred thousand men who could run,  
In Judah, four seventy thousand strong,  
Levi and Benjamin, Joab left out, felt wrong.  

God was displeased, struck Israel with fear,  
David cried, "I've sinned, my mistake is clear!  
Take the guilt from your servant, I plead,  
I've done foolishly, seen the error of my deed."  

Yahweh spoke through Gad, "Go to David and say,  
'Three choices I give, pick one for the day:  
Three years of famine, or months before foes,  
Or three days of pestilence — let your answer disclose.'"  

David said, "It's distress I feel in my heart,  
Let me fall into Yahweh's hand, His mercy's a part.  
But never let me fall to man's wrathful plan."  
Yahweh sent a plague, seventy thousand lost in the land.  

An angel at Jerusalem, ready to destroy,  
Yahweh relented, said, "Enough, my envoy."  
David saw the angel, sword drawn in the sky,  
He and the elders in sackcloth, they cried.  

David pleaded, "It was I who sinned, let it be,  
Not these people, O Lord, turn your hand on me."  
The angel told Gad, "Go speak to the king,  
Build an altar to Yahweh on the threshing floor ring."  

David went up as Gad had declared,  
Ornan saw the angel, his sons were scared.  
David approached, Ornan bowed to the ground,  
"Sell me this place, stop the plague that's around."  

Ornan said, "Take it all, oxen, wood, and wheat,  
My king, do what's right, your way is complete."  
But David replied, "No, I will pay,  
Won't offer Yahweh what costs me no way."  

Six hundred shekels of gold weighed and paid,  
An altar was built, offerings made.  
Yahweh answered with fire from the sky,  
The angel sheathed his sword, no more tears to cry.  

David saw Yahweh's answer at the site,  
Sacrifices made under Ornan's light.  
The tabernacle was in Gibeon, high place and grand,  
But David feared the angel's sword, stayed his hand.
