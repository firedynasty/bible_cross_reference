The Korahites stood strong, keepers of the door,  
Meshelemiah and his sons, ready for more.  
Zechariah, Jediael, Zebadiah, they came,  
Jathniel, Elam, Jehohanan, Eliehoenai, each with a name.  

Obed-Edom was blessed, sons he had eight,  
Shemaiah and the rest, they were great.  
Mighty men of valor, Shemaiah's kin,  
Othni, Rephael, Obed, Elzabad, with courage within.  

Sixty-two from Obed-Edom, strong for the call,  
Meshelemiah's eighteen, prepared to stand tall.  
Hosah of Merari had sons on his side,  
Shimri led them, though firstborn defied.  

Casting lots for the gates, east, north, and south,  
Obed-Edom took the storehouse, wisdom from their mouth.  
To the west stood Shuppim and Hosah's crew,  
Watchmen on alert, always something to do.  

Ahijah had watch over treasures so grand,  
For God's house, all the riches on hand.  
Sons of Ladan, Gershonites in line,  
Jehieli's house, treasures divine.  

From Moses to Shebuel, treasures they tended,  
Dedicated by kings, battles had ended.  
David, the captains, the plunder they gave,  
To repair Yahweh's house, the offerings they'd save.  

Chenaniah's sons for Israel's affairs,  
Judges and officers, resolving all cares.  
Hebronites with valor, beyond Jordan's west,  
Hashabiah and brothers, serving the best.  

Jerijah chief among Hebron's might,  
In David's fortieth year, they shone bright.  
Two thousand seven hundred, leaders in their stride,  
Overseeing tribes, with God and king allied.
