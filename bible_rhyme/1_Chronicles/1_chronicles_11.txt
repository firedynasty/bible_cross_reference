Israel's gathered, speaking bold and clear,  
To David in Hebron, "We're your kin, we're near.  
Even with Saul, you led us, we recall,  
God said, 'You'll shepherd, be the prince of all.'"  

Elders met David, a covenant they made,  
Anointed him king, with Yahweh's word conveyed.  
To Jerusalem, they marched with a plan,  
Jebusites defied, but David, he ran.  

"Who strikes first?" David asked, to claim the fame,  
Joab answered swift, earned the captain's name.  
In Zion, David lived, the city bore his name,  
Built it up strong, Joab did the same.  

David grew greater, for Yahweh was his guide,  
With mighty men and Israel by his side.  
Jashobeam, chief, with spear he did thrill,  
Three hundred men he courageously killed.  

Eleazar stood with David, ground to defend,  
Philistines were beaten, victory in the end.  
Three mighty went down to the cave by night,  
Philistine army surrounded, ready to fight.  

David longed for water from Bethlehem's well,  
Three broke through, but he wouldn't drink, you can tell.  
Poured it out to Yahweh, for their sacrifice,  
Said, "I can't drink blood, they've risked their lives."  

Abishai's spear took three hundred down,  
Honored among the three, known in town.  
Benaiah, bold, in a pit with a lion,  
Killed an Egyptian, with courage he's flyin'.  

More honorable than thirty, his name they did hail,  
David set him guard, his loyalty couldn't fail.  
List of mighty men goes long and wide,  
With names like Asahel and Elhanan with pride.  

Joab's brother, Elhanan, and more,  
These were the warriors, ready for war.  
From Asahel to Jaasiel, mighty and bold,  
David's army fierce, the tales are told.
