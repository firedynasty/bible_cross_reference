David called the leaders, the mighty, the strong,  
Gathered in Jerusalem, they all belong.  
"Listen up, my brothers, hear what I say,  
In my heart was a house where God's ark could stay.

But God said, 'David, not for you to build,  
A warrior's hands with blood are filled.'  
Yet from my family, He chose me, the king,  
Judah's line, where praises sing.

Solomon, my son, has been chosen by Name,  
To build God's house, to bring it fame.  
'He'll be My son, I'll be his dad,'  
God said to me, making my heart so glad.

So follow His rules, in God put your trust,  
Possess this land, be righteous and just.  
Solomon, my son, know God deep inside,  
Serve with a pure heart, don't run and hide.

If you seek Him, you'll find Him, it's true,  
But forsake Him, and He'll leave you blue.  
God chose you, hear the call—  
Be strong, build it all."

David gave to Solomon the plans divine,  
For the temple, the courts, the rooms, every line.  
Gold and silver, all weighed right,  
For lamps and bowls that shine so bright.

"All this," David said, "by Yahweh's hand,  
Every detail, I understand.  
Son, be strong, courageous, and stand,  
Yahweh’s with you, a mighty band.

Priests and Levites, ready for the task,  
Every skilled man, just ask.  
Captains and people, at your command,  
To build the house, as God has planned."
