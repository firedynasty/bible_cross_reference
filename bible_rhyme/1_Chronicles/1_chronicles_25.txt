David and his captains, they set the scene,  
Sons of Asaph, Heman, Jeduthun, keen.  
Prophesying with harps, strings, and cymbals clear,  
Skillful sons, each ready, God's voice they’d hear.  

Zaccur, Joseph, Nethaniah, they came through,  
Asharelah under Asaph, prophesied true.  
Under Jeduthun, six sons praised Yahweh's name,  
With harps, thanks, and praise, their hearts aflame.  

Heman’s sons, a long list, Bukkiah leads,  
Fourteen sons, three daughters, where prophecy seeds.  
All under Heman, Yahweh’s house filled with song,  
Cymbals, strings, harps, lifting the praises strong.  

Two hundred eighty-eight, each skilled, they sang,  
Casting lots, each role in harmony rang.  
First lot to Asaph’s Joseph, the second Gedaliah,  
Twelve each, every group, each son, a desire.  

Third to Zaccur, then Izri, the fourth’s call,  
Nethaniah fifth, Bukkiah sixth, in all.  
Seventh Jesharelah, eighth to Jeshaiah’s tunes,  
Ninth to Mattaniah, with Shimei’s boons.  

Tenth was Shimei, Azarel’s eleventh strike,  
Hashabiah twelfth, Shubael’s thirteen alike.  
Mattithiah fourteenth, then Jeremoth’s way,  
Sixteenth to Hananiah, they play.  

Seventeenth, Joshbekashah, Hanani’s next,  
Mallothi nineteenth, their spirits flexed.  
Twentieth to Eliathah, Hothir’s twenty-first,  
Giddalti, twenty-second, with music versed.  

Twenty-third to Mahazioth, Romamti-Ezer closed,  
Each twelve strong, their voices composed.  
In Yahweh’s house, all skillful and aligned,  
Serving with music, their hearts intertwined.
