David huddled with the leaders, he sought their advice,  
"If it's good and God's will, let's make it precise.  
Send word to our brothers all over the lands,  
With priests and the Levites, get them in bands.  

Let's bring back the ark, left in Saul's days,  
All agreed, they said, 'Yeah, this is the way.'  
From Egypt's brook to Hamath's gates wide,  
To bring home the ark, with all Israel's pride.  

To Kiriath Jearim, the sacred place,  
The ark of God's Name, we would embrace.  
On a new cart, it rolled from Abinadab's door,  
Uzza and Ahio, guiding it forth.  

David and Israel, with might they did sing,  
Harps, strings, tambourines, made the air ring.  
But when to Chidon’s threshing place they came,  
Uzza reached out, and Yahweh’s wrath flamed.  

God struck him down, for touching the prize,  
David, displeased, saw the anger in his eyes.  
Called it Perez Uzza, marked with pain,  
"How to bring it home?" David exclaimed.  

So the ark stayed at Obed-Edom's place,  
Three months of blessings, God's favor and grace.  
Yahweh poured blessings on all he had,  
For the ark's presence made the house glad.
