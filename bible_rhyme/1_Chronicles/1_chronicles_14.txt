Hiram sent a crew with cedar trees and skill,  
To build David's house, a king's home on a hill.  

David knew God placed him high on the throne,  
For Israel's sake, not for his alone.  

In Jerusalem, more wives he gained,  
Sons and daughters, his family name proclaimed.  

Shammua, Shobab, Nathan, Solomon,  
Ibhar, Elishua, Elpelet—like a song they run.  

Nogah, Nepheg, Japhia—each a treasure,  
Elishama, Beeliada, Eliphelet, beyond measure.  

The Philistines heard and sought David out,  
Anointed king—his power, they had no doubt.  

In Rephaim's valley, they made a bold raid,  
David heard and to God’s wisdom he prayed.  

"Shall I face the Philistines? Will you deliver them to me?"  
"Go up," God said, "Victory’s yours, like the breaking sea."  

To Baal Perazim, David took the fight,  
Enemies broken, like waters in flight.  

Their idols left behind, David gave the decree,  
Burn them with fire, as a symbol of victory.  

Another raid came, in the valley once more,  
David sought God, like he did before.  

"Don't go up this time, turn around instead,  
Wait by the trees till you hear marching overhead."  

"When you hear that sound, strike with force and fire,  
For God has gone before, as your fierce supplier."  

David followed through, as commanded above,  
From Gibeon to Gezer, he fought with God's love.  

Fame spread wide, fear fell on each land,  
For Yahweh brought victory by David's hand.
