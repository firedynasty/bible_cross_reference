David built up houses in the city's heart,  
A place for God's ark, he made from the start.  
"No one but Levites should carry it, no doubt,  
Yahweh chose them, that's what it's all about."  

He called all Israel to Jerusalem's gate,  
Prepared a place for the ark, didn’t hesitate.  
Gathered Aaron's sons, Levites in file,  
Each clan chief and brothers, marching in style.  

Uriel led Kohath, with a hundred twenty,  
Asaiah with Merari, two twenty plenty.  
Gershom's sons with Joel, one thirty they bring,  
Shemaiah's two hundred, their voices to sing.  

David summoned Zadok and Abiathar,  
Called Levites, spoke clear, he raised the bar.  
"Sanctify yourselves, you heads of the line,  
It's time to bring up the ark, make it shine."  

"Before we failed, God’s anger did rise,  
We’ll do it right now, no more disguise.”  
Levites cleansed, purified for the task,  
To carry God's ark, as Moses did ask.  

The ark on their shoulders, each pole in place,  
The word from Yahweh, they embraced.  
David spoke to Levites, "Music we'll employ,  
Bring out the instruments, lift up in joy."  

Heman, Asaph, Ethan, leading the sound,  
Zechariah and crew, stringed instruments abound.  
Cymbals of bronze, the singers did shout,  
Harps and lyres, their melodies about.  

Chenaniah led the song with his skill,  
Doorkeepers on guard, alert and still.  
Priests with trumpets, sounded the call,  
Bringing the ark, a celebration for all.  

David and the elders, captains in line,  
With joy they marched, the ark divine.  
God helped the Levites, they offered sacrifice,  
Seven bulls, seven rams, a holy device.  

David wore linen, robe shining bright,  
The Levites, singers, all in white.  
Israel shouted, trumpets and strings,  
As the ark entered, their voices did ring.  

Michal watched David, dancing and free,  
But in her heart, disdain was her decree.
