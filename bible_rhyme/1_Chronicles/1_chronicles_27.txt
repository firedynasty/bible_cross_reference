Israel's leaders, standing strong, month by month they marched along,  
Each division twenty-four thousand strong, in their ranks they belong.  

First up, Jashobeam, son of Zabdiel, leading the first month with a tale to tell,  
From the children of Perez, this captain's swell, he leads with pride, and he leads them well.  

Second month's Dodai, the Ahohite, with Mikloth his right-hand knight,  
Their twenty-four thousand ready to fight, standing tall, standing bright.  

Third in line, Benaiah, a priestly son, mighty man, his fame begun,  
With Ammizabad, his son, Benaiah had twenty-four thousand, their work never done.  

Fourth month brings Asahel, Joab's kin, Zebadiah steps in after him,  
Twenty-four thousand more, they begin, moving with courage, these soldiers win.  

Shamhuth the Izrahite takes the fifth month stand,  
Leading his troop of twenty-four grand.  

Ira the Tekoite, sixth in line, with his twenty-four thousand, they shine,  
His father's strength in every spine, they march on, keeping time.  

Helez the Pelonite, Ephraim's pride, claims seventh month, with strength applied,  
Twenty-four thousand at his side, in battle, they will never hide.  

Sibbecai the Hushathite for the eighth month reigns,  
Twenty-four thousand in his chains.  

Ninth month's captain, Abiezer the Anathothite,  
From Benjamin's line, his men are tight.  

Tenth month, Maharai the Netophathite, commands his crew,  
Twenty-four thousand ready, strong and true.  

Benaiah the Pirathonite takes the eleventh role,  
Ephraim's strength, his heart and soul.  

Twelfth month, Heldai the Netophathite, Othniel's kin,  
Twenty-four thousand, they begin.  

Each tribe with leaders, names they boast,  
From Reuben to Dan, each holds their post.  

David, wise, counts not the young,  
"Israel shall grow, like stars among."  

Joab starts a count, but doesn't complete,  
Wrath falls, the task left incomplete.  

Treasures kept by Azmaveth's hand,  
Fields and vineyards, Jonathan's land.  

Shimei, Zabdi, Baal Hanan, too,  
Look after vineyards, olives, trees that grew.  

Over herds and flocks, trusted men stand,  
The king's wealth spread across the land.  

Jonathan, uncle to the king's heir,  
Counselor wise, his advice rare.  

Ahithophel, Hushai, friends to the king,  
Joab leads the army, with might they bring.  

David's kingdom, organized and vast,  
With trusted men, it stands steadfast.
