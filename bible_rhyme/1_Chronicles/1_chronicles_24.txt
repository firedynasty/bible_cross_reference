Aaron's sons were four, Nadab and Abihu,  
Eleazar and Ithamar, they had priestly work to do.  
Nadab and Abihu died, leaving no heirs,  
So Eleazar and Ithamar took over priestly cares.

David with Zadok and Ahimelech in line,  
Divided duties, made everything fine.  
More chiefs from Eleazar's son,  
Sixteen in total, their work had begun.

Ithamar's line had eight heads to show,  
All were chosen fair, by lots they'd go.  
Princes of the sanctuary and God's holy lot,  
Eleazar and Ithamar, their service sought.

Shemaiah the scribe, Levite with a pen,  
Recorded the divisions, again and again.  
Lots drawn in the presence of the king,  
Priests and Levites around, the order they'd bring.

Jehoiarib first, Jedaiah was next,  
Harim third, Seorim fourth in text.  
On through the list, each name to call,  
Twenty-four in all, to serve in the hall.

Into Yahweh's house, by Aaron's decree,  
As God of Israel had set them to be.  
Sons of Levi were ordered too,  
Amram's line, Shubael, Jehdeiah to pursue.

From Rehabiah, Isshiah's the chief,  
Izharites, Shelomoth, Jahath, no grief.  
Hebron's sons, Jeriah, Amariah too,  
Jahaziel, Jekameam, the work they do.

Uzziel had Micah, and Shamir, his lad,  
Micah's brother Isshiah, Zechariah he had.  
Merari's sons were Mahli and Mushi,  
Jaaziah's were Beno, Shoham, their duty.

Mahli's Eleazar had no sons to bear,  
From Kish, Jerahmeel, their names in the air.  
Mushi's descendants, Mahli, Eder, Jerimoth,  
Levitical lines, all in their oath.

As Aaron's line drew lots for their call,  
David, Zadok watched over them all.  
Priests and Levites stood, both young and old,  
In service to God, as the stories were told.
