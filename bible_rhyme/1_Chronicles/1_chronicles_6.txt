Levi's sons were Gershon, Kohath, and Merari,  
Kohath's line: Amram, Izhar, Hebron, Uzziel, a grand story.  

Amram’s kids: Aaron, Moses, Miriam, they lead,  
Aaron's sons: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, Ithamar's seed.  

Eleazar fathered Phinehas, who fathered Abishua,  
Then came Bukki, Uzzi, and Zerahiah, for sure.  

Zerahiah begot Meraioth, who had Amariah to raise,  
Amariah fathered Ahitub, Zadok the priest in those days.  

Zadok birthed Ahimaaz, who fathered Azariah,  
Azariah to Johanan, building the Messiah.  

On and on, the line continues, priests in the holy place,  
Solomon's temple, where they served, each in their grace.  

Aaron’s line offered sacrifices, smoke rose high,  
“Keep the holy fire burning,” God’s command they supply.  

David set them over music, songs of praise they’d sing,  
In the house of Yahweh, their voices would ring.  

Given cities with pasture lands, for the tribes to share,  
To Kohath, Gershom, Merari — fair and square.  

The story of Levi’s sons, serving in their line,  
Faithful in their duties, holding priestly design.  

In captivity, Jehozadak, carried by Babylon's hand,  
But still, the Levites’ legacy in Yahweh’s land would stand.
