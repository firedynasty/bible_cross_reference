"This is Yahweh's spot, the altar for Israel's flame,"  
David spoke, setting up the masons for God's house to frame.  

Gathered foreigners in the land, bricks they laid,  
Iron for nails, bronze unweighed.  

Cedar trees aplenty, Tyre and Sidon did provide,  
For David's vision to be a house worldwide.  

"Solomon's young, the house must amaze,  
I'll prepare it big before my final days."  

Summoned his son, said, "Now it's your turn,  
Build Yahweh's house, let the holy fire burn.  

I wished to build it, but God's words were clear,  
'You've shed blood, too much war, you hear.  

A son of peace will rise, your legacy,  
His name's Solomon, bringing quiet to the sea.  

He'll erect my house, I'll call him my son,  
His throne in Israel will never be done.'"

"Yahweh be with you, build as He planned,  
May wisdom guide you as you lead this land.  

Keep His laws close, and strength will thrive,  
Be strong, don't fear, keep courage alive.  

See, I've gathered gold and silver galore,  
Bronze, iron without weight, timber, and more.  

Workers surround you, skilled in each trade,  
Rise and build, let no moment fade."  

David told the princes, "Help Solomon, don't delay,  
Hasn't Yahweh blessed us, shown us the way?  

Subdue the land, Yahweh's name proclaim,  
Build His sanctuary, magnify His fame.  

Arise and bring the Ark to Yahweh's place,  
His sacred vessels in the holy space."
