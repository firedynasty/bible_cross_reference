Imitate me, just like I follow Christ’s way,  
Remember the traditions, keep 'em tight, don't sway.  

Heads up, man’s led by Christ, woman by her man,  
And Christ by God, that’s the master plan.  

A man who prays with his head covered's wrong,  
For a woman uncovered, it’s like her hair’s gone.  

If she won't cover, might as well cut it all,  
But if that's shameful, let the covering fall.  

Man, in God's image and glory, stands tall,  
Woman shines the man’s glory, that's her call.  

Man wasn't made from woman, woman came from man,  
But together in the Lord, they make the plan.  

Judge for yourselves, should a woman veiled pray?  
Nature shows, long hair for him's not the way.  

For her, long hair's a glory, a covering grand,  
No other custom, in God's assembly, stands.  

When you gather, it ain't the Lord's feast you eat,  
Divisions among you, causing defeat.  

Each takes his own meal, some hunger, some drink,  
"Don't you have homes?" Let that thought sink.  

Received from the Lord, passed to you,  
On betrayal night, Jesus broke bread, said, "Do."  

“Take, eat, this is my body, broken for you,  
Remember me, in what you chew.”  

After supper, He lifted the cup,  
“This is the new covenant, drink up.”  

As you eat and drink, tell of His death,  
But do it right, or you’ll face His wrath.  

Examine yourself, then consume with grace,  
To avoid judgment’s stern embrace.  

Weak and sick are those who don’t discern,  
Punished by the Lord, so we don’t burn.  

When you gather, wait for each other’s time,  
If you’re hungry, eat at home, avoid the crime.  

I'll set the rest in order when I meet you,  
Till then, remember what’s good and true.
