Paul, the apostle, called by God's decree,  
With brother Sosthenes, writing to thee.  

To the saints in Corinth, set apart,  
Grace and peace to you, straight from the heart.  

Thank God for you, in Christ you’re graced,  
Enriched in speech and knowledge, your faith's embraced.  

You're not lacking, gifts you hold tight,  
Waiting for Jesus to reveal His light.  

He’ll keep you blameless to the end,  
God's faithful promise, on Him depend.  

Brothers, I urge, with one voice, unite,  
Let no divisions, keep judgment tight.  

Chloe's folks reported strife,  
“I follow Paul,” “I follow Christ” — causing strife.  

Is Christ split? Was it Paul who died?  
Nah, just Crispus and Gaius I baptized.  

Christ sent me not to baptize, but to proclaim,  
Good news that saves, in His name.  

The cross seems foolish to those lost in night,  
But to us saved, it’s God's power and might.  

God confounds the world's wisdom that fades,  
“Destroy the wise,” as Scripture has laid.  

Jews want signs, Greeks wisdom crave,  
We preach Christ, the crucified, who saves.  

A stumbling block, foolish yet wise,  
God’s weak is stronger than man’s highest highs.  

Not many wise, noble, or strong,  
God chose the lowly, to shame the wrong.  

Boast in the Lord, not flesh or might,  
In Christ, your wisdom, your guiding light.  

“Who boasts, boast in the Lord,” He said,  
In righteousness, sanctified, from the dead.
