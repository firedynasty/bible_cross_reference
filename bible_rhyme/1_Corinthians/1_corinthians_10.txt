Listen up, brothers, don't be unaware,  
Our fathers moved with the cloud, through the sea they did dare.  
Baptized with Moses, in water and sky,  
Ate spiritual food, drank from the rock nearby.  

The Rock was Christ, but God wasn't pleased,  
In the wilderness, many hopes were seized.  
These are examples, so don't desire the wrong,  
Like them, don't lust, keep your heart strong.  

Idolatry's a trap, hear the scripture say,  
"The people ate, drank, then rose up to play."  
No sexual sin, remember the fall,  
Twenty-three thousand gone, heed the call.  

Don't test Christ, don't face the snake's bite,  
Grumbling and death, they weren't in the right.  
These tales were written, to show us the way,  
For the end of the ages, to guide us today.  

Think you're strong? Be careful, don't fall,  
Temptation's common, but God's above all.  
He'll make an escape, so you can endure,  
Flee from idols, keep your motives pure.  

Wise men, listen, judge what I share,  
The cup of blessing, isn't it Christ's care?  
One bread, one body, united we stand,  
Consider Israel, with altar in hand.  

Idols are nothing, but demons they face,  
Don't mix Lord's table with the demon's disgrace.  
Provoke God to jealousy? Are we that bold?  
Not all things build up, though I've been told.  

Seek your neighbor's good, not your own alone,  
Eat what's sold, let your conscience be known.  
"The earth is the Lord's," in fullness, complete,  
But if offered to idols, refrain from that seat.  

Conscience matters, not just your own,  
Why let liberty be judged when thanks are shown?  
Eat or drink, do all to God's glory,  
No stumbling blocks, let love be the story.  

Please all men, not for my gain,  
But for many's profit, salvation sustain.
