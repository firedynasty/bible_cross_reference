Came to you, brothers, no fancy speech,   
Just God's story, simple, within your reach.  
Focused on Christ, his cross, and sacrifice,  
In weakness, I stood, nerves like ice.  

Not words of wisdom, but Spirit's might,  
So your faith rests in God's light.  
Wisdom we share, for the mature in heart,  
Not worldly rulers, whose reigns depart.  

God's wisdom, a mystery set,  
Hidden before the world was met.  
Rulers unaware, they played their part,  
Crucified glory, blind from the start.  

"Eyes didn't see, ears never knew,  
What God prepared for love so true."  
Revealed by Spirit, deep and wide,  
Knowing God's heart, where secrets hide.  

Who knows a man, but his spirit inside?  
So the Spirit of God, in whom we confide.  
Received not world’s spirit, but from above,  
To grasp the gifts of God’s love.  

We speak not with earthly skill,  
But Spirit’s teaching, heart to fill.  
The natural man, he sees not the way,  
Spirit's truth, like night to day.  

The spiritual mind discerns it all,  
Unjudged, answering only to God's call.  
"For who knows the Lord's mind, who can guide?"  
Yet in Christ's mind, we all abide.
