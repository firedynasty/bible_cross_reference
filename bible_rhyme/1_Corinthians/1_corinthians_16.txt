Listen up, about the collection for the saints,  
Just like Galatia, do without complaints.  

Every first day, save what you can,  
So when I arrive, there's no last-minute plan.  

I'll send your gift with letters in hand,  
To Jerusalem, across the land.  

If needed, I'll join for the journey ahead,  
Beside those you trust, as we've said.  

I'll come to you, through Macedonia I'll pass,  
And maybe with you, I'll stay, make it last.  

I hope to linger, not just a quick view,  
"If the Lord permits," I'll be with you.  

In Ephesus, I'll remain till Pentecost,  
An open door waits, though adversaries are crossed.  

If Timothy comes, treat him right,  
He works for the Lord, shining bright.  

Despise him not, but send him in peace,  
For I wait with brothers, till his journey's release.  

Apollos, the brother, I urged him to go,  
But he'll come later, when the time says so.  

Watch and stand firm, in faith hold strong,  
Be courageous and loving, all day long.  

The house of Stephanas, first fruits they bring,  
Serve them well, for the work they cling.  

Stephanas, Fortunatus, and Achaicus came,  
They filled up the gaps, in Jesus' name.  

They refreshed my spirit, and yours too,  
Acknowledge those who are faithful and true.  

The assemblies of Asia send their greeting,  
Aquila and Priscilla, in love, are meeting.  

All the brothers greet you with love's sweet kiss,  
This letter's from Paul, my own hand in this.  

If one loves not the Lord, let him be cursed,  
"Come, Lord!"—in His grace we're immersed.  

May the grace of Jesus be with you all,  
My love in Christ, never to fall. Amen, stand tall.
