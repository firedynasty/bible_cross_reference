Listen up, brothers, I speak to the flesh,  
Not spiritual, newborns in Christ, I confess.  
I gave you milk, no meat in your bowl,  
You weren't ready then, you're still not whole.  

Jealousy, strife, factions cause a mess,  
Aren't you like men, in this worldly dress?  
One shouts, "Paul!" another, "Apollos!" they call,  
Aren't you just fleshly, not spiritual at all?  

Who’s Apollos, who's Paul? Servants for your belief,  
Each one assigned by the Lord, no chief.  
I planted, Apollos gave water divine,  
But the growth, that was God's design.  

The planter, the waterer, they're both the same,  
Their reward comes from the labor they claim.  
We're God's workers, you're His field and His dome,  
Building on Christ, the foundation, our home.  

Foundation’s laid, I've done my part,  
Build with care, let wisdom start.  
Gold, silver, stones, wood, hay, or straw,  
The fire will test, reveal each flaw.  

Work that stands will earn a reward,  
If it burns, you'll suffer, but saved by the Lord.  
You are God's temple, His Spirit inside,  
Destroy His temple? Destruction won't hide.  

Don't be deceived, don't play the fool,  
Seek wisdom from God, not the world’s tool.  
"The wise are caught in their craftiness," He said,  
"Their reasoning's worthless," remember that thread.  

Boast not in men, for all things are yours,  
Paul, Apollos, Cephas, life and more.  
Present or future, all things are claimed,  
You’re Christ’s and Christ is God's, His name proclaimed.
