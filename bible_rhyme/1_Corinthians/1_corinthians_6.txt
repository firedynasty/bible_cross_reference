Why take your neighbor to the unrighteous court?  
The saints will judge the world—so why fall short?  

Don't you know, we'll judge angels high?  
Then handle earthly matters, give it a try.  

Is there no wise one among you?  
Who can resolve disputes, make things right, too?  

Brother against brother, before those who don't believe,  
It's already a defect, can't you perceive?  

Why not be wronged, let it slide?  
But no, you wrong each other with pride.  

The unrighteous won't inherit God's domain,  
Don't be fooled, let me make it plain.  

Once you were lost, but now you're washed and clean,  
Sanctified, justified, in the Spirit unseen.  

"All things lawful," you may say,  
But not all helpful, don't be led astray.  

Foods for the belly, belly for foods,  
God will end both, change your moods.  

Your body, for the Lord, not for the illicit,  
He'll raise us by power, isn't that explicit?  

Your body, a temple, holy, divine,  
You’re not your own, bought on a line.  

Glorify God in body and spirit,  
For both are God's, never forget it.
