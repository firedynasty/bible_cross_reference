Paul wrote, "It's good not to touch, but here's the deal,  
In a world full of lust, let each find their real."  

Man and wife together, affection they share,  
Each body belongs to the other, that's fair.  

"Don't deprive each other, unless for prayer's call,  
Come back, lest Satan tempts one to fall."  

"This isn't command, just advice I give,  
I wish all could live like me, in peace to live."  

But if control's a fight, let them marry, it's key,  
For it's better to marry than burn, you'll agree.  

To the married I say, it's the Lord's command,  
Stay together, don't leave, hold each other's hand.  

If one's unbelieving, yet happy to stay,  
Let peace rule your hearts, don't send them away.  

Sanctified they become, through the bond that's strong,  
If the unbeliever leaves, then let them be gone.  

"God has called us in peace," let that be your guide,  
Live in the calling, in which you abide.  

Circumcised or not, it doesn't matter here,  
Keep God's commands, let that be clear.  

Bondservant or free, in Christ you find grace,  
You were bought at a price, hold fast to your place.  

Concerning the virgins, I speak from trust,  
In these times of trouble, it's good not to rush.  

Are you married? Stay put, are you single? Stay free,  
But if marriage comes, it's no sin, you see.  

Time is short, the world passes away,  
Live for the Lord, in whatever your way.  

Married or single, each has their care,  
Seek to please the Lord, and you'll be aware.  

Be free from distraction, keep your mind clear,  
Decide for yourself, in wisdom persevere.  

Marry if you must, you do well in the end,  
But staying as you are, that too I commend.  

A wife is bound while her husband breathes life,  
But if he passes, she's free from that strife.  

Happier she stays, as she is, I maintain,  
And if God's Spirit's with me, you'll find peace in the same.
