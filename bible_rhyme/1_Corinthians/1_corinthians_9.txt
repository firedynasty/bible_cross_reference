I'm free, an apostle, seen Jesus the Lord,  
You're my work in Him, my proof, my reward.  

If others doubt, you know it's true,  
For you're the seal of what I do.  

Do I get no rights, no drink, no bite?  
Can't I take a wife, live life's delight?  

Do soldiers march without their pay?  
Do farmers plant and turn away?  

The law says don't muzzle the ox as it treads,  
It's for us humans, not just the animal's heads.  

If we sowed spirit, can’t we take your bread?  
Yet we don't claim it, our need instead.  

Temple servers eat from sacred space,  
Those at the altar share the grace.  

God said preachers live from their preach,  
But I don't want my boasting breached.  

If I share Good News, it's not for fame,  
It's my duty, no need for acclaim.  

If I do it willingly, a reward I hold,  
If not, it's a trust, a story told.  

My reward's to preach without a fee,  
Not to misuse my authority.  

Free from all, yet bound to win,  
To Jews, a Jew; to the lawless, kin.  

To the weak, I’m weak, to save at least some,  
I adapt, I shift, wherever they come.  

Run the race for the ultimate prize,  
Exercise control, be wise.  

I run with purpose, not in vain,  
I train my body, endures the strain.  

Lest preaching to others, I face disgrace,  
I strive to finish, to win the race.
