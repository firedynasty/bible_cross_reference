If I speak like angels, or talk like men,  
Without love, I'm just noise, again and again.  

If I see the future, know secrets untold,  
Move mountains with faith, but my heart is cold,  

If I give what I own, even lay my life down,  
Without love, it fades, nothing gained, no crown.  

Love is patient, love is kind,  
No envy, no boasting, keeps no grudge in mind.  

Love won't act wrong, seeks not its own,  
Stays calm, forgives, lets no bitterness be shown.  

It celebrates truth, holds tight, never fails,  
Bears all, believes all, through storms and gales.  

Prophecies will end, tongues will cease,  
Knowledge fades away, but love brings peace.  

For now, we know just part of it all,  
But when completeness comes, the partial will fall.  

As a child, I spoke, I thought, I played,  
But grew to a man, childish ways delayed.  

Now in a mirror, dim shapes we see,  
But soon, face to face, clear it will be.  

Faith, hope, love remain, strong as can be,  
But the greatest of these, love's the key.
