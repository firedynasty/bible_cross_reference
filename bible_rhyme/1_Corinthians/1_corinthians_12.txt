Brothers, listen up, I'm setting the scene,  
Spiritual gifts, let’s keep it clean.  

You once chased idols, led astray,  
But now in Spirit, hear what I say.  

"No one curses Jesus," through Spirit they speak,  
"Jesus is Lord," is the Spirit's peak.  

Gifts are varied, but one Spirit's shown,  
Service different, yet one Lord's throne.  

God works all, in everyone’s sight,  
Manifestation of Spirit, profit's light.  

Wisdom’s word to one, knowledge to another,  
Faith, healing, miracles, prophecy uncover.  

Discerning spirits, tongues that are known,  
Interpretations, Spirit sown.  

The Spirit gives, dividing each part,  
From one body, Christ's unity start.  

Baptized as one, Jew or Greek,  
Free or bond, the Spirit we seek.  

The body’s not single, but members complete,  
Foot can't diss the hand, together they meet.  

Ear can’t envy the eye’s grand place,  
Without each part, the body's a race.  

God set each member, perfect in call,  
Together they stand, none should fall.  

Eye to hand, "I need you, see?"  
Head to feet, "You're part of me."  

Weak parts are essential; honor their role,  
God gives them glory, part of the whole.  

No division, care for each other,  
If one suffers, feel with your brother.  

Rejoice together, in honor shared,  
The body of Christ, each member prepared.  

God set roles: apostles, prophets, and more,  
Teachers, healers, miracles, languages, encore.  

Are all apostles? Can all prophesy?  
Each has a gift, let them all fly.  

Desire the best, let gifts display,  
For there’s a more excellent way.
