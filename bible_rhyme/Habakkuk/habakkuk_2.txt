I stand at my watch, I’m up on the wall,  
Waiting to hear Yahweh's call.  
He said, "Write the vision clear for all to see,  
So even those who run can read and agree.  

The vision's set for an appointed time,  
It’s racing to the end, it’s gonna align.  
Wait, though it may seem to delay,  
It’s surely coming, won’t fade away.  

Look, the proud soul’s all puffed and wrong,  
But the righteous live by faith, they stay strong.  
Wine’s a trap, it makes him roam,  
Desires as deep as Sheol, never at home.  

Nations and peoples he stacks in a heap,  
But soon they'll rise and make him weep.  
"Woe to him who steals and takes,  
How long before his world shakes?"  

Your debtors will rise, make you feel small,  
You’ve plundered nations, now you’ll fall.  
For the blood and violence you’ve spread around,  
The land will echo with a vengeful sound.  

Woe to those who build with evil gain,  
High nests crumble, bringing only pain.  
You’ve brought shame, you’ve cut down souls,  
The walls will cry, the beams speak of your goals.  

"Woe to the city built with blood and sin,  
Yahweh’s watching, can’t keep it in.  
The earth will brim with His glory wide,  
Like waters covering the sea, none can hide.  

"Woe to those who pour out drink,  
To watch their neighbors fall and sink.  
You’ll feel shame, not glory’s light,  
Yahweh’s cup will bring the night.  

Violence to Lebanon, the beasts and the land,  
All the bloodshed, they can’t withstand.  
What’s an idol but a lifeless face?  
It can’t teach, can’t take Yahweh’s place.  

"Woe to him who calls the wood to wake,  
Or tells the silent stone to shake.  
Gold and silver can’t give life,  
No breath within, no end to strife.  

But Yahweh’s in His holy place,  
The earth falls silent before His face!"
