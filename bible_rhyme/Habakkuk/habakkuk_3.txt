Yo, Habakkuk’s in the house, hear the prophet's prayer,  
"Yahweh, I heard your fame, I know you're there."  

In the years renew, make it known, I plea,  
In your wrath, remember mercy, set the captives free.  

God came from Teman, from Paran did He shine,  
Glory filled the heavens, earth sang with a holy line.  

His splendor rose like sunrise, rays from His hand,  
Where power lay hidden, mighty, grand.  

Plague went before Him, pestilence trailed behind,  
He shook the earth, nations trembled, mountains declined.  

Cushan's tents afflicted, Midian shivered with dread,  
"Was Yahweh mad at rivers?" to the sea He sped.  

Uncovered His bow, arrows sworn to fly,  
Split the earth with rivers, mountains feared the sky.  

Waters rose, deep roared, lifted hands on high,  
Sun and moon stood still, arrows lit the sky.  

He marched in wrath, nations felt His ire,  
For His people's salvation, kindled His holy fire.  

Crushed the wicked's head, stripped them bare,  
Pierced them with their own spears, scattered everywhere.  

Trampled the sea with horses, waters churned,  
I heard, my body trembled, soul burned.  

Though the fig tree's barren, no fruit on the vine,  
Yet in Yahweh I’ll rejoice, His joy is mine.  

Yahweh, my strength, makes my feet like a deer,  
In high places He sets me, drives out fear.  

For the music director, stringed instruments play,  
Praise to Yahweh, the Lord, every single day.
