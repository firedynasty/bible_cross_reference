Habakkuk's vision hit his eyes like a bomb,  
"Yahweh, why you silent while violence runs strong?  
I scream out 'Violence!' but where's your salvation?  
I see iniquity, a twisted creation.  

Destruction, violence, strife everywhere,  
The law's paralyzed, justice is rare.  
The wicked surround, the righteous get bent,  
Justice is twisted, not heaven-sent.  

"Look to the nations, watch, be amazed,  
I'm working a work that'll leave you dazed.  
Raising the Chaldeans, bitter and fast,  
Marching the earth, taking lands vast.  

Feared and dreaded, their judgment their own,  
Swifter than leopards, fierce to the bone.  
Horsemen fly like eagles, ready to feast,  
They come for violence, a merciless beast.  

Kings get mocked, princes are jokes,  
Laughs at strongholds, at their walls he pokes.  
Sweeps like the wind, guilty and loud,  
His strength's his god, his head's unbowed."  

"Yahweh, aren't you eternal, my Rock, my Holy One?  
We won't die, you've set him to punish, it's done.  
Pure eyes that can't see evil's grime,  
Why silent while wicked swallow the righteous in time?  

Men like fish, creeping things in the sea,  
Hooked and netted, no ruler, no decree.  
He catches, rejoices, with fish in his net,  
Burns incense to dragnets, living without regret.  

Will he forever empty his net,  
Killing nations, no mercy, no debt?"
