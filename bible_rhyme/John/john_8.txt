Jesus hit the Mount of Olives, early morn,  
To the temple he returned, where the people were drawn.  
He sat and taught, calm and wise,  
The crowd gathered, wanting to realize.  

Scribes and Pharisees brought a woman, caught red-handed,  
"Teacher, we caught her in sin," they demanded.  
"Moses says to stone her, what's your decree?"  
Testing him, trying to trap him, you see.  

Jesus bent down, wrote in the sand,  
Not a word spoken, just his hand.  
They pressed him still, wouldn't let it go,  
He stood and said, "Without sin? Cast the first stone, if so."  

He bent down again, writing on the ground,  
One by one, they left, not a soul around.  
Alone with the woman, he rose and inquired,  
"Where are your accusers? Has no one conspired?"  

"No one, Lord," she replied, trembling and meek,  
"Neither do I condemn you, go, and sin no more," he did speak.  
Then to those gathered, he began to declare,  
"I am the light, follow me if you dare."  

Pharisees questioned, "Your word is untrue,"  
Jesus said, "I know where I'm from, not you.  
You judge by flesh, I judge not,  
But if I did, my judgment's thought."  

They asked, "Where's your Father?" trying to confound,  
"You know neither me, nor the Father around.  
If you knew me, you'd know him too,"  
He spoke in the treasury, his time not due.  

"I'm going away, you'll seek, but die in sin,  
Where I go, you can't come in."  
Confused, they questioned, "Is he planning to die?"  
"You are of this world, I'm from the sky."  

"Unless you believe I am who I say,  
You'll die in your sins, led astray."  
"Who are you?" they asked in surprise,  
"From the start, I told you with no disguise."  

"When you lift up the Son, then you'll know,  
I do as my Father, his words I bestow.  
He sent me, not alone in this fight,  
I do his will, and that's my light."  

Many believed, truth in his tone,  
"If you stay in my word, you'll be free and known."  
"We're Abraham's children, never been slaves,"  
"If you sin, you're bound, but a son saves."  

"The Son makes you free, truly indeed,  
I speak from my Father, you do as you heed."  
Abraham's kin, they claimed with pride,  
Yet sought to kill him, truth denied.  

"If you're Abraham's, do his deeds,  
But you seek my end, your heart misleads."  
"We have God as our Father," they said,  
"If so, you'd love me," Jesus pled.  

"You can't hear me, your ears are shut,  
Your father's the devil, in your rut.  
He lies from the start, no truth in his breath,  
But I speak truth, can't you see the breadth?"  

"Convict me of sin, if there's any there,  
But you can't, 'cause truth is what I declare.  
You're not of God, that's why you don't hear,  
Accusing me falsely, your judgments unclear."  

"You dishonor me, call me a demon's kin,  
But I honor my Father, it's him I’m in.  
Keep my word, and death you'll never taste,  
But your disbelief, what a waste."  

"Greater than Abraham, you say in disbelief?  
If I glorify myself, it's nothing but brief.  
But my Father, he lifts my name,  
You claim to know him, but your words are lame."  

"Abraham rejoiced, he saw my day,  
He was glad, though you push me away."  
"You're not fifty, you've seen him?" they scold,  
"Before Abraham was, I AM," he told.  

Stones in hand, they wanted him gone,  
But he slipped through, his hour not yet drawn.
