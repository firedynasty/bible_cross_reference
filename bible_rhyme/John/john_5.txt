Jesus hit Jerusalem, a feast in the air,  
By the sheep gate, a pool where the sick laid bare.  

Bethesda's the name, five porches wide,  
Sick folks gathered, hoping for a ride.  

An angel stirred the waters, a healing prize,  
But getting in first was the trick to rise.  

A man lay there, sick thirty-eight years long,  
Jesus saw him, knew something was wrong.  

Asked him, "Do you wish to be made well?"  
The man sighed, "No one helps when waters swell."  

Jesus said, "Arise, take your mat, walk tall,"  
The man got up, healed, no longer did he crawl.  

It was Sabbath, the Jews weren't pleased,  
"Carrying that mat? Against the law, indeed!"  

He replied, "The one who healed said to walk,  
Take my mat and go, no need to balk."  

"Who told you this?" they pressed him hard,  
But Jesus had slipped through the temple yard.  

Later Jesus found him, said with grace,  
"You're well now, sin no more, keep pace."  

The man told the Jews, it was Jesus’ name,  
For this, they plotted to bring him shame.  

Jesus spoke, "My Father works, so do I,"  
They sought him more, claiming blasphemy's lie.  

But Jesus laid it down, "The Son sees the Father work,  
And does the same, in this truth, don't shirk."  

"The Father loves the Son, shows him all,  
Greater works are coming, you'll be in awe."  

"As the Father raises up the dead,  
So does the Son, with life he spreads."  

Judgment's given to the Son alone,  
Honor him, as you do the Father's throne.  

"Hear my word, believe, gain eternal life,  
Pass from death to life, leave behind strife."  

The dead will hear, the time's at hand,  
The Son's voice calls, life to every land.  

Authority’s his, to judge and to act,  
A son of man, that’s the fact.  

"Don't be amazed, the time will come,  
Tombs will open, life's rhythm will hum."  

Good to life’s resurrection, bad to judgment’s day,  
Listen and live, find the righteous way.  

"On my own, I do nothing at all,  
I judge as I hear, righteous my call."  

"If I speak of myself, my witness lacks,  
Another testifies, truth fills the cracks."  

"You sent to John, he spoke the truth,  
But I've got a testimony tougher than proof."  

"The works I do, from the Father’s hand,  
Proclaim I've been sent, the truth will stand."  

"You search the Scriptures, seeking life,  
They point to me, but here’s your strife."  

"You won't come to me for life,  
Missing God's love, full of strife."  

"Came in my Father’s name, still you don't receive,  
But another comes, and him you believe."  

"I won't accuse, Moses holds the line,  
He spoke of me, don't miss the sign."  

"Believe Moses, then believe me too,  
Read his words, see what's true."
