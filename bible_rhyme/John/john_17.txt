Looking to heaven, Jesus raised His gaze,  
"Father, it's time for your glory to blaze.  

Glorify the Son, let His light shine bright,  
He glorifies You, in the heavens' sight.  

You gave Him power, authority over flesh,  
To grant eternal life, a gift so fresh.  

Eternal life's knowing You, the one true God,  
And knowing the Christ, through the path He trod.  

On earth, I showed Your glory, Your work was done,  
Now let me share the glory we had before time begun.  

Revealed Your name to those You gave to me,  
They kept Your word, and now they see.  

They know the truth, Your gifts to me,  
I've shared with them, now they believe.  

"I pray for them, not the world at large,  
But those You gave me, under Your charge.  

All mine is Yours, and Yours is mine,  
In them, I'm glorified, divine.  

I leave the world, but they remain,  
Holy Father, keep them, one in Your name.  

While I was here, I kept them true,  
Except the lost one, as Scripture knew.  

Now I come to You, for joy to fill,  
Your word in them, to do Your will.  

The world hates them, they're not of its fold,  
Keep them from evil, as stories unfold.  

Sanctify them with truth, Your word as guide,  
As You sent me, I send them with pride.  

For their sake, I sanctified,  
That in truth, they’re purified.  

I pray not just for them on the scene,  
But all believers, those unseen.  

Let them be one, as we are one,  
So the world knows Your love's never done.  

They have my glory, unified,  
Perfected in love, worldwide.  

"Father, I wish they'd see my place,  
The glory You gave, the love's embrace.  

The world may not know, but I do,  
And they know You sent me through.  

Made Your name known, and it will remain,  
So Your love in them, forever sustain."
