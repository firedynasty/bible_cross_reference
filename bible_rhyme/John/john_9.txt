Walking by, Jesus saw a blind man's plight,  
Disciples asked, "Who sinned, him or his parents, alright?"  

Jesus said, "Neither, it's not about blame,  
But to show God's works, that's the aim of this game.  

While it's day, I must work, 'cause night will fall,  
While I'm in the world, I'm the light for all."  

He spat on the ground, made mud with his hand,  
Covered the blind man's eyes with dirt and sand.  

He said, "Go wash in Siloam's pool,  
So the man went, washed, and came back cool."  

Neighbors stared, said, "Isn't that the guy?  
The one who sat and begged, couldn't see the sky?"  

Some said, "It's him," others said, "No way,"  
He said, "I'm that man, don't be led astray."  

They asked him, "How did you gain your sight?"  
He replied, "Jesus did it, made my world bright."  

They asked, "Where's he now?" He said, "I don’t know,"  
Brought him to the Pharisees, to see how it would go.  

It was the Sabbath, they questioned him again,  
"He put mud on my eyes, I washed, and then—"  

Some Pharisees said, "He's not from God,  
He breaks the Sabbath, that's quite odd."  

Others said, "How can a sinner do such things?"  
Division spread, what truth this brings.  

Asked the blind man, "What do you say?"  
"He is a prophet," he replied without delay.  

The Jews doubted, called his parents in,  
"Is this your son, who was blind since birth began?"  

Parents answered, "Yes, but how he sees,  
We don't know, he's of age, ask him, please."  

Fear gripped their hearts, afraid of the Jews,  
They'd agreed to expel those confessing Christ's news.  

Called him again, "Give God glory! This man's a sinner."  
He said, "I don't know that, but now I'm a winner."  

They pressed him more, "Tell us how he did it,"  
"I told you already, but you don't admit it."  

They mocked, "You're his disciple, we follow Moses' clue,"  
"We know God's voice, not this man you view."  

He said, "That's strange, you don't know his start,  
Yet he opened my eyes, that's a work of art."  

"God hears those who worship and do His will,  
No one born blind's been healed, that was His skill."  

"If he's not from God, he'd do nothing," he shared,  
They threw him out, their arrogance bared.  

Jesus found him, asked, "Do you believe in the Son?"  
"Who is he, Lord, that I may worship the one?"  

"You've seen him, it's me," Jesus did reveal,  
"Lord, I believe," the man did kneel.  

Jesus spoke, "I came to judge, for this reason I stay,  
The blind will see, the seers led astray."  

Pharisees asked, "Are we blind too?"  
He said, "If you were, you’d be clean and true."  

"But you claim to see, so your sin remains,  
In your sight, truth’s shadowed, tied in chains."
