"Don't let your heart be troubled, just believe,  
In God, in me, trust and receive.  

In my Father's house, many homes await,  
If it weren't true, I'd change the debate.  

I'm going to prepare a place, a space for you,  
I'll return and take you where you can be too.  

You know the way, the path lies clear."  
Thomas cried out, "We're lost, Lord, steer."  

Jesus replied, "I'm the way, truth, life,  
With me, to the Father, no more strife.  

To know me is to know Him, see His face."  
Philip said, "Show us, we'll rest our case."  

Jesus asked, "Philip, don't you see?  
Seeing me is seeing the Father, believe in me.  

I speak not alone, the Father works through,  
Believe or see what my works can do.  

Who believes in me will do works profound,  
Greater things, because to my Father I'm bound.  

Ask in my name, I'll do what you pray,  
To glorify the Father in every way.  

Love me, keep commands I give,  
I'll ask the Father, for the Spirit to live.  

The world can't see Him, but you know His call,  
He lives with you, inside you all.  

I won't leave you orphans, I'll come back around,  
The world won't see me, but you'll hear my sound.  

In that day, you'll know, it's all aligned,  
I'm in the Father, and you're intertwined.  

Keep commands, love me, love comes from the Father,  
I'll show myself, love's the truth we gather."  

Judas (not Iscariot) asked, "Why not the world?"  
"If you love me," said Jesus, "truth's unfurled.  

Keep my word, my Father and I will stay,  
Make our home, where love leads the way.  

Words you hear are the Father's, not mine,  
The Spirit will teach, remind you of the line.  

Peace I leave with you, not worldly, but pure,  
Don't be troubled, let peace endure.  

You've heard me say, 'I go, I come,' rejoice and cheer,  
Because I'm with my Father, don't fear.  

I've told you before, so when it comes to pass,  
Your faith will stand, strong and steadfast.  

I won't talk much longer, the prince comes, you see,  
But he holds nothing, no claim in me.  

The world will know my love for the Father shines,  
As He commands, I follow His signs.  

Arise, let's go, it's time to move,  
In love and truth, let's find our groove."
