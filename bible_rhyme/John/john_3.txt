Nicodemus came in the dark of night,  
Said, "Rabbi, we know you're shining bright.  
God's with you, no doubt, from what we see,  
The signs you do, they set you free."

Jesus replied, "You gotta be born anew,  
Or God's Kingdom's hidden from your view."  
Nicodemus puzzled, "Born again? How so?  
Can one return to the womb they know?"

Jesus said, "Born of water and the Spirit's the way,  
That's how you enter the Kingdom one day.  
Flesh births flesh, Spirit births spirit's might,  
Don't be amazed by this new birthright.  

The wind blows, you hear it, don't know its lane,  
So is one born of Spirit, can't be contained."  
Nicodemus asked, "How can this unfold?"  
Jesus said, "Teacher of Israel, not so bold?  

We speak what we know, testify we will,  
Yet you're blind to the witness, standing still.  
If earthly things bring disbelief in your heart,  
How will you grasp heavenly things' art?  

No one's ascended, none but the Son,  
To Heaven, from Heaven, the journey's begun.  
Moses lifted the serpent for all to survive,  
Son of Man must be lifted so believers thrive.  

For God so loved the world, He gave His only Son,  
Believe in Him, eternal life's begun.  
Not sent to judge but to save,  
Through Him, the world won't face the grave.  

Believe and be free, judged you are not,  
Disbelieve, you're judged on the spot.  
Light's come to the world, yet men choose the night,  
Hiding from truth, shunning the light.  

But truth-lovers step into the sun’s embrace,  
Their deeds in God find their rightful place."  
Jesus then with disciples in Judea stayed,  
Baptizing, in water they waded and prayed.  

John was nearby, baptizing as well,  
In Enon’s waters, stories they’d tell.  
His disciples argued with the Jews one day,  
About purification, and the proper way.  

They asked John, "The one beyond the stream,  
He's baptizing, it's like a dream."  
John replied, "Nothing's ours unless above,  
I'm not the Christ, but sent for His love.  

The bridegroom's here, I'm just the friend,  
Rejoicing in the voice, joy without end.  
He must grow greater, I must shrink,  
From above is He, above all we think.  

He speaks of Heaven, yet few believe,  
Those who do, find truth they receive.  
God’s words through Him, Spirit without measure,  
The Father's love, His Son is the treasure.  

Believe in the Son, life eternal you gain,  
Disobey, and God's wrath will remain."
