"I'm the true vine, and my Father farms the land,  
Branches that don't bear fruit, He'll take them in hand.  
But those that do, He'll prune and refine,  
So more fruit will grow, stronger on the vine.  

You're already clean through the words I've shared,  
Stay with me, as a branch, if you truly care.  
"Remain in me, and I'll remain in you,"  
A branch can't bear fruit, if it's cut from the root.  

I'm the vine, you're the branches, hear my shout,  
Stay with me, you'll bear fruit, without me, there's drought.  
But if one drifts away, it withers and burns,  
Gathered and thrown out, no return.  

If you stay in me, and my words in your heart,  
Ask what you desire, I'll do my part.  
"My Father's glorified when you bear fruit,  
Become my disciples, grounded in truth.  

The Father's love for me is massive and true,  
Stay in my love, it's my gift to you.  
Keep my commands, remain in love’s embrace,  
Just as I’ve done, in my Father’s grace.  

I've told you all this to fill you with joy,  
That joy’s yours to keep, none can destroy.  
"Here's my command, love one another,  
Just like I’ve loved you, sister and brother.  

No love is greater than laying down life,  
For friends, you are cherished, beyond all strife.  
You’re my friends if you do what I say,  
No longer servants, friends from this day.  

I chose you, appointed you, to bear fruit that remains,  
Ask in my name, and the gift you’ll attain.  
"This command I give, so love each other,  
Through trials and hate, hold tight to one another.  

If the world hates you, remember it hated me first,  
Not of this world, it’ll never quench your thirst.  
A servant’s not greater than the lord, they’ll say,  
If they persecuted me, you’ll face it too, in your day.  

They’ll do it for my name, unaware of who sent me,  
Without my words, they’d remain sin-free.  
"He who hates me, hates my Father too,"  
They’ve seen my works, but hatred grew.  

But this fulfills what's written in their law,  
'Hated without cause,' they can't ignore.  
"When the Counselor comes, from the Father He’ll pour,  
The Spirit of Truth, revealing more.  

You’ll testify too, you’ve been with me from the start,  
Together we’ll share, truth from the heart."
