Yo, the scene is set, Sea of Tiberias in view,  
Jesus showed up again, crew didn’t have a clue.  

Peter’s like, “I’m going fishing,” got the nods from the crew,  
They piled in the boat; had a job to do.  

But all night long, not a fish in sight,  
Day breaks, Jesus on the beach, shining bright.  

He called out, “Children, got anything to eat?”  
“No, nothing," they replied in defeat.  

“Cast your net on the boat’s right side,” He said,  
They pulled up so many fish, their arms nearly bled.  

John whispers to Peter, “Look, it’s the Lord!”  
Pete jumps in the sea, couldn’t be ignored.  

The rest hauled the fish, a massive net load,  
Onshore, a fire with bread and fish showed.  

“Bring me some fish,” Jesus said, calm and neat,  
Peter dragged the net, fish count complete.  

“Come and eat breakfast,” Jesus invited,  
They knew it was Him, their hearts ignited.  

Jesus broke the bread, gave it around,  
Third time He appeared, risen, heaven-bound.  

After they ate, Jesus spoke to Pete,  
“Simon, do you love me more than these at your feet?”  

“Yes, Lord, you know I do,” Pete replied,  
“Feed my lambs,” Jesus said, eyes open wide.  

Again, He asked, “Do you love me, Simon, son of John?”  
“Yes, Lord,” Pete said, “Our bond's never gone.”  

“Tend my sheep,” Jesus directed anew,  
Third time He asked, cutting Pete through.  

“Lord, you know all things, my love is true,”  
“Feed my sheep,” was what Jesus bade him to do.  

“When you were young, you dressed as you pleased,  
When you’re old, by others you’ll be seized.”  

Signifying Pete’s path—glory to God,  
“Follow me,” Jesus said, direction unflawed.  

Peter turned, saw the disciple who leaned,  
“Lord, what about him?” queried, intrigued.  

“If I want him to stay until I return,  
What’s it to you?” Jesus said, lesson to learn.  

Rumors spread, but truth is the aim,  
The disciple’s witness, forever the same.  

If all Jesus did were written down,  
The world couldn’t hold all, not a single town.
