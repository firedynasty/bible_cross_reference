Third day in Cana, the wedding was hot,  
Jesus and disciples, they took their spot.  

Mary saw the wine was running dry,  
Told Jesus, "No wine, oh my!"  

Jesus replied, "Woman, why involve me?  
My hour's not come, just let it be."  

Mary to the servants, "Do what he says,  
Listen to him, no delays."  

Six stone jars, purifying ways,  
Jesus had plans in those days.  

"Fill them with water," he gave the command,  
The servants filled, no idle hand.  

"Draw some out," he said with a grin,  
To the feast's ruler, the new wine went in.  

The ruler tasted, eyes opened wide,  
"Why save the best, till after the tide?"  

First sign in Cana, his glory revealed,  
Disciples believed, their faith was sealed.  

Off to Capernaum, they went in peace,  
Few days resting, the journey'd cease.  

Passover's time, Jerusalem's call,  
In the temple, saw merchants in thrall.  

He made a whip, cords in his hand,  
Drove out the sellers, took a stand.  

"Don't make my Father's house a trade,  
Take it out, my point is made!"  

Disciples recalled, "Zeal devours me,"  
Righteous anger, plain to see.  

The Jews questioned, "What sign will you bring?"  
"Destroy this temple, three days, a new thing."  

They doubted, "Forty-six years, it took!"  
But Jesus spoke of his own outlook.  

When he rose, they remembered, clear,  
Believed his words, held them dear.  

At Passover feast, crowds were awed,  
Belief in his name, they applauded.  

But Jesus held back, trust withheld,  
He knew all hearts, where secrets dwelled.
