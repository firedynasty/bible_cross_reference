In the beginning was the Word, chilling with God,  
The Word was God, together from the start, no facade.  
Everything made through Him, all creation in His sway,  
Life in Him, shining light, that darkness can't slay.  

A man named John, sent to testify 'bout the light,  
Not the light himself, but pointing out what's bright.  
The true light was coming, into the world it came,  
The world made by Him, yet didn't recognize His name.  

He came to His own, they turned Him away,  
But those who believed got a new family that day.  
Born not of blood, flesh, or man's decree,  
But of God — that's the key, indeed, truly free.  

The Word became flesh, lived among our kind,  
Glory from the Father, full of grace that binds.  
John shouted, “This is the one who comes after me,  
Greater than I, 'cause He was, 'fore I could be.”  

From His fullness, grace upon grace we receive,  
The law through Moses, but grace through Jesus, believe.  
God unseen, but the Son makes Him clear,  
John's testimony, "Who are you?" they cheer.  

"I am not the Christ," John said, standing strong,  
"Not Elijah, not the prophet, you've got it wrong."  
"I'm a voice in the wilderness, clearing the way,  
For the Lord's coming, as Isaiah would say."  

Pharisees questioned, "Why baptize, what's the deal?"  
John answered, "I baptize in water, but soon you'll feel,  
One who stands among you, greater by far,  
I'm not worthy to untie His sandals, He's the star."  

In Bethany, John baptized, near the Jordan's side,  
Saw Jesus approach, "Behold the Lamb," he cried,  
"Who takes away world sin, the one I spoke of,  
The Spirit descended on Him, like a dove from above."  

Two disciples heard, and followed the Lamb that day,  
Jesus turned, asked, "What seek you on your way?"  
"Rabbi, where do you stay?" they sought to know,  
"Come and see," He replied, as the sun dipped low.  

Andrew told Simon, "Found the Messiah, don't delay,"  
To Jesus he went, who renamed him Cephas that day.  
Next day, Jesus found Philip, "Follow me," He said,  
Philip found Nathanael, "It's Jesus, let's tread."  

"Can anything good from Nazareth come?" Nathanael did query,  
"Come and see," Philip invited, not weary.  
Jesus saw him coming, spoke of truth with no deceit,  
"How do you know me?" Nathanael asked, amazed at His feat.  

"Under the fig tree, I saw you, before Philip's call,"  
Nathanael declared, "Rabbi, you're the King of all!"  
"Believe 'cause I saw you there? Greater things you'll see,  
Heaven open, angels dance, on the Son of Man, you'll agree."
