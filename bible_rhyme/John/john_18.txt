Jesus spoke and then he went,  
With his crew past the Kidron, where time was spent.  
Into the garden, peaceful, pure,  
Judas knew the place for sure.  

Judas came with a soldier's might,  
Lanterns, torches filled the night.  
Jesus, knowing what would be,  
Stepped forward, spoke, "Who you seek?"  

"Jesus of Nazareth," they replied,  
"I am he," he said with pride.  
Judas stood with them, betrayal set,  
When Jesus spoke, they stumbled, yet.  

Asked again, they said his name,  
"I told you, it's me, let them go, no blame."  
Peter drew a sword, bold, not meek,  
Cut off an ear, Malchus shrieked.  

"Put it away," Jesus said with sigh,  
"The Father’s cup, should I not try?"  
They seized him, bound him, led away,  
To Annas first, where they'd sway.  

Peter followed, heart unsure,  
While Jesus faced trials to endure.  
At the door, a maid did ask,  
"Are you one of his?" An impossible task.  

"I’m not," Peter said in fear,  
Warming by the fire, the end was near.  
The high priest questioned, seeking blame,  
But Jesus spoke, he knew no shame.  

"Spoke in open, nothing to hide,  
Ask those who listened far and wide."  
A slap on his face, a challenge in air,  
"If I spoke wrong, why do you dare?"  

To Caiaphas, Jesus was led,  
Peter denied again, as the rooster said.  
Early morn, to the Praetorium's hall,  
They sought a way for his fall.  

Pilate asked, "What’s his crime?"  
"Not ours to judge, not this time."  
Back to Pilate, “Are you the King?”  
Jesus answered, truth in his ring.  

"My Kingdom's not of worldly space,  
Else my servants would embrace."  
Pilate puzzled, “A king you might be?”  
"Born for truth, those who hear, see."  

"What is truth?" Pilate queried, lost,  
To the crowd, weighing the cost.  
Custom's call, one man to free,  
"Barabbas, not him," they chose decree.  

So the tale of Jesus unfolds,  
In betrayal, denial, a story told.  
Yet truth and purpose in his voice,  
The people now made their choice.
