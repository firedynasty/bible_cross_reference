Six days before Passover, Jesus came to town,  
Bethany, where Lazarus, raised from the ground.  

Martha served dinner, Lazarus at the seat,  
Mary took nard, anointed His feet.  

The scent filled the room, fragrant and strong,  
Judas questioned, "Why waste? Why not pass it along?"  

But he didn't care for the poor, it was his scheme,  
He held the money box, pursuing his dream.  

Jesus said, "Leave her be, she’s done this for me,  
The poor you’ll always have, but soon I’ll not be."  

Crowds gathered round, hearing He was near,  
Not just for Jesus, but Lazarus to peer.  

Priests conspired, Lazarus to kill,  
Seeing people believe, their hearts filled with chill.  

Next day, the feast, people gathered, palm in hand,  
"Hosanna! King of Israel!" spread across the land.  

Jesus found a donkey, a young colt to ride,  
"Don’t fear, Zion, your King arrives," He cried.  

Disciples puzzled, meaning not yet clear,  
Till glory revealed, and they saw it all here.  

Lazarus’ story spread far and wide,  
The crowd believed, from death he’d been pried.  

Pharisees murmured, "See, we gain none,  
The world follows Him, what have we done?"  

Greeks sought Him out, eager to see,  
Philip and Andrew brought them to Thee.  

"The hour has come," said the Son of Man,  
"Grain must die to live, that’s the plan."  

"Love life, and lose it; hate life, it’s kept,  
Serve me, follow me, the Father accepts."  

"My soul is troubled, shall I ask to be free?  
No, for this purpose, I came to be."  

"Father, glorify!" A voice thundered back,  
"Glorified once, and again, on this track."  

Some said it thundered, others, an angel's speak,  
Jesus explained, "It's for you that I speak."  

"Judgment comes, the prince cast out,  
Lifted up, I’ll draw all about."  

Questions arose, "How can this be?  
The Christ remains—what is this decree?"  

"Walk in the light, don’t let darkness overtake,  
Believe in the light, children of light, partake."  

Though signs were given, belief was rare,  
Isaiah's prophecy fulfilled, it was declared.  

Hearts were hardened, eyes kept blind,  
Even with glory, many stayed behind.  

Secret believers feared the Pharisees' might,  
Loved man’s praise over God’s light.  

Jesus cried out, "Believe in me, see the One above,  
He who sent me, I come with His love."  

"I am the light, banishing night,  
Believe in me, step into the light."  

Reject me now, but hear this call,  
Words I’ve spoken will judge us all.  

For not of myself did I declare,  
But of the Father’s love, His word I share."
