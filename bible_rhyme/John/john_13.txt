Before the feast, when the time had come,  
Jesus knew to the Father he'd run.  
Loved his own to the very end,  
In the heart of Judas, betrayal would send.  

Jesus knew all was in his hands,  
Came from God, fulfilling His plans.  
Rose from supper, garments aside,  
Took a towel, wrapped with pride.  

Water in basin, disciples’ feet he cleaned,  
With the towel, deeply it gleaned.  
Peter asked, "Lord, wash my feet?"  
Jesus replied, "Later you'll know the feat."  

Peter exclaimed, "Never my feet, no way!"  
Jesus said, "Without it, you can't stay."  
"Wash my hands and head too!" cried the man,  
"Already clean, but your feet... that's the plan."  

For one would betray, not all were clean,  
Jesus knew the script and what it would mean.  
Finished washing, he sat down again,  
Asked, "Do you know what I've done, my friends?"  

“Teacher and Lord, you call me right,  
I washed your feet, show love's true light.  
Follow my example, do as I do,  
A servant's no greater, neither are you.”  

"Blessed if you heed, these words to keep,  
Not all I've chosen will hear me speak.  
Scripture fulfilled, heel lifted high,  
So when it happens, you’ll know why."  

Jesus troubled, spirit deep in the fray,  
Said, "One of you will betray."  
Disciples puzzled, wondering who,  
Loved one leaned, asking, "Who, Lord, who?"  

Jesus dipped bread, gave it to Judas there,  
Satan entered, intentions laid bare.  
Said, "Do it quickly," but none understood,  
Thought of the feast, or helping the poor’s good.  

Judas left in the night’s dark shade,  
Jesus spoke of glory displayed.  
Said, "Little children, love one another,  
By this they'll know, you’re sisters and brothers."  

Peter asked, "Lord, where do you go?"  
Jesus said, "Follow later, but now, no."  
Peter insisted, "I'll lay down my life."  
"Will you?" said Jesus, foretelling strife.  

"The rooster won't crow till you've denied,  
Three times you'll say you don't abide."
