The Pharisees heard, Jesus on the rise,  
More disciples than John, took them by surprise.  
Though He didn't baptize, His crew got it done,  
Left Judea, Galilee's where He'd run.  

Through Samaria, had to make the pass,  
In Sychar, by Jacob's well, He took a rest at last.  
A woman came to draw, "Give me a drink," He said,  
Disciples in town, getting food and bread.  

"You're a Jew, I'm Samaritan, why ask me?"  
"If you knew God's gift, you'd ask for living water free."  
"No bucket, the well's deep, how can you provide?  
Are you greater than Jacob, our ancestor's pride?"  

"Drink from this well, you'll thirst once more,  
My water springs eternal, life's core."  
"Give me this drink, no more thirst, no more trip,"  
"Call your husband," He said, with a twist to His lip.  

"No husband," she said. "True," He replied,  
"Five you've had, the one now's not your guide."  
"I see you're a prophet," she began to declare,  
"Where we worship, mountain or city square?"  

"Woman, the hour's near, it's not about place,  
True worship's in spirit, truth's what we embrace."  
"I know Messiah's coming, the one called Christ,"  
He answered, "I am He, the light and the might."  

Disciples returned, wondered why He'd speak,  
No one asked, their thoughts they didn't leak.  
She left her jar, ran to town to say,  
"Could this be the Christ? Come and see today!"  

Disciples urged, "Rabbi, have a bite,"  
"My food's His will, work in His light."  
"You say four months, harvest's on the way,  
Look now, fields ripe, ready to gather today."  

Samaria believed from her story told,  
Came to Him, two days He consoled.  
Many believed from His word's might,  
"This is the Christ, Savior, our light."  

From there to Galilee, He made His way,  
Prophet's no honor, in hometown they say.  
Galileans welcomed, witness to His feats,  
Miracles in Jerusalem, remembered the treats.  

In Cana again, where water turned to wine,  
A nobleman's son, sick, on the decline.  
"Come heal my child," begged the nobleman,  
"Signs and wonders you need?" questioned the Son of Man.  

"Believe," said Jesus, "Your son will live,"  
The man trusted, with faith to give.  
Servants met him, "Your child’s alive!"  
"What hour?" he asked, eager to derive.  

"Seventh hour, fever took flight,"  
Jesus' words, His son's light.  
He believed, his household too,  
Second sign in Galilee, faith grew.
