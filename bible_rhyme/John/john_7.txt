Jesus walked in Galilee, keeping low,  
In Judea, they wished to see his flow.  

Feast of Booths was near, a festive call,  
His brothers urged him, "Show your works to all."  

"Go on to Judea," they said, "make it known,  
Reveal your wonders, don't keep them alone."  

Even his kin didn't believe his claim,  
But Jesus said, "My time's not yet, I remain."  

"The world's hate is mine, but not for you,  
I speak of its evil, and that's the view."  

"You head up to the feast, I'll stay in place,  
My time's not fulfilled, I'm not in a race."  

So he stayed back, let them go ahead,  
Then went in secret, where whispers spread.  

"Where is he?" folks at the feast would ask,  
Some called him good, others a troubling task.  

In the temple, he spoke, deep and wise,  
Jews marveled, "He's unlearned, yet so precise."  

"My teaching's not mine," Jesus did declare,  
"From Him who sent me, it's crystal clear."  

"If you seek His will, you'll know the truth,  
I seek His glory, not personal proof."  

"Moses gave the law, yet you break it still,  
Why seek my life? For making one heal?"  

Crowd retorted, "Demon talk, who's the threat?"  
Circumcision talk, Jesus wouldn't forget.  

"If a boy's healed on the Sabbath, do you frown?  
Judge rightly, not by the looks around."  

"Isn't this the man they want to demise?  
Yet he speaks freely, rulers act surprised."  

"You know me, my roots, where I dwell,  
But I come from Him, who you don’t know well."  

They sought to seize him, yet no harm done,  
His hour not yet, but faith in him won.  

Pharisees heard the whispers, sent the men,  
Jesus spoke of leaving, "You’ll seek me then."  

"Where will he go?" the Jews pondered hard,  
"Away to Greeks?" their thoughts were jarred.  

Last day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried,  
"Thirsty souls, come to me, be satisfied!"  

Living waters flow from belief, he said,  
Spirit not yet given, but it would spread.  

The crowd split, some said "Prophet," some "Christ,"  
Others questioned his origin, the script precise.  

Division grew, but no arrest was made,  
Officers stunned by the words he laid.  

"No man talks like this," was their defense,  
Pharisees mocked, "Cursed ignorance."  

Nicodemus stood, brought the law to light,  
"Judge a man fairly, hear him outright."  

"Are you Galilean too?" they shot back,  
"Find no prophet from there, let's stay on track."  

Each to his own house, the night drew near,  
In quiet homes, the questions lingered clear.
