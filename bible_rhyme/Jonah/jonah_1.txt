Yahweh spoke to Jonah, said, "Get up and go,  
To Nineveh, preach, let that great city know."  
But Jonah took flight, ran the opposite way,  
Down to Joppa he went, found a ship that same day.  

Paid his fare, bound for Tarshish, he fled from the Lord,  
Yet Yahweh sent winds, a storm roared and soared.  
The sailors, they trembled, each cried to their god,  
They tossed cargo over, yet Jonah slept odd.  

The captain shook Jonah, "Wake up, and plead,  
Pray to your God, we’re in desperate need!"  
They cast lots for answers, the lot it found one,  
It fell upon Jonah, his secret undone.  

"Who are you?" they asked, "Why this storm on our seas?"  
Jonah replied, "I'm Hebrew, and Yahweh I please.  
The God of the heavens, who made land and sea,  
It's my flight from Him that caused this decree."  

Fear gripped the sailors, "Tell us, what must be done?  
How to calm this chaos? How to halt this run?"  
Jonah said, "Throw me over; the storm will cease its roar,  
For my cause this tempest, my presence you must ignore."  

Despite Jonah's plea, they rowed, wouldn't quit,  
But the sea grew fiercer, they couldn't conquer it.  
To Yahweh they cried, "Don't blame us, we plea,  
You've done what you willed, let us not share his decree."  

So over went Jonah, into the sea so wild,  
And the ocean grew calm like a peaceful child.  
The men feared Yahweh, they sacrificed and vowed,  
And Yahweh sent a fish that swallowed Jonah, proudly endowed.  

Three days, three nights, in the belly Jonah stayed,  
In the depths of the sea, in the fish he prayed.
