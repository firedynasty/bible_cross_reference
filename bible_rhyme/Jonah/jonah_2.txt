In the belly of the beast, Jonah prayed, feeling low,  
He called out to Yahweh, where else could he go?  

"From the depths of my pain, I cried out for aid,  
And out of the shadows, my cry was relayed.  

You tossed me in waters, the heart of the sea,  
All the waves crashed down, surrounding me.  

I thought I was lost, banished from Your sight,  
Yet I turned to Your temple, hoping for light.  

Deep waters rose up, pulling at my soul,  
With seaweed around me, taking its toll.  

Down to the roots, to the mountain's base,  
The earth held me tight, a relentless embrace.  

But You, Yahweh, my God, You lifted my life,  
From the pit of despair, freeing me from strife.  

When my spirit was weak, Your name I recalled,  
My prayer reached Your temple, my heart enthralled.  

Those who chase lies, mercy they leave,  
But I offer thanks, in You I believe.  

With vows I will honor, Your grace I acclaim,  
For salvation is Yours, forever Your name."  

Then Yahweh spoke, the fish set him free,  
Jonah on dry land, finally released, to be.
