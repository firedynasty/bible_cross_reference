God spoke to Jonah, "Hear my call,  
Head to Nineveh, preach to them all."  

Jonah got up, no time to delay,  
To the great city, made his way.  

A city so vast, three days to span,  
He entered and cried, "Forty days, that's the plan!"  

Nineveh listened, believed, and feared,  
A fast was proclaimed, sackcloth appeared.  

From the least to the great, everyone knew,  
Their king rose up, changed his view.  

Off came the robe, on went the sack,  
Sat down in ashes, no turning back.  

Decree went out, through the land it spread,  
"Don't eat, don't drink," the king said.  

"Man and beast, in sackcloth clad,  
Cry out to God, leave the bad."  

"Who knows," he asked, "if God will spare,  
And turn away His anger's glare?"  

God saw their deeds, the change within,  
He relented, forgave their sin.
