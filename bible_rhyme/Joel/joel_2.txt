Blow the trumpet, sound the alarm, in Zion’s peak,  
Let all tremble, for Yahweh’s day ain’t weak.  

Darkness looms, a day of deep gloom,  
Clouds roll thick, like a shadowy tomb.  

A dawn spreads over mountains, mighty and grand,  
Like none before, nor after, in this land.  

Fire devours, a flame trails behind,  
Eden before them, a wasteland they find.  

Like horses they appear, like horsemen they ride,  
Chariots rumble, like flames they glide.  

People tremble, faces turned pale,  
Warriors run strong, never do they fail.  

They climb like warriors, march straight and true,  
Breaking defenses, none can undo.  

Rushing the city, scaling the wall,  
Through windows they enter, thieves with gall.  

Earth shakes, the heavens quiver,  
Sun and moon darkened, stars lose their shiver.  

Yahweh thunders, his army’s great,  
His command obeyed, none can abate.  

“Even now,” says Yahweh, “return to me,  
With fasting, weeping, and mourning, hear my plea.”  

“Tear your heart, not your clothes, come back to your God,  
Gracious and merciful, forgiving the flawed.”  

Who knows? Blessings might follow,  
Offerings for Yahweh, no more sorrow.  

Blow the trumpet, call the fast that binds,  
Gather the people, leave no one behind.  

Priests weep and plead, “Spare your nation,  
Let not the world mock, with their provocation.”  

Yahweh’s jealous for his people and land,  
He sends grain, wine, and oil by his hand.  

The northern threat now pushed away,  
Into barren lands, their stench will sway.  

Land, don’t fear, rejoice at Yahweh’s deeds,  
Fields grow green, the earth proceeds.  

“Be glad, children of Zion, sing and praise,  
For Yahweh sends rain in measured ways.”  

Threshing floors full, vats overflow,  
Years devoured by locusts, Yahweh will bestow.  

You’ll eat your fill and praise his name,  
Never again will you suffer shame.  

Know Yahweh’s among you, none else, just him,  
“My Spirit I’ll pour, let visions brim.”  

Sons and daughters will prophesy,  
Old men dream, young see visions fly.  

Wonders in heaven, earth’s pillars of smoke,  
Sun turns dark, moon’s blood cloak.  

Call Yahweh’s name, and you’ll be saved,  
In Zion, among those he’s called, you’re engraved.
