In those days, at that time, fortunes restored,  
Judah and Jerusalem, blessings outpoured.  

All nations I gather, to Jehoshaphat's vale,  
Judgment comes down, justice prevails.  

Scattered my people, divided my land,  
Boys for a price, girls traded by hand.  

"What are you to me, Tyre and Sidon?" I speak,  
"Philistia, repayment? It’s judgment you seek."  

Stolen my treasure, gold and my silver,  
Judah's children sold, greed makes you shiver.  

I'll raise them up, from where they were sold,  
Your children in turn, Judah’s hands will hold.  

"Proclaim it now, nations prepare for war,  
Mighty men rise, hear the battle roar."  

Plowshares to swords, pruning hooks to spears,  
Weak ones declare, "I'm strong,” face your fears.  

Nations surround, gather for the fight,  
Yahweh’s might descends, righting the night.  

In the valley they rise, where judgment's decreed,  
Put in the sickle, ripe is the seed.  

The wine press is full, vats overflow,  
Their wickedness great, in the valley they go.  

Multitudes throng, in decision's domain,  
Yahweh’s day nears, in judgment’s plain.  

Sun and moon dark, stars lose their light,  
Yahweh roars from Zion, shaking the night.  

Yet He’s a refuge, a stronghold to stand,  
Israel’s safe, under His hand.  

"You will know I'm Yahweh, dwelling in Zion high,  
Jerusalem holy, strangers won’t pass by."  

Mountains drip wine, hills flow with milk,  
Brooks of Judah, with waters of silk.  

A fountain from Yahweh, Shittim’s thirst quench,  
Egypt desolate, Edom's end is clenched.  

But Judah remains, forever secure,  
Jerusalem stands, generations endure.  

Blood cleansed at last, Yahweh in Zion,  
For His people’s peace, His presence relying on.
