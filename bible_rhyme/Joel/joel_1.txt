Yo, listen up, elders, hear the word,  
Spread it through the land, let it be heard.  

Did this happen in your fathers' days?  
Tell your kids, let the story blaze.  

Locusts swarm, they eat, they feast,  
Devour the land, north, west, and east.  

Wake up, drunkards, feel the pain,  
Sweet wine's gone, it's all in vain.  

A nation strong, countless, fierce,  
Lion teeth, they grip and pierce.  

Vines laid waste, figs stripped bare,  
Branches white, lost in despair.  

Mourn like a bride in sackcloth dress,  
For her lost love, in emptiness.  

Offerings cut from Yahweh's house,  
Priests mourn, for blessings doused.  

Fields destroyed, the land in tears,  
Grain is gone, new wine disappears.  

Farmers, be shamed, cry out loud,  
The harvest gone, beneath the cloud.  

Vines are dry, figs gone too,  
Joy's withered with all we knew.  

Priests, put on sackcloth, wail,  
Lie down mourning, let your cries prevail.  

Call a fast, gather, pray and shout,  
The day of Yahweh's near, no doubt.  

Food is gone, joy's cut away,  
The seeds rot where they lay.  

Animals groan, pastures bare,  
Flames devour, lands in despair.  

"Yahweh, hear my cry, my plea,  
Fire's consumed the land and tree."  

Animals pant, brooks dried up,  
In the wilderness, no water cup.
