This is the law, the holy code, where offerings are slain,  
In the place of burnt fires, where blood does rain.  

The fat is gathered, tail and innards, laid bare,  
The kidneys, the liver, handled with care.  

Priests burn it on the altar, a fire divine,  
It's a trespass offering, Yahweh's holy sign.  

Every male priest may eat, in a sacred place,  
Holy food, holy space, a holy race.  

"Like the sin offering, the trespass is the same,  
The priest who offers it knows the claim."  

The priest keeps the skin, a burnt reward,  
Meal offerings baked, priestly accord.  

"Offering to Yahweh, a peace sacrifice you give,  
Thanksgiving cakes with oil, that’s how you live."  

One cake from each, lifted up high,  
Given to the priest, who sprinkles the blood nigh.  

Eat the flesh on the day it is given,  
Leave none till morning, a law from heaven.  

"A vow, free will, next day you can eat,  
Third day’s meat, burn it with heat."  

Unclean meat? Burned, not for eating,  
Keep Yahweh’s peace offerings, pure in their seating.  

"Touch not the unclean, don't eat in shame,  
Your soul shall be cut off, bear the blame."  

Yahweh spoke to Moses, "Tell Israel, heed,  
No fat, no blood, that is my creed."  

"The fat of the dead, or torn, not for food,  
Eat it not, or be cut off, understood?"  

"Peace offerings to Yahweh, bring with your hands,  
Fat and breast waved, as the Lord commands."  

Priests burn the fat, but Aaron gets the chest,  
The right thigh goes to the priest, part of the rest.  

"For Aaron’s sons, the wave, the heave,  
Portions from Israel, for them I leave."  

Consecration for Aaron, and his sons too,  
From offerings by fire, from Israel's view.  

These are the laws from Sinai's land,  
Offer to Yahweh, obey His command.
