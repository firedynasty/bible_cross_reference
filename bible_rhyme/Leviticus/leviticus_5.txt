When you hear a call, a chance to speak,  
But stay silent, your guilt will peak.  
Touch something foul, unseen by your eye,  
Later you find out, can't deny the why.  

If you touch what's unclean from a man,  
Once you know, guilty you'll stand.  
Swear an oath, good or bad from the lip,  
Forget about it, but guilt's got a grip.  

Admit your wrongs, confess your sin,  
Bring an offering, let atonement begin.  
Can't afford a lamb? No need to freeze,  
Two birds will suffice, Yahweh to please.  

Blood on the altar, a sin offering laid,  
Atonement made, your debts repaid.  
If even the birds are beyond your reach,  
Fine flour will do, no frankincense to breach.  

Priest takes a handful, burns it on the flame,  
Sin's forgiven; Yahweh's mercy's the same.  
Trespass the holy, unaware of the wrong,  
A spotless ram's the price, strong and long.  

Add a fifth more, to make things right,  
Priest makes atonement, sin's out of sight.  
Even unknown sins carry the blame,  
Bring forth a ram, atone just the same.  

Guilt before Yahweh, no matter the scene,  
But forgiveness awaits, washed pure and clean.
