Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, set the word,  
"Tell the people, listen close, make sure it's heard.  

If a man has a discharge, he's unclean, no doubt,  
Whether it flows or stops, that's what it's about.  

Every bed he lies on becomes defiled,  
Everything he touches, gotta wash it, mild to wild.  

Touch his bed, wash your clothes, take a shower,  
Till evening comes, you're unclean, that's the power.  

If you sit where he sat, same rules apply,  
Wash up, clean up, wait till night draws nigh.  

Touch his body, wash your clothes, don't delay,  
Bathe in water, wait it out till the end of day.  

If he spits, wash what you wear,  
Till evening, uncleanness there to bear.  

Ride his saddle, it's unclean,  
Touch what's under, stay unseen.  

Touch without rinsing, wash it all,  
Wait till evening, that's the call.  

Earthen vessel he touches, break it down,  
Wooden one, rinse it, don't let it drown.  

When he's cleansed, count seven days clear,  
Wash his clothes, bathe away any fear.  

On day eight, bring two birds to the gate,  
Priest makes atonement, it's Yahweh's fate.  

If there's an emission, bathe the body whole,  
Wait till evening, let time roll.  

Garments and skin, wash them down,  
Till evening comes, in uncleanness drown.  

Man and woman, if they lie,  
Bathe till evening, let it dry.  

If a woman bleeds, she's unclean for seven,  
Touch her, wait till evening, then you're forgiven.  

Lies on her bed, it's unclean,  
Sits anywhere, same hygiene.  

Touch her bed, wash clothes, bathe,  
Evening comes, then you're saved.  

Touch her seat, same rules apply,  
Wash and wait till night passes by.  

If a man lies with her, impurity flows,  
Seven days unclean, everybody knows.  

If her blood flows beyond the time,  
Treat it like her period, follow the rhyme.  

Unclean for all those days, stay aware,  
Her bed, her seats, all need care.  

Seven days after cleansing, then she's free,  
Bring two birds to the priest, sin offering to see.  

Separate the people from uncleanness within,  
To keep them safe when the tabernacle's in.  

This is the rule for discharge and flow,  
Keep it clean, that's how it goes."
