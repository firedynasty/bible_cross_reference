Yahweh spoke, told Moses, "Here's what to do,  
Tell Israel to bring olive oil, pure and true.  
Beat it for the light, let the lamps ignite,  
From evening to morning, keep 'em burning bright.  

Outside the veil where the Testimony lies,  
In the Tent of Meeting, let the light rise.  
Aaron's on the watch, keeping order tight,  
It's a statute forever, generations in sight.  

Bake twelve cakes, fine flour's the key,  
Two-tenths in each, that's how it'll be.  
Set in two rows, six by six they lay,  
On pure gold tables, in Yahweh’s way.  

Pure frankincense on each row, you know,  
A memorial offering, with fire aglow.  
Every Sabbath, set them in place,  
An everlasting covenant, a holy embrace.  

For Aaron and sons, the bread is for them,  
To eat in a holy place, a perpetual gem.  

Now, a boy of Israel with an Egyptian dad,  
Walked among the camp, things turned bad.  
In a fight with a man, cursed the Name,  
They brought him to Moses, Shelomith's shame.  

Held in custody, till Yahweh's word came,  
"Bring him out," said Yahweh, "he's to blame.  
Lay hands on his head, the witnesses all,  
Let the congregation stone him till he falls."  

"Tell Israel, if you curse your God,  
You bear your sin, it's a heavy rod.  
Blaspheme my Name, death’s the call,  
Foreigner or native, same for all.  

Strike a man mortally, death shall be,  
Strike an animal, repay, life for life, decree.  
Injure your neighbor, as he did, do to him,  
Eye for eye, tooth for tooth, justice grim.  

Kill an animal, make it right,  
Kill a man, death's the light.  
One law for all, foreign or born,  
I am Yahweh, to this you are sworn."  

Moses spoke, Israel listened, did as told,  
Brought out the one who cursed, stones rolled.  
Children of Israel stood, Yahweh's will shown,  
Did as commanded, His justice known.
