No idols, no stones, don't you make a show,  
"For I am Yahweh, your God," don't you know?  
Keep my Sabbaths, my sanctuary respect,  
Walk in my statutes, and your life I perfect.  

Rain in season, fields will bloom,  
Bread so full, no fear, no gloom.  
Peace in the land, you'll lie down unafraid,  
Enemies fall, my power displayed.  

"Five chase a hundred, a hundred chase ten,  
I'll make you fruitful, multiply again.  
Old supplies out, new ones in,  
My tent among you, let blessings begin."  

"I brought you from Egypt, broke your yoke wide,  
Made you walk tall, with nothing to hide.  
But if you ignore, break my commands,  
Terror, fever, spread through your lands."  

"Enemies eat your seed, face set against you,  
Flee when none chase, strength spent without due.  
Sky like iron, soil like bronze,  
No fruit, no gain, just endless wrongs."  

"Walk contrary, plagues will swarm,  
Wild beasts take children, livestock torn.  
Cities desolate, pestilence reign,  
Bread scarce, never ending pain."  

"If you refuse, wrath seven times strong,  
Eat flesh of kin, everything gone wrong.  
High places torn, idols cast down,  
Cities waste, no sweet scents in town."  

"Land enjoys Sabbaths while you're away,  
Enemies rule, but don't dismay.  
If hearts humble, confess your ways,  
Covenants remembered, better days."  

"I'll recall Jacob's, Isaac's, Abraham's vow,  
Land will be yours, even if not now.  
In enemy land, you won't be shunned,  
Yahweh's your God, I won't be outdone."  

These statutes stand from Sinai's height,  
Laws given through Moses, a guiding light.
