When you bring a meal to Yahweh’s domain,  
Fine flour’s the ticket, don’t forget the grain.  
Pour oil on top, with frankincense,  
Bring it to Aaron’s sons, commence.  

A handful taken by the priest's own hand,  
Burned on the altar, sweet aroma so grand.  
What remains is for Aaron and his kin,  
It's sacred, from the fire, let the feast begin.  

Unleavened cakes from the oven's heat,  
Mixed with oil, now it's ready to meet.  
Or wafers anointed, baked with care,  
Offering on the griddle, flour and oil in there.  

Cut in pieces, drizzle oil on the lot,  
When it’s on the pan, give it all you got.  
Bring it forth to Yahweh’s holy place,  
Priest at the altar, giving thanks and grace.  

No yeast, no honey in this holy fire,  
Burn none on the altar, it’s Yahweh’s desire.  
Season with salt, covenant’s embrace,  
Never without it, in the offering’s space.  

First fruits come with fresh grain and fire,  
Crushed and mixed with oil, raising higher.  
Frankincense laid, it’s a meal divine,  
Burned by the priest on Yahweh’s line.  

The aroma rises, the offering’s right,  
In the holy fire, a precious sight.  
Aaron’s sons feast on what’s left behind,  
Offerings to Yahweh, in them we find.
