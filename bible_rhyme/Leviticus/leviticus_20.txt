Yahweh spoke to Moses, clear and bold,  
"Tell Israel this tale, let the truth unfold:  

Anyone who to Molech gives their kin,  
Death comes swiftly, stones will begin.  

I'll turn my face, cut them from the land,  
They defiled my sanctuary, disobeyed my command.  

If you see it happen and choose not to act,  
I'll set my face against you, that’s a fact.  

Mediums and wizards, those who stray,  
Will find themselves cast away today.  

"Sanctify yourselves, be holy, be true,  
For I am Yahweh, sanctifying you.  

Obey my statutes, keep them tight,  
For anyone who curses parents, faces their own plight.  

Adultery strikes, both shall fall,  
The adulterer and adulteress will answer the call.  

Lie with a father’s wife, reveal the disgrace,  
Death waits for both, as they face their case.  

With a daughter-in-law, the same fate befalls,  
Perversion they committed, the judgment calls.  

If a man with a male lies as with a lady,  
It's an abomination, no ifs, ands, or maybe.  

Wife and her mother, a wickedness profound,  
Fire consumes, so wickedness won't be found.  

With an animal, such deeds disgrace,  
Both shall perish, cleanse this place.  

Man and his sister in shameful sight,  
Cut off from the people, face their plight.  

Monthly fountain uncovered brings shame,  
Both are cut off, bearing the blame.  

Close relative's nakedness, a sin to bear,  
Uncle’s wife touched, they'll be childless, beware.  

Brother’s wife, impurity shared,  
No children for them, the burden declared.  

"Keep my statutes, let the land hold you tight,  
Or it’ll vomit you out, in blight.  

Don't follow nations I'm casting away,  
For their customs I abhor, don’t fall astray.  

Inherit their land, flowing with milk and honey,  
I’m Yahweh, your God, setting you apart, it’s no funny.  

Distinguish the clean from the unclean,  
Don't make yourselves abominable, keep pure, keep serene.  

Be holy to me, for I am holy too,  
Set apart, you belong to me, it’s true.  

Mediums and wizards, gone with stones,  
Their blood upon themselves, for their wrongs alone."
