Yahweh spoke to Moses, hear the decree,  
"Tell Israel's people, be holy like Me.  

Respect your mother, honor your dad,  
Keep my Sabbaths, don't make Me mad.  

No idols, no molten gods in sight,  
I'm Yahweh your God, get it right.  

When offering peace, make it true,  
Eat it in two days, the third won't do.  

If left till the third, burn it with fire,  
Eating it then brings no desire.  

Now to the fields, when harvests abound,  
Leave the corners, let kindness astound.  

Don't steal or lie, or deceive your kin,  
Don't swear falsely, it's a sin.  

Wages kept overnight, let them go,  
Don't oppress your neighbor, let justice flow.  

Don't curse the deaf, nor trip the blind,  
Fear your God, keep these in mind.  

Be fair in judgment, no favoritism play,  
Judge in righteousness, that's the way.  

No slander, no danger to your friend,  
I'm Yahweh your God, start to amend.  

Love your neighbor, don't bear a grudge,  
Rebuke him rightly, but don't misjudge.  

Keep my statutes, don't mix the seed,  
Don't cross-breed beasts, take heed.  

No garments woven with two kinds of thread,  
Keep it pure, that's what I said.  

If a man lies with a slave, not free,  
He must bring his offering to Me.  

Plant your trees and wait, three years not a taste,  
In the fourth year give praise, don't haste.  

Eat in the fifth, the increase is due,  
I am Yahweh, I'm guiding you.  

No blood in your meat, no magic tricks,  
Don't cut hair or beard, don't mix.  

No marks or tattoos on your skin,  
Your body's sacred, keep it within.  

Don't sell your daughter to sin or shame,  
Keep My Sabbaths, respect My name.  

No mediums, wizards, let them be,  
I am Yahweh, your God, trust in Me.  

Honor the elderly, rise to greet,  
Treat the stranger as kin, make love complete.  

No false measures, keep it just,  
Fair in balance, in Me you trust.  

Observe My statutes, do what's right,  
I am Yahweh, your guiding light."
