Yahweh spoke to Moses, a message clear,  
"Tell Israel about the feasts they need to hear.  

Six days you work, but rest on the seventh,  
A holy convo, no labor — that's the lesson.  

Appointed times, these feasts are mine,  
Passover kicks off at evening time.  

The very next day comes the unleavened bread,  
Eat it for seven, let no work in your head.  

Offer by fire to Yahweh in His sight,  
On the seventh day, rest again with delight.  

When you hit the land I give, for your harvest to reap,  
Bring the first fruits to the priest, wave 'em high, no sleep.  

A male lamb unblemished, to Yahweh you’ll raise,  
With flour and oil as a fragrant blaze.  

Don't eat the grain till the offering's done,  
This rule's forever, for everyone.  

Count seven Sabbaths, fifty days in stride,  
Bring new meals to Yahweh, let His glory abide.  

Two loaves baked with yeast as a wave,  
With lambs, bulls, and rams, to the fire they gave.  

A goat for sin, two lambs for peace,  
Wave 'em before Yahweh, the blessings increase.  

Proclaim a holy convo, no work this day,  
It's a law forever, from Yahweh's way.  

When you reap, leave some for the poor,  
For the outsider too, Yahweh's love is sure.  

In the seventh month, the trumpets will sound,  
A solemn rest, a holy ground.  

On the tenth day, afflict your soul,  
Atone with an offering, Yahweh makes you whole.  

No work, just rest, on this solemn day,  
Those ignoring get cut off or cast away.  

The fifteenth day, the booths arise,  
Seven days to Yahweh, with joy in your eyes.  

First day and eighth, a holy convo meet,  
Offer by fire, make the festival complete.  

These are Yahweh's feasts, proclaim them all,  
With offerings, vows, and gifts to recall.  

Gather the fruits, celebrate in delight,  
Branches and palm trees, dance in the night.  

Dwell in shelters, seven days of peace,  
Remember Egypt, Yahweh's mighty release.  

Moses spoke these festivals clear and bright,  
To Israel, Yahweh’s appointed feasts took flight.
