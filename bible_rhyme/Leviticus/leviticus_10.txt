Nadab and Abihu, sons of Aaron’s line,  
Took their censers, put fire, thought it'd be fine.  
But it was strange fire, not a command,  
Before Yahweh they stood, it got out of hand.  

Fire blazed forth, Yahweh’s fierce flame,  
Devoured them right there, in His name.  
Moses turned to Aaron, solemn and deep,  
"This is Yahweh’s word, His holiness we keep."  

Aaron stood silent, holding his peace,  
Moses called kin, giving commands with ease:  
"Come close, carry them out from the holy place,  
Outside the camp, in their tunics, no trace."  

To Aaron and sons, Moses clearly said,  
"Don’t mourn as usual, keep your heads;  
Let Israel cry for the fire Yahweh lit,  
Stay in the Tent, for the oil’s on you, admit."  

Yahweh spoke clear to Aaron and his crew,  
"No wine in the Tent, or death comes to you.  
This rule stands forever, through all your days,  
Distinguish the holy, teach Israel My ways."  

Moses spoke on, to Aaron’s sons who remained,  
"Take the meal offering, as Yahweh ordained.  
Eat it beside the altar, no yeast—most holy,  
‘Cause it’s your share, a command not lowly."  

The waved breast, the heaved thigh, eat in clean space,  
Your portion, your sons’, with your daughters in place.  
Wave it before Yahweh, your portion forever,  
As commanded, no changes, remember, be clever."  

Moses then checked, the sin offering’s fate,  
It was gone, all burned, he got irate.  
"Why didn’t you eat it in the holy place?  
It’s for atonement, don’t lose that grace."  

Aaron replied, his voice low and grave,  
"Today’s offerings happened, all that we gave;  
Had I eaten, would it please Yahweh’s sight?"  
Moses heard Aaron, and found it alright.
