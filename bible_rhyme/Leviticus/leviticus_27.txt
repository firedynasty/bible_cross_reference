Yo, listen up, here's what God had to say,  
To Moses, giving rules on that sacred day.  

"When a man makes a vow to Me,  
Here's how the valuations gotta be:  

For a dude aged twenty to sixty's prime,  
It's fifty shekels, that's the price line.  

If she's a woman in that age's groove,  
Thirty shekels, that's how we move.  

A young one from five up to twenty,  
A male gets twenty, ten for female plenty.  

From a month to five, for a little guy,  
Five shekels, for a girl, three, don't ask why.  

Sixty and up, if he's a man,  
Fifteen shekels, for a woman, ten's the plan.  

If he's too poor for what you rate,  
Bring him to the priest, who'll set it straight.  

If it's an animal for sacrifice to Me,  
It's holy, can't swap it, let it be.  

Unclean animals, bring them to the priest,  
He'll value it, from the greatest to least.  

Wanna redeem? Add a fifth to the price,  
That's how it is, don't think twice.  

Dedicate a house, it's the priest's call,  
Good or bad, he'll judge for all.  

Redeem the house, add a fifth, make it yours,  
Follow these rules, don't ignore.  

A field dedicated, check the seed rate,  
Fifty shekels for a barley state.  

From Jubilee year, that's your valuation,  
Otherwise, adjust for time's duration.  

If you sell the field, or don't redeem it,  
It stays with the priests, they will deem it.  

Bought fields, not your own, dedicate with care,  
Priest will value it, fair and square.  

Firstborn animals, they belong to Me,  
No man shall dedicate what I decree.  

Unclean ones, redeem with extra fee,  
Else they're sold, that's the decree.  

Devoted things, can't be sold or bought,  
Holy to Me, don't give it a second thought.  

Tithes of land, seed or fruit,  
Holy to Me, that's the truth.  

Redeem your tithe? Add a fifth to the heap,  
This is how the sacred laws run deep.  

Every tenth of herd or flock,  
Is holy, no matter the stock.  

Don't swap, don't trade, both stay mine,  
These are Yahweh's laws, divine.  

Commandments given to guide Israel's line,  
Upon Mount Sinai, where they heard the sign."
