Yahweh spoke to Moses, here's the plan,  
"Take Aaron, his sons, get the oil, garments in hand.  

Grab the bull and two rams, bread unleavened,  
Gather the crowd at the Tent, make sure it's heaven."  

Moses did what Yahweh had said,  
Congregation gathered, full of dread.  

"This is what Yahweh’s commanded," Moses declared,  
Aaron and sons all got washed and prepared.  

He dressed Aaron in a tunic, sash tied tight,  
Robe, ephod, breastplate, shining bright.  

Turban on his head with a crown of gold,  
As Yahweh commanded, the story unfolds.  

Anointing oil Moses spread all around,  
Sanctified the tabernacle, altar, ground.  

Poured oil on Aaron, sanctified his head,  
Clothed his sons, just like Yahweh said.  

The sin-offering bull, they laid hands on its brow,  
Moses killed it, blood on the altar now.  

Purified the altar, atonement made,  
Burned the bull’s fat, as Yahweh bade.  

Ram of burnt offering, hands laid again,  
Blood sprinkled, cut into pieces then.  

Burned on the altar, aroma sweet,  
As Yahweh commanded, Moses' feat.  

Second ram for consecration, hands laid once more,  
Blood on ear, thumb, toe, poured on the floor.  

Fat and bread waved, offering in the air,  
Burned on the altar, smoke in the air.  

Oil and blood on Aaron, garments too,  
Sanctified the priesthood, holy and true.  

"Boil the meat, eat it at the Tent's door,  
Seven days inside, till vows are no more.  

Do this as Yahweh’s atonement for you,  
Stay in the Tent, keep Yahweh’s commands true."  

Aaron and sons followed every command,  
As Yahweh directed, through Moses' hand.
