Yahweh spoke to Moses, laid it down clear,  
"Speak to Aaron, tell the folks, lend an ear.  

Any man from Israel's line,  
Kills a beast inside or out, it's a crime,  

If he don't bring it to the Tent's door,  
Before the altar, he’s done for sure.  

Blood on his hands, he's cut off alone,  
Not part of the people, like losing a bone.  

Bring your sacrifices, don't stray to the field,  
At the Tent's door, let the peace be sealed.  

The priest will sprinkle, let the aroma rise,  
To Yahweh's altar, like smoke to skies.  

No more to the goat idols, no more games,  
This rule's forever, through all their names.  

Tell them all, don't stray from the path,  
Bring your offerings or face the wrath.  

Eat blood, and my face turns against you,  
Your soul cut off, and that's the truth.  

For life is in the blood, atonement's made,  
On the altar, let that price be paid.  

Tell Israel and every foreign soul,  
No blood in your meal, that's the goal.  

Hunt in the field, take any game,  
Pour out the blood, dust covers the same.  

Life's in the blood, flesh and bone,  
Eat it? Cut off, you're on your own.  

Eat what dies itself or by beast torn,  
Wash your clothes, clean by eve, reborn.  

But if you skip the cleanse, don't make amends,  
You'll bear your guilt till it finally ends."
