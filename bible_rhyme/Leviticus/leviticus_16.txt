Yahweh spoke to Moses after Aaron's sons’ fall,  
"Tell Aaron, don't come near the Most Holy at all,  

Without a bull and a ram for the offerings' flame,  
Clothed in holy linen, washed in water, pure in name.  

Two goats from the people, one ram on the side,  
A bull for his sin, for his household to abide.  

Cast lots for the goats at the Tent's front door,  
One for Yahweh, one to roam the wilds, nothing more.  

"Kill the bull for himself, atonement he must make,  
Take burning coals and incense, let the holy smoke rise and shake.  

Sprinkle bull's blood on the mercy seat east,  
Seven times with his finger, to cover sins' beast.  

Then the goat for the people, do the same as before,  
Sprinkle its blood on the mercy seat, cleanse the Holy's core.  

Make atonement for the tent, where uncleanness dwells,  
Till no transgression lingers, where Yahweh's presence swells.  

No one enters while atonement's in motion,  
For himself, his household, and all Israel, devotion.  

Out to the altar, cleanse it with blood,  
Sprinkle seven times, purge the sin-flood.  

Now the live goat, hands lay on its head,  
Confess all the sins, let the past be shed.  

Release it in the wilderness, to a solitary land,  
Let it carry iniquities, by a ready man's hand.  

Change clothes, wash up, bring offerings to flame,  
He and the people, free from sin's claim.  

Burn the fat on the altar, let fire purify,  
The scapegoat's handler must cleanse before coming by.  

Burn the bull and the goat outside the camp,  
Wash up and return, leaving no sin's stamp.  

"This day shall be forever, on the tenth of the month,  
Do no work, seek atonement, rest from the hunt.  

A Sabbath of solemn rest, afflict your soul,  
Cleanse before Yahweh, be made whole.  

Anointed priests, in holy garments dressed,  
Make atonement for all, give the people rest.  

Forever this statute, once a year keep it clear,  
For Israel's sins, Yahweh’s mercy is near."  

And so it was done, as Yahweh had framed,  
His commands through Moses, faithfully proclaimed.
