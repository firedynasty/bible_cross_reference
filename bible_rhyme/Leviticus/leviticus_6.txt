Yahweh spoke to Moses, right on the scene,  
"If anyone sins and treats their neighbor mean,  
In matters of deposit or bargain gone astray,  
Or steals, oppresses, lies in any way—  

If guilty of sin, they must make it right,  
Return what they took, bring it to the light.  
Add a fifth to the value, pay it back in full,  
On the day they're found guilty, not just when they're cool.  

Bring a ram without defect, that's the deal,  
For the trespass offering, for the priest to seal.  
He'll make atonement, offer it before Yahweh's face,  
Then forgiveness is given, guilt erased.  

Yahweh spoke to Moses once more,  
"Command Aaron, these laws they can't ignore:  
The burnt offering stays on the altar's flame,  
Through the night till morning, the fire stays the same.  

Put on linen garments, remove the ash with care,  
Change clothes, take it outside, clear the air.  
Keep the fire burning, wood each morning lay,  
Burn the fat and offerings in Yahweh's way.  

The meal offering's law, Aaron's sons present,  
Take fine flour, oil, frankincense, as they're meant.  
Burn it on the altar, sweet aroma rise,  
Rest is eaten holy, no yeast in the guise.  

Aaron's sons eat it, in the holy court they meet,  
Without yeast, it's their portion, a perpetual treat.  
Each male from Aaron's line, this is their decree,  
Touching these offerings, set apart, holy shall be.  

Yahweh spoke again, "Aaron's offering's clear,  
When anointed, flour's tenth part, day and year.  
With oil on a griddle, soaked and baked,  
A pleasant aroma to Yahweh, never faked.  

Anointed sons shall offer it, this statute stands,  
Wholly burned to Yahweh, by priestly hands.  
No priest's meal offering shall be consumed,  
It's burned completely, none assumed.  

Yahweh spoke to Moses, once more the news,  
"The sin offering's law, these words you must use:  
Where the burnt offering's killed, sin offering's slain,  
It’s most holy, part of the priestly domain.  

The priest shall eat it, in a holy place,  
Court of the Tent, where sacred's the space.  
Touch its flesh, be holy, wash blood from clothes,  
The vessel that's used, break or clean as it goes.  

Every priestly male eats, it's holy, no joke,  
But if blood enters the Tent, burn it in smoke.
