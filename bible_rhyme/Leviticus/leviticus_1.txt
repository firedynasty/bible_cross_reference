Yahweh called Moses, His voice clear and loud,  
From the Tent of Meeting, amidst the gathered crowd.  

"Speak to Israel's kin, let this message ring,  
When offering to Yahweh, bring the best you can bring.  

From your livestock, your herds, your flock's finest,  
A sacrifice to the Lord, make sure it's the rightness.  

For a burnt offering from the herd, bring a male, no defect,  
At the Tent's entrance, for Yahweh to accept.  

Lay your hand on its head, find atonement in its stead,  
Slay the bull for the Lord, where the sacrifice is led.  

Aaron's sons, the priests, handle the blood with care,  
Sprinkle it on the altar, let it purify the air.  

Skin the offering, cut it piece by piece,  
Fire on the altar, the fragrance released.  

Put the head, the fat, on wood in a row,  
Wash the innards, let the pure water flow.  

Priests burn it all up, a pleasing aroma it brings,  
A burnt offering to Yahweh, where the fire sings.  

If it's from the flock, sheep or goats you bring,  
A male without defect, let the ritual swing.  

On the north side, before Yahweh, let its life go,  
Blood sprinkled round the altar, let the purity flow.  

Cut in pieces, head and fat laid on wood,  
Wash innards with water, offer it good.  

Burn the whole on the altar, let the smoke rise high,  
A pleasant aroma for Yahweh, reaching to the sky.  

For birds as an offering, turtledoves or young pigeons are fair,  
Bring them to the altar, the priest will prepare.  

Wring off the head, let the blood be drained,  
Feathers cast aside, in ashes they’re contained.  

Tear it by wings, but keep it as one,  
Burn it on the altar, to Yahweh, it is done.  

A burnt offering, a fire that pleases Yahweh's sight,  
Sacrifices burning, in the day and in the night."
