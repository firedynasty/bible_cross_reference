Moses, listen, Yahweh's decree,  
To Aaron's sons, here's what it be.  

"Priests, don't defile for the dead,  
Except close kin, hear what's said.  

For mother, father, son, or daughter,  
Brother, sister, then don't falter.  

But if you're chief, don't profane,  
Keep your status, don't stain.  

Shave no heads, cut no flesh,  
Stay holy, keep it fresh.  

Offer Yahweh's fire, bread of God,  
Stay holy, not marred or flawed.  

Don't marry a prostitute or profane,  
Divorced woman? No, refrain.  

Priests offer sacred bread,  
In holiness, they tread.  

A priest's daughter who defiles,  
With fire, judgment piles.  

High priest, anointed, crowned with oil,  
Never let his garments uncoil.  

Don't touch the dead, stay pure,  
Sanctuary rules must endure.  

Marry a virgin, not defiled,  
Priestly seed, keep undefiled.  

Yahweh said, keep them pure,  
For I am holy, that's for sure.  

No defect shall approach the bread,  
Blind, lame, injured - it's been said.  

No deformity shall near,  
The altar, veil, keep it clear.  

Bread still yours to eat, no doubt,  
But don't come near, stay devout.  

So Moses spoke, the law unfurled,  
To Aaron, sons, Israel's world.
