When you bring a peace offering, here’s the deal,  
From the herd, male or female, make it real.  

Spotless and pure, before Yahweh it's laid,  
Hand on the head, then it's slain where the priests prayed.  

Aaron’s sons, priests, sprinkle blood on the altar flame,  
The sacrifice, the fire, to Yahweh’s name.  

The fat on the innards, kidneys, liver’s cover,  
Take it all away, then burn it, let the smoke hover.  

On the altar wood, where the fire's burning bright,  
Offering to Yahweh, aroma's pure delight.  

From the flock now, if lambs are what you bring,  
Spotless, pure, before Yahweh, do your thing.  

Hand on the head, at the Meeting Tent it’s slain,  
Aaron’s sons, the blood around the altar, again.  

Fire offering, the fat and tail, take away,  
By the backbone, entrails, innards, no delay.  

Priests burn it up, the food of fire divine,  
Smoky scent to Yahweh, in the air it will entwine.  

And if it’s a goat, bring it here, no joke,  
Hand on its head, before God, blood soaked.  

Sprinkle on the altar, just like before,  
Fat, kidneys, liver, take it all, restore.  

Priests burn it all, offering’s smoky rise,  
Pleasant aroma, reaching to the skies.  

Always remember, through all generations wide,  
In your homes, no blood, no fat, let Yahweh’s word abide.
