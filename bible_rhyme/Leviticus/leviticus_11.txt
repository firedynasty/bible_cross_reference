Yahweh spoke to Moses and Aaron, real clear,  
“Tell Israel what’s good to eat, lend me your ear.  

From animals that roam and graze the land,  
Those with split hooves and cud in their hand.  

Don’t touch the camel, though it chews smooth,  
No split hoof, unclean, it’s not the move.  

The hyrax, the hare, chew but lack the break,  
Unclean they are, no meat on your plate.  

The pig’s hooves split, but it don’t chew the cud,  
Unclean to you, don’t eat, don’t touch that mud.  

In waters, fins and scales are the key,  
If they lack these, they’re not for thee.  

Detest those swimming without scale or fin,  
An abomination, don’t let them in.  

Birds like eagles, vultures, kites of black,  
Unclean these wings, don’t take them back.  

Owls and gulls, hawks and such,  
Don’t touch these birds, you’ll lose your touch.  

Insects that fly, but walk on all fours,  
An abomination, don’t open those doors.  

Yet locusts, katydids, crickets, and grasshoppers,  
They’re okay to eat, jumpin’ like talkers.  

Touch their carcass, you’re unclean till night,  
Wash your clothes, cleanse away the blight.  

Animals with paws or half-split feet,  
Unclean they are, not for your treat.  

Touch their bodies, unclean till day ends,  
A rinse and repeat, no need to pretend.  

Creeping things that slither and crawl,  
Unclean they are, don’t heed their call.  

If their dead bodies touch your gear,  
Wash those items, till the end is near.  

God said, “I am holy, be like Me,  
Don’t defile yourselves, stay clean, be free.  

I brought you out from Egypt's land,  
Be holy, follow My command.”  

The law is clear for beasts of all kind,  
Distinguish the clean, keep purity in mind."
