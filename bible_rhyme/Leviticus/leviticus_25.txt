Yahweh spoke on Sinai's high,  
To Moses, with a watchful eye,  
"Speak to Israel, loud and clear,  
When you hit the promised sphere.  
Six years, the fields you’ll sow,  
Prune the vines, let the harvest flow.  

But come the seventh, let the land rest,  
A Sabbath for the earth, at Yahweh’s behest.  
Don’t you sow, and don’t you prune,  
Let it rest, beneath the moon.  
For your harvest, don’t you reap,  
Let the grapes and vines keep.  

The land’s Sabbath will provide,  
For you, your kin, and all who abide.  
For livestock and beasts that roam,  
All shall eat; the land’s their home.  

Count seven times seven years,  
Forty-nine, till the Jubilee nears.  
Sound the trumpet on the Day of Atonement,  
Liberty rings, a jubilant moment.  

In the fiftieth, make all things new,  
Return to your land, and your family too.  
Don’t you sow, nor reap what's grown,  
In Jubilee, all is Yahweh-owned.  

If you trade with your neighbor, play it fair,  
Count the years, set the price with care.  
Don’t exploit, fear your God,  
I’m Yahweh, says the Lord.  

Keep my statutes, do them right,  
The land will yield, and you’ll sleep tight.  
If you ask, “What’ll we eat that year?”  
Fear not, in the sixth, the blessing is near.  

The land’s not for sale, it’s mine,  
You dwell with me for a time.  
If kin grows poor, let family restore,  
Redeem the land, seal the score.  

Can’t reclaim? Wait for Jubilee’s sound,  
Then he’ll return, with family around.  
City houses in walls are sold,  
Redeem in a year, or it’s theirs to hold.  

Levites can reclaim their place,  
Fields remain, a sacred embrace.  
Uphold the poor, no interest demand,  
Fear God, the land of Canaan’s at hand.  

If a brother sells himself, don’t enslave,  
He’s a hired hand, treat him brave.  
In Jubilee, he’s free at last,  
He and his kids, leave the past.  

Alien or resident, if they grow rich,  
And your brother’s poor, life’s a switch.  
He can be redeemed by kin’s hand,  
Or wait for Jubilee’s command.  

Children of Israel, marked by Yahweh’s call,  
Servants of God, released from Egypt’s thrall.  
Yahweh’s the name, the God of all."
