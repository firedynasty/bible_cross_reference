Yahweh spoke to Moses with a serious tone,  
"Tell Aaron's sons to leave the holy things alone.  

If anyone unclean approaches this sacred zone,  
That soul will be cut off, I am Yahweh, I alone.  

Leprous or with discharge, they can't eat the holy bread,  
Touching anything unclean till evening, they must tread.  

Once the sun is down and they're washed anew,  
Then they can partake of the holy bread due.  

Don't eat what's dead or torn, don't defile your soul,  
Follow my commandment to keep your spirit whole.  

No stranger or hired hand shall touch this sacred theme,  
But a slave or child born in a priest's dream.  

A priest's daughter married outside isn't in,  
Return home childless, then she may begin.  

If a man eats holy unaware, pay a fifth more,  
The priests won't defile the holy things they store.  

Yahweh told Moses, "Speak to Aaron, make it clear,  
Offer perfect males without blemish, that's the rule here.  

No defects allowed, for peace offerings too,  
Blind, injured, or maimed won't do for you.  

Don't offer with bruised or broken parts,  
No foreigner’s hand should play these sacred arts."  

Yahweh spoke to Moses on how to make it right,  
"When a newborn is eight days, it's ready for the rite.  

Never kill a cow and its calf in one day,  
Sacrifice with thanks so it won't decay.  

Eat it that same day, leave none till dawn,  
Keep my commands, let no name be withdrawn.  

I'm Yahweh, who sanctifies, makes you whole,  
Brought you from Egypt, claimed your soul."
