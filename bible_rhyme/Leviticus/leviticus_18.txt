Yahweh spoke to Moses, laid it down real clear,  
"Tell Israel, 'I'm Yahweh, the God you fear.  

Don't do like they did in the land of Egypt so bold,  
Or follow Canaan's ways, where you're soon to behold.  

Obey my statutes, walk my path, it's what I demand,  
If you do, you'll live by them, under my command.  

No close kin shall you approach, that truth is stark,  
I am Yahweh, let these words leave a mark.  

Don't uncover your father's or mother's skin,  
For that kind of act is a deep-rooted sin.  

Leave your father's wife, your sister, all kin, alone,  
Whether they’re from the homeland, or from abroad, unknown.  

Your grandkids' nakedness, no chance, it's your own,  
Your father's wife's daughter, leave her bones.  

Your father's sister, your mother's sister too,  
These are near kin; you know what they do.  

Your father's brother's wife, she's your aunt, don't touch,  
Your daughter-in-law, your brother's wife—back off, it's too much.  

A woman and her daughter, that's trouble in sight,  
No rival sisters should be married, the same plight.  

No impure woman should you ever dare approach,  
Your neighbor's wife? Don't even encroach.  

Don't sacrifice your kids to Molech's flame,  
For "I am Yahweh," don't profane my name.  

Don't lie with a man as with a woman, that's not right,  
Or with animals—it's a twisted sight.  

Don't defile yourselves, nations before you were cast,  
The land was sickened, they didn’t last.  

Keep my statutes, don't do what they knew,  
Their ways are wrong; they're not for you.  

For those who do these acts, they'll be left alone,  
Cut off from their people, without any home.  

Keep my rules, avoid these customs of shame,  
Don't defile yourselves, "I am Yahweh," I proclaim.
