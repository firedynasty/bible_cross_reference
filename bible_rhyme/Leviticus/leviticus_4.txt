Yahweh told Moses, "Listen, this is the deal,  
Speak to Israel, make their sins real.  

If the priest sins, brings guilt on the crowd,  
A young bull without defect, this offering's allowed.  

Bring it to the Tent, lay hands, then take its life,  
Before Yahweh, the bull meets the knife.  

The priest dips his finger in blood, sprinkle it right,  
Seven times before Yahweh, make the veil bright.  

Blood on the altar's horns, incense sweet,  
Pour the rest at the base where burnt offerings meet.  

Take the bull's fat, kidneys, and liver too,  
Burn them on the altar, that's what you do.  

Skin, meat, head, legs — take it all out,  
Burn it outside the camp, where ashes shout.  

If all Israel sins, but the deed's unknown,  
When the truth comes out, the sin must be atoned.  

Bring a young bull, the elders lay hands,  
Before Yahweh, the bull falls by commands.  

Blood in the Tent, sprinkled seven-fold,  
Horns of the altar, as the ritual's told.  

Fat on the altar, a fire's embrace,  
Forgiveness follows, sin leaves no trace.  

A ruler's sin, unaware at first,  
When it's known, he brings a goat unrehearsed.  

Hands on the goat, offering's site,  
Blood on the altar, a sacred rite.  

All of its fat, like peace offerings laid,  
Atonement made, forgiveness paid.  

Common folks sin, unwitting they do,  
A female goat, without flaw, will do.  

Hands on its head, sin offering's plight,  
Blood on the altar, forgive through the night.  

Fat like before, a sweet aroma's lift,  
Atonement granted, Yahweh's gift.  

If a lamb's brought, female without defect,  
Hands on the head, kill as respect.  

Blood on the altar, pour at the base,  
Fat's removed, burned in its place.  

In all these offerings, forgiveness's the goal,  
Atonement secures, and heals the soul.
