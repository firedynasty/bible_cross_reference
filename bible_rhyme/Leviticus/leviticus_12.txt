Yahweh to Moses, hear what I say,  
"Speak to Israel, guide them this way.  

If a woman births a son, seven days she's unclean,  
Just like her monthly time, you know what I mean.  

On day eight, that's when it's done,  
Circumcise the flesh of her young one.  

Thirty-three days, purifying flows,  
No holy touch until that time goes.  

For a daughter, two weeks she's apart,  
Sixty-six days for the purifying start.  

When purification days are through,  
Bring to the priest, here's what to do:  

A lamb for burning and a dove for sin,  
At the Tent of Meeting, let the cleansing begin.  

The priest offers them up to Yahweh's throne,  
Atonement made, she's cleansed, she's home.  

This is the rule for birthing, girl or boy,  
Let the purification bring cleansing joy.  

If a lamb she can't afford to find,  
Two doves or pigeons will do just fine.  

One for burning, one for atoning,  
The priest makes her clean, no more groaning."
