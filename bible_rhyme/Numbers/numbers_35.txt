In the plains of Moab, by Jordan's flow,  
Yahweh spoke to Moses, let the people know:  
"Command Israel, give Levites their share,  
Cities to live in, with pastures laid bare.  

From city's wall, one thousand cubits wide,  
These lands for livestock, possessions to bide.  
Two thousand cubits, measure east to west,  
North and south too, give the Levites the best.  

Refuge cities, six to be set,  
For those who slay, but not with intent.  
Forty-two more, total forty-eight,  
From Israel's lands, this shall be their fate.  

Cross the Jordan, into Canaan's land,  
Set these cities as refuge, by My command.  
For the slayer's escape, from vengeance they hide,  
'Til judgment's call, let justice decide.  

"Six cities of refuge, three here, three there,  
Beyond Jordan, in Canaan, a place to spare.  
For Israel's children, stranger, and guest,  
These cities offer refuge, a place of rest.  

"If one strikes with iron, stone, or wood,  
And death ensues, he's a murderer, understood.  
The avenger of blood shall do his part,  
When he meets the killer, he'll end his heart.  

"But if it's done without hate or spite,  
Unintentional harm, no malice in sight,  
The congregation will judge, decide their fate,  
The slayer returns to refuge, where he'll wait.  

Stay in the city, 'til the high priest's demise,  
Step outside, face blood's avenger's cries.  
After the priest's death, back to own land,  
This law stands firm, through generations grand.  

"Murder demands witnesses, two or more,  
One voice alone can't settle the score.  
No ransom accepted for murderous guilt,  
Blood cleanses blood, for the land is spilt.  

"Do not defile the land where I rest,  
For I, Yahweh, dwell among the blessed."  
With justice and refuge, the land's laws hold tight,  
In the midst of His children, God sets things right.
