Moses spoke to the tribes with a serious tone,  
"This is what Yahweh has commanded, don't leave it alone.  

When a man makes a vow, binds his soul with a bond,  
He must keep his word, be true and strong.  

And when a young woman pledges in her father's place,  
Her father hears it, stays silent, her vows stay in grace.  

But if her father says no on that very day,  
Her vows won't stand, forgiven, they're cast away.  

If she's married and vows from her lips overflow,  
Her husband hears and is silent, then the promises go.  

But if he forbids her when he hears her say,  
Her vows are void, Yahweh forgives her that day.  

For a widow or divorced, her vows stand alone,  
What she binds stays, no need to atone.  

If she swore in her husband's house, a binding oath,  
And he said nothing, then her vows are both.  

But if he nullifies the day he hears them clear,  
Her vows won't stand, Yahweh's forgiveness is near.  

Every vow, every oath to afflict the soul,  
Her husband can uphold, or make them null.  

If he says nothing day by day,  
They stand firm, no words to sway.  

But if he voids them after hearing her speak,  
He bears the iniquity, strong or weak.  

These are Yahweh’s statutes Moses did share,  
Between husbands, wives, fathers, daughters, everywhere."
