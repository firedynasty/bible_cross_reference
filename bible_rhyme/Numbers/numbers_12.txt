Miriam and Aaron, they spoke out loud,  
Against Moses, over the wife that he vowed.  

"Has God spoken just to him?" they cried,  
"He speaks with us too," but God was not shy.  

Moses, humble, the meekest on earth,  
God heard their words, and He knew their worth.  

Suddenly He spoke, called them to the Tent,  
"Moses, Aaron, Miriam," His message sent.  

In a cloud He came, right at the door,  
Called Aaron and Miriam, and they did implore.  

"Listen now to my words," God declared,  
"I speak in dreams, to prophets prepared.  

But Moses, my servant, he's not like the rest,  
With him, I speak plainly, he's truly the best.  

Why weren't you afraid, to slander his name?"  
God's anger burned, and He left them with shame.  

The cloud lifted, and there it was shown,  
Miriam leprous, her skin like snow, alone.  

Aaron turned, saw his sister's plight,  
"Forgive our sin, we've acted without sight!"  

"Don't let her be like the dead," Aaron pled,  
Moses cried out, "Heal her, God," he said.  

"If her father had spit, she'd hide seven days,  
Let her stay outside, then heal her ways."  

Seven days passed, she was out of the camp,  
The people waited, till her return from the tramp.  

Then they journeyed on, with lessons learned,  
From Hazeroth to Paran, they moved, discerned.
