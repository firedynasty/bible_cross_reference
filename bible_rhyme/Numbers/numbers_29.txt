Seventh month begins, hear the trumpet sound,  
A holy convocation, no work around.  
Burnt offerings rise, aroma sweet and grand,  
A bull, a ram, lambs without a blemish stand.  

Fine flour mixed with oil, a feast they create,  
Three tenths, two tenths, each portion's fate.  
One male goat for sin, atonement's made,  
With new moon offerings, a fire display.  

On the tenth day, souls afflict, work's undone,  
Another holy gathering, fire offering begun.  
A bull, a ram, seven lambs, perfect, pure,  
Meal offerings with oil, Yahweh's allure.  

Fifteenth day brings a feast, seven days of cheer,  
Thirteen bulls, two rams, fourteen lambs appear.  
Meal offering portions, precise they bring,  
One male goat for sin, in Yahweh's sing.  

Second day, twelve bulls, two rams, lambs all around,  
Meal and drink offerings, ordinances abound.  
One goat for sin, offerings proceed,  
Continual burnt offering, Yahweh's creed.  

Third day, eleven bulls, same ram and lamb count,  
According to the number, each sacrifice amount.  
A goat for sin, alongside burnt fire,  
Meal and drink offering, as Yahweh requires.  

Fourth day, ten bulls, two rams still,  
Fourteen lambs, perfect, fulfill His will.  
Meal and drink offering, numbers precise,  
A goat for the sin, continual sacrifice.  

Fifth day, nine bulls, rams, lambs remain,  
Offerings follow, with a goat for the stain.  
Continual burnt offering, steady and true,  
As Yahweh commands, the fire renews.  

Sixth day, eight bulls, two rams, lambs stand tall,  
Meal and drink offerings, the numbers call.  
A goat for sin, burnt offering stays,  
Following Yahweh's ordained ways.  

Seventh day, seven bulls, the ritual's flow,  
Two rams, fourteen lambs in a holy show.  
A goat for sin, offerings persist,  
Continual devotion, Yahweh won't resist.  

Eighth day assembly, solemn, no work be done,  
One bull, one ram, seven lambs, a ritual run.  
Offerings bring aroma, Yahweh's delight,  
With vows and free will offerings, in His sight.  

Moses speaks, the children hear, every command,  
As Yahweh instructed, they understand.
