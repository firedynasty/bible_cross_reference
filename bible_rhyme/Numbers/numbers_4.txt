Yahweh spoke, His voice so clear,  
"Moses, Aaron, lend an ear.  

Count the Kohathites, make it known,  
From thirty to fifty, they've grown.  

In the Tent they'll work, here’s their call,  
Guard the holy things, don’t let them fall.  

Aaron and his sons, take down the screen,  
Cover the ark where it's unseen.  

Use sealskin, then a blue cloth drape,  
Put in its poles, secure its shape.  

On the table, spread the blue,  
Dishes, cups, bread in view.  

Scarlet cloth, sealskin above,  
Cover it all with care and love.  

The lampstand's wrapped in blue and skin,  
Every vessel tucked within.  

The altar too, with blue they wrap,  
Sealskin hiding every gap.  

When all is covered, the camp will move,  
But no one touches, nor should they prove.  

Eleazar, son of Aaron, stands,  
Oil, incense, offering in his hands.  

"Don't cut off Kohath," Yahweh said,  
"Let them live, not be dead.  

Appoint their work, each man’s share,  
But don’t let them see, beware."  

Now, count the Gershonites, Moses, go,  
From thirty to fifty, let it flow.  

They carry curtains, coverings wide,  
Every screen and door outside.  

Aaron’s sons will guide their task,  
In burden and service, they shall bask.  

Under Ithamar’s watchful sight,  
Serve they must with all their might.  

Count Merari’s sons, each one’s name,  
Boards, bars, pillars — they must claim.  

Sockets, pins, cords they bear,  
For the Tent's full repair.  

Under Ithamar’s steady hand,  
They serve as planned in this land.  

Moses, Aaron, leaders stood tall,  
Counting Levites, one and all.  

Kohathites numbered, two thousand plus,  
In the Tent, they served, it’s thus.  

Gershonites counted next in line,  
Two thousand six hundred thirty shine.  

Merari followed, three thousand strong,  
In the Tent they also belong.  

Eight thousand five hundred, Levites all,  
In service, they answered Yahweh’s call.  

Counted by Moses, as Yahweh planned,  
Each one serving, by His command.
