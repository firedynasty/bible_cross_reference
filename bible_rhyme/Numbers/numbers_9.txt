In the wild Sinai, Yahweh spoke with flair,  
To Moses, His voice echoed through the desert air.  
“Tell Israel, keep Passover, mark the date,  
Fourteenth evening, don’t be late.”  

Moses told them, “Do just as He said,  
Passover’s coming, prepare the bread.”  
They obeyed in Sinai, under moonlight's glow,  
Just as Yahweh commanded, in the desert below.  

Some men approached, unclean from the dead,  
“We’re kept back, Yahweh’s feast, can’t be fed.”  
Moses said, “Hold on, let me hear the call,  
Yahweh’s command will settle it all.”  

Yahweh spoke, “If unclean or distant you be,  
Still keep Passover, I decree.  
In the second month, on the fourteenth night,  
Eat with herbs and bread, by firelight.”  

“Leave none till morning, don’t break a bone,  
Follow the statute, you’re not alone.  
But if clean and near, yet fail to keep,  
That soul shall be cut off, sins to reap.”  

“If a stranger wants to join the feast,  
Let him follow every rule, not the least.  
One law for all, stranger or native-born,  
United in the statutes, no one’s torn.”  

When the tabernacle rose, cloud covered it,  
Fire by night, in heaven it lit.  
At Yahweh’s command, they moved or stayed,  
The cloud's position was their parade.  

Day or night, week or year,  
Where the cloud stayed, they held dear.  
Yahweh’s word was their guide,  
In His command they did abide.  

Cloud up, they moved; cloud down, they rest,  
At Yahweh’s word, they did their best.  
In the desert vast, His will they sought,  
Following His lead, in His wisdom they were taught.
