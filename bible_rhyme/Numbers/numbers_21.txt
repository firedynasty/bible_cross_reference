In the South, King Arad had heard the news,  
Israel's coming, so he'd better choose.  
He went to war, took captives in the fight,  
Israel vowed to Yahweh, "Give us might."  

"If you deliver, we'll destroy their town,"  
God listened, brought the Canaanites down.  
Called it Hormah, where they did prevail,  
Onward toward Edom, their spirits frail.  

Complaints rose up, "Why this desert trek?  
No bread, no water, food makes us wreck."  
God sent snakes, venom struck them all,  
Israel cried to Moses, "Pray, don't fall."  

Moses did, and God replied clear,  
"Make a snake, lift it high, no fear.  
Look and live, that bronze serpent's key,"  
Moses raised it up for all to see.  

Israel journeyed, camped in the land,  
Through Oboth, Iyeabarim, by God’s hand.  
On to Zered and beyond the divide,  
Moab's border, where Arnon's waters glide.  

Wars of Yahweh spoke of this turf,  
"Vaheb in Suphah," the valleys' worth.  
To Beer they traveled for God’s supply,  
"Gather 'round, I'll give water," was God’s reply.  

"Singing to the well, spring up!" they cried,  
Princes dug deep, where life abides.  
From Mattanah to Nahaliel they roamed,  
Up to Pisgah, where they called home.  

Israel sent word to Sihon, king strong,  
"Let us pass, we’ll do you no wrong."  
Sihon refused, brought his army's might,  
Israel fought, took the land that night.  

From Arnon to Jabbok, they claimed the ground,  
In Heshbon's cities, victory was found.  
Proverbs spoke, "Heshbon's flame burns bright,"  
Moab's defeat was clear in sight.  

"Woe to you, Moab!" cries of despair,  
Children of Chemosh taken there.  
Israel dwelled where Amorites stood,  
Moshe's spies took Jazer, understood.  

Then to Bashan, Og rose to fight,  
God said, "Fear not, you'll claim his might."  
Just as with Sihon, the land was won,  
Israel prevailed, no foe left to run.
