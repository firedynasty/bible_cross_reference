Aaron, Yahweh spoke, hear these words divine,  
"You and your sons, the priesthood's line.  
Bear the weight of the sanctuary's care,  
Levi’s tribe stands with you, to serve and share.  

Keep commands, guard the Tent — that's your call,  
But don't touch the sacred, or you'll fall.  
Stay close, keep responsibility tight,  
A stranger comes near — they're out of sight.  

Perform your duty, shield from wrath's rise,  
For Israel's peace, heed these ties.  
I've taken Levites, a gift, my decree,  
For the Tent’s service, they're dedicated to me.  

Priesthood's yours, a sacred role to fulfill,  
Strangers come near, they face the chill.  
"Wave offerings, holy, are yours," I say,  
Your sons' forever, in the priesthood's sway.  

Holy offerings, meal, sin, and more,  
Eat like it's sacred — it's yours to the core.  
Wave offerings too, for you and your kin,  
Clean in your house, let the feast begin.  

Best oil, vintage, grain — first fruits are yours,  
Bring to Yahweh, open the stores.  
Firstborn of flesh, man or beast,  
Redeem the unclean — that's the least.  

Cattle, sheep, and goats — they remain,  
Sprinkle blood, burn fat, in Yahweh's name.  
Meat of offerings, it's yours, understand,  
A covenant of salt, forever at hand.  

No land inheritance, Yahweh’s your gain,  
Tithes for the Levites, a service chain.  
Israel keeps distance, sin to avoid,  
Levites serve, their task deployed.  

Moses, my message to the Levites take,  
"From Israel's tithe, a wave offering make.  
Tithe of the tithe, to Yahweh present,  
To Aaron's hands, the offering's sent.  

Give the best, holy in sight,  
As threshing floor and wine press might.  
Eat it anywhere, your service’s due,  
With no sin burden — this is what's true.  

Profane not the holy, respect the name,  
So you won't perish, avoid the shame."
