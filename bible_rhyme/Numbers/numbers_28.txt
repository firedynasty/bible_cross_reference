Yahweh spoke to Moses, words clear to relay,  
“Tell my people, bring offerings, don't delay.  

See my aroma's sweet in seasons set,  
Two male lambs, perfect, each day met.  

One in the morn, one as the sun sets,  
Fine flour and oil, the recipe’s set.  

From Sinai it came, a fire offering's scent,  
A drink poured out, in holiness, spent.  

Evening comes, repeat the morning's due,  
For a pleasant aroma, offering anew.  

On the Sabbath, two lambs without a flaw,  
Double flour and oil, the offering’s law.  

New months call for bulls, rams, and seven lambs more,  
With fine flour and oil, pour as before.  

Wine’s poured in fractions, each measure known,  
Monthly offerings, on altars shown.  

One goat for sin, alongside the daily flame,  
Yahweh's commands, all offerings the same.  

In the first month, Passover's here,  
Unleavened bread for a week is clear.  

A holy gathering, work set aside,  
Burnt offerings pure, Yahweh's delight.  

Two bulls, one ram, seven lambs without blame,  
Flour and oil blend, their offering’s fame.  

Seven days straight, the scent rising high,  
Adding to the daily, reaching the sky.  

The seventh day calls for sacred rest,  
No work, just gather, Yahweh’s blessed.  

First fruits bring, a new feast in weeks,  
Holy convocation, no work, just peace.  

Bulls and rams, seven lambs burn bright,  
Fine flour with oil, perfect in sight.  

One goat for atonement, a sin’s release,  
With the regular offering, Yahweh’s feast.  

Follow these commands, without defect,  
An aroma to Yahweh, in all respect.
