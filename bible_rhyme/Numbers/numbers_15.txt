Yahweh spoke to Moses, clear and strong,  
"Tell Israel when they come along,  

Into the land, I promised to give,  
Make offerings by fire, for me to receive.  

Burnt offerings, vows, free will at your feasts,  
Pleasant aromas rise with the beasts.  

Bring fine flour mixed with oil, it’s divine,  
And wine for the drink, measure it fine.  

For each lamb, each ram, bring it right,  
A meal offering, a sacrifice, a pleasing sight.  

Every bull gets its share, don’t you forget,  
Flour, oil, and wine, make the table set.  

Native-born or foreign, all the same,  
One statute, one rule, in Yahweh’s name.  

When you enter the land, and eat the bread,  
Offer your first dough as a wave, ahead.  

If you err, forget these commands,  
Bring a bull for atonement, to Yahweh’s hands.  

The priest will make atonement, your sins will clear,  
For errors done without knowledge, no need to fear.  

If one sins unwittingly, a goat will do,  
The priest will atone, Yahweh forgives you.  

But if with high hand, a soul defies,  
Cut off they'll be, no more lies.  

In the wilderness, a man gathered sticks,  
On Sabbath he was caught, his fate was fixed.  

Yahweh commanded stoning, no mercy to lend,  
Israel executed, as Yahweh did send.  

Then Yahweh spoke, with wisdom and might,  
"Fringes on garments, a reminder in sight.  

A cord of blue on the edge you'll place,  
Remember my commands, keep your faith.  

I'm Yahweh your God, out of Egypt I brought,  
Be holy to me, remember what I taught."
