The people cried out with a mournful sound,  
Their hearts heavy, they gathered around.  

"Why did we leave Egypt's land,  
Just to die here, by Yahweh's hand?  

Let's go back, find another way,  
Choose a leader, return today."  

Moses and Aaron hit the ground,  
Before the crowd's angry, restless sound.  

Joshua and Caleb, clothes torn,  
Spoke of the land they had sworn,  

"The land's good, so rich and fine,  
Flowing with milk, honey, divine.  

If Yahweh's pleased, He'll lead us in,  
Don't rebel, we'll surely win."  

Yet the crowd, with stones in hand,  
Threatened them, made their stand.  

Yahweh's glory then appeared,  
His voice to Moses, loud and clear:  

"How long will they doubt my might?  
I'll make a nation strong, in their spite."  

Moses pleaded, "What will they say?  
You led them here, show them the way.  

Your name's known, your power's great,  
Forgive them now, don't seal their fate."  

Yahweh replied, "I've heard your plea,  
But those who doubted won't see the land, not through me.  

Caleb, with a spirit true,  
He'll enter in, his seed too."  

Yahweh spoke, wrath in His tone,  
"In this wilderness you'll be alone.  

Forty years, for forty days,  
You'll wander lost in the haze."  

The spies who spread the false report,  
Paid the price in Yahweh's court.  

But Joshua and Caleb stayed alive,  
From that land, they'd not be deprived.  

The people mourned, they rose to fight,  
"We'll go, we've seen the light!"  

Moses warned, "Don't disobey,  
Yahweh's not with you today."  

But they climbed the mountain high,  
Ignoring Yahweh's clear reply.  

The Amalekites came down swift,  
With strength and force, they gave no lift.  

The people fell, defeated, torn,  
Their disobedience left them forlorn.
