Five daughters of Zelophehad stepped near,  
Mahlah, Noah, Hoglah, Milcah, Tirzah clear.  

They stood before Moses, the priest, the crew,  
At the Tent of Meeting, they spoke what was true:  

"Our father died in the wilderness, you see,  
Not with Korah's crowd, just his own decree.  

He left no sons, just daughters here,  
Why should his name disappear?  

Give us a share, let his name survive,  
Among his brothers, let it thrive."  

Moses took their cause up high,  
Before Yahweh, the Most High.  

Yahweh spoke, straight and right,  
"The daughters speak, they have the light.  

Give them a share, rightful and real,  
Among their father's brothers, seal the deal.  

Tell Israel, 'If a man leaves no son,  
His daughter takes the inheritance won.  

No daughter, then it goes to the brothers,  
Still no brothers? To the father's others.  

And if kin are nowhere to be found,  
Pass it on, keep it sound.'  

This rule I declare, just and strong,  
A statute for Israel, where it belongs."  

Then Yahweh said, "Moses, climb high,  
See the land for Israel, under the sky.  

Once you've seen, you'll join the rest,  
Like Aaron, your brother, laid to rest.  

For you defied my word in Zin,  
At Meribah's waters, you didn't begin."  

Moses answered, with a plea,  
"Appoint a leader, God of all we see.  

One to lead, in and out,  
So they're not like sheep, wandering about."  

Yahweh chose Joshua, Spirit-driven,  
"Lay hands on him, the charge is given.  

In front of Eleazar, make it known,  
Commission him, let it be shown.  

Give him authority, let them obey,  
He'll guide by the Urim, lead their way."  

Moses did as Yahweh spoke,  
Hands on Joshua, the Spirit woke.  

He was set before the crowd,  
Commissioned strong, Moses proud.
