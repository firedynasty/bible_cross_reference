Moses set up the tabernacle right, anointed and blessed,  
With furniture pure, altars at their best.  

Princes of tribes stepped up with pride,  
Offering gifts with wagons by their side.  

God said to Moses, "Accept what they bring,  
For the Levites' service, for the Tent's needs, they sing."  

Moses took oxen and wagons, gave Gershon two,  
Four went to Merari, under Ithamar’s view.  

But Kohath got none, they carried on their back,  
The sanctuary's service, no wagon they'd lack.  

Princes brought offerings to the altar that day,  
Each prince on his own, in a dedicated way.  

Nahshon from Judah, first to present,  
Silver, gold, and cattle, his offerings sent.  

Next came Nethanel, from Issachar’s line,  
With trays of silver, and offerings so fine.  

Eliab from Zebulun, with gifts on the third,  
Platter, bowl, and incense, his offerings heard.  

Elizur of Reuben, on day four he came,  
With similar gifts, in Yahweh’s name.  

Shelumiel of Simeon, day five was his call,  
He brought his portion, giving his all.  

Eliasaph from Gad, on day six he gave,  
With offerings rich, his faith displayed.  

Elishama from Ephraim, seventh in place,  
His gifts presented with reverent grace.  

Gamaliel of Manasseh, day eight he brought,  
His offerings, just like those before, were sought.  

Abidan from Benjamin, on the ninth day,  
Silver and gold, in the same array.  

Ahiezer of Dan, with offerings on ten,  
Brought the same gifts as those before him did then.  

Pagiel from Asher, day eleven he stands,  
Bringing the same gifts with devoted hands.  

Ahira of Naphtali, on the twelfth day,  
Completed the cycle, with offerings to lay.  

The altar's dedication, with gifts strong and bold,  
Twelve silver platters, twelve bowls, twelve ladles of gold.  

Bulls, rams, and goats, in numbers they came,  
A dedication complete, each one the same.  

Moses entered the Tent, to Yahweh he spoke,  
God's voice from the mercy seat, his heart awoke.
