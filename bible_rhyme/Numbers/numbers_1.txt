In the Sinai wild, God spoke to Moses and Aaron,  
"Count Israel's tribes, each family there-in.  
Every male, twenty and over, fit for war,  
Line 'em up, one by one, let's explore."

"Head of every tribe stands tall and true,  
For Reuben, Elizur, he's the one for you.  
Simeon's Shelumiel, Judah's got Nahshon,  
Issachar's Nethanel, Zebulun's Eliab, on and on."

From Joseph's sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, we see,  
Elishama and Gamaliel, they're the leaders, free.  
Benjamin’s counted by Abidan’s name,  
Dan’s Ahiezer, in the census game.

Asher, Gad, and Naphtali, don't forget,  
Pagiel, Eliasaph, Ahira, all in the set.  
Princes stand by their tribes, heads of Israel,  
Moses and Aaron took names, the plan was real.

They gathered the tribes, first month, second day,  
Declared their ancestry, in the Sinai way.  
Reuben came first, forty-six thousand strong,  
Simeon next, fifty-nine thousand, they belong.

Gad and Judah, their numbers clear,  
Forty-five thousand and seventy-four near.  
Issachar's fifty-four, Zebulun's fifty-seven,  
Ephraim's forty thousand, counted under heaven.

Manasseh was thirty-two, Benjamin thirty-five,  
Dan had sixty-two, Asher forty-one, alive.  
Naphtali brought fifty-three thousand to bear,  
Each tribe counted, their numbers they share.

Six hundred thousand plus, the warriors stood,  
But Levi was left out, as God said they should.  
"Levi’s tribe, no counting, they'll guard the tent,  
The Tabernacle's keepers, wherever it went."

"The Levites will camp by the Tabernacle's side,  
No wrath will fall, with them as the guide.  
Israel's tribes in camps, standards held high,  
The Levites in charge, and here's the reason why."

So the children of Israel did as they were told,  
Listening to God's words, their actions bold.
