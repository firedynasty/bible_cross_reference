Yahweh spoke to Moses, Aaron's ear,  
"Camp by your banners, keep the Tent near.  

Eastward, Judah's flag in the morning light,  
With Nahshon leading, a powerful sight.  

Seventy-four thousand and six they bring,  
Next to them, Issachar's forces sing.  

Nethanel's tribe stands fifty-four strong,  
Zebulun's Eliab, not far along.  

Fifty-seven thousand, they join the flow,  
Judah's camp moves first in the show.  

Southward, Reuben takes center stage,  
Elizur leads with numbers to gauge.  

Forty-six thousand, they'll stand their ground,  
Simeon's tribe and Shelumiel's sound.  

Fifty-nine thousand in Simeon's stride,  
Gad's Eliasaaph's men march in pride.  

Forty-five thousand, they gather near,  
Reuben's troops second to appear.  

The Tent and Levites move between,  
Each tribe positioned, the camp's serene.  

Westward, Ephraim's banner flies high,  
Elishama's forty-thousand march by.  

Manasseh's tribe with Gamaliel's might,  
Thirty-two thousand ready to fight.  

Benjamin's Abidan, thirty-five so bold,  
Ephraim's numbers a story told.  

One hundred eight, they march with glee,  
Third in the order, a sight to see.  

Northward, Dan leads with Ahiezer's call,  
Sixty-two thousand ready to brawl.  

Asher's troops with Pagiel's hand,  
Forty-one thousand take their stand.  

Naphtali follows, Ahira leads,  
Fifty-three thousand in their deeds.  

Dan's camp moves last, they close the line,  
One hundred fifty-seven in perfect time.  

All were counted, six hundred thousand strong,  
But Levites stood apart, where they belong.  

Israel moved as Yahweh said,  
Each by their standards, by Him led.
