God told Moses, "Aaron's got a task,  
Light those lamps, make the light last."  
Aaron did it, lit up the scene,  
Just like God said, everything was clean.  

The lamp stand's gold, beaten, not fake,  
From base to flowers, the craftsman's take.  
God showed Moses the pattern in sight,  
Every detail made just right.  

God spoke again, "Levites you've got to cleanse,  
Take them out, make amends.  
Sprinkle water, shave them clean,  
Wash their clothes, make them keen."  

"Bring a young bull, fine flour mixed with oil,  
Another bull for sin, make those offerings toil.  
Present the Levites at the Tent's domain,  
Gather the people, make it plain."  

"Lay hands on Levites, for Yahweh they're a wave,  
Aaron will offer, their service they'll save.  
Levites touch bulls, a sin and burnt disguise,  
Atonement made, on Yahweh's rise."  

Set them before Aaron, offer once more,  
Separate them, they're mine to the core.  
"The Levites enter, serve the Tent's call,  
They're mine from Israel, I chose them all."  

"Firstborn are mine, from Egypt's wake,  
Levites are taken for Israel's sake.  
Gift to Aaron, to serve and atone,  
Keep Israel safe, no plague to bemoan."  

Moses, Aaron, all Israel complied,  
Levites purified, washed, and sanctified.  
Wave offering given, atonement done,  
Levites ready, service begun.  

Yahweh spoke, "Levites start at twenty-five,  
At fifty, they retire, no longer strive.  
Assist their brothers, help in the tent,  
This is their duty, how their time is spent."
