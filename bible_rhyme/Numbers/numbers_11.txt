In the camp, folks were complaining, loud and clear,  
God heard them moaning, His fire drew near.  

Flames burned the edges, sparking fear and dread,  
"Cry to Moses!" they said, and the fire was dead.  

Named it Taberah, 'cause there it did ignite,  
But the mixed crowd still craved more bites.  

"Who'll give us meat?" they began to cry,  
"Remember fish, free in Egypt? We sigh.  

Cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, garlic we crave,  
Now it's manna, manna—day by day, wave by wave."  

Manna like seed, looked like bdellium stone,  
Ground in mills, boiled, tasted fresh oil alone.  

Moses heard weeping, it struck him to the bone,  
"Why, God, have you left me carrying this load alone?  

Did I give birth to them, that I should care?  
Where will I find meat? Their cries are everywhere.  

If this is my plight, end my life, let it be,  
I'm too weak, don’t let them see my misery."  

God said, "Gather seventy elders, wise and known,  
Bring them to the Tent, you're not alone.  

I'll share your spirit; they'll carry that weight,  
And for my people, meat is their fate.  

Prepare yourselves, for tomorrow’s feast,  
Not just days but a month, till you say 'cease.'  

You wept to Me, said Egypt was sweet,  
You'll eat your fill, till meat's no treat."  

Moses replied, "Six hundred thousand strong,  
How will you supply meat all month long?"  

God answered, "Has My hand grown short?  
Watch and see; my promise won’t thwart."  

Moses gathered the elders, seventy in line,  
God's Spirit on them, they prophesied, divine.  

Eldad and Medad, still in the camp,  
Prophesied too, igniting a spiritual lamp.  

Joshua cried, "Moses, stop their voice!"  
Moses said, "I wish all could rejoice."  

A wind from God brought quail around,  
Day and night, quail covered the ground.  

They gathered them up, the least ten homers wide,  
But God's wrath struck, a plague did slide.  

Named the place Kibroth Hattaavah in the sand,  
For those who craved, here they stand.  

From there, they moved to Hazeroth's land,  
And settled again, led by God's hand.
