Reuben and Gad with livestock in the land,  
Saw Jazer and Gilead, a place so grand.  
They went to Moses, Eleazar too,  
"Check this land, good for livestock, what can we do?  

If we find favor in your sight,  
Let us stay here, don't make us fight.  
Don't bring us over the Jordan, let us stay,  
It's perfect for our herds, we want to stay."  

Moses replied, "Will you sit while others fight?  
Discourage Israel? That's not right.  
Remember your fathers, who made hearts heavy,  
When spies returned, they weren't ready.  

God's anger burned, He swore in wrath,  
None from Egypt would see the path,  
Except Caleb and Joshua, loyal and true,  
For they followed Yahweh, through and through.  

Now you rise, like your fathers' sin,  
Turning God's anger towards us again.  
You mustn't stray, or else you’ll pay,  
Wander the wilderness another day."  

They spoke up, bold and clear,  
"We'll build sheepfolds, our loved ones near.  
But we'll arm ourselves, go to war,  
Only return when the land's secure.  

Our inheritance lies on this side,  
But we'll fight with you, side by side.  
We promise, Moses, we'll do just as you say,  
We'll help all Israel, come what may."  

Moses said, “If you fight for Yahweh’s cause,  
Then the land will be yours, no pause.  
But if you don't, your sin will shout,  
Your promise broken, your truth found out.  

Build those cities, build those folds,  
Keep your word, as the promise holds.”  
Gad and Reuben agreed, "We’ll obey,  
We’ll do what you command, come what may."  

Moses spoke to leaders, priests, and all,  
"If Gad and Reuben heed the call,  
Give them Gilead if they fight,  
If not, in Canaan, they'll find their right."  

"We'll cross armed, ready for the fight,  
Our land eastward, a pleasing sight."  
Moses gave them kingdoms, a great expanse,  
And cities they built, they seized their chance.  

Gad and Reuben built strong and high,  
Their cities rose to meet the sky.  
Manasseh's sons took more land,  
A promise kept, just as planned.
