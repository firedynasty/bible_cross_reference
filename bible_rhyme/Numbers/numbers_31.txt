Yahweh spoke to Moses, "Take a stand,  
Avenge Israel on Midian, by my command."  
Moses told the people, "Arm up for war,  
Yahweh's vengeance is what we're fighting for.  

One thousand from each tribe, that's the crew,  
Twelve thousand warriors, ready and true."  
Sent with Phinehas, the priest by their side,  
With trumpets ready, they marched with pride.  

They struck Midian down, as Yahweh had said,  
Every man, every king, lay among the dead.  
Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba all fell,  
Balaam too, by the sword's fierce knell.  

They captured women, and children too,  
Took flocks and goods, everything in view.  
Cities burned, encampments in flame,  
They took it all, in Yahweh's name.  

Back they came with captives and spoil,  
To Moses and Eleazar, their reward for toil.  
But Moses was angry at the leaders of war,  
"Why spare the women, who led us to fall?  

Kill every male child, and a woman who knows a man,  
Keep the virgins alive, that's the plan.  
Camp outside, purify on the third and seventh day,  
Cleanse your clothes, in every way."  

Eleazar spoke, "Here's what Yahweh commands,  
All metals through fire, water purifies the lands.  
On the seventh day, wash and be clean,  
Then return to camp, as Yahweh's deemed."  

Yahweh spoke, "Count up all the plunder,  
Divide it in halves, don't make a blunder.  
A levy to Yahweh from the warriors' share,  
One in five hundred, be aware.  

A tribute to the priest, a wave offering to make,  
The rest to the Levites, for Yahweh's sake."  
Moses and Eleazar did as told,  
Counting sheep, cattle, donkeys, and gold.  

The captains came near with their gold in hand,  
"Not one man lost, just as Yahweh planned.  
Here's the gold, for atonement's call,  
A memorial to Yahweh, for one and all."  

Moses took the offering, in the Tent it lay,  
A reminder of battle, in Yahweh's way.  
The children of Israel, guided by His light,  
With offerings of gold, in Yahweh's sight.
