Israel's journey, it began with a shout,  
Leaving Egypt’s chains, no doubt they were out.  
With Moses and Aaron leading the way,  
"Go forth," said Yahweh, "and do not delay."

From Rameses they set their stride,  
Passover done, with the Lord as guide.  
Egyptians mourned, their firstborn gone,  
While Israel marched with a hope so strong.

Succoth was their first camp site,  
Then to Etham, where day met night.  
Back to Pihahiroth, by the sea they stood,  
Baal Zephon watched as they crossed for good.

Through the sea's heart, wilderness ahead,  
To Marah's bitter waters and Elim's spread.  
Springs and palms, a brief reprieve,  
Then onward again, they would not leave.

By the Red Sea, in Sin they stayed,  
In Dophkah camp, their fear allayed.  
Alush came and Rephidim dry,  
Sinai's wilderness, under the sky.

Kibroth Hattaavah hosted their cry,  
To Hazeroth, then Rithmah, they fly.  
Rimmon Perez, then Libnah next,  
Rissah to Kehelathah, no longer vexed.

Mount Shepher rose, Haradah too,  
Makheloth, Tahath, the journey grew.  
Terah, Mithkah, and Hashmonah's camp,  
Moseroth where their spirits wouldn't damp.

Bene Jaakan, Hor Haggidgad's call,  
Jotbathah, Abronah, they journeyed all.  
Ezion Geber, Kadesh in Zin,  
Mount Hor saw Aaron, his life did end.

"Speak," said Yahweh, "to those who roam,  
In Canaan’s land, you'll find your home.  
Drive them out, make the land your own,  
Destroy their idols, to me alone."

"Take the land by lot," He decreed,  
"To each tribe, I will heed.  
But leave them not, or they’ll be thorns,  
Harassing you from dusk till morn."

"Inherit well, but heed my voice,  
Or your fate will be their choice."  
Israel's journey, a tale of old,  
A promise kept, as the story's told.
