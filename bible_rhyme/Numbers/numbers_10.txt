"Make two trumpets of silver," said Yahweh with grace,  
"Beat them from metal, for the gathering place.  

Blow them both, and the crowd will come near,  
To the Tent of Meeting, they gather right here.  

One trumpet sounds, and the leaders unite,  
Heads of the thousands, approach in the light.  

Blow an alarm, east camp will roam,  
Blow it again, and the south side goes home.  

For gatherings, just blow, no alarm for the show,  
Aaron's sons, the priests, are the ones in the know.  

In war's grim face, sound the alarm loud,  
You'll be remembered by Yahweh, stand proud.  

In joy, in feasts, and new month delights,  
Blow over offerings, and peace ignites.  

In the second year, second month, day twenty,  
The cloud rose up, they journeyed plenty.  

Out from Sinai, they followed the lead,  
The cloud rested in Paran, they moved with speed.  

Judah led first under Nahshon's command,  
With tribes by their side, in covenant they stand.  

Gershon and Merari took down the tabernacle frame,  
Reuben advanced, as Elizur called names.  

Kohathites carried the sacred, no delay,  
Others set up before they made way.  

Ephraim's camp moved, Elishama in charge,  
Manasseh and Benjamin followed the large.  

Dan’s rear guard with Ahiezer’s call,  
Asher and Naphtali, they moved them all.  

Moses spoke to Hobab, "Come travel our way,  
To the land Yahweh promised, come without delay."  

Hobab replied, "I’ll return to my own,  
To familiar land where I’ve grown."  

Moses implored, "Don’t leave us, we need your sight,  
Guide in the wilderness, be our guiding light.  

Good from Yahweh, you’ll share in this too,  
Journey with us, and He'll be good to you."  

Three days they traveled, Ark of Covenant ahead,  
Seeking a resting place, where they'd be led.  

Cloud of Yahweh by day, shading the way,  
From camp they marched, in His presence they stay.  

"Rise up, Yahweh," Moses declared,  
"Scatter the foes, let none be spared."  

When resting, he prayed, "Return to us now,  
To Israel's thousands, with mighty vow."
