The heads of the tribes came near, had something to say,  
Stood before Moses, no delay.  

"We got a command from Yahweh to keep it fair,  
Inheritance by lot, make sure it’s square.  

Zelophehad’s daughters got land in their name,  
But if they marry outside, things won’t stay the same.  

Their land will shift to another tribe's claim,  
Our fathers' share will lose its fame.  

When jubilee hits, their land will move,  
Our inheritance won’t be smooth."  

Moses replied, "The tribe of Joseph speaks true,  
Here's what Yahweh commands for you:  

‘Let Zelophehad’s daughters choose their own,  
But only within their father's zone.  

No land will jump from tribe to tribe,  
Keep the inheritance inside your vibe.  

Every daughter with land must marry near,  
Stay in the family, keep it clear.  

No inheritance will shift around,  
Each tribe will keep its ground.’"  

Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah, they heard the call,  
Married their kin, kept the land for all.  

Their inheritance stayed with Manasseh's line,  
The tribe's future, clear and fine.  

These are the words Yahweh spoke through Moses's hand,  
To guide the tribes in the promised land.
