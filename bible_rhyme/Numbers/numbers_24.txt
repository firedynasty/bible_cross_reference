Balaam saw pleasing Yahweh was the game,  
No divination now, just the wilderness to claim.  

Lifted his eyes, saw tribes in their places,  
Spirit of God came, filled him with graces.  

"Words of God I hear, vision of the Almighty,"  
"Falling down, eyes open, all truth in clarity."  

"How good are your tents, Jacob, Israel's homes,"  
"Like valleys, gardens, where clear water roams."  

"Water flows from his buckets, seeds in the stream,"  
"King higher than Agag, kingdom's esteem."  

"God brings him strong out, like a wild ox might,"  
"His foes' bones crushed, arrows take flight."  

"He lies like a lion, who dares wake his rest?"  
"Blessed are those who bless, cursed spurned at best."  

Balak’s anger flared, clapped his hands in wrath,  
"I called you to curse, but blessings you path."  

"Flee to your place, no honors await,"  
"Yahweh blocks your honor, seals your fate."  

Balaam replied, "Didn't I say it clear?"  
"Can't stray from Yahweh, His words I revere."  

"House filled with riches, silver, and gold,"  
"Can't change the message Yahweh has told."  

"I'll go to my people, but listen up now,"  
"I'll tell of the future, hear this vow."  

"Vision afar, a star from Jacob's side,"  
"Scepter rises, Moab crushed with pride."  

"Edom, Seir, shall fall to the hand,"  
"While Israel valiantly takes command."  

"Amalek's first but ends in demise,"  
"Kenite's stronghold, still wasted, it lies."  

"Kittim's ships will come, afflict the shore,"  
"Destruction follows, the end of war."  

Balaam stood, returned to his own space,  
Balak too left, with anger's trace.
