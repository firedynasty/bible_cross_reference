Israel camped by Moab’s plain,  
Where Balak saw what they had gained.  

Moab feared, their numbers vast,  
Said to Midian, "They won’t last."  

"An ox licks grass, so they consume,   
Balak’s reign may find its doom."  

Messengers sent to Balaam’s land,  
"Curse them, for they’re too grand."  

"From Egypt, they came to be,  
Covering earth like a vast sea."  

"Curse them, so I might win,  
Drive them out, take back my kin."  

Moab’s elders, rewards in hand,  
To Balaam spoke Balak’s command.  

"Lodge here, I’ll seek Yahweh’s voice,  
I’ll bring back His choice."  

God asked Balaam, "Who are these men?"  
"Brought by Balak, to curse with pen."  

"Don’t go, don’t curse, they’re blessed,"  
Balaam to Moab, "Yahweh says no quest."  

Princes returned, "He won’t engage,"  
Balak sent more with greater wage.  

"Come curse, gain great honor,  
I’ll do whatever you ponder."  

Balaam said, "Even treasure, I can’t defy,  
Yahweh’s word is my guide."  

"Stay, I'll seek Yahweh’s night reply,"  
God said, "Go, but heed My word, don’t lie."  

Balaam rose, saddled the donkey,  
God’s anger burned, angel stood ready.  

Donkey saw angel, sword in hand,  
Turned from the path, out to the land.  

Balaam struck, turned her back again,  
She saw the angel in the narrow lane.  

Crushed his foot, he struck with might,  
Angel blocked left and right.  

Donkey lay down, Balaam’s wrath grew,  
Struck her hard, but she knew.  

Yahweh opened her mouth to speak,  
"What did I do, for this harsh streak?"  

"You mocked me, if I had a sword,  
I’d have killed you, with this cord."  

"Am I not your trusty steed?  
Have I ever been in need?"  

"No," he admitted, then saw with fright,  
Yahweh’s angel in the light.  

"Why strike her thrice?" the angel asked,  
"Your perverse way is my task."  

"Donkey saved you, turned away thrice,  
Else you’d be gone, and she’d suffice."  

"I have sinned," Balaam confessed,  
"If it's wrong, I’ll turn, if you suggest."  

"Go, but speak what I say,"  
So Balaam went on his way.  

Balak met him at Moab’s gate,  
"Why delay? Honor awaits."  

Balaam said, "I’m here to speak,  
What God says, I will repeat."  

To Kiriath Huzoth they came,  
Balak sacrificed, in Yahweh’s name.  

Next morn, to Baal’s high place,  
Balaam saw the people’s face.
