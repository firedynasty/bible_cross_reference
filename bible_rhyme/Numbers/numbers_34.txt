Yo, Yahweh spoke to Moses, made it plain,  
"Tell Israel, when you hit Canaan, claim your domain.  

From the wilderness of Zin, your south side starts,  
Border runs east from the Salt Sea, mark the charts.  

Southward, up Akrabbim, loop through Zin,  
Past Kadesh Barnea, Hazar Addar, it begins.  

Swing from Azmon to Egypt's brook, you see,  
And there your border ends, at the sea.  

West side's the great sea, let it ride,  
That's your western edge, ocean-wide.  

Now north from the great sea, Mount Hor is your mark,  
To Hamath’s entrance, Zedad's where you embark.  

Ziphron to Hazar Enan, north line's complete,  
That's where your boundary takes a seat.  

East starts at Hazar Enan, down to Shepham true,  
Past Riblah, east of Ain, where it all comes through.  

Down to the Jordan, ending Salt Sea's embrace,  
Your land outlined, borders in place.  

Moses told Israel, "This land's your lot,  
Yahweh's gift to nine tribes, half-tribe's got a spot.  

Reuben, Gad, Manasseh already claimed their share,  
East of Jordan, Jericho, sunrise glare.  

Yahweh spoke again, said, "Check these names,  
Eleazar, Joshua, they'll play the dividing games.  

One prince per tribe, they’ll split it fair,  
Judah's got Caleb, Jephunneh's heir.  

Simeon's Shemuel, Ammihud's his dad,  
Benjamin's Elidad, Chislon's lad.  

Dan's got Bukki, son of Jogli’s crew,  
Manasseh's Hanniel, Ephod's son, true.  

Ephraim's got Kemuel, Shiphtan's child,  
Zebulun’s Elizaphan, Parnach's styled.  

Issachar's Paltiel, Azzan’s his kin,  
Asher’s Ahihud, Shelomi's within.  

Naphtali's Pedahel, Ammihud's seed,  
Yahweh's command, dividing's their deed.  

These are the ones, Yahweh decreed,  
To split Canaan's land, fulfill the need."
