Moses heard Yahweh's voice, clear and true,  
"Tell Israel this, here's what they should do.  

When a man or a woman vows to be a Nazir,  
Separate to Yahweh, let their path be clear.  

Leave wine and drink behind, no grapes, no seed,  
From vine to skin, nothing fermented to feed.  

Through all their days, no razor touch their head,  
Let their hair grow long, for Yahweh they're led.  

Stay away from the dead, keep clear, be set apart,  
Even family’s touch can’t defile the heart.  

If death should come close, and the vow is stained,  
On cleansing's day, shaved head remains.  

Two birds to the priest, on the eighth they bring,  
Sin and burnt offerings, atonement they sing.  

A year-old lamb, for trespass made right,  
For days now void, restore them to light.  

When the vow is fulfilled, to the Tent come near,  
Offer lambs without defect, peace is clear.  

Bread unleavened, cakes with oil mixed,  
Priest presents the offerings, everything fixed.  

The Nazirite shaves, hair on fire it lies,  
Under peace sacrifice, smoke to the skies.  

Priest takes the ram's shoulder, cakes, and wafer,  
Waves before Yahweh, holy hands of the shaver.  

Now the vow's complete, wine they can take,  
Nazirite law fulfilled, no mistake.  

Yahweh’s words to Moses, Aaron must hear,  
"Bless Israel now, make my promise clear.  

Yahweh bless you, keep you always near,  
Shine His face upon you, grace you hold dear.  

Lift His countenance, give you peace and rest,  
My name on Israel, through you they'll be blessed."
