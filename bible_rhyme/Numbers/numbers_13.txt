Yahweh spoke to Moses, here's what He said,  
"Send men to Canaan, see where you're led.  
Pick a prince from each tribe, a leader in rank,  
To spy out the land, from river to bank."

Moses obeyed, sent them out from Paran,  
Leaders of Israel, each a strong man.  
From Reuben, Shammua, son of Zaccur,  
Simeon's Shaphat, son of Hori, for sure.  

Judah's Caleb, son of Jephunneh, bold,  
Issachar's Igal, son of Joseph, we're told.  
Ephraim's Hoshea, son of Nun, strong and keen,  
Benjamin's Palti, son of Raphu, on the scene.  

Zebulun's Gaddiel, son of Sodi, stands,  
Manasseh's Gaddi, son of Susi, in the plans.  
Dan's Ammiel, son of Gemalli, in the zone,  
Asher's Sethur, Michael's son, not alone.  

Naphtali's Nahbi, son of Vophsi, strides,  
Gad's Geuel, son of Machi, he abides.  
Moses called Hoshea, Joshua by name,  
Sent them to Canaan, fortune or fame.  

"Head south," he said, "and climb high,  
Check out the land, strong or weak, tell me why.  
See the cities, camps or strongholds they keep,  
The soil, the wood, the secrets that sleep."

So up they went, from Zin's wild sand,  
To Rehob's gates, surveying the land.  
To Hebron they trekked, where Anak's sons stay,  
Grapes and figs from Eshcol, carried away.

Forty days later, they came back to report,  
To Moses and Aaron, told tales of the sort.  
"The land flows with honey, milk so sweet,  
But giants and walls, make us retreat."

Caleb rose up, stilled the crowd's fear,  
"Let's go and take it, the victory's near!"  
But the others said, "They're stronger than we,  
The land eats its own, it's not meant to be."

"The Nephilim are there, giants so tall,  
We felt like grasshoppers, ready to fall."  
Yet their tale spread fear, a shadow so wide,  
As the children of Israel, trembled and sighed.
