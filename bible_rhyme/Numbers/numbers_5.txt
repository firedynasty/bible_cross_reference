Yahweh spoke to Moses, in a voice so clear,  
"Take the unclean out, don't let them near.  
Every leper, each one with a discharge,  
And those who've touched death, must exit large.  

Male and female, put them outside,  
So in this camp, My presence won't collide."  
The children of Israel did what was said,  
As Yahweh commanded, they moved ahead.  

God spoke again, "If anyone sins,  
Confess and make it right, let the restitution begin.  
Add a fifth to the guilt, and pay it back,  
If no one's left to pay, to the priests it’ll track.  

The heave offerings, they belong to the priest,  
Every holy gift, from greatest to least."  
Yahweh spoke more, "If a wife’s untrue,  
And it’s hidden, but suspicion’s in view,  

Bring her to the priest with an offering in hand,  
No oil or incense, just barley—understand.  
The priest will take dust, and holy water in a cup,  
Before Yahweh she’ll stand, with her hair all messed up.  

He’ll speak these words, as serious as can be,  
‘If you're faithful, then drink, and you’ll go free.  
But if you’ve strayed, this water will make you curse,  
Your body will change, as it quenches your thirst.’  

She’ll say, ‘Amen, Amen,’ to the spoken decree,  
The priest writes the curse, in the water it’ll be.  
She drinks it down, and if guilt’s on her soul,  
Her body will show it, take its toll.  

But if she’s clean, she’ll be free to bear life,  
This law of jealousy cuts like a knife.  
Before Yahweh, the truth will unfold,  
Iniquity will bear its weight, as the story is told."
