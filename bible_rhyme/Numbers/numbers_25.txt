In Shittim, Israel stayed, but trouble was ahead,  
They fell for Moab's daughters, into mischief they led.  

Called to false gods' altars, they ate, kneeled, and bowed,  
Joined with Baal Peor, and Yahweh's wrath was loud.  

God told Moses, "Hang the chiefs up high,  
Let my anger turn away, let justice touch the sky."  

Moses said to the judges, "Deal with every man,  
Who followed Baal Peor, strike them where they stand."  

Then came a man with a Midianite dame,  
Right before all eyes, no shame in their game.  

Phinehas saw it, his heart in a flame,  
Grabbed his spear, to put an end to the shame.  

He struck them both, stopped the plague's cruel course,  
Twenty-four thousand gone, oh the remorse.  

God spoke to Moses, this is what He said,  
"Phinehas has turned my wrath, no more bloodshed.  

For his zeal, my covenant of peace he receives,  
An everlasting priesthood, for all his family's leaves."  

The man was Zimri, from Simeon's line,  
The woman, Cozbi, Midian's design.  

God told Moses, "Fight the Midianite fight,  
For their deceit with Peor, set things right."
