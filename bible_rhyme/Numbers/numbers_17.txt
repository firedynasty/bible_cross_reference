Yahweh spoke to Moses, his voice clear and bold,  
"Gather rods from each tribe, let the story unfold.  
One for each house, twelve rods in line,  
Each name written clearly, each sign to define.  

Aaron's name on Levi's, for that tribe's head,  
Place them in the Tent where my presence is spread.  
The rod that buds, it's the one I've chosen,  
Silence the murmurs, their complaints will be frozen."  

Moses spoke, and each prince gave,  
Twelve rods in total, each tribe's stave.  
Aaron's rod there among the rest,  
In the Tent before Yahweh, the ultimate test.  

Next day came, Moses entered the Tent,  
Behold Aaron's rod! Blossoms it sent.  
Sprouted, budded, bloomed, almonds ripe,  
A miracle shown, an end to the gripe.  

Moses brought the rods, showed them all,  
Each man took his rod, saw Aaron's call.  
Yahweh said, "Put Aaron's rod back in its place,  
A sign against rebellion, to limit their space."  

Moses did just as Yahweh had bade,  
To quell their complaints, the foundation laid.  
The people cried out, "We're doomed, we're lost!  
Approach the tabernacle, and we'll pay the cost!  

Will we all perish, is this our end?"  
With fear in their hearts, on Yahweh they depend.
