Samuel spoke, Israel listened, ready for war,  
Against the Philistines, camped by Ebenezer's door.  
The Philistines stood tall, ready for the fight,  
Israel went down hard, four thousand lost that night.  

Back at camp, elders cried, "Why this defeat?  
Let’s fetch Yahweh's ark, bring victory on our feet."  
To Shiloh they sent, for the ark of His might,  
With Eli’s sons, they brought it back in sight.  

When the ark arrived, Israel shouted loud,  
The earth shook, echoing the proud.  
Philistines heard and trembled in fear,  
"The ark of God is here!" they cried, “Woe, oh dear!”  

“They struck Egypt once, can we withstand their power?  
We must fight strong, this is our hour!”  
So they braced for battle, with courage they fought,  
Israel fled, thirty thousand lives there were caught.  

The ark was seized, Eli’s sons fell,  
A messenger ran, their fate to tell.  
Eli sat waiting, heart tense with dread,  
Hearing news of the ark, he fell and was dead.  

Phinehas' wife, near birth, heard the news,  
Her husband gone, she had much to lose.  
She gave birth and named her child with a sigh,  
Ichabod, “God’s glory has left,” was her cry.  

"The glory has departed," she whispered, forlorn,  
For the ark was lost, leaving Israel torn.
