David rolled into Nob, met Ahimelech, the priest,  
Trembling came Ahimelech, asked, "Why you here, solo beast?"  

David replied, "The king's command's secret, see,  
Sent my young men ahead, now it's just you and me.”  

He asked, "What's on hand? Five loaves, if you got ‘em,  
Or whatever you can spare, I'm here at the bottom."  

Ahimelech said, "No common bread to share,  
Only holy bread, if your men stayed fair."  

David assured, "Three days pure, no women near,  
Even on a common road, in holiness we steer."  

The priest handed him holy bread, no other kind,  
Only the show bread, hot bread left behind.  

Doeg the Edomite, Saul's best herdsman, lingered there,  
Caught in a moment, detained in Yahweh's care.  

David asked, "Got a spear or sword to spare?  
The king's haste left me unarmed, caught unaware."  

Priest said, "Goliath's sword is here, wrapped tight,  
Take it if you want, no weapon matches its might."  

David claimed it, fled from Saul's fierce wrath,  
Headed for Achish, king of Gath.  

Achish's servants whispered, "Ain't this David, the king?  
They sang of him in dances, his praises they'd sing."  

David heard and feared, heart gripped with dread,  
Acted insane, scribbled, spittle down his head.  

Achish scoffed, "You bring me a madman to see?  
Am I short of fools? Why bring him to me?"  

With that, David's ruse bought him a reprieve,  
Madness his cover, he was allowed to leave.
