David thought in his heart, "One day, Saul's hand  
Will end me, so I'll flee to Philistine land.  
With Saul in despair, searching no more,  
I'll escape his grasp, be free, for sure."  

So David arose, with six hundred bold,  
To Gath's king Achish, like a story told.  
With his wives and men, he settled there,  
In Achish's land, without a care.  

Saul heard the news, David had fled,  
He stopped his pursuit, thought David was dead.  
David to Achish made a plea,  
"Let me live in a town, away from royalty."  

Achish gave him Ziklag, a place to reside,  
Ziklag, a gift now, in Judah's pride.  
For a year and four months, David stayed,  
Raiding the lands, in the Philistine shade.  

Geshurites, Girzites, Amalekites too,  
David struck them down, none were left to view.  
Sheep, cattle, donkeys, camels he'd take,  
No man or woman left for truth to break.  

Back to Achish, who asked, "Whom did you fight?"  
"South of Judah, Jerahmeelites, Kenites," was his light.  
No captives alive, to Gath he brought,  
"None to tell tales, none to report."  

Achish believed him, said with glee,  
"Israel hates him, he'll serve only me."
