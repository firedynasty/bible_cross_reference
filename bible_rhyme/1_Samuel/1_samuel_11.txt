Nahash the Ammonite came to Jabesh's gate,  
Men of Jabesh pleaded, “Serve you, if you let us relate.”  

Nahash replied, “Only if your right eyes are torn,  
I’ll disgrace all Israel, leave them forlorn.”  

Jabesh elders said, “Give us seven days to see,  
If no help arrives, we’ll bow to your decree.”  

Messengers ran to Gibeah, spoke to the crowd,  
Tears filled their eyes, weeping so loud.  

Saul came from the field, asked, “Why the cries?”  
Heard Jabesh's plight, anger did rise.  

God’s Spirit filled Saul, his rage did ignite,  
He cut oxen in pieces, sent them in the night.  

“Come after Saul, follow Samuel’s call,  
Or your oxen will suffer, the same fate befall.”  

Fear of Yahweh united them, as one they did stand,  
Three hundred thousand with Judah’s mighty band.  

Messengers said, “Jabesh, rescue comes ‘morrow,  
By the sun’s heat, no more sorrow.”  

Jabesh replied, “Tomorrow, we’ll come out to you,  
Do what seems good; let’s see what you’ll do.”  

At dawn Saul divided them, three companies strong,  
Struck the Ammonites, battles did throng.  

By noon’s heat, the foe scattered far and wide,  
No two together, nowhere to hide.  

Voices rose, “Who doubted Saul’s reign?  
Bring them here, let them feel the pain!”  

Saul declared, “No death today,  
Yahweh has saved Israel in a mighty way.”  

Samuel said, “To Gilgal, let’s renew the crown,”  
There they made Saul king, peace offerings went down.  

In Gilgal, joy echoed, sacrifices laid,  
Saul and Israel rejoiced, a kingdom remade.
