Samuel mourning, tears for Saul,  
God said, "Move on, I've made the call.  

Fill your horn, take some oil, go to Jesse's place,  
Among his sons, a new king I'll embrace."  

Samuel worried, "Saul will kill me if he knows."  
God replied, "Take a heifer, let the purpose be the shows."  

"Call Jesse to the sacrifice, I'll guide your hand,  
Anoint the one I'll choose, as I've planned."  

Samuel did as God commanded, reached Bethlehem's gate,  
Elders trembling, "Do you come in peace or with fate?"  

"Peacefully," said Samuel, "Sanctify, prepare,  
Join the sacrifice, Jesse and sons, I'll see them there."  

Eliab stood tall, Samuel thought he was the one,  
But God said, "Don't judge by looks, his heart's not won."  

Abinadab passed by, but God shook his head,  
Then came Shammah, "Not this one," He said.  

Seven sons presented, none were the king,  
Samuel asked, "Got any left? Let the youngest bring."  

David tending sheep, summoned to the scene,  
Ruddy with handsome features, eyes keen.  

God declared, "Arise! Anoint this one, he's the key."  
Samuel poured the oil, God's Spirit stirred mightily.  

Saul's spirit troubled, an evil one near,  
His servants whispered, "Let's find peace, draw him near."  

"Seek a harp player, skilled with the sound,  
To soothe Saul's spirit when the evil comes around."  

One servant spoke, "Jesse's son, David's his name,  
A warrior, wise talker, with him God's flame."  

Saul sent for David, a donkey loaded for the trip,  
Bread, wine, young goat, in Jesse's grip.  

David stood before Saul, found in his eyes,  
Became armor bearer, winning favor, no disguise.  

When the spirit troubled Saul, David played his harp’s grace,  
Saul felt refreshed, evil spirit erased.
