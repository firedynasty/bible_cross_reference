Seven months Philistines had the ark in their land,  
Calling priests and diviners to lend a hand,  

"What to do with Yahweh's ark?" they asked,  
"Where to send it, what's the task?"  

"Don't send it empty, give a trespass fee,  
Five golden tumors, and mice there'll be."  

For the lords of Philistine, for the plague on you all,  
Images to glorify Israel's God so tall.  

"Don't harden your hearts like Pharaoh's crew,  
Release His hand, let it not trouble you."  

"Prepare a new cart, two cows without yoke,  
Tie them there, separate calves—no joke."  

Place the ark on the cart, the gold by its side,  
Send it on its way, let Yahweh guide.  

"If it finds its border, Beth Shemesh ahead,  
Then it's He who brought this dread."  

So the men took the cows, tied them with care,  
Put the ark on the cart, offering to bear.  

Cows headed straight, lowing all the way,  
Philistine lords followed, in dismay.  

Beth Shemesh folk harvesting in the field,  
Eyes lifted, saw the ark revealed.  

The cart stopped in Joshua's land so wide,  
Wood split, cows sacrificed, nothing to hide.  

Levites took the ark, placed it on the stone,  
Offered sacrifices, worship shown.  

Philistine lords saw and went back home,  
Ark returned, and the message was known.  

Golden tumors, golden mice, cities all aligned,  
To the great stone where Yahweh's ark reclined.  

But they peeked inside, Beth Shemesh men,  
Yahweh struck them, mourning began.  

"Who can stand before this holy God?" they cried,  
"To whom shall the ark go?" they sighed.  

They called Kiriath Jearim folks, "Come here,  
The Philistines returned the ark, come take it near."
