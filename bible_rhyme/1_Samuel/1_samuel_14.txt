Jonathan whispers, "Let's roll to the Philistine's base,  
Keep it quiet, don't tell Dad, let's pick up the pace."  

Saul's under a pomegranate tree, just chillin' with six hundred men,  
Ahijah the priest is with them, wearing the ephod again.  

Jonathan and his armor bearer squeeze through rocky crags,  
Seneh and Bozez are their names, these rocks have their own tags.  

Jonathan says, "Let's confront those uncircumcised,  
Yahweh's got no limits, many or few, we'll rise."  

His armor bearer nods, "I'm with you, heart to heart,  
Let's go to those Philistines, let's play our part."  

"If they call us to come, that's our sign, Yahweh's plan,  
If they come to us, we stay put, that's the way it’ll stand."  

They showed themselves, Philistines mocked, "Come up, let's see,  
Jonathan climbs, armor bearer follows, with Yahweh's decree."  

Jonathan slays, armor bearer cleans up, twenty men down,  
Tremors shake the ground, Philistines wear a frown.  

Saul's lookout sees the chaos, "Who's missing? Do a call,"  
Jonathan and his armor bearer, not with Saul.  

"Bring the Ark," says Saul, the priest's hand withdraws,  
Philistines in confusion, it's time to hit 'em with force.  

Hebrews in Philistine camp flip sides,  
Israelites from Ephraim's hills join the tide.  

Yahweh saves the day, yet Saul's curse lies thick,  
No food till sunset, his rule's got a trick.  

Honey drips in the woods, people fear the oath,  
Jonathan tastes, eyes bright, breaking the troth.  

"Father's troubled the land," Jonathan cries,  
"If we had eaten, more Philistines would lie."  

The folks are faint, pounce on plunder,  
Eating with blood, Saul's anger starts to thunder.  

"Roll a stone," he cries, "Eat right, don't sin,  
Kill at the altar, cleanse from within."  

Saul plans a night raid, but God stays silent,  
"Let's find the sin," Saul says, determined and defiant.  

Lot falls on Jonathan, who says with a sigh,  
"I tasted the honey, now I must die."  

Saul declares, "You will surely fall,"  
The people rise up, "No way, not at all!"  

Jonathan saved from death by the people's plea,  
Philistines retreat, they set themselves free.  

Saul fights all around, takes on every foe,  
Philistines, Moab, and more, wherever he'd go.  

His sons, his daughters, his captains, they stand,  
But Saul's eyes always open for mighty men across the land.
