Young Samuel served before Yahweh's own,  
Eli old, with vision nearly gone.  

Rare were visions, God's word was still,  
In the temple, God’s lamp hadn't gone nil.  

Samuel laid where the ark had its place,  
Yahweh called out in the quiet space.  

"Here I am," Samuel ran to Eli’s side,  
"I didn't call," Eli replied, "let it slide."  

Once more, "Samuel!" the call was clear,  
Again to Eli, "I didn't call, my dear."  

Samuel knew not Yahweh's voice yet,  
But Eli perceived what Samuel met.  

Eli advised, "Next time, just say,  
'Speak, Yahweh; your servant hears today.'"  

Yahweh called, "Samuel! Samuel!" He said,  
"Speak; for your servant hears," Samuel pled.  

"Behold," said Yahweh, "a thing I shall do,  
Israel will tingle when they hear it too.  

Against Eli's house, it's all set to unfold,  
The iniquity known, the truth must be told."  

Samuel lay till dawn, afraid to share,  
Eli called, "My son, what did Yahweh declare?"  

"Tell me all, hide nothing from my view,"  
Samuel told it all, Eli knew it was true.  

"It is Yahweh, let Him do what's best,"  
Samuel grew, God’s words he possessed.  

From Dan to Beersheba, all knew the name,  
Samuel, a prophet, in Yahweh's fame.  

Yahweh returned, in Shiloh’s light,  
Revealed to Samuel, God's word in sight.
