Philistines on the rise, Israel in retreat,  
Mount Gilboa where they faced defeat.  
Saul's sons they fell, Jonathan too,  
Philistines took them, nothing they could do.  

Arrows flew, Saul was distressed,  
He called to his bearer, "End this, bring me rest."  
But fear gripped him, the task he declined,  
So Saul took his sword, a tragic end defined.  

His armor bearer followed, loyal to the core,  
Saul and sons gone, their story no more.  
Israel saw, fled cities, left behind,  
Philistines moved in, new homes to find.  

Next day the victors came, to strip the dead,  
Saw Saul's broken body, took his head.  
Armor displayed in Ashtaroth's shrine,  
Body on Beth Shan, a dark design.  

Jabesh Gilead heard of Saul's fate,  
Valiant men rose, never too late.  
Through the night they marched, on a rescue quest,  
Took bodies home, laid them to rest.  

Bones buried under tamarisk's shade,  
Seven days they fasted, farewell made.
