David and his crew came back to Ziklag on the third,  
Only to find it burned, a sight so absurd.  
The Amalekites raided, took the young and the old,  
Left the place in ashes, their plan so bold.  

No lives were lost, but captives they did take,  
David's heart sank, for his wives were at stake.  
The men cried out, their sorrow so deep,  
Until they had no more tears left to weep.  

In distress and fear, whispers of blame,  
They talked of stoning him, speaking his name.  
But David turned to God, heart full of might,  
Strengthened by Yahweh, ready to fight.  

He called for Abiathar, "Bring the ephod here,"  
And sought the Lord's guidance, his path to clear.  
"Should I chase them down? Will I win this race?"  
"Pursue them," God said, "you’ll reclaim your place."  

So David set out with six hundred strong,  
But two hundred were faint, couldn't go along.  
With four hundred left, they crossed the brook,  
In search of their families, every corner they'd look.  

Found an Egyptian, weak in the field,  
Fed him well, his secrets revealed.  
"I was left for sick, a servant's fate,  
We raided Ziklag, now I narrate."  

"Can you lead us there?" David did inquire,  
"Swear you won't kill me," was his lone desire.  
The Egyptian agreed, led David right down,  
To find the raiders spread all around.  

Feasting and dancing on the spoils they had,  
David struck them down, his wrath was mad.  
From twilight till evening, he fought with skill,  
Except for four hundred who fled on camels, quick to spill.  

David reclaimed all, not a thing was missed,  
His wives, the young, their plundered list.  
"This is David's bounty," the people said,  
As they returned with flocks, well fed.  

To the ones who stayed behind, David shared,  
"We split this evenly, as Yahweh cared."  
"From this day forward, let it be known,  
In battle or baggage, the share's our own."  

David sent gifts, shared the joy and the gain,  
To Judah's elders, they received his reign.  
To Bethel, Ramoth, and places of old,  
To all who supported, their friendship bold.
