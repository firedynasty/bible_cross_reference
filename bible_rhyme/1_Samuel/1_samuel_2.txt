Hannah prayed with a heart full of joy,  
In Yahweh she found strength, no one could destroy.  
"My mouth's wide with triumph, my foes in retreat,  
In Your salvation, my heart skips a beat.  

No one holy like Yahweh, no rock like our Lord,  
Proud words are empty, let humility be stored.  
Yahweh's knowledge weighs every act,  
Mighty bows break, while the weak gain tact.  

Those once full, now work for their bread,  
The hungry are fed, the barren has spread.  
Yahweh brings life and takes it away,  
He humbles and lifts, come what may.  

From dust to thrones, the needy He lifts,  
Raising them high with royal gifts.  
For the earth's pillars belong to Yahweh’s might,  
The wicked in darkness, silenced from sight.  

Those who clash with Yahweh will shatter and fall,  
He thunders on high, His justice for all.  
Strength to His king, His anointed He'll raise,  
Elkanah returned home, giving God the praise.  

But Eli's sons were wicked, they knew not the Lord,  
Priests taking the offerings they could not afford.  
With forks they took, not waiting their turn,  
Despising God's gifts, they refused to learn.  

Yet Samuel served, clothed in linen fine,  
With a robe from his mother, year after year in line.  
Eli blessed them, praying for more,  
Yahweh answered, giving children galore.  

Eli's sons sinned, he heard of their deeds,  
Sleeping with women, ignoring his pleas.  
"Why commit sins, bringing shame to our name?"  
But they ignored him, seeking no change.  

A man of God came with a message so stark,  
"Didn't I choose you, give you a mark?  
Why disrespect My offerings, choosing your kin?  
Now, far be it from Me, for you've let sin in.  

Your house will fall, no old man will last,  
Sorrow will come, your glory will pass.  
Hophni and Phinehas, in one day will die,  
I’ll raise a priest true, under My eye.  

Those left will bow, seeking bread and plea,  
For honor and hope in My presence to be."
