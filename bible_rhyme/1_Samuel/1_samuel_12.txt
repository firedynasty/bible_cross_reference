Samuel stepped up, addressed the crowd,  
"I've heard your cries, and made you proud.  
A king now walks before your eyes,  
I'm old and gray, but wise, I advise.  

Witness now, before the Lord and king,  
Did I take your ox, your donkey, anything?  
Did I defraud, oppress, or take a bribe?  
If so, I'll restore and make it right."  

"You've done us no wrong," the people said,  
"Yahweh's witness, let it be spread."  
Samuel reminded, "Moses, Aaron too,  
Led you out of Egypt, remember their view.  

But once you forgot, God let foes reign,  
Sisera, Philistines, Moab's claim.  
You cried out, confessed sins and plea,  
God sent Jerubbaal, Bedan, and me.  

When Nahash came, you sought a king,  
Though Yahweh was your everything.  
Behold the king you chose in haste,  
But honor God, or face disgrace.  

Stand still, and watch this mighty scene,  
It's wheat harvest, the sky will glean.  
I'll call to Yahweh, thunder will roll,  
To show how your wickedness takes its toll."  

Samuel called, and thunder did roar,  
Rain poured down, fear struck their core.  
"Pray for us, Samuel!" the people cried,  
"To Yahweh, lest we die inside."  

"Fear not, though you've done wrong,  
Stay with Yahweh, keep your hearts strong.  
Don't chase after things that won't fulfill,  
Yahweh’s with you, He loves you still.  

I'll keep praying, teach you His way,  
Fear Yahweh truly, let your heart obey.  
Consider His deeds, so mighty, so grand,  
But stray into evil, and you'll lose this land."
