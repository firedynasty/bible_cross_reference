Samuel poured the oil, let it drip and glide,  
Kissed his head, said, "You're the prince, no need to hide.  

When you leave me, find two men by Rachel's tomb,  
They'll say, 'The donkeys are found, and your dad's in gloom.'  

Head to Tabor's oak, three men you'll greet,  
Goats, bread, and wine they carry, gifts so sweet.  

They'll hand you loaves, just take it, no fight,  
Next stop, God's hill, where prophets ignite.  

You'll join in with music, a lute, a tambourine,  
Spirit of Yahweh strong, you'll see what I mean.  

You'll prophesy with them, be changed inside,  
God's spirit with you, no need to hide.  

"Go to Gilgal, wait for me there,  
In seven days, I'll show you with care."  

Saul turned away, with a heart brand new,  
Prophets met him, God's spirit flew.  

People asked, "Is Saul a prophet too?"  
Confused, they wondered, "What's he gonna do?"  

Uncle inquired, "Where have you been?"  
Saul replied, "Off to find donkeys again."  

He kept the kingdom talk hidden away,  
Samuel gathered the tribes for Yahweh's say.  

"You asked for a king, rejected God above,  
Now stand before Yahweh, show him your love."  

Benjamin was chosen, Saul was the one,  
But he hid in the baggage, avoiding the sun.  

They found him there, taller than the rest,  
Samuel said, "See the one Yahweh's blessed?"  

People cheered, "Long live the king!" they cried,  
Samuel wrote the rules, then sent them aside.  

Saul went home, with followers true,  
But some doubted, "What can this guy do?"  

Yet Saul held his peace, with patience he stood,  
While Yahweh's plan unfolded as it should.
