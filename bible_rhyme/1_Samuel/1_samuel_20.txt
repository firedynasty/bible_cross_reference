David ran to Jonathan, his heart full of dread,  
"What have I done wrong? Why do I feel dead?"  
Jonathan replied, "Nah, you won't die.  
Dad would tell me, he's not that sly."  

David insisted, "He hides things from you,  
He knows we're close, he'd keep it from view.  
Just one step from death, I swear it's true,  
By Yahweh, I'm telling you."  

Jonathan pledged, "I'll do what you need."  
David planned, "At the new moon, I must take heed.  
Hide in the field, for three days wait,  
If Saul's calm, I'm safe, if not, checkmate."  

"Deal kindly," David asked, "We're in covenant bound,  
If there's sin in me, let me hit the ground."  
Jonathan replied, "No way, my friend,  
If danger comes, I'll warn you to the end."  

"Who'll tell me if Saul's feeling rough?"  
Jonathan answered, "Let's get out in the brush."  
He said, "By God, I'll find out Dad's mood,  
If he's kind or if things have turned rude."  

"If bad news comes, I'll let you know,  
Yahweh be with you, wherever you go.  
Show kindness to me, and my house too,  
When all your foes have been subdued."  

Jonathan made a pact, sealed it tight,  
He loved David, as his own soul's light.  
"Tomorrow's the feast, your absence will show,  
Three arrows I'll shoot, to let you know."  

"If close, you're safe, return with pride,  
If far, then flee, God's words will guide."  
David hid, the new moon came,  
Saul sat down, noticed David's name.  

Thought, "Must be unclean," said not a word,  
Next day asked Jonathan, his anger stirred.  
Jonathan explained David's absence, with care,  
Saul's wrath flared up, a heated affair.  

"You shame our name, as long as he breathes,  
Your throne stays vacant, like fallen leaves."  
Jonathan asked, "Why kill the innocent?"  
Saul hurled his spear, his fury spent.  

Jonathan left, anger in his stride,  
Grieved for David, his loyalty tried.  
He met him in the field, arrows flew,  
A sign for David, of what to do.  

He sent the boy, arrows gathered tight,  
Only Jonathan and David knew that night.  
The boy departed, David rose in tears,  
They embraced, understanding their fears.  

Jonathan said, "Go in peace, my kin,  
Yahweh's sworn between us, through thick and thin."  
David left, the bond remained true,  
Jonathan turned, back to the city he withdrew.
