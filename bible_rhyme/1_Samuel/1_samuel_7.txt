The men of Kiriath Jearim came real quick,  
Took Yahweh's ark, up the hill, it was slick.  
Into Abinadab’s house, it found its rest,  
Eleazar consecrated, to guard it with zest.  

For twenty long years, the ark stayed in place,  
Israel lamented, seeking Yahweh’s grace.  
Samuel spoke up, to Israel's hearts,  
"Turn to Yahweh, that's where it starts.  

Put away idols, serve Him alone,  
He’ll deliver you, make His power known."  
Israel listened, cleared out the old,  
Served Yahweh only, their faith emboldened.  

Samuel called, "Meet me at Mizpah's gate,  
I'll pray to Yahweh, let's set things straight."  
They gathered, they fasted, water poured down,  
Confessed their sins, as Samuel wore the crown.  

Philistines heard, they gathered with might,  
Israel feared, in the looming fight.  
They pleaded with Samuel, "Cry out to our God,  
To save us from their threatening squad."  

Samuel took a lamb, offered it whole,  
Cried to Yahweh, pouring out his soul.  
As the Philistines neared, ready for war,  
Yahweh thundered loudly, shook them to the core.  

Israel chased them, struck them down hard,  
Pushed them back, 'neath Beth Kar's guard.  
Samuel set a stone, Ebenezer its name,  
"Yahweh helped us," they all proclaimed.  

Philistines subdued, stopped crossing the line,  
Yahweh's hand held firm, in Samuel's time.  
Cities restored, peace with Amorites,  
Samuel judged, through days and nights.  

Yearly he traveled, Bethel to Mizpah round,  
Judgment and justice in every town found.  
Back to Ramah, his home so dear,  
Built an altar to Yahweh, year by year.
