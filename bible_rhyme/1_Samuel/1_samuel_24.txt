Saul came back from the Philistine's chase,  
He heard David's hiding in En Gedi's wild place.  

Three thousand men, Saul gathered, on the run,  
To find David, hiding from the sun.  

To the sheep pens, Saul went for some rest,  
Unknowing, in the cave, David's close, a test.  

David's men whispered, "Today's the day,  
Yahweh said your enemy's in your way."  

David crept close, cut Saul's robe with ease,  
But guilt hit hard, like a strong, fierce breeze.  

"Yahweh forbid," he said with regret,  
"To harm the anointed, that's not a safe bet."  

David held back his men, kept the peace,  
Saul left the cave, the tension ceased.  

David stepped out, shouted to Saul,  
"My lord, the king!" He made his call.  

Face to the ground, he showed respect,  
"Why believe their lies, and reject?"  

"Yahweh delivered you here today,  
But I spared you, chose the righteous way."  

"My father, see your robe in hand,  
No evil here, but you misunderstand."  

"Yahweh judge between us, true and grand,  
But my hand won't harm, I'm not that man."  

"Who does the king pursue, a dog, a flea?  
Let Yahweh decide, deliver me."  

Saul wept, his voice trembled and shook,  
"Is that you, David?" A softer look.  

"You're more righteous than me, you've done me good,  
When I sought evil, you never would."  

"Yahweh delivered me, yet you let me be,  
May He reward your kindness to me."  

"I know now you'll be king, the kingdom's yours,  
Swear you won't erase my legacy's course."  

David swore, Saul left on his way,  
But David stayed strong, in the stronghold to stay.
