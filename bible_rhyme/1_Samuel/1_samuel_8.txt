Samuel got old, his sons took the stand,  
But they strayed from his path, corrupted the land.  
In Beersheba they judged, but they chased after gold,  
Took bribes, twisted justice, breaking the mold.  

Israel's elders came, gathered in Ramah's town,  
"You're old, your sons fail, so bring us a crown.  
Make us like others, a king on the throne,"  
But Samuel was troubled, felt all alone.  

He prayed to Yahweh, seeking His say,  
Yahweh spoke clear, "Listen, don't dismay.  
They reject not you, but me as their King,  
Since Egypt they've turned, now doing this thing.  

Hear their demand, but make sure they know,  
The king's heavy hand, how his power will grow."  
Samuel warned them, spoke Yahweh's decree,  
"The king will take sons, for his chariots they'll be.  

Captains and soldiers, harvesting lands,  
Crafting his weapons, working with hands.  
Daughters for perfume, baking, and more,  
Fields and vineyards, he'll take and store.  

A tenth of your seed, vineyards and fruit,  
To officers, servants, he'll recruit.  
Your servants and flocks, he'll claim as his own,  
You'll be his servants, your freedoms overthrown.  

You'll cry out loud, for the king you chose,  
But Yahweh won't hear, you'll reap what you sow."  
Yet the people refused, their hearts were set,  
"We want a king, like the others, no regret.  

To judge us, to lead, to fight our fight,"  
Samuel took their words, repeated them right.  
Yahweh said, "Let them have their way,  
Give them a king, as they say."  

Samuel sent them all back to their home,  
Israel would have a king, not alone.
