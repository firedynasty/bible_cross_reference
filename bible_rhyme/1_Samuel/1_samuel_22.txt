David fled to Adullam's cave, on the run,  
His brothers and kin heard, and they all came as one.  
Distressed, in debt, discontented souls,  
Gathered round David, he took on control.  

Four hundred men, a mighty crew,  
David led them, knew what to do.  
To Moab he traveled, his parents in tow,  
"Let them stay here till God's plan I know."  

While with Moab's king, they found refuge strong,  
Gad the prophet spoke, "Judah is where you belong."  
David moved to Hereth, in the forest he stayed,  
Meanwhile Saul heard where David had laid.  

Saul sat in Gibeah, spear in hand,  
Questioning servants, trying to understand.  
"Will Jesse's son give you fields and rank?  
Yet you all conspire, keeping my flank."  

Doeg the Edomite, eager to please,  
Told Saul about David, his escape with ease.  
"Ahimelech helped him, gave him bread,  
And Goliath's sword, the Philistine dread."  

Saul summoned the priests, with fury and fire,  
"Why conspire with David, my wrath you'd inspire?"  
Ahimelech pleaded, "David's loyal and true,  
Unaware of his plan, it's not what I knew."  

Saul's anger burned, "You shall die,  
You and your house, under Yahweh's sky."  
His guards hesitated, fearing the guilt,  
But Doeg struck down, the innocent spilt.  

Nob was destroyed, a city in pain,  
Priests, women, children, all were slain.  
But Abiathar escaped, David's side he found,  
"Saul killed Yahweh's priests," he said, heart-pounding sound.  

David felt the weight, "I knew Doeg would tell,  
Their deaths are on me, in this living hell.  
Stay by my side, fear not, my friend,  
With me you're safe, till this chase will end."
