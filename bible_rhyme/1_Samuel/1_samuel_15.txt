Samuel approached Saul with Yahweh’s decree,  
"Listen now, you’re the king, anointed by me.  

Remember Amalek and their old attack,  
Go destroy them all, leave nothing to come back."  

Saul gathered troops, counted them all,  
Two hundred thousand strong, heeding the call.  

To Amalek’s city, Saul set the scene,  
Ambushed the valley, his intent was keen.  

To the Kenites he said, "Depart from their side,  
You showed us kindness, now go and hide."  

Saul struck Amalek down, from Havilah to Shur,  
Spared Agag, but destruction was meant to be pure.  

Kept the best cattle, the sheep and the lambs,  
Ignored God’s command, took riches in hands.  

Yahweh’s word to Samuel, heavy with grief,  
Saul has turned, causing me disbelief.  

Samuel confronted Saul, who claimed to obey,  
But sounds of livestock gave his words away.  

Saul blamed the people, said they’d sacrifice,  
"But Yahweh prefers obedience over vice."  

"Rebellion’s like witchcraft, stubbornness like sin,  
Since you rejected God, He’s rejecting your kin."  

Saul admitted wrong, "I feared men, not the Lord,  
Please pardon my sin, to worship restore."  

Samuel refused, "Yahweh’s word you did spurn,  
He’s torn the kingdom, you get no return."  

Saul’s robe tore, as Samuel turned away,  
"The kingdom’s given to a better man today."  

Saul pleaded again, "Honor me before my own,  
Come back with me, let my worship be known."  

Samuel went with Saul, setting his face,  
Then Agag was brought, with deceptive grace.  

Samuel spoke sharp, "Your sword made women cry,  
Now it’s your mother’s turn, and here you die."  

Samuel cut Agag, judgment was swift,  
Both parted ways, in somber rift.  

Samuel mourned, saw Saul nevermore,  
Yahweh grieved the king He once bore.
