The Ziphites whispered to Saul, "David hides on the hill,  
In the desert's shadow, waiting, keeping still."  

Saul got up, took his three thousand men,  
Into Ziph’s wild lands, to find David again.  

He camped on Hachilah's hill, by desert's side,  
David watched from afar, knew he couldn't hide.  

Sent out his spies, learned Saul was near,  
Gathered his courage, banished his fear.  

David asked Ahimelech, Abishai, his crew,  
"Who'll go with me to where Saul's army grew?"  

Abishai stepped up, "Brother, I'll stand,  
With you at night in Saul's encamped land."  

So, by night, they crept, where Saul lay still,  
Spear by his head, sleeping, no ill will.  

Abishai whispered, "God delivered him here,  
Let me pin him down with one thrust, no fear."  

But David said, "No harm to God's chosen,  
Can't strike the anointed, leave him unbroken."  

"As the Lord lives, He’ll decide Saul's fate,  
But don't raise your hand, let’s just take."  

Took the spear, the jar, from near Saul's head,  
Tiptoed away while God's deep sleep spread.  

From the mountain afar, David then cried,  
Called out to Abner, with anger, he chided:  

"Aren't you meant to guard the king's life?  
Someone snuck in, could’ve ended the strife!"  

“Look for the spear and the jar, now gone,  
You’ve failed your duty, the threat was strong.”  

Saul heard the voice, "Is that you, my son?"  
David replied, "It's me, O king, what have I done?"  

"Why chase me down, what wrong have I set?  
If God sent you, let’s make amends yet."  

"But if men cursed me, drove me away,  
Don’t spill my blood, near Yahweh I’ll stay."  

Saul acknowledged, "I've sinned, my son,  
Come back, I won’t harm, this chase is done."  

David called back, "Here’s the spear, proof in my hand,  
Let your men fetch it, on righteousness we stand."  

"May God reward as He sees fit,  
I spared your life, and that’s legit."  

Saul responded, "Bless you, my son,  
You’ll do great things, your battles well won."  

David turned, went his own way,  
While Saul, in his place, chose to stay.
