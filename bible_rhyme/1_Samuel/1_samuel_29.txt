Philistines gathered, armies at Aphek's gate,  
Israelites by Jezreel, they await their fate.  

Philistine lords pass in ranks, all in line,  
David with Achish, in the rear, biding time.  

Princes see David, they ask, "Who are these guys?"  
Achish replies, "It's David, no surprise.  

He's been with me for years, no fault in him found,  
He left Saul's side, stuck with me, battle-bound."  

Princes are angry, "Send him back home,  
Don’t want him with us, let him roam.  

In the fight, what if he turns on us all?  
Could he win back Saul with our heads as his haul?  

Isn't this David of the songs they sing,  
‘Saul slayed thousands, David ten thousand he'd bring?’”  

Achish calls David, says, "By Yahweh you’re true,  
Your comings and goings, they’re good in my view.  

But lords aren't pleased, so don't take offense,  
Return in peace, it makes the most sense."  

David protests, "What wrong have I done?  
I've stood by you, faithful as the sun.  

Why can't I fight with you, against your foes?"  
Achish responds, "I know how this goes.  

You're like an angel, bright in my sight,  
But princes say no, they won't let you fight.  

Rise up early, take your men, and go,  
At morning's light, depart soft and slow."  

David rose early, his men by his side,  
Back to Philistine land, they did glide.  

Philistines marched on, to Jezreel they pressed,  
The battle awaited, with tension and unrest.
