Saul spoke to Jonathan, his son, and the crew,  
"Let's kill David," but Jonathan knew what to do.  

He delighted in David, so he gave him a clue,  
"My father's out for blood, so here's what you should pursue.  

Hide in the morning, find a secret place,  
I'll talk to Dad about you, see what I can trace."  

Jonathan pleaded, "Father, don't commit this sin,  
David's done you no wrong, he's always been all in.  

He faced the Philistine, risked his life in the fight,  
Yahweh gave us victory, you saw it, felt the light.  

Why harm the innocent, spill blood without a cause?"  
Saul heard his son's words, took a moment, paused.  

"As Yahweh lives," swore Saul, "David won't die."  
Jonathan brought David back, all beneath the sky.  

War broke out once more; David fought, never stalled,  
Philistines fell in numbers, they ran, he stood tall.  

But an evil spirit on Saul, with a spear in his hand,  
David played the harp, calming with his band.  

Saul aimed to pin him, but David made a slip,  
The spear hit the wall; David took a quick dip.  

He fled in the night, Michal helped him out,  
"Save your life tonight, or tomorrow there's doubt."  

Through the window he went, down to the ground,  
While Michal tricked Saul's messengers, running around.  

Teraphim in the bed, with goats' hair as a head,  
"David's sick," she said, as they checked the bed.  

"Bring him to me in the bed," Saul's command,  
But they found the statue, not the man.  

Saul questioned Michal, "Why let him go free?"  
"He threatened me," she said, "with death's decree."  

David fled to Samuel, told him the tale,  
Together they went to Naioth, no trail.  

Saul's men came for David, but God's Spirit fell,  
They prophesied wildly, caught in the spell.  

Three times Saul sent, same result each ride,  
The Spirit hit them hard, prophesying with pride.  

Saul himself went, seeking the prophet's abode,  
God's Spirit struck him, with a heavy load.  

Stripped bare, he too prophesied, day and night,  
"Is Saul among the prophets?" they said with slight.
