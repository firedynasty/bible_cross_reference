Philistines gathered, armies ready for war,  
In Judah at Socoh, their battle roar.  
Saul and Israel camped in Elah’s field,  
Mountains on each side, neither side would yield.  

Goliath stepped out, tall and fierce,  
Six cubits and a span, in bronze gear pierced.  
A helmet of bronze, mail weighing him down,  
Bronze on his legs, javelin renowned.  

Spear like a beam, iron heavy with might,  
His shield bearer marching, ready to fight.  
He called to Israel’s ranks with a sneer,  
"Choose a man to fight, bring him here!"  

"If he kills me, we'll serve you, fair play,  
But if I prevail, you'll serve us this day."  
Israel trembled, fear in the air,  
Big words from Goliath, beyond compare.  

David, son of Jesse, youngest of eight,  
To Bethlehem he went, the sheep were his fate.  
His brothers with Saul, in battle they stood,  
David brought food, as Jesse said he should.  

Forty days, Goliath taunted with ease,  
Jesse sent David, "Bring news, if you please."  
David rose early, the sheep left behind,  
To the battlefield, with war on his mind.  

Army against army, battle cries loud,  
David ran to his brothers through the crowd.  
Goliath appeared, taunts in his breath,  
Israel’s men fled, fearing death.  

"The king will reward who ends Goliath’s reign,  
Riches, his daughter, his family’s gain."  
David asked, "Who defies the living God’s crew?"  
The people replied, "Here's what they’ll do."  

Eliab scolded, "Why leave the sheep’s den?"  
David replied, "Is there not cause, my friend?"  
Saul heard the words David did speak,  
And sent for the boy, the giant to meet.  

"Don’t let hearts fail, I’ll fight the Philistine,  
I’ve fought lions and bears, my courage keen."  
"The Lord saved me then, and He’ll save again,"  
Saul said, "Go, and may God’s strength attend."  

David tried on Saul’s gear, but it felt wrong,  
"I’ll go as I am, with my faith strong."  
Five stones he took, sling in his hand,  
Faced the Philistine, ready to stand.  

Goliath laughed, "Am I a dog you pursue?"  
David declared, "The Lord fights with me, not you."  
"This day you’ll fall, the world will know,  
There's a God in Israel, His power will show."  

David ran forward, stone in the sling,  
Goliath’s forehead hit, down did he swing.  
David stood over him, sword in hand,  
Cut off his head, victory grand.  

Philistines fled, Israel gave chase,  
Shouts of triumph, they won the race.  
David brought the head to Jerusalem town,  
"The son of Jesse," he said with renown.
