Jonathan and David, their souls intertwined,  
A bond so deep, love of a kind.  
Saul took David, wouldn't let him stray,  
In his father's house, he'd no longer stay.  

Jonathan gave a robe, his sword, his bow,  
A covenant of friendship began to grow.  
David shined wherever Saul sent him,  
Wisdom and valor, his light wouldn't dim.  

Women sang, danced with joy in the street,  
Praising David for his Philistine feat.  
"Saul's thousands, David's ten!" they declare,  
Saul's anger burned, the jealousy flared.  

"What's left but the kingdom?" Saul complained,  
From that day on, his watch remained.  
Spirit troubled Saul, David played his hand,  
But Saul's spear flew, like a deadly command.  

Twice David escaped, his life he saved,  
Saul's fear grew, 'cause with David, Yahweh stayed.  
So Saul made him a captain, sent him afar,  
But David's wisdom shone like a star.  

Israel and Judah loved their new man,  
Out and back, with honor he ran.  
Saul offered Merab, his eldest bride,  
But David was lowly, his claim denied.  

Yet Michal loved him, Saul's plan was set,  
"Let Philistines strike, let David fret."  
"Be my son-in-law," Saul's servants conveyed,  
David's heart pondered, his doubt displayed.  

"No dowry needed," Saul's plot unveiled,  
"A hundred foreskins, Philistines impaled."  
David rose, Philistines fell in score,  
Two hundred brought, he fulfilled the chore.  

Michal became his, Saul's fear increased,  
With Yahweh and love, David's power released.  
David outshined, his wisdom displayed,  
His name esteemed, Saul's schemes were delayed.
