David heard a shout, Philistines at Keilah's door,  
Threshing floors robbed, couldn't take it anymore.  

He asked God, "Should I fight, should I strike?"  
God said, "Go save Keilah, it's time to hike."  

David's men were scared, "We're afraid right here,  
How much worse in Keilah, with Philistine fear?"  

David asked God once more, for a sign,  
"Go down," God said, "Victory is thine."  

David and his crew, to Keilah they sped,  
Philistines beaten, left many for dead.  

With livestock in hand, Keilah was free,  
David the hero, proud as can be.  

Then Abiathar came, with ephod in hand,  
He joined David, in enemy land.  

Saul heard the news, David in a trap,  
"Towns with gates and bars, I've got him, perhaps."  

Saul gathered his troops, to Keilah they went,  
David knew the plan, mischief was meant.  

David called Abiathar, ephod right there,  
"God, will Saul crush the city, should I beware?"  

God replied clear, "Saul's coming down,  
Keilah's men will hand you right to the crown."  

David gathered six hundred, got out fast,  
Saul heard he escaped, let the moment pass.  

David hid in Ziph, wild lands and hills,  
Saul searched daily, but God willed stills.  

Jonathan found David, in the woods they met,  
"Don't fear my father, king you'll be set."  

They made a pact, right before the Lord,  
Jonathan left, David stayed by his sword.  

Ziphites told Saul, "David hides in our place,  
Come get him, O king, end this chase."  

Saul blessed them, for their loyalty showed,  
"Find his lair, where he's abode."  

Saul's men moved fast, while David slipped away,  
To the wilderness of Maon, where he'd stay.  

Saul closed in near the desert's edge,  
David fled fast, close to the ledge.  

But word came quick, the Philistines raid,  
Saul turned back, David's escape was made.  

Sela Hammahlekoth, the place was named,  
David to En Gedi, where his men remained.
