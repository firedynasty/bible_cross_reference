In the land of Benjamin, Kish was the man,  
A mighty dude with a courageous plan.  
His son was Saul, tall and grand,  
No one like him in all the land.

Kish's donkeys roamed and strayed,  
"Saul," he said, "go, find where they've laid."  
Through Ephraim's hills, they made their way,  
Searched Shalishah, Shaalim, no luck that day.

In Zuph, Saul said, "Let's head home,  
Dad will worry, left alone."  
But the servant said, "Hold up, bro,  
A man of God nearby, let's ask where to go."

Saul replied, "What can we bring?  
Our bread is gone, got not a thing."  
The servant showed a silver piece,  
"Let's give this, and gain some peace."

"Good plan," said Saul, "let's track him down,"  
Maidens said, "The seer's in town."  
They hurried on, with hope in sight,  
Found Samuel before the night.

God spoke to Sam the day before,  
"From Benjamin, I'll send one more.  
Anoint him prince, he'll lead the fight,  
Against Philistines, with might and right."

Saul met Sam at the city's gate,  
"Where's the seer?" he asked, not late.  
Samuel said, "You're eating with me,  
Tomorrow, all you need, you'll see."

"Don't stress the donkeys, they're back home,  
Israel's riches are yours alone."  
Saul said, "But I'm just a Benjamite,  
The smallest tribe, why this delight?"

Samuel set them in the place of honor,  
Thirty gathered, as they pondered.  
The cook brought forth the special meat,  
Saved for Saul, now ready to eat.

On the rooftop, they talked till dawn,  
Samuel called, "Time to move on."  
At the city's edge, Sam took pause,  
"Send the servant, hear God's cause."

As Saul stood still, the message clear,  
The journey began with no more fear.
