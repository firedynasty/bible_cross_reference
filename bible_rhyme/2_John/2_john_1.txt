To the chosen lady, and her kids so true,  
Loved by me, and by those who know truth too.  
For truth's sake, it stays with us, won't sever,  
Grace, mercy, peace from God, now and forever.  

I rejoice 'cause I see your children, sure and spry,  
Walking in truth, as the Father’s command flies high.  
I beg you, dear lady, not some new demand,  
But love one another, as was first planned.  

This is love, walking His commandments straight,  
From the start, follow His words, never abate.  
Many deceivers roam the world, causing a fuss,  
Denying Christ in the flesh, don't let them mislead us.  

Watch yourselves, keep what we’ve gained in sight,  
To get the full reward, hold on tight.  
Those who stray from Christ's teaching lack God’s own,  
But those who stay have the Father and Son on the throne.  

If anyone comes, without this teaching bright,  
Don’t welcome them in, don’t invite.  
For to welcome them, is to partake,  
In their evil deeds, the wrong path they take.  

There's much to say, but not with ink today,  
Hope to see you face to face, joy on display.  
Your sister's children send greetings, all the same,  
Amen to that, in truth and love's name.
