Nehemiah speaks, son of Hacaliah's line,  
In Chislev's month, twentieth year, Susa's palace shine.  
Hanani, my brother, from Judah did arrive,  
I asked of the Jews, those who managed to survive.

"The remnant's in trouble," they said with a sigh,  
Jerusalem's walls, broken, gates burned, oh my!  
I heard it, I wept, couldn't hold back the tears,  
Fastened my heart to God, prayed through the years.

"God of heaven," I cried, "great and awesome you are,  
Keeping love and promise, never straying far.  
Hear my plea, open eyes, as I pray night and day,  
Confessing all sins that led us astray.

We broke your commands, corrupt through and through,  
Forgot the laws Moses taught us to pursue.  
Remember your promise, scattered if we stray,  
But if we return, you'll gather us one day."

"By your power, great and strong, you redeemed your own,  
These are your people, let your mercy be shown.  
Listen, Lord, to your servant's humble plea,  
Grant mercy now, as cupbearer I be."
