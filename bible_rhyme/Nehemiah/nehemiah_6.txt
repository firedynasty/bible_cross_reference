Enemies heard, the wall was done, no breaches left, not a single one.  
Sanballat and Geshem tried to sway, "Come to Ono, let's meet today."  

They meant me harm, I saw the game, "Great work here, I can't be tamed."  
Four times they called, four times I said, "Why should the work stop? I'm ahead."  

Fifth time's a letter, open wide, "Rebellion's brewing," they all lied.  
"King in Judah," they accuse, "Come, let's talk, you can't refuse."  

I shot back fast, "You're making it up, None of these lies are gonna disrupt."  
They tried to scare us, weaken our hands, "God, make us strong, help us withstand."  

Shemaiah spoke, a trap laid bare, "Hide in the temple, danger's there."  
"Should I flee?" I said, strong and bold, "I'll stay my ground, not be controlled."  

God didn't send him, I saw the ploy, Tobiah and Sanballat's hired decoy.  
They wanted fear, wanted me to sin, "God, remember their evil spin."  

And then we finished, the wall was high, in fifty-two days beneath the sky.  
Enemies trembled, nations shook, "God's work, His hand," they overlooked.  

Judah's nobles wrote to Tobiah, his letters flew like a secret fire.  
Tied by marriage, they spoke him well, while he tried threats, their stories fell.
