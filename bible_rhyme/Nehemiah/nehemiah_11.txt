Princes in Jerusalem, that's where they stay,  
The rest cast lots, to see who'd make their way.  

One in ten moved to the holy ground,  
While others in different cities were found.  

Blessings on those who chose to dwell,  
In Jerusalem, where stories they’d tell.  

Chiefs of the province, they took their stand,  
In city or country, they claimed their land.  

Judah’s sons, like Athaiah, Uzziah’s seed,  
In Jerusalem, they lived, with courage and creed.  

Maaseiah, from the line of Baruch he came,  
Sons of Perez, valiant, known by name.  

Benjamin’s boys, led by Sallu with pride,  
Nine hundred twenty-eight, they reside.  

Joel was overseer, Judah second in command,  
Together they ruled this sacred land.  

Priests like Jedaiah, Seraiah, they served,  
God's house their realm, never unnerved.  

Brothers worked, eight hundred twenty-two,  
Adaiah's clan, chiefs, stood strong and true.  

Amashsai’s kin, mighty men indeed,  
One hundred twenty-eight, they lead.  

Levites like Shemaiah, kept things intact,  
Shabbethai, Jozabad, with oversight exact.  

Mattaniah began the thanks and prayer,  
Levites in the city, they were always there.  

Gatekeepers like Akkub stood at the gate,  
One hundred seventy-two, they watched and wait.  

In cities of Judah, others took their claim,  
Temple servants at Ophel, known by name.  

Uzzi the overseer, making sure it’s right,  
Singers had provisions, every day and night.  

Pethahiah by the king’s side, handling affairs,  
Children of Judah lived in towns with their cares.  

From Kiriath Arba to Beth Pelet wide,  
Through Ziklag to En Rimmon, they reside.  

Benjamin from Geba to Bethel’s ground,  
All these towns, their roots were found.  

In fields and valleys, craftsmen laid their stake,  
From Levite divisions, new homes they’d make.
