Sanballat heard we were up on the wall,  
Got angry, indignant, mocked us all.  

He sneered to his crew with a laugh and a jeer,  
"What can these weak Jews do here?  

Will they build it up, can they sacrifice?  
Revive burnt stones from trash, ain't gonna suffice."  

Tobiah chimed in, adding more to the plot,  
"Fox on their wall? It'll crumble on the spot."  

We called out to God, "Hear us, we're despised,  
Turn their own shame on them, let it be realized.  

Don't hide their sins, keep it clear in view,  
They insulted the builders, oh Lord, it's true."  

But still we built, wall rising in height,  
With hearts full of purpose, our minds set right.  

Enemies heard, they were seething with rage,  
Conspired to fight, make confusion their stage.  

But we prayed to our God, set a watch day and night,  
Judah warned us, "Strength fades, rubble in sight."  

Enemies whispered, "They won't see us creep,  
We'll stop their work, their dreams we'll sweep."  

Neighbors warned us, "Beware, they're near,  
Wherever you turn, attacks will appear."  

I posted guards low, in the open space,  
Families armed, ready to embrace.  

I rose up and spoke, "Don't be afraid,  
Remember the Lord, let courage invade.  

Fight for your kin, for your homes and your land,  
The Lord is mighty, take your stand."  

Enemies found out, their plans fell apart,  
Back to the wall, with courage in our heart.  

Half worked hard, while the others stood guard,  
Spears and shields ready, the task wasn't hard.  

Builders worked with swords at their side,  
Trumpet bearer close, in us he relied.  

I warned the people, "The wall is vast,  
When you hear the trumpet, gather fast.  

God fights for us, don't lose sight,  
Work till stars appear, through the night."  

No rest for us, our clothes stayed on,  
With our weapons ready from dusk till dawn.
