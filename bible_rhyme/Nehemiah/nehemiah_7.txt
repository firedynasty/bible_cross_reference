The wall was built, the doors set tight,  
Gatekeepers, singers, Levites in sight.  

I put my brother Hanani and Hananiah there,  
In charge of Jerusalem, with faithful care.  

To them I said, "Don't open the gates till it's hot,  
Bar the doors while guards watch the spot."  

The city was wide, but not many stayed,  
Few homes were built, foundations had laid.  

God whispered in my heart, a task He gave,  
Gather the nobles, write the names of the brave.  

Found the book of those who came before,  
Out of Babylon, to their cities they swore.  

Zerubbabel led, with Jeshua and more,  
Nehemiah, Azariah, and noble men galore.  

Children of Parosh, two thousand strong,  
Shephatiah's crew, three seventy-two along.  

Arah's lot, six-fifty-two in their stride,  
Pahathmoab's tribe, led by Jeshua, with pride.  

Elam's sons, twelve hundred fifty-four,  
Zattu's kin, eight hundred forty-five more.  

Counted them all, their numbers we knew,  
Israel's children, the faithful and true.  

Priests, Levites, singers, and gates' crew,  
Temple servants and the rest came too.  

Together they stood, forty-two thousand proud,  
Plus their servants, singing voices loud.  

They had horses, mules, camels in line,  
Donkeys aplenty, all in time.  

The heads of houses gave to the cause,  
Gold and silver, without pause.  

Darics and minas, gifts they did bring,  
Priests' garments, a holy offering.  

So priests and Levites in cities took place,  
In the seventh month, God's people, His grace.
