Zerubbabel's crew, they rose up with a shout,  
Priests and Levites, they were stepping out.

Leading the charge were Seraiah and Jeremiah,  
Ezra, Amariah, Malluch, Hattush, on fire.  

Shecaniah, Rehum, Meremoth, in line,  
Iddo, Ginnethoi, Abijah, shining fine.  

Mijamin, Maadiah, and Bilgah in the game,  
Shemaiah, Joiarib, Jedaiah, carrying the flame.  

Sallu, Amok, Hilkiah, Jedaiah too,  
Heads of priests in Jeshua's days, they knew what to do.  

Levites came next: Jeshua, Binnui, Kadmiel,  
With Judah, Mattaniah, they rang the thanksgiving bell.  

Bakbukiah and Unno, their brothers close,  
In their appointed roles, they did the most.  

Jeshua fathered Joiakim, who fathered Eliashib,  
Eliashib to Joiada, passing on the crib.  

Joiada to Jonathan, his line did flow,  
Jonathan to Jaddua, steady as they go.  

Priests in Joiakim's day, heads of homes, you see,  
Seraiah's house had Meraiah, Jeremiah had Hananiah, free.  

Ezra had Meshullam, Amariah had Jehohanan,  
Malluchi had Jonathan, Shebaniah had Joseph's clan.  

Harim had Adna, Meraioth had Helkai's name,  
Iddo had Zechariah, Ginnethon had Meshullam's fame.  

Abijah's son was Zichri, Miniamin and Moadiah's Piltai,  
Bilgah's line was Shammua, Shemaiah had Jehonathan nearby.  

Joiarib's Mattenai, Jedaiah's Uzzi,  
Sallai's Kallai, Amok's Eber, flying high.  

Hilkiah's Hashabiah, Jedaiah's Nethanel,  
Levites were recorded, like a story to tell.  

Hashabiah, Sherebiah, Jeshua son of Kadmiel,  
In praise and thanks, they did excel.  

Mattaniah, Bakbukiah, Obadiah, Meshullam strong,  
Gatekeepers guarding, where they belong.  

In Joiakim's days, with Nehemiah, the boss,  
Ezra the scribe, no matter the loss.  

Dedication day at Jerusalem's wall,  
Levites gathered, with glad thanksgivings all.  

Singers from regions, villages they came,  
To praise with harps, and lift up God's name.  

Priests and Levites purified all,  
People, gates, and wall — they stood tall.  

Princes of Judah up on the wall,  
Two great companies, giving it their all.  

One group to the right, toward the dung gate stride,  
Hoshaiah and half the princes walked with pride.  

Azariah, Ezra, Meshullam in their lane,  
Judah, Benjamin, Shemaiah, Jeremiah, no shame.

Priests with trumpets, Zechariah leading the pack,  
Brothers with instruments, never looking back.  

Ezra the scribe, leading the parade,  
To the water gate, east they'd invade.  

The other company met them there,  
Half the people followed, climbing the stair.  

Above Ephraim’s gate, the old gate they passed,  
Fish gate to sheep gate, standing steadfast.  

Two companies gave thanks in God's own abode,  
Priests and singers, lifting the load.  

Great sacrifices offered, joy so profound,  
Women, children, with joy they resound.  

Rooms for treasures, offerings stored,  
Priests and Levites, their duty restored.  

Singers, gatekeepers, according to command,  
David and Solomon, singing hand in hand.  

In days of old, they praised God’s might,  
Zerubbabel, Nehemiah, kept it tight.  

Portions to singers, gatekeepers they gave,  
Levites and Aaron’s sons, for whom they saved.
