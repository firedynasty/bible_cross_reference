Eliashib, high priest, rose with his crew,  
Built the sheep gate, sanctified it too.  
Set up its doors, from Hammeah to Hananel's tower,  
With sacred might, they wielded holy power.  

Next to him, Jericho's men took their place,  
Zaccur came with hammer, adding to the race.  
Hassenaah's sons built the fish gate, beams laid,  
Doors, bolts, and bars—every part displayed.  

Meremoth, son of Uriah, took his turn,  
Meshullam, Berechiah's son, made it firm.  
Zadok, Baana's boy, fixed what he found,  
Tekoites repaired, their nobles stood down.  

Joiada and Meshullam, old gate restored,  
Beams, doors, bolts, and bars—strength they poured.  
Melatiah from Gibeah, Jadon from Mizpah,  
Fixed the governor's place, working near and far.  

Uzziel, the goldsmith, built beside the wall,  
Hananiah, the perfumer, answered the call.  
Rephaiah of Jerusalem's half did not fail,  
Jedaiah fixed up by his own house's trail.  

Malchijah and Hasshub repaired more space,  
Tower of furnaces renewed in their pace.  
Shallum and daughters worked side by side,  
Building Jerusalem, their hearts open wide.  

Hanun from Zanoah mended the valley gate,  
Next, Malchijah at the dung gate sealed fate.  
Shallun at the spring gate, pool of Shelah's shore,  
Repairing steps to David's city, they swore.  

Nehemiah, Azbuk's son, fixed tombs of old,  
Levites joined in, their work bold and bold.  
Brothers of Keilah, repairing in their lands,  
Ezer of Mizpah, built with skilled hands.  

Baruch, Zabbai's son, worked with a will,  
From Eliashib's door, the gap to fill.  
Priests from the plains took up their tools,  
Benjamin, Hasshub, keeping repair rules.  

Azariah by his house, Binnui went on,  
Building walls strong, from dawn to dawn.  
Palal, standing firm by the king's court wall,  
Pedaiah, Parosh’s son, heard the call.  

Temple servants in Ophel, eastward to the gate,  
Tekoites built more, they did not wait.  
Above the horse gate, priests did their job,  
Each one working, no time to sob.  

Zadok, Immer's boy, at his house repaired,  
Shemaiah at the east gate, his duty declared.  
Hananiah, Hanun, and then Meshullam too,  
Repaired near their rooms, each work crew.  

Malchijah, a goldsmith, merchants at the line,  
Between corners and sheep gate, they shone, divine.  
From goldsmith to merchant, the city was restored,  
Walls standing strong—a testament to the Lord.
