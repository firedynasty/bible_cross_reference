Opened the book, the words were read,  
The law was heard, here's what it said:  

"Ammonites, Moabites, can't join this game,  
'Cause bread and water they never came."  

They hired Balaam to curse and fight,  
But God turned curses into blessings bright.  

Heard the law, they took a stand,  
Separated the mixed from Israel's land.  

Priest Eliashib, with Tobiah's hand,  
Gave him a room, in God's house it would stand.  

But I wasn't there, I was with the king,  
Returned to see what this mess would bring.  

Saw Eliashib's deed, Tobiah's domain,  
Threw out his stuff, to cleanse again.  

Put God's vessels back in their place,  
Restored the offerings, to show His grace.  

Levites' portions were gone, not paid,  
Left their work, to the fields they strayed.  

I asked the rulers, "Why's God's house forsaken?"  
Brought them back, got the gears awaken'.  

Tithes of grain, new wine, oil too,  
Stored in treasuries, where they were due.  

Set the faithful to manage that stash,  
"Remember me, God, for my deeds, not trash."  

Saw work on the Sabbath, carts rolling in,  
Testified against them, calling out sin.  

"Why do this wrong, and profane the day?"  
I shut those gates, kept burdens away.  

Warned those merchants camping round,  
"Stay away, or I'll bring you down."  

Levites purified, guarding the gates,  
"Remember me, God, my kindness is great."  

Saw Jews with wives from lands afar,  
Children’s tongues mixed, setting the bar.  

Contended hard, plucked hair, and swore,  
"Don't mix daughters and sons anymore."  

Solomon's tale, a lesson learned,  
Foreign wives his heart had turned.  

Chased out the priest with foreign kin,  
"Remember them, God, the faults within."  

Cleansed them from outsiders' sway,  
Set priests and Levites to work and pray.  

Kept the offerings, first fruits, all good,  
"Remember me, God, I've done what I could."
