On the twenty-fourth day of the month, they gathered in a scene,  
Fasting, wearing sackcloth, with dirt, they leaned.  
Israel stood apart, from foreigners they estranged,  
Confessed their sins, their fathers' wrongs, unchanged.  

They stood up in place, reading the law's decree,  
A fourth of the day in worship, confessing earnestly.  
Jeshua, Bani, on the stairs they stepped,  
Cried to Yahweh, as their voices leapt.  

"Stand up and bless Yahweh, our God forever praised,  
His glorious name, above all blessing raised!  
He's Yahweh alone, made heaven's high dome,  
Earth and its fullness, sea's endless foam."  

He chose Abram, and called him Abraham,  
Found a faithful heart, made a covenant grand.  
The land promised, from Canaanite to Jebusite,  
Given to his seed, in God's righteous sight.  

Saw their pain in Egypt, by the sea they cried,  
Signs and wonders against Pharaoh, in pride.  
Split the sea apart, on dry land they crossed,  
Their enemies drowned, in the waters tossed.  

Pillar of cloud by day, fire by night,  
Guided them steady, in Yahweh's light.  
He came down at Sinai, gave right rules,  
Statutes and commands, through Moses' tools.  

Bread from the sky, water from the stone,  
Promised land to take, as their own.  
But they turned proud, necks hardened and stiff,  
Ignored commandments, God's gifts they missed.  

Made a calf of gold, "This is your God!" they cried,  
But Yahweh's mercy, stayed by their side.  
Pillar of cloud stayed, by night fire's glow,  
His Spirit instructed, manna did flow.  

Forty years sustained, clothes and feet intact,  
Kingdoms and peoples, were their lands backed.  
Children multiplied, stars in the sky,  
Entered the land, promises not a lie.  

Took cities and lands, filled with good things,  
Yet disobedient, they rebelled against the King.  
Prophets killed, laws cast aside,  
Delivered to foes, their cries amplified.  

When they cried, God heard their plea,  
Sent saviors to set them free.  
But after rest, evil returned,  
Enemies ruled, lessons unlearned.  

Yet God's mercy, never did cease,  
Though they turned away, He offered peace.  
Kings and priests, fathers too,  
Ignored commandments, hearts untrue.  

"Behold, we're servants in this fruitful land,  
Kings hold power, as sin expands.  
In distress we stand, yet make a vow,  
A covenant sealed, before God now."
