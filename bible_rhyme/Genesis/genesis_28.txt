Isaac called Jacob, blessed him with a plan,  
"Don't take a Canaanite woman, find your clan.  

Rise and go to Paddan Aram, to Bethuel's place,  
From Laban's daughters, choose a wife with grace.  

May God bless you, make you a nation so grand,  
Give you Abraham’s blessing, inherit the land."  

Off Jacob went, journey set for Laban's gate,  
Esau saw the blessing, began to contemplate.  

He noticed Jacob obeyed, followed the call,  
And realized the Canaan wives didn’t please at all.  

So Esau turned to Ishmael, and took a new bride,  
Mahalath, his cousin, right by his side.  

Jacob left Beersheba, headed towards Haran,  
Stopped as the sun set, laid his head on a stone, man.  

He dreamed of a ladder, reaching high to the skies,  
Angels moving up and down, to his surprise.  

Yahweh stood above, speaking clear and profound,  
"I’m God of your fathers, on this land you’re bound.  

Your kin will spread wide, like dust on the earth,  
Through you every family will find their worth.  

I’m with you always, won't leave you alone,  
Until every promise and blessing is known."  

Jacob woke up, his heart filled with awe,  
"This place is God’s house, the gate to all law."  

He took the stone, set it up high,  
Anointed it with oil, as the night passed by.  

Called the place Bethel, where dreams were made,  
Vowed to God, if He’d guide him in the path he laid.  

"If God stays with me, gives me food and attire,  
Brings me home in peace, as I desire.  

Then this pillar will be God's house, no doubt,  
And a tenth of all blessings, I'll give as my shout."
