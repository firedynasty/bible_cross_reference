Jacob looked up, saw Esau drawing near,  
With four hundred men, a sight to fear.  
He split the kids between Leah, Rachel, and crew,  
Servants first, then Leah, Rachel and Joseph in view.

He walked ahead, bowed seven times on the ground,  
Till near his brother, no fear to be found.  
Esau ran, embraced, necks entwined,  
Kissed him, and both hearts aligned.

Esau looked, saw the women and kids, "Who are these?"  
Jacob said, "Gifts from God, given with ease."  
Servants came near, bowing low,  
Leah and kids followed, moving slow.

Esau asked, "Why all this crew?"  
Jacob replied, "To find favor with you."  
Esau said, "Brother, enough I own,  
Let what you have remain your own."

Jacob pleaded, "If I've found favor, take this from me,  
I've seen your face, like God’s it be."  
"God’s been gracious, I have my share,"  
Esau accepted, a bond so rare.

Esau said, "Let’s journey, I'll lead the way,"  
Jacob responded, "Pace is key for today.  
Kids are tender, flocks are young,  
We’ll move gently, till the journey's done."

Esau offered help, Jacob declined,  
"Find favor in your sight, in peace I'm aligned."  
Esau returned to Seir, his own route clear,  
While Jacob to Succoth, shelters to rear.

In Shechem's land, peace was found,  
Bought land from Hamor, tent on the ground.  
Built an altar, strong and real,  
Called it El Elohe Israel.
