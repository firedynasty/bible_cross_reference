Famine hit hard, no food in sight,  
Father said, "Go, buy some more, make it right."  
Judah spoke up, with boldness and grace,  
"The man said, 'Don't show up without your brother's face.'"  

"If Ben comes along, we'll buy what we need,  
But without him, we can't proceed."  
Father asked, "Why'd you reveal the other brother's name?"  
"We answered his questions; we played no game."  

Judah promised, "Send the boy with me,  
We'll return alive, you'll see."  
"I'll take the blame if I fail this test,  
We delayed too long; now let's do our best."  

Father gave in, "If it must be done,  
Take gifts and silver, take every one."  
"Bring your brother, and go to the man,  
Hope for mercy, that's the plan."  

The brothers left, and met Joseph's face,  
He saw Benjamin and said, "Prepare the place."  
Joseph's house was where they'd dine,  
But fear struck them, "What’s his design?"  

They explained their plight to the house steward's ear,  
"We found money in sacks, that’s why we fear."  
He replied, "Peace, don’t be alarmed,  
Your God gave treasure, you're not harmed."  

Simeon joined them, freed from the chain,  
Feet washed, donkeys fed, to ease their strain.  
Gifts prepared, they waited for noon,  
When Joseph returned, they'd see him soon.  

Joseph asked, "Is your father okay?  
The old man you spoke of, is he still in play?"  
"Yes, he's alive," with a humble bow,  
Joseph saw Ben, his heart did allow.  

"Is this your brother, the youngest of all?"  
"God bless you, my son," he couldn’t stall.  
He hurried away, tears in his eyes,  
Joseph hid, in his room he cries.  

Face washed clean, he returned on the scene,  
"Serve the meal," he said, keeping it serene.  
Seated by age, from eldest to young,  
Wonder filled them, among their tongue.  

Joseph sent portions, but Ben's plate overflowed,  
Five times the share, their joy showed.  
They drank and laughed, in Joseph’s hall,  
United in feast, they cherished it all.
