"Abraham!" God called, putting him to the test,  
"Here I am," said Abe, ready for the quest.  

"Take your son, your only one you love so true,  
To Moriah's land, it's an offering I ask of you."  

Early morning rise, with donkey saddled tight,  
Took two young men, and Isaac, journeying to the height.  

Third day dawned, Abe saw the place afar,  
Told the young men, "Stay here, we'll worship under the star."  

Wood on Isaac's back, fire and knife in hand,  
Together they walked, through desolate land.  

Isaac asked, "Father, where's the lamb we need?"  
"God will provide," Abe said, "trust in His lead."  

At the place, the altar built, wood laid in order,  
Bound Isaac, laid him down, fulfilling the border.  

Hand stretched out, knife ready to strike,  
An angel's voice called, halting the hike.  

"Abraham, Abraham!" in the sky it resounded,  
"Here I am," he answered, his heart astounded.  

"Don't touch the boy, for now I truly see,  
You fear God, withholding nothing from me."  

Eyes lifted, a ram caught in the thicket,  
A substitute offering, God provided the ticket.  

"Yahweh Will Provide," Abe named that place,  
On God's mountain, provision's embrace.  

From heaven again, the angel did declare,  
"By My own name, Abe, you'll be blessed beyond compare.  

Your descendants like stars, like sand on the shore,  
Enemies' gates yours, nations blessed evermore."  

Back to his young men, they rose and went on,  
In Beersheba, Abe's journey carried on.  

News came to Abe, Milcah bore sons to Nahor,  
Uz, Buz, and Aram, new family lore.  

Rebekah from Bethuel, the family tree grew,  
With Gaham and Maacah, to Abraham the news flew.
