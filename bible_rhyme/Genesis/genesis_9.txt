God blessed Noah and his sons with might,  
"Be fruitful, multiply, fill the earth right.  

Animals and birds, they'll fear your command,  
Every creature and fish, delivered to your hand.  

Every moving thing will be food, that's true,  
Like the green herb I gave, everything's for you.  

But flesh with blood, don't eat, it's not right,  
I'll require blood for life, by day or by night.  

Whoever sheds blood, theirs will be shed,  
For man reflects God, in whose image they're bred.  

So be fruitful and multiply, roam the earth wide,  
Increase and fill it, with life on every side."  

God spoke to Noah, His voice clear and grand,  
"My covenant I make, with you and your band.  

With your offspring, with creatures, every single one,  
From the birds to the beasts, under the sun.  

Never again shall flood destroy all the earth,  
This is my promise, a new era's birth.  

I set a rainbow, a sign in the cloud,  
A covenant of peace, forever vowed.  

When clouds roll in, and the rainbow shines,  
I'll remember the promise, through all the times.  

The rainbow's a sign, of the bond I’ve sown,  
Between me and all life, forever known."  

Shem, Ham, Japheth stepped from the ship's berth,  
From these three sons, came the whole earth.  

Noah became a farmer, with vineyard first-rate,  
Drank wine, got drunk, in his tent he laid.  

Ham saw his father's state, then spread the tale,  
But Shem and Japheth walked backward, to prevail.  

Covered their father, with respect and care,  
Faced away, not seeing his bare.  

Noah awoke, knew what had been done,  
Cursed Canaan, Ham's troublesome son.  

"Blessed be Yahweh, God of Shem," he cried,  
"Let Canaan serve, with Japheth by his side.  

May God enlarge Japheth, in Shem's tents abide,  
Let Canaan be servant to brothers worldwide."  

Noah lived long, three-fifty more years flew,  
Nine hundred fifty in all, then he bid adieu.
