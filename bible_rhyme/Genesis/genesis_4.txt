In the beginning, Eve knew her man, and birthed Cain,  
With Yahweh's touch, their family was gained.  

Next came Abel, keeper of the sheep,  
While Cain tilled the ground, his harvest to reap.  

Time rolled on, offerings were made,  
Cain brought fruits, while Abel's flock was displayed.  

God favored Abel, Cain's face fell in pain,  
"Why you angry?" God asked, "Why the disdain?"  

"Do well, lift up; sin's at your door,  
Desire's for you, but rule it, for sure."  

Cain whispered to Abel, "Let's head to the field,"  
There brother turned against brother, and Abel was killed.  

"Where's Abel?" God asked, Cain played coy,  
"Am I his keeper, or his decoy?"  

"What have you done?" the blood cries out,  
Now cursed from the ground, no more will it sprout.  

Fugitive, wanderer, cursed by the earth,  
Cain cried, "Too great, this punishment's worth!"  

God marked Cain with a protective sign,  
"No one will harm you, vengeance is mine."  

Cain left, settled East of Eden's gate,  
To Nod he went, to face his fate.  

Cain’s line grew, Enoch was born,  
A city he built, legacy adorned.  

Then came Lamech, with two wives by his side,  
Adah and Zillah, with stories to bide.  

Jabal and Jubal, and Tubal Cain,  
Crafting, music, and tools they gained.  

Lamech's voice echoed, "Hear my claim,  
Seventy-seven times, vengeance I proclaim."  

Adam knew Eve, Seth was her joy,  
"God replaced Abel, whom Cain destroyed."  

Seth had Enosh, calling Yahweh's name began,  
A new chapter for God's chosen clan.
