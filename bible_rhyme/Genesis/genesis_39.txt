Joseph was sold down to Egypt land,  
By Ishmaelites, to Potiphar's hand.  
Yahweh was with Joseph, making him thrive,  
In his master's house, he came alive.  

Potiphar saw Yahweh's hand was there,  
So Joseph was made overseer with care.  
Blessings flowed through the house like a stream,  
In fields and home, all lived the dream.  

Potiphar's wife cast her eye on Joe,  
Said, "Come lie with me," but he said, "No."  
"My master's trust is great, he's given me all,  
Except for you, that'd be my fall."  

Day by day, she spoke, tried to sway,  
But Joseph's resolve, it wouldn't fray.  
One day inside, the house was bare,  
She grabbed his cloak, but he wasn't there.  

Left his garment, ran outside fast,  
She called the men, her tale was cast.  
"The Hebrew mocks, he tried with me,  
I cried out loud, and he did flee."  

Garment in hand, she spun the tale,  
Repeated it 'til it couldn't fail.  
Potiphar listened, anger lit flame,  
Joseph to prison, that's where he came.  

But Yahweh with Joseph, grace did show,  
Favor in prison, the seeds did sow.  
Keeper of prisoners gave him control,  
Yahweh made sure Joseph played his role.  

In every task, Joseph would shine,  
'Cause Yahweh's hand made it all align.
