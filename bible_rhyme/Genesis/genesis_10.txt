After the flood, the world began anew,  
Sons of Noah, their families grew.  

Japheth’s sons, Gomer and Magog, to name,  
Madai, Javan, Tubal, adding to the fame.  

Gomer’s kids were Ashkenaz, Riphath, Togarmah,  
Javan’s line stretched far, to Kittim and Dodanim afar.  

Islands and nations split by tongue,  
Each in their lands, their stories sung.  

Ham’s line came with Cush, Mizraim's might,  
Put, Canaan, spreading in the world’s sight.  

Cush’s children like Raamah, Seba stood tall,  
Nimrod, a mighty hunter, known to all.  

"Babel," he built, kingdoms arose,  
Erech and Accad, where his prowess shows.  

Mizraim’s offspring, tribes wide and vast,  
From Ludim to Philistines, their lineage cast.  

Canaan’s legacy, cities spread wide,  
Sidon to Gaza, where borders abide.  

Shem, the elder, his children were led,  
Elam, Asshur, and Arpachshad bred.  

Aram fathered Uz, Hul, Gether, Mash,  
Eber’s line was Peleg, Joktan in a flash.  

Joktan’s sons, Almodad through Jobab's tale,  
In the east, their lands prevail.  

From Mesha to Sephar, mountains high,  
Shem’s children spread, reaching the sky.  

Noah’s sons birthed nations, each a proud story,  
Divided the earth, in their lands, their glory.
