Famine in the land, same as before,  
Isaac heads to Gerar, seeking more.  
God appears, says, "Stay, don’t go south,  
In this land, I’ll bless you, word of mouth.  

Your offspring’s stars will light up the sky,  
Through them, every nation will rise high.  
'Cause Abraham listened to what I said,  
My laws, my voice, he always led."  

Isaac in Gerar, playing it sly,  
"She’s my sister," was the cautious lie.  
Feared for his life, Rebekah’s beauty bright,  
Thought the men might kill, take her in the night.  

Abimelech peeped from his window tall,  
Spotted Isaac and Rebekah, not sister at all.  
Called him out, "Why lie to us here?  
Could've brought guilt, made us live in fear!"  

Abimelech warns, "Touch them you’ll be dead,"  
God’s blessings fell, and Isaac was fed.  
Sowed and reaped, land gave its yield,  
Wealth grew large, blessings unsealed.  

Philistines filled wells with earth and stone,  
Jealous of the riches Isaac owned.  
Abimelech said, "You’re too strong, go away,"  
Isaac moved and settled, no delay.  

Dug the wells, his father’s days renewed,  
Water springing forth, blessings pursued.  
Esek, Sitnah were wells they fought,  
Rehoboth came, space they sought.  

God said, "Fear not, I’m with you still,  
For Abraham’s sake, blessings will fill."  
Built an altar, called Yahweh’s name,  
Isaac’s faith unshaken, ever the same.  

Abimelech came to make things right,  
"We see God’s with you, no need to fight.  
Let’s make a pact, no harm, just peace,  
With Yahweh’s blessing, may we increase."  

Feast was shared, oaths were sworn,  
Peaceful was the day, a new morn.  
Servants dug a well, water found,  
Called it Shibah, blessings abound.  

Esau’s choices, Judith and Basemath,  
Brought grief to Isaac, a heavy path.  
But Isaac stood firm, blessings were near,  
With God’s promise, he had no fear.
