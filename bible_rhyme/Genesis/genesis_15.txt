In a vision, God's word came, "Abram, don't fear,  
I'm your shield, your reward, your fortune's near."

Abram said, "Lord, what will you give me?  
I'm childless, and Eliezer's all I see."

"No children have come my way, just so you know,  
A servant's my heir, can't let my legacy go."

God spoke again, "This man won't take the lead,  
Your heir's your own flesh and bone, indeed."

He led Abram outside, stars filled the night,  
"Count them if you can, they'll guide your sight."

"Just like these stars, your offspring will be."  
Abram believed, righteousness given free.

"I am Yahweh, brought you from Ur's land,  
To give you inheritance, a promise grand."

Abram asked, "Lord, how will I know for sure?"  
God replied, "Bring me offerings, pure and secure."

A heifer, goat, ram, dove, and pigeon laid down,  
Abram split them, set them on the ground.

Birds of prey swooped, but Abram stood guard,  
Chased them away, kept his heart hard.

As the sun set, darkness and fear came,  
Deep sleep fell, Abram heard God proclaim:

"Your offspring will roam, as strangers they'll serve,  
Afflicted for years, but wealth they'll deserve."

"I'll judge the nation they’ll serve with toil,  
But you'll rest in peace, safe in the soil."

Fourth generation, they'll return to this space,  
Amorite iniquity's not yet full in this place."

Darkness descended, and what did he see?  
A smoking furnace, torch lit the debris.

That day, God made a covenant so bold,  
"To your offspring, this land I hold.

From Egypt's river to Euphrates so wide,  
Kenites to Jebusites, all yours," God confide.
