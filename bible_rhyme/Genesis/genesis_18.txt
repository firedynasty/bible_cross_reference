By the oaks of Mamre, heat of the day,  
Abraham sat, in the tent he'd stay.  

Three men stood near, he lifted his eyes,  
Ran to greet them, no surprise.  

"My lord, if I've found favor, don't depart,  
Let me bring water, refresh your heart."  

"Fetch some bread, take a rest under the tree,"  
"Very well, do as you say," they agreed with glee.  

To Sarah he rushed, "Knead some dough, make it quick,"  
Ran to the herd, picked a calf, tender and slick.  

Butter, milk, the calf prepared, served with pride,  
Stood by the tree, as they ate, side by side.  

"Where's Sarah, your wife?" they inquired with care,  
"In the tent," he replied, she was hiding there.  

"I'll return next year, Sarah will bear a son,"  
Sarah laughed inside, disbelief had begun.  

"I'm old, my lord's old, how can this be?"  
"Why did Sarah laugh? Nothing's too hard for Me."  

Sarah denied, "I didn't laugh," fear in her tone,  
"No, but you did," Yahweh said, truth like a stone.  

Men rose, looked toward Sodom, on they would go,  
Abraham walked with them, his anxiety wouldn't slow.  

"Shall I hide my plans from Abraham, my friend?  
Through him, all nations will bless, to the end."  

"Sodom's cry is great, their sin so dire,  
I’ll see if the deeds match the reports that inspire."  

The men turned to Sodom, but Abraham stayed,  
"Will the righteous with the wicked be slayed?"  

"What if fifty righteous are found in the city?"  
"For their sake, I'll spare, isn't that a pity?"  

"What if five less, only forty-five remain?"  
"For forty-five, I’ll spare, again and again."  

"Don't be angry, Lord, if I say it right,  
What if only forty are in sight?"  

"No destruction for forty," Yahweh replied,  
"What if thirty?” Abraham cried.  

"No harm for thirty," Yahweh did assure,  
"What if twenty, can you promise me more?"  

"For twenty's sake, the city stands strong,"  
"One last time, Lord, don't find me wrong."  

"What if ten righteous dwell in this place?"  
"For ten, I'll spare it, free from disgrace."  

Yahweh departed, His words were complete,  
Abraham returned, his heart in retreat.
