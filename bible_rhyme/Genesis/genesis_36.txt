Esau's tale begins with the land he knew,  
Wives from Canaan, daughters in view.  
Adah bore him Eliphaz, Basemath had Reuel,  
Oholibamah gave him Jeush, Jalam, and Korah as well.  

Esau gathered his wives and his kin,  
Took all his livestock, possessions thin.  
Moved from Jacob, couldn't stay as one,  
Too much wealth, too much sun.  

In Seir's hills, Esau found his place,  
He's Edom now, a brand new face.  
Eliphaz, Reuel, sons they had,  
Building clans, making Dad glad.  

Eliphaz's kids: Teman, Omar, Zepho bright,  
Gatam, Kenaz, with Amalek in sight.  
Reuel’s sons: Nahath, Zerah in line,  
Shammah, Mizzah, Basemath's design.  

Chiefs arose, each with his call,  
From Eliphaz and Reuel, they stand tall.  
Oholibamah's children took their stance,  
Jeush, Jalam, Korah in the dance.  

Seir's Horites joined the tale,  
Lotan, Shobal, chiefs prevail.  
Anah found springs, a desert gift,  
Zibeon's donkeys felt the lift.  

Kings of Edom ruled and fell,  
Bela, Jobab, each a story to tell.  
Hadar stood last, with Pau his claim,  
Mehetabel beside, a queen's name.  

Chieftains, kings, a legacy grand,  
Esau's descendants ruled the land.  
This is Esau, Edom's sire,  
In Seir's hills, his heart's desire.
