"Get in the ship, Noah, bring your whole clan,  
I've seen your righteousness across this land.  

Seven pairs of the clean, two unclean, male and mate,  
Birds of the sky, seven and seven, populate.  

In seven days, rain will pour, forty nights, forty days,  
Every living thing washed away in this watery blaze."  

Noah did as was told, at Yahweh's command,  
Six hundred years old when the flood hit the land.  

Into the ship, Noah with family walked,  
Floodwaters rising, none of them balked.  

Clean and unclean, birds and beasts did creep,  
By twos, they entered, no time to sleep.  

Seven days passed, floodwaters arrived,  
Fountains burst open, sky's windows wide.  

Forty days of rain, a deluge of might,  
Noah and kin sheltered from the night.  

Every creature, every kind took their place,  
In the ship, safe from the flood's fierce embrace.  

God shut them in, sealed them tight,  
Waters rose, lifting the ship into flight.  

Floodwaters climbed, mountains submerged,  
All life outside, in the waves, were purged.  

Breath of life, stilled in nostrils, all gone,  
Only Noah and crew lived on at dawn.  

For one fifty days, waters covered the land,  
Noah afloat, saved by God's hand.
