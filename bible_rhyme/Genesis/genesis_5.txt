In the day God made man, His likeness He crafted,  
Male and female He blessed, His image never shafted.  
Named them Adam, the first of human creation,  
He lived long, fathered Seth, his next generation.  

Adam clocked nine hundred thirty, then he died,  
Seth took the baton, Enosh was next in stride.  
Seth lived nine twelve years, then he laid to rest,  
Enosh fathered Kenan, in life's eternal quest.  

Enosh's days numbered nine hundred and five,  
Kenan came next, kept the lineage alive.  
Lived nine hundred ten, then to the earth he returned,  
Fathered Mahalalel, life's lessons were learned.  

Mahalalel lived long, passed the torch to Jared,  
Eight ninety-five years before he finally perished.  
Jared birthed Enoch, who walked with God on high,  
Nine sixty-two years, then he waved goodbye.  

Enoch was special, three hundred sixty-five,  
Walked with God closely, then wasn’t found alive.  
God took him up, a mystery untold,  
A legacy divine, a tale to behold.  

Methuselah, his son, lived the longest span,  
Nine sixty-nine years, the life of a wise man.  
Fathered Lamech, who in time bore a son,  
Named Noah, "He'll comfort us," the prophecy begun.  

Lamech's days were seven seventy-seven,  
Then went to rest, a foreshadow of heaven.  
Noah at five hundred, fathered three,  
Shem, Ham, and Japheth, beginning of history's spree.
