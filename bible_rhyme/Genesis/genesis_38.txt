Judah left his brothers, down he went,  
Met Hirah, an Adullamite he knew well spent.  

Saw a daughter of Shua, Canaanite by name,  
Took her as his own, life's never the same.  

She bore a son, Er was his name,  
Conceived again, Onan joined the game.  

Once more, Shelah made three,  
All at Chezib, a growing family tree.  

Judah found Er a bride, Tamar was chosen,  
But Er's wickedness left Yahweh unspoken.  

Onan, Judah's words were clear,  
"Go to Tamar, for your brother’s heir."  

Onan refused, spilled on the ground,  
Yahweh saw evil, struck him down.  

Judah told Tamar, "Stay till Shelah's grown,"  
She lived with her father, feeling alone.  

Judah's wife passed, he found his rest,  
Went to Timnah with Hirah, his guest.  

Tamar heard news, a plan she'd devise,  
Veiled, she waited, in a clever disguise.  

Judah thought her a harlot, face concealed,  
"Come to me," he said, as the truth stayed sealed.  

A goat he'd promise, a pledge she'd require,  
His signet, cord, and staff she'd acquire.  

Conceived by Judah, Tamar departed,  
Returned to widow's garb, as she'd started.  

Hirah sought the pledge, couldn’t find her there,  
"No prostitute," they said, "she wasn't anywhere."  

Three months later, whispers in the air,  
"Tamar's with child, bring her to despair."  

Judah said, "Burn her," but she had the proof,  
"The man to whom these belong, here's the truth."  

Judah saw, and admitted wrong,  
"She's more righteous," he said, all along.  

Twins in her womb, a struggle revealed,  
Perez and Zerah, destiny sealed.  

Midwife marked the first with scarlet thread,  
But Perez broke through, Zerah followed instead.
