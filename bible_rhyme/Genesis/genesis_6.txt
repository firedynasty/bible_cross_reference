People multiplied on the ground, daughters were born,  
God's sons saw beauty, took wives, the world was torn.  
Yahweh spoke, "My Spirit won't strive with man,  
He's flesh, his days numbered to a hundred twenty span."

Nephilim roamed, mighty men of renown,  
God’s sons and daughters, legends all around.  
But wickedness grew, man's thoughts were dark,  
Yahweh grieved deeply, His heart left a mark.  

"I'll destroy man," Yahweh declared with regret,  
"Man and beast, all that I’ve met.  
I’m sorry I made them," His sorrow was told,  
But Noah found favor, a story to unfold.  

Noah was righteous, blameless in his time,  
He walked with God, his life a steady rhyme.  
Three sons he fathered: Shem, Ham, Japheth,  
But the earth was corrupt, violence was its breath.  

God saw the earth, corrupt and vile,  
"An end to all flesh," He warned with a trial.  
"Make a ship of gopher wood, rooms inside,  
Seal it with pitch, let it be your guide.  

Its length three hundred, width fifty, thirty tall,  
Roof it well, door on the side, heed this call.  
Lower, second, third levels in the ship reside,  
For a flood comes, waters worldwide."

"Establish my covenant," God promised to Noah,  
"Enter the ark, you and your kin, stay in the flow-a.  
Two of each creature, male and female,  
Birds, beasts, creeps, on your ship they’ll sail.  

Gather food, enough for all to survive,  
Sustain yourselves till new life can thrive."  
Noah obeyed, did all God ordered,  
Faithful to the task, his resolve never faltered.
