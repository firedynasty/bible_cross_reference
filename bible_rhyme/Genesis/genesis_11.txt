The earth spoke as one, one language, one tone,  
From the east they traveled, in Shinar they found their home.  
"Let's make bricks," they said, "burn them good and tight,"  
Building a city and a tower reaching high in the night.  

"Come on," they chanted, "let's make a name,  
So we won't be scattered, lost to fame."  
But Yahweh came down, took a look at their plan,  
"One people, one tongue, now it's time to span.  

What they start, they’ll complete, nothing will bind,  
Let's mix up their speech, confuse their mind.”  
So Yahweh descended, with a twist of His hand,  
Scattering them far across every land.  

Babel it was called, for there speech was tangled,  
From that place, all nations were dangled.  
Now, Shem's generations unfold,  
Two years post-flood, Arpachshad took hold.  

Shem lived on, five hundred years more,  
Sons and daughters he had, a great score.  
Arpachshad, thirty-five, fathered Shelah in line,  
Lived four hundred more, with a family fine.  

Shelah had Eber, after thirty years passed,  
Four hundred years more, his legacy amassed.  
Eber fathered Peleg, at thirty-four years,  
Then lived many more, with sons and their peers.  

Peleg to Reu, thirty was his age,  
Two hundred years more, his life's full page.  
Reu had Serug, thirty-two on the clock,  
Two hundred more years, in life's endless walk.  

Serug to Nahor, at thirty he lived,  
Two hundred more, to his kin he did give.  
Nahor had Terah after twenty-nine years,  
One hundred nineteen more, with family cheers.  

Terah had Abram, Nahor, and Haran his son,  
Haran fathered Lot, but life was undone.  
In Ur of the Chaldees, Haran met his fate,  
While Terah was living, his life couldn't wait.  

Abram wed Sarai, Nahor took Milcah's hand,  
Sarai was barren, no child to command.  
Terah took Abram, Lot, and Sarai in tow,  
From Ur to Canaan, but in Haran they'd slow.  

Two hundred five years, Terah's life spanned,  
And there in Haran, his life did land.
