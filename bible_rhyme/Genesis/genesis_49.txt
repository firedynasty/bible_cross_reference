Jacob called his sons, "Come near, let's chat,  
I'll tell you what's coming, where you'll be at.  

"Reuben, my first, my strength and pride,  
But boiling like water, you won't abide.  

"Simeon, Levi, brothers in crime,  
Their swords struck cruel in furious time.  

"Judah, your brothers' praise will be yours,  
A lion's might, your hand restores.  

"The scepter stays with you, a throne you'll keep,  
Till the ruler arrives, nations at his feet.  

"Zebulun by the sea, a ship's embrace,  
Sidon will mark the border of your place.  

"Issachar, strong donkey, saddlebags low,  
A restful land, but to labor you'll go.  

"Dan will judge, a serpent in stealth,  
Biting the horse, toppling wealth.  

"Though Gad is pressed, he'll rise to strike,  
Asher's food, royal delight.  

"Naphtali runs free, bearing beauty,  
Joseph's vine flourishes beyond duty.  

"Archers attacked, but his strength endured,  
Blessings from above, his future assured.  

"Benjamin's a wolf, devouring at dawn,  
Dividing plunder when the day is gone."  

Twelve tribes, each with their father's prose,  
Blessed with his words, the path he chose.  

"I'm gathered soon, so bury me too,  
With my ancestors, in the cave you knew.  

In Machpelah, there find my place,  
With Abraham, Sarah, in Canaan's space."  

Jacob spoke, then gathered his breath,  
Lifted his feet, and welcomed death.
