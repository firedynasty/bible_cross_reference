Abraham moved south, foreign lands he'd explore,  
Between Kadesh and Shur, he settled in Gerar's core.  
Called Sarah his sister, not his wife in disguise,  
Abimelech took her, a king's unexpected surprise.  

God showed up in a dream, said, “Behold, you're dead,  
For the woman's a wife, not a sister,” God said.  
Abimelech hadn't touched her, he pleaded his case,  
“Will you slay the righteous, bring guilt to this place?  

He called her his sister, she said he's her brother,  
I acted in innocence, one way or the other.”  
God answered, “I know, that's why I intervened,  
Kept you from sinning, unseen but keen.  

Now give her back, she's a prophet's wife,  
He’ll pray for you, restore your life.  
If you refuse, know that death's your fate,  
For you and your kingdom, if you hesitate.”  

Abimelech rose early, told his servants the tale,  
Fear spread through the land, faces turned pale.  
He summoned Abraham, asked, “What have you done?  
Brought a great sin, no wrong had I begun.”  

Abraham confessed, “Thought God’s fear was none,  
They'd kill me for Sarah, my life would be gone.  
She’s truly my sister, just not of my mother,  
A complex truth, but yes, she’s my lover.”  

Abimelech gifted sheep, cattle, and more,  
Restored Sarah to Abraham, peace they swore.  
“My land is open, live where you please,”  
To Sarah he said, “Your worth, all will see.”  

Abraham prayed, God healed the king's kin,  
Opened their wombs, new life could begin.  
For Yahweh had shut them, because of this plight,  
But now peace restored, all things set right.
