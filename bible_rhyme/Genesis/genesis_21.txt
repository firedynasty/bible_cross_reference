God visited Sarah just as He said,  
And she bore a son when her hope had fled.  

Abraham named his boy Isaac, with pride,  
Circumcised him on the eighth day, as God had prescribed.  

Old man Abraham, a hundred, when Isaac arrived,  
Sarah laughed, said, "Who'd have thought I'd keep this dream alive?"  

The child grew and was weaned, a feast set the stage,  
But Sarah saw Hagar's son mocking, fueled her rage.  

She said to Abraham, "Send them away!  
The servant's child won't share Isaac's day."  

Troubled was Abraham, caught in the plight,  
But God said, "Listen, Isaac's your light.  

Yet Hagar's boy, a nation I'll create,  
For he's your child, his future is great."  

Early morning, bread and water in hand,  
Abraham sent Hagar wandering this land.  

In Beersheba's wild, the water ran dry,  
Hagar wept, couldn't watch her son die.  

God heard the boy, sent an angel to say,  
"Hagar, fear not, I'll show you the way.  

Lift up the boy, a great nation he'll be,  
Open your eyes, a well you will see."  

With water renewed, the boy drank with grace,  
Grew in the wild, an archer he embraced.  

Abimelech saw God with Abraham’s might,  
Asked for truth, a covenant to write.  

Abraham agreed, yet complained of a well,  
Abimelech knew not, swore tales to quell.  

Sheep and cattle, a covenant they made,  
Seven ewe lambs, a witness displayed.  

Beersheba named for oaths they swore,  
Abimelech and Phicol left with a roar.  

Abraham planted a tamarisk tree,  
Called on Yahweh, forever to be.  

In Philistine lands, for many days did he dwell,  
A foreigner living, with stories to tell.
