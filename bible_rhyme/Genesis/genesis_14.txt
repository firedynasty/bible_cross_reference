In the days of kings with lands to claim,  
In the Valley of Siddim, they played their game.  

Chedorlaomer ruled with might,  
But in thirteen years, the kings took flight.  

Four kings rode, rebellion in sight,  
Struck giants and foes, left them in fright.  

The kings of Sodom and Gomorrah made their stand,  
Against Chedorlaomer’s fierce command.  

The valley was slick with tar and doom,  
Some kings fled, others met their tomb.  

Goods of cities were taken away,  
Along with Lot, in disarray.  

One escaped to Abram, told his tale,  
By Mamre’s oaks, he vowed to prevail.  

Three hundred eighteen, his men trained to fight,  
Pursuing by night, with might and sight.  

They struck in the shadows, scattered their foes,  
Rescued Lot, returned with those.  

The king of Sodom met him with relief,  
Melchizedek came, his words like a chief.  

"Bread and wine, a blessing I bring,  
To Abram, blessed by the Most High King."  

"So bless the Almighty, who gave you strength,  
Delivered your foes at full length."  

Abram bowed, gave a tenth to show,  
His gratitude to Melchizedek’s glow.  

The king of Sodom with an offer bold,  
"Give me the people, keep the gold."  

But Abram replied, his vow held high,  
"To Yahweh I’ve promised, no riches I’ll buy."  

"Not a thread nor strap shall I take,  
For it's the Lord’s name I won’t forsake."  

His men’s portions alone he'd let stand,  
Aner, Eshcol, Mamre, they'd take their hand.
