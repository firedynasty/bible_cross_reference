Abraham took a wife, Keturah's her name,  
Bore him Zimran, Jokshan, and a bustling fame.  
Jokshan fathered Sheba and Dedan too,  
Midian had sons, a crew to pursue.  

To Isaac, Abraham gave all his share,  
Sent the others east, with gifts to spare.  
Lived a full life, a hundred seventy-five,  
Gave up his spirit, no longer alive.  

Isaac and Ishmael, buried him deep,  
In Machpelah's cave, where ancestors sleep.  
Field of Ephron, bought fair and square,  
With his wife Sarah, Abraham rests there.  

Isaac's blessed, by God's own decree,  
Lived by Beer Lahai Roi, peacefully.  
Ishmael's history carried on,  
Hagar's son, from dusk till dawn.  

Named his sons from Nebaioth to Kedemah,  
Twelve princes in villages, spreading afar.  
Ishmael lived full, one hundred thirty-seven,  
Passed from the earth, gathered in heaven.  

Isaac's story unfolds, Abraham's line,  
Rebekah's his bride, fate intertwined.  
Prayed to God, her womb was barren,  
God heard, blessed with life, his love and caring.  

Two nations in her womb, a heavenly test,  
"One will be stronger; the elder will serve the rest."  
Twins were born, Esau came first,  
Red and hairy, for greatness, a thirst.  

Jacob followed, gripping the heel,  
Isaac was sixty, when twins were revealed.  
Esau, a hunter, loved by Dad,  
Jacob, tent dweller, made Mom glad.  

Esau came hungry, from fields afar,  
Saw Jacob's stew, "Feed me, I'm scarred!"  
"First, your birthright," Jacob did say,  
Esau, famished, gave it away.  

Sworn and sold, for bread and stew,  
Esau ate, despised what he knew.  
Rise and fall, a tale of strife,  
In birthright sold, Esau's life.
