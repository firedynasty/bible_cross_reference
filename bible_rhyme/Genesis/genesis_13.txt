Abram left Egypt, with his wife and Lot so grand,  
Into the South, with riches, livestock in hand.  

His journey took him back to Bethel's holy ground,  
Where he'd pitched his tent, and Yahweh's name was found.  

Lot was there too, with flocks and tents in tow,  
But the land was too cramped, their wealth began to show.  

Herdsmen clashed, their voices raised in heated claim,  
Canaanites and Perizzites mingled in the same.  

Abram spoke to Lot, "Let's not fight, we're kin,  
Choose your path left or right, so peace can begin."  

Lot gazed out, saw the Jordan plain so lush and wide,  
Before Yahweh's wrath on Sodom's sinful side.  

He picked his place, journeyed east, they parted ways,  
Abram stayed in Canaan, in Yahweh's gaze.  

The men of Sodom, wicked, against Yahweh's will,  
While Abram in Canaan, the promise to fulfill.  

Yahweh spoke to Abram, with vision bold and grand,  
"Look around, all directions, this is your land.  

Your offspring countless as the dust beneath your feet,  
This promise I give, will never know defeat.  

Rise, walk the land's length and breadth, see what I bestow,  
For this land's yours forever, as far as you can go."  

Abram settled by Mamre's oaks, in Hebron's embrace,  
Built an altar to Yahweh, in that holy place.
