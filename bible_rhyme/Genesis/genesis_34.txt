Dinah stepped out to see the land,   
Shechem saw her, took her hand.   
He laid with her, brought her low,   
Yet love for Dinah in him did grow.   

To his father, Shechem made his plea,   
"Get her for my wife, she’s meant for me."   
Jacob heard the cries but kept his peace,   
His sons in the field, no release.   

Hamor came to Jacob’s place,   
The sons returned, anger on their face.   
“This wrong done to our kin!” they cried,   
A tale of disgrace they couldn't abide.   

Hamor pleaded, "Please comply,   
Let my son wed, don't deny.   
Join with us, be one kin,   
Trade and prosper, let's begin."   

Shechem vowed, “Tell me the price,   
I'll pay in full, no sacrifice,   
Just give Dinah as my bride,   
I'll meet your terms, I won't hide.”   

But Jacob’s sons with deceit replied,   
“We can’t give sister to your uncircumcised.   
Do as we do, that’s our play,   
Then we’ll join you, without delay.”   

Hamor and Shechem liked the plan,   
To the city gate, they spoke to every man.   
“Let’s join with them, it’ll be grand,   
Their wealth will be ours, like we planned.”   

Every male did heed the call,   
Circumcised, one and all.   
On the third day, in pain and strife,   
Simeon and Levi took to life.   

With swords they came, swift and keen,   
Hamor and Shechem, no more seen.   
They took Dinah, left the town,   
Jacob's sons brought the city down.   

Plundered riches, took their kin,   
Retaliation for the sin.   

Jacob scolded, "What have you done?   
I’m outnumbered, we can't outrun!"   
The brothers stood, defiant gleam,   
“Should he treat her like a dream?”
