When Abram hit ninety-nine, God appeared clear and bright,  
Said, "I'm God Almighty, walk before me, live right.  
I'll make my covenant, it's between me and you,  
I'll multiply you greatly, watch what I do."

Abram hit the ground, face down in awe,  
God spoke again, laying down the law,  
"With you my covenant will stand, it's true,  
Father of nations, that's who you'll be, you."

"No more Abram, Abraham's your new name,  
Nations and kings from you, they'll proclaim.  
I'll make you fruitful, more than you know,  
The land of Canaan, to your offspring, I'll bestow."

God said, "Keep my covenant, you and your kin,  
Every male circumcised, the sign of what's within.  
At eight days old, seal the deal on their skin,  
Born or bought, let the covenant begin."

"Break the covenant? That soul's cast out,  
In your flesh, my promise, there's no doubt."  
"For Sarai, call her Sarah, her name's renewed,  
Mother of nations, through her my promise pursued."

Abraham laughed, "Can I father a child, this old?  
Will Sarah at ninety, a son behold?"  
He pleaded for Ishmael, but God was clear,  
"Sarah will bear Isaac, my promise sincere."

"For Ishmael, I've heard, he'll be great too,  
Twelve princes will rise, a nation they'll pursue.  
But with Isaac, my covenant firmly remains,  
Next year, Sarah's joy, my promise sustains."

God ascended, His words etched in Abraham's mind,  
Abraham acted, not leaving one behind.  
Every male in his house, circumcised that day,  
At ninety-nine, he led, showing the way.

Ishmael at thirteen, joined the obedient line,  
In Abraham's camp, the covenant's sign.  
In unity, they stood, God's promise held tight,  
Circumcised together, in the same daylight.
