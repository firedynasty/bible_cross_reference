Two angels hit Sodom when evening fell,  
Lot at the gate, knew something to tell.  
He rose up quick, got on the ground,  
"Come to my house, stay around."  

They said, "Naw, we'll rest on the street,"  
But Lot insisted, so they took a seat.  
He made a feast, bread without leaven,  
But Sodom's men brought no heaven.  

They gathered 'round, pounding the door,  
"Bring out the men, we want more."  
Lot stepped out, shut the door fast,  
"Don't do this evil, let it pass."  

"Take my daughters," Lot did plead,  
"These men are under my roof, take heed."  
But they pushed, wanting more,  
Angels pulled Lot in, slammed the door.  

Blinded the crowd, great and small,  
"Who else you got? We’re saving y’all."  
"The city's doomed, we’ll make it fall,"  
"Get your kin, save them all."  

Lot ran, warned his kin,  
“Yahweh’s wrath, it’s coming in!"  
But they laughed, thought it a joke,  
Judgment wasn't a thing they awoke.  

Morning light, angels pressed,  
"Get your family, flee the mess."  
Lot lingered, fear took hold,  
Angels grabbed, mercy bold.  

"Escape for life, don’t look back,  
To the mountains, don’t you slack."  
Lot begged, "Let’s detour,  
To a small city, I'll be sure."  

Angels agreed, "Hurry there,  
I’ll spare that place, heed my prayer."  
Fire fell, sulfur and smoke,  
Cities fell, earth awoke.  

Lot’s wife turned, a salty end,  
Too late now to comprehend.  
Smoke rose high, Abraham saw,  
God’s true judgment, without flaw.  

Lot lived mountain-high,  
Daughters plotted, no men nearby.  
Wine flowed, plans were laid,  
Preserve the line, no delay made.  

Firstborn, then the young,  
Lay with their father, songs unsung.  
Moab and Ben-Ammi born,  
Two nations from daughters’ scorn.
