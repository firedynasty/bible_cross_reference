"Maintain justice, do what's right," Yahweh said,  
"My salvation's coming, righteousness widespread."  

Blessed is the man who holds it tight,  
Who keeps the Sabbath, walking in the light.  

Foreigners joining, don't despair inside,  
Yahweh won't leave you, won't cast you aside.  

To the eunuchs keeping Sabbath, pleasing His way,  
"An everlasting name," Yahweh will say.  

Foreigners loving, serving Yahweh's name,  
To His holy mountain, they'll proclaim.  

Joy in His house, their prayers ascend,  
Offerings accepted, on His altar, they'll blend.  

"My house a prayer for all," Yahweh's decree,  
"Gathering outcasts, more with thee."  

Animals of the field, come and devour,  
Blind watchmen lie, dreaming at this hour.  

Mute dogs slumber, never enough they crave,  
Shepherds lost, each to his own path they pave.  

"Come, get wine, drink strong and hearty,"  
"Tomorrow's just today, a never-ending party."
