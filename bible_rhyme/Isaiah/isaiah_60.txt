"Arise, shine, your light's come, it's true,  
Yahweh's glory's rising, shining on you.  
Darkness wraps the earth, thick and deep,  
But Yahweh's light on you will never sleep.  

Nations drawn to your brilliant display,  
Kings step up to the dawn of your day.  
Look around, see them gather near,  
Sons and daughters from far will appear.  

You'll see and glow, heart swelling wide,  
As the sea’s abundance flows to your side.  
Wealth of nations, riches untold,  
Camels bring gifts, frankincense, and gold.  

Flocks of Kedar, rams will serve,  
To my altar, their offerings will curve.  
"Who flies like clouds, like doves on the breeze?"  
Islands and ships bring sons across seas.  

Foreigners building, kings at your call,  
Once struck in wrath, now my mercy's your all.  
Gates wide open, never shut tight,  
Wealth of the nations pours in day and night.  

Nations against you will fade and break,  
Cypress and pine to your beauty's sake.  
Those who mocked, now bowing low,  
Call you Yahweh’s City, Zion aglow.  

Once forsaken, now you’ll shine bright,  
A joy for ages, in Yahweh's sight.  
Milk of nations, royal supply,  
Know I’m your Savior, never pass by.  

Bronze for gold, iron for silver trade,  
Peace and righteousness your rulers made.  
No more violence, no more woe,  
Walls of Salvation, Gates of Praise will show.  

Sun and moon, no longer your light,  
Yahweh's your everlasting sight.  
Mourning's over, your sun will stay,  
Your moon won't fade, night turns to day.  

All righteous, the land you’ll claim,  
Branch of my planting, lifting my name.  
Little to thousands, small to strong,  
Yahweh will make it, won't take long."
