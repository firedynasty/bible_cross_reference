In the year Tartan rolled into town,   
Sargon sent him to throw Ashdod down.   

Yahweh spoke to Isaiah, "Go, my son,   
Shed your sackcloth, let your feet run."   

Isaiah stepped, naked and bare,   
For three long years, he didn't care.   

Yahweh said, "As Isaiah walked,   
Egypt and Ethiopia must heed what's talked.   

The king of Assyria will lead them away,   
Captives will march, nothing to say.   

Naked and bare, young and old,   
Their shame exposed, stories told.   

Dismayed they'll be, their hope in despair,   
Ethiopia and Egypt left bare.   

On that day, the coast land cries,   
'Where's our help? Where's our prize?'   

'We fled for shelter, we sought escape,   
From Assyria's grip, yet how can we reshape?'"
