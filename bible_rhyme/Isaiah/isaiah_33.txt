Woe to those who destroy, not yet destroyed,  
Those who betray, though they’ve never been annoyed.  
When you're done with your deeds, the tables will turn,  
The destruction and betrayal you will soon learn.  

"Yahweh, be gracious, we wait for your might,  
Our strength every morning, our salvation in fright."  
Thunder rolls, people scatter in dread,  
Nations flee when His presence is bred.  

Plunder, like caterpillars, men will seize,  
Locusts leap on the spoils with ease.  
Yahweh's on high, Zion's filled with justice deep,  
Stability and wisdom, treasures we keep.  

Valiant ones cry, peace ambassadors mourn,  
Desolate highways, covenants are torn.  
Land in languish, Lebanon fades away,  
Sharon's a desert, Bashan and Carmel decay.  

"Now I will rise," Yahweh's voice booms,  
"I'll lift myself up, in my power you'll consume.  
Chaff and stubble you'll conceive and bear,  
Your breath a fire, consuming air."  

Like burning lime, people's downfall we see,  
Far and near hear His might, acknowledge His decree.  
Sinners in fear, trembling in Zion's face,  
Who can withstand the everlasting blaze?  

Righteous walkers, blameless talkers, they survive,  
Despising oppression, rejecting the bribe.  
Dwell on high, fortress made of rock,  
Bread and water, sure as a clock.  

Eyes see the king, in beauty's embrace,  
A land once distant, now a visible place.  
Heart remembers terror, where are the counters now?  
Gone is the speech you can't comprehend, somehow.  

Zion, your festivals, Jerusalem stands,  
A quiet tent, its stakes held by strong hands.  
Yahweh with majesty, rivers wide,  
No ship will steer, no oars will glide.  

Yahweh our judge, lawgiver, and king,  
He saves us, His praises we sing.  
Rigging untied, sails failing to spread,  
Great plunder divides, the lame are well-fed.  

None will say, "I am sick," in this forgiven city,  
Inhabitants dwell free from iniquity.
