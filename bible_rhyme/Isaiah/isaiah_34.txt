Come gather 'round, all nations, lend your ear,  
Listen close, let the world and its contents hear.  

Yahweh's anger's fierce, the armies face His wrath,  
They're handed to slaughter, no escape, no path.  

Bodies cast out, the stench rises high,  
Mountains melt in the blood, red rivers never dry.  

The sky's army dissolves, rolls up like a scroll,  
Leaves fade from the vine, like a story untold.  

"My sword's drunk its fill," the judgment's set,  
Targets Edom next, "On the cursed they'll be met."  

Yahweh’s sword drips blood, soaked and fat,  
Lambs and goats fall, rams are laid flat.  

Great slaughter in Edom, Bozrah's the scene,  
Blood drunken land, dust greasy sheen.  

A day of vengeance, a year for Zion’s cause,  
Streams turn to pitch, sulfur in the dust draws.  

It burns unquenched both night and day,  
Smoke rises forever, waste it will stay.  

Pelicans and porcupines will call it home,  
Owls, ravens dwell where no nobles will roam.  

Thorns in the palaces, nettles in the forts,  
Jackals own the courts, ostriches in retorts.  

Desert beasts and wolves have their meeting ground,  
Wild goats call out, night creatures find sound.  

Arrow snakes nest, hatch, and lay,  
Kites gather with mates, in this decay.  

Search Yahweh's book, nothing will miss its place,  
His words command, His spirit gathers grace.  

The lot is cast, measured by His hand,  
Generations hold what's been planned.
