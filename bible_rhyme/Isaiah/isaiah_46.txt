Bel bows low, Nebo drops to the ground,  
Their idols are heavy, they're dragging around.  

Animals bear the weight, a burden once light,  
But now they're captive, no freedom in sight.  

"Listen up, Jacob's house, you left in the cold,  
From birth to gray hairs, I'm your strength to hold.  

I created, I carry, through youth to old age,  
I bear and deliver, my promise, my pledge."  

"Who can you compare to me, none can relate,  
Gold from the bag, silver to create.  

They hire a smith, make a god, bow low,  
Yet it stands still, no help in the woe.  

Cry out all you want, it can't move, can't save,  
Remember this truth, from cradle to grave.  

Recall the past, know I'm the only one,  
From start to end, my will is done.  

From east, like a bird, I call on my own,  
I've spoken, I'll act, my plans are known."  

"Listen, stubborn hearts, you're far from the mark,  
But my righteousness nears, igniting the dark.  

Salvation's on the way, it won't be delayed,  
Zion will rise, my glory displayed."
