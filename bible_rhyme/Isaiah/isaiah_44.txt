"Listen up, Jacob, my chosen crew,  
I'm Yahweh, who made and formed you.  
Don't be scared, Jacob, my servant true,  
Jeshurun, I've picked you too.  

I'll pour water on the thirsty land,  
Spirit on your kids, blessings so grand.  
They'll spring up like willows by the stream,  
Claiming my name, living the dream.  

One shouts, 'I'm Yahweh's!' loud and proud,  
Another writes 'to Yahweh,' vows avowed.  
Here's what Yahweh, Israel's King, now says,  
"I'm the first, last, in all my ways.  

Besides me, there's no other God,  
Who can match my power, spread abroad?  
Don't fear, I've shown you times of yore,  
I'm your Rock, nothing else to explore."  

Carved images, they got no soul,  
Makers of idols, they dig their own hole.  
Smith swings the ax, works in the coal,  
Arm gets weary, can't fill the bowl.  

Carpenter draws, marks with a line,  
Shapes a man, beauty so fine.  
Cuts down cedars, plants trees tall,  
Uses wood for heat, bread, and all.  

Part becomes fire, warms his bone,  
But then makes a god, prays on his own.  
Eyes shut, hearts can't understand,  
Worshiping lies made by their hand.  

"Remember, Jacob, you're my own,  
I made you, my servant, never alone.  
Blotted out sins like clouds in the sky,  
Turn back to me, your redemption's nigh.  

Heavens, sing out! Earth, shout loud!  
Mountains and forests join the crowd.  
For Yahweh redeemed Jacob, and yes,  
In Israel, his glory will manifest.  

I, Yahweh, your Redeemer, who formed you,  
Spread the heavens, earth by my view.  
Confuses liars, makes wise men fools,  
Confirms my word, breaks all the rules.  

Says Jerusalem will rise and build,  
Judah's cities, their promise fulfilled.  
Dries up rivers, speaks to the deep,  
Says of Cyrus, 'My shepherd, keep.'  

Jerusalem's foundation will be laid,  
And the temple's promise, unafraid."
