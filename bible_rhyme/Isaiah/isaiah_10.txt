Woe to those who write decrees, unjust and cold,  
Stealing rights from the poor, leaving widows in the fold.  
What will you do when judgment comes near,  
Fleeing for help, yet trembling with fear?  

The Assyrian, my rod of wrath, I will send,  
Against a nation who refuses to bend.  
He'll plunder and prey, tread them down in the street,  
But his own heart desires a complete defeat.  

He boasts, “Aren’t my princes all kings?  
Calno like Carchemish, see what power brings!”  
As idols abound in kingdoms far and wide,  
He plans to treat Jerusalem like Samaria's pride.  

But when my work on Zion's done,  
Assyria’s proud heart will be overrun.  
He says, “By my hand, my wisdom, my might,  
I’ve robbed and ruled, turned day into night.”  

But should the axe boast over the hand that swings?  
Or the saw claim power over the one it brings?  
So Yahweh of Armies will send leanness and fire,  
Burning thorns and briers, consuming all desire.  

The forest's glory, both soul and flesh,  
Will fall, few trees left, light and fresh.  
In that day, Israel's remnant will lean,  
On the Holy One, not on the unseen.  

A remnant will return to the mighty Lord,  
Though like sand, only a few will board.  
For Yahweh, the Lord, will make an end,  
Throughout the earth, His judgment will send.  

“Fear not, Zion, though Assyria strike,  
For soon my wrath will take a hike.”  
Yahweh will rise against them, as of old,  
Breaking their yoke, His power bold.  

Through Aiath, Migron, and Michmash they pass,  
Ramah trembles, trembling like glass.  
But Yahweh of Armies will lop the tall,  
Bringing them low, the mighty will fall.  

Cutting down forests, the thickets will thin,  
By the Mighty One’s hand, Lebanon will spin.  
The lofty will be humbled, the proud laid bare,  
For Yahweh's power is everywhere.
