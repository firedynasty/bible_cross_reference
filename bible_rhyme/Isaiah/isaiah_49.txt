Listen up, islands, hear my call,  
Yahweh named me from the womb, y'all.  

My mouth's sharp, like a sword in hand,  
Hidden in His shadow, part of His plan.  

He called me out, "You’re my servant, my pride,  
In you, Israel, I will be glorified."  

But I said, "I worked, nothing gained,  
Yet my justice is with Yahweh, unstained."  

He formed me, made me strong,  
To bring Jacob back where they belong.  

"It’s light work to restore Israel's fate,  
You’ll shine for nations, salvation's gate."  

To him who’s despised, Yahweh proclaimed,  
"Kings and princes will rise, know your name."  

"I’ve answered in time, helped on salvation’s day,  
Preserved you for people, your strength won’t decay."  

To the bound, "Come out!" to darkness, "Reveal!"  
They’ll feed on treeless heights, with pasture to heal.  

No hunger, no thirst, the sun won’t scorch,  
For mercy guides them with springs that torch.  

"I’ll make mountains roads, highways elevated,  
From far they’ll come, as I’ve related."  

Sing, heavens, earth, break in song,  
Yahweh comforts, makes the weak strong.  

But Zion cries, “Forgotten, forsaken!”  
"Can a mother forget? Her love unshaken."  

"Engraved on my hands, your walls remain,  
Children return, those who hurt will wane."  

Look around, they gather near,  
"Like a bride, you’ll dress, no need to fear."  

Your land, once waste, now too small,  
The devoured flee; you’ll stand tall.  

"Who bore these?" you’ll say, in disbelief,  
Alone you were, now joy relieves.  

Yahweh's hand lifts up high,  
Nations bring your children nigh.  

Kings and queens, bow to the earth,  
Know Yahweh’s promise, mighty in worth.  

"Can the strong keep their captive hold?  
No, I'll rescue; your story’s retold."  

Oppressors will feast on their own,  
All flesh will know Yahweh’s throne.  

Yahweh saves, Redeemer, Mighty One,  
Jacob's strength, His will be done.
