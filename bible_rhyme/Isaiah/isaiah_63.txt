Who comes from Edom, garments dyed in red?  
Striding strong, with glory spread.  
"It is I, righteous in speech, mighty to save,"  
Clothing stained like one who treads in a wine wave.  

"I've trod the press alone, no one at my side,  
In anger, I trampled, in wrath I did stride.  
Their lifeblood on my clothes, stained deep,  
A day of vengeance in my heart I keep.  

No one to help, none to uphold,  
My arm brought salvation, my wrath took hold.  
Trod down the people, made them drunk in my ire,  
Their lifeblood outpoured, a tale of my fire."  

I'll speak of Yahweh's kindness, his praise I'll sing,  
All he's bestowed, the goodness he brings.  
"Surely these are my children, honest and true,"  
He became their Savior, in love he drew.  

In affliction, he was there, an angel to redeem,  
In love and pity, he bore them, like a dream.  
But they rebelled, his Spirit they grieved,  
He turned an enemy, from blessings they were relieved.  

Remember Moses, leading them through the sea,  
His Holy Spirit, among them, set them free.  
Parting waters, in glory, with strength he came,  
He led them safe, to build his name.  

Look down from heaven, see us here below,  
Where's your zeal and power, your love to show?  
You're our Father, Redeemer from the start,  
Even if Abraham doesn't know, you're in our heart.  

Why let us wander, why our hearts turn cold?  
Return, Yahweh, for your people of old.  
Adversaries tread where we once stood tall,  
Now like strangers, not called by your name at all.
