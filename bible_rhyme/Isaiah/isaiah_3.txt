Yahweh of Armies, removing the town's bread and water,  
From Judah and Jerusalem, no support, just slaughter.  

Warriors and judges, prophets, elders, all gone,  
Captains and counselors, like dust at dawn.  

"I'll make children your princes, let kids take the reign,"  
Oppression and chaos, like a flood, brings pain.  

Neighbors turn on neighbors, young against old,  
A brother pleads, "Lead us, take control."  

But he cries, "I'm no healer, got nothing to give,  
Don't look at me to rule, I'm just trying to live."  

Jerusalem's crumbling, Judah's fallen low,  
Their words and deeds against Yahweh, a bold show.  

Their faces tell tales, sins they parade,  
Woe to those souls, disaster they've made.  

But tell the righteous, "Good!" Their deeds bear fruit,  
Woe to the wicked for they reap what they loot.  

Children oppress, women rule, leaders lead astray,  
Yahweh stands to judge, hear what He'll say:  

"You've eaten the vineyard, left the poor in despair,  
Crushed my people, do you even care?"  

Says Yahweh, "Zion's daughters, proud and vain,  
With jingling feet, their arrogance plain.  

I'll bring sores to their crowns, and bald their heads,  
Strip their beauty, adornments, their fine threads.  

Rings, robes, perfumes, all taken away,  
Sweet spices rot, and sackcloth's here to stay.  

Your men will fall by the sword, mighty to weak,  
Her gates will mourn, desolate, with futures bleak."
