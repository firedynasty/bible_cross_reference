Yahweh rides on a cloud, swift as night,  
To Egypt's land, where idols take fright.  

"Egypt will tremble, hearts melt inside,  
Brother against brother, no place to hide."  

Their spirits fail, their wisdom torn,  
Seek idols and charmers, feeling forlorn.  

"A cruel lord, a fierce king's hand,  
Will rule with power over the land."  

Waters dry up, rivers run foul,  
Nile's fields vanish, meadows disavow.  

Fishermen mourn, nets empty, bare,  
Workers in cloth, in sorrow they stare.  

Zoan's princes, foolish and blind,  
Counsel once wise, now left behind.  

"Where's your wisdom?" Yahweh demands,  
"Let them tell you, if they understand."  

Egypt's astray, lost in dismay,  
Like a drunkard, they stagger each day.  

No work remains, no head or tail,  
Fear grips the hearts, courage will fail.  

Five cities speak the Canaan tongue,  
Swear to Yahweh, where hope had sprung.  

Altar and pillar stand in the land,  
A sign and witness, Yahweh's command.  

They cry for help, a savior He sends,  
Strikes and heals, making amends.  

Egypt returns, to Yahweh they call,  
Assyria and Egypt, worship for all.  

Israel joins, a blessing unfolds,  
"Egypt, my people," Yahweh holds.  

Assyria, His work, Israel, His own,  
Together they stand, in blessings they've grown.
