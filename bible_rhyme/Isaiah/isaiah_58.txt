Cry out loud, don’t hold back, lift up your voice,  
Tell my people 'bout their sins, let ‘em hear your choice.  
They seek me daily, act like they care,  
As if they do right, like they’re always fair.

They ask for my judgments, draw near with delight,  
But they wonder, “We fast, why's our plight outta sight?”  
"You see, on your fast day, you chase your own gain,  
Oppress your workers, ignore their pain.

You fast with strife, fistfuls of hate,  
Think that's the way to get through Heaven’s gate?  
Is this the fast that I’ve chosen for you?  
A day to bow, sackcloth, ashes too?

Nah, I chose to break wicked chains,  
Undo the yokes, free folks from their pains.  
Share your bread, bring the homeless in,  
Clothe the naked, that's where it begins.

Then your light breaks out like morning’s embrace,  
Healing comes quick, righteousness makes its case.  
Call out to Yahweh, and He'll reply,  
‘Here I am,’ He’ll say, when you cry.

Stop the pointing, the wicked speech,  
Pour out to the hungry, practice what you preach.  
Your light will rise in the darkness, shout,  
Yahweh will guide, no doubt.

You'll be a garden, watered and bright,  
A spring that flows, day and night.  
Rebuild the ruins, raise up the past,  
Repairer of Breach, a title that’ll last.

Turn from your ways on the Sabbath day,  
Delight in Yahweh, let His honor stay.  
Ride the high places, fed with Jacob’s grace,  
For Yahweh’s spoken, and that’s your place."
