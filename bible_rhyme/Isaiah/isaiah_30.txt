"Woe to the rebellious ones," Yahweh declares with might,  
"Counsel you take, but not in My sight.  
You form alliances without My Spirit's grace,  
Adding sin upon sin, you're losing the race.  

You head down to Egypt, not seeking My view,  
Trusting Pharaoh's strength, but shame'll come through.  
The refuge of Egypt, a shadow to deceive,  
Princes at Zoan, ambassadors' reprieve.  

Ashamed, you'll be of a people no aid,  
A burden you carry, alliances mislaid.  
Through lands of lions, vipers in flight,  
Riches on donkeys, camels in sight.  

Egypt's vain help, no purpose, no gain,  
"Rahab who sits still," I've called her in vain.  
"Write it on tablets," a record to last,  
A rebellious people, ignoring the past.  

Seers they tell, "Don't see!" Prophets they implore,  
"Prophesy lies, pleasant things at the core."  
"Get out of the way," they demand, so bold,  
Turning from the path, their stories unfold.  

Because you despise, trust oppression's embrace,  
Your wall will crumble, shatter without trace.  
Like a potter's vessel, in pieces you'll fall,  
Nothing left to gather from the crumbling wall.  

"Rest and return," the Holy One calls,  
"Strength in quietness," but deaf to His halls.  
"We'll flee on horses," you state and you boast,  
But swift are your foes, and you'll flee the most.  

One thousand will run from the threat of one,  
Left like a beacon when the fleeing's done.  
Yet Yahweh waits, gracious and just,  
Mercy He holds for those who trust.  

Dwell in Zion, no tears shall you shed,  
He'll hear your cry, and answer instead.  
Adversity's bread, affliction's drink,  
Yet teachers visible, to guide when you think.  

Turn right or left, you'll hear Him say,  
"This is the path, walk in this way."  
Throw away idols, silver and gold,  
Unclean they are, let the new story unfold.  

Rain for your seed, bread from the earth,  
Livestock in pastures, a sign of rebirth.  
Oxen and donkeys eating savory feed,  
Brooks on mountains, fulfilling your need.  

Moon like the sun, sun sevenfold bright,  
Yahweh binds wounds, His people in sight.  
From afar His name comes, anger and smoke,  
Lips with fire, nations to provoke.  

A song in the night, a feast in His name,  
Flutes to the mountain, joy in acclaim.  
Yahweh's voice, strong, will make enemies fear,  
With tambourines and harps, His justice is near.  

For Assyria's stroke, His rod will descend,  
Yahweh's breath, like sulfur, will bring it to end.  
The pyre is prepared, deep with much wood,  
His stream of sulfur, kindles it good."
