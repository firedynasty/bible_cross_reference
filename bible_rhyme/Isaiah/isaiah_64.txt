Tear the heavens, come on down, let mountains quake and shake the ground.  
Like fire on brushwood, flames ignite, make water boil, show your might.  

Make your name known to foes, renowned, let nations tremble all around.  
You did amazing things of old, came down, the mountains you controlled.  

No ear has heard, no eye has seen a God like you who intervenes,  
For those who wait, you pave the way, for the righteous, clear the day.  

"You were angry, we confessed, for long in sin we were oppressed.  
Now we ask, can we be saved? Our deeds like rags, we're enslaved."  

Like leaves we fade, blown by the breeze, our sins like winds, with no ease.  
No one calls your name aloud, from us, your face, you have shrouded.  

Yet you, Yahweh, are our Father, the clay to mold, the hand you offer.  
"Don't be furious, don't hold the past, we're your people, we ask at last."  

Your holy cities, now laid bare, Zion's wilderness, no one there.  
Jerusalem's a desolate scene, a house once praised, now unseen.  

Burned with fire, pleasant places waste, "Will you stay silent, leave us disgraced?"  
Yahweh, will you hold back your hand, or punish us as you have planned?
