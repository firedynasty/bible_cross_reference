Yo, Isaiah saw Babylon’s fate,  
A banner high on the mountain, don’t wait.  

"Lift your voice, wave your hand,"  
God's army rolls like waves on the sand.  

Commanded, consecrated, they march with pride,  
A noise from the mountains, like a rising tide.  

Yahweh’s army, ready for the fight,  
Weapons of wrath, descending from the height.  

"Yahweh’s day is at hand!" they cry,  
Destruction’s here, see the Almighty’s sky.  

Hands go weak, hearts start to shiver,  
Pain like labor, faces all a-quiver.  

"Behold, Yahweh’s day is cruel and fierce,  
To make the land a desolate pierce."  

Stars hide their shimmer, the sun turns dark,  
The moon won’t shine, igniting Yahweh’s spark.  

"I’ll punish evil, bring the proud low,  
Make the world tremble, let the fear grow."  

Rare as gold, people vanish in fright,  
The earth shakes in Yahweh’s might.  

Like hunted gazelles, they scatter and flee,  
No safety found, no sanctuary.  

The sword strikes down any caught,  
Houses ransacked, everything distraught.  

"Behold, here come the Medes, they don’t want your gold,  
Bows break the young, their hearts cold."  

Babylon’s glory, destined to fall,  
Like Sodom and Gomorrah, won’t stand tall.  

Empty, desolate, no life to see,  
No tents, no flocks, just wild and free.  

Jackals and ostriches roam the night,  
Palaces silent, no pleasant light.  

Her time is near, her days are through,  
Babylon’s end, nothing left to do.
