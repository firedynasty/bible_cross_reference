Woe to the proud crown of Ephraim's crew,  
Drunk on the wine, their glory askew.  
A fading flower in the valley so fine,  
Lost in their drink, overcome by the vine.   

Behold, the Lord sends one fierce and strong,  
Like hail and a storm, sweeping along.  
The pride of Ephraim, trampled underfoot,  
Beauty fades like a fig, soon to be took.  

In that day, Yahweh will shine bright,  
A crown of glory, a beacon of light.  
Justice and strength for those who fight,  
Turning the battle back at the gate, with might.  

Priests and prophets, stagger and sway,  
Lost in their visions, led astray.  
Tables full of filth, vomit and grime,  
Who will learn knowledge, in this time?  

"Precept on precept, line after line,"  
He'll speak in stammers, not in the divine.  
"This is the rest," He said, but they turned away,  
So they’ll stumble and fall, night and day.  

Hear the Lord's word, you who mock,  
Thinking your lies are a shelter, a rock.  
"We've made a deal with death," you say,  
But Yahweh declares a different way.  

"Behold, in Zion, a cornerstone set,"  
Justice and truth, the measure met.  
Your covenant with death, it won't stand,  
Swept away by hail and mighty hand.  

The scourge will pass, terror in its wake,  
Morning by morning, the earth will shake.  
The bed's too short, the blanket's too tight,  
Yahweh will rise, in power and might.  

So heed the speech, listen and hear,  
The voice of the Lord, crystal clear.  
Does the farmer plow forever, no end?  
He knows when to sow, when to mend.  

God instructs in wisdom, in plan,  
Threshes with care, by His hand.  
From Yahweh comes counsel, profound,  
In His wisdom, true answers are found.
