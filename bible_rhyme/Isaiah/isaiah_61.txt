Yahweh's Spirit's on me, I'm anointed for the humble mission,  
Preachin' good news, lifting hearts with precision.  

Sent to bind the broken, set the captives free,  
Proclaimin' liberty, release for all to see.  

Declare the year of favor, vengeance day on the mark,  
Comfort for the mourners, light in Zion's dark.  

Garlands for ashes, joy for mourning's weight,  
Praise replaces heaviness, righteousness they cultivate.  

Rebuild old ruins, raise what’s left behind,  
Repair the cities, from past sorrows unwind.  

Strangers feed your flocks, foreigners in your fields,  
Priests of Yahweh you'll be, the glory of nations yields.  

Double for your shame, rejoice where others scoff,  
Everlasting joy, in their land they'll top it off.  

"For I, Yahweh, love justice, hate the crooked scheme,  
Reward in truth is given, an everlasting dream."  

Offspring known worldwide, blessed by Yahweh's decree,  
All will see and know, His blessings come to be.  

Rejoice in Yahweh, my soul's joyful tune,  
Clothed in salvation, righteousness’ bloom.  

Like earth births its buds, like gardens spring anew,  
Yahweh sparks righteousness, praise for nations too.
