For Zion’s sake, I won't keep still,  
For Jerusalem's good, I climb this hill,  
Till her righteousness shines like dawn's first light,  
And her salvation like a lamp burning bright.  

Nations will see your righteous ways,  
Kings will behold your glorious days.  
You'll get a new name from Yahweh's own breath,  
A crown of beauty, a diadem of faith.  

No longer Forsaken, nor Desolate,  
Hephzibah and Beulah, your new state.  
For Yahweh delights in all that you do,  
Your land's like a bride, made new.  

Watchmen on walls, they never sleep,  
Day and night, their vigil they keep.  
Call on Yahweh, don’t take a break,  
Till Jerusalem's praise, the earth will shake.  

Yahweh's sworn by His own strong hand,  
"Your grain won't feed a foreign land.  
The wine you've worked for, it's yours to enjoy,  
In My courts, with songs of joy."  

Go through the gates, prepare the way,  
Build the highway, clear stones away.  
Lift up a banner, let it unfurl,  
Yahweh proclaims to the whole wide world:  

"Daughter of Zion, see your salvation near,  
With His reward, His presence here."  
Called "Holy People, Yahweh’s Redeemed,"  
"Sought Out, Not Forsaken," you'll be esteemed.
