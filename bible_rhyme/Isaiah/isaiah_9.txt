No more gloom for her who was in pain,  
Old days brought contempt like driving rain.  

But now the land shines, it's glorious to see,  
By the sea's way, Galilee's set free.  

People in darkness have seen a bright light,  
In death's shadow, now things are right.  

You've grown the nation, joy increased,  
Like harvest time, their happiness released.  

The yoke and staff, oppressor's rod you broke,  
Like Midian's day, their power's a joke.  

Armored men and bloody clothes in the fray,  
Fuel for the fire, all burned away.  

For unto us a child is born,  
A son is given, a new dawn.  

"Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God," he'll be,  
"Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace," we see.  

His rule and peace will never cease,  
On David's throne, justice and peace.  

The zeal of Yahweh will make this real.  
God's word to Jacob, it will reveal.  

Ephraim and Samaria, so full of pride,  
"The bricks have fallen, but we'll rebuild wide."  

Yahweh sets adversaries high and fierce,  
Enemies surround, their defenses pierced.  

The Syrians up front, Philistines in the rear,  
Israel devoured, God's hand still near.  

Yet no turning back to the one who struck,  
Yahweh ignored, they're out of luck.  

So He'll cut off head and tail in a day,  
The leaders' lies lead them astray.  

No joy for the youth, no compassion's call,  
For their mouths speak folly, wickedness in all.  

Wickedness burns like a fire untamed,  
Thorns and briers, all are claimed.  

Yahweh's wrath burns the land like none,  
Brothers devour, the hunger's not done.  

Manasseh hits Ephraim, Ephraim strikes back,  
Together on Judah, they both attack.  

For all this, His anger remains still,  
Yet His hand is stretched, His will fulfill.
