Sing out, barren, lift your voice high,  
Shout and rejoice, let your praises fly.  

For more are your children, oh desolate soul,  
Than those of the wife, your numbers roll.  

"Stretch wide your tent, make room, don't hold back,  
Strengthen your stakes, prepare for the pack.  

You'll spread out right and left, your seed will reign,  
Possess the nations, cities they'll claim.  

Fear not, no shame will cloud your way,  
Forget your past, move into a new day.  

Your Maker's your husband, the Holy One's here,  
The God of the earth, with you, near.  

Like a wife cast aside, now called back again,  
For a moment I left, now mercy's your friend.  

Though wrath hid my face, my kindness will last,  
No flood like Noah's, the storm has passed.  

Mountains may shift, hills may fall,  
But my peace and promise will stand tall.  

You storm-tossed and alone, see what I bring,  
Foundations of sapphires, your heart will sing.  

Pinnacles, rubies, gates that shine,  
Walls of jewels, all divine.  

Your children taught by Yahweh's hand,  
In peace and righteousness, they'll stand.  

Oppression and terror, they'll not find you,  
Together they gather, but they'll fall through.  

"I made the blacksmith, the coals he'll fan,  
Forged a weapon, it's part of my plan.  

But no weapon formed will prevail,  
Every tongue against you will fail.  

This is your heritage from me,  
Yahweh's servants, righteously free."
