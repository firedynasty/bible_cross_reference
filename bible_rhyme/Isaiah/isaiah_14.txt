Yahweh's got compassion, Jacob's in His sight,  
Israel's chosen, set in their own land, that's right.  
Foreigners join, unite with Jacob's crew,  
Israel holds them, servants, handmaids too.  

Captives flip the script, now they're in control,  
Oppressors get ruled, tables turned, that's the goal.  
Rest from sorrow, trouble fades away,  
Yahweh grants peace, a brand new day.  

"How's the oppressor ceased? Babylon's power gone,  
Yahweh’s broken wickedness, the rulers drawn."  
Nations shook in anger, now the earth's at rest,  
Trees rejoice, no axe to test their best.  

From beneath, Sheol stirs, kings rise to greet,  
"Are you weak like us?"—challenge they repeat.  
Your pride's brought down, maggots lay your bed,  
Fallen from heaven, ambitions now dead.  

"I'll ascend to the heights, above the stars I'll climb,  
Sit on the mountain, 'Most High' in my mind."  
Yet Sheol calls, depths of the pit await,  
Eyes stare, "Is this the man who shook the state?"  

Destroyed your land, your people fall,  
No name for evildoers, gone once and for all.  
"Rise against them," Yahweh’s voice is loud,  
Babylon swept clean, destruction like a shroud.  

Yahweh's plan is firm, no one can deflect,  
Assyrian's broken, burden off the neck.  
His hand is stretched, no one turns it back,  
Against His will, no power can attack.  

Philistia, don't cheer, though your rod's torn,  
Out of the serpent, a fiery one is born.  
Poor will eat, needy lie in peace,  
Your root fades with famine, no more release.  

The gate howls, city cries, smoke rises high,  
From the north it comes, no straggler defies.  
Yahweh's built Zion, a refuge for the afflicted,  
In His foundation, safety is predicted.
