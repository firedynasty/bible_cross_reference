The wilderness shouts, dry land's got a smile,  
Desert's blooming roses, stretching for miles.  

It blossoms with joy, with song and pride,  
Lebanon's glory, Carmel, Sharon by its side.  

They see Yahweh's glory, a sight so grand,  
Strengthen weak hands, make firm every stand.  

"Strong hearts!" to the fearful, "Fear not, hear the call!  
Your God comes with power, to save you all."  

Eyes of the blind, they open wide,  
Deaf ears hear, no need to hide.  

The lame one leaps, like a deer so spry,  
Mute tongues sing, waters flow and fly.  

Burning sands turn to pools, thirst gone,  
Grass grows where jackals lay at dawn.  

A highway rises, called "The Holy Way,"  
For those in truth, it's the chosen day.  

Lions absent, no beasts roam here,  
The redeemed walk on, in joy and cheer.  

Yahweh's ransomed return, singing to Zion,  
Everlasting joy crowns them, like a lion.  

Gladness and joy, they hold and sway,  
Sorrow and sighing, chased far away.
