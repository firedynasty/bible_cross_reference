Yahweh asks, "Where's that divorce you think I gave?  
Did I sell you off? No way, it’s sin that made you slaves.  

When I came calling, why was there no one around?  
Is my arm too short to lift you from the ground?  

I dry the seas with just a rebuke, make rivers dry,  
The fish rot away, no water, they die.  

I cover the heavens in darkness, sackcloth's their sheet,  
The Lord Yahweh speaks words that weary souls greet.  

Morning by morning, He wakes my ear to learn,  
His teachings I hear, His wisdom I yearn.  

I didn't rebel, didn't turn my back in fear,  
Faced the beatings, the shame, the spit, stayed near.  

The Lord Yahweh helps me, I won't be ashamed,  
Face like flint, strong, His trust proclaimed.  

Who'll accuse me? Stand up, let's see,  
The Lord helps, who can condemn me?  

Adversaries age like clothes, moths eat them away,  
Trust Yahweh, walk in light, come what may.  

But those who light their own fire, torches ablaze,  
Will lie down in sorrow, lost in their own haze."
