On that day you'll declare, "Yahweh, thanks I give,  
Your anger's turned away, now I truly live.  

Behold, God is my salvation, my shield,  
I trust, I'm not afraid, my soul's healed.  

For Yah, Yahweh, is strength and song,  
He's my salvation, where I belong.  

With joy you'll draw from salvation's well,  
In that day you'll shout, let the stories swell.  

"Give thanks to Yahweh, call His name loud,  
Declare His works among the crowd.  

Proclaim His name, exalted and high,  
Sing to Yahweh, let praises fly.  

He's done excellent things, let the earth know,  
Cry and shout, let the praises show.  

You who dwell in Zion, let your voices ring,  
The Holy One of Israel, among you, the King!"
