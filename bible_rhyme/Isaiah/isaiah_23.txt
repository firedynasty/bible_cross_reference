Howl, ships of Tarshish, hear the news so dire,  
Tyre's laid waste, no house, no entry to inspire.  

From Kittim's land, the word is revealed,  
Coast dwellers silent, their fate is sealed.  

Merchants of Sidon, across the sea they sail,  
Nile's harvest their wealth, nations prevail.  

Sidon, now ashamed, listen to the sea,  
"I've birthed no one, no youths raised by me."  

Report to Egypt brings nothing but pain,  
The fall of Tyre, a loss, a gain.  

To Tarshish flee, coast inhabitants wail,  
Is this your ancient city, once joyful, now frail?  

Who plotted against Tyre, crowned with might,  
Princes and traders, earth's honor in sight.  

Yahweh planned it, to humble pride’s throne,  
All earth's honor, brought to the ground alone.  

Pass like the Nile, Tarshish’s own child,  
No restraint now, roam the wild.  

His hand on the sea, kingdoms shaken,  
Yahweh commands, Canaan's stronghold taken.  

"You'll rejoice no more, Sidon's daughter oppressed,  
Go to Kittim, find no rest."  

Chaldeans rise, once naught but a name,  
Assyrians built them, towers of fame.  

Howl, ships of Tarshish, your fortress undone,  
Seventy years Tyre forgotten, under the sun.  

Like a song of a prostitute, Tyre will sing,  
With harp in hand, memories to bring.  

After seventy years, Yahweh will see,  
Tyre will trade, like before she'll be.  

Her goods for Yahweh, not stored away,  
For those before Him, food and clothes to stay.
