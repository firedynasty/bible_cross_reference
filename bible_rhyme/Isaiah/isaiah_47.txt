Babylon, sit down in the dust, no throne to call your own,  
Your days of being delicate, those days have flown.  

Grab the millstones, grind that flour, remove your veil,  
Lift your skirt, wade those rivers, your secrets they'll unveil.  

Your shame exposed, your nakedness in sight,  
Vengeance comes with no mercy, no one spared tonight.  

"Our Redeemer," Yahweh speaks, "The Holy One's decree,  
Daughter of the Chaldeans, now you're sitting silently."  

No longer the mistress, kingdoms slip away,  
Anger with my people, I let you have your way.  

But you showed no mercy, laid a heavy yoke,  
Claimed you'd be a princess forever, spoke like a joke.  

Heed the warning, pleasure seeker, sitting secure,  
Saying, "I'm the only one," your heart that's pure no more.  

Loss will hit you suddenly, widowhood and pain,  
Sorcery and enchantments, your efforts all in vain.  

Trusted in your wickedness, thinking unseen,  
Your wisdom twisted, now disaster unforeseen.  

"Mischief falls upon you," God declares it loud,  
Desolation sudden, darkening your proud.  

Stand with your enchantments, see if they prevail,  
Astrologers and stargazers, their efforts will fail.  

Like stubble in the fire, they'll burn away,  
No warmth, no comfort, nothing left to say.  

Your labors are in vain, traffickers depart,  
No one there to save you, each will break apart.
