Let me spit a tale 'bout a vineyard so fine,  
On a fruitful hill, where the sun would shine.  
Dug deep, cleared stones, planted the best vine,  
Built a tower, pressed wine, all was divine.  

But wild grapes grew, not what he sought,  
"Jerusalem, Judah, judge what I've wrought.  
What more could I do? Why wild, not sweet?  
Now I'll tear down hedges, let it face defeat.  

Walls will crumble, trampled, laid bare,  
Briers and thorns will grow, I declare.  
No rain from the clouds, let it waste away,  
For the house of Israel has gone astray.  

Justice was the goal, but oppression was found,  
Righteous cries lost, distress all around.  
Woe to those who stack houses high,  
Till no room's left, and alone they sigh.  

Yahweh whispers, "Desolate they'll be,  
Great homes vacant, that's what I decree.  
Ten acres yield little, seeds bring no gain,  
Early risers chase drink, night brings the same."  

Music rings, wine flows at the feast,  
But Yahweh's works, they've all ceased.  
People captive, knowledge gone astray,  
Honored men famished, thirst rules the day.  

Sheol's mouth wide, eager to consume,  
Glory and pomp descend into gloom.  
Man brought low, the proud eyes humbled,  
Yet Yahweh's justice stands unjumbled.  

Lambs graze freely, strangers eat the leftovers,  
Woe to those tangled in lies and cover.  
They mock, "Let Him work fast, let us see,  
Bring the Holy One's words, let them be."  

Calling evil good, and good they defame,  
Light for dark, sweet for bitter, who do they blame?  
Wise in their eyes, they sip and boast,  
Justice denied, the innocent lost.  

Tongues of fire devour dry land,  
Roots rot away, dust in hand.  
They've scorned Yahweh's law, despised His word,  
His anger burns, still stretched His hand.  

He calls nations from afar, with a whistle they come,  
Swiftly, sharp arrows, the battle's begun.  
Hooves like flint, wheels whirl like a storm,  
Roar like lions, their prey they transform.  

Roaring sea day, darkness and distress,  
Clouds mask the light, in chaos they confess.
