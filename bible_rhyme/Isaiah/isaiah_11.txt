A shoot rises from Jesse's line, divine and true,  
A branch from his roots, bearing fruit anew.  

The Spirit of Yahweh will rest on this one,  
Wisdom and understanding, like no other son.  

Counsel and might, knowledge so deep,  
In the fear of Yahweh, his soul will steep.  

Not by what he sees, his judgment won't sway,  
He'll hear beyond sounds, in a righteous way.  

He'll judge for the poor, with equity and grace,  
With his words, the wicked he'll erase.  

Righteousness and faithfulness, his belts so tight,  
In his presence, darkness turns to light.  

Wolves and lambs together will play,  
Leopards and goats, in peace, will stay.  

Little child leading, with tender hand,  
Calves and lions, in harmony stand.  

Cows and bears grazing side by side,  
Lions munching straw, no need to hide.  

Children by cobras, fearless and free,  
On the holy mountain, no harm will be.  

"Full of my knowledge, the earth shall be,  
Just like waters cover the deep sea."  

Nations will seek Jesse's root, the sign,  
A banner for people, his glory will shine.  

Yahweh will gather, from lands far and wide,  
His people once scattered, now unified.  

Envy's gone, no more strife, Judah and Ephraim find new life.  
Together they'll march, the foes they'll outpace,  

Yahweh's hand moves with mighty grace.  
Egyptian sea tongues, split by his might,  

Seven streams laid bare, for the feet in flight.  

A highway for those who return from afar,  
Just like the days of Egypt's scar.
