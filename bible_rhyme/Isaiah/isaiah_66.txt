Heaven's my throne, earth's my footstool, you see,  
"What house will you build? Where's my place to be?"  
My hand made it all, everything you know,  
But the humble and contrite, they're the ones I'll show.  

An ox killed is like a man's life taken,  
Sacrificing lambs, with evil mistaken.  
Offerings of pigs' blood, incense to idols,  
They chose their ways, embraced vile titles.  

I’ll bring their fears, delusions they'll find,  
Called out, they ignored, my voice left behind.  
They did what I hate, what I can't delight,  
Now here comes their reckoning, day and night.  

Listen, those trembling at my word's sway,  
"Your brothers hate, cast you away.  
They’ll say, 'Let Yahweh's joy appear,'  
But disappointment’s all they’ll hear."  

A voice from the city, temple's echoing sound,  
Yahweh repays enemies as justice comes around.  
Before pain struck, a child was born,  
Who’s ever seen a land in a day form?  

"Will I bring to birth, not deliver the life?"  
Says Yahweh, your God, the one ending strife.  
Rejoice with Jerusalem, let joy fill your heart,  
In her abundance, you shall take part.  

Peace like a river, nations' glory will stream,  
Carried in comfort, life's sweet dream.  
"As a mother comforts, so shall I,"  
In Jerusalem, tears will dry.  

You'll see it, rejoice, your bones will thrive,  
Yahweh's hand with his servants alive.  
He comes with fire, chariots in flight,  
Judgment fierce, like flames in the night.  

Yahweh’s sword, it will cut through all,  
Many will fall, to their end they’ll crawl.  
Those purifying in gardens, pig’s meat they eat,  
Abominations end in their defeat.  

"I know their works, their thoughts, their deeds,  
Time is near, nations will heed.  
A sign among, to the world they'll go,  
To declare my glory, make it known."  

Brothers from nations, offerings they bring,  
To Jerusalem's mountain, where praises ring.  
"I’ll select priests, Levites true,  
As the new heavens and earth come through."  

From moon to moon, Sabbath's rest,  
All flesh will worship, in Yahweh's nest.  
They'll see transgressors, bodies unquenched,  
In eternal fire, their fate entrenched.
