Woe to those who head to Egypt for aid,  
Trust in horses, think their power won't fade.  
Chariots many, horsemen strong,  
But forget Yahweh, and that’s where they're wrong.  

God’s got wisdom, brings disaster in line,  
His words stay true, rise against evil time.  
Egyptians are men, not divine or grand,  
Their horses are flesh, not spirit in hand.  

When Yahweh's hand stretches out, beware,  
Both the helper and helped will fall, a snare.  
"Like a lion growling over its prey,  
I’ll fight on Zion, guard it day by day.  

Like birds in flight, I'll protect this place,  
Deliver and keep Jerusalem in grace."  
Come back, Israel, from your revolt,  
Cast away idols, break the gold and silver bolt.  

"The Assyrian falls, not by man's sword,  
A force unseen will bring him toward  
Defeat. He’ll run, his young will bow,  
In terror his rock will crumble now."  

Says Yahweh, within Zion his fire will burn,  
Jerusalem's furnace, where the tables turn.
