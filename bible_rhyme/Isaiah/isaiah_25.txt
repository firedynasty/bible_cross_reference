Yahweh, my God, your name I raise high,  
For wonders you've done, planned long before the sky.  

You turned a city into rubble, a fortress to dust,  
A stranger's palace, no more—a place unjust.  

Strong people will honor, nations in awe,  
For you've been a refuge, a haven without flaw.  

In storms and heat, you’re the cool, calming breeze,  
A shade from the chaos, you put hearts at ease.  

You silence the noise, like heat in dry land,  
Clouds bring relief, with your mighty hand.  

On this mountain, a feast of the best,  
Choice meats and wines, where all are blessed.  

You'll tear the veil covering nations wide,  
Death swallowed forever, tears wiped aside.  

"Behold our God! Our Savior at last!"  
In Yahweh’s salvation, our joy will be vast.  

Your hand rests on the mountain, the proud brought low,  
Moab trodden down, like straw where waters flow.  

Spreading hands like a swimmer, pride will be met,  
High walls crumbled, dust settles the debt.
