Hear, house of Jacob, called by Israel's name,  
Out of Judah's water, but truth's not your claim.  

You swear by Yahweh, mention His might,  
But truth and righteousness are out of sight.  

You call the holy city your home,  
Rely on God, Yahweh of Armies known.  

I spoke of old things, declared them clear,  
Suddenly they happened, as you hear.  

You’re obstinate, with iron necks,  
Your brow's like bronze, but still, I check.  

I told you before they came to pass,  
So you won't claim idols made them last.  

You've heard it all, now see and declare,  
New things I've shown, beyond compare.  

Created now, not from before,  
You didn't hear, but I've opened the door.  

Yes, from the past, ears were shut tight,  
But I've known your ways weren’t right.  

For my name, I hold my wrath,  
Refined, not as silver, but in affliction's path.  

For my sake alone, I act with grace,  
My glory I won't let another embrace.  

"Listen, Jacob, Israel called by me,  
I'm the first, the last, eternally.  

My hands laid earth's foundation wide,  
Heavens spread out at my side.  

Gather and hear, among them, who can say?  
Yahweh’s love moves Babylon his way.  

I’ve spoken, called him forth,  
Prosperous paths he's set to go forth.  

Come close, hear my decree,  
I spoke openly, from start, see?  

Yahweh, your Redeemer, speaks true,  
Leads you wisely, teaches what’s due.  

Oh, if you'd listened, peace like a river you'd see,  
Righteousness waving like the sea.  

Offspring as sand, grains without cease,  
Their name enduring in my peace.  

Leave Babylon, flee with songs of praise,  
"Yahweh's redeemed Jacob," in joyous ways.  

No thirst in deserts, waters from rock,  
Split the stone, let the waters unlock.  

"There’s no peace," Yahweh’s words ring,  
"For the wicked," echoes the King.
