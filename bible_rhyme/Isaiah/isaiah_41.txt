"Keep silent and still, islands, hear my decree,  
Let the people gather strength, then come talk with me.  
Let's meet up for judgment, settle what's right,  
Who from the east was called, with kingdoms in his sight?  

He hands over nations, kings bow to his sword,  
Like dust and stubble, they fall to his word.  
He pursues, passes safely, no paths known before,  
Who's behind this power? It's Yahweh, for sure.  

The islands tremble, earth's ends shake in fear,  
Neighbors rally together, “Be strong!” they cheer.  
The carpenter boosts the smith's aims,  
Hammer strikes, "It is good," they proclaim.  

"But you, Israel, my servant, my chosen one,  
Jacob, offspring of Abraham, my work begun.  
From the earth's corners, I called you near,  
'You're my servant, chosen, no need to fear.'  

Don't be afraid, I'm with you, won't let you fall,  
Upholding you with righteousness, standing tall.  
Your foes will be shamed, lost without a trace,  
Those who war against you, vanish without a face.  

For I, Yahweh, hold your hand, say to you,  
'Don't be afraid, I'll help you through.'  
You worm Jacob, men of Israel, hear me say,  
"I'll help you," says Yahweh, your Redeemer today.  

I've made you sharp, a new threshing tool,  
Mountains crumble, hills become like a fool.  
Winnow them, winds carry them away,  
Rejoice in Yahweh, glory in Him today.  

The thirsty seek water, tongues failing in need,  
I, Yahweh, will answer, meet the plea.  
Rivers on heights, springs in the valleys wide,  
Wilderness pools, dry lands with waters supplied.  

Cedars, acacias, myrtles, oil trees in the land,  
Cypress, pines, box trees, together they stand.  
See, know, consider, Yahweh's hand at play,  
The Holy One of Israel, creating this display.  

"Produce your cause," Yahweh's challenge rings,  
"Bring your reasons, declare the things.  
Show us the past, let us understand,  
Declare what's coming, take a stand."  

Behold, you are nothing, your works hold no weight,  
Choosing false gods leads to an abominable fate.  
"I raised one from the north, he calls my name,  
He treads on rulers, like clay they became.  

Who predicted this, that we might know?  
Not one declares, no insight to show.  
To Zion, I say, 'Look, see what's done,'  
I bring good news, Jerusalem, it’s begun.  

I see no counselor, no word to impart,  
Their deeds are vanity, confusion at heart."
