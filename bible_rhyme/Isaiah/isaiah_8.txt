"Write this down, grab a pen, make it known,  
For Maher Shalal Hash Baz on a tablet of stone."  
Witnesses stand, Uriah and Zechariah's call,  
The prophetess bore a son, Yahweh's words fall.  

“Name him now,” Yahweh said straight to me,  
Before he speaks, Assyria takes what's free.  
Damascus falls, Samaria's riches fly,  
The king of Assyria's marching by.  

The people ignored the whispering stream,  
Chose Rezin and Remaliah's son as their dream.  
So the Lord sends the floodwaters high,  
Assyria’s glory, like rivers, they rise.  

Judah's drowning, waters up to the neck,  
Wings spread wide, Immanuel's land a wreck.  
“Make your noise, battle strong, break in two!  
Arm yourselves, but God’s with you.  

Yahweh warned me with a firm hand's grip,  
“Don't call conspiracy, don’t let your fears slip.  
Respect Yahweh alone, hold Him in dread,  
He'll be sanctuary, or a snare ahead.”  

Israel will trip, stumble, and fall,  
Jerusalem caught, a trap for all.  
Seal up the law, keep it tight,  
I’ll wait for Yahweh, though He hides from sight.  

I and my children, signs in this land,  
From Yahweh of Armies, who makes His stand.  
“Seek no spirits, no wizards who chirp,  
Seek God for the living, not shadows in the dirt.”  

"Turn to the law, the covenant’s might,  
If they're not speaking it, there's no morning light.  
Hungry and strained, they'll curse and they'll frown,  
Look up, find darkness, the deep shadows drown."
