Yo, I'm sought by the ones who didn't ask,  
Found by the seekers shadowed in the past.  
"See me, see me," I called on a flock,  
Who never bore my name, yet still I rock.  

Hands stretched wide to a wild, rebel clan,  
Walking a twisted path, ignoring the plan.  
They spark my fury, face me with their schemes,  
Sacrificing in gardens, dreaming cursed dreams.  

Chillin' with the dead, secret spots at night,  
Eating swine's flesh, broth of things not right.  
"Stay away," they say, "I'm holier than you,"  
Smoke in my nose, flames burning through.  

"Look, it's written, can't stay silent, no way,  
Gonna repay, fold sins in your bay.  
Your sins, your fathers', together they climb,  
Incense on the mountains, blasphemy in rhyme."  

Yahweh speaks, "There's new wine in the vine,  
Don't destroy it, blessings in line.  
From Jacob's seed, my mountains they'll claim,  
Chosen ones dwelling, in my holy name.  

Sharon's fields for flocks, Achor's resting grace,  
For those who've sought me, found their place.  
But you who forget, turning Fortune's card,  
Destined for the sword, life hits hard."  

"When I called, you turned away,  
Did evil deeds, didn't hear what I say.  
So Yahweh says, my servants will dine,  
While you hunger, thirst on the vine.  

They'll rejoice, you'll wail in the night,  
Your name a curse, fading from sight.  
New name for my servants, a truth in their heart,  
Old troubles forgotten, fresh start.  

Behold, new heavens, new earth I create,  
Past wiped away, don't contemplate.  
Jerusalem, my joy, her people my delight,  
No more tears, no cries in the night.  

No infant short-lived, no elder with lost days,  
Children living long, in blessed ways.  
Build homes, plant vines, eat their yield,  
Their work enjoyed, nothing concealed.  

Labor not in vain, nor birth in despair,  
Yahweh’s blessed offspring, in His care.  
Before they call, I’ll answer, I’m near,  
While they speak, I’m ready to hear.  

Wolf and lamb together will feed,  
Lion eats straw, serpent on dust it will lead.  
No harm, no destruction on my holy hill,  
Yahweh speaks, peace, be still."
