In the days of kings, I bring Isaiah's rhyme,  
Judah and Jerusalem, in a troubled time.  

Heaven, earth, lend an ear, hear Yahweh speak,  
"I raised you up, but now my heart is weak.  

The ox knows its master, the donkey its crib,  
But my people, Israel, don't give a rib."  

Sinful nation, iniquity's heap,  
Children of evildoers, in darkness deep.  

"Why continue to rebel, face pain and strife?  
Your head is sick, heart faint, in a broken life."  

From foot to head, wounds untended, sore,  
Your land lies desolate, cities burn evermore.  

Strangers devour, Zion's left alone,  
A shelter in the vineyard, like a melon grown.  

"If not for my remnant, you'd be Sodom and flame,  
Gomorrah's fate, with no name or claim."  

"Hear, rulers of Sodom, listen, people of sin,  
What are your sacrifices? Can't let you in.  

I'm tired of rams, the fat of the beast,  
Blood of bulls, lambs, goats—my spirit's ceased.  

Stop trampling my courts, no more empty praise,  
Incense is foul, in your wicked ways.  

Hate your New Moons, your feasts bring no joy,  
When you spread your hands, I won't employ.  

Wash clean your hands stained with blood,  
Cease evil deeds, stem the flood.  

Seek justice, help the oppressed,  
The fatherless and widow, give them rest."  

"Come, reason with me," Yahweh's words resound,  
"Sins like scarlet, but snow's purity found.  

Be willing, obedient, and feast on the land,  
Refuse, rebel, you'll fall by the sword's hand."  

Faithful city turned to vice,  
Justice gone, murderers entice.  

Silver to dross, wine mixed thin,  
Princes rebellious, thieves let in.  

Yahweh speaks, the Mighty One's call,  
"Relief from foes, my hand will fall.  

Purge your dross, cleanse your tin,  
Restore judges, counselors—begin again.  

City of righteousness, faithful town,  
Zion redeemed, justice will crown."  

Transgressors, sinners, consumed in flame,  
Forsake Yahweh, extinguish your name.  

Ashamed of oaks, confounded by your choice,  
Gardens dry, no water, no voice.  

Strong like tinder, work like a spark,  
Burn together, no quenching the dark."
