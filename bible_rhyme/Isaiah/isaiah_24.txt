Yahweh's got the earth, flips it around,  
Makes it empty, scatters folks all over town.  

Priests and people, all the same fate,  
Servants and masters, equal in their state.  

The earth's drained dry, laid utterly bare,  
For Yahweh spoke, and now it's in despair.  

The earth mourns, fades away, world grows thin,  
High folks languish, feeling it deep within.  

Polluted lands from laws they broke,  
An everlasting promise, gone up in smoke.  

"The curse devours," Yahweh said, it's true,  
Few men remain, the guilty's due.  

New wine mourns, vines are weak,  
Merry hearts sigh, joy's grown bleak.  

Tambourines silent, no more rejoice,  
The harp's joy fades, gone is the voice.  

Wine without songs, drinks turn sour,  
In a city confused, in its final hour.  

Crying in streets, joy's gone away,  
Desolation hits, gates in decay.  

Like olives shaken, remnant few,  
Shout Yahweh's majesty from seas' view.  

"Glorify Yahweh," from east, rise and sing,  
Righteous glory from earth's edge they bring.  

But I cry, "Woe is me!" Treachery's reign,  
Fear, the pit, the snare, mix in the pain.  

Flee the fear, fall in a pit's snare,  
Earth's foundations tremble, beware.  

Earth's torn apart, stagger like a drunk,  
Disobedience heavy, down it sunk.  

Yahweh punishes high and low,  
Prisoners gathered, no place to go.  

Moon and sun in shameful plight,  
For Yahweh reigns on Zion's height.  

Glory before His elders, stands,  
The earth in awe of His mighty hands.
