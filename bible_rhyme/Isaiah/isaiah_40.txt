"Comfort, comfort my people," God declares,  
"Speak to Jerusalem, calm all her cares.  
Her war is done, her sins are pardoned,  
Double for her sins, from Yahweh's hand it's garnered."

A voice cries out, "Prepare the way in the wild,  
Level the highway, make it smooth and styled.  
Every valley raised, each mountain laid low,  
Uneven ground will level, rough places will flow."

Yahweh's glory revealed, all flesh will see,  
For Yahweh's mouth spoke with certainty.  
A voice says, "Cry!" and asks, "What shall I cry?"  
"All flesh is grass, all glory will die."

The grass withers, flowers fall away,  
Yahweh's breath blows, they won't stay.  
But God's word stands firm, forever in place,  
Announce this news, go, make haste.

Go to Zion, climb high, shout strong,  
Tell Jerusalem, declare the song.  
"See, your God comes, mighty and bold,  
With reward and recompense, his story is told."

He feeds his flock like a shepherd would,  
Gathers lambs close, as a shepherd should.  
Who measures waters, gauges the sky,  
Weighs mountains, scales, no counsel nearby?

The nations are droplets, mere specks of dust,  
Islands lift like feathers, in him we trust.  
All offerings fall short, nothing's enough,  
Who compares to God, who shapes us from rough?

Workmen cast idols, but God stands alone,  
He rules from above, on heaven's throne.  
Haven't you heard? Haven't you known?  
He stretches heavens, his power is shown.

Princes are nothing, judges fade away,  
Scarcely planted, they wither, blow astray.  
"To whom will you liken me?" Holy One asks,  
Lift your eyes, see creation, His mighty tasks.

Why say, "My way's hidden, my justice is lost?"  
Yahweh's everlasting, he counts no cost.  
He gives strength to the weak, power to none,  
Youth may faint, but His will be done.

Those who wait on Yahweh will renew and rise,  
With wings like eagles, they soar the skies.  
They'll run without weariness, walk without fall,  
Trust in Yahweh's strength, He empowers all.
