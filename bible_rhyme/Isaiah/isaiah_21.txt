Whirlwinds from the south, sweeping wild and free,  
From the wilderness, a vision I see.  
Treachery abounds, destruction in place,  
Elam, rise up, attack with pace.  

My thighs ache, anguish takes its toll,  
Like a woman's labor, seizing my soul.  
Pain so deep, I can't hear or see,  
My heart flutters, horror grips me.  

Twilight's peace turned to trembling fear,  
Princes, rise up, the moment is near.  
"Go," said the Lord, "set a watchman high,  
Let him declare what meets his eye."  

Horsemen in pairs, troops coming strong,  
Listen well, as they march along.  
He roared like a lion, "I stand my ground,  
Day and night, I watch and sound."  

"Fallen is Babylon," the echoes resound,  
Images broken, idols hit the ground.  
Threshing and grain, Yahweh's decree,  
What I've heard, now you see.  

Voices call from Seir, a watch of the night,  
"The morning comes, so does the night.  
Inquire if you will, come back and see,  
What the watchman’s eyes decree."  

Arabia's burden, in forests they hide,  
Dedan's caravans, seeking a guide.  
Water for the thirsty, bread for the run,  
Fleeing the swords, the battle's begun.  

"Within a year," the Lord spoke clear,  
Kedar's glory will disappear.  
Few archers remain, the strong will fall,  
Yahweh declared it, the God of all.
