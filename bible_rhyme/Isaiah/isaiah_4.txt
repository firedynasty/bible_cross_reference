Seven women grabbing one man tight,  
"We'll feed ourselves, wear what feels right.  
Just give us your name, erase our shame,  
Let us walk in honor, not in blame."  

In that day, Yahweh's branch shines so bright,  
A beauty and glory in survivors' sight.  
Zion's left standing, called holy and true,  
Names of the living in Jerusalem's view.  

The Lord will wash Zion's daughters clean,  
Purge the city's blood, unseen to seen.  
With justice and burning, He'll make it right,  
Purifying Jerusalem, day to night.  

Yahweh creates a cover, Mount Zion's crown,  
Cloud and smoke by day, fire shining down.  
A canopy of glory, a protective embrace,  
Shade from the heat, a sheltering space.  

Refuge and shelter from storm and rain,  
In Yahweh's presence, peace will reign.  
The story wraps with a canopy bright,  
Zion's safe, in His glorious light.
