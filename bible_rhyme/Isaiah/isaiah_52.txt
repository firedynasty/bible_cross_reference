Awake, awake! Take your stand, Zion, strong and bold,  
Put on those garments, beautiful, Jerusalem's tale unfolds.  

Shake off the dust, rise up, lift your head,  
Break those chains, Zion's daughter, lift your dread.  

For Yahweh said, "You were sold with no price,  
Redeemed without money, I'm rolling the dice."  

"My people went to Egypt, found a place to reside,  
Oppressed by Assyrians, with no cause, no pride."  

"What do I do now?" Yahweh questions and cries,  
"My people taken for nothing, while mockers arise."  

"They'll know my name, they'll hear my call,  
It's me who speaks, behold it all."  

Beautiful are the feet on the mountain who bring  
News of peace, salvation, Zion's God as king.  

Watchmen raise voices, in unison they sing,  
Seeing eye to eye, Yahweh back to Zion will bring.  

Break into joy, sing all the waste places,  
Yahweh has comforted, redeemed Jerusalem's spaces.  

He bared his holy arm for nations to see,  
All ends of the earth witness his decree.  

Depart, depart, leave unclean things behind,  
Cleanse yourselves, Yahweh's vessels refined.  

No hasty retreat, no flight in the night,  
For Yahweh leads the charge, Israel's guard in sight.  

"My servant acts wisely, lifted high he'll stand,  
Exalted and honored across the land."  

Astonished they were, at his marred visage,  
Yet he'll cleanse nations, kings silenced in homage.  

Seeing the unseen, understanding the unheard,  
His impact profound, his truth is the word.
