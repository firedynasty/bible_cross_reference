The righteous drop, and no one sheds a tear,  
Merciful souls gone, but no one draws near.  

They step into peace, resting where they lay,  
Walking upright, they find their own way.  

"But come close, you children of deceit,  
Offspring of sin, the ones you can't beat.  

Who do you mock, with that wide, sneering face?  
Aren't you the kids of lies, a real disgrace?  

Among the oaks, you burn with desire,  
Under the trees, your actions conspire.  

Into the valley, the innocent you slay,  
Hidden in rocks, you lead them astray.  

Among smooth stones, you claim your share,  
Pouring out offerings, pretending to care.  

High on the mountains, your bed you make,  
To sacrifice there, your vows you break.  

Behind the doors, memorials stand,  
Exposed to others, breaking the command.  

With the king, you oil and perfumes display,  
To the depths of Sheol, you find your way.  

Though weary, your strength you revive,  
Yet you never say, 'Is this how to survive?'  

"Whom do you fear, that you won't recall?  
Silent I've been, but you don't heed my call.  

I’ll declare your deeds; they won't save you,  
Cry out, and let those you trust pull you through.  

But the wind will snatch them away with ease,  
He who seeks me will possess, will seize.  

"Build up, build up, clear the road ahead,  
Remove the blocks, where my people tread."  

The High One speaks from eternity's place,  
"I dwell with the humble, who seek my face.  

I won’t contend forever, nor stay mad,  
The spirit would faint, the souls would be sad.  

I was angry at greed, I hid, I struck,  
They kept on their path, disregarded their luck.  

I've seen their ways, yet healing's my plan,  
I'll lead and comfort, every mourning man.  

I create peace, near and far, I decree,"  
Says Yahweh, "I will heal thee."  

But the wicked churn like a restless tide,  
No peace for them, in waters they hide.  

"There is no peace," says God, "for the wicked's way,"  
In their troubled seas, they forever sway.
