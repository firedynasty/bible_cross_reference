Yo, listen up, all you thirsty souls,  
Come to the waters, let your bellies be whole.  

No money? No problem, buy and eat,  
Wine and milk without price, that's a treat.  

Why spend cash on stuff that won't fill?  
Chase what satisfies, your soul will thrill.  

"Turn your ear, come hear my voice,  
In my covenant, you'll rejoice."  

Given a leader, a witness so true,  
Call out nations who never knew you.  

Run to Yahweh, your God so near,  
The Holy One will glorify you here.  

Seek Him now, while He's easy to find,  
Call on Him, don't be left behind.  

"Let the wicked forsake his path,  
Return to God, avoid His wrath."  

"My thoughts ain't yours, nor my ways like yours,"  
Says Yahweh, the One who endures.  

"As high as heaven, above the plain,  
My words bring life, like the falling rain."  

"My word goes out, won't return void,  
It'll prosper and grow, bringing joy."  

You'll walk out with peace, joy in your stride,  
Mountains and hills sing, trees clap on the side.  

Thorns replaced by cypress, myrtle blooms instead,  
For Yahweh's fame, everlasting and widespread.
