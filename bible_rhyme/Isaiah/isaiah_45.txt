Yahweh speaks to Cyrus, his chosen hand,  
"Nations will bow, kings won't withstand.  

Doors open wide, gates won't close,  
I'll smooth the rough, break iron and foes.  

Treasures of the dark, secrets revealed,  
So you'll know it's me, Yahweh, who's real.  

For Jacob and Israel, I've called your name,  
I gave you a title, though unknown all the same.  

I am Yahweh, none else, no other God to be,  
I strengthen you, though you don't know me.  

From east to west, let all understand,  
I am Yahweh, the only in this land.  

Light and dark, peace and calamity, I create,  
From heavens above, righteousness will precipitate.  

Woe to those challenging their Maker's hand,  
Like clay questioning the Potter's plan.  

"Ask what's coming, I'll share my design,  
The work of my hands, all things align.  

Earth I made, and man upon it,  
Heavens stretched by my hands, every bit.  

Raised in righteousness, his path made straight,  
Build my city, free exiles, by no debate."  

Labor and riches will follow, chains released,  
They'll know, "God is in you," praises increased.  

Idol-makers confounded, all will see,  
Israel saved by Yahweh eternally.  

Yahweh, who made earth not to waste,  
Speaks clear, not hidden, not in vain chased.  

"Gather now, draw near, you who escaped,  
Idols save not, hear truth unshaped.  

From old I've spoken, Yahweh alone,  
No other savior, no other throne.  

"Look to me, be saved, ends of the earth,  
All knees will bow, knowing my worth.  

In righteousness, my word stands strong,  
In Yahweh alone, strength and song.  

Israel justified, in Yahweh's light,  
Rejoice forever, in his might."
