Who'll believe the word we bring? Who saw Yahweh's mighty swing?  
Like a plant in dry land, he rose, no beauty, no grand shows.  

Despised and rejected, he knew sorrow's face,  
Men turned away, left him without grace.  

He carried our pain, bore the weight of disease,  
Yet folks said he was struck, brought to his knees.  

But pierced for our wrongs, crushed for our lies,  
His punishment brought peace, by his wounds we rise.  

Like lost sheep, we all strayed away,  
Yahweh placed on him our sins that day.  

Oppressed and silent, never spoke a word,  
Led like a lamb, not a sound was heard.  

Taken by judgment, who would have guessed,  
Cut off from life, for our sins he pressed.  

Buried with the wicked, near the rich he lay,  
Though he did no violence, no deceit in his way.  

Yahweh was pleased to let him bear this strife,  
Through pain an offering, yet he sees new life.  

After suffering, he sees the light, satisfied,  
Justifies many, bearing sins, he rides.  

With the great a portion he will gain,  
Shares the spoils through all the pain.  

Poured out his soul, counted with the wrong,  
Bore sins of many, interceded strong.
