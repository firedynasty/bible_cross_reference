"Listen up, seekers of the right,  
Look to your roots, the rock's in sight.  
Abraham's call, when he was alone,  
Blessed and made many, he's not on his own.

Yahweh comforts Zion with grace,  
Wilderness blooms, Eden's embrace.  
Joy and gladness singing loud,  
Thankfulness rising, melody proud.

"Listen, my nation, hear my decree,  
A law from me, justice for all to see.  
Righteousness near, salvation's out,  
Islands will wait, in my arm they shout.

Lift your eyes, the heavens scan,  
Earth will fade, like worn-out land.  
But my salvation's here to stay,  
Forever righteous, come what may.

"Those who know the righteous path,  
Fear not man nor insults' wrath.  
Moths and worms will eat away,  
But my salvation's here to stay.

Awake, awake, Yahweh's might,  
Break the sea, pierce the night.  
Redemption song, to Zion they sing,  
Joy everlasting, on their heads it clings.

"I am the comforter, fear not man,  
Forgotten your Maker, who laid the plan?  
Fear no more the oppressor's might,  
For Yahweh brings you to the light.

Captives freed; bread won't fail,  
I stir the sea, hear the gale.  
Words in your mouth, shadow of my hand,  
Foundations laid, Zion's my land.

Awake, Jerusalem, rise and stand,  
Drunken from wrath, Yahweh's hand.  
No guide among sons, no comforting call,  
Desolation and famine, who will stand tall?

Sons faint, streets their bed,  
Yahweh's wrath fills their head.  
But hear, afflicted, the change is near,  
Taken the cup, away with fear.

No more will you drink the staggering bowl,  
It's for those who walk over your soul.  
Yahweh's justice, hear the call,  
Stand strong, rise above it all."
