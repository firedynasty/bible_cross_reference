In the days of Ahaz, son of Jotham, son of Uzziah's line,  
Two kings rose against Judah, but could not break its spine.  

David's house in fear, hearts trembling in the breeze,  
Like trees in the forest, swaying with unease.  

Then Yahweh called Isaiah, said, "Go forth and meet,  
Ahaz by the waterway, where the upper pools greet.  

Tell him, 'Keep calm, stay cool, don't let fear control,  
These smoldering torches won't consume your soul.  

Rezin and Remaliah scheme to split the land,  
Yahweh declares, 'Their plan won't stand.'"  

"The head of Syria, Damascus, it'll fall,  
Sixty-five years and Ephraim stands no more at all.  

If you don't believe, you won't find your ground,  
Without faith, foundations aren't sound."  

Yahweh then spoke, "Ask a sign from the sky or the deep,"  
Ahaz replied, "I won't ask, I won't make God leap."  

Yahweh sighed, "Hear this, house of David's crew,  
You'll get a sign, the virgin's child, Immanuel is true.  

He'll eat butter and honey, choose good over sin,  
Before he knows it, those feared lands won't win.  

Yahweh brings days of old, when brother split from kin,  
The Assyrian king will come, a new era will begin.  

In that day, Yahweh's call will bring the fly and the bee,  
Resting in valleys, on all hedges, wild and free.  

The Lord will shave with a razor sharp and cold,  
Assyria's king will strip the hair, strong and bold.  

A man will keep a cow, two sheep by his side,  
Milk's abundance will bring butter, honey will abide.  

The land once rich with vines, now briers and thorns,  
Arrows and bows will roam where peace was born.  

Fields once cultivated, now fear the thorn's rise,  
Oxen and sheep will tread where hope lies."
