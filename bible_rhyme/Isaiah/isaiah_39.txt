Merodach Baladan, Babylon's king,  
Sent gifts, heard Hezekiah's bell did ring.  
Recovered from sickness, feeling fine,  
Hezekiah showed off all that's mine.  

Silver, gold, spices, oil, and more,  
Opened up his house, showed every door.  
Isaiah the prophet came and asked,  
"What did they say? From where, at last?"  

"From far-off Babylon," Hezekiah replied,  
"They've seen my treasures, nothing to hide."  
Isaiah spoke with a word from Yahweh,  
"Days are coming, hear what I say.  

All your riches, all stored away,  
To Babylon will go, none will stay.  
Sons of yours taken, made eunuchs then,  
In the Babylon palace, among those men."  

Hezekiah nodded, "Yahweh’s word is true,  
For now, there’ll be peace, in days I view."  
Thus the story ends, a lesson clear,  
What you show today may disappear.
