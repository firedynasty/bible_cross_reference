"Behold my servant," said the Lord of might,  
"My chosen one, in whom I find delight."  
Spirit on him, justice he will bring,  
No shouting, no loud voice, no street echoing.  

He won't break a bruised reed, nor dim wick snuff,  
Faithful in justice, strong and tough.  
Won't fail, won't tire till earth's justice is done,  
Islands await his law, they'll run.  

God who stretched the heavens wide and far,  
Gave breath to all beneath the star.  
"I called you righteous, held your hand,"  
"Made you a light, a covenant grand."  

"Blind eyes opened, prisoners set free,  
Out of dark dungeons, to light they'll see."  
"I am Yahweh, that name's mine alone,  
Glory won't share, nor praise to stone."  

"Old things are done, I declare the new,  
Telling you 'fore they come through."  
Sing a new song, let earth's praises rise,  
Seas and islands, lift to the skies.  

Wilderness, villages, shout, lift voice,  
Kedar's people, mountains rejoice.  
Glory to Yahweh, praise in the land,  
Mighty like a warrior, he'll stand.  

"I've been silent long, held back my shout,  
Now I cry like a woman, let it out.  
Mountains crushed, rivers dry,  
Blind'll see light, paths straightened nigh."  

"Idol worshippers turned back in shame,  
They'll see their gods can't stake a claim."  
"Hear, you deaf, look, blind, to see,  
Who's more blind than my servant be?"  

"See much, observe not, ears open wide,  
But listen not," the truth they hide.  
Yahweh's law lifted, righteousness raised,  
Yet people snared, in prisons caged.  

Who'll hear, who'll listen for the hour near?  
Jacob plundered, Israel in fear.  
Yahweh's wrath burned like fire untamed,  
Yet heeded not, nor heart inflamed."
