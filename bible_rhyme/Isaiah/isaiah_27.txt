Yahweh's got a sword, hard and strong,  
Leviathan's gonna learn where it belongs.  

In that day, hear the vineyard's sweet call,  
"I, Yahweh, keep it, protect it all.  

Watering it every moment, night and day,  
No harm shall come, no enemy's play.  

I'm not wrathful, but briers beware,  
I'll march on them, burn them, I'm fair.  

Hold my strength, make peace with me,  
Come, let it be, come, let it be."  

Jacob will root, Israel's bloom in sight,  
Filling the world, fruit shining bright.  

Did they fall like those who struck them down?  
Or did they rise, wearing a new crown?  

Sins washed away by the east wind's blast,  
Altar stones crushed, their past is past.  

The city stands empty, deserted like a wild,  
A lone calf lies, where branches are piled.  

When boughs wither, broken, burned to flame,  
No compassion left, but who's to blame?  

Yahweh shall thresh from river to stream,  
Gathering Israel, fulfilling the dream.  

In that day, hear the trumpet's sound,  
Assyria's lost, Egypt's found.  

They'll worship Yahweh on the holy hill,  
In Jerusalem, hearts fulfilled.
