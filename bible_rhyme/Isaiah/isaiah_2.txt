Isaiah saw a vision, clear as day,  
Judah and Jerusalem in a major way.  

"Yahweh's house on mountains high,  
Above the hills, it touches the sky.  

Nations stream, they all will say,  
'To Yahweh's mount, let's find our way.  

He'll teach His paths, His righteous ways,  
From Zion's heart, His law displays.'"  

Judge the nations, make the call,  
Swords to plows, no war at all.  

"House of Jacob, walk in light,  
Leave behind the darkest night."  

But they've turned, filled from the east,  
With diviners, seeking a foreign feast.  

Silver, gold, treasures stored,  
Chariots plenty, idols adored.  

Man is low, pride will fall,  
Yahweh's glory over all.  

"Hide in rocks, dust your bed,  
Fear Yahweh's majesty," He said.  

Every cedar, every oak,  
Mountains high, His fire stokes.  

Lofty towers, walls of might,  
Ships of Tarshish, fade from sight.  

Pride bows down, idols fade,  
In Yahweh's glory, all is made.  

Men will flee to rocks and holes,  
As Yahweh's terror shakes their souls.  

Gold and silver, idols cast,  
To bats and moles, things of the past.  

Caves and clefts, there they'll hide,  
Yahweh's glory won't subside.  

"Stop trusting man, whose breath will end,  
In Yahweh alone, make Him your friend."
