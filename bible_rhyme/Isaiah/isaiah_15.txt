In the night, Moab’s land lays bare,  
Ar and Kir in ruin, despair everywhere.  

Up to Bayith, Dibon, they climb to weep,  
Moab wails for Nebo, sorrow runs deep.  

Heads bald, beards shorn, mourning in sight,  
In sackcloth they roam, both day and night.  

Heshbon cries out, Elealeh too,  
Voices rise, trembling hearts break through.  

"My heart cries for Moab!" Zoar they flee,  
Up Luhith’s ascent, their tears like the sea.  

Waters of Nimrim, desolate and dry,  
Grass withered away, hope's in short supply.  

Gathered goods carried, across the brook they sway,  
Borders wailing loud, as hope fades away.  

Dimon’s waters, stained with red,  
Yet more to come, more dread ahead.  

"A lion comes," the warning’s clear,  
For Moab's last, the end draws near.
