A king will come with justice in his hand,  
Princes ruling wisely across the land.  

A man like a shelter from the fierce storm’s lash,  
Streams in a desert, shade from the sun’s harsh.  

Eyes wide open, ears that truly hear,  
Hearts understanding, tongues speaking clear.  

Fools won't be called noble, scoundrels lose their fame,  
Folly's their language, with error they shame.  

Fools speak folly, hearts plot their deceit,  
Empty the hungry, let the thirsty’s need repeat.  

Scoundrel’s ways are twisted, plotting wicked plans,  
Destroying the humble with lies from their hands.  

But noble minds conceive noble dreams,  
And in their noble acts, truth gleams.  

"Rise up, you women, hear my call," I say,  
"You careless daughters, heed my words today.  

Soon you'll be troubled, harvest will fail,  
Tremble, strip off ease, your comforts pale."  

Beat your breasts for fields once lush and fine,  
Thorns and briers take over the vine.  

Desolate palaces, joy's city now empty,  
Wild donkeys and flocks roam aplenty.  

Until the Spirit rains from on high,  
Turning wilderness to fields lush and wide.  

Justice will make its home in barren lands,  
Righteousness will flourish with steady hands.  

Peace and quiet confidence will remain,  
My people dwell safe in the new domain.  

Hail may flatten, but blessings flow,  
For those who sow by waters, seeds they sow.
