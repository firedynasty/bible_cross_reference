Send those lambs to the land's ruler's hand,  
From Selah to Zion’s mountain, upon the sand.  

Like birds wandering, nests scattered and gone,  
Moab's daughters drift to the fords of Arnon.  

"Give counsel! Seek justice! Shade like night,  
In the midday sun, keep the outcasts from sight."  

"Let my outcasts dwell, shelter Moab from strife,  
For the oppressor's vanished, threats lose their life."  

A throne will rise in kindness, true and bold,  
In David's tent, where justice's tale is told.  

Moab's pride, we've heard it loud,  
Arrogance and wrath, but it’s empty and proud.  

Moab will wail, for Moab cries in pain,  
Raisin cakes of Kir Hareseth, mourned in vain.  

Fields of Heshbon and Sibmah's vine,  
Nations broke branches, now nothing's fine.  

I’ll weep like Jazer for Sibmah's lost vine,  
Tears fall on Heshbon, in sorrow they entwine.  

Joy's gone from the fields, no songs in the air,  
Wine presses are silent, no harvest to share.  

My heart plays for Moab, like a harp it cries,  
For Kir Heres, my soul in sorrow lies.  

When Moab prays at high places, seeking grace,  
His hopes fall empty, no victory to embrace.  

Yahweh spoke of Moab in days long past,  
Now He says, “Three years will see Moab’s glory cast.”  

The multitude shrinks, its glory turned slight,  
Moab's pride diminishes, fading from sight.
