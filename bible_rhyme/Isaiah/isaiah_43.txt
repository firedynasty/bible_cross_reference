Yahweh speaks to Jacob, to Israel's name,  
"Fear not, I've redeemed you, you're mine to claim.  

When waters rise, I'll be by your side,  
Through fire you'll walk, unburned, in stride.  

For I am Yahweh, your God, your Savior,  
Egypt given in ransom, Ethiopia's favor.  

You've been precious, honored in my sight,  
In exchange for you, nations take flight.  

Fear not, I'm with you, east to west I'll call,  
North give 'em up, south don't stall.  

Bring sons from afar, daughters from earth's ends,  
All called by my name, my glory transcends.  

Bring the blind who see not, the deaf who don't hear,  
Nations gather round, let truth appear.  

"You are my witnesses," Yahweh's decree,  
"Chosen to know, believe in me.  

Before me no God, none after, I stand,  
I alone save, by my own hand.  

I've declared, saved, and shown from the start,  
No strange god among you, my witnesses' part.  

From time's dawn, I'm He, none can resist,  
I will work, who dares to insist?"  

Yahweh, your Redeemer, Holy Israel's King,  
Says, "For you to Babylon I've sent and will bring.  

I am Yahweh, your Holy One, Creator, King, your own,  
Making paths through seas, armies overthrown.  

Forget old things, behold the new,  
A way in wilderness, rivers in view.  

Wild beasts honor me, for waters I give,  
To quench my chosen, to help them live.  

Yet, Jacob, you did not call, grown weary of my way,  
No offerings brought, no sacrifices to lay.  

No sweet cane or fat for my delight,  
But burdened me with sins, day and night.  

I, even I, erase your wrongs, for my sake,  
Your sins forgotten, it's a new break.  

Let us reason, plead together, make your case,  
First fathers sinned, teachers disgraced.  

Princes defiled, Jacob becomes a curse,  
Israel an insult, for better or worse."
