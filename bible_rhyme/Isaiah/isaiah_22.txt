In the valley of vision, what’s up on the roof?  
Shouts in the city, but no sword as proof.  

Rulers ran, arrows fly, bound and gone,  
“Look away, let me weep, for my people are done.”  

Confusion and treading, walls break, cries high,  
Elam’s quiver, chariots ride, shields rise to the sky.  

Valleys filled, horsemen at the gate,  
Judah’s cover gone, now armor's the state.  

Breaches in David’s city, waters pooled,  
Houses counted, broken, walls renewed.  

Reservoirs built, but they forgot the plan,  
Ignored the One who knew, from the very first span.  

Called to weep, mourn, sackcloth, and tears,  
But they feast, “Tomorrow we die, so cheers!”  

Yahweh whispers in my ear, can't overlook,  
“Your sin stays till death, by the book.”  

“Go to Shebna,” says the Lord, “Ask him why  
He carves a high tomb, like he'll never die.”  

Yahweh will toss him, spin him ‘round,  
Like a ball thrown far, he'll hit the ground.  

Chariots lost, pride in disgrace,  
Out of office, out of place.  

Eliakim called, robe, and belt bestowed,  
Key of David on his shoulder, the path showed.  

He'll open doors no one can close,  
A nail in place, where glory grows.  

Glory hangs, vessels from cups to pitcher,  
But one day the nail will give, can’t hold the fixture.  

“The nail will fall,” Yahweh declares,  
“The burden cut off, for I’m aware.”
