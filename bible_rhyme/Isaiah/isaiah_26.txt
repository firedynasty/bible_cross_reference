We got a strong city, walls of salvation stand tall,  
"Open the gates for the righteous who heed my call."  
Keep your mind steady, peace on the way,  
Trust in Yahweh, an everlasting Rock each day.  

High cities brought down, laid low to the dust,  
The poor tread over, their steps are just.  
The just walk straight, paths made right,  
In your ways, Yahweh, we seek you at night.  

"My soul yearns in the dark, my spirit seeks light,  
When your judgments rain, the world learns what's right."  
Show favor to the wicked, they just won’t see,  
Your hand is lifted, Yahweh’s majesty.  

"Peace ordained for us, you’ve done all our work,"  
"Other lords ruled us, now only your name we affirm."  
The dead won’t rise, their memory fades,  
You’ve grown the nation, Yahweh, your glory cascades.  

In trouble, they seek you, prayers rise in need,  
Like a woman in labor, we cry, and plead.  
We birthed the wind, no deliverance found,  
But your dead will live, rise from the ground.  

"Awake, sing from the dust, earth will release,  
Enter your rooms, hide till the storm sees peace."  
For Yahweh comes out, justice to impart,  
The earth will reveal the blood, the truth in every heart.
