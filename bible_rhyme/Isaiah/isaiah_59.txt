Yahweh’s arm ain't short, it can still save,  
His ear ain't dull, He hears every wave.  

But sins have built a wall, a great divide,  
Made Him hide His face, where could He abide?  

Your hands, stained with blood, fingers in deceit,  
Lips spilling lies, tongues wicked on repeat.  

Justice's gone missing, truth can't find its way,  
Chasing empty dreams, birthing sin each day.  

Hatching deadly eggs, spinning webs so sly,  
Eating brings death, vipers lurking by.  

These webs can’t clothe, works of sin won't hide,  
Violence in their hands, righteousness denied.  

Feet rush to evil, swift to spill blood,  
Thoughts full of ruin, paths lead to the flood.  

Peace is unknown, justice can't be found,  
Paths twisted and crooked, silence all around.  

Justice far, light has turned to night,  
Groping like the blind, lost without sight.  

Roaring like bears, bitter moans fill the air,  
Looking for justice, but none is there.  

Sins stacked high, they testify and scold,  
Transgressing, denying, hearts grown cold.  

Justice turned back, truth's on the street,  
Uprightness locked out, defeat's complete.  

Truth lacking, those who stand alone are prey,  
Yahweh sees this mess, displeased by the decay.  

No intercessor found, no one to save,  
His own arm brings salvation, His righteousness brave.  

Righteousness as armor, salvation on His head,  
Vengeance clothing Him, with zeal as His thread.  

Repaying deeds, wrath for foes,  
From east to west, His glory grows.  

"A Redeemer's coming," Yahweh's voice resounds,  
"To Zion, to those turning around."  

"My covenant's firm, My Spirit won't flee,  
My words in your mouth, for eternity."
