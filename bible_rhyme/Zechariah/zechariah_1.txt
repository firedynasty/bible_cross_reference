In the eighth month's glow, second year of Darius' show,  
Yahweh spoke to Zechariah, words began to flow.  

"Yahweh's displeased with your fathers, hear me now,"  
"Return to me," He said, "I'll come to you somehow."  

"Don't be like them, who ignored my call,"  
They didn't listen, and they faced the fall.  

"Fathers are gone, prophets don't last,"  
But my words remain, standing fast.  

They repented, realizing their fate,  
"Yahweh dealt with us, as our ways relate."  

Shebat's eleventh month, the vision's clear,  
A man on a red horse, standing near.  

Among myrtle trees in a ravine's fold,  
Red, brown, white horses, mysteries to be told.  

I asked, "What are these, my Lord?"  
The angel said, "I'll show, heed my word."  

The man among trees spoke, "Yahweh's spies,"  
They roam the earth, under the skies.  

Reporting back, "The earth's at peace,"  
But the angel asked, "When will mercy release?"  

Yahweh answered with comfort and grace,  
"I burn with jealousy for Zion's place."  

"Angry at nations, too much they've done,"  
"My mercy returns, Jerusalem won."  

"My house rebuilt, lines drawn with care,"  
"My cities prosper, Yahweh's there."  

I lifted my eyes, four horns I saw,  
"What are these?" I asked in awe.  

"Horns scattered Judah, Israel's plight,"  
Four craftsmen come to set things right.  

"To cast down nations, terrify, disband,"  
For Judah's sake, they'll take a stand.
