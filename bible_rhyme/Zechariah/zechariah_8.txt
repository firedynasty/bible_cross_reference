The word of Yahweh came, strong and true,  
"Jealous for Zion, with a wrathful hue."  

"I’ve returned to Zion, where truth shall reign,  
In Jerusalem's heart, my presence will remain.  

Old men, old women, strolling the street,  
Boys and girls playing where laughter meets.  

If it’s marvelous to you, why not to me?"  
Yahweh of Armies declares with decree.  

"From east and west, my people I'll save,  
In Jerusalem they'll dwell, as I pave the way.  

Be strong, hear this from prophets of old,  
When no wages were paid, nor peace up for hold.  

I won't be as I was in the days gone past,  
The seed and the vine, their fruit will last.  

Once a curse, now a blessing you'll be,  
So fear not, let your hands work free.  

I thought of wrath when your fathers strayed,  
But now I plan good, so don't be afraid.  

Speak truth to your neighbor, let peace preside,  
Avoid false oaths, let no evil reside.  

The word of Yahweh comes once more,  
Fasts turn to feasts, joy to the core.  

Many will come from cities and lands,  
Seeking Yahweh's favor, in united bands.  

In those days, ten will hold fast  
To the skirt of a Jew, for God is with you, at last."
