In the fourth year of Darius, the scene was set,  
Yahweh's word to Zechariah, on the date you won't forget.  

From Bethel they came, Sharezer and Regem Melech too,  
Seeking Yahweh's favor, wondering what to do.  

"Should we weep in the fifth month, again this year,  
As we've done in the past, shedding many a tear?"  

Then Yahweh's word came, strong and clear,  
"Speak to the people, let all the priests hear.  

When you fasted and mourned, was it for me?  
Or was it for yourselves, can't you see?  

When you eat and drink, isn't it self-driven?  
Aren't these the words by prophets once given?  

Back when Jerusalem was bustling with life,  
And around her, cities free from strife."  

Then Yahweh spoke again, with a message profound,  
"True judgment, kindness, let them abound.  

Don't oppress the widow, the fatherless, or the poor,  
Show compassion, keep evil from your core."  

But they turned away, refused to hear the call,  
Hardened their hearts, unyielding as a wall.  

Yahweh’s wrath was stirred, from neglect and defiance,  
He called, they ignored, now they face the consequence.  

"Just as they ignored, and turned from my voice,  
I'll scatter them far, they've left me no choice.  

A whirlwind will take them to lands unknown,  
For the desolate land is what they have sown."  

Thus the scene is set, the message is clear,  
Actions have consequences, heed what you hear.
