Eyes lifted high, saw a man with a line,  
"Where you headed?" I asked, in the divine.  

He said, "To measure Jerusalem, width and length,"  
An angel stepped out, with heavenly strength.  

"Run, tell that young man, hear what I say,  
Jerusalem's vast, no walls in the way."  

"Many will gather with livestock and kin,  
I'll be a fire, my glory within."  

"Come on, flee north, leave Babylon's land,  
I've scattered you wide, like dust from the hand."  

"Zion, escape! The call is loud,  
Let's move from Babylon, join this crowd."  

For Yahweh declares, with armies in tow,  
"Who touches you, feels my wrath, you know."  

"Shake my hand, watch them fall and break,  
Those who served will plunder for your sake."  

"Sing, daughter of Zion, rejoice with me,  
I'm coming to dwell, you'll see who I'll be."  

"Many nations will join, they'll all be mine,  
I'll dwell among you, everything's fine."  

"Judah's my choice, my land, my delight,  
Jerusalem chosen, shining so bright."  

Be silent, all flesh, Yahweh awakes,  
From His holy place, a move He makes.
