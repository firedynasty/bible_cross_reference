Yahweh speaks, listen clear, hear His call,  
He stretched the heavens wide, made earth stand tall.  
Inside man, His spirit He formed with care,  
Now His words to Israel, hear them declare:

"Jerusalem a cup all nations will drain,  
Judah too, feeling the siege's strain.  
On that day, Jerusalem's weight they'll meet,  
All who touch it will suffer defeat.  

Horses in terror, riders losing their minds,  
My eyes on Judah, sightless foes left behind.  
Chieftains whisper, 'Inhabitants strong,'  
Their strength in Yahweh, where they belong.  

On that day, Judah's leaders like flames,  
Devouring nations, spreading their names.  
Jerusalem's place, secure and tight,  
"Yahweh saves," Judah's future bright.  

Feeble as David, defense is sure,  
Like Yahweh’s angel, steadfast and pure.  
Nations gathered, but destined to fall,  
Jerusalem's foes, Yahweh will call.  

Grace like rain, pouring down,  
On David's house, grace profound.  
They'll look to Me, pierced with grief's sting,  
Mourning deep, a bitter cry they sing.  

In that day, mourning fills the air,  
Like Megiddon's tears, the valley's despair.  
Each family, separate in their pain,  
David, Nathan, Levi—mourning's refrain.  

Apart they stand, wives and kin,  
In solitude, their mourning begins.  
Every family, each their part,  
Tears and sorrow, heavy on the heart."
