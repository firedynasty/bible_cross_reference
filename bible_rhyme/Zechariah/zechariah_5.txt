I looked up high, what did I see?  
A scroll flying by, as plain as can be.  

He asked, "What do you see there, my friend?"  
"A flying scroll," I said, "twenty cubits long end to end."  

"This is the curse," he declared with power,  
"For thieves and liars, their final hour."  

"It enters the thief's home, no place to hide,  
Tearing down walls, swallowing pride."  

"Lift your eyes," the angel drew near,  
"See what appears, don't you fear."  

"What is it now?" I asked in awe,  
"An ephah basket, that's what I saw."  

A lead weight lifted, a woman inside,  
"This is Wickedness," he said, open wide.  

He tossed her down, sealed her tight,  
Locked in the basket, out of sight.  

Two women flew with wind in their wings,  
Stork-like they soared, carrying things.  

"Where do they take her?" I asked the guide,  
"To Shinar," he said, "where she will reside."  

A house to be built, her own domain,  
In the land of Shinar, she'll remain.
