In that day, a spring will flow, David's house will know,  
A cleansing for sin, Jerusalem's glow.  

Yahweh of Armies speaks with might,  
"Idols' names gone, lost from sight."  

Prophets and impure spirits, out they'll go,  
“The land will be pure, just so you know.”  

If a prophet dares to rise and lie,  
Parents will say, "For this, you must die."  

No hairy cloaks to deceive and pretend,  
"I’m no prophet, just a farmer, my friend."  

"What are these wounds?" someone will ask,  
"In my friends' house, they gave me this task."  

"Awake, sword, strike my shepherd," Yahweh cries,  
Sheep will scatter, as confusion flies.  

Two parts gone, but one remains,  
In the fire, I'll purify, break the chains.  

Silver refined, gold tested, strong,  
They'll call Me, I'll listen, where they belong.  

"It is my people," Yahweh will declare,  
"We are His, in His constant care."
