I lifted my eyes, and what did I see?  
Four chariots rolling between bronze peaks, wild and free.  

The first had red horses, fierce and bold,  
In the second, black horses, dark as coal.  

The third rode in with steeds of white,  
And the fourth, dappled, a powerful sight.  

I asked the angel, "My lord, what’s the deal?"  
He said, "These are the four winds, heaven's seal."  

The black horses charge to the north land,  
The white behind, as if hand in hand.  

The dappled head south, spreading wide,  
While strong ones roam the earth with pride.  

He spoke, "Go, walk the earth, roam and track!"  
So they moved, forth and back.  

Then he called, "Those in the north,  
They've quieted my spirit from henceforth."  

Yahweh’s word came, bold and clear,  
"Gather from captivity, bring them near.  

Heldai, Tobijah, Jedaiah, come today,  
To Josiah’s house, where they from Babylon stay.  

Take silver, take gold, craft a crown,  
Place it on Joshua, the high priest renowned.  

Tell him, 'Yahweh of Armies declares,  
The Branch will rise, building temple stairs.  

Yes, he will build, he’ll bear the glory,  
He’ll sit on the throne, that's the story.  

He'll rule and priest, in peace unite,  
The counsel of peace shall be their light.  

Crowns for Helem, Tobijah, Jedaiah, Hen,  
A memorial in Yahweh’s temple, amen.  

From afar, they’ll come to build Yahweh’s place,  
You'll know Yahweh sent me in this grace.  

It’ll happen if you heed and hear,  
Yahweh’s voice, loud and clear."
