Open up, Lebanon, let the fire consume your trees,  
Cypress and cedar fall to their knees.  

Oaks of Bashan, hear the cries in the wood,  
The strong have fallen, their pride misunderstood.  

Hear the shepherds wail, their glory's gone,  
Lions roar, the Jordan's pride is withdrawn.  

Yahweh declares, "Feed the destined flock,  
Buyers kill with impunity, stock by stock."  

"Shepherds sell and count their gold,  
No pity shown, just stories told."  

"I won't spare the people," declares Yahweh strong,  
"Men turn on each other, right or wrong."  

I fed the destined, the oppressed in their plight,  
Held two staffs close, called 'Favor' and 'Union' tight.  

Three shepherds cut off, in a month, they were done,  
Weary of them, their loathing for me begun.  

I said, "I won't feed, let the dying die,  
And those left consume, no need to ask why."  

Broke Favor's staff, covenant shattered wide,  
The flock knew Yahweh's word couldn't hide.  

"If you deem it right, give me my pay,  
If not, then keep it," I did say.  

They counted out thirty silver coins, my due,  
Yahweh said, "To the potter, throw them, untrue."  

In Yahweh’s house, to the potter, went the price,  
Union's staff broke, end of brotherhood's ties.  

Yahweh spoke, "Take the foolish shepherd's gear,  
A new shepherd rises, one who won't care."  

"Won't mend the broken, nor tend the flock,  
He'll feast on the fat, tear apart the stock."  

"Woe to the shepherd who deserts his post,  
Sword on his arm, his eye's vision lost."
