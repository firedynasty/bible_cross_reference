A day of Yahweh's wrath, the nations drawn near,  
Jerusalem's plunder divided, instilling fear.  

The city falls, homes rifled, women in tears,  
Half the folks in chains, but some remain here.  

"Yahweh fights," a battle like days of old,  
Mount Olives splits, a mighty tale to be told.  

Flee through the valley, like an earthquake's flight,  
Yahweh comes with His holy ones, a powerful sight.  

No light or frost, uniqueness in the air,  
Evening brings light, His presence everywhere.  

Living waters flow, from Jerusalem they stream,  
East and west they go, like a vivid dream.  

Yahweh reigns as King, earth under His sway,  
On that day, His name alone we will say.  

Land like Arabah, the heights raised high,  
Jerusalem dwells safe, peace in the sky.  

A plague strikes down those who dared to fight,  
Flesh, eyes, tongue consume, a terrible sight.  

Panic spreads, neighbors turn hand on hand,  
Judah fights bravely, wealth in the land.  

Gold, silver, cloth, the nations' riches stored,  
Animals stricken too, by Yahweh’s word.  

Survivors each year will come and kneel,  
Worship the King, Yahweh, whose power is real.  

No rain for those who refuse the feast,  
Egypt and others, punished like the least.  

On horses' bells, "HOLY TO YAHWEH" rings wide,  
Every pot made holy, Yahweh’s pride.  

No Canaanite found in Yahweh’s sacred place,  
His house purified, full of grace.
