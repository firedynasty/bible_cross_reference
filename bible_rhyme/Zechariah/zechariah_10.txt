"Ask Yahweh for rain in spring's embrace,  
He makes storm clouds, showers fall to grace.  

Teraphim's lies, diviners deceive,  
False dreams they spin, but no reprieve.  

Sheep wander lost, no shepherd's care,  
Oppressed they roam, burdened by despair.  

"My wrath's for shepherds, goats I’ll scold,  
Yahweh's flock will be bold.  

From Judah comes the stone, the nail, the bow,  
Mighty rulers, together they grow.  

Tread down the streets, mud under their feet,  
Fight with Yahweh, their victory's sweet.  

"I'll strengthen Judah, Joseph I'll save,  
In mercy's light, they'll leave the grave.  

"I am Yahweh, their God who hears,  
Ephraim rejoices, sheds no tears.  

Children see joy like wine's delight,  
In Yahweh's name, hearts take flight.  

"I'll signal, gather, redeem anew,  
Increase their number, as they once grew.  

Scattered afar, they'll remember my name,  
With children return, they'll never be the same.  

"From Egypt's grip, from Assyria's hand,  
I'll lead them to Gilead, Lebanon's land.  

"Through seas of trouble, I'll make a way,  
Nile's depths dry, pride will sway.  

Strength in Yahweh, they'll walk in his fame,  
Up and down, in his holy name," says Yahweh.
