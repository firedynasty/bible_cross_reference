The angel came back, shook me from my sleep,  
Woke me up like a man from slumber deep.  

"What do you see?" he asked me with a gaze,  
"I see a gold lamp stand with its seven blaze,  

A bowl on top, seven lamps alight,  
Pipes to each lamp, shining bright.  

Two olive trees stand, one left, one right,"  
I asked, "What are these?" in the angel's sight.  

"Don't you know?" the angel asked, sincere,  
I replied, "No, my lord, make it clear."  

Then he spoke with Yahweh's word to say,  
"Not by might or power, but my Spirit's way."  

"Who are you, mountain, rising in pride?  
Before Zerubbabel, you'll be flat and wide.  

He'll bring the capstone, with shouts of grace,  
Calling 'Grace, grace!' in this sacred place."  

Yahweh's word came straight to me,  
"Zerubbabel's hands laid the foundation, see?  

He'll finish the work, his hands will do,  
Then you'll know Yahweh sent me to you.  

Who despises the day of things so small?  
These seven shall rejoice, watch the plumb line fall.  

Yahweh's eyes, they search the earth,  
Back and forth, seeing every worth."  

I asked again, "What are these olive trees,  
Side by side with the lamp stand, if you please?"  

Twice I asked, "What are these branches of gold,  
Pouring oil from spouts, so bold?"  

He replied, "Don't you know?" once more,  
I said, "No, my lord, I implore."  

"These are the anointed ones," he said to me,  
"Standing by the Lord of earth, faithfully."
