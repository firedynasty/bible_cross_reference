I saw Joshua stand before the angel's light,  
With Satan on his right, ready to fight.  
God spoke to Satan with a rebuke so bold,  
“Yahweh rebukes you, Satan, behold!  

This is a stick plucked from the fire’s heat,"  
Joshua's garments were filthy, at his feet.  
Angel said, "Remove those filthy clothes,  
Your sins are gone, and new robes I bestow.”  

“Put a clean turban on his head," was my call,  
They clothed him fresh, standing tall.  
Yahweh’s angel spoke, words clear and bright,  
“Walk in my ways, you'll judge with right.  

Keep my courts, you'll have a place to stand,  
Among these here, in this holy land.  
Joshua, hear, you and your crew,  
For I’ll bring the Branch, something new.  

Before Joshua, a stone with seven eyes,  
Yahweh's engraving makes the iniquity fly.  
In one day, sins will vanish away,  
Then each neighbor, beneath vine and fig stay.”  

Neighbors gathered, peace in sight,  
Under the fig, in Yahweh’s light.
